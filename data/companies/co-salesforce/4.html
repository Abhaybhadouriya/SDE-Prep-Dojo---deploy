Worked example: the payment processing platform

An MTS candidate who was selected (2026-08) got this HLD. Source: post 8475325.

  1. The prompt. Design a large-scale payment processing platform end to end.
  2. How it ran. A high-level architecture first, then detailed discussion of request flow, scalability, reliability, consistency, idempotency, failure handling, retries, database choices, and monitoring.
  3. What was probed most. The post says most follow-ups came from design trade-offs and non-functional requirements, not from the boxes.
  4. Lesson. Write the NFRs with numbers early (latency, availability, no double charge), then use them to justify every choice: idempotency keys, a ledger, retries with backoff and reconciliation.

Full design on this site: Payment System.

Scenario: The bank timed out

Your service called the bank, waited 10 seconds and timed out. The user taps "Pay" again.
Weak answer: Retry the charge.
Strong answer: Mark the payment PENDING with its idempotency key, never charge again blindly; query the bank for the status of the first attempt, and reconcile later from the bank's report. The second tap reuses the same key and returns the same result.
  • How do you design the ledger?
  • What is your retry policy for webhooks?
