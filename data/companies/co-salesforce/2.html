The loop: rounds, what each tests, and the order

Most reports describe the same order. Rounds are eliminatory: you hear back after each one.

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • OA: online assessment, a timed test before the live rounds.
  • HM: hiring manager round.
  • FR / NFR: functional and non-functional requirements.
  • Level codes on this page (AMTS, MTS, SMTS): see the shared level map for which counts as SDE-1, SDE-2 or senior.

LLDClasses, interfaces, patterns, schemaLogging framework, Spotify classes, parking lot. Write a lot in the shared doc.
HLD / system designRequirements, scale, storage, failure handlingAbout 60 minutes (post). A round labelled LLD can turn into HLD (post).
Hiring managerOwnership, depth of your own project, values fitOften a deep CV review or an HLD of your own project; one HM round was mostly an HLD (job scheduler).

Timeline. The posts rarely give dates between rounds, so we do not quote a number of weeks. What they do say: each round gates the next, and the hiring-manager round can be on-site.
