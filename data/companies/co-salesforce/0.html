The story: one big building, many locked rooms

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine one giant office building. Thousands of different companies rent rooms in it. They share the lifts, the water pipes and the electricity, but nobody can walk into another company's room. Salesforce is that building for business software: many customer companies ("tenants") share the same servers, and each one must feel safe and fast. That is why their first value is Trust, and why their interviewers love questions about limits, logins and not losing data.

From your world

Multi-tenancy is like VRFs on a router. Many customers share one box, but each has its own routing table and must never see the others' traffic. A per-tenant API limit is like per-port policing: one noisy customer must not starve the rest. You already think this way; in the interview you just say "tenant" instead of "VRF".

This page is for SDE-1 and SDE-2 candidates. At Salesforce India the titles are AMTS (entry level), MTS and SMTS. Posts treat AMTS as roughly SDE-1, MTS as roughly SDE-2 (one report literally says "MTS (SDE-2)"), and SMTS as a senior SDE-2. This mapping is community wording, not an official ladder, so confirm the level with your recruiter.
