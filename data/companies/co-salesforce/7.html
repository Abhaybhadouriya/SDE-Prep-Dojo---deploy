Behavioral angle: Salesforce values as STAR stories

Salesforce publishes five core values: Trust, Customer Success, Innovation, Equality and Sustainability (see the official values page). The hiring-manager round mixes these with a deep dive into your own project. Below, each value is paired with a story shape that fits a systems or networking engineer moving to SDE. Fill in your own real facts and numbers.

ValueStory shape (STAR)What to stress
TrustS: a bug could leak one customer's traffic or config into another's. T: you owned the fix. A: reproduced it, fixed the isolation check, added a regression test, told support honestly. R: no repeat, measured by your own numbers.Honesty, security, isolation. Map it to tenants.
Customer SuccessA field escalation (a link flap or crash at a big customer). You found root cause from logs and core dumps, shipped a workaround the same day and a real fix later.Talking to the customer, not just the code.
InnovationYou automated a slow manual lab test or upgrade check with Python and cut the cycle time.Why you chose that tool; what you measured.
EqualityYou mentored a new hire, wrote the on-boarding doc, or made sure a quiet teammate's idea got credit.Concrete actions, not slogans.
SustainabilityYou made a daemon use less CPU or memory, or removed waste (idle lab hardware, duplicate jobs).Efficiency is a fair angle; do not overclaim.

Have a 2-minute answer to "why move from networking to software engineering?" Tie it to things you already do: writing C daemons, debugging distributed protocols, owning production incidents.
