Common mistakes and tips from the posts

What hurt candidates
  • Theory without hands-on depth in a metrics design led to a rejection (post).
  • Rapid-fire basics (REST, HTTPS, JWT) ate the round; only about 20 minutes were left for the design (post).
  • Preparing only LLD when the "LLD" round turned into HLD (post).
  • A design rated only "okay" even though DSA and LLD were strong; the candidate still moved on, but it was close (post).
What worked
  • Strong Hire on Airbnb: 5-minute hold then confirm, a lock against double booking, search fed by CDC into a search index, payments kept separate (post).
  • Stating NFRs early on the payment platform, because they drove the follow-ups (post).
  • Knowing why you picked every technology in your own project; the HM round can become a CV deep dive.
  • Writing more in the shared doc during LLD so the interviewer can follow you.

Prepare 30-second answers for REST vs RPC, what HTTPS adds, and how JWT works, so basics do not eat your design time. Security and identity (hld-14) covers the security parts.
