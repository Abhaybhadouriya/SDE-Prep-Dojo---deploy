Top 10 to prioritise (and why)

Ranked by how recent, how often, and how close to Salesforce's product (multi-tenant SaaS, APIs, security).

  1. Distributed rate limiter (d02) Asked at SMTS in 2026 and drilled very deep: token bucket, leaky bucket, the fixed-window boundary spike, sliding log vs sliding counter. It is also Salesforce's own world: per-tenant API limits. Open the design.
  2. Payment processing platform (d10) MTS, 2026, selected. Idempotency, retries and DB choice; most follow-ups came from NFRs. Open the design.
  3. Authentication / login system (d60) Two reports (2025-12 and 2026-02). Trust is the #1 value, so security questions are natural here. Open the design.
  4. Booking family: Ticketmaster, Airbnb, venue rental (d14 / d40 / d67) Three separate reports. The Airbnb answer that got Strong Hire used a short hold plus confirm and a lock to stop double booking. Open the design.
  5. Notification system (d03) Asked when the candidate expected LLD; deep dives on failure handling and DB choice. Open the design.
  6. Job scheduler with worker capacity (d25) Came up in a hiring-manager round: allocation rules and max capacity per worker. Open the design.
  7. Flash sale (single product, fixed start) (d09) Consistency over availability; low latency at the start second. Open the design.
  8. Uber / ride hailing (d06) SMTS, 2026-11, the newest report: driver matching and maps. Open the design.
  9. Metrics collection / monitoring (d20) A rejection where answers felt theoretical. Good place to use your real ops experience. Open the design.
  10. Classics: Dropbox, TinyURL, stories feed (d24 / d01 / d05) Each asked once; cheap to prepare because the site has full pages. Open the design.
