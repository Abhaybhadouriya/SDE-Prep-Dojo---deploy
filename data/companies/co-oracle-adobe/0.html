The story: two very different shops

Explain like I'm 5

Oracle is like a company that rents out giant warehouses and the roads between them (its cloud, called OCI) and also sells the filing cabinets inside (databases). Adobe is like an art shop that makes the brushes and paper (Photoshop, Acrobat) and now sells them as a subscription over the internet. Oracle interviewers like questions about plumbing: logs, schedulers, gateways, limits. Adobe interviewers like a question that starts small (a class or an API) and then grows until it serves millions of users.

From your world

OCI is a cloud: virtual networks, load balancers and gateways are networking products. Your routing, NAT and load-balancing background is a real advantage there. When Oracle asks you to design an API gateway or log ingestion, think of it as a control plane plus a data plane, exactly like a router.

Level names. Oracle posts use IC2 (roughly SDE-1) and IC3, often titled SMTS (roughly SDE-2). Adobe posts use MTS-2 (roughly SDE-2) and CS-1, Computer Scientist 1 (a step above MTS-2). These mappings are community wording, not official ladders; confirm with your recruiter.
