Common mistakes and tips from the posts

What hurt candidates
  • Invented capacity numbers got called out in an Oracle Health round (post).
  • Running out of time on a schema-heavy Goodreads design at Adobe (post).
  • Spending most of an Adobe round on the HLD of one's own project left only 15 minutes for a URL shortener; rejected (post).
  • Assuming a "coding only" invite means no design (post).
What worked
  • Adobe MTS-2 offer: start from classes and APIs, then scale step by step and keep going until the interviewer runs out of edge cases (post).
  • Oracle BookMyShow: go straight to seat locking when asked, instead of reciting the full template (post).
  • Knowing streaming basics (windows, Flink vs Spark) for top-K and logs.
  • Preparing LRU/LFU cache code for Oracle, since design and code are mixed.
