Adobe: four values

Adobe's values are Create the future, Own the outcome, Raise the bar, Be genuine, announced in a 2023 company post (Adobe blog). Story shapes for a systems or networking engineer moving to SDE:

Adobe valueStory shape (STAR)
Create the futureYou proposed a new tool or approach (for example a Python test harness or telemetry pipeline) and got it adopted.
Own the outcomeYou took a customer crash from report to root cause to shipped fix, even though part of it was in another team's code.
Raise the barYou added code review rules, tests or a postmortem habit that cut repeat bugs; give your own real numbers.
Be genuineA time you said "I was wrong" or "I don't know yet" and what you did next.

Adobe hiring-manager rounds in 2026 leaned on AI integration. Be ready to explain how you use AI tools while coding and how you would add an AI feature safely (cost, latency, bad outputs). See AI system building blocks (hld-17).

Oracle: ownership and depth

We did not find an official Oracle interview-values list to map to, so we do not invent one. The posts point to what matters instead: an HLD of your own current project, clear reasons for each technology choice, and calm behaviour in a bar-raiser style final round. Prepare three stories: a hard production bug you owned, a design you changed after feedback, and a time you pushed back on a deadline with data.
