The loop: rounds, what each tests, timeline

Uber's official backend guide lists: a 30-minute recruiter call, a HackerRank coding test, a 60-minute "Business Phone Screen" that tests coding plus design and architecture, then two 60-minute coding interviews (algorithms, then depth in your specialization) and a 60-minute Collaboration & Leadership interview. Candidate posts for SDE-2 add a separate LLD/machine-coding round and a separate HLD round, which is often run as the bar raiser.

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • OA: online assessment, a timed test before the live rounds.
  • HM: hiring manager round.
  • YOE: years of experience.
  • FR / NFR: functional and non-functional requirements.

Timeline: posts do not report durations consistently, so we will not invent a number. Two patterns do repeat: onsite rounds are often split (in-person coding and LLD, then a virtual HLD, see 8476675), and each stage is a decision point, so you may stop early. Ask your recruiter for the exact order and the competencies for your level; the official guide says they will share them.
