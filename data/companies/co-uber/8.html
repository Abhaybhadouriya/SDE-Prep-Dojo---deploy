Common mistakes and tips from the posts

Mistakes that cost offers
  • Hitting the database on every price tick; the fix was a separate threshold-detection step that forwards only matching events (8243609).
  • Naming Redis pub/sub and WebSockets without knowing how they connect (7682158).
  • Bringing CAP theorem into a single-node cache question (8110219).
  • Weak scale estimation and too few ranking signals (7381396).
  • Assuming the round label is right: an "LLD" invite turned into HLD (6892974).
  • Ignoring coding prep because "HLD is what matters": a strong-hire HLD still ended in reject after a no-hire LLD (7423162).
Habits that worked
  • Ask in minute one: "HLD or LLD? Which parts should I go deep on?"
  • Draw a minimum working design within ~10 minutes, then let the interviewer pick deep dives.
  • Practise recovering mid-design when the interviewer tightens a constraint (latency, scale); prepared templates alone were not enough (8509983).
  • Keep LLD code runnable and thread-safe; practise one concurrency problem per day.
  • For geo prompts, be able to explain geohash with a child-level analogy (7217970).
