The loops: rounds, what each tests, and the order

The big difference from US product companies: machine coding. You write a working, extensible program in 1.5 to 2 hours, then defend it. HLD is usually a separate 1-hour round. Most loops are progressive: you only move on after clearing each round.

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • OA: online assessment, a timed test before the live rounds.
  • HM: hiring manager round.
  • YOE: years of experience.
  • FR / NFR: functional and non-functional requirements.
  • GTT: good-till-triggered order.
  • square-off: closing intraday positions automatically.
