Common mistakes and tips from the posts

What hurt candidates
  • Going beyond the stated requirements in machine coding (post).
  • A message-queue fan-out for a feed at billions of users was rejected by the interviewer (post).
  • Running out of time before the likes and comments part of Instagram (post).
  • Using a write-back cache for view counts but not batching writes to the DB (post).
  • A design judged too basic, without enough on failures and states (post).
What worked
  • Mentioning idempotency and reconciliation early in any money flow.
  • WAL-style ledger, date sharding and async aggregation for T+1 settlement; selected (post).
  • Clear structure over raw scale; selected at Paytm (post).
  • For novel prompts (Shazam), reasoning from first principles instead of forcing a template.
  • Expecting the interviewer to push the design to several exchanges or segments at Groww.
