Behavioral angle

These companies do not run a published values framework in the interview the way Amazon does, so we do not invent one. The hiring-manager rounds in the posts test three things. Here is how a systems or networking engineer can answer.

What the HM probesStory shape (STAR)
Flaws in your own project, and how you would redo it (Razorpay)Pick a real design weakness (for example a single-threaded control daemon that became a bottleneck). Explain how you found it, what you shipped, and what you would design differently today.
Correctness under failure (all)A time a retry or failover caused duplicates or lost state (a route flap, a double config push). How you made it idempotent or added a check.
Ownership and speed (CRED, Groww)You shipped a tool or fix fast, with tests, and supported it in production.
AI tool usage (CRED screen, Paytm HM)How you use AI assistants, how you check their output, and where you refuse to use them (security, money paths).
Why fintech, why SDEReliability is your trade: networks and payments both must never lose or duplicate a message.
