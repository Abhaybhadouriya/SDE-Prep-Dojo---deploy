The story: a money post office that must never lose a letter

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine a post office that carries money instead of letters. If a letter gets lost, someone is sad. If money gets lost, or sent twice, someone is very angry and the bank auditor comes. So the money post office writes everything in a big notebook, gives every envelope a unique number, and checks its notebook against the bank's notebook every night. PhonePe, Paytm, Razorpay, Groww and CRED are such post offices, and their interviewers ask: "what happens if the envelope is sent twice, or the reply never comes back?"

From your world

A payment flow is a reliable transport protocol. An idempotency key is a sequence number: a retransmit with the same number is recognised and dropped. A lost ACK is the "unknown" payment state: you cannot tell if the other side got it, so you query or reconcile. Nightly reconciliation is like comparing checksums of two routing tables. A stock tick fan-out is multicast: one sender, millions of listeners, and you drop stale packets rather than queue them.
