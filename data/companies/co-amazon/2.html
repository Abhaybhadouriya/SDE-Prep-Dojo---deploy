The loop for SDE-1 and SDE-2

Amazon's official SDE II prep page describes an online assessment (about 90 minutes for two coding questions, about 20 minutes of system design scenarios and a short work-style survey on the LPs), then a loop of four 55-minute interviews, with a decision usually within about five business days. Candidate reports match this shape and add detail on what each round really tests.

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • LP: Amazon's Leadership Principles, asked as behavioural questions in every round.
  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • Bar Raiser: an interviewer from outside the team who guards the hiring bar and can veto.
  • OA: online assessment, a timed test before the live rounds.
  • HM: hiring manager round.
  • YOE: years of experience.
  • FR / NFR: functional and non-functional requirements.

  • Timeline seen in posts: OA within a week of applying; loop one to three weeks later; hiring drives in India run the whole loop in one or two days.
  • Debrief: all interviewers meet together, so one weak LP round can sink strong technical rounds (post 7646418: HLD rated strong, overall reject on Bar Raiser LPs).
  • Down-levelling is real: mixed HLD feedback can turn an SDE-2 loop into an SDE-1 offer (post 6883459).

Round labels are often wrong. A slot called "LLD" became an HLD (inventory system, post 8512004), and an HLD slot asked for code. In the first minute, ask: "Would you like a high-level architecture or class-level design?"
