What the HLD round expects at each level

Level 3 - Intermediate
SDE-1 (L4)
  • Usually no full HLD round; design shows up inside LLD or as a small "design X for one user" task.
  • Reported prompts: rate limiter (post 7024182), AWS billing for one user (post 8520348), office occupancy tracker (post 6841339).
  • Judged on clear entities, a sensible schema, APIs, and saying why you chose each piece.
  • Know the building blocks: load balancer, cache, SQL vs NoSQL, queue. See Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP.
SDE-2 (L5)
  • A full 40-45 minute HLD after 15-30 minutes of LPs. The prompt is often one line ("Design Netflix").
  • You drive: functional and non-functional requirements, back-of-envelope numbers, entities, APIs, diagram.
  • Then deep dives the interviewer picks: concurrency (double booking, overselling), DB choice, caching, failure and retry, monitoring.
  • Expect pushback until every choice has a reason (post 8584198: Strong Hire after relentless "why?").

The flow candidates describe as "what worked" is the same one we teach in The 45-Minute Interview Framework: requirements, then estimation (Back-of-Envelope Estimation), then API and data model, then a simple happy-path diagram, then scale it one bottleneck at a time.

Amazon interviewers often draw on Bluescape or an Excalidraw-like board. Practise drawing boxes and arrows in a browser tool before the day (post 7176045).
