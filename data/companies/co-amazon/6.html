Top 10 to prioritise for Amazon

Ranked by how often the family appeared in our 105 reports, then adjusted for how "Amazon-flavoured" it is. Counts are small numbers; the order inside the middle of the list is not precise.

#DesignReportsWhy it is on the list
1News Feed (Instagram/Facebook)8Most asked Amazon HLD in our data. Push vs pull fan-out, celebrity users, cache of feed IDs. Also asked as Twitter, Instagram, Reddit with age limits.
2Ticket Booking (BookMyShow)6Booking with limited seats is the largest family. Interviewers ask optimistic vs pessimistic locking and holds with a timeout.
3Time-Slot Reservation (CoWIN / Rooms / Service Centres)5Same concurrency core as tickets: CoWIN vaccine slots, adventure-park rides, meeting rooms, vehicle service centres.
4Log Aggregation (ELK at scale)5Log ingestion, error monitoring and alerting. One report spent 15 minutes on backpressure. Natural fit for a networking engineer.
5Distributed Rate Limiter4Asked at SDE-1 and SDE-2. Know token bucket vs sliding window, Redis counters, and the 'queue instead of reject' twist.
6File Sync (Dropbox/OneDrive)4Google Drive, Dropbox and Google Photos: chunking, metadata DB vs blob store, sync and conflicts.
7Ride Hailing (Uber/Ola)4Rider-driver matching, employee shuttle and live flight tracking. Location updates, geo index, matching service.
8Video Streaming (Netflix)4Netflix (and Spotify / Amazon Music 3 more times): CDN, transcoding, search, recommendations.
9Device Settings Sync & Backup/Restore (Alexa)3Amazon-only style: device settings sync and backup/restore. One selected SDE-2 report (post 6923887, the worked example above) is one of these.
10E-commerce Checkout (Amazon)3Cart, checkout and order creation, plus inventory oversell (see also Inventory Management (Walmart) and Flash Sale (Flipkart Big Billion Days)).

Runners-up worth one pass each: Chat App (WhatsApp), Collaborative Editor (Google Docs), Recommendation System (Amazon / Netflix), Distributed Job Scheduler, Amazon Locker / Store Pickup, Notification System, IoT Telemetry & Real-time Heatmaps (Smart Meters / Drivers). The patterns that appear in most of these are Pattern: Dealing with Contention, Pattern: Fan-out (Push vs Pull) and Pattern: Counting, Ranking & Windowed Aggregation.
