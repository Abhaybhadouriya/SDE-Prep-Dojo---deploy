Worked example: the device config sync question

One selected SDE-2 report (July 2025, India) is a good model of an Amazon-style "team-specific" prompt. The interviewer asked for a service that moves a user's settings from an old device (phone, Alexa, home devices) to a new one.

  1. Requirements first. User signs in on the new device, picks which preferences to copy, and the device is ready to use. Non-functional: secure, scalable, setup finished in a few minutes.
  2. Entities. User, Device (old and new), Settings bundle.
  3. APIs and storage. Register device, list settings, start transfer, report status.
  4. The pushback. The interviewer asked how to cut storage cost. The accepted idea was to skip a permanent cloud copy and pull settings straight from the old device at setup time, keeping only metadata in the cloud.
  5. LPs in the same round: Customer Obsession and Dive Deep.

Similar prompts were reported again in 2025-2026: keeping up to 10 devices in the same state (post 6708128), backup and restore to a new device (post 8382886), and a team-specific sync service (post 8499681). Our design page: Device Settings Sync & Backup/Restore (Alexa).

Lesson: Amazon likes problems from its own products (devices, retail, logistics, AWS). If you know the team, read its product pages and guess the design question.
