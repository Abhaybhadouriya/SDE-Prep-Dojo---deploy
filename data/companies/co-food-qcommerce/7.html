Behavioral angle

We did not find an official interview-values list for these companies to map to, so we do not invent one. The rounds that judge behaviour are the Zomato culture-fit round with the manager, the Zepto HM round (HLD of your past project), and Swiggy's bar raiser, which reports describe as going from product requirements to technical design (PRD to TRD). Story shapes for a systems or networking engineer:

What they probeStory shape (STAR)
Speed with careYou shipped a hotfix fast during an outage, then followed with tests and a postmortem.
Turning a fuzzy ask into a design (Swiggy PRD to TRD)A product or customer request ("make failover faster"), how you turned it into requirements, numbers and a design.
Your past project as an HLD (Zepto HM)Draw your router or controller software as services, queues and stores; explain one trade-off.
Peak-load thinkingA time traffic spiked (a route storm, a big customer's migration) and how you protected the system.
Why consumer techYou like real-time, physical-world systems: riders, stock and orders are a distributed system you can see.
