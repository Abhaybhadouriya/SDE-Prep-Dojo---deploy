Common mistakes and tips from the posts

What hurt candidates
  • Following a public quick-commerce breakdown closely and still getting negative feedback at Swiggy SDE-3; memorised answers are not enough (post).
  • A long introduction that squeezed the design time (post).
  • Naming tools you cannot explain: Bloom filter and Elasticsearch internals got probed (loop note).
  • Not finishing because a DSA warm-up ate the time; the interviewer then probed only a few decisions (post).
What worked
  • Explaining the reason behind each choice (Kafka vs direct call, index choice); selected at Blinkit SDE-1 (post).
  • Setting your own requirements first, then absorbing the interviewer's additions (peak-time priority, several payment methods); selected at Zepto SDE-2 (post).
  • Clear dedupe and retry story for notifications, with Kafka trade-offs; selected at Zomato District (post).
  • For ad pacing, an hourly weighting idea was accepted (post).
