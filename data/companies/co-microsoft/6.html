Top 10 to prioritise for Microsoft

With only 31 reports, most families appear once or twice. We ranked by count, then by how "Microsoft" the problem is (Teams, Office, Azure infrastructure). Treat the list as a set, not a strict order.

#DesignReportsWhy it is on the list
1URL Shortener (TinyURL)3Asked three times, always with a twist: multi-region, Snowflake-style IDs, failure scenarios. Simple prompt, deep follow-ups.
2Collaborative Editor (Google Docs)3Google Docs, comments on a document, and real-time state sync. OT vs CRDT is the core deep dive.
3Distributed Rate Limiter2Algorithms and trade-offs, plus a rough code sketch. Also appears in debugging-flavoured rounds.
4Distributed Configuration Service2Push config updates to N services: watch vs poll, versioning, safe rollout. Very Azure-like.
5Authentication & SSO Service2Multi-tenant OTP service over SMS/WhatsApp/email, multiple clients per user.
6Proximity Service & Nearby Friends (Yelp)2Find restaurants or stores within 10 km; geohash or quadtree, then code part of it.
7Team Chat with Channels & Presence (Slack / Teams)1Teams messaging: WebSockets, big groups, ordering. Microsoft's own product, so expect it.
8Distributed Cache (Redis-like)1Distributed LRU cache, often merged with LLD (O(1) ops, concurrency, unit tests). See also Distributed Key-Value Store (Dynamo).
9Distributed Unique ID Generator (Snowflake)1Highly available unique IDs at huge throughput; clock skew, machine IDs.
10Notification System1Notification system in a merged HLD+LLD round; also metrics and sensor alerting (Metrics & Monitoring (Prometheus-like)).

Infrastructure flavour matters: Distributed Key-Value Store (Dynamo), S3-like Object Storage, Metrics & Monitoring (Prometheus-like) and Replication, Consensus & Coordination cover the "sharding, replication, consistency" theory that one report asked as a standalone round (post 8477383).
