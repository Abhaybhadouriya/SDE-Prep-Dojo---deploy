The story: a team that loves learners

Explain like I'm 5

Picture a big workshop that builds Windows, Xbox, Teams and the Azure cloud. When they hire a new builder, a few builders each give you a puzzle. At the end you meet a senior builder, the "As Appropriate" person, who decides if you will grow well with the team. They care that you can explain why you build things a certain way, and that you love learning new things.

Microsoft's loop is usually a coding test, two or three technical rounds (DSA, low-level design and high-level design) and a final AA (As Appropriate) or hiring-manager round. Microsoft's official interview tips stress showing your reasoning, asking clarifying questions and a growth mindset. This page uses 31 HLD reports from 2025-06 to 2026-10.

From your world

Azure teams build networking, storage and virtualisation at huge scale, so a systems background is valued. Several reports show team-specific questions: an Azure Storage principal engineer asked how a file is laid out on disk and how free blocks are tracked with bitmaps (post 8300781). That is kernel file-system knowledge you already have.
