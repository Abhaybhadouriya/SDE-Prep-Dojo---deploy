What the HLD round expects at each level

Level 3 - Intermediate
SDE-1 (L59/L60)
  • Usually no standalone HLD; design is a short discussion inside an OOD round.
  • Only one SDE-1 report in our data: search autocomplete; the candidate gave a trie plus top-k and was unsure whether HLD was expected (post 6869693).
  • Prepare: one clean OOD answer, the building blocks in Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP, and how a request moves through a web service.
SDE2 (L61/L62)
  • A full HLD, often 60-75 minutes, sometimes with LLD and code for one component in the same round.
  • Interviewers reject textbook answers and push into components and failures (post 7392060).
  • Common deep dives: sharding and replication, consistency vs availability, multi-region IDs, WebSockets vs long polling, OT vs CRDT.
  • Thinking aloud matters: a candidate who had never prepared a rate limiter still passed by reasoning step by step (post 7635697).

Microsoft design rounds often add LLD: "now write the class for this component" or "how would you unit test it?" (post 7541629). Read HLD vs LLD: Handling Combined Design Rounds before the loop.
