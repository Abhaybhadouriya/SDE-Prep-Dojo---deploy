Behavioural angle: growth mindset and the AA round

Microsoft's official interview tips ask candidates to prepare stories that show the situation, what you did, the outcome and what you learned, and to explain your reasoning out loud. The culture words they list include growth mindset, customer obsession, diversity and inclusion, and One Microsoft. The AA round mixes these with a deep dive of a project you built, sometimes with an HLD of that project (post 7541629, post 7769548).

ThemeStory idea for a systems / networking engineerTypical question
Growth mindsetYou moved from kernel / networking into cloud-native systems and taught yourself Docker, Kubernetes and Kafka.Tell me about something you learned quickly.
Customer obsessionYou changed a feature after visiting or calling a customer whose network kept failing.A time you used customer feedback to change direction.
One Microsoft (collaboration)You worked with hardware, QA and support teams to close a field bug.A time you worked across teams.
Diversity and inclusionYou made sure a quieter teammate's design idea got heard in review.How do you include different views?
AccountabilityYou owned a regression you caused, fixed it and added a test.Tell me about a mistake you made.
Handling ambiguityYou started a project with unclear requirements and defined them with stakeholders.A time the goal was unclear.
ConflictYou disagreed on a design with a senior engineer and resolved it with data.A time you disagreed with someone senior.
AI in your workflowHow you use code assistants, and how you check their output.How do you use AI tools day to day? (asked in AA rounds, post 8507349)

End every story with "what I learned and what I do differently now". That reflection is exactly what "growth mindset" means to the interviewer.
