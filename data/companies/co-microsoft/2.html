The loop for SDE-1 and SDE2

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • HM: hiring manager round.
  • AA: Microsoft's final "as appropriate" round with a senior leader.
  • YOE: years of experience.
  • Level codes on this page (L60, L61, L62, L63, L64): see the shared level map for which counts as SDE-1, SDE-2 or senior.
