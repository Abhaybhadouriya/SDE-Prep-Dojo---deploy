Worked example: design Microsoft Teams messaging

A selected SDE2 report (March 2026) shows what a Microsoft HLD deep dive feels like:

  1. Real-time delivery. WebSockets vs long polling; the candidate chose WebSockets with a gateway tier that keeps connections.
  2. Big groups. Group chats with 10,000+ members change fan-out: write once to the channel, deliver to online members, let offline members pull.
  3. Storage. Chat history partitioned by conversation in a wide-column NoSQL store (Cassandra-like).
  4. Ordering. Per-conversation sequence numbers instead of wall-clock timestamps, because clocks on servers drift.
