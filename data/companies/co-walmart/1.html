Be careful with this page: our sample is small (10 reports) and senior-heavy. Half the reports are for Senior Software Engineer (including IN4). The other half are Software Engineer III, which candidates compare to SDE-2: one post uses that title and four call it "SDE-3" (one of them with 1.10 YOE), so the shared level map counts them as SDE-2. None is Software Engineer II (about SDE-1). So the prompts list is useful, but the SDE-1 / SDE-2 expectations below are partly inferred from the senior reports and from one detailed SE-III loop description (7192540).

Walmart does not publish a public engineering interview guide like Uber or Atlassian do. Its official hiring page covers applying and tracking status in the Careers portal. Confirm round order with your recruiter.
