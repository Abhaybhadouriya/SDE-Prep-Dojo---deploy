Common mistakes and tips from the posts

Mistakes that cost offers
  • Over-engineering the scaling part in the last 10 minutes instead of closing cleanly (rejected, 7620235).
  • Holding on to a first approach the interviewer did not accept; you must pivot and argue trade-offs (7008972).
  • Drawing a message queue without saying why it is there (6782621).
  • Weak LLD in a combined round: patterns, synchronized methods and entities were all expected (5410592).
Habits that worked
  • Ask the recruiter for each round's competency list (7192540).
  • Ask scope questions early: text vs images, nested replies, sharing (6146111).
  • State your consistency choice ("eventual is fine for feeds") before the DB design.
  • For hiring drives: sleep well, eat, and treat each round as a fresh start - each one eliminates.
