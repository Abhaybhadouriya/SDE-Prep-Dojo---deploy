The loop: rounds, what each tests, timeline

Atlassian's official engineering interview guide describes: a coding interview in two parts (Data Structures and Code Design), a 60-minute systems design interview, a manager interview, a values interview (often run by someone outside engineering), and a final Hiring Committee that works independently of the interviewers and looks only at feedback and CV.

Words on this page (each is also in the glossary):

  • STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result: the shape of a behavioural story.
  • Karat: an outside company that runs first-round technical screens.
  • Level codes on this page (P30, P40, P50): see the shared level map for which counts as SDE-1, SDE-2 or senior.
