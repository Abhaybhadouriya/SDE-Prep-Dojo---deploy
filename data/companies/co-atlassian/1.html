We logged 17 public Atlassian design reports, almost all from India and almost all at P40 (Atlassian's level that candidates compare to SDE-2) or P50. We found no P30 (SDE-1) design report, so the P30 column below is our inference from the official guide, not from posts. 17 is a modest sample, but the pattern is unusually strong: 9 of 17 are tagging systems and 3 more are "popular Confluence pages".

Level names: Atlassian uses P30, P40, P50 and so on. Candidates map P40 to SDE-2; the official guide only says "Software Engineer" and "Principal Engineer" and does not publish the mapping.
