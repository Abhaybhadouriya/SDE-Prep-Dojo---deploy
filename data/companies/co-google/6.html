Top 10 to prioritise (judgement, not frequency)

With so few reports, this list is our reasoning, not a count. It favours designs whose core is also a common coding problem, because that is how design reaches L3/L4.

#DesignWhy
1URL Shortener (TinyURL)Unique short-string generation appeared as an L4 coding problem; ID generation is a classic follow-up.
2Distributed Cache (Redis-like)Cache variants (LRU / LFU with custom eviction) are frequent coding problems with design follow-ups.
3Top-K / Heavy HittersTop-K and windowed counting is the usual 'now scale it' follow-up for stream problems.
4Distributed Rate LimiterToken / leaky bucket was asked in a Google round; also a natural time-window coding problem.
5Video Upload & Transcoding (YouTube)Two reports: image/video hosting and closed-captioning for YouTube.
6Search AutocompletePrefix tries are a common coding topic and the base of Google-style autocomplete.
7Web CrawlerCrawling, dedup and politeness: BFS on a huge graph, very Google.
8Distributed Unique ID Generator (Snowflake)Distributed unique IDs; pairs with the URL shortener.
9Ride Hailing (Uber/Ola)Employee cab allocation and routing was reported as a Google design prompt.
10Collaborative Editor (Google Docs)Google Docs is a Google product; if you get a real design round, be ready.

Time split for L4: about 80% DSA, 15% G&L stories, 5% design follow-up practice, unless your recruiter confirms a system design round.
