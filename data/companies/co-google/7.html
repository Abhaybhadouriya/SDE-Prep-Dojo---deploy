Behavioural angle: Googleyness & Leadership

Candidates describe G&L as standard behavioural questions with follow-ups, answered in STAR form. Question themes below come from reports such as post 8522064 and post 8564735. Several candidates rated it their strongest round; it still gates the onsite, so do not skip it.

ThemeStory idea for a systems / networking engineerExample question
Giving and receiving feedbackYou gave a teammate direct feedback on a risky patch and helped them fix it.Have you given critical feedback? How was it taken?
Hard technical problemA race condition or memory corruption you traced through kernel logs.Describe a challenging technical problem and how you solved it.
DisagreementYou and a senior engineer disagreed on a protocol design; you settled it with a test.Tell me about a disagreement with a colleague or manager.
AmbiguityA customer escalation with no reproduction steps; you defined the problem first.How do you work when a project is unclear?
Helping othersYou coached a struggling new hire through the codebase.A time you helped an underperforming teammate.
Unreasonable demandsA release date that ignored test time; how you negotiated scope.Tell me about reasonable and unreasonable demands from a manager.

"Leadership" here does not mean managing people. It means taking initiative, helping others and acting well without full information.
