The story: the puzzle club

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine a club where everyone loves puzzles. To join, you solve puzzles with a few members, one at a time, and you chat with one member about how you work with friends. Then a group of members who never met you reads the notes and decides. If they say yes, you pick which table in the club to sit at. At the younger levels, nobody asks you to draw a whole city; they want to see how you think through a puzzle.

That is Google at L3 (SWE II, roughly SDE-1) and L4 (SWE III, roughly SDE-2). The loop is mostly coding plus one Googleyness & Leadership (G&L) round. Interviewers write feedback, a hiring committee decides, and then team matching finds you a team. A full system design round is normally an L5+ thing.

From your world

Google coding problems often hide a systems question inside: "drop repeated device events within 10 seconds" is a dedup window, like suppressing duplicate syslog messages or flapping-link alarms. When the follow-up asks "what if there are a million devices?", that is your design moment.
