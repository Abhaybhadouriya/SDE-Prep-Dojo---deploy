What "design" means at L3 and L4

Level 3 - Intermediate
L3
  • No system design round reported.
  • Spend nearly all prep on DSA and on G&L stories.
  • Know just enough design vocabulary (cache, queue, shard) to answer a scale follow-up in two sentences. Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP is enough.
L4
  • Usually no standalone HLD; design arrives as the last 15-25 minutes of a coding round (post 7693483).
  • Typical follow-ups: "the stream has a million devices", "it no longer fits in memory", "make it work across machines".
  • A cache with a custom eviction rule (post 8265899) or a unique-string generator (post 7685567) are coding problems with a design core.
  • If your loop does include system design: start simple, add components only when a bottleneck justifies them, and leave room for new requirements (post 6668243).

A good answer to a scale follow-up has three parts: what breaks first (memory, one machine's CPU, network), how you split the work (shard by key), and what you give up (exactness, ordering). Practise this in Pattern: Counting, Ranking & Windowed Aggregation and Pattern: Scaling Writes.
