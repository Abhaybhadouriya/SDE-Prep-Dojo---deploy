Common mistakes and tips from the posts

Mistakes that cost offers
  • Preparing only LLD or only HLD (7232996).
  • Jumping to Elasticsearch before a simple SQL search, then not being able to explain it (6737785, 5373870).
  • Skipping capacity estimation and losing time on the drawing tool (6153487).
  • Missing the full API and schema list in a booking design; feedback named the design round as the rejection reason (6168500).
  • Machine-coding code that does not run, or has no clear extension point.
Habits that worked
  • FR, NFR, then estimation, then entities, APIs, schema, then HLD - in that order (6160355).
  • Practise on Excalidraw or another online board before the day.
  • Treat the round as a two-way discussion; refine step by step (6112758).
  • For machine coding: in-memory store behind an interface, a driver/demo class, and 2-3 design patterns you can name.
  • Schedule around Big Billion Days if you can.
