Behavioral angle: values and the HM round

Flipkart has no separate "values round" in our reports; behaviour is judged mainly in the hiring manager round, which usually opens with a deep dive into your own project (often "draw your system's HLD"). Flipkart's careers site lists five values; use them to pick and frame your stories.

ValueMeaning, simplyStory from a networking/kernel career
AudacityThink big, take bold bets.You proposed replacing a slow manual config process with an automated pipeline, and drove it to production.
Bias for ActionMove with urgency; ideas count when shipped.During a customer outage you shipped a safe workaround in hours, then the real fix in the next release.
Customer FirstSee the problem from the customer's side.You changed a CLI error message after seeing operators misread it in the field.
IntegrityBe fair and honest, even when it costs you.You reported a test gap you had missed before it reached a customer.
InclusionRespect differences; help everyone belong.You mentored a new hire from a different background through the kernel code base.

Prepare your own project's HLD on one page: components, data flow, scale numbers, one failure you handled, and what you would redesign. The HM round turned into exactly this in several reports (6136429). For a router/switch background, draw the control plane, data plane and management plane as "services" - interviewers like seeing you translate.
