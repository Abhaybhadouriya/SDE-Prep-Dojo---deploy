The story: building with a timer

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine a building game with a sand timer. A teacher says "build a playground for a million kids" and turns the timer: you have 45 minutes. First you ask what the kids want, then you draw a simple playground, then the teacher pokes at it: "What if everyone runs to the slide at once?" If you finish too fast and leave no time for poking, or spend all your time on one swing, you lose points. Meta's design round works the same way.

Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) uses levels: E3 (new grad, roughly SDE-1), E4 (mid, roughly SDE-2) and E5 (senior). From E4 the loop has a 45-minute design round of one of two kinds: System Design (scale and infrastructure) or Product Architecture (APIs, data model and user experience). This page uses 38 reports from 2025-04 to 2026-11.

From your world

The infra track feels like designing a network control plane: fan-out of updates, hot spots, counters at huge rates, failure domains. The product track feels more like designing the management API and data model of a router: what objects exist, how clients read and update them.
