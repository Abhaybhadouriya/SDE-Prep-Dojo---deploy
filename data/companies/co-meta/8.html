Common mistakes and tips from the posts

Scenario: the interviewer jumps to follow-ups before your diagram is done

Minute 12 of a news-feed design; the interviewer asks about ranking and comments while your boxes are half drawn (post 7374944).

Follow them deep into ranking for 20 minutes and never finish the core flow. Feedback: "did not drive" (post 7194393).

Answer briefly, park it ("I'll come back to ranking in the deep dive"), finish the end-to-end flow in five minutes, then return to their question.

Likely follow-ups
  • How do you paginate the feed?
  • How do comments appear in real time?
  • Picking components before numbers. Size QPS and storage first (post 8617675).
  • Finishing too early. Leave time for probing (post 6976759).
  • Over-answering a prepared question. A candidate who had prepared the ad click aggregator worried about going beyond what was asked (post 7235648). Answer the question in front of you.
  • Getting steered off course. Steer back politely when the interviewer wanders (post 7160577).
  • Product track too infra-heavy. A product architecture round wanted APIs and user experience more than infrastructure (post 7616684).
  • Tip: Dropbox at Meta is about sync; prepare change notification and conflict handling (post 6992607, post 7265904).
  • Tip: curveballs happen: a single-process download library in the design slot (post 7221120). Clarify scope and design at the right size.
