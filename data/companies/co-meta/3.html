What the design round expects at each level

Level 3 - Intermediate
E4 (SDE-2)
  • Drive a complete, working design in 45 minutes with some help allowed.
  • Clarify for about 5 minutes, then quantify capacity before picking components (post 8617675).
  • Clean APIs and data model; one or two solid deep dives (DB choice, caching, fan-out).
  • Justify trade-offs; interviewers grade the reason, not the tech name.
E5 (senior)
  • Drive with almost no hints; handle the interviewer pulling you into deep dives early (post 7194393).
  • Reach two deep dives; missing the second was part of a down-level (post 7260223).
  • Discuss failure modes, consistency choices and operational concerns without being asked.
