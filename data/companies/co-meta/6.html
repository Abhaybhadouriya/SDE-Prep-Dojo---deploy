Top 10 to prioritise for Meta

Ranked by count in our 38 reports. The top three are tied at four reports each. These overlap strongly with the "Hello Interview" style answer keys several candidates said they used.

#DesignReportsWhy it is on the list
1News Feed (Instagram/Facebook)4Facebook / Instagram feed and post privacy. Asked in both tracks; Meta's own product.
2File Sync (Dropbox/OneDrive)4Dropbox / Drive: interviewers focus on file sync after upload and change notification (SSE vs WebSocket).
3Trending Topics (Twitter/X)4Top-K trending hashtags, songs or videos. Classic infra prompt.
4Ticket Booking (BookMyShow)3Ticketmaster / concert booking: contention on seats, often small scale by design.
5Real-time Leaderboard3LeetCode live leaderboard and game scoreboards: sorted sets, sharded counters.
6Notification System2Notification system; one asked for alerting 1M users for a flash sale.
7Food Delivery (Swiggy/Zomato)2DoorDash / Uber Eats: order lifecycle and real-time tracking.
8Ad Click Aggregator2Ad click aggregator: streaming aggregation, idempotent counting.
9Online Judge / Coding Platform (LeetCode)2LeetCode online judge and coding contests: queue, sandboxed workers.
10Live Comments & Reactions (FB Live / Hotstar)1YouTube Live comments: pub/sub fan-out, WebSocket vs SSE. Pairs with Distributed Counter (Likes / Views).

Next tier: Top-K / Heavy Hitters, Price Tracker & Alerts, Post Search (Twitter / Facebook Search), Online Auction (eBay-style Bidding), Online Multiplayer Game (Chess), Video Streaming (Netflix). Core patterns: Pattern: Real-time Updates, Pattern: Fan-out (Push vs Pull), Pattern: Counting, Ranking & Windowed Aggregation.
