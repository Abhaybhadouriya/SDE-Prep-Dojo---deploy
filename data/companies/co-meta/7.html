Behavioural angle

Meta's behavioural round is a full 45-minute interview. Reports describe themes of conflict, ambiguity, growth and the scope of your impact. For E4, the scope should be "my project and its users"; for E5, "my team and neighbouring teams". Use STAR and quantify.

ThemeStory idea for a systems / networking engineerTypical question
ConflictYou and another team disagreed on an API change; you found a compromise with data.Tell me about a conflict with a coworker.
AmbiguityYou took a vague customer complaint and turned it into a clear, scoped fix.A time you worked with unclear requirements.
Impact and scopeA tool or fix that helped many customers or teams, with a number.What is the most impactful project you led?
Growth / feedbackFeedback that changed how you work (for example, writing design docs first).Tell me about feedback you received.
FailureA release that caused an outage; what you changed after.Tell me about a time you failed.
Moving fastYou shipped a hotfix quickly but safely during an escalation.A time you had to deliver fast.

Your level is partly decided here. Stories that show only individual tasks can pull an E5 loop down to E4. Show decisions you drove and who they affected.
