Worked example: top-K trending hashtags

Trending / top-K was asked four times in our data (post 7049812, post 7101440, post 6988841, post 6966899) and top-K heavy hitters once more (post 7160577). It is the classic Meta infra prompt.

  1. Requirements. Top 10 or top 100 hashtags for the last hour, day or week; a few seconds of delay is fine; approximate counts may be fine.
  2. Numbers. Posts per second times hashtags per post gives events per second. That number decides whether one counter service can cope.
  3. Pipeline. Post events into a log (Kafka), stream workers count per time window, partitioned by hashtag; a merge step keeps the global top-K in a sorted set.
  4. Deep dives. Hot hashtags (split one key across workers, then merge), exact vs approximate (count-min sketch), sliding vs tumbling windows.
