The story: fire drills and piano scales

Explain like I'm 5

At school you practise the fire drill when there is no fire. You walk the same route, line up in the same spot, and the teacher times it. On the day of a real alarm, nobody has to think: their feet already know the way. Piano players do the same with scales: boring, repeated, timed, until the fingers move by themselves.

An LLD round gives you about ten minutes to turn a paragraph into a class diagram before you must move on to APIs and code. That is not enough time to invent a method on the spot. You need the method already in your hands: actors, use cases, nouns, sort, relationships, API. These drills are the scales.
