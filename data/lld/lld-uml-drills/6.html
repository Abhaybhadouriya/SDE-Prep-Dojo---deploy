Drill 3: Parking lot

Prompt (10 min). A multi-floor parking lot has spots of different sizes (bike, car, large). Vehicles enter at any of several gates, get a ticket for a suitable free spot and pay on exit by the hour; rates differ by vehicle type and may get weekend pricing later. Show free spots per floor on a display board. The parking lot is the most frequently reported LLD problem in our research, with 17 reports across companies including Amazon, Microsoft, Razorpay, Uber and Walmart.

Model answer: parking lot

Clarify. Actors: Driver, Attendant/Admin, Payment provider. Use cases: enter and get ticket, exit and pay, view free spots, add floor/spot. Assume a vehicle fits its own size or larger; out of scope: reservations.

Sort. Entities: ParkingLot (orchestrator), Floor, Spot, Ticket. Values: Vehicle (plate + type), Money. Enums: VehicleType, SpotSize. Interfaces: SpotAllocationStrategy, FeeStrategy. Instance trap: no CarSpot/BikeSpot subclasses: spots differ only in a size field. Hidden split: Spot (permanent) vs Ticket (one visit, links vehicle, spot and entry time).

API. Ticket park(Vehicle v, GateId g) fails with NoSpotAvailable; Receipt unpark(TicketId t) fails for unknown or already-closed tickets and is idempotent per ticket; int freeSpots(int floor, SpotSize s) is a read-only query for the board.

Concurrency. Two gates allocate the last car spot at the same moment. Make "find free spot and mark occupied" atomic: a lock per floor, or an atomic compare-and-set on the spot. Display board updates can be Observer notifications.

Likely follow-ups
  • Electric spots with chargers.
  • Lost ticket.
