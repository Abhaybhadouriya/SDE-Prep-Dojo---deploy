How it is asked

Before you start: this case assumes OOP from C structs (objects as structs with an ops table) and interfaces vs abstract classes. The patterns it uses are explained on their own pages: Strategy, Facade, Chain of Responsibility. The locking uses read-write locks.

Autocomplete is asked as LLD with code (Adobe, Microsoft), as a "design plus working solution" round (PhonePe), and as an HLD at scale (our Search Autocomplete system design). The wildcard router is Atlassian's signature Code Design round: 60 minutes, your IDE, tests expected, and follow-ups that escalate, so design for the next requirement. Round styles are covered in How LLD rounds work.

Practice problems that are the same core: LeetCode 642 Design Search Autocomplete System (characters one at a time, # ends a sentence, top 3 by frequency then alphabetical), 1268 Search Suggestions System, 208 Implement Trie and 211 Add and Search Words (a single-character wildcard in the query, the same idea as our findAll).

The Atlassian reports share one lesson: the round is graded on design quality and tests and on reaching the follow-ups. A working, tested put / match by minute 35 matters more than a clever trie by minute 55. Each round can level you independently, so this one alone can cost a level.
