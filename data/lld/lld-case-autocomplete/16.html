  • Trie node: children, optional entry, cached top-k of the subtree.
  • Ranking is a Strategy; ties broken by text for determinism.
  • Incremental cache is exact while scores only rise; delete or decay means rebuild.
  • Reads O(prefix + k) under a shared lock; writes O(L x k log k) under an exclusive one.
  • Router node: literal map, one param (with name), one *, one **.
  • Precedence literal, param, *, **, with backtracking and undo of captures.
  • Reject duplicates and conflicting param names at registration.
  • Wildcard in the query: same trie, DFS over literal children.
