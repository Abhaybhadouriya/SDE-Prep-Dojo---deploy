Common mistakes

  • Scanning the whole subtree on every keystroke without saying it is O(subtree); short prefixes are the slowest.
  • Ranking hard-coded in the search instead of a Strategy.
  • Caching top-k and then adding delete without rebuilding, so removed phrases keep showing.
  • Unstable tie-breaking (hash order), so tests flake.
  • Router without backtracking: correct on the happy path, 404 on /a/b/c.
  • Storing * and :id as ordinary child keys, so precedence and param names get tangled.
  • Overwriting duplicate routes silently instead of failing at put.
  • Spending 50 minutes on autocomplete in a router round: the Atlassian reports say follow-ups decide the level.
