Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Works and is tested: precedence, backtracking, conflicts, top-k, ties, removal, all by tests you wrote.
  • Ranking is a Strategy, chosen at construction; no mode flags in the trie.
  • Read path is O(prefix + k) and you can say why the cache stays exact (scores only go up) and what breaks it (delete, decay).
  • Router node has a slot per segment kind; precedence literal, param, *, **; failed branches undo their param captures.
  • Bad input fails early: duplicate or conflicting routes at put, empty phrases at record, clear messages.
  • Thread safety explained: read-write lock, snapshots out, copy-on-write as the router alternative.
  • Follow-ups reached or at least designed for (wildcard query, middleware, methods).
  • Readable code: small methods, no god class, no premature generality.

Atlassian's own interview guidance puts thinking and trade-offs first, expects tests, and says a missing line is not fatal. The general machine-coding criteria are in workattech/flipkart-machine-coding-round.
