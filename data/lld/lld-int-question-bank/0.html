The story: the driving test

Explain like I'm 5

Before a driving test you practise with flash cards: "What does a red octagon mean?" Easy cards first, then harder ones like "Two cars reach a crossing at the same time. Who goes first?" You do not read the whole rule book every night. You flip the cards, say the answer out loud, and put the ones you missed back in the pile.

This page is that pile of cards for low-level design. Pick a topic, flip a card (click a question), say your answer out loud before you open it, then compare. Cards you missed go back in the pile for tomorrow.

The answers here are short on purpose: they are what you would say in an interview in about a minute. When a card shows a real gap, follow its link to the lesson page, which goes deeper. Interview pages for single topics go further on each area: OOP, principles, patterns, concurrency, plus the recent interview reports.

From your world

You already have answers that most candidates do not. A vtable is a struct file_operations. Observer is a notifier chain. Chain of Responsibility is netfilter. CAS is cmpxchg. RCU is immutability plus atomic pointer swap. Lock ordering is what lockdep checks. Whenever a card asks for "a real example", reach for the kernel one first: it shows you understand the idea and not just the textbook words.
