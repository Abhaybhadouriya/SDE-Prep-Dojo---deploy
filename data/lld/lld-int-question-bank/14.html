Remember this

  • Say your answer out loud before opening a card; recall practice is what makes the bank work.
  • Four beats per answer: define, example, trade-off, link to your design or the kernel. About a minute, then stop.
  • Concurrency is the most common follow-up: name the invariant, the race, the lock and its scope.
  • Pattern questions are about intent: who changes the behaviour, what it adds, what it controls.
  • Principles serve change: add an interface where the problem says things vary, and nowhere else.
  • For case follow-ups, say which class changes and which does not before you type.
  • Use the company filter in the last week, and re-do every card you missed.
