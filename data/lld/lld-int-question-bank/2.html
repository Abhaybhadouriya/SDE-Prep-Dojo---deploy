The shape of a good one-minute answer

Almost every card can be answered with the same four beats. Step through how they build up for "What is the Strategy pattern?".

  1. Define it in one sentence. No history, no list of synonyms.
  2. One concrete example, in code-shaped words the interviewer can picture.
  3. The cost or the case where you would not use it. This is what separates SDE-2 answers.
  4. Tie it to something real: your design on the whiteboard, or a kernel structure you know well.
  5. Stop. A short complete answer invites the follow-up you have prepared; a long one invites interruptions.
