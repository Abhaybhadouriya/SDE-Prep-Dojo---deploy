Common mistakes

  1. Five if/else branches for overlap One condition on half-open intervals covers every case. Extra branches hide bugs.
  2. Closed intervals by accident Using <= makes 10-11 clash with 11-12, and a test case fails.
  3. Scanning every booking Fine for 10, not for 10,000. Keep bookings sorted per room.
  4. Check, then lock, then insert The check must be inside the lock, or two bookings both pass it.
  5. Locking participants in request order Two meetings with the same people in a different order deadlock. Sort first.
  6. Sending notifications while holding locks A slow email blocks every booking for those people.
  7. Half-booked series Inserting weeks one by one and failing at week 3 leaves weeks 1-2 booked. Check all, then insert all.
  8. Room and its bookings in one class Readers of capacity end up needing the lock. Split facts from mutable state.
  9. A global Singleton scheduler It makes tests share state. Create one in main and pass it in.
