With half-open intervals, which pair does NOT overlap?

  1. 10:00-11:00 and 10:00-11:00
  2. 10:00-12:00 and 10:30-11:00
  3. 10:00-11:00 and 11:00-12:00
  4. 10:00-11:00 and 10:59-11:30
11:00 < 11:00 is false, so back-to-back meetings do not overlap. The others are partial overlap, containment and identical.

A room's bookings are in a TreeMap keyed by start. To check a new slot, which bookings must you compare with?

  1. The one starting at or before the new start, and the next one after it
  2. All of them
  3. Only bookings with the same start
  4. Only the first and last booking of the day
Bookings in one room never overlap each other, so only the two neighbours can clash with the new slot.

Thread 1 locks alice then bob; thread 2 locks bob then alice. What fixes the resulting deadlock with the least code?

  1. Make the scheduler a Singleton
  2. Use a ConcurrentHashMap for meetings
  3. Sort participants by user id before locking
  4. Call listeners before locking
A global lock order removes the circular wait, one of the four conditions a deadlock needs.

A weekly series of 4 clashes in week 3 in the requested room. What does our design do?

  1. Books weeks 1 and 2 and throws
  2. Books weeks 1, 2 and 4
  3. Moves week 3 to Wednesday
  4. Books nothing in that room and tries the next candidate room
tryReserveAll checks every occurrence first under one lock and inserts only if all are free.

Why are listeners called after all locks are released?

  1. A slow listener (email) would otherwise block other bookings for those rooms and people
  2. To make the output order random
  3. Listeners need the locks themselves
  4. Java does not allow calls under a lock
Keep critical sections short; side effects like I/O go outside them.

Busy blocks are 9:00-10:00 and 9:30-11:00; the window is 8:00-12:00; length 15 minutes. Which free slots come out?

  1. 8:00-9:00 and 11:00-12:00
  2. 8:00-9:00 only
  3. 8:00-9:00, 10:00-11:00, 11:00-12:00
  4. 10:00-12:00 only
After the first block the cursor moves to 10:00, then max(10:00, 11:00) = 11:00 after the second, so 10:00-11:00 is not free.
