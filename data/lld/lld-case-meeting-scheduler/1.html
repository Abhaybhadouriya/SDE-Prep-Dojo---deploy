Read the table for the pattern. The core is always the same: rooms, bookings, an overlap check, no double booking. The follow-ups split into two families: room-side (capacity, "any free room", which room to prefer) and people-side (participants' conflicts, recurrence, invites, find-a-time, sharing). Concurrency ("two users book the same room at once") shows up in the Salesforce statement and the Microsoft discussion.

Interviewers love this opening: "Rooms must never be double-booked, so that is a hard invariant with a lock. People may or may not be allowed to double-book; I will make that a policy. Room choice is a strategy, and invites are events."
