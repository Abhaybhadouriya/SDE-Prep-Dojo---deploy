Class diagram

Filled diamond = composition (the part lives and dies with the whole). Hollow diamond = aggregation: the part has its own lifetime and may be shared (a strategy object can outlive the context and serve many contexts); whether it can be swapped at runtime is a separate question, said in prose. Dashed line with a hollow triangle = implements. Plain arrow = association. Dashed arrow = dependency ("uses" or "creates").

Why split Room from RoomCalendar? A Room is a fact (name, seats) that many threads read freely. A RoomCalendar changes all the time and needs a lock. Mixing them forces every reader of "how many seats?" to think about locking. Same idea as keeping a read-only struct net_device_ops apart from the per-device statistics.
