Recurring meeting: all or nothing (step through it)

  1. The recurrence strategy expands the first slot into four occurrences.
  2. Focus is taken in week 3. tryReserveAll checks every occurrence first and books nothing if any one clashes, so no half-booked series is left behind.
  3. The scheduler tries the next room, Huddle. All four are free, and they are checked and inserted under Huddle's single lock, so no other booking can sneak in between week 1 and week 4.
  4. Say the trade-off: expanding occurrences is simple and makes overlap checks exact, but an unbounded series ("every week forever") needs a stored rule plus a horizon, which is how calendar standards such as iCalendar RRULE work.
