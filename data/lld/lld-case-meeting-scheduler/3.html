Identify the entities

Underline the nouns (candidate classes) and the verbs (candidate methods) in the agreed requirements, then drop actors and plain values. Step through it.

  1. Underline the nouns. Each is a candidate class; not all survive.
  2. Underline the verbs. The class that owns the data a verb needs gets the method: overlaps() belongs to TimeSlot, book() to the scheduler.
  3. Merge roles into one User. Split "room" into the immutable facts and the mutable calendar, because they have different lifetimes and only the calendar needs a lock.
  4. Final list: 7 classes, 2 values, 3 interfaces. Do not add Building, Floor or Calendar-of-calendars unless asked.
