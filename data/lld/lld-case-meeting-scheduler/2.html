Clarify requirements

Spend 5 to 8 minutes here. The scheduler is a problem where one unasked question (can a person be in two meetings at once?) changes the locking design, so ask before you draw.

Functional (P0 - must work in the demo)

  1. Rooms Each room has an id, a name, a capacity and a floor.
  2. Book An organizer books a meeting for a time slot with a list of participants, either in a named room or in "any room that fits".
  3. No double booking A room never holds two meetings whose times overlap. Back-to-back (10:00-11:00 then 11:00-12:00) is allowed.
  4. Capacity The room must seat organizer + participants.
  5. Cancel Only the organizer can cancel; the room and everyone's calendar are freed.
  6. Query Free rooms for a slot and size; a room's schedule for a day.

Functional (P1 - say you can add them, build if time allows)

  • Recurring meetings: daily or weekly, N times (Zepto asked twice).
  • Invites and RSVP (accept, decline) with notifications (ThoughtSpot).
  • Participant conflicts: reject a meeting if someone is already busy (Microsoft), or allow it as a policy.
  • Find a free time for a group of people inside a window (Zepto).
  • Room choice rule: smallest room that fits, or nearest floor.

Non-functional

  • Correct under concurrency: fifty people booking the same room and slot at the same moment produce exactly one booking.
  • No deadlock: two bookings that share participants must never wait on each other forever.
  • Fast checks: an overlap check is O(log n) per room using a sorted map, not a scan of every booking.
  • Extensible: a new room-choice rule or recurrence rule is a new class.
  • Testable: no hidden clock; every time is passed in.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Time zones and daylight saving (store instants in UTC; display is a UI concern).
  • Persistence, UI, email delivery, calendar sharing permissions (a good follow-up).
  • Editing one occurrence of a series (the "exception date" follow-up, see extensibility).
