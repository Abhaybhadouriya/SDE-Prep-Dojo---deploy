Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Runs: one command plays the sample and prints every card and the result.
  • Cricket correctness: wides and no-balls re-bowled, balls faced right, strike after odd runs and over end, all out at n-1 wickets, chase ends at once.
  • Separation: rules in Innings, flow in Match, printing only in listeners.
  • Model: stats per innings, not on Player; Ball immutable; no God class.
  • Extensible: a new output with zero edits; a new delivery kind in two places.
  • Validation: bad tokens, bad bowlers and calls in the wrong state fail with clear messages.
  • Tests: at least the 8 edge cases above, deterministic because input is tokens.
  • Communication: you named what is out of scope and how you would add it.
