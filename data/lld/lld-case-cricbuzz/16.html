Remember this

  • Match orchestrates, Innings owns the rules, Ball is an immutable parsed value, listeners only observe.
  • Split each delivery into team runs, batter runs, extras, legal ball, ball faced and crossed.
  • Wides and no-balls are re-bowled; a no-ball is a ball faced, a wide is not.
  • Swap strike on odd runs and at the end of every over; a new batter comes in on strike.
  • Stats live per innings, not on Player. Overs are derived from legal balls.
  • Store ball events; scorecards, commentary and undo all derive from them.
  • One writer per match; readers get immutable snapshots.
