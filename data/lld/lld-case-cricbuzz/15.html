Common mistakes

  • Counting a wide as a ball of the over, or as a ball faced.
  • No end-of-over swap, so one batter faces every over.
  • Stats on Player, which breaks the second match and every test that reuses players.
  • Printing inside the scoring code, so the "print after every over" rule and the rules are tangled.
  • Parsing and applying at once, so a bad token leaves the score half-updated.
  • Continuing after the target is passed, or after all out (n-1 wickets, not n).
  • Batter/Bowler subclasses: an all-rounder breaks the hierarchy on day one.
  • Not running the sample: the Udaan input catches most rule bugs in seconds.
