Clarify the requirements

Functional (P0)

  1. get(key) Return the value or null (miss). Counts as a use for LRU/LFU.
  2. put(key, value) Insert or overwrite. If full, evict one entry chosen by the policy first.
  3. Fixed capacity in number of entries, given at construction.
  4. O(1) average time for get and put with LRU (and with LFU).
  5. Pluggable eviction LRU and LFU at least; adding FIFO or another must not touch the cache class.

P1 (PhonePe-style extensions)

  • TTL: entries expire a fixed time after they were written (or last accessed: ask).
  • Read-through: on a miss, load from a backing store, cache it, return it.
  • Write policy: write-through (store updated on every put) or write-back (store updated on eviction or flush).
  • Multi-level: a small fast L1 in front of a larger L2 in front of the store.
  • remove(key), stats (hits, misses, evictions), thread safety.

Non-functional

  • Correct under concurrent use; no entry lost on eviction in write-back mode; testable without sleeping (inject a clock).

Out of scope

Distribution across machines, consistent hashing, replication, persistence to disk. That is the distributed cache HLD.

Questions to ask

  • Capacity by count or by bytes? (Count; weight-based is an extension.)
  • Does overwriting a key count as a use? (Yes in our design.)
  • LFU ties: which of two keys with the same count goes? (The older one: LRU within the frequency.)
  • TTL from write or from last access? Lazy expiry on read only, or also a background sweep?
  • On a miss with a backing store, may two threads both load the same key? Is that acceptable?
  • May I use LinkedHashMap / OrderedDict, or must I write the list myself? (Usually: write it.)
