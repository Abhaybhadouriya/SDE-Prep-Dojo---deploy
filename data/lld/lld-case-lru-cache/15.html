Common mistakes

  • Only naming LRU in a cache design and not writing it (the Amazon story).
  • Hard-coding one policy inside Cache (the Groww story).
  • A singly linked list or list.remove(key) on an ArrayList: O(n) per access.
  • Forgetting to update order on overwrite, or updating the value map but not the policy on eviction (the two drift apart; later a "victim" key is not in the map).
  • ReadWriteLock with gets under the read lock: corrupts the list.
  • Loading from the store while holding the lock: one slow query freezes the whole cache.
  • Losing dirty entries in write-back on eviction or expiry.
  • Tests that sleep for TTL: slow and flaky. Inject a clock.
