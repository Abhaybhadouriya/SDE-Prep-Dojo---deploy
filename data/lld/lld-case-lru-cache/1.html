How it is asked

Three shapes: "implement LRU" as a 20-30 minute coding question inside a DSA or LLD round; "design a cache" as a 60-minute LLD round where the LRU code is expected inside the design; and a 2-hour or take-home machine-coding round with pluggable eviction, TTL and write policies.
