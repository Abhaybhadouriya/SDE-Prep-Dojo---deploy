Identify the entities

  1. Write down the problem in one or two sentences.
  2. Nouns: Cache, Entry, capacity, EvictionPolicy, BackingStore, expiry and write mode.
  3. Verbs: the public ones (get, put, remove, flush) go on Cache; evict goes on the policy; load and save on the store.
  4. Capacity is a field. Expiry and write mode are configuration: a TTL number, a WritePolicy enum, and a Clock you can replace in tests.
  5. The final model. Note that the policy only ever sees keys; values stay in the cache's own map.
