Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Correct matching: price first, then time; trade at the resting price; partial fills; remainder rests or is cancelled correctly.
  • Right data structures: sorted map of FIFO queues; O(log P) best price; O(1) head.
  • Money safety: integer paise; check and block atomically; refund the gap; release on cancel; conservation test.
  • Separation: exchange knows no money; broker knows no matching; listener interface between them.
  • Extensible rules: one class per rule; adding a rule touches no old code.
  • Concurrency: lock per symbol, lock per account, stated lock order, no nested account locks.
  • State: clear order statuses and legal transitions.
  • Working demo + tests, including rejection paths.
  • Communication: names the pattern and the reason; offers what production engines do differently.
