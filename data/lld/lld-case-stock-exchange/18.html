Quiz

Two sell orders rest at 100.00: A arrived first, B second. A buy for 1 share at 101.00 arrives. What happens?

  1. B fills at 101.00
  2. A fills at 100.00
  3. A fills at 101.00
Same price, so time decides: A. The trade is at the resting price, 100.00.

Which structure gives the best ask fastest and keeps time order at each price?

  1. An unsorted list of orders
  2. A hash map from order id to order
  3. A sorted map from price to a FIFO queue
Sorted map finds the best price; the FIFO queue keeps arrival order. The id hash map is an extra index for cancel.

A market buy for 10 finds only 8 shares on the ask side. Default behaviour?

  1. Fill 8, cancel 2
  2. Rest 2 on the bid side at the last price
  3. Reject the whole order
Market orders are immediate-or-cancel by default: fill what you can now, cancel the rest. Rejecting everything is fill-or-kill.

Why does the broker block funds at order time?

  1. To make matching faster
  2. So two orders cannot both spend the same money before either trades
  3. Because the exchange needs the cash
Check-and-block in one critical section prevents a double spend; settlement then draws from the blocked amount.

Which lock order is used, and why is it deadlock-free?

  1. Account, then book, then second account
  2. All accounts first, sorted by id
  3. Book, then one account at a time; placement never holds an account lock while taking the book lock
No thread ever waits for a book while holding an account, and no thread holds two accounts: no cycle, no deadlock.

Which pattern lets you add price alerts without changing the exchange?

  1. Observer (a new TradeListener)
  2. Singleton
  3. Builder
The exchange publishes trades to any listener; an alert service is just another listener.

What single test catches double settlement, missing refunds and races?

  1. Checking the last price
  2. Total cash and total shares (free + blocked) are unchanged after many random concurrent orders
  3. Counting trades
Trading moves money and shares between users; it never creates or destroys them. Conservation is the invariant.
