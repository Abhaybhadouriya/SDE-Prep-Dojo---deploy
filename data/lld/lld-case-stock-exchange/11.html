Extensibility: the follow-ups

  1. Stop-loss orders. Keep a separate trigger book (price -> orders). A TradeListener watches the last price; when it crosses a trigger, it submits the stored order as a market or limit order.
  2. IOC / FOK time-in-force. A field on Order. IOC is what our market order does. FOK (fill or kill) needs a dry-run: check available quantity at acceptable prices before matching.
  3. Modify order. Cancel + new order; losing time priority is the standard rule (except when only reducing quantity).
  4. Price alerts ("notify me above 1504"). Already in PriceTicker: an Observer of trades. Scale-out version: HLD: Stock Price Alerts.
  5. Market open/close (Flipkart). A market State (PRE_OPEN, OPEN, CLOSED) checked by a new OrderRule; listing only while open.
  6. Brokerage and charges (Groww variant). A ChargeCalculator strategy per plan (flat, percent, intraday vs delivery) applied at settlement.
  7. Self-trade prevention. In the match loop, skip (or cancel) a resting order from the same user.
  8. Pro-rata matching. Extract the loop behind a MatchingPolicy interface.
  9. Roles. Admin-only listStock: check a role on the caller in the broker facade.
