Common mistakes

  • Using double for prices. Use integer paise or ticks.
  • One sorted list of all orders sorted on every insert. Use price levels.
  • Trading at the incoming order's price, or averaging the two.
  • Forgetting time priority (a HashMap or TreeSet without a sequence number at one price).
  • Checking funds without blocking them, so two quick orders both pass.
  • Mixing money into the order book; then every test needs users and wallets.
  • Leaving empty price levels in the map, so "best price" points at nothing.
  • A global lock around everything: correct but every symbol waits for every other.
  • Nested account locks in settlement in arbitrary order: deadlock under load.
