Clarify requirements

Functional
  • List a stock with an opening price (admin only).
  • Register users; add money; hold shares (portfolio).
  • Place limit orders (buy/sell at a price or better) and market orders (best available now).
  • Match by price, then time; trade at the resting order's price; allow partial fills.
  • Cancel an open order; see order status and trade history per user.
  • Reject orders outside a price band (+-20% of last trade), without funds, or without shares.
  • Publish trades and the last price (ticker, alerts).
Non-functional
  • Correctness first: money and shares are conserved; never sell shares you do not have; never spend cash twice.
  • Deterministic: same order sequence gives the same trades (needed for replay and audits).
  • Concurrent: many users place orders at once; different symbols must not block each other.
  • Low latency matching: O(log P) to find a price level, O(1) at the head of a level.
  • Money as integers (paise), never double.

Out of scope: real clearing and settlement (T+1), market-maker logic, circuit breakers across the whole market, persistence, network protocols (FIX). For the distributed version see HLD: Stock Exchange and HLD: Stock Broker App.

Questions to ask

  1. Exchange, broker, or both? Decides where you spend your 90 minutes.
  2. Order types? Limit and market are enough; stop-loss and IOC/FOK are follow-ups.
  3. At what price does a trade happen? The standard answer is the resting (maker) order's price. Confirm.
  4. Market order with not enough liquidity? Fill what you can and cancel the rest (IOC) is the usual choice.
  5. Price band base? Last traded price or previous close? (We use last traded.)
  6. Can a user trade with themselves? Real exchanges prevent self-trades; ask if it matters.
  7. Persistence and roles? In-memory is fine; admin vs user can be a simple role check.
