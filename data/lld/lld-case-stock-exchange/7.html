Core flow: placing an order through the broker

  1. The app calls place. The broker reads the last traded price; it needs it for the band rule and, for market buys, to decide how much cash to block.
  2. Under the user's account lock, the rules run (quantity, price band, funds/shares). If all pass, the money (or the shares, for a sell) moves from free to blocked. Checking and blocking happen under the same lock, so two orders cannot spend the same rupee.
  3. The order goes to the exchange, which takes the per-symbol lock and runs the matching loop. Other symbols are not blocked.
  4. Every trade is published to the listeners in trade order. The broker settles: buyer's blocked cash goes down, shares go up, the gap between block price and trade price comes back; the seller's blocked shares go down and cash goes up.
  5. The book lock is released and the order (now FILLED, PARTIALLY_FILLED with the rest resting, or CANCELLED for a market remainder) comes back. For a market order the broker then releases what is still blocked.
