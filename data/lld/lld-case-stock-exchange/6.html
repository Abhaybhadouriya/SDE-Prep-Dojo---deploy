Matching, step by step

  1. The book has three resting sells. Two are at 1505.00; O2 arrived before O3. A limit buy for 12 at up to 1510.00 arrives.
  2. Best ask is 1500.00, which is at or below 1510.00, so they cross. Trade 10 at 1500.00 (the resting price, not the buyer's limit). O1 is filled and the 1500 level disappears.
  3. Next best ask is 1505.00, still crossing. At this price O2 came first, so O2 trades 2 and keeps 3. O3 is untouched: time priority.
  4. The buy is filled. The broker had blocked 12 x 1510.00 = 18 120.00; the trades cost 18 010.00, so 110.00 goes back to free cash. No money is ever "in flight".
  5. Each trade is published to listeners in order. If the buy had wanted 30 shares, the remaining 18 would rest on the bid side at 1510.00. A market order never rests: what cannot fill is cancelled.
