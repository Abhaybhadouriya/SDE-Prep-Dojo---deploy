Identify entities

  1. Nouns. Order is the centre: id, user, symbol, side, type, price, quantity, filled, status and an arrival sequence number. A Trade is an immutable record of one match.
  2. Verbs. Placing is a broker action (it must check money). Submitting and matching are exchange actions. Settling is the broker reacting to a trade.
  3. Things that vary. Risk rules grow (lot size, circuit limits, margin), so each is an OrderRule. Many parties care about trades, so trades are published to TradeListeners.
  4. The key design move: keep the exchange ignorant of money. It matches orders and publishes trades. The broker blocks, settles and keeps history. This split is what "Zerodha vs NSE" means, and it keeps each class small.
