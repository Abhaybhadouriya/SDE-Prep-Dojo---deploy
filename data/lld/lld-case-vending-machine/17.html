Common mistakes

  • A switch on state in every method, or boolean flags (hasMoney, isDispensing) that can contradict each other.
  • Silently ignoring illegal events instead of refusing them with a message.
  • Dropping coins into the cash box on insert, so a refund needs change and a failed sale leaves money in the till.
  • Dispensing before checking change, so the customer gets the product but not the money.
  • Greedy change with limited coins, and no plan when change is impossible.
  • Double for money.
  • States holding data (balance inside HasMoneyState), so state objects cannot be shared and transitions lose data.
  • Maintenance allowed mid-purchase, or restocking without a mode at all.
  • A Singleton machine "because there is one".
