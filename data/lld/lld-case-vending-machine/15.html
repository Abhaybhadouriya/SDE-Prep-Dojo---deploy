Evaluator rubric checklist

  • State pattern used and justified: one class per state, transitions decided by the states, no switch(state) in the machine.
  • Illegal events handled in every state, with clear messages, never silently ignored.
  • Atomic purchase: validate stock, money and change first; commit after. A failed sale changes nothing.
  • Correct change from a limited cash box; you can explain why greedy is not enough.
  • Refund returns exactly what was inserted.
  • Money conserved, integers for money, no floating point.
  • Separation: Inventory, CashBox, ChangeMaker, states, context; I/O outside the domain.
  • Thread safety explained (per-event lock, session ownership) and the coarse lock implemented.
  • Extensible to card payment, select-first flow, out-of-service and coffee machine without rewrites.
