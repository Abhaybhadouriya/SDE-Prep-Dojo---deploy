Identify the entities (nouns and verbs)

  1. Write one or two sentences describing the whole machine, including the operator.
  2. Underline the nouns: customer, coin, product, slot, change, refund, operator.
  3. Underline the verbs. Insert, select and cancel are customer events; restock and load are operator events; dispense and make change happen inside.
  4. Drop or reshape nouns: the customer and the operator are actors who call methods, not classes. Change and refund are lists of coins. The operator's presence is a machine state (MAINTENANCE).
  5. What varies by state becomes the State interface; how to pick change coins varies too, so it becomes a ChangeMaker strategy. The machine itself is the context that holds the data.
