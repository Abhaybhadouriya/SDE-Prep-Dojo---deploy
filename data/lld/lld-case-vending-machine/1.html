How it is asked

The vending machine is the standard "state machine" LLD question, often the first one after games like tic-tac-toe. It appears as a whiteboard round (classes, attributes, method signatures, class diagram) and as an IDE round where the interviewer wants objects, methods and a design pattern.

Do not worry about these names yet: each pattern (and words like idempotency, "doing it twice has the same effect as once") is explained, with a link, in the Patterns section below.

Open-source LLD collections also tag Flipkart for this problem, and the free lecture series in adityatandon15/Low-Level-Design-Course teaches it as the State-pattern lecture. The coffee machine is the same problem with recipes (ingredients instead of slots); see the coffee machine case once it is published.

The MakeMyTrip report is the lesson: the candidate's design worked, but "the interviewer wanted a design pattern". For this problem the State pattern is expected; name it early and explain why (behaviour of every button depends on the state). The Amazon report is the other lesson: when no requirements are given, the clarifying questions are the first half of the round.
