  • Asked at Uber (TTL counters, getRandom), Razorpay and Salesforce (in-memory DB), 2024-26.
  • Entry = value + absolute expiresAt + version. Expired = absent on every read (lazy).
  • Active expiry: min-heap of (expiresAt, key, version), bounded batch per write, stale items skipped by version.
  • Transactions: per-session stack of write maps with tombstones; rollback pops one; inner commit merges; outer commit is atomic.
  • Optimistic commit: validate the versions you read; first committer wins; retry on conflict. Blind writes do not conflict.
  • Read-write lock: reads shared and never delete; writes, commit and purge exclusive; listeners after unlock.
  • Snapshot = Memento: opaque, cheap with immutable entries, TTLs stay absolute.
