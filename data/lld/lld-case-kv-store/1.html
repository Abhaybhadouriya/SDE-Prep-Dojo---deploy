A community post from September 2025 (7220995, not an interview report) shares a transactional KV store with thread-local nested transactions and says many posted solutions were not fully correct; the usual bugs are covered in common mistakes. Close cousins: LRU cache (eviction instead of expiry), rate limiter (time windows) and text editor (undo with Command or Memento).

The sentence that wins this round: "Every entry stores an absolute expiry and a version. Reads treat expired entries as absent, and a min-heap of (expiresAt, key, version) lets a sweep remove them in time order, skipping stale heap items by version. Transactions are a per-session stack of write layers with tombstones; the outer commit validates the versions it read and applies the write set atomically under the write lock."
