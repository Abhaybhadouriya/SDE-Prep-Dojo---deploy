Mistake 1 and 2 on a clock: how 90 minutes go wrong

Most "gate" failures are time failures. Here are two runs of the same 90-minute machine-coding round. The top one is a pattern we see in rejection posts; the bottom one is the plan the guides recommend.

  1. Plan for 10 minutes, not 25. Entities, one service, maps for storage, and the two or three places that will change. Write them as empty classes in the IDE, not on paper.
  2. Get one flow running by minute 30. "Onboard user, create order, print status" end to end, ugly but working. Now you can never submit nothing.
  3. Compile and run after every feature, at least every 10-15 minutes. The machine-coding time plan is the reference timeline. Forty errors at minute 75 is how good designs fail.
  4. Write the driver before the bonus. The driver is your demo script for the evaluator. Without it they test your code their way.
  5. Add patterns where a second variant exists. The brief says "pricing may vary by plan"? That is your Strategy. Nothing says it varies? A plain method is fine.
