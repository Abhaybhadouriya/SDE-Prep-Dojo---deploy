The consolidated rubric: what graders actually score

Companies word it differently, but the boxes are nearly the same everywhere. Here is the 2026 Flipkart machine-coding brief as one candidate copied it: demoable and functionally correct code, readability, entity modelling, modularity and extensibility, separation of concerns, abstractions, exception handling and code comments; edge cases should "fail gracefully" (Flipkart brief, posted 2026-02-18). A CRED round listed only four: completely working code, enough unit tests, extensibility, and OOP design principles (CRED report, 2023-08-31).

We merged the official pages, the briefs, the workat.tech and HelloInterview guides, and the candidate posts into one list. The "evidence" column counts distinct sources that name the item. It is a rough measure of how often each box shows up, not a weight any company has published.

Box on the clipboardWhat the evaluator actually doesWho says so
1. It runs and demosOpens your zip, runs main or the tests, feeds the sample cases. A compile error ends the review early.Flipkart and PhonePe briefs; workat.tech; a PhonePe candidate was told working code is absolutely necessary or there is no evaluation (2023-06-23)
2. P0 is completeTicks each "must have" in the brief. Bonus features earn nothing if a P0 is broken.Flipkart brief: attempt bonus only after P0 (2026-02-18)
3. Sensible entitiesLooks for one class per concept with its own data and rules, not a bag of maps inside one service.Flipkart brief "proper entity modeling"; Cleartrip report (2026-07-21)
4. Separation of concernsChecks that parsing, storage, business rules and printing live in different places.Flipkart HR feedback "SRP and separation of concerns not followed" (2023-04-01)
5. ExtensibilityAsks "add a new X" and watches how many files you touch. One new class: good. Edits in five switch statements: bad.Flipkart 2024 brief: absorb new requirements with minimal changes (2024-05-28); Razorpay rejection (2025-06-26)
6. Errors fail gracefullyFeeds an unknown id, a double cancel, an illegal state change. Expects a clear exception or message, not a stack trace or silent wrong answer.Flipkart brief; Cleartrip interviewer tested already-cancelled and invalid order ids (2026-07-21)
7. Concurrency where it mattersAsks "two users book the last seat at the same time". Looks for the lock at the right granularity, not just a keyword.Flipkart brief; Uber report: concurrency "was the real test" (2026-08-29)
8. TestsAtlassian expects your test framework set up before the round; CRED ships pre-written tests you must pass.Atlassian official; CRED (2024-10-17)
9. CommunicationListens for the reasoning behind each choice and for alternatives you rejected.Atlassian official; Microsoft hiring tips; HelloInterview

The order matters. Boxes 1 and 2 are gates: fail them and the rest is rarely read. Boxes 3-7 are scores: they separate "hire" from "strong hire". A Flipkart candidate who was rejected wrote that the evaluator "seemed to care more" about working features than about patterns (2025-04-19); another, who passed, said Flipkart prioritises correct running code over neat OOP (2026-02-19). Two different people, same lesson.
