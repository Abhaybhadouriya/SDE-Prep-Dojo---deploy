The twelve mistakes that sink most rounds

We read every post in our collection of 311 LLD and machine-coding reports (LeetCode Discuss, June 2024 to November 2026) that gave a reason for a bad outcome, plus a few older posts that are widely shared. Of the 311, 48 report a negative outcome, and only a handful say why. Those reasons, plus the red flags in the workat.tech and HelloInterview guides, collapse into twelve mistakes.

12Losing time to tooling (typos, syntax, language switch)Gate 1-2Atlassian: an autocorrect typo cost 5-7 minutes and the time for follow-ups (2026-05-25; that candidate was still hired, but lost the chance to show extensibility); Uber: master one language (2026-08-29)One language, a warmed-up IDE, a project template.

Feedback is noisy. One Razorpay candidate got "no validations or edge cases" as a con even though, by their account, they checked many edge cases live with the interviewer (2025-06-26). An Uber candidate heard "negative LLD feedback" after giving working code, and still got an offer after the debrief (2025-05-22). Treat any single verdict as one data point. Fix the patterns that repeat across many posts.
