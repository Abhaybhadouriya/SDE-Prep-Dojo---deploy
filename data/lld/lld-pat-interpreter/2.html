Intent

Interpreter (GoF behavioral pattern): for a small rule language, make one class per grammar rule, build a tree of those objects for each expression, and evaluate the expression by asking the root of the tree to evaluate itself.

In plain words: write each grammar rule as a class (And, Or, Not, Compare). A parsed sentence becomes a tree of those objects, an abstract syntax tree (AST). Evaluating the sentence is a recursive call on the root.

Interpreter is the only GoF pattern that refactoring.guru leaves out of its catalogue, and our research put it in the "mention only" tier for interviews. It still earns a page because the interview question behind it is common.
