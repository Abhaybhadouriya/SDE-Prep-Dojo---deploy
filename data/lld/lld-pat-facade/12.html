Remember this

  • Facade = one simple entry point over a complicated subsystem of many classes.
  • Knowledge flows one way: the facade knows the subsystem; the subsystem does not know the facade.
  • It orchestrates (order, defaults, cleanup); it does not own business rules.
  • Return your own types, inject the subsystem parts, and let experts bypass it.
  • Split a growing facade by use case or audience before it becomes a god class.
  • Facade simplifies many; Adapter converts one; Mediator coordinates peers that call back into it.
  • Kernel bridge: system calls, request_firmware(), and a driver's open/stop callbacks are facades.
