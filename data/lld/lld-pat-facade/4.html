How it works: one placeOrder() call

  1. The client makes one call with the business inputs. It does not know which services exist.
  2. The facade reserves stock first, so we never charge for something we cannot ship.
  3. Then it charges payment. If this fails, the facade releases the reservation: the cleanup rule lives in one place.
  4. Then it books shipping and gets a tracking number.
  5. Then it notifies the user.
  6. The client gets one result object. Order, defaults and compensation were the facade's job. (In a distributed system this sequence becomes a saga with an orchestrator, which is a facade-shaped service.)
