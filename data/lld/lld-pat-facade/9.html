Related and confused-with patterns

PatternWrapsDirection of knowledgeOne-liner
FacadeMany classesOne way: facade knows subsystem; subsystem does not know facade"Simplifies a subsystem for its clients."

Decorator / ProxyOne objectSame interface as the wrapped object"Same interface, more behaviour / guarded."

Singletonn/aA facade is often a single instance"Usually one facade per subsystem; inject it, do not hard-code a global."

Facade vs Mediator is the classic trap (you meet Mediator properly later, in the behavioral patterns). A mediator is a central object that a group of peers talk through instead of to each other; those peers are called its colleagues. In a facade, the subsystem classes work fine without the facade and never call it; the facade adds a convenient front. In a mediator, the colleagues are built to talk only through the mediator (an air-traffic tower, a chat room), and the mediator contains the interaction rules between them. Ask: "Do the parts call back into it?" Yes means Mediator.
