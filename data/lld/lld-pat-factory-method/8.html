Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. A factory method is usually stateless. If the creator caches the product it made (lazy creation), the usual lazy-init rules apply: holder idiom or double-checked locking with volatile.
  • Testing. The classic seam: in a test, subclass the creator and override createChannel() to return a fake. You test the base class's algorithm without any real channel.
  • Cost. One virtual call per creation. The real cost is structural: one creator subclass per product. When that list grows, move to a parameterised factory or registry.
