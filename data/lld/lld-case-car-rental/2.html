Clarify requirements

Functional requirements

  1. Branches and vehicles the company has branches; each vehicle belongs to one branch and has a type (hatchback, sedan, SUV, bike), seats, an hourly and a daily rate.
  2. Search find vehicles that match filters (branch, type, seats, max price) and are free for the whole requested time range, cheapest first.
  3. Book reserve a specific vehicle for a time range; the price is fixed at booking time.
  4. No double booking two active reservations of one vehicle may never overlap, even under concurrent requests.
  5. Lifecycle cancel (before pick-up), pick up, return. A late return pays a late fee.
  6. History list a user's reservations.

Non-functional requirements

  • Correct under concurrency: this is the main grading point after "it runs".
  • Extensible pricing and filters: weekend surcharge, coupons, new filters without editing the service.
  • Money as integers (rupees or paise), never double.
  • In memory for the round, with a clear seam where a database would go.

Out of scope

  • Payments (assume a gateway returns success), KYC and licence checks, GPS tracking, damage claims, maps. Say them out loud so the interviewer can pull one in if they want.
