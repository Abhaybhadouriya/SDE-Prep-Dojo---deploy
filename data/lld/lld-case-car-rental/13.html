Evaluator rubric checklist

Tags: must = missing it usually fails the round; strong = separates a pass from a strong pass; bonus = extra credit if time allows.

  • must Runs with a demo that shows search, book, rejection, cancel and return.
  • must Overlap logic correct for edges, containment and back-to-back slots.
  • strong No double booking under concurrency, with an explanation of the lock scope.
  • must Availability derived from reservations, not a flag.
  • strong Extensible search (Specification or equivalent) and pricing (Strategy).
  • strong State transitions guarded: illegal ones throw.
  • strong Money as integers; time as proper date-time types.
  • strong Clear errors: unavailable, not found, invalid state, each with a message.
  • strong Tests, including one concurrency test.
  • bonus Database story ready: schema, the overlap query, the constraint or row lock.

Red flag that fails the round: An isAvailable flag on the car instead of reservations with time slots (it cannot answer "is it free next Tuesday?"), or an overlap check with <= that rejects back-to-back bookings.
