Common mistakes

  • isAvailable flag on the car instead of time-range reservations.
  • Wrong overlap check: four if-branches that miss containment, or inclusive ends that reject back-to-back bookings.
  • Check and insert not atomic, or atomic with one global lock that serialises the whole fleet.
  • Holding a lock during payment or any network call.
  • double for money; Date strings for time.
  • A search() with ten parameters and nested ifs.
  • Subclass per vehicle type (class SUV extends Vehicle) when types differ only in data.
  • Mutable reservation status set from outside (r.setStatus(CANCELLED)) with no transition checks.
  • No concurrency test: saying "it is thread-safe" without proving it.
