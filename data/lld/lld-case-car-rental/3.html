Identify entities

  1. Nouns: User, Branch, Vehicle, Reservation and the time range itself (TimeSlot). The time range deserves its own type because it carries the overlap rule.
  2. Verbs: search, book, cancel, pick up, return. They live on a service (the facade the driver calls); lifecycle checks live on Reservation itself.
  3. What varies: the filters a user applies, the price rules, surcharges, and later the storage. Each gets an interface.
  4. What is derived, not stored: "is this car available?" is a question about reservations and a time range, not a boolean on the car. A separate Inventory class adds nothing a map does not already do.

The biggest modelling mistake in this problem is boolean isAvailable on Vehicle. A car is not "available"; it is available for a time range. A flag cannot answer "free next Saturday?", and it goes stale the moment a booking ends. Store reservations; derive availability.
