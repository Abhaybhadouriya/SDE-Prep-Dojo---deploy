Remember this

  • Lock = one at a time. Condition = wait until true. Semaphore = at most N. Latch = wait for K events. Spinlock = wait without sleeping.
  • A lock gives exclusion and visibility; reads of shared mutable data need the lock too.
  • Default to synchronized. Use ReentrantLock for tryLock, timeouts, interruptible waits, fairness or several Conditions; unlock in finally.
  • Always while (!predicate) await();. Change state, then signal, while holding the lock.
  • Separate Conditions per reason let you use signal(); with one wait set use notifyAll().
  • Mutex has an owner; semaphore does not. Semaphores count resources and signal between threads.
  • Deadlock fix: global lock order; tryLock + timeout as the safety net.
  • Spin only for tiny sections on another CPU; never sleep or do I/O while holding a lock.
