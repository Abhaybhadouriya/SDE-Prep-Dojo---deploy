Resources

  • java.util.concurrent.locks (Java 21 API) doc - the official overview of Lock, Condition and ReadWriteLock.
  • ReentrantLock (Java 21 API) doc - fairness, tryLock barging, the lock/try/finally idiom.
  • Condition (Java 21 API) doc - read the note on spurious wakeups and the bounded-buffer example.
  • Semaphore and CountDownLatch doc - both include short usage examples.
  • JLS chapter 17: Threads and Locks doc - the formal source for monitors, wait sets and happens-before.
  • cppreference: std::condition_variable doc - the C++ version, with the predicate overload of wait().
  • Python threading doc - Lock, RLock, Condition, Semaphore, Barrier.
  • Linux kernel: generic mutex design and spinlocks doc - optimistic spinning and why mutexes replaced semaphores; the bridge from your world.
  • The Little Book of Semaphores (free PDF from the author) book - dozens of puzzles solved with semaphores only; great practice for the classic-problems lesson.
  • Java Concurrency in Practice (book site) book - the standard book; chapters 13-14 cover explicit locks and condition queues. Buy or borrow it; do not use pirated copies.
  • AlgoMaster: concurrency interview track course - a free topic list to check your coverage against.
  • kousiknath/LowLevelDesign repo - MIT-licensed Java solutions with blocking queues, a connection pool and a thread pool that use these primitives.
