Extensibility: the follow-ups interviewers ask

Follow-upHow it plugs in
Driver must accept within 15 s; else offer to the nextAdd OFFERED to TripStatus. tryReserve() sets the driver to OFFERED; a ScheduledExecutorService timeout (or a reject) releases the driver and offers the next ranked candidate; accept moves to DRIVER_ASSIGNED. Same CAS, one more state.
Surge pricing per zoneSurgeFare gets a SurgeProvider (zone to multiplier, computed from demand/supply). Fare is quoted at request time and stored on the trip so the rider pays what they saw.
Best rated, least idle, or cheapestNew DriverMatchingStrategy classes; or a weighted composite: score = a x distance + b x (5 - rating) + c x idle time.
Ride pooling (Uber Pool)A Trip has many riders and an ordered list of stops; a driver has capacity; matching checks detour limits. A big change: say so, and keep it out of P0.
Scheduled ridesA request with a future time goes to a scheduler that calls requestRide a few minutes before. Nothing else changes.
Payments (cash, card, UPI, wallet)PaymentStrategy called after COMPLETED, with an idempotency key per trip. See payment system.
RatingsAfter COMPLETED, both sides rate; driver rating is a running average (store sum and count, update atomically).
Cancellation feeA CancellationPolicy strategy: free within 2 minutes of assignment, a fee after. Called from cancelTrip.
