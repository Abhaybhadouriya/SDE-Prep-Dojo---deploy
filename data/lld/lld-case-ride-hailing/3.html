Identify the entities

  1. Read the problem statement and underline as you go.
  2. Nouns are candidate classes: Rider, Driver, Cab, Trip, Location, Fare, System.
  3. Verbs become methods. "Assign the nearest" is behaviour that varies (nearest, best rated) - a strategy. "Fare depends on" also varies - a second strategy.
  4. Merge or drop: in most rounds a driver owns exactly one cab, so Cab becomes a CabType enum on Driver. "System" is the service facade, not a domain class.
  5. The final list. Fare is not an entity; it is a number on Trip produced by a FareStrategy.

Split Driver from Rider even though both are "users". They have different data and different rules. If the interviewer insists on a shared User, use composition (a Driver has a profile) rather than a deep inheritance tree.
