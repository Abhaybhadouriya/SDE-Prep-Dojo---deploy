Clarify the requirements

Functional (P0)

  1. Register riders and drivers. A driver has a cab type (MINI, SEDAN, PREMIUM), a location and a rating; drivers can go online and offline and update their location.
  2. Request a ride Rider, pickup, drop, cab type. The system picks an available driver of that type within a radius using the current matching strategy, or fails with "no driver available".
  3. Trip lifecycle REQUESTED, DRIVER_ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED; CANCELLED allowed only before the trip starts. Illegal moves are rejected.
  4. End the trip Compute the fare from cab type, distance and minutes with the current fare rule; free the driver at the drop location.
  5. Concurrency Two simultaneous requests must never get the same driver; a rider has at most one active trip.

P1 (bonus)

  • Surge pricing; best-rated matching; driver accept / reject with a timeout; trip history; ratings after a trip; payment methods.

Non-functional

  • Correct under concurrency (no double booking, no lost trips).
  • Extensible: new matching or fare rules without touching the service.
  • Fast matching: discuss how to avoid scanning every driver when there are 100,000 of them.
  • In-memory, a driver/main that demos every P0 flow, including the race.

Out of scope (say it)

Maps and real routing (we use straight-line distance on a grid), real payments, push notifications, the HLD of location ingestion (that is the ride-hailing HLD).

Questions to ask

  • Does the driver have to accept, or is assignment automatic? (P0: automatic; P1: offer with timeout.)
  • How is "nearest" measured: straight line, or ETA? (Straight line for the round; ETA behind the same strategy later.)
  • What happens when no driver is free: fail fast, or queue the request? (Fail fast; the client retries.)
  • Can a rider cancel after the trip starts? Is there a cancellation fee?
  • Fare formula: base + per km + per minute, by cab type? Rounding? (Integer paise.)
  • How many concurrent requests should the demo prove? (We show 40 riders and 5 cabs.)
