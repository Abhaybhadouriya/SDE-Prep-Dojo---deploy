How it is asked

Ride hailing is the fourth most-asked LLD problem in our list. At Uber it is a running-code round where concurrency is the real test; at Amazon and Salesforce it is more often a whiteboard round about entities, the trip state machine and fare rules.

The July 2026 Uber candidate got "No Hire" mostly because requirements kept changing mid-round. Defend yourself the same way a good spec does: write the P0 list and assumptions at the top of the file in the first 5 minutes and point at it when a new requirement arrives ("good idea, that is an extension; here is where it plugs in"). Then keep coding.
