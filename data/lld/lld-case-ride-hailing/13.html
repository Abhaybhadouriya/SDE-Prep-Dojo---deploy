Evaluator rubric checklist

Tags: must = missing it usually fails the round; strong = separates a pass from a strong pass; bonus = extra credit if time allows.

  • must Runs: main demos request, start, end, cancel, failure cases and the race.
  • must No double booking, shown by a real concurrent test, not just claimed.
  • strong Atomic claim separated from lock-free search; the lock (or CAS) scope is small and named.
  • must State machine in one place; illegal transitions impossible.
  • strong Strategies for matching and fare, swappable at runtime; no if (type == ...) pricing chains.
  • strong Money in integer paise, fare formula stated.
  • strong Clean ownership: Driver owns its status, Trip owns its state, the service orchestrates.
  • bonus Can discuss scale (geo index) and the offer/timeout extension without a rewrite.

Red flag that fails the round: No concurrent test for double assignment: you say the driver claim is atomic but never run two riders against one driver, or the trip status is a plain field any caller can set to anything.
