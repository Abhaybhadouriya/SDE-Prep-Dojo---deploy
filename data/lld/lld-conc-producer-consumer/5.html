Way 3: just use BlockingQueue

In real code, and in most machine-coding rounds unless told otherwise, you use java.util.concurrent.BlockingQueue. Its methods come in four families. Learning this grid saves you in every queue question:

Two habits matter. Never call new LinkedBlockingQueue<>() without a capacity in a design answer; you just built the unbounded queue the warning above talked about. And remember that the JDK documents a memory guarantee for these queues: actions before putting an item happen-before actions after another thread takes it, so the item you hand over is safely published.
