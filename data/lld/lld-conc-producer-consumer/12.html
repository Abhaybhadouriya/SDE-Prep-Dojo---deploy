Remember this

  • Producer-consumer = a bounded buffer between threads; it decouples rates, absorbs bursts and keeps order.
  • Always bound the queue. Unbounded queues turn overload into an out-of-memory crash.
  • Hand-written: ring array + lock + notFull/notEmpty, while loops, unlock in finally. With one wait set use notifyAll.
  • In real code use ArrayBlockingQueue (or a bounded LinkedBlockingQueue); know the add/offer/put/offer-timeout grid.
  • Shutdown: one poison pill per consumer after all producers finish; interrupts for "stop now".
  • Backpressure policy is a design decision: block, time out and reject, drop newest, drop oldest.
  • SPSC ring = lock-free with release/acquire on head and tail; it is your NIC descriptor ring.
