Where this shows up in LLD rounds

Producer-consumer is the most common concurrency building block in recent LLD and machine-coding reports. Our research of public interview posts found it in at least 8 separate asks:

The same building block appears at system scale in Design a Message Queue (Kafka itself), where the buffer becomes a replicated log on disk.

How to drive the concurrency part of these rounds: (1) name the queue and its bound, (2) say what blocks and what times out, (3) say how workers shut down (pill or interrupt), (4) say what happens on overload, (5) mention ordering (one queue per key if per-key order matters). Five sentences, and you have answered the follow-ups before they are asked.
