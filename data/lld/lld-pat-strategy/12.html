  • Strategy = put each way of doing one job behind one interface; the context holds one and delegates.
  • It replaces a growing if/else on a mode field and makes the context closed for change.
  • Someone still chooses: keep that choice in one factory, registry or DI container.
  • Prefer stateless strategies (shareable, thread-safe); one instance per context if they hold state.
  • In Java, Comparator; in Python, key=; in Linux, tcp_congestion_ops.
  • vs State: the client picks a strategy; states pick the next state themselves.
  • vs Template Method: composition at runtime vs inheritance at compile time.
