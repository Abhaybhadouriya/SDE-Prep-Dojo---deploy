Structure: one context, one interface, many algorithms

  • Strategy (FeeStrategy): the common interface for every algorithm. Usually one method, sometimes two or three closely related ones.
  • Concrete Strategies (HourlyFee, FirstHoursFlatFee, WeekendSurcharge): one class per algorithm. Each is small and can be unit-tested alone.
  • Context (ExitGate): holds a reference to a strategy and calls it. It owns everything else (ticket handling, receipts) and never asks which concrete strategy it holds.
  • Client: creates or looks up the strategy and gives it to the context, through the constructor, a setter, or a factory / registry.

How data reaches the strategy is a design choice. Here the context passes what is needed (feeRupees(stay)), so strategies stay stateless and shareable. The alternative is to pass the whole context (fee(gate)) and let the strategy pull what it wants, which is flexible but couples the strategy to the context's API.
