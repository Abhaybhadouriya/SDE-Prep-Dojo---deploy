Related and confused-with patterns

Compared withLooks similar becauseThe difference
StateContext delegates to an interface; the class diagram is the sameIn Strategy the client picks the algorithm and the strategies do not know each other. In State the state objects decide the next state as events arrive, and they know their neighbours. Ask: "does the delegate change because of what happened to the object?" If yes, State.
Template MethodBoth vary part of an algorithmTemplate Method varies steps by inheritance, fixed at compile time (one subclass per variant). Strategy varies the whole algorithm by composition, swappable at runtime.
CommandAn object that wraps behaviourCommand wraps what to do (a request, maybe queued, logged or undone). Strategy wraps how to do a job the context already decided to do.
BridgeObject holds a reference to an interfaceBridge splits two whole hierarchies (abstraction and implementation). Strategy is one context with pluggable algorithms.
DecoratorSwap or add behaviour without editing a classDecorator changes the skin (wraps the object, same interface outside). Strategy changes the guts (the object calls a pluggable part inside).
SpecificationPluggable rulesA Specification answers yes or no ("is this product eligible?") and combines with and/or/not. A Strategy computes a result.
