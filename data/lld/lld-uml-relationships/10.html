Remember this

  • Six lines: dependency (dashed, open arrow), association (solid), aggregation (hollow diamond), composition (filled diamond), generalization (solid, hollow triangle), realization (dashed, hollow triangle).
  • Diamonds sit at the whole. Triangles point at the more general type. Open arrows show who holds the reference.
  • Field test: a field means association or stronger; parameter-only means dependency.
  • Composition = the whole creates the part, owns it alone and it dies with the whole. Aggregation = the part has its own life and may be shared.
  • In C: composition = embedded struct, aggregation = pointer + refcount, realization = ops table, generalization = embedded base + container_of.
  • Say which requirement made you choose. Context, not the nouns, decides aggregation vs composition.
  • Many-to-many with data becomes its own class (Enrollment, Loan, Ticket).
