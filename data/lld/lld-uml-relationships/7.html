Choosing the right line: a five-question ladder

When you look at two classes in an interview, walk this ladder from the top. Stop at the first "yes".

The confusions interviewers probe

PairAsk yourselfExample
Association vs dependencyIs it a field (kept) or a parameter (borrowed)?Order.customer field = association; Invoice.print(Printer p) = dependency
Aggregation vs compositionCan the part exist alone, or be shared?Playlist ◇ Song (song shared); Building ◆ Floor
Aggregation vs associationIs it really "whole-part", or just "knows"?Department ◇ Professor vs Professor — Student
Generalization vs realizationIs the parent a class (with state) or a pure interface?extends AbstractVehicle vs implements Comparable
Generalization vs composition"Is a" or "has a"? Would every use of the parent accept the child?Stack is not a Vector; Car has an Engine

Context changes the answer. Is a Room composed in a Hotel? In a booking app, yes: rooms do not move between hotels. Is an Engine composed in a Car? For a car rental app, yes. For a scrapyard app that resells engines, no: aggregation. Always say which requirement made you choose. That sentence earns more credit than the diamond.
