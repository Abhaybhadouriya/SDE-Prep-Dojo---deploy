The six lines at a glance

Here is the whole vocabulary on one picture. In every row the left box is A and the right box is B. Read the line as a sentence from left to right.

Two tricks keep them straight:

  1. Solid vs dashed. A solid line means a lasting structural link: a field, or a parent class. A dashed line means something weaker: "uses for a moment" (dependency) or "promises to follow" (realization).
  2. Where is the shape? Diamonds sit at the container. Triangles sit at the more general type. Plain open arrows point the direction you can navigate (who holds the reference).
