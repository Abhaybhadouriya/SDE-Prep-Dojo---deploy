How SRP connects to the rest of SOLID

  • Open/Closed. Once tax lives in its own TaxPolicy, adding a new tax regime is a new class, not an edit. SRP finds the seam; OCP uses it.
  • Liskov. Small, focused classes have simple contracts, and simple contracts are easier for subtypes to honour (LSP).
  • Interface Segregation. SRP applied to interfaces: a fat interface serves many kinds of clients; split it per client.
  • Dependency Inversion. The extracted repository and notifier are injected as interfaces, which is DIP in action.
  • Patterns. Facade (the thin InvoiceService), Repository and Strategy (TaxPolicy) all fall out naturally from an SRP split.
