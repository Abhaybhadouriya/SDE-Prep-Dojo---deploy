Remember this

  • SRP: a class should have one reason to change, which means it answers to one actor (a team or role).
  • It is not "one method per class". Functions do one thing; classes serve one actor.
  • Smells: vague names, mixed imports, field clusters, divergent change, tests that need I/O mocks, descriptions with "and".
  • Fix with Extract Class; push I/O behind interfaces; keep a thin coordinator and a wiring-only main.
  • Over-splitting causes shotgun surgery and anemic models. Split along real actors, not guesses.
  • Kernel bridge: net_device_ops vs ethtool_ops, and protocol layering, are SRP by caller.
