Control coupling in a class diagram

Control coupling deserves its own picture, because it is the one you will write without noticing. A boolean or an int "mode" parameter means the caller is choosing a branch inside the callee. The caller now depends on the callee's internal structure, and every new mode edits the callee.

The fix on the right is the Strategy pattern. The cheapest fix, when there are only two fixed cases, is simply two methods: renderCsv(rows) and renderTable(rows). Pick the cheaper fix unless new formats are predicted.
