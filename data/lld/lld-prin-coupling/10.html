Remember this

  • Coupling = links between modules (keep low). Cohesion = how well parts of one module belong together (keep high).
  • Test: "what breaks if this changes?" counts coupling; "can I describe it without 'and'?" checks cohesion.
  • Coupling ladder, worst to best: content, common, external, control, stamp, data, message.
  • Cohesion ladder, worst to best: coincidental, logical, temporal, procedural, communicational, sequential, functional.
  • Common fixes: private fields + methods (content), inject immutable values (common), separate methods or Strategy (control), pass only what is used (stamp), split Utils by job (coincidental).
  • Point dependencies at stable abstractions; Ce, Ca and instability are the vocabulary.
  • Kernel: syscall ABI and ops tables are narrow stable seams; inside a subsystem coupling is tighter on purpose.
