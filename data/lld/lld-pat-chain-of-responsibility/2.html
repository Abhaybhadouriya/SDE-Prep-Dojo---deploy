Intent

Chain of Responsibility (GoF behavioral pattern): line up several handlers, each holding a link to the next, and give the request to the first one. Each handler either deals with it or passes it down the line. The sender talks only to the head of the line and never learns which handler, if any, took the request.

Three styles share the name, and saying which one you mean earns points:

StyleRuleExample
Classic (GoF)Exactly one handler handles it; the first one that can, stops the walkApproval levels: manager, then director, then VP; help desk escalation
Pipeline / filter chainEach handler does its part and passes it on, but any one may stop itHTTP middleware, servlet filters, netfilter hooks, the ATM dispenser
Pass-through to allEvery handler sees every request; none stops itA logger with console, file and pager handlers. Close to Observer.
