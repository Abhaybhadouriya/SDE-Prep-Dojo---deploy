Remember this

  • CoR = an ordered chain of handlers; each handles, passes on, or stops the request.
  • Three styles: classic (first able handler stops), pipeline (each does a part, any can stop), pass-through (all see it).
  • The sender knows only the head; order is configuration.
  • Always end with a terminal default; validate chains for cycles.
  • ATM with limited notes: greedy can fail; back off and plan-then-commit.
  • vs Decorator: can stop vs always forwards. vs Observer: ordered and stoppable vs broadcast.
  • Kernel: netfilter hooks with priorities and verdicts; notifier chains with NOTIFY_STOP.
