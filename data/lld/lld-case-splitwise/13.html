Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Runs and demos every P0 feature from a main/driver, with no manual setup.
  • Correct money: integer minor units, rounding rule stated, shares sum to the total.
  • No switch on split type in the service; a new split is a new class.
  • Validation before mutation; a bad request leaves no trace; custom exception with a clear message.
  • Clear ownership: sheet owns debts, strategy owns division, service owns orchestration and locking.
  • Readable names, small methods, no god class, no dead code, no public mutable fields.
  • Edge cases tested (the table above), not just the happy path.
  • Thread safety explained and at least the coarse version implemented.
  • Can explain extensions (groups, SHARE, notifications, DB) without rewriting.

These match the criteria in the Flipkart machine-coding guideline and the workat.tech round description: demoable code, functional correctness, modularity and extensibility, error handling (workattech/flipkart-machine-coding-round). More: what evaluators check.
