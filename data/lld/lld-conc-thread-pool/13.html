Remember this

  • Pool = tasks + bounded queue + workers + rejection policy. It is producer-consumer plus a lifecycle.
  • Submit order: core thread, then queue, then extra threads up to max, then reject. An unbounded queue means max is never used.
  • Avoid newFixedThreadPool (unbounded queue) and newCachedThreadPool (unbounded threads) in production. Build the executor yourself.
  • Policies: Abort (throw), CallerRuns (backpressure), Discard, DiscardOldest. Pick one on purpose.
  • Sizing: CPU-bound ≈ cores; I/O-bound ≈ cores × (1 + wait/compute); check with Little's law and the downstream limits.
  • Shutdown: shutdown → awaitTermination → shutdownNow, and restore the interrupt flag.
  • submit hides exceptions inside the Future. Always get() or log them.
  • Pass your own executor to CompletableFuture for blocking work. Never block on a Future from the same small pool.
