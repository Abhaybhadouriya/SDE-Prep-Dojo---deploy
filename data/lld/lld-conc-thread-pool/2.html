Anatomy: four parts of every pool

Every thread pool, in any language, has the same four parts. Here they are as a UML class diagram of the pool we will build. The Executor interface is the only thing callers see. Rejection is a Strategy: an interface with four small implementations you pick at construction time.

  1. Task a unit of work: Runnable (no result) or Callable<T> (returns a value, may throw).
  2. Queue a BlockingQueue that holds tasks waiting for a free thread. It is the producer-consumer buffer from Producer-Consumer & Blocking Queues.
  3. Workers threads that loop: take a task, run it, repeat. An idle worker sleeps inside take().
  4. Policy what happens when the queue is full and no more threads are allowed.
