How it works: computing tax for one cart

  1. The client creates the operation it wants, a TaxVisitor, and hands it to the cart. The client never asks "what kind of item is this?".
  2. The cart loops over its items and calls item.accept(tax). The cart only knows Item; the virtual call lands in Electronics.accept for the headphones.
  3. Electronics.accept calls v.visitElectronics(this). The method name already says "Electronics"; the virtual call on v picks the tax version.
  4. TaxVisitor.visitElectronics computes 2000 x 0.18 = 360 and returns it through accept back to the cart.
  5. The cart repeats the same two hops for each item and returns the total, 380. Swap in ShippingVisitor and the same loop returns shipping instead.
