Clarify the requirements

Spend 5 to 7 minutes here and write the answers in a comment at the top of your file. In this problem the questions are the design: each answer becomes a strategy or a test.

Functional (P0)

  1. Board A grid of rows x columns, given at start. Reject sizes below 1 x 1.
  2. Snake Starts with length 1 at a given cell (default (0,0)) moving in a given direction (default right).
  3. Move Each tick the head moves one cell in the current direction; the body follows.
  4. Turn The player can change direction. A 180-degree turn (right to left) is ignored when the snake is longer than 1.
  5. Food Exactly one food is on the board. Eating it grows the snake by 1 and adds 1 to the score. Then the next food appears, never on the snake.
  6. Game over When the head leaves the board (walls) or hits the body. After that, moves do nothing.
  7. Output Score, head and tail positions, and a printed board.

P1 (typical follow-ups, only after P0 runs)

  • Wrap-around boundary: leaving the right edge enters on the left.
  • Growth rule changes: grow every N moves even without food, or grow by 2 per food.
  • Random food instead of a fixed list; special food worth more points or with a timer.
  • Obstacles on the board; levels where the tick gets faster.
  • Real-time loop with keyboard input from another thread; multiplayer (two snakes).

Non-functional

  • O(1) per move regardless of snake length (the Zomato ask). Food placement may cost more, but say how much.
  • Testable: the game advances by an explicit tick(), not by a hidden timer, so tests are deterministic.
  • Extensible: boundary, food and growth rules change without editing Game.
  • Safe when input and ticks run on different threads.

Out of scope (say it)

Graphics and sound, persistence, high-score servers, networking for multiplayer. Rendering is a render() string; a UI would subscribe as a listener.
