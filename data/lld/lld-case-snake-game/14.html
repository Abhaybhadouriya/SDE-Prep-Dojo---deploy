Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Clarified boundary behaviour, food source, reverse turns and the tail rule before coding.
  • O(1) move with a stated reason (deque for order, set for membership), not "I used a list".
  • Correct tick order: next cell, wall, growth, self check with the tail exception, advance, food, win.
  • Runs: a driver or tests play real games, print head, tail and board.
  • Unit tests for the corner cases, not only the happy path.
  • Error handling: bad setup throws; ignored inputs return false; no move after the end.
  • Separation: rules, snake storage, time (loop) and output (listener, render) are separate.
  • Extensible: wrap-around, random food and growth rules are one class each.
  • Concurrency story: who writes what, which lock, why the buffer is bounded.

General machine-coding criteria (working demo, modularity, testability, error handling) are listed in workattech/flipkart-machine-coding-round; the round formats are compared in how LLD rounds work.
