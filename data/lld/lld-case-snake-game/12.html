Random food placement cost: while the board is mostly empty, a random cell is free with high probability, so a few tries are enough. When the snake fills most of the board, random tries keep missing, so after 8 misses we scan for free cells: O(rows x cols) but only near the end. If the interviewer wants O(1) placement always, keep a list of free cells plus a map from cell to index in that list; removing a cell swaps it with the last element (O(1)), and picking random food is free[rand(size)].
