Related and confused-with patterns

PatternQuestion it answersHow it differs
Specification"Does this item satisfy the rule?"Returns a boolean; composable with and/or/not
Strategy"How do I do this job?"One interchangeable algorithm that does work (price, rank, route). A spec only judges. A ranking Strategy often runs after a Specification filter.
Composite"Treat one and many the same"Specification is Composite applied to predicates.
Interpreter"Evaluate sentences of a small language"A spec tree is a tiny boolean-expression interpreter; Interpreter is the general form (arithmetic, parsing).
Chain of Responsibility"Who handles this request?"Ordered handlers with actions, stop on first handler. Specs have no order semantics and no actions.
Decorator"Add behaviour, same interface"NotSpec looks like a decorator (wraps one spec), but the point is boolean composition, not added behaviour.
Repository"Where are the items stored?"Pass a spec to repo.findAll(spec) instead of growing findByXAndY methods.
