Structure: leaves and combinators

  • Specification (Spec<T>): the interface. One question plus the combinator methods.
  • Leaf specifications (PriceAtMost, SeatsAtLeast, InCity): each tests one rule, holds its parameters, and is easy to unit-test.
  • Composite specifications (AndSpec, OrSpec, NotSpec): hold child specs and combine their answers.
  • Client (a search service, a repository, a validator): builds or receives a spec and calls isSatisfiedBy, or hands it to a store that can translate it.
