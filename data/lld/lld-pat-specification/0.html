The story: the toy-box sorting cards

Explain like I'm 5

You have a huge toy box. Mum gives you little cards. One card says RED. One says HAS WHEELS. One says SMALLER THAN MY HAND. You hold a toy up to a card and the card answers yes or no.

Then she shows you a trick. Clip two cards together with a paper clip that says AND: now the pair only says yes for red toys with wheels. Use a clip that says OR, and either card saying yes is enough. A NOT sticker flips the answer. With five cards and a few clips you can ask for "red cars, or anything small that is not a ball", without making a new card for every question.

Each card is a specification. The clips are composite specifications. A search screen with checkboxes, a coupon rule built by an admin, and find / -name '*.log' -size +1M are all the same idea.

From your world

You write specifications every week. A tcpdump filter such as tcp and (port 80 or port 443) and not host 10.0.0.5 is a tree of small predicates joined by and, or, not; libpcap compiles it into a BPF program that the kernel runs on each packet. An iptables rule with several -m matches is an AND of match modules, and ! negates one. find has -a, -o and !. In each case nobody writes a new filter function for every combination; they combine small tested pieces.
