  • Specification = a business rule as an object with isSatisfiedBy(candidate).
  • Leaves test one thing; And, Or, Not combine; any tree is itself a spec (Composite).
  • Building the tree and evaluating it are separate, so rules can be stored, printed, explained and sent to a database.
  • Stops the findByXAndYAndZ explosion: repo.findAll(spec).
  • Filter with specs, rank or price with a strategy.
  • At scale, push the tree down to the store (SQL, search index); short-circuit and order leaves cheap-first in memory.
  • Kernel bridge: tcpdump/BPF filters, iptables matches and find expressions are spec trees.
