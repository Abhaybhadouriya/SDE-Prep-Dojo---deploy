AI-assisted rounds and AI bans (2025-26)

Two opposite rules now exist. Know which one you are under before you start.

AI banned
  • Flipkart's posted 2026 guidelines: AI tools such as ChatGPT or Copilot strictly not allowed; internet only for syntax (post 7588244 (2026-02-18)).
  • A June 2026 Flipkart (Cleartrip) drive: AI prohibited, code explained on a common call (post 8331479 (2026-06-13)).
  • Rounds are screen-shared and recorded. Close assistants and disable IDE AI completion before you start.
AI allowed or graded
  • Razorpay Lead BE, 90 min: AI tools allowed; graded on AI usage, LLD logic and code quality (post 7575649 (2026-02-13)).
  • Razorpay SDE-2 online assessment: AI code assistant allowed for a git-like VCS (post 7273127 (2025-10-13)).
  • Microsoft SDE-2 LLD: the interviewer asked the candidate to use AI and watched the prompting (post 8462091 (2026-08-15)).
  • CRED "AI-native" onsite + 24-hour prototype with AI allowed (post 7463243 (2026-01-03)).

A playbook when AI is allowed

  1. Design first, without the tool. Say the entities, the interfaces and the hard part (the shared state). This is the part they grade as "LLD logic".
  2. Ask small. "Write a comparator that sorts by rating then name." "Generate 15 driver commands covering these edge cases." Not "build a search engine".
  3. Review out loud. Point out what you change: a God class split, a missing validation, a non-atomic check-then-act.
  4. Own the risky code. Locks, state transitions and money math you write and test yourself.
  5. Keep the checkpoint loop. AI makes large changes fast; run the driver after every accepted change. One 2026 candidate ran over time debugging a remove path (post 7575649 (2026-02-13)).

A good line to say: "I'll let the assistant draft the parser and the test data; the allocation logic and its locking I'll write myself, because that's where the bugs would hurt." It shows judgement, which is what these rounds are testing.
