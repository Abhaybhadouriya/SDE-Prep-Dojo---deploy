Related and confused-with patterns

Adapter, Facade, Decorator, Proxy and Bridge all "wrap something". Interviewers ask you to separate them by intent:

PatternInterface it exposesWhy it existsOne-liner
AdapterDifferent from the wrapped objectMake an existing thing fit"Changes the interface."
FacadeNew, simpler, over many objectsHide a complicated subsystem"Simplifies a subsystem."
DecoratorSame as the wrapped objectAdd behaviour, stackable"Same interface, more features."
ProxySame as the wrapped objectControl access (lazy, remote, cache, auth, rate limit)"Same interface, guarded."
BridgeAbstraction over a pluggable implementationLet two hierarchies vary independently"Designed up front; Adapter is retro-fitted."

Adapter is also often created by a factory ("give me the gateway for this provider") and then chosen like a Strategy. Those patterns combine well: the factory picks, the adapter translates, the strategy interface is the Target. The look-alike patterns page compares all of these side by side.
