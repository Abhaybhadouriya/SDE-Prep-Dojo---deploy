Structure: four participants

  • Component (Beverage): the interface clients use. Keep it small; every decorator must implement all of it.
  • ConcreteComponent (Espresso, Chai): the plain object being decorated. It knows nothing about decorators.
  • Decorator (AddOn): implements Component, holds a Component, and by default forwards every call. It exists so concrete decorators only override what they change.
  • ConcreteDecorators (Milk, ExtraShot, LargeSize): each adds one responsibility before or after forwarding.
