Intent

Decorator (GoF structural pattern, also called Wrapper): give one object extra responsibilities at run time by wrapping it, instead of creating a subclass for every combination of features.

Three key ideas. Same interface: the decorator implements the component's interface, so the client cannot tell a wrapped object from a plain one. Has-a, not just is-a: the decorator holds the object it wraps and forwards to it. Dynamic and stackable: wrapping happens at run time, one wrapper per feature, combined in any number and order. This is "favour composition over inheritance" in its purest form (see composition).
