Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. Each decorator must be safe on its own. Stateless wrappers are fine; stateful ones (counters, buffers, caches) need their own locking.
  • Testing. Test each decorator alone around a stub component, then one test for order where order matters (discount then tax is not tax then discount).
  • Cost. One indirect call and one object per layer. Deep stacks also make stack traces and debugging harder, which is the more practical cost.
