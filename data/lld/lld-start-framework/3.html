Step 1 - Clarify (5 min)

Prompts are deliberately vague: "Design a meeting room booking system." Amazon reports in our data say requirements are often withheld on purpose. Your first job is to turn one sentence into a spec you both agree on. Ask about these five areas, write the answers down, and read them back:

  1. Actors and core actions. Who uses it and what do they do? ("Employees book rooms; admins add rooms.")
  2. Rules. What is allowed and what is not? ("No overlapping bookings in a room; capacity must fit.")
  3. Choices that vary. Where could there be more than one way? ("How do we pick among free rooms?") These become your change points in step 5.
  4. Errors and edge cases. What happens when nothing fits, a booking is cancelled twice, an interval is invalid?
  5. Concurrency and scale. "Can two people book at the same moment?" (Almost always yes - say so.) "One office or many?"

Then write three short lists. For the meeting room problem:

P0 (must)P1 (if time)Out of scope
Add rooms (id, capacity, floor). Book a slot for N people; system picks a room. Cancel a booking. No overlaps. Safe under concurrent bookings.Room preference rules (same floor). Recurring meetings. List a user's bookings.Calendar invites, email, UI, persistence, time zones, payments.

Say the out-of-scope list out loud. It is the cheapest way to look senior: it shows you saw those features and chose not to build them, instead of forgetting them.
