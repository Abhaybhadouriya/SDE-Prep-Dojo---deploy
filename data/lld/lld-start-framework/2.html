Where these steps come from

Different courses slice the round differently, but they agree on the order. In our own words, this is how each maps onto the 8 steps:

SourceIts steps (paraphrased)Maps to our steps
ByteByteGo OOD book, free framework chapterGather requirements; find core objects (nouns, verbs, walk a use case); class diagram and code; deep dive1; 2; 3+6; 7+8
Hello Interview delivery frameworkRequirements (~5 min); entities and relationships (~3); class design starting from the orchestrator (~10-15); implement key methods (~10); extensibility (~5)1; 2; 3+4; 6; 7
AlgoMaster: answering an LLD problemClarify; entities; classes and relationships; interfaces and methods; a central coordinator; implement; concurrency; edge cases1; 2; 3; 4+5; 4; 6; 8; 6
workat.tech machine coding~15 min understand and clarify; ~90 min code; ~15 min demo while tests run1-5; 6; demo of 6-7

Two pieces of advice repeat across all of them, and they are worth stating up front. Start the class design from the orchestrator - the one class callers talk to - and work down to the entities it needs. And trace every field and method back to a requirement; anything you cannot trace is probably scope creep.
