Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. Changing a tree while another thread walks it is a race. Use one lock for the tree, immutable trees rebuilt on change, or an RCU-style scheme (the kernel's dentry cache is read under RCU).
  • Testing. Cover the leaf, the empty composite, deep nesting, and the invariants: no cycles, and a failed add leaves the tree unchanged.
  • Cost. A recursive size() is O(n) on every call. Cache the total in each composite and invalidate up the parent chain on change. Very deep trees risk stack overflow; walk them iteratively.
