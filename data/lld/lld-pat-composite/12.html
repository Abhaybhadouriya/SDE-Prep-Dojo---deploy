  • Composite = treat one item and a group of items the same way, through one Component interface.
  • Participants: Component, Leaf, Composite, Client. The Composite's children are typed as the Component, which makes the tree recursive.
  • Composite operations: act, call the same operation on each child, combine (sum, max, concat, all).
  • Safety (add/remove on the Composite only) is the usual choice; transparency puts them on the Component and leaves throw.
  • Guard the invariants: one parent per node, no cycles, detach before move; cache aggregates for big trees.
  • Composite combines many children; Decorator wraps one. Interpreter is a Composite with interpret().
  • Kernel bridge: the dentry tree, device tree, sysfs kobjects and cgroups are composites.
