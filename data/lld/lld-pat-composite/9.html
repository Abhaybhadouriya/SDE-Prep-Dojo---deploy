Related and confused-with patterns

PatternShapeHow it differs from Composite
DecoratorChain: each wrapper holds one componentSame "holds a Component and is a Component" trick, but the goal is adding behaviour, not grouping. Composite has many children and combines their results.
IteratorWalk a collectionOften used with Composite to walk the tree without exposing its internals (depth-first, breadth-first).
VisitorOperations moved out into a visitor classWhen you keep adding new operations to a stable tree (size, then search, then export), Visitor adds them without editing every node class.
InterpreterGrammar as a treeA Composite whose nodes are grammar rules and whose operation is interpret().
Chain of ResponsibilityLinked handlersA request can travel up a composite through parent pointers (an event bubbling up a widget tree is CoR on top of a Composite).
FlyweightShared small objectsLeaves that repeat (characters in a document tree) can be shared flyweights to save memory.
BuilderStep-by-step constructionA good way to build a complex composite tree (a nested menu, a document) without exposing the add calls everywhere.

One-liner for the most common confusion: Composite combines many children; Decorator wraps one. The look-alike patterns page puts these side by side.
