Remember this

  • Double booking is a check-then-act race. The fix is always: make "is it free? then take it" one atomic step.
  • Thread-safe collections make single calls safe, not sequences. Use putIfAbsent / replace(k, old, new) or a lock.
  • Pessimistic: lock first, others wait. Per-seat or per-show locks; striping bounds memory; sort ids for multi-seat.
  • Optimistic: read version, write only if unchanged, re-read on conflict. No locks held, no deadlock; bad under heavy contention.
  • Holds: FREE, HELD (until expiresAt), BOOKED. Check expiry lazily, re-check on confirm, make confirm idempotent, inject the clock.
  • Many servers: the database is the lock. Conditional UPDATE, version column, FOR UPDATE (NOWAIT / SKIP LOCKED), and a UNIQUE constraint as the net.
  • Never hold a lock (Java or row) across a payment call.
