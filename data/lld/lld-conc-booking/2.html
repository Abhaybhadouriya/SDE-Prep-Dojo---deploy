Two vocabulary words for the whole page:

  • Pessimistic locking: assume a conflict will happen. Lock the resource first, then read, check and write, then unlock. Others wait.
  • Optimistic locking: assume a conflict is rare. Read without locking, remember the version you saw, and when you write, write only if the version is unchanged. If it changed, you lost: re-read and retry, or tell the user.

Neither is "better". Pessimistic is simple and fair under heavy contention (a hot concert seat). Optimistic is faster when conflicts are rare (most seats in most shows) and never holds a lock while waiting, so it cannot deadlock.
