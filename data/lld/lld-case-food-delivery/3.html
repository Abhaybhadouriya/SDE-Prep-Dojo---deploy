Identify entities: nouns, verbs, and what varies

A reliable way to get classes from a statement: underline the nouns (candidate classes and fields), circle the verbs (methods), and box the phrases that say "based on a strategy", "different ways" or "later we may add" (interfaces). Step through it.

  1. Nouns become classes. Not every noun earns a class: "capacity" is just two integers on Restaurant, and "Customer" can be a plain id string until it needs behaviour.
  2. Verbs become methods, and each method goes to the class that owns the data it changes: tryReserve lives on Restaurant because Restaurant owns inUse (GRASP "Information Expert": put the method on the class that owns the data it needs).
  3. Phrases that admit several answers become interfaces. The order's life ("placed, dispatched, picked up") becomes a state machine.
  4. The bonus requirement "notify customers and partners on status change" becomes an Observer: the service does not know who listens.
