How it is asked

Two very different rounds share this problem. In India product companies (Flipkart above all) it is a machine-coding round: about 30 minutes of problem briefing, 90 minutes to write working in-memory code in your own IDE, and a later evaluation call where you run it and extend it. At Zomato and Salesforce it shows up as a whiteboard LLD discussion: entities, relationships, APIs, state transitions and concurrency, with patterns cross-questioned.
