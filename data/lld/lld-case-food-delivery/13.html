Evaluator rubric checklist

Compiled from the criteria stated in Flipkart machine-coding statements in our reports (for example the 2026-02 Flipkart round) and the candidates' write-ups above, in our words:

  • [ ] Runs and is functionally correct. Every P0 feature has a demo line in the driver. (Weighted highest.)
  • [ ] Entity modelling. Restaurant, Order, DeliveryPartner, OrderStatus are separate; no "god" class holding everything as maps of maps.
  • [ ] Separation of concerns. Selection rule, state rules, notification and dispatching each live in one place.
  • [ ] Extensibility. A new strategy is one new class; you can show it live.
  • [ ] Concurrency. Shared counters guarded; a race test or at least a clear explanation.
  • [ ] Exceptions. Named exception, clear messages, no stack trace in the demo for expected failures.
  • [ ] Readability. Short methods, honest names, a few comments on the "why".
  • [ ] Edge cases. Unknown ids, zero quantities, illegal transitions.
  • [ ] No DB, no UI, no frameworks. In-memory, standalone.
