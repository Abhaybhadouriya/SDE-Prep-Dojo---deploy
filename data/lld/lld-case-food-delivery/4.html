Two modelling choices that interviewers probe:

  • Why is the price stored on the Order? Because the menu can change after the order. Order.total is a snapshot, like an invoice. Re-reading the menu at delivery time would bill the customer a price they never saw.
  • Why is OrderStatus an enum with a transition table and not five State classes? Here each state has no behaviour of its own except "which next states are legal". An enum with canMoveTo() is the smallest correct State implementation. When states gain behaviour (a READY order auto-cancels after 30 minutes; a PICKED_UP order streams location), promote each to a class with the same interface. See State pattern: enum or classes.
