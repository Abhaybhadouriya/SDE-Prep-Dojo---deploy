Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. Copying a template while another thread edits it gives a torn copy. Freeze templates after registration (treat them as read-only), or lock around the copy.
  • Testing. Mutate the copy and assert the template did not change (the deep-copy test). Assert that intentionally shared immutable parts are the same object.
  • Cost. A deep copy is O(size of the object graph). Share the immutable parts and copy only the mutable ones, or use copy-on-write, to cut it.
