Three shapes of the same question. (1) "Implement one algorithm, cleanly" (Amazon). (2) "Make the algorithm pluggable and support per-API limits" (Microsoft). (3) "Make it thread-safe and talk about memory and the distributed version" (Microsoft, Walmart). Design for (2) from minute one and you can answer all three.
