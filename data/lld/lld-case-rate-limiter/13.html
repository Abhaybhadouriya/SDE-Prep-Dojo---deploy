The 90-minute machine-coding plan

For a 60-minute round, compress steps 3-6 and skip the leaky bucket and C-style extras. The order matters more than the exact minutes: something must run by minute 50.

MinutesDoOutput
0-8Clarify: client key, per-API and combined limits, bursts OK, hard vs soft, single processRequirement comment at the top of Main.java
