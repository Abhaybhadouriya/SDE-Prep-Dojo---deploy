The key testing idea: never sleep in a rate limiter test. A ManualClock makes every result exact and the suite runs in milliseconds. Tests that call Thread.sleep(1000) are slow and flaky on a loaded CI machine.

Floating point in buckets. Tokens are a double because refill is fractional (5 per second = 0.005 per ms). That is fine for a decision. If you need exact accounting, store tokens in a fixed-point integer such as "milli-tokens" or nanoseconds of credit, the way GCRA stores a single "theoretical arrival time".
