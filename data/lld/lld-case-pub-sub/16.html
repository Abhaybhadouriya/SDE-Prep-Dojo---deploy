  • Log + offsets: partitions are append-only, each group remembers "next offset" per partition.
  • Fan-out across groups, sharing inside a group; one worker per partition keeps per-key order.
  • Publish = append under a short per-partition lock + signal; never call consumers from it.
  • Commit after processing = at-least-once; consumers must be idempotent.
  • Failures: bounded retries with backoff, then a dead-letter topic with origin and reason.
  • Strategies for partitioning and retry, Observer for subscribers, Facade for the broker.
  • Condition variable waits in a loop with a timeout; stop() flips a flag, interrupts, joins.
