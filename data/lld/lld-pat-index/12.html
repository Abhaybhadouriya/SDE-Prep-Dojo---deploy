Remember this

  • A pattern = name + problem + solution shape + trade-offs. A recipe idea, not a library.
  • GoF: 23 patterns - 5 creational, 7 structural, 11 behavioral. Simple Factory is an idiom; refactoring.guru lists 22 (no Interpreter).
  • Core rules: program to an interface; favour composition; encapsulate what varies.
  • Choose by asking where the change lives: what is created, how a rule works, who reacts, the shape around objects, how a request travels.
  • Tier 1 for interviews: Strategy, Factory, Singleton, Observer, State, Builder, Decorator.
  • Every pattern in your design must point at a requirement. Two or three is normal.
  • You already know many from C: ops tables (Strategy), notifier chains (Observer), netfilter (Chain), TCP states (State).
