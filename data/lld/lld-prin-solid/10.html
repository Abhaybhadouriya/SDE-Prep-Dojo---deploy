Remember this

  • SOLID exists to make change cheap: touch as little working code as possible when a requirement arrives.
  • S: one reason (one actor) to change. Smell: a class you describe with "and".
  • O: add classes, do not edit working ones. Smell: growing if/else on type. Cure: interface + registry (Strategy).
  • L: a subtype keeps every promise of its parent. Smell: overrides that throw. Cure: narrower parent, split hierarchy.
  • I: small role interfaces. Smell: dummy methods. Kernel: net_device_ops vs ethtool_ops.
  • D: business logic depends on interfaces it owns; details are injected. Kernel: VFS and file_operations.
  • Apply SOLID where change is likely or testing needs a fake; skip it elsewhere (YAGNI). Finish the working demo first.
