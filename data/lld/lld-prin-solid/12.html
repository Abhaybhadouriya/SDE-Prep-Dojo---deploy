Resources

  • Wikipedia: SOLID doc - history and links to each principle's article.
  • Robert C. Martin: The Principles of OOD blog - the original index of the principles by their author.
  • The Single Responsibility Principle (Clean Coder blog, 2014) blog - "reason to change" explained as people/actors.
  • Liskov & Wing: A Behavioral Notion of Subtyping (1994) paper - the formal LSP, for the curious.
  • Martin Fowler: Inversion of Control Containers and the Dependency Injection pattern blog - DIP vs DI vs IoC.
  • Refactoring.Guru: Strategy doc - the pattern behind most OCP fixes (link only; our examples are our own).
  • kshitijmishra23/low-level-design-concepts repo - SOLID and pattern examples in Java.
  • LeetCode Discuss: SOLID principles using relatable real-life examples (2024-05-28) blog - a community write-up to compare with your own examples.
  • LWN: Object-oriented design patterns in the kernel, part 1 and part 2 blog - ops tables, the kernel's way of doing O, I and D.
  • Linux VFS overview doc - the file_operations contract used in the L and D bridges.
