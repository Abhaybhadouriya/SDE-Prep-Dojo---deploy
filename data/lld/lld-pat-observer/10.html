Related and confused-with patterns

Compared withLooks similar becauseThe difference
Publish-subscribe / event bus (case study)One-to-many notificationIn Observer the subject holds the observer list and calls them directly. In pub-sub a broker sits in the middle: publishers and subscribers know only topic names, never each other, and delivery may be async, durable and cross-process. Observer is the in-process building block; pub-sub is the system.
MediatorObjects coordinate through a middle objectObserver broadcasts and does not care what observers do. A Mediator contains coordination logic ("if the checkbox is ticked, enable the button") and knows the participants.
Chain of ResponsibilityA list of handlers is walkedIn CoR usually one handler takes the request and the walk stops. In Observer every observer is told. Notifier chains sit in between: everyone is told unless someone returns NOTIFY_STOP.
StrategyAn injected interfaceA strategy is one pluggable algorithm the context needs to do its job; observers are any number of parties that want to hear about its job.
CommandCallbacks as objectsA command is a request to do something; an event is a fact that something happened. Event names are past tense (StockChanged), command names imperative (ChangeStock).
