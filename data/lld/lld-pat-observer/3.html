Structure: a list and one callback interface

  • Subject (Inventory): keeps the list of observers, offers subscribe and unsubscribe, and calls notify after a change.
  • Observer (StockListener): the callback interface, usually one method.
  • Concrete Observers: each reacts in its own way and filters what it cares about.
  • Event (optional but common): an immutable record of what changed, so observers do not have to call back into the subject.

Most real systems push a small, immutable event and let the observer pull more if it needs it.
