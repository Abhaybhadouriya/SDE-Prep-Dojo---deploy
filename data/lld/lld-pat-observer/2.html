Intent

Observer (GoF behavioral pattern): let any number of objects sign up with one object to hear about its changes. When that object (the subject) changes, it calls each registered listener through one small interface, without knowing what any of them do with the news. Also known as publish-subscribe in its in-process form, and as listeners, callbacks or event handlers in most frameworks.

In plain words: flip the dependency. Instead of the subject calling five named services, each service registers itself with the subject through one small interface. The subject depends only on that interface and on a list.
