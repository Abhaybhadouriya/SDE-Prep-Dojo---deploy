How it works: one stock change, three reactions

  1. Each observer subscribes through the same interface. The subject stores them in a list and returns a handle (an AutoCloseable) that will unsubscribe.
  2. A restock arrives: setQty("PS5", 2). The subject updates its own state first, then builds an immutable event: PS5 went from 0 to 2.
  3. The subject walks a snapshot of the list. Both back-in-stock observers see "0 to non-zero" for their SKU and send an SMS.
  4. The low-stock alert fires. The analytics observer throws; the subject catches it per observer, logs it, and carries on, so one broken listener cannot starve the others.
  5. The back-in-stock observers are one-shot: inside their callback they closed their own handle. Because the list is copy-on-write, removing during the walk is safe. The next change notifies only the remaining listeners.
