Remember this

  • Same shape, different intent: name patterns by the problem they solve, never by the diagram.
  • Three questions: what problem, who chooses the delegate and when, what happens to the call.
  • Strategy vs State: client chooses vs states choose. Strategy vs Template Method: composition vs inheritance.
  • Decorator adds and always forwards; Proxy controls access; Adapter converts one interface; Facade simplifies many.
  • Adapter is retro-fitted; Bridge is designed up front. Composite has many children; Decorator has one.
  • CoR links may stop the request; Decorator links never do. Command is "what to do"; Strategy is "how".
  • Observer: subject knows listeners. Pub-Sub: broker in the middle. Mediator: central rules.
  • Undo: Memento stores state, Command stores reversible operations.
