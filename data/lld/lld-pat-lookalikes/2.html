The one rule: classify by intent, then by who decides

When two patterns share a structure, ask in this order: (1) What problem is being solved? (2) Who chooses the delegate, and when? (3) What happens to the call: always forwarded, maybe stopped, converted, or broadcast?

Those three questions settle almost every pair on this page. The GoF book itself groups patterns by purpose (creational, structural, behavioural), not by shape, for this reason.
