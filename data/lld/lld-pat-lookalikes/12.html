In interviews

Comparison questions come in two forms: "X vs Y?" and "which pattern is this?" while reviewing your own design. Answer both with the same three-beat structure: shared shape, different intent, one concrete example each. Pairs that come up naturally in our case studies:

  • Vending machine, elevator, ATM: State, and why it is not Strategy.
  • Parking lot, ride hailing: pricing Strategy next to a ride or ticket State machine.
  • Text editor: Command vs Memento for undo.
  • Logger: Chain of Responsibility (level filtering) vs Decorator (formatters).
  • Pub-sub and notification service: Observer vs Pub-Sub.
  • File system: Composite (directories) vs Decorator (encrypted or compressed files).

Strong answer template: "Both have [shape]. The difference is intent: [A] exists to [problem A], [B] to [problem B]. The quick test is [question]. For example [A example] versus [B example]." Fill it for Strategy vs State: "Both delegate to an interface. Strategy lets the client choose an algorithm; State lets the object change its behaviour as its mode changes. Test: who replaces the delegate. Comparator versus the TCP state machine."
