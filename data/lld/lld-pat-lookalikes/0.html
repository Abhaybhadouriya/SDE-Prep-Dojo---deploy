The story: three people holding your phone

Explain like I'm 5

Three people are each holding your phone for you.

The first one put a sparkly case on it. It is still your phone, it does everything it did before, plus it looks nicer. You could add a second case on top.

The second one is your parent. You ask for the phone, and they decide: "Not at dinner time", or "Okay, but only for ten minutes". Same phone, but someone now controls when you can use it.

The third one has a travel plug. Your charger has a round pin and the wall socket is square, so the plug sits between them and makes them fit.

From far away all three look the same: a person in the middle, holding your phone. Up close, they want completely different things: add something, control access, make it fit.

Those are Decorator, Proxy and Adapter. On a UML diagram they are almost the same three boxes. That is why "what is the difference between X and Y?" is one of the most common pattern questions in interviews: it checks whether you learned shapes or learned reasons.

From your world

In C every one of these patterns is "a struct with a pointer to an ops table". file_operations, tcp_congestion_ops, a netfilter hook array and a notifier chain all look alike in a header file. You already tell them apart by asking who chooses the ops table, when, and what happens to the call. That is exactly the skill this page trains.
