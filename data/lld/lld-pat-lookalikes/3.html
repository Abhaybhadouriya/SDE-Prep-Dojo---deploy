The master table: 12 pairs at a glance

#PairSame shapeThe question that separates them
1Strategy vs StateContext delegates to an interfaceWho picks the next delegate: the client (Strategy) or the current state (State)?
2Strategy vs Template MethodA varying step in an algorithmIs the step plugged in by composition at runtime (Strategy) or overridden by a subclass (Template Method)?
3Decorator vs ProxyWrapper with the same interfaceDoes it add behaviour and stack (Decorator) or control access to one real object (Proxy)?
4Adapter vs FacadeWrapper over other codeConvert one interface into an expected one (Adapter) or simplify a whole subsystem (Facade)?
5Adapter vs BridgeIndirection to an implementationRetro-fitted after the fact (Adapter) or designed up front so two sides vary (Bridge)?
6Factory Method vs Abstract FactoryCreation hidden behind a callOne product chosen by a subclass (Factory Method) or a family of products from one factory object (Abstract Factory)?
7Observer vs Pub-SubOne event, many receiversDoes the subject know its observers (Observer) or does a broker sit between (Pub-Sub)?
8Observer vs MediatorObjects coordinateBroadcast with no logic (Observer) or a central object that decides who does what (Mediator)?
9Command vs StrategyAn object that wraps behaviourIs it what to do, maybe later or undone (Command), or how to do one job (Strategy)?
10Composite vs DecoratorRecursive compositionMany children forming a tree (Composite) or exactly one wrapped child (Decorator)?
11Chain of Responsibility vs DecoratorA chain of linked handlersMay a link stop the request (CoR), or does every link forward it (Decorator)?
12Memento vs Command (undo)Both support undoStore a snapshot of state (Memento) or store an operation that knows how to reverse itself (Command)?
