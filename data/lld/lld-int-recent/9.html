  • Parking lot, notification system, ride hailing, pub-sub and Splitwise are the top five in 2024-26. Build each one end to end at least once.
  • More than half of the reported rounds expect code that runs. Practise in an IDE with a timer, not only on paper.
  • The top problems share two hooks: a contested resource (lock story) and several kinds of something (interface story). Prepare both for every problem.
  • Concurrency moved from "bonus" to "core requirement" in several 2026 prompts.
  • AI rules differ: some 2026 rounds require AI and grade how you use it; Flipkart's posted 2026 guidelines ban it. Ask.
  • The tail is long (most problems appear once). A repeatable method beats memorised answers.
  • Counts are self-reports and lean to Indian product companies and SDE-2. Use them to order practice, not to predict.
