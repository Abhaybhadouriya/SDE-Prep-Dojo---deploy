How this list was built

  1. Collect. We searched LeetCode Discuss through its public GraphQL API for interview-experience posts that mention LLD, low level design, machine coding, OOD (object-oriented design) or code design, posted from 2024-06 to 2026-11.
  2. Extract. From each post we took only the LLD or machine-coding round: company, level, round format, length, platform, the problem, follow-ups and the outcome the poster stated. One post can hold more than one round.
  3. Normalise. Different wordings of the same task were merged under one canonical name ("Design Uber", "cab booking", "premium cab allocation" all count as Ride hailing).
  4. Verify. Every post id was re-checked on 2026-11-23 and still resolves. The outcome column was re-checked on 2026-11-24 against the post text; outcomes a post does not state are shown as "not stated".
  5. Rewrite. Requirement lines are our own short summaries, never copied text. Author names are left out on purpose.

Read the numbers honestly. These are self-reports from people who chose to post, so they lean towards Indian product companies and towards SDE-2 (people post more after bigger loops). Outcomes are what the poster said: 200 of 311 did not say. A problem with 1 report is not "rare at that company" - it may just be rarely posted. Use counts to order your practice, not to predict your exact question.

Where a post gave no round length, the data file fills in the company's standard slot (60 minutes, or 90 for Flipkart and PhonePe machine coding). Round formats are explained in How LLD rounds work.
