Core flow: assign, wait, resolve, pull next

  1. The driver asks the facade to assign issue I2 (a GOLD issue).
  2. The service takes its lock, checks I2 is still OPEN (so two callers cannot assign it twice) and collects the agents whose expertise includes GOLD. Only a1 qualifies.
  3. It hands the issue and the experts to the strategy. The service never decides itself. FreeFirst finds no free expert and returns the busy one with the shortest waitlist: a1.
  4. Because a1 is busy, I2 moves to WAITING (the state machine checks OPEN -> WAITING is legal) and joins the back of a1's waitlist.
  5. Later the driver resolves I1, a1's current issue. The service checks that I1 really is a1's current issue, then moves it to RESOLVED.
  6. The agent records I1 in its history and pops the next id from its waitlist: I2.
  7. The service moves I2 to IN_PROGRESS. All of this happened under one lock, so nobody saw a1 with two issues or with none while I2 waited.

Why the strategy gets only the experts

The service filters by expertise before calling the strategy, so every strategy gets a list where any answer is valid. A strategy can then never assign a GOLD issue to a payments-only agent by mistake, and a new strategy only has to answer "which of these?". If an interviewer asks for a strategy that also considers non-experts (for example "overflow to a generalist after 10 minutes"), that is a different rule and belongs in a separate eligibility step, a small chain of filters before the pick.
