Common mistakes

  1. isBooked on Seat Breaks with two shows on one screen.
  2. Checking all seats, then booking each in a separate step Classic check-then-act: someone grabs a seat in between.
  3. Holding a lock while waiting for payment One slow payment freezes the whole show.
  4. No expiry on holds Abandoned checkouts block seats forever; the show looks sold out while it is half empty.
  5. Relying only on a cleanup thread for expiry If it is late, seats stay blocked; use lazy expiry.
  6. Partial holds by accident Taking A1, failing on A2 and leaving A1 held.
  7. "synchronized on the whole service" Correct but serialises every show in the city; say why per-show is enough.
  8. Charging after the hold expired Check before charging; refund path if it still happens.
  9. No state machine Cancelling a pending booking or paying twice silently corrupts state.
