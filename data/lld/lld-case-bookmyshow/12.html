In a 45 to 60-minute whiteboard round (Amazon, Salesforce, Zepto), spend 8 minutes on requirements, 10 on the class diagram, 10 on the hold/pay sequence, and keep at least 15 minutes for the concurrency discussion: that is where this problem is graded. Code hold() and confirm() if asked for code; they are the 40 lines that matter. See the machine-coding round guide.
