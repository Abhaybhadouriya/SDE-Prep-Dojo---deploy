Clarify requirements

Functional (P0)

  1. Catalogue Cities have theatres; theatres have screens; a screen has seats of a type (regular, premium, recliner). A show is a movie on a screen at a time.
  2. Browse List shows for a movie in a city; list available seats for a show.
  3. Hold A user picks one or more seats; the system holds all of them for 5 minutes or none of them, and quotes a price.
  4. Pay and confirm If payment succeeds while the hold is valid, the booking is confirmed and the seats are sold.
  5. Expire A hold that is not paid in time frees its seats automatically.
  6. Fail and cancel A declined payment frees the seats; a confirmed booking can be cancelled, freeing the seats.

Functional (P1)

  • Pricing that varies: by seat type (P0), plus demand-based surcharge, weekend or morning-show prices.
  • Several payment methods (UPI, card, wallet).
  • Notifications on confirmation and cancellation.

Non-functional

  • No double booking, ever. This beats everything else, including latency.
  • High contention on hot shows: a first-day first show can have thousands of users on the same 300 seats.
  • No charge without a seat: never take money for an expired hold.
  • Testable expiry: time comes from an injected clock, so a test can say "six minutes later".

Out of scope

  • Real payment gateways, refunds processing, user accounts and login, recommendations, reviews, food add-ons, distributed deployment (discussed, not built).
