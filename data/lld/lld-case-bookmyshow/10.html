Extensibility: the follow-ups

Follow-upHow it plugs in
Demand pricing, weekend pricing, convenience feeMore PricingStrategy decorators (demand is in the code)
Wallet, net banking, gift cardsMore PaymentStrategy classes; split payments as a composite strategy
Notify on confirm / cancel / show changeBookingListener implementations; move the calls onto a queue for production
Never "charged but no seats"Split PaymentStrategy.pay() into authorize(), capture() and voidAuth(). BookingService.pay() becomes: authorize, then inventory.confirm() under the show lock, then capture; if confirm fails, void the authorization (no refund needed). The outbox refund stays as the last line of defence
Extend a hold ("2 more minutes")extend(hold) on the inventory: under the lock, if still held by this hold, write a new expiry; cap the number of extensions
Waitlist for a sold-out showA per-show FIFO of user ids; on release or cancel, notify the head (Observer) and give them a short priority hold
Auto-assign "best available" seatsA SeatSelectionStrategy (centre-most contiguous block) feeding seat ids into the same hold()
Concerts with general admission (no seat numbers)A counting inventory: an atomic counter per category plus holds with TTL, same state machine
Multiple platforms selling the same seats (CRED follow-up)The seat owner (cinema or airline) is the single source of truth; platforms call its hold API and receive a lease, and nobody keeps a private copy of availability
PersistenceA JdbcSeatInventory implementing the same interface with the SQL above; the service does not change
