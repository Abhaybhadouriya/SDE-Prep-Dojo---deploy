Core flows

Hold, then pay

Never hold a lock across the payment call. A payment can take tens of seconds. The hold (a timestamped state on the seat) is what reserves the seat during payment, not a mutex. The mutex is only held for the microseconds it takes to check and write the seat states.

The life of one seat and one booking (step through it)

  1. start() holds the seats: every requested seat moves Free to Held with the same hold id and expiry. The booking is PENDING.
  2. Payment succeeds before the expiry: confirm() checks every seat is still held by this hold, then moves them to Booked. The booking is CONFIRMED.
  3. The user leaves or the card is declined: release() frees the seats now (FAILED); or nobody does anything and the TTL passes, after which the seats simply count as free (EXPIRED).
  4. A confirmed booking can be cancelled: seats go back to Free. Any other move (cancel a PENDING one, pay a CONFIRMED one) throws.
