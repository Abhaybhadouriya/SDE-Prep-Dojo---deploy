Identify the entities

  1. Nouns from the statement: candidates for classes.
  2. Verbs: candidates for methods. hold, confirm and release are the heart of the problem.
  3. The key modelling insight: seat A1 exists once physically but is sold separately for every show. Booking state is keyed by (show, seat), never stored on Seat.
  4. The final split: a read-mostly catalogue, and a small, heavily contended inventory behind one interface with two implementations.

The most common modelling bug: putting isBooked on Seat. The moment there are two shows on the same screen, booking A1 for the 6 pm show would book it for 9 pm too. Interviewers watch for this.
