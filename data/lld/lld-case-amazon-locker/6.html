Allocation, expiry and the race (step through it)

The same station as the demo: 2 small, 2 medium, 1 large. Watch the free lists, which are the only thing the strategy reads.

  1. Construction builds one deque of free slots per size. Finding a slot is "first non-empty list at or above my size": at most three checks.
  2. Exact size first. No scan over slots, no sorting: free.get(SMALL).pollFirst().
  3. When a size is empty, best fit moves up one class, exactly like kmalloc falling to a larger cache would waste space but succeed. A large parcel never moves down.
  4. Pickup frees the slot onto the tail (FIFO reuse spreads wear across doors). Expiry changes only the delivery's state: the parcel is still physically inside.
  5. Collection is the only path that frees an expired slot. Codes for those parcels are removed at the same time, under the same lock.
  6. The race the interviewer will ask about. "Check free count, then pop" is a check-then-act; it must be one critical section. Our station methods are synchronized, so it is.
