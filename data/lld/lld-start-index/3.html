From prompt to code: the whole round in one picture

Every LLD answer, whatever the problem, walks the same path. Click through it once now; the framework page gives each step a minute budget.

  1. Requirements. Turn one vague sentence into a short P0/P1 list, plus what is out of scope, plus the concurrency question.
  2. Entities. Nouns in the requirements become candidate classes; verbs become methods.
  3. Change points. Ask "what will vary?" and put an interface there. That is where patterns come from - not from a list.
  4. Code. Write the classes plus a small main() that demos the flow. In a machine-coding round this must run.
  5. Extensibility check. The interviewer adds a requirement; a good design absorbs it by adding code, not editing old code.
