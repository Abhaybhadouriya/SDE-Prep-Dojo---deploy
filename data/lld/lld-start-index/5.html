How every page climbs from Level 1 to Level 5

Each page starts with a story a child could follow and climbs to its own badge level. You can stop at any divider and still have learned something whole.

LevelWhat you get on an LLD pageExample (Strategy pattern)
L1 KidA story, no jargon"A phone with swappable camera lenses."
L2 BeginnerReal names, a class diagram, small codeInterface PricingStrategy with two classes.
L3 IntermediateBefore/after code, trade-offs, where it fitsReplacing an if/else chain; when an enum map is enough.
L4 AdvancedConcurrency, lifecycle, look-alikes, real librariesStrategy vs State vs Bridge; Comparator in the JDK.
L5 InterviewQuestions with "how to answer" and follow-ups, quizzes, scenarios"Add surge pricing to your ride app without touching old code."
