Reading paths: SDE-1 vs SDE-2

The two levels are asked the same problems; the difference is depth. In the 311 recent LLD round reports we collected from LeetCode Discuss (June 2024 to November 2026), 224 were for SDE-2, 41 for SDE-1 and 42 for SDE-3 or senior roles, and 4 did not state a level (full data). So SDE-2 expectations are the ones most written about.

SDE-1 path (about 13 hours of reading)
  • Rounds, framework
  • OOP: from C, polymorphism, interfaces, composition
  • SOLID at a glance, class diagrams, relationships
  • Patterns: overview, Strategy, Observer, Factory, Singleton, State
  • Thread-safety basics
  • Cases: Tic-Tac-Toe, Snake & Ladder, Parking Lot, Library, Vending Machine, LRU Cache
SDE-2 adds (about 18 more hours)
  • All five SOLID pages and coupling & cohesion
  • Sequence and state diagrams, API design
  • Decorator, Command, Chain of Responsibility, Builder, Adapter, look-alikes
  • Concurrency: locks, producer-consumer, double booking, thread-safe LRU / rate limiter
  • Cases: BookMyShow, Splitwise, Ride hailing, Pub-sub, Notifications, Rate limiter, Elevator
  • Machine-coding guide, mistakes, mocks

The hour estimates add up the reading times shown in the sidebar for those pages; practice time is extra. If you have only one week, use the crash variant in the 4-week study plan.
