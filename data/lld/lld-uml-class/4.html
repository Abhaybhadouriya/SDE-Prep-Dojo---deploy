Multiplicity: how many?

Multiplicity says how many objects can sit at one end of a relationship (or in one attribute). It is written as a range lower..upper.

WriteMeansCode shape
1exactly onerequired field, set in constructor, never null
0..1zero or onenullable field or Optional
* (same as 0..*)any numbercollection, may be empty
1..*at least onecollection, non-empty (check it)
2..4a specific rangecollection + size check (e.g. players in a game)

Multiplicities are cheap to write and they turn into checks: a Level constructor that rejects an empty spot list is the code form of 1..*. When an interviewer asks "can a spot hold two bikes?", the answer is already on your diagram.
