Types can be primitives (int, Boolean), other classes (Vehicle), enums, or collections. For collections, UML prefers a multiplicity on a single type (spots: ParkingSpot [1..*]) over a language type (List<ParkingSpot>), but in an interview either is understood. Pick one style and keep it.

Whiteboard shortcuts: you cannot write italics or underlines with a marker. Write {abstract} next to the name, and {static} or $ before a member. Say what you mean once; nobody will mark you down for a shortcut.
