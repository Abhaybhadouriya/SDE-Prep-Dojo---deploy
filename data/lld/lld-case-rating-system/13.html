Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Correct averages: sum and count stored, no integer division, rounding stated and tested.
  • Deterministic order with an explicit tie-break, and the tie-break is a swappable policy.
  • Each stage lands without rewriting the earlier ones; earlier tests still pass.
  • Tests written early and grouped by behaviour, not one giant main.
  • Validation at the boundary with a clear exception.
  • Separation: ranking, bucketing and formatting live in different places.
  • Thread safety at least explained (atomic per-key update), ideally implemented.
  • Communication: asked before assuming, named trade-offs (sort on read vs maintain a sorted structure).
