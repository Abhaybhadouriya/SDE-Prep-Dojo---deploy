Two styles of company. Product companies with a "Java + LLD" or C++ round (Walmart, Adobe, Goldman Sachs, Mastercard, Sprinklr) ask direct concept questions and short code: singleton, abstract vs interface, diamond, virtual destructor. Machine-coding companies (Flipkart, PhonePe, Meesho, Cleartrip) rarely ask definitions; they grade OOP inside your code: entity modelling, separation of concerns, interfaces at variation points, exception handling. A Meesho candidate reported being judged on completeness first, then OOP (LeetCode Discuss, 2025-04-17). Prepare both.

Amazon, Microsoft and Walmart appear in the tags below because their LLD/OOD rounds start from these fundamentals (for example Microsoft's "What's OOP and SOLID?" opener, LeetCode Discuss, 2024-09-12). Amazon candidates ask whether the LLD round wants UML or code (LeetCode Discuss, 2026-07-07); prepare to show classes plus key methods in real code.
