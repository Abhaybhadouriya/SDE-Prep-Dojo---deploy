Identify the entities (nouns and verbs)

Underline nouns (candidate classes) and verbs (candidate methods), then drop nouns that are only attributes. This is the method from From use cases to classes, applied step by step.

  1. Start from the requirement sentence. Read it twice.
  2. Underline nouns. Each is a candidate class: User, Product, Cart, Coupon, Rule, price, stock, Order.
  3. Underline verbs. They become methods on the facade, and they tell you who owns which behaviour.
  4. Drop nouns that are only data: "price" is a field of Product. "User" needs no class in P0; a user id keys the cart.
  5. "Coupon" splits in two, because two things vary independently: when it applies (a Rule) and how much it takes off (a DiscountStrategy). The price becomes an immutable Bill built in layers.

The key modelling choice: a coupon is a rule plus a strategy. "10% off up to 500 on carts over 1000 until 30 Nov" is minSubtotal(1000).and(validUntil(30 Nov)) + PercentOff(10%, cap 500). Any combination is a new line of configuration, not a new class. Twelve coupon variants do not need twelve subclasses.
