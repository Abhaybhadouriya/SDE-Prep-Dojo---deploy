Common mistakes

  • double for prices, then a total like 539.1999 in a hidden test.
  • A switch on coupon type inside applyCoupon, or one subclass per rule-and-type combination.
  • Coupon order depends on the order codes were typed, so the same cart gives two totals.
  • Validating only at apply time, so a coupon stays valid after the cart changes or the date passes.
  • Total going negative when a flat coupon is larger than what is left.
  • Decrementing stock line by line and failing on the third line with the first two already taken.
  • Reserving stock on add-to-cart with no expiry, so abandoned carts lock the shelf.
  • Building the rule framework first and running out of time before checkout works.
