  1. Read locks are slower than write locks
  2. HashMap does not support read locks
  3. get() moves the node to the front, so concurrent gets corrupt the list

In a token bucket, why must refill, check and take happen in one critical section?

tokens and lastRefill form one invariant; splitting the step lets the limit be exceeded.
