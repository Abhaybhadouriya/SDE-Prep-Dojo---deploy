Remember this

  • Recipe: shared state, invariants, compound actions, granularity, ready-made blocks, a stress test that checks invariants.
  • LRU get is a write. A read lock on get corrupts the list. One lock is the baseline; shards or buffered reads scale it.
  • Thread-safe parts composed together are not thread-safe. Make the whole operation atomic.
  • Token bucket: per-key bucket via computeIfAbsent, refill + check + take in one critical section, lazy refill, injected monotonic clock.
  • count++ loses updates. AtomicLong for exact values, LongAdder for hot stats (like percpu_counter), a lock when part of an invariant.
  • Sliding-window counter: a ring of per-second buckets, reset on reuse, sum the fresh ones.
  • Start coarse, measure, then go finer. Fine-grained brings approximate semantics and lock-order rules.
