The 7-day crash plan

Interview in a week? Cut theory to what is asked, and spend most time on cases. This plan needs about 3 to 4 hours a day. You will skip most UML pages and half the patterns; that is acceptable for SDE-1 and risky but survivable for SDE-2.

DayDo
1Rounds, Framework, From C Structs to Objects, Polymorphism, Interfaces, Composition. Build: Tic-Tac-Toe.
2SOLID, Class Diagrams, Relationships, then the eight core patterns: Strategy, Observer, State, Factory, Singleton, Builder, Decorator, Chain of Responsibility.
3Thread Safety Basics, Locks, Double Booking. Build: Parking Lot (timed).
4Build: LRU Cache, Movie Booking.
5Build: Splitwise, Notification Service, Rate Limiter.
6Rounds by Company, Common Mistakes. Mock 1 on your company's most-reported problem.
7Cheat Sheet, Question Bank (skim). Mock 2 on a new case. Early night.

Interviewers love a candidate who finishes a smaller design that runs over one who draws a perfect diagram and has no code. In a crash week, practise finishing: P0 features working end to end, then extensions.
