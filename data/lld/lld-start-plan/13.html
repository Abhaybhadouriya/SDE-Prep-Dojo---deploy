Three roadmap talks from popular LLD channels; useful to compare with this plan.

  • awesome-low-level-design repo - a large free list of concepts, patterns and problems with solutions in several languages.
  • AlgoMaster: LLD course roadmap course - a free curriculum in a similar order to ours.
  • workat.tech machine coding practice course - timed machine-coding problems in the Flipkart round format.
  • Flipkart machine coding round notes repo - what the 90-minute round expects, from the workat.tech team.
  • Hello Interview: LLD delivery framework doc - a free guide to pacing an LLD interview.
  • ByteByteGo: A framework for the OOD interview book - the free chapter of the Object-Oriented Design Interview book.
  • refactoring.guru pattern catalog doc - illustrated explanations of every classic pattern; good second reading for week 2.
  • LeetCode Discuss: frequently asked LLD questions blog - a community-curated problem list to cross-check against week 3.
