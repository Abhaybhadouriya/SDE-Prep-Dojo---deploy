Why these ten cases in week 3? We collected 311 self-reported LLD and machine-coding rounds from LeetCode Discuss (2024 to 2026, details on Recently Asked LLD Questions). Parking lot, notification system, ride hailing, pub-sub, Splitwise, LRU cache and movie booking were the most reported problems, in that order. Elevator and Snake & Ladder are added because they are the cleanest practice for the State pattern and for game loops.
