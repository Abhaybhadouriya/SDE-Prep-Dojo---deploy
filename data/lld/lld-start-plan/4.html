Week 2: design patterns (days 8-14)

Goal: hear a requirement and name the pattern within seconds. "Pricing rules change per city" means Strategy. "Tell all subscribers when the price drops" means Observer. "The vending machine behaves differently when it has no coins" means State. Learn the problem each pattern solves first; the class diagram follows from it.

From your world

Day 14's kernel patterns page is your secret weapon in interviews. "Strategy is like tcp_congestion_ops: cubic and BBR plug into the same slots", "Observer is the netdevice notifier chain", "Chain of Responsibility is the netfilter hook list" - one such line shows the interviewer you have used the idea in production code, not only read about it.

Do not try to memorise all 23 classic patterns equally. The case studies in this module, like the problems in our interview-report set, keep reaching for about eight: Strategy, Observer, State, Factory, Singleton, Builder, Decorator and Chain of Responsibility. Give those two builds each; skim the rest.
