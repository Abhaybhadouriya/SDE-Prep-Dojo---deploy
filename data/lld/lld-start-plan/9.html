Checking you are ready

A plan is only as good as the check at the end of each week. Use this rubric on your Sunday review build. Score each row 0 (missing), 1 (partly) or 2 (solid). It mirrors what evaluators write in machine-coding feedback, covered on What Evaluators Check.

CheckWhat "2" looks like
RequirementsP0/P1 list written before code; at least two clarifying questions asked
ModellingEntities, enums and interfaces named well; no god class; relationships correct
PatternsEach pattern solves a stated requirement; none added "for show"
Working codeCompiles, driver runs P0 flows, handles 2+ edge cases with clear errors
ExtensibilityA new type or rule needs one new class, not edits across files
ConcurrencyShared state identified; a correct, explained locking choice
CommunicationExplains trade-offs while coding; finishes on time

Fall behind? Do not shift everything by a day. Drop the lowest-value items first: Interpreter, Flyweight, Memento, Visitor, the use-case and activity diagrams, and the second half of the question bank. Never drop the week-3 builds or the week-4 mocks.

After the four weeks

Keep a maintenance rhythm until the interview: one new case from the Case Study Index every two days (timed), and one page of the Question Bank a day. If you are also preparing system design, alternate LLD days with the HLD study plan; the two share requirements gathering and API design.
