Remember this

  • Four weeks, four goals: model it (OOP, SOLID, UML), know the tools (patterns), build it safely (concurrency + 10 cases), do it on a clock (mocks).
  • Every day is read, build, recall, log. The build is the part that counts.
  • Practise about eight patterns deeply; know the others by purpose.
  • The ten core cases come from what candidates actually reported being asked: Parking Lot, Notification, Ride Hailing, Pub-Sub, Splitwise, LRU, BookMyShow, Rate Limiter, Elevator, Snake & Ladder.
  • A mock is a mock only with a real timer, no peeking and talking out loud.
  • Crash week: 25% theory, 50% cases, 25% mocks. Finish P0 end to end before extras.
  • Your kernel background maps straight onto vtables, ownership, refcounts and locking; say those bridges out loud in interviews.
