  • JLS chapter 17: Threads and Locks doc - happens-before (17.4.5) and final field semantics (17.5).
  • Wikipedia: Double-checked locking doc - the bug and fixes across Java, C++ and other languages.
  • cppreference: std::call_once doc - one-time init in C++.
  • CopyOnWriteArrayList (Java 21 API) doc - snapshot iterators and when the copying cost is worth it.
  • Linux kernel: What is RCU? doc - the kernel's copy-on-write with grace periods; the bridge from your world.
  • Refactoring.Guru: Singleton doc - an illustrated overview of the pattern itself (read it there; we do not copy it).
  • Java Concurrency in Practice (book site) book - chapter 3 (sharing objects, safe publication) and the immutability rules. Buy or borrow it.
  • iluwatar/java-design-patterns repo - MIT-licensed examples, including a double-checked-locking pattern folder.
