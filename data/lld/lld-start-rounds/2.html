What 311 recent reports say

We collected 311 distinct, self-reported LLD and machine-coding rounds posted on LeetCode Discuss between June 2024 and November 2026 and tagged each with its format. These are candidate posts, not official data: they lean toward India-based SDE-2 candidates who post online. Still, the split is clear.

109A: whiteboard / doc (incl. LLD+HLD)
86B: machine coding (incl. take-home)
84C: running code in a normal slot
32hybrids: Java+LLD, DSA+LLD, AI-assisted

Two things stand out. First, more than half the rounds (170 of 311) expected code that runs - machine coding, take-home, LLD coding and code design together. "I will just draw the classes" is not a safe plan. Second, the level mix: 224 reports were SDE-2, 41 SDE-1 and 42 SDE-3 or senior. LLD is mainly an SDE-2 filter, but SDE-1 candidates at Flipkart, PhonePe and Microsoft report it too.

Limits of this data. Self-reported, unverified, and biased toward people who post on LeetCode (mostly India-based, mostly product companies). Reddit and Glassdoor were not used because they block automated reading. Treat the numbers as "what is common", not "what will happen to you".
