The format is the Flipkart one described in the machine-coding round guide: a group call explains the problem for about 30 minutes, you code for 90 in your own IDE with an in-memory store and a driver class, you upload a zip, and a later call runs your code and asks for one extension and the concurrency story.

Interviewers love this opening: "A slot is capacity plus a FIFO waitlist. The rules a booking must pass are a list I can extend, ranking is a strategy, and every state change happens under one lock, with notifications sent after the lock is released."
