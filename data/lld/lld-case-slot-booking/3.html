Identify the entities

Underline the nouns (candidate classes) and the verbs (candidate methods) in the agreed requirements, then drop what is just a value. Step through it.

  1. Nouns from the agreed requirements: nine candidates.
  2. Verbs: these become methods, and two of them (limit, notify) become interfaces.
  3. Merge and drop: a doctor and a gym centre are the same thing for booking purposes, so one Provider class with a category. A seat and a waitlist are not entities; they live inside Slot.
  4. Where each verb lands. The service is the facade; the variable parts sit behind small interfaces.
