Cancel with waitlist promotion

Book: three outcomes (step through it)

  1. Cheap state checks first: is the slot open, and has it not started yet (by the injected clock)?
  2. Run every rule. Any one can throw a BookingException with a clear message, and nothing has changed yet.
  3. A free seat: create a CONFIRMED booking, add it to the slot, bump the provider's counter for "trending".
  4. No seat but the waitlist has room: create a WAITLISTED booking at the back of the deque, queue a "waitlisted #n" message.
  5. Both full: reject. All of this ran under one lock, so the seat count cannot change between the check and the insert.
