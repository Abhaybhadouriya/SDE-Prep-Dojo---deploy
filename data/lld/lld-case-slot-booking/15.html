Common mistakes

  1. Check outside the lock Reading free(), then locking to insert, still overbooks.
  2. Closed intervals Using <= makes back-to-back slots clash and fails the sample test.
  3. Promotion without re-checking rules The promoted user may now have a clash or no quota left.
  4. Counting waitlisted entries as quota A Silver user stuck on three waitlists cannot book anything.
  5. Forgetting to restore quota on cancel PhonePe states it explicitly.
  6. A cutoff on leaving the waitlist Usually not intended; ask.
  7. Hard-coded sort in the service The ranking is the one thing every prompt says will change.
  8. Notification calls inside the critical section Slow I/O under the lock.
  9. No driver or only happy paths The evaluator runs edge cases; print rejections with reasons.
