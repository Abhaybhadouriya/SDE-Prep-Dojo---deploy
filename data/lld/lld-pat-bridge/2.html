Intent

Bridge (GoF structural pattern), in our words: split one class hierarchy into two, the what (the abstraction) and the how (the implementation), and connect them with a reference, so each side can grow new subclasses without multiplying the other.

The GoF words are confusing, so translate them. Abstraction does not mean "abstract class" here; it means the high-level side that clients use (the kind of notification, the remote, the VFS file API). Implementation does not mean "the code"; it means the low-level platform the abstraction runs on (the channel, the device, the filesystem). The pattern says: give each its own hierarchy, and let the abstraction hold a reference to an implementation instead of inheriting one.

In one line: prefer composition over inheritance when two dimensions vary.
