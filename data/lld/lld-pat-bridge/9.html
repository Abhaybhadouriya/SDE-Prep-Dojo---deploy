Related and confused-with patterns

Compared withLooks similar becauseThe difference
AdapterAn object forwards calls to another objectAdapter is retro-fitted to make two existing, mismatched interfaces fit. Bridge is designed up front so two sides can grow apart. You often write an Adapter to plug a third-party library in as one Concrete Implementor of a Bridge.
StrategyContext holds a reference to an interfaceStrategy swaps one algorithm (behavioural). Bridge splits two whole hierarchies (structural), and the abstraction side has its own subclasses.
Abstract FactoryDeals with platform familiesAbstract Factory creates the matching implementor objects for a platform; Bridge uses them. They are often combined.
StateDelegation to a swappable objectIn State the delegate changes itself as the object moves through states; in Bridge the client or configuration picks the implementor.
Template MethodHigh-level steps call low-level primitivesTemplate Method gets the primitives by inheritance (subclass overrides). Bridge gets them by composition, so they can change at runtime.
