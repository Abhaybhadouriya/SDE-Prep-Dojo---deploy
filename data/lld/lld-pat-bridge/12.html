  • Bridge = split "what" (abstraction) from "how" (implementation) into two hierarchies joined by a reference.
  • It turns M x N subclasses into M + N and allows switching the implementation at runtime.
  • Keep the Implementor interface primitive; put shared policy on the abstraction side.
  • Kernel: struct file + file_operations, net_device + net_device_ops, sock + proto.
  • Java: JDBC and SLF4J are the usual examples.
  • vs Adapter: designed up front vs retro-fitted. vs Strategy: two hierarchies vs one swappable algorithm.
  • Do not add it for a single dimension or a single implementation.
