Structure: two hierarchies and one reference

  • Abstraction (Notification): the high-level API clients call. Holds a reference to an Implementor and builds its behaviour out of Implementor calls only.
  • Refined Abstraction (Otp, OrderUpdate, Promo): variations of the high-level behaviour (wording, opt-out rules, urgency).
  • Implementor (Channel): the low-level interface. It usually offers primitive operations; the abstraction combines them into richer ones.
  • Concrete Implementors (SmsChannel, ...): one per platform, vendor or driver.

The Implementor interface does not have to look like the Abstraction's interface. In our example the abstraction offers send(User), while the implementor offers deliver(to, title, body) and maxChars(). The abstraction uses maxChars() to trim a long promo for SMS and push, so trimming logic is written once for every channel.
