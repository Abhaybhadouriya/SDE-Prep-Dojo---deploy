  • Answer in five beats: what varies, pattern name, three boxes, smallest code, trade-off plus the option you rejected.
  • Singleton is asked most, as code: holder idiom by default, enum for reflection and serialization safety, DCL only with volatile.
  • Strategy vs State: who switches. Decorator vs Proxy: add vs control. Adapter vs Facade: shape vs size. Composite vs Decorator: many children vs one.
  • Factory with a switch is OK; a registry makes it truly open for extension.
  • Observer always comes with follow-ups: unsubscribe, failure isolation, async queue, then a broker (Pub-Sub).
  • Over-design is a real rejection reason. A pattern is a cost you pay for a change you can name.
  • Your kernel work already uses these: ops tables (Strategy/Bridge), notifier chains (Observer), netfilter (Chain), protocol FSMs (State).
