Identify the entities

  1. Nouns first. "Feed" is the interesting one: it is not stored as such in a pull design, it is computed.
  2. Verbs. Two of them, "see feed" and "rank", carry the design choices; the rest are simple map updates.
  3. The classes. Post and Comment share voting, so a small Votable base holds it. "How to build a feed" and "how to order it" are two separate Strategy interfaces, because they change for different reasons.
  4. Nouns that do not earn a class. A vote is a map entry, a follower is a graph edge, a page is a tiny value object.

Two strategies, not one. Candidates ("which posts may appear") and ranking ("in what order") are independent. Flipkart needs global candidates with an engagement order; Uber needs following-only candidates with a per-user order; a system at scale switches candidates from pull to push without touching ranking. Splitting them is the design decision that impresses.
