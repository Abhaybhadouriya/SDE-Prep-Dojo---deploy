Common mistakes

  • One getFeed() that scans every post and filters by author. It works for the demo and fails the first complexity question.
  • Ranking hard-coded inside getFeed() with if (mode == ...). Uber's per-user twist then means editing the service.
  • Mixing "which posts" and "what order" in one class, so switching pull to push changes ranking code.
  • Counting votes as a plain integer with no record of who voted: double votes and impossible vote changes.
  • Using wall-clock time in the model and no tie-breaker: flaky order and untestable code.
  • Printing inside the model instead of returning data; the console or driver should format output.
  • Forgetting your own posts in your feed (LeetCode 355 expects them).
  • Not handling unfollow in push: the old posts keep showing.
  • Spending the first 30 minutes on the console parser before the feed works.
