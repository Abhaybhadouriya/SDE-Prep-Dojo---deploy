Related and confused-with patterns

PatternRelation to IteratorOne-liner
CompositeA tree of parts; Iterator is how you walk it (depth-first, breadth-first)"Composite is the shape, Iterator is the walk."
VisitorBoth touch every element. Iterator gives elements to you; Visitor sends a new operation to each element, chosen by its type"Iterator: next item. Visitor: new operation per type."
Factory Methoditerator() is a factory method: each collection builds its own iterator class"The collection is the iterator factory."
MementoAn iterator's position can be saved as a memento and restored later (resume a scan)"Bookmark the cursor."
Generator / streamA language feature that writes an iterator for you"Iterator without the class."
