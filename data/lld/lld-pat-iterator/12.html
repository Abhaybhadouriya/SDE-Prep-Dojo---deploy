Remember this

  • Iterator = walk a collection one item at a time without seeing how it is stored.
  • Participants: Iterator, ConcreteIterator (holds the cursor), Aggregate (iterator()), ConcreteAggregate.
  • iterator() is a factory method; each iterator has its own position, so several walks can run at once.
  • External = caller pulls (hasNext/next); internal = collection pushes into your function (forEach).
  • Fail-fast (modCount check, throws), snapshot (copy, never throws), weakly consistent (concurrent, no copy).
  • Delete through the iterator: it.remove(), it = v.erase(it), list_for_each_entry_safe.
  • Custom iterators: keep minimal state, make hasNext() side-effect free, compose by wrapping.
  • Kernel bridge: list_for_each_entry and seq_operations are iterators.
