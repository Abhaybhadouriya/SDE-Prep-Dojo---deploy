Intent

Iterator (GoF behavioral pattern, also called Cursor): hand out a small separate object that walks a collection one element at a time, so callers can loop over it without knowing whether it is an array, a linked list, a tree or a paged API.

Behavioral means the pattern is about how objects share work and talk to each other. Here the collection hands the job of "walking" to a separate object. Two ideas follow from that:

  • The collection stays private. Callers see hasNext() / next(), never arrays, nodes or page cursors.
  • Many walks, many orders. Each iterator keeps its own position, so two loops over the same list do not disturb each other, and one collection can offer several traversals (forward, reverse, shuffled, in-order, breadth-first).
