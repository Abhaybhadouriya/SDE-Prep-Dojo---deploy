The story: two cashiers, one notebook

Explain like I'm 5

A cinema has one seat left and two cashiers. Each cashier looks at the notebook: "1 seat left". Each one turns to their customer and says "yes, it's yours!" Each one then writes "0 seats left". Two happy customers, one seat. Nobody lied; they just both looked before either one wrote.

Now a second problem. Cashier A has her own little sticky note where she copies numbers to save walking to the notebook. Cashier B changes the notebook, but A keeps reading her sticky note and never notices. She is not slow or wrong; she just cannot see the change.

The fixes are simple once you can name them. Rule one: only one cashier can hold the notebook while looking and writing (that is atomicity). Rule two: when you put the notebook down, everyone must throw away their sticky notes and look again (that is visibility).

Every bug on this page is one of those two problems, or their quieter cousin, ordering: the steps happened, but another thread saw them in a different order than you wrote them.
