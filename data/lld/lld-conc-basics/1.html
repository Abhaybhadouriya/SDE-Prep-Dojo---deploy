Why LLD interviews care

A solution that passes a single-threaded driver can still be wrong. Once the interviewer says "now assume many users at once", every shared object in your class diagram is suspect: free-spot lists, seat maps, wallet balances, rate-limiter counters. In the reports we collected, "prevent double allocation of the same resource" was the most common concurrency follow-up (9 distinct reports, including Uber cab allocation and Microsoft parking lot), and Walmart LLD rounds often slide into volatile and double-checked locking.

This page is the foundation for the rest of the concurrency track: locks and conditions, atomics and concurrent collections, safe singletons and immutability and preventing double booking.

From your world

You already know all of this under other names. A Java thread is a kernel task sharing one mm_struct with its siblings, so every heap object is shared memory, like data shared between two CPUs in a driver. The race on count++ is the reason atomic_t exists. The stop-flag bug is the reason READ_ONCE() exists. What changes in Java is the vocabulary and the rulebook: the Java Memory Model (JLS chapter 17) plays the role that memory-barriers.txt and the LKMM play in the kernel.
