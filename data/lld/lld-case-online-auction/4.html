Class diagram

  • AuctionHouse is the only entry point. It holds the registries (buyers, sellers, auctions), checks who is asking, calls the auction, then notifies listeners. It also owns the timer that closes auctions at their end time.
  • Auction owns everything about one item: limits, cost, status, the live bids, the participants, a history log and a ReentrantLock. All its methods take the lock, so a check and the change that follows are one atomic step.
  • AuctionRules is the Strategy: validate (is this amount allowed now?), best (current leader, for open auctions), pickWinner and allowsWithdraw. HighestUniqueBidRules is the Flipkart rule; EnglishAuctionRules is the open ascending auction with a minimum increment.
  • Bid and AuctionResult are immutable values. Money is held as whole rupees for bids and as paise (a long) for profit, so 0.2 x cost never meets a double.
  • Buyer keeps a counter of past auctions; isPreferred() is the bonus rule, passed into pickWinner as a predicate so the rules never need the buyer registry.
