Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Runs: one command plays both samples and prints the winners and profits.
  • Correct rules: highest unique bid, inclusive limits, cost charged once and kept on withdraw, the bonus tie-break.
  • Money: integers (paise), formatted only at the edge.
  • Separation: rules in a Strategy, data and locking in Auction, validation and notification in the house, parsing in the driver.
  • Extensible: a second auction type added without editing Auction or the house.
  • Validation: unknown users, bad amounts, wrong state, wrong seller, all with clear messages.
  • Thread safety: per-auction lock around check-and-store; idempotent close; listeners outside the lock.
  • Tests: the samples plus edge cases, deterministic (injected clock).
