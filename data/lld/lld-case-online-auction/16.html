  • House = Mediator (all requests), listeners = Observer (all events), AuctionRules = Strategy (the winner rule).
  • Keep live bids and paying participants apart; a withdrawal removes from one, not the other.
  • Highest unique bid: group by amount, walk from the top, stop at the first amount with one bidder.
  • Money in paise as a long; format only when printing.
  • One lock per auction around read-best, validate and store; reject stale bids.
  • Close is idempotent; the deadline is checked on every bid with an injected clock.
  • Notify listeners after the lock is released.
