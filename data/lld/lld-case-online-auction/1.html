How it is asked

Before you start: this case assumes OOP from C structs (objects as structs with an ops table) and interfaces vs abstract classes. The patterns it uses are explained on their own pages: Mediator, Strategy, Observer, State. Why rules are composed, not inherited: composition over inheritance.

The auction question comes in three flavours. Find out which one you have in the first minute:

  • Sealed-bid machine coding (Flipkart style): buyers submit secret bids, a rule picks the winner at close. The trick is the rule ("highest unique bid", "lowest bid, earliest wins") and the money calculation.
  • Open ascending ("English") auction: every new bid must beat the current highest by an increment, bidders are told when they are outbid, and there may be a reserve price and auto-bidding. The trick is the race on the highest bid and the closing timer.
  • HLD auction (Meta, Amazon): millions of bidders, a hot item at the last second, consistency of the winning bid. In an LLD round you show the per-auction lock and say where the HLD starts (our HLD Online Auction page).
