Clarify requirements (first 5-10 minutes)

Read the statement twice. Then say the rules back in your own words and write them as a comment at the top of Main.

Functional requirements

  1. Register buyers and sellers by name.
  2. Create an auction with an id, lowest and highest bid limits, a participation cost and its seller. Many auctions run at the same time.
  3. Bid: a registered buyer creates a bid inside the limits. A buyer has at most one live bid per auction; they may update it or withdraw it until the auction closes.
  4. Participation cost: a buyer pays it once per auction on the first successful bid. It is not refunded on withdraw. The seller gets 20% of it; the platform keeps the rest.
  5. Close the auction and return the winning bid: the highest amount bid by exactly one buyer. If no amount is unique, there is no winner.
  6. Profit or loss for the seller: winning bid + bidders x 0.2 x cost - (low + high) / 2, or only bidders x 0.2 x cost when there is no winner.
  7. Bonus: a buyer who took part in more than 2 past auctions is preferred. On a tie at the top amount, if exactly one of the tied buyers is preferred, they win; otherwise fall to the next amount.

Non-functional requirements

  • Correct money: no floating point for rupees; the sample answers (-12.40 and 0.40) must come out exactly.
  • Extensible: other auction types (open ascending, lowest unique, reverse auctions) without touching the bid code.
  • Thread-safe: auctions run in parallel; two bids on one auction must not corrupt it.
  • Demo-able: one command runs the sample test cases and prints the results.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Payments and refunds, user login, persistence, search, auto-bidding, reserve prices, and pushing updates to millions of phones. Each one gets a line in Extensibility.
