Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Asked about seat reuse, RAC/WL, partial booking and fairness before coding.
  • Modelled availability per segment (mask or interval overlap), not per seat.
  • Booking state lives per (train, date, class); catalogue objects are immutable.
  • All-or-nothing per PNR, with a real rollback path.
  • Promotion in queue order, RAC before WL, skipping passengers who do not fit.
  • One lock per journey, short critical section, nothing slow under the lock.
  • Fair ordering explained (fair lock or a queue).
  • Strategies for berth picking and fares; observer for notifications.
  • Runnable driver with checks, including a concurrency test.
  • Can draw the schema and the overlap SQL when asked.
  • Clear names, small methods, exceptions for errors (no magic return codes).
