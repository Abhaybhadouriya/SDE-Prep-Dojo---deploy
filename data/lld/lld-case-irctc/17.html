Stops are NDLS(0), AGC(1), GWL(2), BPL(3), NGP(4). What is the mask for AGC to BPL?

  1. 0b1110
  2. 0b0110
  3. 0b0111
  4. 0b1100
Stops 1 to 3 occupy segments 1 and 2: bits 1 and 2 = 0b0110.

A berth has mask 0b0011. Which request fits it?

  1. NDLS-AGC
  2. AGC-GWL
  3. GWL-NGP
  4. NDLS-NGP
GWL-NGP is 0b1100; 0b0011 AND 0b1100 = 0.

After a CNF cancellation, who is served first?

  1. The RAC queue, in order
  2. The waitlist, in order
  3. The newest booking
  4. Whoever asks next
RAC passengers already hold a seat and are next for a berth; their freed halves then go to WL passengers.

What is the right unit of locking?

  1. One global lock
  2. One lock per berth
  3. One lock per user
  4. One lock per train + date + class
Everything a booking touches (masks, RAC and WL queues) belongs to one journey and class; different journeys never interact.

Why are listeners called after the inventory lock is released?
