The strongest test here is the invariant checker: rebuild every berth mask and both queues from the tickets, compare with the inventory's own arrays, and assert that no waiting passenger fits a free place. Run it after any random sequence of operations, single-threaded or concurrent. It catches mask drift, double sales and missed promotions without you predicting the exact outcome.
