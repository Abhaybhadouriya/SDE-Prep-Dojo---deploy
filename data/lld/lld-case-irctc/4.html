Class diagram

What each class owns, top to bottom:

  • Route and the segment mask. A route is the ordered list of stops of one train. mask(i, j) returns one bit per segment between stop i and stop j. Routes, stations, berths and trains are catalogue data: immutable, shared by every date, never locked.
  • Berth. A record: number, type (LOWER, SIDE_UPPER ...), quota (general or tatkal) and whether it is an RAC berth sold as two halves. It holds no booking state; that lives in the inventory.
  • JourneyInventory, the single locked aggregate. One object per train + date + class. It owns everything that changes: one occupied mask per berth, the two masks of each RAC berth, the RAC queue, the WL queue and the fair lock. allocate, cancel and availability are its only entry points; promotion runs inside cancel, under the same lock.
  • BerthPicker, the placement strategy. Given the free berths for a trip, it picks one. PreferenceBestFitPicker prefers the passenger's berth type, then the berth that already has the most sold segments (best fit keeps whole berths free for long trips); FirstFreePicker takes the first.
  • FareRule and TatkalSurcharge. DistanceFare prices by km and class. TatkalSurcharge is a FareRule that holds another FareRule, asks it for the base fare and adds the surcharge, so more surcharges can be stacked the same way.
  • ReservationService, the facade. The one class callers use. It searches through TrainCatalog (trains plus the station -> trains inverted index), finds or creates the JourneyInventory, prices the trip, issues the PNR as a Booking of PassengerTickets, and tells the BookingListeners about every status change after the inventory lock is released.
