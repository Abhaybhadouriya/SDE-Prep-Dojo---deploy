Common mistakes

  • A booked flag on Seat. Breaks seat reuse and every date after the first.
  • Closed intervals. Treating NDLS-GWL and GWL-NGP as overlapping wastes half the capacity; stops are boundaries, segments are what you sell.
  • One global lock for all trains, or none at all. Lock per journey.
  • Calling SMS/payment under the lock. One slow call freezes the tatkal rush.
  • Stopping promotion at the first passenger that does not fit, leaving a free berth unsold.
  • Forgetting to renumber RAC and WL positions, so two people both show "WL 1".
  • No rollback when the third passenger of a PNR fails.
  • Hard-coding LocalDateTime.now(), which makes the tatkal window untestable.
  • Spending 20 minutes on fares and user accounts before the allocator exists.
