Cancel and promote (step through it)

  1. The family of four cancels. Their four berths now have NDLS-NGP free (their mask is cleared with AND NOT).
  2. The RAC queue is served first, in order: Dev and Esha each get a full berth, and their halves of side-lower B7 are released.
  3. Then the waitlist, in order: WL 1 and WL 2 find the last two free berths and become CNF directly.
  4. WL 3 finds no berth, but a now-empty RAC half fits, so Hari becomes RAC 1. Numbers are recomputed for both queues.
  5. Nine status changes in total, delivered to listeners once the inventory lock is released. This is exactly the demo output below.

Skip, do not stop. The promotion loop walks the whole queue. If WL 1 travels NDLS-NGP but the only gap is GWL-NGP, WL 1 stays and a later WL 2 travelling GWL-BPL may be confirmed. Stopping at the first passenger that does not fit would leave a free berth unsold and break the "no missed promotion" rule. Ask the interviewer which policy they want; real systems also differ by quota.
