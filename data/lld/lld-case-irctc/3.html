Identify the entities

  1. Nouns from the statement are class candidates.
  2. Verbs become methods. book, cancel and promote are where all the logic lives.
  3. Two modelling insights: a segment is just a bit position between stop i and i+1, and booking state belongs to a journey (train on a date, in a class), never to the Berth record itself.
  4. The final split: immutable catalogue records, one contended JourneyInventory per journey and class, and small strategy interfaces for the parts that vary.

The modelling bug interviewers wait for: boolean booked on Seat, or a Booking table checked with "is there any booking for this seat?". The first breaks seat reuse and multiple dates; the second needs an interval overlap query (from < myTo AND to > myFrom). Say the overlap rule out loud, then show the bitmask as the fast in-memory form of the same check.
