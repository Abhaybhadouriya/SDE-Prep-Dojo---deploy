The story: the bowling alley shoe rack

Explain like I'm 5

At a bowling alley you do not buy new shoes every time you play. There is a rack of shoes behind the counter. You ask for a pair, you bowl, you hand them back. The person at the counter sprays them clean and puts them on the rack for the next player.

If all the shoes are out, you wait in line until someone returns a pair. If the alley is very busy they might tell you "sorry, come back in ten minutes". And if one player walks home wearing the shoes, the rack slowly gets emptier until nobody can play.

That rack is an object pool. The shoes are expensive to make, so we reuse them. Borrowing is called acquire, handing back is release, the spray is the reset, waiting in line is a blocking pool, "come back later" is a timeout, and the player who walks home is a leak. Every part of this page is one of those words.

From your world

You have written kmem_cache_create("my_flow", sizeof(struct my_flow), ...) and then kmem_cache_alloc() / kmem_cache_free() in a hot path. That is an object pool: same-size objects, a free list, no trip to the general allocator each time. And if you ever worked on a block or storage driver, mempool_t is a pool with a guaranteed reserve, so I/O that frees memory can always make progress even when the allocator is failing.
