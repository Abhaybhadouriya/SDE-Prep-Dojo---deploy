Structure: pool, reusable, client

  • ObjectPool: keeps the idle objects, counts the borrowed ones, enforces max, and decides what happens when it is empty (create, wait, or fail).
  • Reusable: the expensive object. It must be resettable to a clean state.
  • Factory (often three small functions): how to create a new one, how to validate that an old one still works, how to reset it between users.
  • Lease (also called handle or proxy connection): what the client actually holds. Releasing through the handle, instead of calling pool.release(obj) by hand, is the single best defence against leaks.
  • Client: acquires, uses, releases. It never constructs or destroys the reusable itself.
