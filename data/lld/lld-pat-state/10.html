Related and confused-with patterns

Compared withLooks similar becauseThe difference
StrategyIdentical class diagram: a context delegating to an interfaceIn Strategy the client picks the algorithm, strategies are independent and usually do not change during a call. In State the delegate changes as a result of events, the states pick their successors and know about each other. Pricing is Strategy; idle/has-money/dispensing is State.
BridgeDelegation to a swappable objectA Bridge implementor is chosen once by configuration and has its own hierarchy; a State changes itself throughout the object's life.
CommandEvents as objectsCommands are the inputs (insert coin, select); States decide what those inputs mean now. They combine well: a queue of commands fed to a state machine.
MementoAbout "state"Memento saves and restores an object's data snapshot (undo). The State pattern is about behaviour that depends on the lifecycle stage.
Singleton / FlyweightShared state instancesStateless state objects are often shared (one IDLE for every machine). That is a flyweight use, not a reason to make them global singletons with getInstance().
