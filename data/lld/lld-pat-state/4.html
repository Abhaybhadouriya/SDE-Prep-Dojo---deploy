How it works: one purchase, four states

  1. The machine starts SOLD_OUT. A coin is returned: SoldOut does not override insertCoin, so the interface's default "return the coin" runs.
  2. An operator refills. SoldOut.refill adds stock and calls m.setState(IDLE). The state chose its successor; the machine did not.
  3. A Rs 20 coin. Idle.insertCoin adds to the balance (data lives in the context) and moves to HAS_MONEY.
  4. The buyer picks B2 (Rs 35). HasMoney.select checks price against balance and stays put: not every event causes a transition. Another Rs 30 brings the balance to 50.
  5. A1 is selected. HasMoney.select moves to DISPENSING and starts the motor. A coin now is answered with "busy": the same event means something different here.
  6. The motor reports done. Dispensing.dispensed refunds Rs 30 change and picks the next state: IDLE if any stock is left, SOLD_OUT if not.
