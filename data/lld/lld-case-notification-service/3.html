Identify entities: nouns become classes, verbs become methods

Underline the nouns and verbs in the requirement list, then merge, drop and rename. Click through.

  1. Start from your clarified statement, not from a memorised diagram.
  2. Nouns are candidates. Twelve is too many, and that is the point of this step.
  3. Verbs become methods on the class that owns the data they touch: render on the template registry, record on the service that owns history.
  4. Merge and drop. Customer, seller and courier differ only in their addresses, so they are one User. "Retry" is not a thing, it is a behaviour around sending, which is a hint for Decorator.
  5. Final model: one facade, a registry, an event bus, one sender interface with three wrappers, and plain immutable records. Each interface sits exactly where the interviewer will ask for a change.
