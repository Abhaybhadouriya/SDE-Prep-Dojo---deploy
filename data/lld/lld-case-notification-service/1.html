The notification system is the 2nd most reported LLD problem in our sample of 311 public LeetCode Discuss interview reports from June 2024 to November 2026 (11 reports, 10 companies). It shows up in every format: 60-minute whiteboard or doc rounds (classes, APIs, sometimes a DB schema), 90-minute machine coding (running code), and in 2026 an AI-assisted LLD round.

Two lessons from these reports. The Cleartrip candidate built a "Kafka kind of" design for a 90-minute order-notification prompt, ran out of time and was rejected: the interviewer wanted a demoable solution. And the Amazon candidate covered requirements, entities and classes, but the feedback was still weak: at SDE2 the interviewer expects you to drive into the hard parts yourself (failures, duplicates, preferences, priority, extensibility). This page is built to hit both: a small core that runs in 45 minutes, and a clear list of hard parts with code for each.
