Common mistakes

  • A switch (channel) in the service, or one subclass per channel per notification type.
  • Sending inside submit on the caller's thread: the order service now waits for Twilio and fails when Twilio fails.
  • Unbounded or blind retries: no backoff, no attempt limit, retrying "invalid number" forever.
  • No idempotency: every upstream retry is another SMS to the customer.
  • Ignoring preferences and missing addresses, or throwing an exception that stops the remaining channels.
  • Holding the queue lock while calling a provider.
  • Thread.sleep polling for scheduled jobs instead of a timed condition wait.
  • Real time in tests: a test that waits an hour, or a flaky rate-limit test.
  • Over-building: Kafka, microservices and a template engine in a 90-minute round. Build the core, name the rest.
