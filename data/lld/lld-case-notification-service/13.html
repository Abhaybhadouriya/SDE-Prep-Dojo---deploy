If you are at minute 45 without the event-to-delivery path running, drop the background worker and rate limiter, keep processDue(), and finish retries and history. A running synchronous core with a clean ChannelSender interface scores higher than a threaded design that does not run. That is the lesson of the Cleartrip report above.

The 60-minute design-only version

For Amazon, Swiggy, Adobe or Salesforce-style doc rounds: requirements (8 min), entities and class diagram (15), one sequence diagram for "order placed" (8), APIs (5), then spend the rest on the hard parts in this order: failures (retry, failover, retryable flag), duplicates (idempotency key), preferences, priority and scheduling, rate limiting, concurrency. Draw the decorator stack; it is the picture interviewers remember.
