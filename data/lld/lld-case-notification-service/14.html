Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Asked who triggers, which channels, providers per channel, priority meaning and duplicate policy before designing.
  • "What to send" (request, template) is separate from "how to send" (channel sender); no switch on channel in the core.
  • New channel or provider is a new class plus registration (Strategy), shown or explained.
  • Retries are bounded, use backoff and distinguish retryable from permanent errors; failover across providers exists.
  • Callers do not wait for providers; a worker sends; the lock is not held while sending.
  • Idempotency key prevents double sends on upstream retries; atomic add, not check-then-act.
  • User preferences and missing addresses produce explicit statuses, not exceptions or silence.
  • Priority and scheduling handled by one clear ordering rule that the candidate can state.
  • History per channel with status and provider; queryable by user.
  • Time is injected; tests and demo do not sleep; assertions in main.
  • Knows the limit of the LLD: names the HLD changes (durable queue, Kafka, many workers) without coding them.
