  • ISP: clients should not be forced to depend on methods they do not use. Judge from the caller's side.
  • Smells: UnsupportedOperationException, empty method bodies, instanceof checks before calls, giant mocks.
  • Fix: split by client into role interfaces; one class may implement many roles; compose with extends A, B when a client really needs several.
  • Cost of fat interfaces: lying types, change ripple (rebuild/redeploy), hard tests, hidden coupling.
  • Kernel file_operations: a deliberate fat table made safe by NULL slots and one checking caller; the kernel still segregates by subsystem.
  • Do not over-split: group methods used together; split when a second client or implementer needs less.
  • ISP fixes often fix LSP violations too; a client needing seven narrow interfaces hints at an SRP problem.
