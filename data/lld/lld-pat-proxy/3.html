Structure: subject, real subject, proxy

  • Subject (QuoteService): the interface the client depends on.
  • Real subject (ExchangeQuoteService): does the actual work.
  • Proxy: implements Subject, holds or creates the real subject, and controls access. A virtual proxy creates the real subject itself; others receive it in the constructor.
  • Client: only knows the Subject. Usually it does not even know a proxy is there, because a factory or the DI container hands it out.

Holding the target as the interface type, not the concrete class, lets proxies stack: auth in front of rate limit in front of cache in front of lazy creation. Each one does one job.
