Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. Lazy and caching proxies hold shared state. Never hold a lock across the forwarded call; keep one in-flight load per key (our CachingQuoteProxy).
  • Testing. Put a fake real subject behind the proxy that counts calls, and inject a fake clock for TTLs and rate windows, exactly as our demo does.
  • Cost. One indirect call per layer, plus memory for caches (bound them). A remote proxy hides latency, so make timeouts and names say "remote".
