How it works: one call through a chain of proxies

  1. The screen calls quote("INFY") on what it thinks is a QuoteService. The protection proxy checks the user's role first.
  2. The rate-limit proxy counts the call in the current one-second window and lets it through (1 of 3).
  3. The caching proxy looks up INFY. Nothing cached yet, so it delegates.
  4. The virtual proxy sees the real service has never been created, creates it now (connects to the feed), and forwards the call.
  5. The real service answers. On the way back the caching proxy stores the quote with a 500 ms TTL.
  6. The screen gets a normal Quote. A second call within 500 ms stops at the cache; the exchange is never touched.

Order matters. With RateLimit before Cache, cache hits also use up the limit (protecting your own servers). With Cache before RateLimit, only real calls are counted (protecting the vendor's quota). Decide which one you are guarding and say it out loud in an interview.
