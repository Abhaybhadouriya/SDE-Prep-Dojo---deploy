In interviews: where it shows up

Builder is a tier 1 pattern in our research (named again and again in LLD reports). It rarely carries a whole design, but it is the natural tool for the one "big" entity in a problem. Listen for: "many optional fields", "configurable", "immutable", "request/order object", "add-ons".

Case studyWhat gets built
Food deliveryOrder with items, coupons, tip, instructions, delivery slot.
Car rental / Hotel bookingReservation with dates, add-ons (GPS, child seat, breakfast), insurance.
Notification serviceNotification with channel, template, params, priority, retry policy.
Meeting schedulerMeeting with optional room, attendees, recurrence rule, reminders.
LoggerLogger configuration: level, formatter, sinks, async buffer size.
Coffee machineA custom drink. Good place to discuss Builder (data) vs Decorator (pricing behaviour).

In a machine-coding round, do not build a builder for every class. Use it for the one or two entities with many optional fields, say why in one sentence, and move on. Interviewers mark down pattern stuffing.
