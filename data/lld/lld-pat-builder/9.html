Related and often confused patterns

PatternQuestion it answersHow you call itPick it when
BuilderHow do I put one complex object together?Several steps, then build()Many options, validation, immutability
Factory Method / Simple FactoryWhich class should I create?One call returns a ready objectThe concrete type varies
Abstract FactoryWhich family of classes?One factory object, many create methodsSeveral products must match
PrototypeCan I copy a configured one?template.copy() then tweakMany objects differ slightly from a template
DecoratorHow do I add behaviour at run time?Wrap objects in objectsCoffee + milk + sugar pricing; behaviour changes, not just data
Fluent interfaceStyle onlyMethods return thisNot every chain is a builder: streams and jQuery are fluent but not builders

Builders combine well: a builder's build() can call a factory to pick the concrete class; a Composite tree (menus, file systems, documents) is often assembled with a builder; and a protobuf-style toBuilder() gives you a prototype-like "copy then change" path.
