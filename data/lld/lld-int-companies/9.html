Remember this

  • Two questions place every company: how long is the slot, and will the code be run?
  • Flipkart, PhonePe, Razorpay, Meesho, Groww, CRED: 90-120 min machine coding, in memory, driver or tests, then a review call.
  • Uber and Atlassian: 60 min, running code; Atlassian adds tests and staged follow-ups.
  • Amazon and Zepto: design-first rounds; Amazon shares the hour with Leadership Principles.
  • Microsoft and Salesforce vary by interviewer; Walmart mixes design with Java concurrency; Google has no separate LLD round.
  • AI: banned in Flipkart's posted 2026 rules, allowed and graded in some Razorpay, CRED and Microsoft rounds. Never assume.
  • Ask the recruiter the four questions (run? IDE? tests? AI?) before you practise.
