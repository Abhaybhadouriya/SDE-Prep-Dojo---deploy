The story: three judges, one cake

Explain like I'm 5

You bake the same chocolate cake for three judges.

Judge one never tastes it. She asks you to explain your recipe and why you picked those eggs. Judge two only tastes it: if it is raw inside, you fail, no matter how nice your recipe card is. Judge three tastes a slice, then says, "Now make it without sugar. Now make two." Your kitchen must be tidy enough to change fast.

Before you bake, you want to know which judge you have. That is what this page tells you, company by company.

From your world

Think of interop certification labs. One lab reviews your design document and state machine diagrams. Another plugs your box into their test rig and runs a script: if a link does not come up, nothing else matters. A third keeps adding test cases to the plan as you pass each one. Same protocol stack, three test plans. You would read the lab's test plan before you go. This page is the test plan for each company's LLD round.
