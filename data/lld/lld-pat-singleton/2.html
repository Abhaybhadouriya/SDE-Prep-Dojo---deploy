Intent

Singleton (a GoF creational pattern): make sure a class has only one instance, and give the whole program one global way to reach it.

Two words carry the weight. Creational means the pattern is about how objects are made. Global access means any code can reach the object without it being passed in. The first part (only one) is often useful. The second part (global access) is the one that causes most of the trouble, as you will see in when not to use it.
