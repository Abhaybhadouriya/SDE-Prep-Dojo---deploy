Remember this

  • Three parts: private constructor, static field, static accessor.
  • Thread-safe choices in Java: eager, synchronized method, DCL + volatile, holder idiom, enum. Prefer enum or holder.
  • DCL without volatile can publish a half-built object; volatile = release on write, acquire on read.
  • Breakers: reflection, serialization, cloning, class loaders. Defences: constructor guard, readResolve, no Cloneable, or just an enum.
  • C++: Meyers singleton (function-local static) is thread-safe since C++11. Python: a module is a singleton.
  • "Only one" is fine; "global access" is the anti-pattern. Create once at startup and inject.
  • DB connection: never a singleton connection; a single pool.
