Identify the entities

Underline the nouns (candidate classes) and verbs (candidate methods) in the agreed requirements, then throw away the ones that are only values or actors. Step through it.

  1. Underline the nouns. Each is a candidate class.
  2. Underline the verbs. Give each to the class that owns its data: debit() to the bank, reserve() and eject() to the dispenser, the user actions to the ATM.
  3. Drop actors and values. Notice the requirement words "only after" and "while": they name states, and states become classes.
  4. The final list. A cassette is one NoteHandler. Do not add Customer, Screen, Keypad or CardReader classes unless the interviewer wants hardware modelled.
