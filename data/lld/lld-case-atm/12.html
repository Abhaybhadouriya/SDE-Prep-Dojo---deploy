The 2026 post above asks how this fits in a 60-minute LLD round. Our split for that: 6 minutes of questions, 8 minutes for the state diagram and class list, 30 minutes of code (the state interface with two or three states, the chain with backtracking, the withdraw method with the reversal), and 15 minutes of discussion on idempotency, the joint-account race and extensions. The interviewer would rather see the chain and the withdraw order done right than five states with greedy dispensing. More in how LLD rounds work.
