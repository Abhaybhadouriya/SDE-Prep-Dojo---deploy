Common mistakes

  1. Greedy-only dispensing It fails the 600 case. Plan with backtracking, then take.
  2. Changing cassette counts while planning A failed plan then leaves the counts wrong. Plan on a copy or return a plan.
  3. Eject before debit A declined debit after the cash is out is lost money.
  4. No reversal path "Assume the hardware works" is the answer that fails the money-safety part of the rubric.
  5. Reading the balance at the ATM and deciding there Two ATMs on one account overdraw it. The bank decides in one atomic step.
  6. Transaction ids that are unique only per ATM The bank's idempotency check treats another ATM's T1 as a retry and skips the debit.
  7. State checks in every method if (state != AUTHENTICATED) throw copied five times is the switch the State pattern removes.
  8. Bank codes and paise in ATM code Keep "51" and the times-100 inside the adapter.
  9. Modelling every piece of hardware CardReader, Keypad, Screen, Printer classes eat 20 minutes and earn nothing unless asked.
