Withdraw: the order of the three steps is the design

When things go wrong (step through it)

  1. The dispenser cannot make the amount (in the demo: 4,500 when only 4,000 is left). Nothing has been promised to the bank yet, so we simply decline.
  2. The bank says no (insufficient funds, daily limit, blocked card). The notes we set aside go back into the counts. They never left the cassette.
  3. The debit succeeded but the feeder jams. The customer has no cash, so we send a reversal with the same transaction id and release the notes. The receipt says REVERSED.
  4. The rule behind all three: money moves on both sides or on neither. Real regulators enforce it too: in India the RBI requires a debit-without-cash ATM failure to be reversed within T+5 days, with compensation of 100 rupees per day of delay after that (see resources).
