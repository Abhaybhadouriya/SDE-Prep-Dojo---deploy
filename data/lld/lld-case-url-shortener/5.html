From your world

CodeGenerator is tcp_congestion_ops chosen by name: the socket code (here, shorten) calls through the pointer and does not care whether cubic or bbr (counter or hash) is behind it. UrlRepository is the VFS split between policy and storage: the VFS decides what an operation means and calls inode_operations, and ext4 or tmpfs decide how it is stored; saveIfAbsent is the one primitive every backend must make atomic, like ->create failing with -EEXIST.

Patterns we deliberately skipped: inheritance per generator type (a HashShortener extends UrlShortener would copy the whole service to change one function; a strategy changes only that function), a Singleton shortener (tests build many, each with its own fake clock and repository), and a Proxy cache in front of the repository (a real system adds one for hot links; in one process the map already is the cache).
