Common mistakes

  • Answering the HLD question in an LLD round (or the reverse): ask first, then design classes.
  • Check-then-insert for codes: a race that overwrites links.
  • Unbounded retry loops on collision.
  • MD5 "because it is unique": no hash cut to 7 characters is unique.
  • Reading the system clock directly: expiry becomes untestable without sleeping.
  • Returning 404 for an expired link, or counting a click on it.
  • Dedupe without normalizing, or deduping TTL links into permanent ones.
  • A URLManager god-class that validates, generates, stores and counts.
  • 301 redirects with analytics as a requirement.
  • clicks++ on a plain field under concurrent requests.
