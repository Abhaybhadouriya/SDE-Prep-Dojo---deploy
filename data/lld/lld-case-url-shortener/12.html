Extensibility: the follow-ups

Follow-upChange
"Codes must not be guessable, but keep the counter"A ScrambledCounterGenerator: map the counter through a reversible permutation of [0, 627) (multiply by a number coprime to 627, mod 627, or a small Feistel network). Still unique, no longer sequential.
"Store in MySQL / DynamoDB"A new UrlRepository; saveIfAbsent becomes INSERT with a unique key (or a conditional put). Service unchanged.
"Clicks per day / per country"A ClickListener that aggregates by (code, day, country); or one that publishes events to a queue.
"Block malicious URLs"A chain of UrlChecks run by the validator (Chain of Responsibility): scheme, length, blocklist, safe-browsing lookup.
"Rate limit per user"Wrap the service in a Proxy that checks a token bucket per owner before shorten (see Rate Limiter).
"Cache hot links"A caching UrlRepository decorator with an LRU in front of the real one (Decorator, LRU Cache).
"Edit the target of a link"Make longUrl replaceable through a update(code, owner, newUrl) that swaps the entity atomically (replace(code, old, new)). Fix the dedupe index too.
"Links per user, with paging"A second index owner -> codes in the repository.
