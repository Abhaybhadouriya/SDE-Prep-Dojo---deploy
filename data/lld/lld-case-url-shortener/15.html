Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Scope: said "LLD, in memory, storage behind an interface" and listed what HLD would add.
  • Strategy for codes, with the trade-offs of counter, hash and random.
  • Atomic claim: no check-then-insert; bounded retries on collision.
  • Expiry with an injected clock; 404 and 410 are different.
  • Validation and errors: scheme, host, length, self-links, alias rules, owner checks; specific exceptions.
  • Analytics that are correct under concurrency (LongAdder), with a hook to grow.
  • Runs and is tested: forced collisions and the expiry boundary are tested, not just happy paths.
  • Separation: validator, generator, repository and facade each have one job.
