The self-grading sheet

Score each row 0, 1 or 2 straight after the mock, before reading the walkthrough. The rows are the consolidated rubric from what evaluators check. The thresholds are our own practice scale, not any company's. Weights: highest, high, med or n/a say how much a row counts in that mock; gate means pass/fail: if a gate row scores 0, the other rows do not count (the evaluator never reaches them; see what evaluators check).
