The story: the fire drill

Explain like I'm 5

A school does not wait for a real fire to find out if the children know where to go. It rings the bell on a normal day. Everyone walks out, a teacher holds a stopwatch, and afterwards they talk about what went wrong: "the door at the back was stuck", "two classes took the same stairs".

A mock interview is a fire drill for your LLD round. The real round is too important to be your first try. You ring the bell yourself, start the stopwatch, and find the stuck doors while it is still cheap.

From your world

This is fault injection before release. You would not ship a new driver without pulling the cable during a test run to see the error path. A mock pulls the cable on you: the interviewer says "now two riders request at once" and you find out whether your design has an error path. The self-grading sheet at the end is your test report.

The prompts are based on real reported rounds (cited with dates; candidate posts are self-reported and author names are left out). The scripts, answers and code are our own. The minute splits are what candidates describe and what the guides suggest, not official rules.
