Why does the Flipkart-style mock lock synchronized (report) instead of the whole service?

  1. Locks on the service are not allowed in Java
  2. One report's check-and-move stays atomic while different reports proceed in parallel
  3. It makes the code compile faster
Smallest correct lock scope: the race is per report, so the lock is per report.

Why use BigDecimal or Decimal for the subscription prices?

  1. They are faster than double
  2. Interviewers require them by rule
  3. Binary floating point cannot represent most cents exactly, so totals and rounding drift
Money needs exact decimal arithmetic and an explicit rounding mode.
