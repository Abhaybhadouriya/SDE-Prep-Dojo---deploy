Core flow: an ambulance from the east

  1. The emergency receiver reports a vehicle from the east while NS is green. The controller takes its lock and hands the event to the current state object.
  2. GreenState sees that EAST is not in its phase. It queues the emergency and switches to YELLOW. The NS signals go yellow; the conflict monitor sees one live phase.
  3. Three ticks later the yellow time is over. AllRedState turns NS red. Nothing is green or yellow anywhere: cars already in the junction get out.
  4. Two ticks later AllRedState ends. It polls the emergency queue, finds EAST, and selects the phase that contains it (EW).
  5. EmergencyState turns EW green and holds it until cleared, but never longer than maxEmergencySeconds (120 s by default). The ambulance passes. A second emergency from the north now would be queued.
  6. When the vehicle has passed, emergencyCleared moves to YELLOW for EW, then ALL_RED, then the next normal phase (or the next queued emergency). If the "cleared" message is lost, the max-preempt timer does the same and logs a fault: real controllers have this timer so that one lost radio message cannot stall the junction forever.

The response time is bounded: at most yellow + all-red (5 s here) from the request to green, and zero if the vehicle's road is already green. Say this number in the interview; it is the property the emergency services care about.
