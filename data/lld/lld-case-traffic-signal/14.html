Evaluator rubric checklist

  • State pattern for modes, Strategy for timing, and you can say why they are separate.
  • Safety by construction: every phase change goes through yellow and all-red; the signal refuses unsafe changes.
  • Emergency override that is still safe, with a stated worst-case response time and a plan for two emergencies.
  • Adaptive timing with bounds (min and max green), so no road starves.
  • Fault mode and a clean restart.
  • Testable time: injected ticks, no sleeps, no Singleton timer.
  • Separation: signal, phase, controller, states, strategies, listeners; no I/O in the domain.
  • Thread safety explained (one lock or an event queue) and implemented.
  • Extensible to left turns, schedules, pedestrian countdown and coordination.
