Common mistakes

  • Booleans instead of states: isEmergency, isFault, isYellow checked in every method, and combinations nobody thought of.
  • Emergency jumps straight to green for the ambulance's road, skipping yellow and all-red.
  • Timing hard-coded inside the states, so "adjust to traffic" means editing the state machine.
  • Thread.sleep inside the domain, or a Singleton timer, so tests take minutes.
  • Adaptive timing without a max, which starves the minor road at rush hour.
  • Modelling each light as an independent thread with its own timer, which can drift into conflicting greens.
  • No fault mode and no answer for two emergencies.
