Clarify the requirements

Functional (P0)

  1. Junction Four approaches (north, south, east, west) grouped into phases that may be green together: NS and EW.
  2. Cycle Each phase: GREEN for its green time, YELLOW for 3 s, then ALL RED for 2 s (clearance), then the next phase.
  3. Safety Never two phases green or yellow at once. A signal never goes from GREEN straight to RED, or from YELLOW back to GREEN.
  4. Timing Fixed green time, or adaptive: long enough to clear the longest queue in the phase, between a minimum and a maximum.
  5. Pedestrians A WALK button makes the next green of that phase last at least the walk time.
  6. Emergency override An emergency vehicle from direction d gets green as soon as it is safe: hold green if d is already green, else yellow then all-red then green for d, held until cleared, with a maximum preemption time (a lost "cleared" message must not stall the junction). Several emergencies are served in order.
  7. Fault mode On a fault every signal flashes; on repair: all red, then restart the cycle.

P1

  • Protected left-turn phases; time-of-day plans (rush hour, night); coordination of several junctions ("green wave"); central monitoring.

Non-functional

  • Safety first: the design makes an unsafe light combination impossible, and an independent monitor checks it.
  • Deterministic and testable: time comes in as tick() calls, so a test can run an hour of traffic in a millisecond.
  • Extensible: a new timing rule, mode or phase layout is a new class or a configuration, not an edit of every method.
  • Thread safe: the clock, the vehicle sensors, the pedestrian button and the emergency receiver are separate inputs.

Out of scope (say it)

Hardware drivers for lamps and loops, city-wide optimisation, cameras and fines, a UI. Sensors are just calls to arrive(direction, cars).

Questions to ask

  • How many roads and which movements may run together? Left turns protected?
  • Fixed times or adaptive? What data do we get (queue counts, arrival rates), and how often?
  • Yellow and all-red durations? Minimum and maximum green?
  • Pedestrian crossings: a button per crossing, or every cycle?
  • Emergency: from which signal (radio, siren detector)? What if two arrive from crossing roads?
  • What happens on a power or lamp failure? (Flashing mode.)
  • One junction, or many coordinated ones?
