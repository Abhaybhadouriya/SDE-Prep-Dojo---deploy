  • Two levels of state: signal colours (enum with guarded transitions) and controller modes (State pattern).
  • Every phase change goes GREEN, YELLOW, ALL_RED, next GREEN; the emergency path too.
  • Timing is a Strategy: fixed, adaptive (clamp between min and max green), schedule.
  • Emergency: hold if already green, else clear safely; worst case = yellow + all-red; queue the rest.
  • Fault: everything flashes; repair goes through all-red and restarts the cycle.
  • Time is injected with tick(); no sleeps, no Singleton timer.
  • An Observer conflict monitor and a random test prove safety.
