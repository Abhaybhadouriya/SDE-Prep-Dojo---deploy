  • GRASP answers "which class owns this method?" with nine principles in three groups: who (Expert, Creator, Controller), judge (Low Coupling, High Cohesion), change (Polymorphism, Pure Fabrication, Indirection, Protected Variations).
  • Information Expert first: the class with the data does the work. Feature Envy and a.getB().getC() chains mean you skipped it.
  • Creator: whoever contains, records, closely uses or has the init data for X builds X. Complex creation goes to a factory.
  • Controller coordinates; it does not compute. A bloated controller is a god class.
  • Pure Fabrication keeps storage, time, IDs and vendor calls out of domain classes - but do not drain all logic into services.
  • Protected Variations = a stable interface at each predicted point of change; Indirection (interface + adapter) is how.
  • Kernel analogues: sk_buff helpers (Expert), syscall entry (Controller), file_operations (Polymorphism), tcp_congestion_ops (Protected Variations), slab caches (Fabrication).
