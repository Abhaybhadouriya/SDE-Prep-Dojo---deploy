Our running example: a parking lot

The parking lot is one of the most asked LLD questions (see the full case study). We keep it small here so the reasoning stays visible:

  • A lot has floors; a floor has spots of size SMALL, MEDIUM or LARGE.
  • Bikes fit any spot, cars need MEDIUM or bigger, trucks need LARGE.
  • On entry the gate gives a ticket; on exit the driver pays by the hour (rounded up), then the spot is freed.
  • Pricing rules and payment providers will change (weekend flat rate, surge pricing, new wallets).

Here is the finished model, with each class coloured by the GRASP principle that justified it. The rest of the page explains every colour.

Read the diagram as a list of decisions. canFit() is on ParkingSpot, not on ParkingLot. minutesParked() is on Ticket, not on the controller. There is a TicketRepository even though no real parking lot has one. Each of those choices has a GRASP reason, and you should be able to say it.
