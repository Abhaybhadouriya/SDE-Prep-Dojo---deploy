4 and 5. Low Coupling and High Cohesion: the two judges

These two are not about "who" - they are how you judge any candidate assignment.

  • Coupling = how much a class knows about and depends on other classes. Low coupling means a change in one class rarely forces changes in others, and you can test a class alone.
  • Cohesion = how closely the responsibilities inside one class belong together. High cohesion means a class has one clear purpose you can say in a sentence.

Use them as a tie-breaker. Two places could hold findSpot(): the controller (it already knows the lot) or the floor (it owns the spots). Putting it on the controller adds a dependency from the controller to ParkingSpot and makes the controller less focused. Putting it on the floor adds no new links. Low Coupling and High Cohesion both vote for the floor - and so does Expert.
