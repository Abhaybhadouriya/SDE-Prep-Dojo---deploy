When the principles disagree

GRASP principles pull against each other. Knowing the common conflicts, and which side usually wins, is what separates an SDE-2 answer from an SDE-1 answer.

ConflictExampleUsual winner and why
Expert vs High CohesionTicket has all the data to save itself or print a receiptCohesion: storage and formatting go to fabrications (TicketRepository, ReceiptPrinter), because they change for different reasons (SRP).
Expert vs Low CouplingSpot could compute the fee since it knows its size, but it would need to know pricing rulesCoupling: keep the fee in the pricing policy and pass it the ticket.
Indirection vs simplicityAn interface for Floor with one implementationSimplicity (YAGNI). Add indirection only where change is predicted or testing needs a seam.
Controller vs cohesionOne facade controller with 15 operationsSplit into use-case controllers once it stops being explainable in one sentence.
Creator vs complex creationThe lot creating vehicles of 10 types from a config fileA factory (pure fabrication) takes over creation.

A 4-step routine for any new method

  1. Name the responsibility in plain words: "compute the fee for a ticket at exit time".
  2. Ask Expert: which classes hold the data? If one, start there. If several, split the job between them.
  3. Check the judges: does this add a new dependency? Does the class still have one purpose? If either fails, consider a fabrication.
  4. Ask about change: will this rule vary by type or over time? If yes, put it behind polymorphism or an interface (Protected Variations).

GRASP versus SOLID and GoF

They fit together, from general to concrete. GRASP is the reasoning about who should own what. SOLID gives rules about the shape of classes and their dependencies (SRP is close to High Cohesion; OCP and DIP are kinds of Protected Variations). GoF patterns are named, reusable structures that apply them: Strategy is Polymorphism plus Protected Variations, Adapter is Indirection, Facade is a facade Controller, Repository and Factory are Pure Fabrications, Observer gives Low Coupling between publisher and subscribers.
