The story: a town without signboards

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine an old town where no shop has a sign. Nobody writes "Bakery" over the door. But every morning there is a shop that smells of bread, has an oven at the back and sells loaves through a window. Everyone in town knows it is the bakery.

Then a visitor arrives with a guidebook that lists "Bakery", "Post office", "Pharmacy". At first the visitor is lost, because there are no signs. After a walk around, the visitor laughs: every shop in the guidebook is right here; they just never put names on them.

The Linux kernel is that town, and the pattern catalogue is the guidebook. You, as a kernel or networking engineer, are the local who already knows every shop. This page simply puts the signboards up, so that in an interview you can say "this is the Observer pattern, and I've used it as register_netdevice_notifier()".
