Remember this

  • DRY = one authoritative place for each piece of knowledge; coincidental duplication is fine; rule of three; the wrong abstraction is worse than a copy.
  • KISS = the simplest design that works; simple is not the same as easy; in machine coding: in-memory, plain classes, wiring in main().
  • YAGNI = no presumptive features (costs: build, delay, carry, repair); it does not excuse skipping tests or refactoring.
  • Law of Demeter = call only this, parameters, objects you create, and your own fields; fix chains with Tell, Don't Ask / Hide Delegate.
  • Fluent builders and streams are not Demeter violations; 30 forwarding methods is the middle-man smell.
  • They conflict: decide by which cost is largest (change, reading, carrying, coupling); add seams on evidence.
  • Ask-then-act from outside is also a race; tell the owner to do it atomically (like kref_put).
