Common mistakes

  • Delete that misses postings, so removed documents come back in results.
  • Different tokenizers at index time and query time ("Pool," indexed, "pool" searched).
  • One giant if chain on nullable filter parameters inside search().
  • Sorting inside the index or hard-coding the order, so the "now sort by date" follow-up means a rewrite.
  • No tie-breaker, so pages repeat or skip items.
  • Deleting sold listings when the statement wants them in shortlists.
  • Doubles or strings for money and size; parse units once at the edge into integers.
  • Updating the store and the index in two separate locked steps, so readers see half an update.
  • Asking the interviewer for "future features" instead of building seams: the 2025 Razorpay rejection notes say the interviewer wanted the basics done cleanly first.
