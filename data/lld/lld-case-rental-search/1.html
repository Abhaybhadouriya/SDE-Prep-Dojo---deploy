How it is asked

Before you start: this case assumes OOP from C structs (objects as structs with an ops table) and interfaces vs abstract classes. The patterns it uses are explained on their own pages: Specification, Strategy, Builder, Facade. The locking uses read-write locks.

Search shows up in three shapes. Listen for which one you have:

  • Listing search with structured filters (Flipkart "Property Hunt", NoBroker-style rentals, car or bike rental search): location, price range, size range, BHK, type, sort, shortlist. The test is clean, composable filters.
  • In-memory text search engine (Razorpay, asked again and again): add and delete documents, search words, order by frequency or by created/updated time. The test is the inverted index and, especially, delete.
  • Search with ranking (Microsoft, Amazon, PhonePe): prefix matches rank above substring matches, promoted products first, incremental updates without a rebuild.

In the 311 interview reports we collected for 2024-06 to 2026-11, "in-memory search engine" was named 4 times (all Razorpay), "search with ranking" twice (Microsoft, Amazon) and "property listing" once (Flipkart). Merged, that makes it a rank 36 problem overall but the single most repeated Razorpay machine-coding question. About NoBroker: it is the product this page is named after (rental listings with locality, rent and BHK filters), but the NoBroker interview reports we found asked other things (Java basics, BookMyShow, LinkedIn), so we do not claim NoBroker asks it.

Read the rejection notes in the table. Nobody failed on the algorithm. They failed on delete not working, code that was not modular or extensible, missing validation and misreading the problem. This page is built around those four.
