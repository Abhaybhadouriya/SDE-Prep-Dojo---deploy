  • Every search is: candidates from the most selective index, filter with Specifications, rank with a Strategy, cut a page.
  • Inverted index = term to (id, weight), plus a forward map so remove is exact.
  • AND search intersects from the shortest posting list.
  • Filters compose (and/or/not); new filter = new Spec. Orders are comparators ending with the id.
  • Sold is a status, not a delete. Units are parsed to integers at the edge.
  • One read-write lock keeps store and indexes in step; immutable listings let you sort outside it.
  • Promotions and boosts belong in ranking, never in matching.
