Level 4: threads, testing and cost

  • Threads. Concrete factories are normally stateless and immutable, so one instance can be shared by every thread. Choose the family once at start-up, not per request.
  • Testing. One FakeGatewayFactory swaps the whole family for fakes in one line. Add a family-consistency test: every concrete factory returns parts that work together.
  • Cost. One virtual call per product. The known weakness is change cost: adding a new product kind means a new method on every factory (N edits).
