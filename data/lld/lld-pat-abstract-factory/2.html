Intent: one object that makes a consistent family

Abstract Factory is one of the 23 "Gang of Four" (GoF) patterns from the 1994 book Design Patterns. Its intent, in our words:

Give the client one interface with a creation method for each kind of product. Each concrete factory implements that interface for one family. The client picks a factory once and then only talks to interfaces, so the whole family can be swapped at once and parts from different families can never mix.

Three words to keep straight:

WordMeaningIn our example
Product kindA role the client needs. One abstract interface each.Charger, Refunder, WebhookVerifier
FamilyA set of concrete products that belong together.AcmePay classes, ZenPay classes
FactoryThe object that makes every kind for one family.AcmePayFactory, ZenPayFactory

Think of a grid: product kinds are the columns, families are the rows. A concrete factory is one row. The client holds a row and asks it for columns.
