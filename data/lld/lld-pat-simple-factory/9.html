The pairing "Factory + Strategy" is the most common combo in LLD rounds: the config says "HOURLY", the factory returns an HourlyFeeStrategy, and the parking lot calls strategy.fee(ticket). The factory answers "which one", the strategy answers "how". More pairs on Look-Alike Patterns.
