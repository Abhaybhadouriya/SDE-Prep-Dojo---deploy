Structure: who knows whom

The single most useful thing to notice in this picture: the client box has no line to any concrete class. That is the whole point. If you can draw this in the interview, the interviewer knows you understand why the factory exists.

Participants:

  1. Product (Vehicle) - the interface or abstract base. Callers program to this.
  2. Concrete products (Bike, Car, Truck) - hidden behind the factory. Their constructors can even be package-private.
  3. Factory (VehicleFactory) - one creation method; either a switch on the key or a lookup in a map of maker functions.
  4. Client - passes a key, gets a Vehicle, never names a concrete class.
