Clarify the requirements

Spend 5 minutes here. The library sounds familiar, which is exactly why candidates skip clarifying and then build the wrong reservation rule.

Functional (P0: must work in the demo)

  1. Catalogue Add books (isbn, title, author, subject) and copies (barcode, rack). Search by any part of title, author or subject, showing how many copies are free.
  2. Members Student or faculty. Students may hold 2 books for 14 days; faculty 5 books for 30 days.
  3. Borrow Picks a free copy (or the copy held for this member). Rejected when the member owes dues, is at the limit, already holds this title, or no copy is free.
  4. Return Computes the fine by member type and adds it to dues. If someone is waiting, the copy goes on hold for them and they are notified; otherwise it goes back on the shelf.
  5. Reserve Only when no copy is free; FIFO queue per book; no duplicates; not for a title you already hold.
  6. Renew Once, and only if nobody is waiting for the title.
  7. Holds expire A hold lasts 3 days; then the copy passes to the next person in the queue, or back to the shelf.

Non-functional

  • Correctness first: never lend more copies than exist; never lose a reservation.
  • Extensible: new member types, new fine rules and new notification channels without editing the service.
  • Thread safe: two members borrowing the last copy, a return racing with a borrow.
  • Testable time: the service takes "today" from outside, so tests can jump to day 18 without waiting.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

Login, payment gateway (dues are just a number), barcode scanners, inter-branch transfer, e-books. Name where each would plug in.

Questions to ask

  • Reserve a book (any copy) or a specific copy? (A book. Copies are interchangeable to members.)
  • Can you reserve when a copy is on the shelf? (No: borrow it.)
  • What happens to a returned copy when someone is waiting? (Held for them, not shelved.)
  • How long is a hold? What if the member never comes? (3 days, then the next in line.)
  • Do unpaid fines block borrowing? Any amount, or above a threshold? (Any amount, in our version.)
  • Are fines per day, capped, with a grace period? Different for faculty? (Yes, and that is our Strategy.)
