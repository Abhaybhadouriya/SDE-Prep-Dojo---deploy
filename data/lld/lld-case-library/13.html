Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Book and BookCopy separated; search and reservations on the book, loans on the copy.
  • Runs and demos borrow, return, reserve, hold hand-off and fines from a driver, including rejections.
  • Explicit copy states with illegal transitions refused.
  • Rules that vary are pluggable: fines by Strategy, notifications by Observer, limits by member type.
  • All checks before any change: a rejected borrow leaves copies, queues and loans untouched.
  • Time is injected, so fines and expiry are tested deterministically.
  • Thread safety explained: last-copy race handled, notifications outside the lock.
  • Readable code: small methods, clear names, no god class, no Singleton for its own sake.

The same headings appear in the published Flipkart machine-coding guideline (demoable, correct, modular, extensible, error handling): workattech/flipkart-machine-coding-round.
