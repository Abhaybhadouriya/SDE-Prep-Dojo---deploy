Identify the entities (nouns and verbs)

Same method as From use cases to classes: nouns become candidate classes, verbs become methods, and nouns that are only data become fields.

  1. Start from the requirement sentence. Read it twice.
  2. Underline nouns: Member, Book, Copy, Loan, Fine, Reservation, rack, due date.
  3. Underline verbs. They become the facade's methods.
  4. Drop nouns that are only data: the rack is a field of a copy; the due date is a field of a loan.
  5. "Fine" varies by member type, so it becomes a strategy. "Reservation" is just a FIFO queue of member ids per book; a class would add nothing in P0. Notifying becomes a listener interface.

The one modelling choice that decides the round: Book and BookCopy are separate classes. Search, reservation and "already holds this title" work on the Book; borrowing, returning, holding and racks work on the copy. Candidates who put a count field on Book cannot answer "which copy is overdue?" or "which copy is held for Ben?".
