A 4-week practice plan

Assumes about 90 minutes a day on weekdays and a longer mock at the weekend. Each case gets one timed attempt (alone, with a timer), one read of our page, and one re-do a few days later.

WeekCasesFocusWeekend mock
1Tic-Tac-Toe, Snake & Ladder, Vending Machine, ATMEntities in 10 minutes; states drawn before code; a driver with assertions every timeSnake & Ladder as a 90-minute machine-coding round

3BookMyShow, Car Rental, Splitwise, Meeting SchedulerHolds with TTL, pessimistic vs optimistic, money that adds up, interval overlapBookMyShow whiteboard, 45 minutes, with 15 minutes of concurrency questions
4LRU Cache, Logger, Rate Limiter, Pub-Sub, Ride HailingData structures, worker threads, thread-safe policies; your target companies' favouritesA full mock from Mock LLD Interviews

Targeting one company? Swap the last week's list for that company's problems from LLD Rounds by Company. For example Atlassian's code-design round favours rating systems, snake game and routers that grow through follow-ups; PhonePe and Flipkart favour long machine-coding statements like customer-issue resolution and food ordering.
