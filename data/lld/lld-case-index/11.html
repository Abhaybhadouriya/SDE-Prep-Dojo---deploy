Resources

  • workat.tech machine-coding practice course - Flipkart-style statements (Snake & Ladder, Splitwise, Trello, Parking Lot) to practise against a clock.

  • Hello Interview: LLD in a hurry blog - a free overview of the interview and its delivery framework.
  • AlgoMaster LLD roadmap course - a free curriculum whose order matches ours closely.
  • ByteByteGo: Object-Oriented Design Interview book - the paid course's official page; its public table of contents is one of our ranking sources (link only).
  • refactoring.guru pattern catalogue doc - illustrated reference for every pattern named in the table.
  • LeetCode Discuss: Machine Coding Resources blog - a community list of practice problems and repos.
  • LLD Question Bank - 150+ follow-up questions across all cases, filterable by company and level.
