Remember this

  • The most common concurrency ask in LLD rounds is "two requests grab the same thing" (10 of our 38 reports).
  • Five steps: shared state, invariants, granularity, mechanism, prove and test.
  • Check-then-act must become one atomic step: CAS, putIfAbsent, a conditional DB update, or a lock around both.
  • The lock belongs to the invariant: per spot, per seat, per driver, per user.
  • Two resources at once means lock ordering (by id) or all-or-nothing.
  • Pessimistic when conflicts are common, optimistic when they are rare; add holds with a TTL for bookings.
  • Queues and pools need a shutdown protocol; in C++, close, join, then destroy.
  • "Just synchronized" is a starting point, not an answer: say what it costs and how you would shrink it.
