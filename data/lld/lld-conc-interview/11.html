Rapid-fire: one-line answers

QuestionOne-line answer
Process vs thread?A process owns an address space; threads share one address space and each has its own stack and registers.
Is i++ atomic?No: read, add, write. Use an atomic or a lock.
sleep() vs wait()?sleep keeps any locks; wait releases the monitor and needs it held.
What is a reentrant lock?The owner can take it again without deadlocking itself; it must release it as many times.
What does ThreadLocal give you?One copy per thread, so no sharing; leaks in pools if not removed.
Daemon thread?Does not keep the JVM alive; killed abruptly at exit.
Livelock?Threads keep reacting to each other and make no progress (both step aside forever); fix with random back-off.
Starvation?A thread never gets the resource because others always win; fix with fairness or ordering.
Why prefer immutable objects?Nothing to race on; safe to share once published (final fields).
CopyOnWriteArrayList fits when?Many reads, rare writes (listener lists): every write copies the array.
Virtual threads (Java 21)?Cheap threads scheduled by the JVM on a few carrier threads; great for blocking I/O, no help for CPU-bound work.
Spinlock vs mutex?Spin when the hold time is shorter than a context switch and you cannot sleep; otherwise sleep.
