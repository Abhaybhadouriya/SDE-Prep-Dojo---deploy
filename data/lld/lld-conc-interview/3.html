Pessimistic vs optimistic: the question Microsoft and Paytm asked

When state lives in a database, "lock" means a row lock or a version check. A Microsoft SDE2 candidate chose pessimistic locking for the parking lot's concurrency follow-up (LeetCode Discuss, 2025-11-17); a Paytm senior candidate was asked how to prevent double booking in concert ticketing (LeetCode Discuss, 2026-11-11).

PessimisticOptimistic
HowSELECT ... FOR UPDATE, or an in-memory lock per seatVersion column; UPDATE ... WHERE id=? AND version=?; in memory: CAS
Best whenConflicts are common (hot seats, the last item in a flash sale)Conflicts are rare (editing a profile, most seats)
CostWaiting, lock hold time, deadlock risk if two rows are locked in different ordersWasted work and retries under contention

Ticketing extraAdd a temporary hold with a TTL (seat held for a few minutes while the user pays), released by a timer or on payment failure. Deep dive: Preventing Double Booking.
