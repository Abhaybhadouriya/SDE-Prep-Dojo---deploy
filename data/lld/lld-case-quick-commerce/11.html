Extensibility: the follow-ups they ask

Follow-upChange
"Prioritise orders at peak time" (Zepto 2024-09)A priority queue for packing (subscribers or paid-express first, then oldest); a LeastLoadedStoreSelector that weighs distance against the store's open-order count
"Several payment methods: card, wallet, cash on delivery"A PaymentMethod strategy. COD has no payment step: confirm immediately at create (commit right away) and collect at the door
"Split the basket across two stores"Order gets sub-orders per store. Reserve each; if one fails, release the ones already reserved (a mini saga). Usually rejected for 10-minute delivery because it needs two riders
"Subscription orders" (Walmart)A Subscription (basket, schedule) whose scheduler calls createOrder; optionally reserve ahead for tomorrow's delivery slot
"Substitute out-of-stock items"A SubstitutionPolicy consulted when hasAll fails at every store
"Low-stock alerts"Observer on commit: when available drops below a threshold, notify the restock service
"Riders"Per-store Dispatcher with a FIFO of PACKED orders and free riders, as in Food Delivery
"Many app servers"Move counters to the DB (conditional UPDATE above) or Redis (Lua script does check + reserve for all lines atomically)

The HLD side (stock sync from the warehouse system, event streams, regional sharding) is in Quick Commerce system design and Inventory Management.
