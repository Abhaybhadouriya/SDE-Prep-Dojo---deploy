Clarify the requirements

Functional (P0)

  1. Catalog and stores: add products (SKU, price), add dark stores with a location, restock a SKU at a store.
  2. Create an order for a basket (SKU to quantity) at a customer location: pick a store within range that has every line in stock, reserve all lines there atomically, and return the order in CREATED with a hold expiry (for example 5 minutes).
  3. Payment result: success converts the reservation into a deduction (CONFIRMED); failure releases it (CANCELLED). Duplicate success callbacks must not deduct twice.
  4. Timeout: unpaid orders past expiry become EXPIRED and their stock is released.
  5. Fulfilment: PACKED, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED. Cancel before dispatch; cancelling after payment puts the units back on the shelf.
  6. Query available stock per store and SKU, and order status.

Non-functional

  • Never oversell: for every SKU at every store, 0 <= reserved <= onHand at all times.
  • High contention on a few hot SKUs (milk, bread at 8 am); different SKUs must not block each other.
  • No deadlock when baskets list SKUs in different orders.
  • In-memory, testable: time is injected (a clock), so tests can fast-forward expiry.

Out of scope (say it)

Splitting one basket across stores, substitutions, delivery routing, pricing and coupons, real payment integration, persistence. Each is an extension below.
