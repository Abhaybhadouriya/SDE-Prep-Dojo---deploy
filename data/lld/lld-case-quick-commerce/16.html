Common mistakes

  • Quantity on Product instead of per (store, SKU).
  • Check in one call, decrement in another without a lock around both (classic check-then-act).
  • Reserving line by line and leaving earlier lines reserved when a later line fails.
  • Locking SKUs in basket order: deadlock when two baskets list them differently.
  • No expiry, or expiry by Thread.sleep per order (thousands of sleeping threads). Use a sweeper plus a lazy check.
  • Non-idempotent payment handler: duplicate webhooks deduct twice.
  • Cancel that always "releases", regardless of whether the stock was reserved or already deducted, so counters drift.
  • Real time in tests: tests that sleep 5 minutes, or that cannot test expiry at all. Inject a clock.
