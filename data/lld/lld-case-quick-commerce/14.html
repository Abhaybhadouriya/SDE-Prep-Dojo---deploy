Evaluator rubric checklist

  • [ ] Demoable and correct: every P0 flow in the driver, including expiry with a fake clock.
  • [ ] Stock modelled per (store, SKU) with on-hand and reserved kept separate (or a stated alternative).
  • [ ] All-or-nothing reservation for multi-line baskets.
  • [ ] No oversell under concurrency, shown by a thread test.
  • [ ] No deadlock: lock ordering stated and implemented.
  • [ ] Idempotent payment callback; late payment handled.
  • [ ] State machine owns legal transitions; inventory effect chosen by the state left.
  • [ ] Extension points: store selection strategy, clock; no hard-coded "nearest" inside the service.
  • [ ] Readable: named exceptions, small methods, comments on the invariants.
