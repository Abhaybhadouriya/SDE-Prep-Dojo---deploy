How it is asked

At Zepto it is a 60-minute whiteboard LLD, usually run by a third-party interviewer, that expects a class diagram, APIs and a database schema, with requirements added midway. Flipkart runs it as a 90-120 minute machine-coding round (Flipkart Minutes). Meesho asks the inventory half as machine coding with explicit concurrency. Walmart asked a grocery variant with subscriptions.

What they are really testing: whether you separate physical stock from promised stock, and whether your reserve is all-or-nothing and race-free. A design that decrements stock only at payment oversells; one that decrements at create and forgets to release leaks stock until the store looks empty.

The rider half of the problem (assign to a free partner, queue when none is free) is the same Dispatcher you build in Food Delivery; this page focuses on inventory, which is where the hard questions are.
