  • Stock belongs to (store, SKU): onHand and reserved; available = onHand - reserved; invariant 0 <= reserved <= onHand.
  • Reserve at create, commit at payment success, release on failure, cancel-before-payment or expiry; return to shelf on cancel after payment.
  • Multi-SKU reservation: lock in sorted SKU order, check all, then reserve all.
  • Payment, cancel and expiry race through one guarded state transition; only the winner touches inventory.
  • Expiry = sweeper + lazy check; time comes from an injected Clock.
  • Store selection is a Strategy with a retry loop, because the availability read is only a hint.
