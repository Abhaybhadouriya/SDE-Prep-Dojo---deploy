Identify entities

  1. Nouns. The interesting one is hidden: "stock" is not a field of Product, because the same SKU has different stock in every store. It is its own class, keyed by (store, SKU).
  2. Verbs. "Block", "deduct" and "release" are three different operations on two counters. Name them exactly; interviewers listen for whether you have three operations or one.
  3. What varies: the store-selection rule (nearest, least loaded, cheapest delivery), the lifecycle, and time itself. An injected Clock turns "after 5 minutes" into a unit test that runs in milliseconds.
  4. The modelling move that unlocks the rest: StockItem{onHand, reserved} per store and SKU, with available = onHand - reserved derived, never stored.
