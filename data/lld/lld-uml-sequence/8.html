Remember this

  • Participants across the top are objects (:Type); lifelines go down; time flows down.
  • Filled head = synchronous call (caller waits); open head = asynchronous (caller continues); dashed = reply.
  • Activation bars show who is busy, and who is blocked waiting.
  • Fragments: alt = if/else, opt = if, loop = for/while, break = early exit, par = parallel. Guards go in [brackets].
  • Draw one use case, happy path first, then the one or two failure branches that matter.
  • Every arrow must match a method on the receiver and a reference held by the sender: the diagram checks your class design.
  • RFC ladder diagrams (TCP handshake) and ftrace call graphs are sequence diagrams you already read.
