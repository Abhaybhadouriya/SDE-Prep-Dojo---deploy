Structure: one final method, abstract steps and hooks

  • AbstractClass (Importer): holds the template method run(), which calls the steps in order. It is final so no subclass can reorder or skip steps.
  • Abstract steps (parse): no default; every subclass must provide one. These are sometimes called primitive operations.
  • Hooks (isValid, afterSave): have a default (often "do nothing"); subclasses override them only if they care.
  • Invariant steps (save): private or final; the same for everyone.
  • ConcreteClass (CsvImporter, KeyValueImporter): fills in the steps it needs.

Keep the number of abstract steps small. Each abstract step is a duty for every subclass. If a subclass has to implement five steps and three of them are empty, turn those three into hooks with defaults.
