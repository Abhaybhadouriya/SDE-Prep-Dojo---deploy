  • Factory Method is often a step inside a template method: the skeleton calls createX() and the subclass decides which object to create.
  • Bridge: the abstraction's high-level methods call low-level primitives, like a template method, but the primitives come from a separate object (composition), so they can change at runtime.
  • Chain of Responsibility: the chain walk itself is often a final template method in the base handler (see the logger in that page).
  • Composition over inheritance: the general principle behind "prefer Strategy when variations multiply".
