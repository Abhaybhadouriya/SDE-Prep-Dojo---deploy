A naming trap: Spring's JdbcTemplate and RestTemplate are called "templates", but you pass them callbacks instead of subclassing them. They fix the skeleton (open connection, run, map rows, close, translate errors) like Template Method, but plug the variable part in by composition, which is closer to Strategy. It is a good example to bring up when the interviewer asks you to compare the two.
