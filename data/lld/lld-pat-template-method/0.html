The story: the cake recipe card

Explain like I'm 5

Grandma has one recipe card for every cake: 1. mix flour and eggs, 2. add the flavour, 3. bake 30 minutes, 4. decorate if you want. The card never changes. Step 2 is a blank: chocolate cake puts cocoa there, lemon cake puts lemon. Step 4 says "if you want": a plain cake skips it.

Nobody is allowed to bake first and mix later. The order belongs to the card. You only fill in the blanks.

The card is the template method: a fixed list of steps in a fixed order. The blanks are abstract steps (you must fill them). "If you want" steps are hooks (you may fill them; there is a default). Each cake is a subclass.

From your world

When you write a PCI network driver, you do not write the code that scans the bus, matches device IDs, creates sysfs entries and handles a failed probe. The driver core does that, in a fixed order, and calls your probe() at the right moment. You fill in the blanks (probe, remove, the net_device_ops); the framework owns the skeleton. "Don't call us, we'll call you": that is the idea behind Template Method.
