Intent

Mediator (GoF behavioral pattern): put one coordinator object in the middle of a group of objects that would otherwise all talk to each other. Each member reports to the coordinator and hears back from it, never from the others, so the rules of who reacts to what live in one place and can change without touching the members.

Two things move into the mediator: the references (who can reach whom) and the coordination logic (what should happen when one colleague does something). Colleagues keep their own behaviour, such as how a bidder decides to bid or how a plane flies, but they no longer decide how others react.
