Structure: four participants

  • Mediator (AuctionMediator): the interface colleagues call. Keep it small: a few "something happened" methods.
  • ConcreteMediator (AuctionHouse): knows all colleagues, keeps shared state (current price, leader), and decides who gets told what.
  • Colleague (Bidder): does its own job and reports events to the mediator instead of calling peers.
  • ConcreteColleagues: different colleague types can share one mediator (in a dialog: a checkbox, a text field and a button).

The interface is optional when there is only one mediator, but it makes colleagues testable with a fake mediator and lets you swap rules (an English auction vs a sealed-bid auction) without touching Bidder.
