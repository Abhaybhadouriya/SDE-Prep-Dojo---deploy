MediatorBoth ways: colleagues report to it, it tells colleagues what to doThe mediator coordinates"Peers talk through a hub that decides."
ObserverOne way: subject to subscribersEach subscriber decides how to react; the subject just announces"Broadcast, no coordination."
FacadeOne way: client into a subsystemSubsystem classes do not know the facade exists"Simpler door into a subsystem."
Event bus / pub-subPublishers to topics to subscribersNobody; the bus only routes"A mediator with no rules."
Chain of ResponsibilityAlong a line of handlersEach handler decides to handle or pass on"Pass it down the line."

The Mediator vs Observer line is the one interviewers push on. A mediator is often implemented with observer-style callbacks (colleagues subscribe to the mediator), but the difference is intent: Observer lets one object announce to many without caring what they do; Mediator owns the rules of how a group cooperates. Facade differs because the subsystem behind it is unaware of it and traffic is one way. See look-alike patterns for more pairs.
