When to use it, when not to

Use it when
  • Several objects interact in many-to-many ways and the wiring is hard to follow.
  • You want to reuse a component (a bidder, a widget, a driver) in another context without its peers.
  • The coordination rules change more often than the parts (auction formats, form validation, runway rules).
  • There is a shared resource or shared state that one owner should guard.
Avoid or watch out when
  • God object. The mediator grows until it holds everyone's logic. Keep colleague behaviour in colleagues; split one mediator per use case (bidding, payment, shipping).
  • Only two objects talk: a direct call is simpler.
  • Communication is a simple one-way broadcast with no rules: Observer or an event bus is enough.
  • Single point of failure and bottleneck: in a distributed system, the hub needs replicas, timeouts and back-pressure.
