How it works: one bid, step by step

Asha leads at 1000. Ravi bids 1100. Watch who talks to whom:

  1. Ravi does not look for the other bidders. He tells the only object he knows, the auction house, that he wants to bid 1100.
  2. The mediator applies the rules in one place: the auction is open, someone leads, and the bid beats price plus the increment.
  3. The shared state lives only in the mediator, so it cannot drift out of sync between bidders.
  4. The previous leader gets a targeted message: "you were outbid". The mediator decides who gets which message.
  5. Everyone else gets a different message: the new price. Ravi gets nothing because his bid succeeded.
  6. Adding a fourth bidder, a sealed-bid rule or an anti-sniping timer changes only the mediator.
