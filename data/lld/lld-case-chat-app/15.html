Common mistakes

  • Drawing WebSockets and Cassandra in an LLD round, or classes in an HLD round. Ask first.
  • One status field on Message, so group receipts are impossible.
  • Ordering by System.currentTimeMillis(): ties and clock skew.
  • A separate offline queue per user that can drift from the receipts; "pending" should simply be "receipt is SENT".
  • Pushing to devices under the chat lock, or under one global lock for the whole server.
  • Receipts that can go backwards when a late DELIVERED arrives after READ.
  • if (chat.type == GROUP) in every method instead of one overridden rule.
  • Forgetting the removed member, so a group message never reaches READ, and forgetting the late joiner, so new members read old secrets.
