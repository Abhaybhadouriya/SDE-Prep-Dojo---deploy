Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Asked LLD versus HLD and scoped transport, storage and scale out loud.
  • Runs and demos 1:1 and group sends, offline delivery, ticks, late join, admin rules and paging from a driver, including rejections.
  • Receipt per recipient with a forward-only state machine; overall status derived, not stored.
  • Ordering by a per-chat sequence number, assigned under the chat's lock; history paged by seq.
  • Mediator and Observer used for a reason: users never reference each other; devices are pluggable.
  • Direct and group chats differ only where they must (one overridden rule), no type switches.
  • Thread safety explained: lock per chat, fan-out after unlock, at-least-once push with device ack and dedupe, no callbacks under locks.
  • Readable code: small methods, clear errors, no god class, no Singleton.

The same headings appear in the published Flipkart machine-coding guideline (demoable, correct, modular, extensible, error handling): workattech/flipkart-machine-coding-round.
