Identify the entities (nouns and verbs)

Same method as From use cases to classes: nouns become candidate classes, verbs become methods, and nouns that are only data become fields or enum values.

  1. Start from the requirement sentence. Read it twice.
  2. Underline nouns: User, Message, Chat, Group, Device, Tick, Admin, online, member.
  3. Underline verbs. They become the server's methods, because every one of them involves more than one user.
  4. Drop nouns that are only data: "admin" is a set inside a group; "online" is the server's map of connected devices; "member" is a map from user to the sequence number they joined at.
  5. "Tick" becomes the Receipt state per recipient. "Device" becomes the Observer interface. Direct and group chats share most behaviour but differ in membership rules, so they are two subclasses of one Chat.

The modelling choice that decides the round: the receipt lives on the message, one per recipient, not as one status field. A single field cannot answer "who in the group has not read it yet?", and it breaks the moment a second recipient exists.
