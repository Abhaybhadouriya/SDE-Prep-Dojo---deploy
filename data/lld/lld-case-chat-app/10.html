The storage follow-up has a neat answer worth memorising: in production you rarely store a receipt per message per member. You store, per member per chat, two numbers: deliveredUpTo and readUpTo. A message's tick for that member is derived by comparing its seq. That works only because sequence numbers are dense and ordered, which is why we insisted on them.
