Remember this

  • DI = a class receives its dependencies instead of creating or looking them up.
  • DIP is the principle, IoC the broad idea, DI the technique, Spring/Guice/Dagger the tools.
  • Default to constructor injection with final fields; setters only for optional dependencies.
  • One composition root (usually main) knows concrete classes and builds the graph bottom-up.
  • Inject the clock, randomness and ID generators: they are the hidden dependencies that break tests.
  • Service locator pulls from a global registry: hidden dependencies, late failures. ServiceLoader is a plug-in locator for the edges.
  • In interviews: wire by hand, mention that a container would automate it.
  • Kernel bridge: probe() receives the device; drivers register ops tables and the core calls them.
