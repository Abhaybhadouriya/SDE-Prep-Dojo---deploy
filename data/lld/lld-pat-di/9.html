From your world

You have already written injectable C: a function that takes const struct net_ops *ops instead of calling e1000_xmit() directly can be unit-tested by passing a table of fake functions. The kernel's "Hollywood principle" is everywhere: you register callbacks (module_init, probe, notifier chains, ndo_*) and the core calls you when it decides. That is Inversion of Control; DI is the narrower habit of passing in what a function needs rather than reaching for globals.
