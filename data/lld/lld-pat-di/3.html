Structure: the class, its abstractions, and the composition root

  • Client / service (OrderService): declares what it needs, usually as constructor parameters typed by interfaces.
  • Abstractions (PaymentGateway, Notifier, Clock): the contracts.
  • Implementations: real ones for production, fakes for tests.
  • Injector / composition root: creates implementations, decides lifetimes (one shared repository, a new request object per call) and connects the graph. By hand in main() ("pure DI"), or by a container.
