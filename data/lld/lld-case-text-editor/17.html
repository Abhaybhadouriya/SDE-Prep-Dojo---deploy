Remember this

  • Store the change, not the world: every edit is a Command with an exact inverse.
  • Two stacks; a new edit clears redo; each entry also saves the cursor.
  • Gap buffer for normal files (O(1) at the cursor), rope or piece table for huge ones.
  • Up and down need a goal column; page keys are N vertical moves that stop at the ends.
  • Compound edits are a MacroCommand applied right to left and undone in reverse.
  • Group typed characters per word; break the group on a move or undo.
  • Memento for checkpoints, Flyweight for glyphs, OT or CRDT only when collaboration is asked.
