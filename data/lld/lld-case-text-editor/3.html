Identify the entities (nouns and verbs)

  1. Describe the whole editor in two sentences, including what the screen does.
  2. Underline the nouns: user, text, cursor, document, edit, history, glyph.
  3. Underline the verbs. Type, delete and replace change the text; moves change only the cursor; undo and redo walk the history; draw is rendering.
  4. Reshape the nouns. The user is an actor. "Text" needs a storage structure (the gap buffer). The cursor is just an offset kept by the editor. An "edit" becomes an object with execute and undo. "History" is two stacks of those objects.
  5. The final list. Moves are deliberately not commands: they never enter the history. Replace-all is a Macro (composite) of smaller commands, so it undoes in one step.
