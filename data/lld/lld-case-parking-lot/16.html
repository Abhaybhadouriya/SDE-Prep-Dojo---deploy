  • Parking Lot is rank 1: 17 reports in 2024-26 across Amazon, Microsoft, Uber, Salesforce, Swiggy, Razorpay, Oracle and Walmart.
  • Entities: ParkingLot (service), Level (owns free sets), ParkingSpot, Vehicle, Ticket, Receipt; enums for size and type.
  • Two strategies: allocation (nearest-first, best-fit) and pricing (hourly, peak). Follow-ups become new classes.
  • A Simple Factory turns input strings into vehicles and pricing rules; Observer feeds display boards.
  • Concurrency: make "find and take" one atomic step; claim the plate with putIfAbsent; consume a ticket with remove(key, value).
  • Database follow-up: pessimistic with SKIP LOCKED under high contention, optimistic version checks when conflicts are rare.
  • Pass time in and avoid a global Singleton, so the design is testable.
