In a 60-minute "design and code" round (Microsoft, Uber), compress it: 8 minutes of questions, 7 minutes of class diagram, 35 minutes of code for park/unpark/fee with one strategy each, 10 minutes for the concurrency and extension discussion. The Oracle candidate who coded everything in the last 20 minutes shows why the diagram must be quick.
