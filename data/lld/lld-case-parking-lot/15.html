Common mistakes

  1. Drawing for 30 minutes, coding for 10 Microsoft and Uber want running code. Keep the diagram to the boxes above and start coding by minute 15.
  2. A class per vehicle and a class per spot size with no behaviour Six empty subclasses read as over-engineering. Use enums with data.
  3. A boolean isFree on the spot plus a free list on the level Two sources of truth drift apart. Keep one.
  4. Scanning all spots on every park Works for 20 spots, not for 5,000. Keep free sets per size.
  5. Pricing inside unpark with if/else The Amazon follow-up then means editing the core. Use a strategy.
  6. Calling LocalDateTime.now() inside the service Tests cannot control time. Pass time in, or inject a Clock.
  7. "I will add synchronized" as the whole concurrency answer Name the race, the shared state, the granularity and the alternative.
  8. Forgetting the error paths Full lot, double exit and duplicate plate are where graders look first.
  9. A global Singleton by reflex It costs testability; mention it, then inject.
