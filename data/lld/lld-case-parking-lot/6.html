Core flows

Park: from gate to ticket

Unpark: from ticket to receipt (step through it)

  1. The exit gate hands in ticket T2 and the exit time.
  2. The lot looks up the ticket, rejects an exit time before entry, then removes the ticket with remove(id, ticket). That call is atomic: if two gates race with the same ticket, exactly one wins.
  3. The fee strategy prices the stay. The lot does not know or care whether it is hourly or peak pricing.
  4. The spot goes back into its level's free set. If it was already there, that is a bug, and release throws.
  5. Listeners hear about the exit; the display board refreshes its counts.
  6. The gate gets a receipt. The plate claim is removed, so the same car may enter again later.
