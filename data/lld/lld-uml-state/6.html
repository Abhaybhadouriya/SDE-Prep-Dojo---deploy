Activity diagrams: when the steps matter more than the state

A state diagram follows one object through its life. An activity diagram follows one process through its steps, possibly across many objects and in parallel. It is a flowchart with two extra powers: fork/join bars for parallel work and swimlanes that show who does each step.

This picture makes a design point that is hard to see elsewhere: payment and stock reservation run in parallel, so either can fail while the other succeeded, and the failure path must compensate (refund the charge, release the stock). That is the same idea as the saga pattern in distributed systems.
