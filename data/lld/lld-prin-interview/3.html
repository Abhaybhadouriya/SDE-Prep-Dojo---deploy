The five-beat answer

Use it for every principle question. Example for ISP, in about a minute: (Define) "Clients shouldn't depend on methods they don't use." (Smell) "You see it when an implementer writes empty or throwing methods." (Fix) "Split into role interfaces - Printer, Scanner - and let a multifunction device implement both." (Trade-off) "Don't go to one method per interface; split along how clients actually use it." (Example) "The kernel's file_operations does the C version: optional ops are NULL, and the VFS checks before calling."
