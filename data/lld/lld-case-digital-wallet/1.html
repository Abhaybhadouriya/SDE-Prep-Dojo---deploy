The Groww rejection is a warning: "it works" is the floor, not the bar. What lifts a wallet answer is the invariants you can prove (money conserved, each key applied once, ledger replays to the balance) and the failure stories you can tell (gateway timeout, retry, partial failure, reversal after the receiver spent the money).
