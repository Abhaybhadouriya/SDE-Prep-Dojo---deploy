  • Every movement is a transfer with two legs; the outside world is a system wallet. Sum of all balances = 0.
  • Append-only ledger; balance is a projection; reversal is a new opposite transaction.
  • Idempotency key registered atomically first; repeats get the original; conflicting repeats are rejected.
  • Transfer under both wallet locks in id order: no lost update, no half transfer, no deadlock.
  • Gateways: our interface + one Adapter per vendor; a router (Strategy) picks one by method and rules.
  • Unknown gateway outcome stays PENDING; retry with the same key; reconcile.
