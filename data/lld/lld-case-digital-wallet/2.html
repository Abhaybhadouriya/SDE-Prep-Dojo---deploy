Clarify requirements

Functional
  • Create one wallet per user.
  • Deposit (top up) through a payment method: UPI, card (later net banking).
  • Withdraw to bank; transfer between users; debit fails on low balance.
  • Every request carries an idempotency key; a repeat returns the original result.
  • Reverse a transfer (support / dispute flow).
  • Balance and statement, with filter and sort (PhonePe).
  • Rules per payment method: per-transaction limits, method allowed or not (CRED).
Non-functional
  • Correctness over everything: no money created or lost; balances never negative.
  • Exactly-once effect under client retries and duplicate deliveries.
  • Atomic transfers: both legs or neither.
  • Thread-safe; no deadlock on opposite transfers (A to B while B to A).
  • Auditable: an append-only ledger from which every balance can be rebuilt.
  • Money as integer paise; never double.

Out of scope: KYC, real bank integration, multi-currency, interest, cross-shard transfers, reconciliation jobs with the bank (mention them). The distributed version (sharded ledger, sagas, reconciliation) is in HLD: Digital Wallet, UPI & Double-Entry Ledger and HLD: Payment System.

Questions to ask

  1. Wallet only, or the gateway too? Is "top up" a real external call I must model?
  2. Who generates the idempotency key? Usually the client, per user action. What if the same key comes with a different amount? (We reject: conflict.)
  3. Transaction types? Deposit, withdraw, transfer; Groww's EXPENSE/RECEIPT are transfers to and from a merchant or system account.
  4. Can a wallet go negative? User wallets no; system accounts yes.
  5. Reversal rules? Only successful transfers; what if the receiver already spent it? (We fail the reversal and keep the original.)
  6. Limits? Per transaction, per day, per method.
  7. History? Filters (type, date, counterparty) and sorts (amount, date).
