Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Money type: integer paise; no floating point anywhere near balances.
  • Atomic transfer: both legs under both locks; debit can fail, credit cannot.
  • Idempotency: atomic key registration; replay returns the original; conflicting replay rejected.
  • Ledger: append-only, two entries per success, balance rebuildable, conservation tested.
  • Deadlock-free: wallet locks in a global order; lock hierarchy stated.
  • Gateway design: our interface, adapters per vendor, router as strategy; no vendor type leaks into the service.
  • Failure handling: business failures recorded with reasons; unknown outcomes stay PENDING.
  • Reversal: new opposite transaction, idempotent, only for SUCCESS.
  • Working demo + tests including concurrency.
  • Extensibility story: new method, offers, limits, fail-over, without editing post().
