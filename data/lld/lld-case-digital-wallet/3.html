Identify entities

  1. Nouns. A Wallet holds a balance. A Transaction is the request and its outcome. A LedgerEntry is one immutable line in the notebook.
  2. Verbs become service methods. Each money-moving method takes an idempotency key as its first argument: that is part of the contract, not an afterthought.
  3. Payment providers vary and have SDKs we do not control, so we define our own PaymentGateway interface and write an adapter per provider. Statement filters and sorts are small strategies (lambdas).
  4. The insight that simplifies everything: model the outside world as a system wallet. A deposit is a transfer from the bank wallet; a withdrawal is a transfer to it. One posting routine then handles every case, and "sum of all balances = 0" becomes a test.
