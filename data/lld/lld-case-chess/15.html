Common mistakes

  • One giant isValidMove with a switch on piece type. The opposite of what the round tests.
  • Forgetting king safety (pins, moving into check) or implementing it per piece.
  • Mixing attacks and moves for pawns: pawns capture diagonally and move straight; castling is never an attack.
  • Setting status by hand in a few places instead of deriving it after every move and undo.
  • Spot/Cell classes with getters and setters only, and a ChessManager god class doing everything.
  • Over-design for a 25-minute slot: factories of factories, a Strategy per rule, a Singleton board. Amazon's feedback in our sample said exactly this.
  • Undo that forgets en passant (captured pawn is not on the destination square) or the castling rook.
  • No way to set up a position, so nothing can be tested except from the start.
