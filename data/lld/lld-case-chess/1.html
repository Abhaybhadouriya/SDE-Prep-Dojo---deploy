Two reports pull in opposite directions, and both are lessons. Amazon's 25-minute slot punished over-design (a Strategy class for every rule before a single move works). Microsoft punished under-design (classes that compile but do not separate responsibilities). The fix for both: say the requirements, show the 6-7 core classes and one clear extension point, and go deep only where asked.
