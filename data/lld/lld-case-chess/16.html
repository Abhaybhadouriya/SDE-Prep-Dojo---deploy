  • Piece = own movement rule (polymorphism); Board = squares + isAttacked; Game = turns, king safety, status, history.
  • Sliding pieces share one loop (directions); stepping pieces share one loop (offsets); pawn and king add specials.
  • Move is a Command: execute saves what it changes, undo restores it; that also implements king-safety checks.
  • Status is derived: in check x has a legal move gives ACTIVE / CHECK / CHECKMATE / STALEMATE.
  • Perft 20 / 400 / 8,902 proves the rules.
  • Online: per-game serialization, expectedPly versioning, snapshots for readers.
