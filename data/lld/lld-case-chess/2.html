Clarify requirements

Functional

  1. Setup Two players, white and black; the standard starting position; white moves first.
  2. Move A player moves a piece from one square to another, for example e2e4. Only legal moves are accepted, with a clear reason otherwise.
  3. Rules Each piece's movement; captures; blocking for sliding pieces; a move may not leave your own king in check.
  4. Special moves Castling, en passant, promotion (queen by default, choice allowed).
  5. End of game Detect check, checkmate and stalemate; allow resignation; no moves after the game ends.
  6. History Keep the move list; support undo (take-back).

Non-functional

  • Extensible: a new piece type or variant rule without editing the game loop.
  • Testable: set up any position, play moves, assert the result; no UI needed.
  • Correct: move generation verified against known counts.
  • Speed is not a goal (no engine, no bitboards) unless the interviewer asks.

Out of scope (say it)

  • AI opponent, ratings, matchmaking, chess clocks (mention clocks as a follow-up).
  • Draw by threefold repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material (easy follow-ups; see extensibility).
  • Networking and real-time play (that is the HLD page Online Multiplayer Game (Chess)).
