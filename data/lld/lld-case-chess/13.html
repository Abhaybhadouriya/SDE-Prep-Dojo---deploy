Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Requirements and scope stated; asked about special moves, undo and the expected depth.
  • Movement rules are polymorphic: no switch on piece type in Game or Board.
  • Responsibilities split: Piece (own rule), Board (squares and attacks), Game (turns, check, status, history).
  • "Cannot leave own king in check" handled generally, not per piece.
  • Move as an object (Command) with undo; history kept.
  • Status derived after each move; terminal states reject moves.
  • Validation with clear error messages; no silent ignores.
  • Value objects for positions; no mutable shared state leaking out.
  • Can extend: new piece, draw rule, bot player, observers, online play with per-game locking.
  • Not over-designed for the time slot (no managers, no singleton, no pattern for its own sake).
