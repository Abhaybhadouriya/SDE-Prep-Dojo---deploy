Core flows

A move, from request to new status (castling)

  1. The Game rejects early: game over, malformed input, empty square, wrong colour. Each has its own message, so the UI can tell the user why.
  2. The Game asks the piece for its candidate moves. The king adds castling only if it and the rook never moved, the squares between are empty, and e1, f1, g1 are not attacked. It asks the Board, which owns all pieces.
  3. King safety is checked the simple way: execute each candidate on the real board, ask "is my king attacked now?", undo. Command makes this cheap and exact, including captures and en passant.
  4. The matching move is executed for real. Castling moves two pieces; the Move remembers both so undo can reverse both. The Move is pushed on the history stack.
  5. The turn flips and the status is recomputed for the side to move: in check or not, any legal move or not. Four combinations give ACTIVE, CHECK, CHECKMATE and STALEMATE.
