Identify entities: nouns and verbs

  1. Start from the clarified statement.
  2. Nouns. "Piece" splits into six types with different behaviour: the clearest polymorphism signal in all of LLD.
  3. Verbs become methods on the class that owns the data: Piece.attacks, Board.isAttacked, Game.move, Move.undo.
  4. Drop or shrink what has no behaviour. Many published designs add a Cell/Spot class; a position value plus a 2-D array is simpler and loses nothing.
  5. The final model: four classes with behaviour, three small value types.
