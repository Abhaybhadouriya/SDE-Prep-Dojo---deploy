Clarify requirements (first 5-10 minutes)

"Design Jira" is unbounded. Pick a slice, say it back, and write it as a comment at the top of Main.

Functional requirements

  1. Users and boards a board has a key (like PAY), a name, members and a workflow. Only members can act on it or be assignees.
  2. Issues story, task or bug with title, description, priority, labels, reporter and an assignee (optional). Keys are generated: PAY-1, PAY-2.
  3. Subtasks one level deep (as in Jira). Removing a parent removes its subtasks.
  4. Workflow statuses and allowed moves are configurable per board (a to-do list has 2 states, a Scrum board has 4). Rules can block a move: "needs an assignee to start", "all subtasks done before done".
  5. Sprints plan, start, complete. One active sprint per board. On completion, unfinished issues go back to the backlog.
  6. Search filter by status, assignee, priority, label, text, sprint, and combine with and / or / not; sort by priority.
  7. Activity log every change (create, edit, assign, move, sprint change, remove) is recorded with who, when, from and to. Query by issue and by user. History survives deletion.

Non-functional requirements

  • Correct under concurrency: many users on one board; no duplicate keys, no lost updates, no torn reads.
  • Extensible: new workflows, rules, filters and notification channels without editing the core.
  • Auditable: the log is append-only and written in the same critical section as the change.
  • Testable: injected clock, in-memory store, deterministic demo.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Comments, attachments, roles beyond membership (admin, viewer), notifications by email, persistence, a UI, story points and burndown charts, custom fields. Each gets one line in Extensibility.
