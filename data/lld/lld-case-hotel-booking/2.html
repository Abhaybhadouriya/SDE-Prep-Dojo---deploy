Clarify requirements

Spend 5 to 8 minutes here. A hotel problem can grow into Airbnb, Booking.com and a payments company at once, so the main job of this phase is to cut scope and write the cut down.

Functional (P0 - must work in the demo)

  1. Catalogue Hotels in cities; each hotel has rooms of a few types (single, double, suite) and a nightly rate per type.
  2. Search Given city, room type, check-in, check-out and number of rooms, list hotels that can sell that many rooms for every night, with the total price, cheapest first.
  3. Hold Reserve rooms for a guest for 15 minutes while they pay. Held rooms are not for sale to anyone else.
  4. Confirm When payment succeeds, the hold becomes a confirmed booking. A retry with the same payment reference must not book twice.
  5. Expire A hold that is not paid in 15 minutes is released automatically.
  6. Cancel A guest can cancel; the hotel's cancellation policy decides the refund, and the rooms go back on sale.

Functional (P1 - say you can add them)

  • Pricing rules per hotel: weekend surcharge, long-stay discount, seasonal rates (done in the code as stackable rules).
  • Notifications on every status change (email, SMS, the hotel's own system).
  • Choosing a specific room number, modifying a booking's dates, waitlists, reviews.

Non-functional

  • No overbooking, ever: two guests must never both get the last room for the same night, even if they click at the same moment.
  • Search is read-heavy: many searches per booking; searches must not block bookings in other hotels.
  • Idempotent confirm: payment callbacks are retried by gateways; the second call must be harmless.
  • Testable: time comes from an injected clock, so tests can say "16 minutes later".
  • Money is exact: store paise (integer minor units), never floating point.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Real payment processing (we accept a payment reference from the gateway callback).
  • Persistence, distributed deployment, caching, ranking by reviews (that is the HLD version).
  • Room assignment at check-in, housekeeping, loyalty points.
