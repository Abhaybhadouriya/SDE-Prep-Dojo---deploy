In a 60-minute whiteboard round like Zepto's, where schema and APIs are also expected: 7 minutes of requirements, 10 of entities and class diagram, 8 of schema and APIs, 25 of code for hold/confirm/cancel with the inventory check, and 10 for locking and the follow-ups. Expedia's version ran long (about two hours, HLD then LLD), so expect the interviewer to move into search and pricing breakdown once the core holds up. The general playbook is in the guide to LLD round types.
