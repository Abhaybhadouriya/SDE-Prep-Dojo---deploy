Common mistakes

  1. Checking only the check-in night A stay that is free on night one and full on night three gets booked. Check every night; free is the minimum.
  2. Counting check-out day as a night Back-to-back stays get rejected and the hotel looks half empty. Use half-open ranges.
  3. Booking after payment instead of holding before it Two guests pay for one room. Hold first, with a TTL.
  4. A timer as the only way holds expire If the timer is late, a stale hold blocks a room or a late payment succeeds. Check expiry on every read and on confirm.
  5. Check and take outside one lock The classic overbooking race, now across several nights.
  6. A pricing class per combination of rules Use decorators around one strategy.
  7. A boolean "isBooked" on each room plus a booking list Two sources of truth, and a room is booked per night, not forever.
  8. Prices as double Refunds drift. Use paise or BigDecimal.
  9. Calling email or SMS inside the lock One slow provider freezes the hotel's bookings.
  10. Starting with Airbnb Reviews, hosts, maps and ranking eat the hour. Cut scope out loud in minute two.
