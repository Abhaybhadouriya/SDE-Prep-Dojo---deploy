Remember this

  • Inventory is counts per room type per night; a stay fits if the minimum free count over its nights is enough.
  • Stays are half-open [check-in, check-out): check-out day is free for the next guest.
  • Hold before payment with a 15-minute TTL; expire lazily on reads and on confirm, not only with a timer.
  • One Booking with a status table: HELD to CONFIRMED, CANCELLED or EXPIRED; CONFIRMED to CANCELLED; final states are final.
  • Pricing is a Strategy and rules stack as Decorators; cancellation is a second Strategy per hotel; Observer for notifications.
  • Concurrency: check and take every night under one per-hotel lock; in SQL, a guarded update or row locks in night order.
  • Confirm is idempotent per payment reference; money is integer paise; time is injected.
