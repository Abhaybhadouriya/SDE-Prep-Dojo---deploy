Hold, then pay: the happy path

The booking lifecycle (step through it)

  1. hold() takes the rooms out of inventory immediately and stamps holdExpiresAt = now + 15 min. The booking starts in HELD.
  2. The payment gateway's callback calls confirm(id, paymentRef). HELD becomes CONFIRMED. A second call with the same reference returns the booking unchanged (idempotent).
  3. If 15 minutes pass first, the sweep (or the late confirm itself) moves HELD to EXPIRED and gives the rooms back. A confirm that arrives later fails, and the caller must refund the payment.
  4. Cancel works from HELD (nothing paid, refund 0) and from CONFIRMED (the hotel's policy decides the refund). Either way the rooms go back on sale.
  5. Final states are final. Because every transition goes through one canMoveTo table, a race between "expire" and "pay" can only end one way: whichever takes the hotel lock first wins, and the other is rejected.
