Evaluator rubric checklist

  • No class per drink: recipes are data; the menu can be loaded from config.
  • Decorator used correctly: the wrapper implements Beverage and holds a Beverage; add-ons stack and repeat; base objects are not mutated.
  • Factory hides construction: callers use names; a registry, not an if-else chain.
  • All-or-nothing inventory: a refused order changes nothing; every shortage reported.
  • Thread safety proven: one critical section for check and subtract; a concurrency test that would fail without it.
  • At most N in parallel, by construction (fixed pool or semaphore), and measured.
  • Low indicator and refill work, alerts fire once per crossing, listeners outside the lock.
  • Hardware behind an interface; failure path releases or accounts for ingredients.
  • Integration tests for every requirement line; integers for money and quantities.
