  • Drinks are recipes (data); add-ons are Decorators that implement Beverage and hold a Beverage, so they stack and repeat.
  • A Factory with a name registry builds "latte + shot + caramel"; new drinks are one register call.
  • Inventory: check every ingredient and subtract all of them inside one lock; refused orders change nothing and list every reason.
  • Reserve at acceptance on the caller's thread; brew later on one of N outlet threads (a fixed pool = at most N in parallel).
  • Never brew or call listeners while holding the inventory lock.
  • Low indicator = list below the mark; alert = Observer fired once per crossing.
  • Payment, modes (State), sizes and JSON config are additions, not rewrites.
