Common mistakes

  1. Busy waiting while (true) { check(); sleep(10); } burns CPU and is late by up to 10 ms. Use a timed condition wait.
  2. Sleeping without re-checking A new earlier job arrives while the dispatcher sleeps until a later one. Signal on submit and recompute.
  3. Running the task under the lock One slow job freezes the whole scheduler.
  4. One thread per job 10,000 jobs means 10,000 threads. Use a fixed pool.
  5. Pushing every due job into a FIFO executor Priorities silently stop working.
  6. Catching nothing An exception escapes, the worker thread dies, and the pool quietly shrinks.
  7. Wall-clock time An NTP step stalls or bursts the scheduler. Use a monotonic clock.
  8. Forgetting downstream of failure Children of a failed job wait forever. Define SKIPPED.
  9. Half-admitted graphs Validate the whole batch, including cycles, before admitting any job.
