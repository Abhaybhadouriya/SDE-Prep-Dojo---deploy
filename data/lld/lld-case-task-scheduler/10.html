From your world

The timer wheel is the kernel's answer to "a heap is O(log n) and I have a million TCP retransmit timers, most of which get cancelled". Buckets by expiry, O(1) insert, O(1) cancel, and the cost of being a little late. LWN's "Reinventing the timer wheel" describes the 2015 rework that accepted less precision for far timers. Mention this trade when the interviewer pushes on scale.
