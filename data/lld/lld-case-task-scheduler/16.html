  • Three questions, three structures: when (time heap), which (priority heap of due jobs), who (fixed worker pool).
  • One dispatcher thread does a timed wait until the head is due and re-checks after every signal.
  • Acquire a worker permit before picking a job, so priority is decided when a worker can start.
  • Task = Command, Schedule = Strategy, JobSpec = Builder, JobState = state machine, JobListener = Observer.
  • DAG = Kahn's algorithm driven by completions; reject cycles before admitting anything; skip downstream of failure.
  • Never run tasks or listeners under the lock; cancel lazily; use a monotonic clock.
  • Asked recently at Flipkart, Salesforce, Microsoft, Rubrik and Goldman Sachs; priority and thread safety are the standard follow-ups.
