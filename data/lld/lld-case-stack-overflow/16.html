  • Flipkart asked this as "Flipkart Overflow" (2023, 2024); the 2023 reject was for "modular but not extensible".
  • Question and Answer extend Post: votes, comments and score are written once.
  • Votes are a map voter -> type: duplicates are impossible, changes are reversible.
  • Who may act = a list of permission rules; points = a reputation policy; filters = predicates; notifications = listeners.
  • Stack Overflow's values: +10 per upvote, -2 per downvote (-1 to the voter on answers), +15 / +2 for accepts, floor 1; upvote at 15, downvote at 125.
  • Per-post lock for votes, atomic integer for reputation, no nested locks.
  • Search = inverted index; at scale, votes become events aggregated asynchronously.
