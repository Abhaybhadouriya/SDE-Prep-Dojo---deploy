Common mistakes

  1. A vote counter instead of a voter map You cannot detect duplicates or reverse a change.
  2. Copy-pasted vote logic for questions and answers Two places to fix every bug. Use a shared Post.
  3. All rules as if-statements in the service The exact reason for the 2023 Flipkart rejection.
  4. Reversal by different code than application Reputation drifts. One applyVote with a sign.
  5. Paying the acceptor twice when the accept moves between answers.
  6. Notifying the person who acted The answerer does not need "someone answered".
  7. Scanning all questions on every search Keep an index from words to ids.
  8. Taking a user lock inside the post lock and the reverse elsewhere: a deadlock waiting to happen. Use an atomic.
