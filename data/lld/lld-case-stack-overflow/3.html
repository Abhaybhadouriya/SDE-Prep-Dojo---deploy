Identify the entities

Underline the nouns and verbs in the agreed requirements. Step through it.

  1. Nine nouns from the requirements.
  2. Nine verbs; each becomes a service method or a small interface.
  3. Merge and demote: topic and tag are the same string; question and answer share almost everything, so both extend Post. A vote is not an entity with its own life here, just a value in a map.
  4. Where each verb lands. The variable parts (permissions, points, filters, notifications) each sit behind one interface.

Should Vote be a class? In a database it is a row (voter, post, type, time). In memory, a Map<voterId, VoteType> on the post gives "one vote per user" for free and makes the duplicate check a single lookup. Say both.
