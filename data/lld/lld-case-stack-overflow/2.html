Clarify requirements

Spend 5 to 8 minutes here. The first question decides the whole permission model: is this Flipkart Overflow (topic subscriptions gate actions) or Stack Overflow (reputation gates actions)? We support both with one interface.

Functional (P0 - must work in the demo)

  1. Users Register; subscribe to and unsubscribe from topics (tags).
  2. Ask A question has a title, a body and at least one tag.
  3. Answer Anyone allowed can answer; the asker and earlier answerers follow the thread.
  4. Vote Up or down on a question or an answer; one vote per user per post; no voting on your own post; a user may change or retract a vote.
  5. Feed Questions in my topics, newest first, filtered by topic, answered or unanswered.
  6. View A question with its answers in order: accepted first, then score, then oldest.

Functional (P1 - bonus)

  • Accept an answer (asker only; can change their mind).
  • Comments on questions and answers.
  • Reputation per Stack Overflow's rules, with privilege thresholds.
  • Keyword search, notifications on new answers, accepts and comments.

Non-functional

  • Consistent under concurrency: 100 users upvoting at once gives a score of 100 and exactly +1,000 reputation; one user clicking 20 times gives one vote.
  • Extensible: a new rule (moderators, "no voting on closed questions") or a new reputation table is a new class.
  • Readable demo: a driver that replays the prompt's test sequence and prints what changes.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Edits and revision history, bounties, badges, close votes, moderation queues (good follow-ups).
  • The daily reputation cap of 200 from votes (mention it; it is a decorator on the policy).
  • Persistence, full-text ranking, spam detection.
