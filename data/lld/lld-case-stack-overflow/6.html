Core flows

Vote: the one method everybody gets wrong

Answer, accept, notify (step through it)

  1. An answer adds its author to the thread's followers and notifies everyone else who follows.
  2. The second answerer triggers notifications to the asker and the first answerer, never to themselves.
  3. Only the asker may accept. The answerer gets +15 and the acceptor +2 (none if it is the asker's own answer).
  4. The view orders answers: accepted, then score, then oldest. The ordering is one comparator, easy to swap.
