How it is asked

Snake & Ladder comes in two shapes. In a machine-coding round (Flipkart, PhonePe, Swiggy style) you get 90 minutes to write working, modular code with a demo, and the evaluator then adds a feature live. In an LLD round with running code (Microsoft, Salesforce) you discuss the design for 10-15 minutes and then code most of it in 45 minutes. The PhonePe report is the important pattern: the evaluator adds a feature to your running code. Your design is judged by how small that change is. Plan for the follow-ups listed in Extensibility before you write the first class.

Across the 16 problem lists we surveyed, Snake & Ladder appears in 9 GitHub collections and 6 course syllabi (our survey of LLD repos and courses). It is usually the second problem people practise, right after Tic-Tac-Toe.
