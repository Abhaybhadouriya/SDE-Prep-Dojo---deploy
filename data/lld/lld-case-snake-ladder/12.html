90-minute machine-coding plan

MinutesDo thisDone when
0-8Read, restate rules, ask the 7 questions, write requirements and out-of-scope as a comment at the top of Main.Interviewer agrees with the list.
8-15Nouns/verbs, quick class sketch (Game, Board+Builder, Jump, Dice, Player, Rules, Listener). Name the follow-ups you will design for.Diagram on paper or in comments.
15-30Jump, Board, Builder with validation and resolve().Board builds and resolves the chain.
30-40Dice, RandomDice, FixedDice, Player.Compiles.
40-60Game.playTurn() with classic rules, queue, overshoot, win; ConsolePrinter; demo in main.Checkpoint 1: full classic game runs. Commit or save a copy.
60-72Rules: extra roll on six, three sixes cancel, play until one left.Demo 2 runs.
72-82Tests with FixedDice: chain, overshoot, validation, sixes, ranks.Checkpoint 2: all checks pass.
82-90Clean names, remove dead code, prepare the walkthrough: requirements, class diagram, one turn, how to add a follow-up.You can demo in 3 minutes.

If you are behind at minute 60, skip the bonus rules and write tests for the classic game. A working, tested core beats half-finished features in every rubric we have seen. See the guide to LLD round formats.
