Evaluator rubric checklist

Tags: must = missing it usually fails the round; strong = separates a pass from a strong pass; bonus = extra credit if time allows.

  • must Runs: the demo plays a full game from main with no edits.
  • must Correct rules: chains, exact finish, round robin, stop at winner.
  • strong Separation: Board knows no players; Dice knows no board; Game does no printing.
  • strong Extensible: two dice and a new output format need no edits to Game.
  • strong Validated input: invalid boards fail fast with a clear message.
  • strong Testable: scripted dice; at least 5 meaningful checks.
  • strong Naming and size: small methods, no God class, no magic numbers (100 and 6 come from inputs).
  • strong Error handling: exceptions with messages, safety limit on the loop.
  • bonus Communication: you said which follow-ups you designed for, and which patterns you skipped and why.

Red flag that fails the round: The game loop prints and decides at the same time: rules, turn order and System.out tangled in one method, so you cannot test a move without reading console output.
