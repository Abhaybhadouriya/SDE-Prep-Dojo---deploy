Common mistakes

  • Letting callers pick sinks (log(msg, Sink.FILE)). Breaks the abstraction; a real rejection reason.
  • A giant switch(level) inside log() that knows every sink. Every new sink edits it.
  • One subclass per combination (JsonFileLogger, PlainConsoleLogger...). Use two strategies instead.
  • Unsynchronised file writes: lines interleave under load; or the opposite, one global lock around formatting and I/O.
  • Unbounded async queue: a slow disk turns into an out-of-memory crash.
  • No shutdown path: the last seconds of logs (the ones you need after a crash) are lost.
  • Creating the message before the level check, or building strings for disabled debug calls.
  • Lazy singleton without proper synchronisation (plain if (instance == null)).
  • Mutable LogMessage shared with a writer thread.
