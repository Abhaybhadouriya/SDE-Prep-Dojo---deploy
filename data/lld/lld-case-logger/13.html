Evaluator rubric checklist

  • Requirements: asked about sync/async, sinks, thresholds; wrote out-of-scope.
  • Working code: demo runs end to end and shows levels, two sinks, reconfiguration.
  • Small caller API: log(level, msg) and shortcuts; no sink choice in calls.
  • Separation: formatter and sink are separate strategies; adding one touches no old class.
  • Patterns named with a reason: CoR, Strategy, Singleton, Decorator, Builder; plus one honest alternative.
  • Thread safety: immutable message and config, per-sink lock, safe singleton, concurrent registry.
  • Performance: early level check; async with a bounded queue and an explicit drop policy.
  • Lifecycle: idempotent close, flush on shutdown, old chain closed on reconfigure.
  • Testability: injected clock, memory sink, assertions or unit tests.
  • Error handling: a failing sink does not crash the app or stop other sinks.
