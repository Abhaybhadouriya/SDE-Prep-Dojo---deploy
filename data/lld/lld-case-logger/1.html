How it is asked

Logger is a favourite because it is short to state and has a lot of depth: patterns (Chain of Responsibility, Strategy, Singleton, Decorator), concurrency (many threads, one file) and performance (async writes). Reports from LeetCode Discuss in our research set (posts from 2024-06 to 2026-11):

Two lessons from these reports. First, you must produce the requirements when none are given (Amazon). Second, keep the public API tiny: log(level, message) plus the shortcuts. Routing to sinks is configuration, not a caller's choice (PhonePe). Also be ready to explain your pattern choice and an alternative calmly, because interviewers sometimes have one picture in mind (Razorpay).
