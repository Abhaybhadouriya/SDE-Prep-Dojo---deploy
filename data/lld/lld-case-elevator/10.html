Extensibility: follow-ups

Follow-upChange
Zones (Microsoft: floors 1-20 to car 1, 21-40 to car 2)Give each car a served-floor range. A ZonedDispatch filters candidates by range, then delegates to the cost strategy (a decorator over DispatchStrategy). Car calls outside the car's zone are rejected.
Capacity 20 / weight limitThe car tracks load from a sensor (or boarding events). When full, shouldStopHere skips hall calls (car calls still stop) and the call is re-dispatched; dispatch skips full cars.
At most 5 stops per riderA rule on CarCall: count calls from this rider's session; validate in submit. Without rider identity, cap stops per car instead.
Serve a request in O(log N) (Amazon)Already there: stops are TreeSets, so "next stop above" is higher(floor) in O(log N), not a scan of all floors.
Cancel the stale hall call on other carsWhen any car opens its doors at floor f leaving in direction d, the controller removes (f, d) from every other car. The controller already hears onDoorsOpen; it becomes a listener of its own cars.
Fire service / emergencyA new FireRecallState: go to the ground floor without stopping, open doors, ignore calls. The controller switches every car into it.
Morning up-peak, evening down-peakSwap the dispatch strategy at runtime (a strategy chosen by a clock), or park idle cars at the lobby with an IdleParkingPolicy.
Destination dispatch (keypad in the lobby)A new request type DestinationCall(from, to) whose execute chooses a car and adds both stops; the panel shows "take car B". Existing types do not change.
Door held open / obstructionDoorsOpenState resets its dwell counter while the sensor is blocked; after N resets, raise an alert event.
Real time instead of ticksA scheduler calls tick() every 500 ms (a ScheduledExecutorService); nothing else changes, which is why ticks are a good seam.
