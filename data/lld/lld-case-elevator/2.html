Clarify requirements

Functional requirements

  1. Building floors min..max (say 0 to 10) and N cars, each starting at some floor.
  2. Hall calls each floor has an UP and a DOWN button (no UP on the top floor, no DOWN on the ground floor). A press asks for some car to come and go that way.
  3. Car calls each car has floor buttons. A press asks this car to stop at that floor.
  4. Dispatch the system assigns every hall call to exactly one car.
  5. Movement a car serves stops in its current direction before turning around; it never moves with the doors open and never leaves the shaft.
  6. Maintenance a car can be taken out of service: it finishes the riders it has, then parks and takes no new calls.
  7. Displays floor displays and logs learn about moves and door openings.

Non-functional requirements

  • Safety first: no movement with doors open, no floor outside the shaft, no lost hall call. These are invariants the tests check.
  • No starvation: every request is served eventually, under any load.
  • Concurrent buttons: presses arrive on many threads at once; none is lost or served twice.
  • Swappable scheduling: dispatch and movement rules change by building and by time of day.
  • Testable: time advances in ticks you control, so a test can replay a scenario exactly.

Out of scope (say it out loud)

  • Motor control, acceleration, door sensors and weight sensors as hardware; fire service and earthquake modes (mention as a state you could add); a UI. We simulate one floor per tick and a one-tick door dwell.
