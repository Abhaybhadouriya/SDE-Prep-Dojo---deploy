Quiz

A car at floor 4 has car calls for 6 and 2 in a building with floors 0 to 10. How many floors does SCAN travel?

  1. 6
  2. 14
  3. 8
SCAN goes 4 to 6, on to the top (10), then down to 2: 2 + 4 + 8 = 14. LOOK turns at 6 and travels 6.

A car is going UP and passes floor 5, where someone pressed DOWN. There are more stops above. What should it do?

  1. Pass and pick them up on the way down
  2. Stop and let them in
  3. Ignore the call forever
A hall call in the other direction is served only when the car is about to turn anyway.

Which object decides which car answers a hall call?

  1. The hall button
  2. The elevator's current state
  3. The controller's DispatchStrategy
The controller (Mediator) asks the strategy to choose among cars that accept calls.

Why does submit() put a command in a queue instead of updating the car directly?

  1. Queues are faster than method calls
  2. So only the controller thread ever touches car state, with no locks on cars
  3. Because records cannot call methods
Single writer: button threads only enqueue; one thread applies every change.

Every car is out of service and someone presses UP. What should happen?

  1. The call waits and is served when a car returns
  2. It is dropped
  3. An exception is shown to the person
Unassigned calls are retried on every tick; a call is never lost.

How do the State and the Strategy objects differ in this design?

  1. States are interfaces; strategies are classes
  2. Strategies have more methods
  3. In this design, a state returns its own successor; a strategy is chosen from outside and stays
Same shape, different intent: states model the car's changing condition, and in this design they swap themselves as events happen; the movement strategy is an algorithm the controller picks. (GoF also allows the context to drive state transitions; that is still the State pattern.)

Which kernel idea matches "buttons enqueue, one controller thread processes"?

  1. Copy-on-write fork
  2. Interrupt top half queues work for a softirq / bottom half
  3. Page cache readahead
Do the minimum in the interrupt, then let one context that owns the data do the rest.
