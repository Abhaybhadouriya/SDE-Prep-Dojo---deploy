Evaluator rubric checklist

Tags: must = missing it usually fails the round; strong = separates a pass from a strong pass; bonus = extra credit if time allows.

  • must Runs with a demo that shows dispatch, stops and door openings over time.
  • must States named and illegal combinations impossible (no moving with doors open).
  • must Hall calls and car calls modelled separately; hall calls kept by direction.
  • strong Scheduling explained: LOOK or SCAN, why, and how to swap.
  • strong Dispatch beyond nearest car, with direction taken into account.
  • strong No lost calls: validation, unassigned retry, maintenance handled.
  • strong Threading model stated and safe: who writes car state, how presses arrive.
  • bonus Extensible: zones, capacity, new states fit without rewriting.
  • strong Deterministic tests (ticks, seeded randomness), including one concurrency test.
  • must Clear naming and small methods; no 200-line move().

Red flag that fails the round: One giant move() with nested ifs on direction and door state, or any path where a car can move with its doors open.
