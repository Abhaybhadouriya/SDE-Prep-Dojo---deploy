Identify entities

  1. Nouns from the prompt: the controller (the building's brain), the elevator car, hall buttons, car buttons, floors, doors and displays.
  2. Verbs: press, dispatch, move or stop, open and close doors, show the floor. Press belongs to the controller (one entry point), moving and doors belong to the car's state, showing belongs to listeners.
  3. What varies: which car answers a call (DispatchStrategy), when a car turns around (MovementPolicy), what a car does on each tick (ElevatorState), and who wants to be told (ElevatorListener). Each gets an interface.
  4. What is not a class: a floor is just an int in range; a door is the DOORS_OPEN state plus a dwell counter; a button is the Request object it creates. Classes with no behaviour only add wiring.

The biggest modelling mistake here is one big Elevator.move() with nested ifs on isMoving, doorOpen, goingUp and maintenance booleans. Four booleans are 16 combinations, most of them illegal (moving with the door open). Name the legal states, give each one its own small class, and the illegal combinations cannot be written down.

Two kinds of request look alike but are not the same: a hall call (floor 3, going UP) asks the system for any car and is dispatched; a car call (car 1, floor 8) belongs to one car and is never dispatched. Keep them as two types. A car also stores hall calls by direction, in two sets (hallUp, hallDown), because a car going up should not stop for someone who wants to go down, unless it is about to turn around anyway.
