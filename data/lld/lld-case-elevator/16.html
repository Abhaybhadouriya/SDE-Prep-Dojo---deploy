Remember this

  • Hall call = floor + direction, dispatched to some car; car call = floor, belongs to one car.
  • Stops are sorted sets per direction; "next stop" is higher/lower in O(log N).
  • State pattern: IDLE, MOVING, DOORS_OPEN, OUT_OF_SERVICE; only MOVING moves the car.
  • Two Strategies: MovementPolicy (LOOK/SCAN, when to turn) and DispatchStrategy (which car).
  • LOOK from 4 with calls 6 and 2: 6 floors; SCAN: 14.
  • Buttons create Command objects; the controller (Mediator) runs them on its one thread.
  • Single writer: any thread may submit, only the controller thread touches car state.
  • Never drop a call: unassigned calls are retried; maintenance finishes riders first.
