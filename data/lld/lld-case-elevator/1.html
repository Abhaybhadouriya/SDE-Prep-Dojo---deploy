How it is asked

The elevator is the most common "state machine plus scheduling" LLD question. It appears in 10 of the 16 course and problem lists we surveyed, and in the reports below. It is usually a 60-minute whiteboard or document round (Microsoft) rather than a 90-minute machine-coding round, but interviewers expect you to reason about states, scheduling and concurrency precisely, so code like this is the best preparation.
