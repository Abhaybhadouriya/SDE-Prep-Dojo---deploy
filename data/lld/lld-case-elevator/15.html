Common mistakes

  • Booleans instead of states (isMoving, doorOpen, goingUp), so moving with the doors open is one missed if away.
  • One request type for hall and car calls, and then dispatching car calls to a different car.
  • A FIFO queue of requests served in arrival order: the car zig-zags up and down. Stops are sets sorted by floor, served by direction.
  • Nearest car only, ignoring direction.
  • Dropping a call when no car is free, or when a car goes into maintenance with calls assigned.
  • A thread per car with shared mutable state and no story for dispatch reading every car.
  • Real Thread.sleep time in the core logic, which makes tests slow and flaky.
  • Classes for Floor, Door and Button with no behaviour, while the real decisions sit in one huge method.
