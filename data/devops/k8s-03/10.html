Common mistakes and gotchas

Good to know (SDE-2)

Creating bare pods in production. Nobody recreates them if the node dies. Always use a controller (Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, Job).

Putting the app and its database in one pod. They then scale together and die together. One pod per independently scaled component.

Hard-coding pod IPs. They change on every replacement. Use a Service name.

Liveness probe that checks dependencies, or a liveness probe with no startup probe on a slow app: restart storms. Readiness for "can I serve", liveness only for "am I wedged".

Ignoring SIGTERM. Your app gets killed after 30 seconds and drops requests on every deploy. Handle SIGTERM, use the exec form in the Dockerfile, and add a short preStop sleep.

Reading kubectl logs instead of --previous after a crash. The current container just started and has no error yet; the crash is in the previous one.

You might confuse this with... a pod vs a container vs a node. A node is a machine. A pod is a group of containers that share an IP, placed on one node. A container is one process tree from one image. "Restart the pod" is not really a thing: you restart a container, or you replace a pod.
