The story: peas in a pod

Explain like I'm 5

Open a pea pod. Inside are a few peas. They grew together, they live in the same shell, and when you pick the pod, all the peas come with it. You never pick half a pod.

In our school story, a pod is a desk. Usually one kid sits at a desk. Sometimes two kids must sit together, for example a kid and a helper who reads to them. Kids at the same desk share crayons (they share files) and can whisper to each other without shouting across the room (they talk on localhost). The desk has one address label, so the postman delivers to the desk, not to each kid. If the desk is moved to another room, both kids move together, and the desk gets a new address label.

A pod is the smallest thing you can deploy in Kubernetes. It is a group of one or more containers that always run together, on the same node, and share a network address and some storage. Most pods have exactly one main container. Kubernetes never schedules a single container; it schedules pods. (The name comes from a "pod of whales", a nod to Docker's whale logo.)
