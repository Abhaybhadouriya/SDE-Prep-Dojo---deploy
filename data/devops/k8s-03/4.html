Pods are cattle, not pets: the changing IP

Must know (SDE-1)

Learner question 4: why do pods get new IP addresses when they restart, and how does anything find them? Be precise about "restart":

  • When a container restarts inside the same pod, the pod keeps its IP, because the sandbox (and its network namespace) stays.
  • When a pod is replaced (deleted, evicted, its node died, a new version rolled out), it is a brand-new pod with a new name, often on another node, and the CNI plugin gives it a new IP from that node's range.

Kubernetes treats pods as disposable ("cattle, not pets"). So nothing should call a pod by its IP. Instead, a Service gives a group of pods (chosen by labels such as app: web) one stable virtual IP and DNS name, and keeps an up-to-date list of the current pod IPs behind it.

How a Service turns its virtual IP into these pod IPs (DNAT rules, EndpointSlices, CoreDNS) is the topic of Services & Cluster Networking.
