Quick quiz

Which Linux namespace do all containers in a pod always share?

  1. The mount namespace (same filesystem)
  2. The network namespace (same IP and ports)
  3. The user namespace
They share one network namespace, held by the pause container. Each container still has its own root filesystem.

The app container in a pod crashes and is restarted by the kubelet. What happens to the pod IP?

  1. It changes
  2. The pod moves to another node
  3. It stays the same, because the sandbox and its network namespace stay
Container restart is inside the same pod. Only a replacement pod (new object) gets a new IP.

What is CrashLoopBackOff?

  1. A container waiting reason: it keeps exiting and the kubelet waits longer between restarts
  2. A sixth pod phase
  3. An error from the scheduler
The phase stays Running. The back-off goes 10 s, 20 s, 40 s... up to 5 minutes.

A readiness probe fails. What does Kubernetes do?

  1. Restarts the container
  2. Removes the pod from Service endpoints until it passes again
  3. Deletes the pod
Readiness controls traffic, not life. Liveness is the one that restarts.

A container exits with code 137. What is the most likely cause?

  1. A syntax error in the app
  2. It finished its job successfully
  3. It was SIGKILLed, often by the OOM killer for exceeding its memory limit
137 = 128 + 9 (SIGKILL). Check describe for "OOMKilled". 143 would be SIGTERM.

You need a log shipper that starts before the app and stops after it. What do you use on modern Kubernetes?

  1. An init container with restartPolicy: Always (native sidecar)
  2. A second regular container
  3. A separate Deployment
Native sidecars (GA in 1.33) give the ordering that regular extra containers do not.

Your app is SIGKILLed after exactly 30 seconds on every deploy and drops requests. Most likely cause?

  1. The liveness probe is too slow
  2. PID 1 is a shell that does not forward SIGTERM, so the app never shuts down and the grace period runs out
  3. The node is out of memory
Shell-form CMD makes /bin/sh PID 1. Use exec-form CMD or tini, handle SIGTERM, and add a preStop sleep.
