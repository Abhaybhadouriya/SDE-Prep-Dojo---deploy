Remember this

  • On-call = one named owner per window, with primary + secondary, a handoff note and a humane load (about 2 incidents per 12-hour shift at most).
  • Escalation policy: tiered paging with ack timeouts (for example 5 min, then 10 min), plus permission to escalate by hand.
  • Page only on urgent, actionable, user-visible symptoms (SLO burn rates). Causes go to dashboards; slow problems go to tickets.
  • Severity levels agreed in advance; when unsure, pick the higher one. Declare early.
  • Roles: Incident Commander coordinates, Ops lead changes production, Comms lead updates on a cadence, a scribe keeps the timeline.
  • Mitigate first (roll back, flag off, fail over), root-cause later.
  • Runbooks linked from alerts make response independent of one person's memory.
  • Blameless postmortem: impact, timeline, contributing factors, detection, action items with owners, priorities and due dates. Fix systems, not people.
