Measuring incident response

Good to know (SDE-2)
MetricMeaningImproved by
MTTD (time to detect)Start of impact to first alertSymptom-based SLO alerts, synthetics
MTTA (time to acknowledge)Alert to a human owning itEscalation policy, healthy rotations, low noise
MTTM / MTTR (time to mitigate / restore)Start to users being fine againFast rollback, feature flags, runbooks, clear IC
Pages per shiftOn-call loadDeleting noisy alerts, fixing top page sources
Action item completion rateDo we actually learn?Owners, due dates, weekly review
Repeat incidentsSame cause twiceFinishing the prevent items

Treat these as trends for the team, never as targets for individuals. If people are judged on MTTR, they will declare incidents late or close them early, and the numbers will lie.
