Gotchas

  • Hero culture. One person who "always fixes it" is a single point of failure and will burn out. Spread knowledge through runbooks, shadowing and rotations.
  • The IC who debugs. The moment the commander opens a terminal, nobody is coordinating. If the IC is the only expert, hand off the IC role.
  • Silent channels. Twenty people in the incident channel and no update for 40 minutes. Fixed cadence, even for "no change".
  • Root-causing before mitigating. Roll back first; the logs will still be there afterwards.
  • "Human error" as the root cause. Always ask what made the error easy and its effect large.
  • Action items nobody owns. They rot. Owner, ticket, due date, review.
  • Postmortem as punishment. If writing one feels like a trial, people will avoid declaring incidents. Leadership must praise good postmortems publicly.
  • Pages without runbooks. An alert with no runbook link is a puzzle at 3 a.m. Add the link or downgrade the alert.
