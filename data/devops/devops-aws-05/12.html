Remember this

  • Lambda is event-driven glue: short, stateless handlers, billed per millisecond, scaled by AWS.
  • S3 and CloudWatch Logs push asynchronously; Kinesis and SQS are pulled by an event source mapping.
  • Subscription payloads are base64 + gzip + JSON; skip CONTROL_MESSAGE; never subscribe to your own logs.
  • Concurrency = rate x duration; protect neighbours with reserved concurrency and the sink with caps.
  • Batches fail as a unit unless you use partial batch responses; on Kinesis also bisect and cap retries.
  • Every source is at least once: idempotent handlers, plus a DLQ or on-failure destination you alarm on.
  • In a VPC, the function needs a NAT gateway or VPC endpoints to reach AWS APIs.
  • High, steady volume, big state or long work: use a long-running consumer instead.
