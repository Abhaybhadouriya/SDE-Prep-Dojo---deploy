Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Sync = phone call (caller waits, both must be up). Async = letterbox (drop a message, reader picks it up later).
  • Messaging gives three superpowers: decoupling (time, location, number), buffering spikes, and fan-out.
  • Consumer lag = written but not yet processed = queue depth. Watch whether it keeps growing.
  • Kafka is a log, not a queue: reading does not delete, each group has its own offset, and you can replay.
  • Async costs: eventual consistency, duplicates (so be idempotent), per-partition ordering, dual writes, harder debugging.
  • Keep what the user waits for synchronous; make the rest asynchronous.
