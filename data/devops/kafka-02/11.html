You might confuse this with...

ThisIs not the same asThe difference
PartitionReplicaPartitions hold different slices of a topic (scale). Replicas are copies of one partition on other brokers (safety).
TopicQueueA topic keeps records after they are read and serves many consumer groups; a classic queue deletes on acknowledgement.
PartitionSegmentA partition is the whole ordered log; a segment is one file (up to 1 GiB by default) that the log is cut into on disk.
OffsetTimestampThe offset is the position in one partition and always increases. Timestamps are set by producers and can be out of order.
BrokerControllerBrokers store partitions and serve clients. Controllers (KRaft) manage cluster metadata and leader elections. A small node can do both.
Kafka partitionDatabase shardSimilar idea (split by key), but a partition is an append-only log you read by position, not a table you query.
