  • FinOps loop: Inform (tag and allocate), Optimize (act), Operate (owners, budgets, reviews).
  • Untagged spend is the first KPI; activate cost allocation tags early; accounts split cost best.
  • You pay for what you reserve. In Kubernetes that means requests; right-size with VPA in Off mode first.
  • Right-size on p95 over weeks, check memory, then consider family and Graviton.
  • Spot: up to 90% off, two-minute notice; only for stateless or checkpointed work; diversify pools; keep an On-Demand floor.
  • Compute Savings Plan up to 66% and flexible; EC2 Instance SP up to 72% and locked to a family and Region; neither guarantees capacity.
  • Commit to the floor after right-sizing; ladder purchases; watch coverage and utilisation.
  • S3 lifecycle: Standard to IA to Glacier to Deep Archive; mind 90/180-day minimums and the 128 KB default.
  • Stopped is not free: volumes, snapshots and public IPs keep billing.
  • Data transfer follows the route table: S3 gateway endpoints beat NAT; keep chatty traffic inside one AZ.
