Quick quiz

A Kubernetes cluster shows 20% CPU usage but new pods stay Pending. The most likely cause?

  1. The control plane is overloaded
  2. Pod CPU requests are far above real usage
  3. The nodes need a kernel upgrade
The scheduler packs by requests. Inflated requests make nodes look full while they are idle.

How much warning does EC2 give before reclaiming a Spot Instance (with stop or terminate behaviour)?

  1. None
  2. 30 seconds
  3. Two minutes
  4. One hour
The interruption notice comes two minutes ahead via EventBridge and instance metadata.

You will move from c5 to Graviton m7g and partly to Fargate next year. Which commitment fits?

  1. Compute Savings Plan
  2. EC2 Instance Savings Plan for c5
  3. Standard Reserved Instances for c5
Only the Compute Savings Plan follows usage across families, Regions, Fargate and Lambda.

Pods in a private subnet upload 20 TB a month to S3 in the same Region. What removes the NAT per-GB charge?

  1. A bigger NAT gateway
  2. An Elastic IP per pod
  3. A second internet gateway
  4. An S3 gateway VPC endpoint in the private route tables
Gateway endpoints have no hourly or processing charge, and their prefix-list route is more specific than 0.0.0.0/0.

You archive build artifacts to Glacier Deep Archive and delete them after 14 days. What happens?

  1. You save money
  2. You are billed for the 180-day minimum anyway
  3. The delete is refused
Deep Archive has a 180-day minimum storage duration; early deletes pay a prorated fee. Expire short-lived data in Standard.

What should you do before buying a 3-year Savings Plan?

  1. Buy at the peak of the busiest hour
  2. Move all workloads to Spot
  3. Right-size, then commit to the steady floor
Committing before right-sizing locks waste in; committing to the peak leaves the plan under-utilised.

An instance is stopped. Which of these still bills?

  1. Its EBS volumes and any public IPv4 address
  2. Its vCPU hours
  3. Nothing
Compute stops, but storage and public IPv4 addresses keep billing until deleted or released.
