Gotchas

  • Commit after right-sizing, never before. A 3-year commitment on an over-sized fleet locks the waste in.
  • Averages lie. Right-size on p95/p99 over weeks, including month-end batch jobs, or you will cause throttling or OOM kills.
  • Memory is invisible by default. EC2 CloudWatch metrics have no memory without the agent; shrinking on CPU alone can break a memory-bound service.
  • Stopped is not free. EBS volumes, snapshots and public IPs bill while instances are stopped.
  • Spot and stateful do not mix. A single-replica database on Spot is an outage waiting for a two-minute notice.
  • Spot without diversification gets reclaimed all at once when one popular instance type runs dry.
  • Glacier minimum durations charge you for 90-180 days even if you delete early; small objects under 128 KB are skipped by default for a reason.
  • NAT for S3 is the classic surprise line; gateway endpoints are free.
  • Cross-AZ chatter is billed both ways; a service mesh retrying across AZs multiplies it.
  • Tag activation is not retroactive; activate cost allocation tags before you need the report.
  • Cost is a trade-off, not a target. Removing replicas until you miss the SLO moves cost into incidents; judge headroom cuts against the error budget (hld-13 SLOs), and make cost a line in design reviews (tools like Infracost comment the cost delta of a Terraform plan on the pull request).
