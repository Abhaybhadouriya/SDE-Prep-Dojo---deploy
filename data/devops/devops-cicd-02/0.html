The story: the bakery with one oven

Explain like I'm 5

A bakery sells the same cake in five shops. Every morning the head baker follows one recipe card. She mixes, bakes, and then tastes a slice. If the taste is right, she puts the cake in a box and writes a batch number on it, like "Batch 42". The box goes on a big shelf in the warehouse.

Now the shops ask for cake. Nobody bakes again. The van just takes Batch 42 from the shelf to the first shop. If customers there are happy, the van takes the very same Batch 42 to the other four shops. If something is wrong, everyone knows exactly which batch to pull back, because the number never changes and nobody is allowed to scribble on a box after it is sealed.

The recipe card is pom.xml. The baking steps are the Maven lifecycle. The head baker who works every morning without being asked is Jenkins or GitHub Actions. The warehouse is Nexus or Artifactory. And "bake once, ship the same box everywhere" is the rule called build once, promote many.

Hold on to that picture. Every section below is one part of the bakery, with its real name, real config and the mistakes that bite real teams.
