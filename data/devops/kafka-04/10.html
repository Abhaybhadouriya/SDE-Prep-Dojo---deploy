Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Inside a group, one partition goes to exactly one consumer; across groups everyone gets everything.
  • Max parallelism of a group = number of partitions. Extra consumers sit idle.
  • The group coordinator is the broker leading the __consumer_offsets partition that the group hashes to.
  • Rebalances: eager stops everyone; cooperative and KIP-848 move only what changes. Static membership skips rebalances on quick restarts.
  • Commit after processing = at-least-once; make handlers idempotent. The committed offset is the next record to read.
  • auto.offset.reset=latest by default; empty-group offsets expire after 7 days.
  • Lag = log-end offset - committed offset, per partition. Watch the trend and the time behind.
