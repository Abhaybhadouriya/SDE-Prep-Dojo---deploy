Quick quiz

A topic has 6 partitions. Group "billing" has 8 consumers. How many consumers are reading?

  1. 8
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 1
Each partition goes to one consumer in the group, so only 6 can be busy. The other 2 are idle standbys.

Groups "billing" and "analytics" both read topic orders. A record is consumed by billing. What happens for analytics?

  1. It never sees that record
  2. It still reads it, with its own offset
  3. It sees it only if billing commits
Groups are independent. Reading does not delete anything; each group keeps its own offsets.

You processed records 0 to 99 of a partition. Which offset do you commit?

  1. 100
  2. 99
  3. 0
The committed offset is the next record to read, so last processed + 1.

A brand-new group with default settings subscribes to a topic holding a week of data. What does it read first?

  1. The oldest record
  2. A random partition
  3. Only new records written after it joined
auto.offset.reset defaults to latest, so without a committed offset the group starts at the end.

Processing a batch takes 7 minutes with default settings. What happens?

  1. Nothing, heartbeats keep it alive
  2. The consumer exceeds max.poll.interval.ms (5 min), leaves the group and triggers a rebalance
  3. The broker deletes the batch
Heartbeats prove the process is alive; the poll interval proves it is making progress. Missing it kicks the member out.

Which setting lets a pod restart without causing a rebalance?

  1. enable.auto.commit=false
  2. auto.offset.reset=earliest
  3. max.poll.records=1
  4. group.instance.id (static membership)
A static member that returns with the same ID before the session timeout gets its partitions back with no rebalance.

In the KIP-848 protocol, who computes the partition assignment?

  1. The group coordinator on the broker
  2. The group leader consumer
  3. The KRaft controller
KIP-848 moves assignment to the broker-side group coordinator (assignors uniform or range). The classic protocol used a client-side leader.

Log-end offset is 5,000 and the group committed 4,200 on a partition. What is the lag?

  1. 4,200
  2. 800
  3. 9,200
Lag = log-end offset minus committed offset = 800 records.
