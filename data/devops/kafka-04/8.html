Common mistakes and gotchas

  1. New group sees nothing auto.offset.reset=latest is the default. Use earliest when you want history, and remember it applies only when no committed offset exists.
  2. Rebalance storms Processing longer than max.poll.interval.ms, long GC pauses, or a Kubernetes HPA flapping between 3 and 6 pods. Each causes repeated rebalances and duplicate work.
  3. Committing offset N instead of N+1 You re-read the last record every restart. Client helpers (like committing a message object) add 1 for you.
  4. Auto-commit with async processing Offsets get committed for work still in flight: silent data loss on a crash.
  5. More pods than partitions Paying for idle consumers. Check with --describe --members how many partitions each member holds.
  6. Same group.id for two different services They split the data between them and each sees only part of it. One group per logical application.
  7. Forgetting close() A killed process without a clean leave forces the group to wait for the session timeout before its partitions move. Handle SIGTERM and close the consumer (in K8s, keep terminationGracePeriodSeconds long enough).
  8. Offsets expired A group idle for more than 7 days loses its offsets and restarts from auto.offset.reset. That can mean a day of reprocessing, or skipping everything.
