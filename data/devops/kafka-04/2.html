Who decides which consumer gets which partition?

must know

Every group has a group coordinator. It is not a special server. It is one of the normal brokers, chosen like this: Kafka stores every group's offsets in an internal topic called __consumer_offsets (50 partitions by default). The group is mapped to one of those partitions with hash(group.id) % 50, and the broker that leads that partition is the coordinator for the group. Different groups land on different brokers, so the coordinator work is spread across the cluster.

The coordinator tracks who is in the group (heartbeats) and runs the rebalance. The actual "who gets what" math is done by an assignor. Watch a group grow, one consumer at a time. Click Next or Play.

  1. One consumer. C1 starts alone in group billing, so the coordinator gives it all 4 partitions.
  2. C2 joins. The group membership changed, so the coordinator starts a rebalance: the assignment must be recomputed.
  3. Split. The assignor gives C1 two partitions and C2 the other two. Each resumes from the group's committed offsets.
  4. C3 joins. A "sticky" assignor moves as little as possible: only P3 moves, from C2 to C3. P0, P1 and P2 keep their owners.
  5. C4 joins. Now 4 consumers for 4 partitions, one each. Only P1 had to move.
  6. C5 joins. There is nothing left to give. C5 gets zero partitions and waits as a standby.
  7. C2 crashes. C2 stops heartbeating. After the session timeout (45 s by default) the coordinator removes it and P2 goes to the idle C5, which resumes from P2's last committed offset.

The assignors you will hear about

Three words first (each gets its own picture in the next section): eager = during a rebalance every consumer drops all its partitions and the whole group pauses; cooperative (or incremental) = only the partitions that change owner pause; new protocol (KIP-848) = the broker computes the assignment plan, not one of the consumers. The KIP numbers are just the Kafka Improvement Proposal documents where each idea was designed.

The consumer default is partition.assignment.strategy=[RangeAssignor, CooperativeStickyAssignor]. The first one in the list wins, so out of the box you get Range with eager rebalancing. The second entry is there so a team can switch to cooperative with a rolling restart. With the new protocol you do not set this at all; the broker uses uniform unless the client asks for another with group.remote.assignor.
