  • Topics are named domain.event; the family is orders.created, .enriched, .retry, .dlq. Auto-create is off.
  • Key = what must stay in order. ShopStream uses customer_id. Watch for hot keys; never change the partition count casually.
  • Partitions cap a group's parallelism: 6 partitions, at most 6 useful consumers per group.
  • RF 3 + min ISR 2 + acks=all + idempotence: survive one broker loss, no loss, no producer duplicates.
  • Kafka transactions give exactly-once Kafka to Kafka only. The DB hop uses at-least-once + upsert.
  • Transient errors: retry topic with backoff and a cap. Poison: DLQ now, with error and origin headers.
  • State the guarantee per hop, never as one word.
