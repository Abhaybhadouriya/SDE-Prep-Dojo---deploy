Every component, in one table

ShopStream has five programs of our own, one Kafka cluster, one database and four tools. Each of our programs is about one to two hundred lines of Python, and each one exists to show one Kafka idea. Click a file name to read the real code.

Notice the shape. No program talks to another program directly. Every arrow goes into or out of a topic. That is what "event-driven" means in practice: services agree on topics and event formats, not on each other's addresses. You can stop, restart or replace any single box without the others noticing more than some lag.
