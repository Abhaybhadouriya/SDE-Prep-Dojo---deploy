Design trade-offs and what changes at scale

Every choice in ShopStream has a reasonable alternative. Use the sentence pattern "I picked X because A; the cost is B; if C happens I would switch to Y."

At 10x and 100x

  • 10x orders (a big sale). The enricher and sink scale out to 6 instances each; beyond that you would have needed more partitions from the start. The sink batches up to 500 rows per DB transaction already; next you would look at the database (connection pooling, partitioned tables).
  • Many teams. Add a Schema Registry, topic ownership and naming conventions, quotas per client, and ACLs (who may write orders.created).
  • Multiple regions. One Kafka cluster per region, MirrorMaker 2 for disaster recovery, and a clear statement of how much data you can lose on a region failure (the RPO, recovery point objective: the most recent minutes of data you accept losing).
  • Long history. Tiered storage (production-ready since Kafka 3.9) moves old segments to object storage, so 7 days can become 90 without buying 13 times the disks.

Real companies did exactly these steps. Walmart, with more than 25,000 consumers, found rebalances causing lag and built a messaging proxy that pushes records to consumers while keeping per-key order. Uber built a push-based consumer proxy for similar reasons. Both are the same idea as the new Kafka share groups. The CDC pipeline design shows the outbox/CDC variant end to end.
