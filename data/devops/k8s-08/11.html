Remember this

  • Container layer: gone on restart. emptyDir: gone with the pod. PV: outlives pods.
  • Pods claim (PVC, namespaced), PVs supply (cluster-scoped), StorageClasses are recipes for CSI drivers.
  • PVC to PV binding is one-to-one and done by the PV controller in kube-controller-manager.
  • Three verbs: provision (external-provisioner, CreateVolume), attach (attach/detach controller, VolumeAttachment), mount (kubelet, NodeStage + NodePublish).
  • RWO = one node (not one pod). RWOP = one pod. RWX needs a shared filesystem.
  • WaitForFirstConsumer creates the disk in the pod's zone; after that the pod is pinned to that zone.
  • Reclaim Delete deletes the real disk with the PVC; use Retain plus backups for precious data.
  • Kafka, databases: one PVC per replica (StatefulSet / operator), durability across zones from app-level replication.
