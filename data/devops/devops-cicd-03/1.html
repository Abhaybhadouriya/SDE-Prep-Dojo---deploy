From your world

Rolling is ISSU card by card. Blue-green is VRRP/HSRP: two gateways, one virtual IP, flip the master and flip it back if the new one misbehaves. Canary is unequal-cost load sharing: put a weight of 5 on the new next hop and 95 on the old one, watch the counters, then shift. GitOps is a daemon that keeps running-config equal to a startup-config that lives in git, and reverts any hand edit made on the box.
