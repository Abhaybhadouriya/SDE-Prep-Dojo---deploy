The story: moving house without losing a letter

Explain like I'm 5

Your family is moving to a new house, but letters keep arriving every day, and Grandma sends one every morning. If you just pack up and leave, Grandma's letters go to an empty house and get lost.

So you do it in small, safe steps. First you get the new house and put a mailbox there, but you still live in the old one (expand). Then you ask the post office to deliver to both houses. You carry the old letters from the old house to the new one, a few boxes each day (migrate). When every letter is in the new house, you start reading your letters only there. When you are sure nobody sends anything to the old address any more, you finally give the old house back (contract).

At every step, if something goes wrong, you can walk back to the old house and nothing is lost. Only the very last step, giving the old house back, cannot be undone. That is why you do it last, and only when you are sure.

The houses are database columns. The letters are rows. Grandma is your live traffic, which never stops. And the reason you cannot "just move" is the same reason you cannot just rename a column: during a release, the old version and the new version of your app are running at the same time.
