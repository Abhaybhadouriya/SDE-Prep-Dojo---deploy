Real teams, real choices

CompanyWhat they didThe lesson
AtlassianMoved StreamHub from Kinesis to Kafka at about 145 B events/dayManaged is easy to start; at huge volume, cost and control can flip the choice
FlipkartTalked publicly about migrating parts of its async bus from Kafka to Pulsar (2024 talk)Multi-tenancy and storage-compute split matter for a shared platform
SlackPut Kafka in front of its Redis job queue after Redis ran out of memory in an outageKafka as a durable shock absorber; Redis stays the fast work queue
Uber, WalmartBuilt consumer proxies that read Kafka and push to services, with retries and DLQsKafka partitions limit work-queue parallelism; KIP-932 now targets this gap
PinterestMoved some big pipelines from Kafka to MemQ on S3Trading latency for cost is a valid design choice

Notice that none of these teams "picked the best system". Each one fixed a specific pain (cost, memory, parallelism, tenancy) and paid a specific price. That is the attitude interviewers want to hear.
