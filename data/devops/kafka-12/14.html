Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Two shapes: a queue hands out work and deletes it; a log keeps history and readers keep their own offsets.
  • Kafka: partitioned log, replay, many consumer groups, ordering per partition, big ecosystem, real ops cost.
  • RabbitMQ: exchanges route to queues (direct, topic, fanout, headers), push + ack, quorum queues for durability, Streams for replay.
  • SQS: zero-ops queue, visibility timeout, at-least-once; FIFO orders per message group; SNS fans out to many queues.
  • Pulsar: stateless brokers + BookKeeper storage, tenancy and geo-replication built in, more moving parts.
  • Kinesis shard = 1 MB/s or 1,000 records/s in; Event Hubs speaks the Kafka protocol.
  • Answer with "X because A, cost B, switch to Y if C" - never "X is the best".
