Quick quiz

A new analytics service must process the last 3 days of order events. Which shape makes this easy?

  1. A queue, because messages wait there
  2. A log, because messages are kept and the new group can start from an old offset
  3. Either, it makes no difference
Queues delete messages once acked, so history is gone. A log keeps data for the retention period and a new consumer group can start from the beginning or a timestamp.

In RabbitMQ, which exchange type copies a message to every bound queue and ignores the routing key?

  1. direct
  2. topic
  3. fanout
  4. headers
Fanout is broadcast. Direct needs an exact key match, topic matches patterns with * and #, headers matches header values.

An SQS worker takes 2 minutes per job but the visibility timeout is 30 seconds. What happens?

  1. The message reappears and another worker processes it too, causing duplicates
  2. SQS waits for the worker automatically
  3. The message is moved to the DLQ immediately
When the timeout expires without a delete, the message becomes visible again. Set the timeout above the worst-case job time or extend it with ChangeMessageVisibility.

What is Pulsar's main architectural difference from Kafka?

  1. Pulsar has no partitions
  2. Pulsar brokers are stateless and data lives in a separate BookKeeper storage layer
  3. Pulsar stores data only in memory
Pulsar separates serving (brokers) from storage (bookies), so each layer scales independently. Kafka brokers store their partitions on local disk.

A Kinesis stream must accept 30 MB/s of writes. What is the minimum number of shards?

  1. 3
  2. 15
  3. 30
  4. 60
Each shard accepts up to 1 MB/s (or 1,000 records/s) of writes, so you need at least 30 shards, plus headroom in practice.

Your team already runs Kafka 4.4 and needs a work queue where 20 workers share one slow topic with 4 partitions. What should you look at first?

  1. Kafka share groups (KIP-932)
  2. A classic consumer group with 20 consumers
  3. Global ordering with one partition
A classic group gives at most 4 active consumers for 4 partitions. Share groups let many consumers read the same partitions with per-record acks, at the cost of ordering.

Which statement about SQS Standard queues is true?

  1. They guarantee exactly-once delivery
  2. They are at-least-once with best-effort ordering, so consumers must be idempotent
  3. They keep messages forever for replay
Standard queues can deliver a message more than once and may reorder. Retention is at most 14 days and there is no replay.
