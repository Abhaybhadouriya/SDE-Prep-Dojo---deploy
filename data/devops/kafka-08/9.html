Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Kafka 4.x = KRaft only. ZooKeeper was removed in 4.0; 3.9 is the bridge for migration.
  • Controllers form a Raft quorum (3 or 5; 2N+1 tolerates N failures). One is active; others are hot standbys.
  • Metadata is a log (__cluster_metadata); brokers are observers that fetch it. Pull, not push.
  • process.roles = broker, controller, or both (combined: dev only). node.id identifies every node.
  • Broker heartbeat 2 s, session/lease 9 s by default; then fenced and leaders move to ISR/ELR members.
  • Lose the quorum majority: existing traffic continues, but no metadata changes or leader elections.
  • Snapshots (.checkpoint) keep the metadata log short and restarts fast.
  • Tools: kafka-metadata-quorum.sh, kafka-storage.sh format, kafka-metadata-shell.sh, kafka-dump-log.sh --cluster-metadata-decoder.
