Common mistakes and gotchas

good to know

MistakeWhat happensDo this instead
Running 2 or 4 controllersSame failure tolerance as 1 or 3, more cost.Use an odd number: 3 or 5.
All controllers in one rack/AZOne power or network event kills the quorum.Spread them over failure domains.
Combined mode in productionBroker load (GC, disk) slows the controller; cannot scale separately.Dedicated controller nodes or node pools.
Different CLUSTER_ID on one nodeNode refuses to join ("cluster ID mismatch").Format every node with the same ID.
Wiping a controller's disk and restarting itA blank voter can endanger committed data; KRaft blocks auto-format for this reason.Remove it from the quorum, reformat with --no-initial-controllers, add it back.
Reading old ZooKeeper tutorials--zookeeper flags and zookeeper.connect do not exist in 4.x.Always use --bootstrap-server (or --bootstrap-controller).
Not alerting on ActiveControllerCountNo active controller goes unnoticed until a broker fails.Alert if the cluster-wide sum is not exactly 1.
