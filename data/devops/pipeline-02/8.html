Step 6: add load, bad orders and a hot key

The load generator uses only the Python standard library, so it runs on your laptop with no installs. It sends orders from a pool of 16 threads and prints a summary every 5 seconds.

python loadgen/loadgen.py --rps 20 --seconds 120
$ python loadgen/loadgen.py --rps 20 --seconds 120
sent ok=99 err=0
sent ok=199 err=0
sent ok=300 err=0
...
DONE ok=2400 err=0

About 20 orders per second for two minutes. Random customers C-1 to C-5000, one to three items each. Now watch: the sink logs batches (upserted 37 rows ...), and after a minute analytics starts closing windows.

Poison messages go to the DLQ

python loadgen/loadgen.py --rps 20 --seconds 60 --bad 0.05          # 5 % unknown products -> DLQ

Five percent of orders now contain P-DOES-NOT-EXIST. The API accepts them (it validates the shape, not the catalog). The enricher raises PoisonMessage and routes them to orders.dlq. Look at them with their headers:

docker compose exec kafka-1 /opt/kafka/bin/kafka-console-consumer.sh --bootstrap-server kafka-1:19092 \
  --topic orders.dlq --from-beginning --max-messages 2 --property print.headers=true --property print.key=true
error:unknown product P-DOES-NOT-EXIST,origin:orders.created/4/388,failed-at-ms:1795429120417	C-2817	{"created_at":"...","customer_id":"C-2817","items":[{"product_id":"P-DOES-NOT-EXIST","quantity":2}],...}
error:unknown product P-DOES-NOT-EXIST,origin:orders.created/0/402,failed-at-ms:1795429121090	C-77	{"created_at":"...","customer_id":"C-77","items":[...],...}
Processed a total of 2 messages

Every DLQ record says what went wrong and exactly where it came from (topic, partition, offset), and keeps the original bytes. Within a minute Prometheus will fire the DLQReceivingMessages alert (visible at localhost:9090/alerts). The good orders around them were processed normally: one bad record never blocks a partition.

A hot key

python loadgen/loadgen.py --rps 20 --seconds 60 --hot-customer 0.5  # hot key: one partition gets half the traffic

Half the orders now come from C-HOT. In kafka-ui, open orders.created, partitions: one partition's message count races ahead of the other five. With a single enricher you will not feel it. With six enrichers, one would do half the work while five share the other half. That is the hot partition problem from page 01, made visible.
