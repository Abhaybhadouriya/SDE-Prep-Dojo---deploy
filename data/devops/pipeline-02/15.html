  • docker compose up -d --build starts it; docker compose ps -a checks it; down -v wipes it.
  • Healthchecks plus a one-shot topics-init container give a safe start order: brokers healthy, topics created, then services.
  • Containers use kafka-N:19092; your laptop uses localhost:9092,9192,9292. Advertised listeners must be reachable from the client.
  • kafka-consumer-groups.sh --describe shows CURRENT-OFFSET, LOG-END-OFFSET and LAG per partition: the core health view.
  • Same order_id twice = two Kafka records, one Postgres row (upsert + commit after the DB).
  • Lag = fill rate minus drain rate. Scale consumers up to the partition count; beyond that they sit idle.
  • Poison goes to orders.dlq with error and origin headers, and the partition keeps flowing.
