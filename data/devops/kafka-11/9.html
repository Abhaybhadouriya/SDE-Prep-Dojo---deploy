Common mistakes and gotchas

  1. Shipping with defaults. RF 1 and min ISR 1 mean one disk failure loses data. Set RF 3, min.insync.replicas=2, acks=all.
  2. Too few partitions, then "just add more". Keyed topics re-route keys and lose ordering for a while. Size for growth on day one.
  3. Thousands of partitions "to be safe". Each one costs files, memory, replication work and recovery time.
  4. Alerting on absolute lag. Alert on lag growth and on time-behind against your promise and your retention.
  5. Restarting the next broker too early. Wait for under-replicated partitions to reach 0 every time.
  6. Adding brokers and expecting relief. Nothing moves until you reassign partitions (or let Cruise Control do it).
  7. Reassigning without a throttle. The copy traffic starves real clients.
  8. One load balancer in front of all brokers. Clients must reach the leader of each partition directly; use per-broker listeners.
  9. Giving the JVM all the RAM. Kafka wants a modest heap and a large page cache.
  10. Turning on TLS and wondering why CPU jumped. Encryption happens in user space, so the zero-copy sendfile path no longer applies. Budget CPU for it.
  11. Deleting files in the log directory to free space. Change retention instead and let the broker delete whole segments.

You might confuse this with... partition vs replica: partitions split the data for parallelism (different data); replicas copy one partition for safety (same data). Adding partitions adds throughput; raising replication factor adds safety and costs disk and network.
