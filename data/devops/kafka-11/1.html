Where the knobs live

A message travels a short path: producer (your app that writes) -> broker (the Kafka server that stores it) -> consumer (your app that reads). Every tuning knob sits at one of those three places. If you remember the picture, you can always find the right knob.

Three questions decide almost every setting:

  1. How much data per second? This decides partition count, batch size and compression.
  2. How bad is losing a message? This decides replication factor, acks and min.insync.replicas.
  3. How fast must a message arrive? This decides linger.ms, fetch settings and how much batching you accept.

Defaults are made for laptops, not production. A fresh broker has default.replication.factor=1, min.insync.replicas=1 and auto.create.topics.enable=true. That means one copy of your data and topics appearing from typos. The usual production recipe is replication factor 3, min.insync.replicas=2, acks=all, and auto-create turned off.
