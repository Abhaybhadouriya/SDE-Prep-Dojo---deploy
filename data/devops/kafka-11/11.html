Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Partitions >= max(T / producer-per-partition, T / consumer-per-partition), and >= max consumers in a group. Measure, then add growth headroom.
  • Partitions only go up. Adding them re-maps keys and breaks per-key ordering for a while.
  • Disk = ingress per day x retention days x replication factor + headroom. Outbound network is usually several times inbound.
  • Batching (batch.size, linger.ms, compression) trades latency for throughput. Keep acks=all.
  • Page on offline partitions, under-min-ISR and controller count; watch URP, ISR churn, idle percent, p99 latency and disk.
  • Lag means nothing until you turn it into time; alert on growth and on approaching retention.
  • Rolling restart: one broker at a time, wait for URP = 0. New brokers get no data until you reassign.
  • Strimzi runs Kafka on Kubernetes through CRs; external clients need one address per broker.
