Remember this

  • Docker runs containers on one machine; Kubernetes runs and looks after containers on a whole fleet.
  • You declare desired state; controllers loop forever making actual state match (reconciliation).
  • Level-triggered loops fix drift even when events are missed.
  • Six jobs: scheduling, self-healing, service discovery, rolling updates, autoscaling, config/storage.
  • Container restart is local (kubelet, seconds); node-failure replacement is global (controllers, about 5 minutes by default).
  • Kubernetes namespace is not a Linux namespace.
  • It is not free: for one or two small services, a simpler managed platform is often the better answer.
