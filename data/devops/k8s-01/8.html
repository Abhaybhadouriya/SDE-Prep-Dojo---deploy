Must know (SDE-1)

The ideas came from Borg (and a later research system, Omega): run everything as containers, describe jobs declaratively, and let a central system place and heal them. Kubernetes rebuilt those ideas in the open with a public API. Today every big cloud sells it as a managed service: Amazon EKS, Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) and Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE).
