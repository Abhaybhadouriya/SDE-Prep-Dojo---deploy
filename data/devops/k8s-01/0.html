The story: the toy shop at night

Explain like I'm 5

You run a toy shop. You want 3 shop helpers at the counter all day. On day one you stand there and watch. When a helper goes on a break, you call a new one. When the shop gets busy, you call two more. When a helper falls sick, you find a replacement. You are tired, and at night you have to sleep.

So you hire a manager robot. You give it one note: "Always keep 3 helpers at the counter. Each helper wears a red apron." Then you go to sleep. The robot looks at the counter again and again. It counts. "I see 2 helpers. The note says 3. I will call one more." It never gets bored and it never sleeps.

You did not tell the robot how to do anything step by step. You only told it what you want. That is the big idea behind Kubernetes.

In the real world, the "helpers" are copies of your program running in containers (a container is a program packed in a box with everything it needs; see What is a Container?). The "counter" is a group of servers. The "note" is a small text file. The "robot" is Kubernetes.

Kubernetes comes from a Greek word that means helmsman or ship pilot: the person who steers the ship. That is why its logo is a ship's wheel. People shorten it to K8s because there are eight letters between the K and the s.
