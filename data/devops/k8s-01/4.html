From your world

Self-healing has two very different layers, and interviewers like it when you separate them. Container restart is local: the kubelet on that node sees the process exit and restarts it, like a supervisor process (or a hardware watchdog) restarting a stuck daemon. Replacement on another node is global: the control plane notices a whole node is gone and asks for new copies elsewhere, like an HA pair failing over to the standby chassis. The first takes seconds; the second takes minutes by default, because Kubernetes waits to be sure the node is really dead (a hold-down timer, to avoid flapping). Page 02 has the exact timers.
