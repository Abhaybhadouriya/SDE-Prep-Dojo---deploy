How to answer any Kubernetes question

Use the same five-beat shape every time. It stops you from either rambling about internals or giving a one-line textbook answer.

What a strong answer sounds like
  • Names the process that acts (scheduler, kubelet, controller-manager).
  • Uses one number you are sure of (5-minute eviction default, 10 s back-off start).
  • Mentions a failure mode without being asked.
  • Ends with a trade-off: "I would pick X because A; the cost is B."
What loses points
  • "Kubernetes heals it automatically" with no who or how.
  • Listing commands with no reasoning.
  • Ten minutes on iptables when they asked about rollouts.
  • Inventing numbers. Say "rule of thumb" or "I would measure".

Tip: when you do not know, reason out loud from first principles. "I have not used that feature, but given that everything goes through the API server and controllers reconcile, I would expect it to work like this... and I would confirm in the docs." Interviewers score the reasoning.
