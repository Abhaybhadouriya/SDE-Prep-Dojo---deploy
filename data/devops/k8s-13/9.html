Remember this

  • Answer shape: define, name the component, explain the mechanism, say what breaks, give an example.
  • Practise "kubectl apply, step by step" until it takes 60-90 seconds. It is the single best question to prepare.
  • Numbers you can trust: back-off 10 s to 5 min; node grace about 50 s plus 300 s toleration; probe period 10 s; HPA every 15 s; rolling update defaults 25%; grace period 30 s; etcd quota 2 GB.
  • Control plane down does not kill running pods (non-stop forwarding).
  • CPU over limit = throttled (invisible), memory over limit = OOMKilled (137).
  • Kafka consumers on Kubernetes scale on lag, capped at the partition count.
  • In design rounds, Kubernetes is a tool: requirements, traffic path, state, scaling, operations, cost.
  • Use your networking background as translation, then switch to Kubernetes words.
