The story: the school office

Explain like I'm 5

Our big school has an office and many classrooms.

In the office there is a front desk. Everyone who wants anything must go to the front desk. Nobody walks into the back room. Behind the desk is the big register book where the school writes down everything: which kids exist, which desk each one sits at, what the rules are. There is a seating teacher who looks for new kids with no desk and picks a classroom with free chairs. And there are hall monitors, each with one job: "keep 3 art kids", "check every classroom is still open", and so on. They keep reading the register and asking the front desk to fix things.

In each classroom there is a class helper. The helper keeps asking the front desk: "Is anyone new supposed to sit in my room?" When the answer is yes, the helper sets up the desk and seats the kid. Every few seconds the helper also shouts "my room is still here!" so the office knows the room did not burn down.

The office is the control plane. The classrooms are worker nodes. The front desk is the API server, the register is etcd, the seating teacher is the scheduler, the hall monitors are controllers, and the class helper is the kubelet.
