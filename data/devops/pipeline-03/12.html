Remember this

  • Classify every failure: poison goes to the DLQ, transient goes to a retry topic. Never block a partition.
  • Retry topics need an attempt cap, exponential backoff with jitter, and pause() instead of sleep.
  • A retry topic breaks per-key order; park the key if order matters.
  • A DLQ needs headers (error, origin), an alert, an owner, long retention and a redrive path.
  • Kafka EOS = transactional producer + offsets in the transaction + read_committed readers + stable transactional.id for fencing.
  • EOS stops at Kafka's edge. For databases: commit offsets after the DB, and upsert on a unique key.
  • Window by event time; close windows with a watermark plus allowed lateness; keep state in a changelog-backed store in production.
