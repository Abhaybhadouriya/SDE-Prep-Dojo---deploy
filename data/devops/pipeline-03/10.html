Common mistakes and gotchas

  1. Auto-commit on. The default enable.auto.commit=true commits on a timer, not when your work is done. You get loss (committed before processing, then crash) and duplicates (processed, crash before the next commit). Always commit after the work.
  2. Sleeping in the poll loop. A time.sleep(60) retry inside the consumer stalls the partition and, past max.poll.interval.ms, gets the consumer kicked out of the group. Use a retry topic plus pause().
  3. No read_committed downstream. Transactions are useless if the reader uses the default read_uncommitted: it will see records from aborted transactions.
  4. Forgetting to rewind after an abort. The consumer position does not roll back with the transaction. Seek back to the committed offsets, as rewind() does.
  5. Hard-coded or random transactional.id. Hard-coded: replicas fence each other. Random per start: no zombie fencing at all, because the new instance never matches the old one. Use a stable per-instance id (pod name).
  6. Thinking EOS covers the database. It does not. External side effects need idempotency keys or upserts.
  7. A plain INSERT in the sink. Every crash between DB commit and offset commit then makes a duplicate row.
  8. A DLQ with no alert, no owner and short retention. It becomes a silent bin where data goes to die.
  9. Grouping windows by processing time. A lag spike or replay then piles hours of orders into "now".
  10. Treating every error as retryable. A malformed record loops through the retry topic forever. Classify errors; cap attempts.
