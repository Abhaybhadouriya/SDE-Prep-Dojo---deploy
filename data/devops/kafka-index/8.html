Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Kafka is a shared, append-only log of events kept on disk; producers write, consumers read at their own pace.
  • Reading does not delete. Old data goes away only by retention (7 days by default) or compaction.
  • It replaces N x M point-to-point pipes with N + M connections to one hub.
  • Kafka 4.x runs without ZooKeeper (KRaft mode). Current line: 4.4.
  • Must-know pages: 01-07, 12 and 13. Then build the pipeline project.
