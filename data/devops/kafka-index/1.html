Why companies need it: the spaghetti problem

Before Kafka, LinkedIn had the classic problem every growing company hits. Every system that made data was wired directly to every system that needed it. Four sources and four destinations means up to sixteen custom pipes, each with its own format, its own retry logic and its own way of breaking at 3 a.m. Kafka replaced the spaghetti with a hub: everyone writes to Kafka once, and everyone reads from Kafka once. Sixteen pipes become eight.
