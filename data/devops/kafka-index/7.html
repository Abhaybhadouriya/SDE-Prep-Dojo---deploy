How to study this track for interviews

Every page follows the same climb: a story first, then the real names with a picture, then how it works inside, then interview questions. Use it like this:

  1. Read the story and the first diagram. If you can retell the story to a friend, you have the intuition.
  2. Click through every stepper. The animated diagrams show the moving parts (a record being batched, a partition leader failing). Pause on each step and predict the next one.
  3. Type the lab commands. Break things on purpose: read a topic with two consumers, kill the broker mid-send, add partitions and watch keys move.
  4. Say the 60-second answer out loud. Interviews are spoken. Record yourself once per page.
  5. Do the quiz, then the Q&A. Answer each question out loud before you open the model answer.

When Kafka comes up in a design round, interviewers listen for three phrases early: "the partition is the unit of ordering and parallelism", "I key by X so all events for one X stay in order", and "delivery is at-least-once, so my consumers are idempotent". You will be able to say all three, and defend them, by page 06.
