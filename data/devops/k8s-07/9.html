Remember this

  • Same image everywhere; settings come from ConfigMaps, credentials from Secrets. Both are namespaced and limited to 1 MiB.
  • Consume as env vars (fixed at start) or volume files (refreshed by the kubelet, eventually; never with subPath).
  • The kubelet swaps a ..data symlink atomically when files change; your app still has to reload.
  • Roll config like code: versioned names, checksum annotations, immutable: true.
  • Secrets are base64, not encrypted. Protect with RBAC, encryption at rest (KMS v2 envelope encryption), node scoping, tmpfs files.
  • External stores (Vault, cloud managers) via External Secrets Operator or CSI; Sealed Secrets/SOPS for git; best of all, workload identity.
  • Missing key = CreateContainerConfigError; check with kubectl describe pod.
