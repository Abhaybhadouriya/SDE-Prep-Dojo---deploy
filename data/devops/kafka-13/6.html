Whiteboard drill: the Kafka picture in 30 seconds

The learner input asked for one diagram you can redraw from memory, at most six boxes. Practise drawing it in this order while you talk. Click Play.

  1. Producer and key. Say what produces events and what the key is, and why ("all events of one order stay in order").
  2. Topic and partitions. Three rows with offsets. Mention ordering is per partition.
  3. Durability line. One sentence: replication factor 3, min ISR 2, acks all.
  4. Consumer groups. Two groups that read the same data independently. Parallelism = partitions.
  5. Failure path. Retry topics and a DLQ so one bad record never blocks a partition.
  6. Monitoring. Lag per group, under-replicated partitions, DLQ depth.
  7. The summary sentence. End with the guarantees. That is the whole drill: about 30 seconds.
