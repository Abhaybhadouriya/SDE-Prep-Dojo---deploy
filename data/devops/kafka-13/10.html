Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Lead with the magic phrase: at-least-once, ordered per partition, key by X, idempotent consumers.
  • Durability recipe: RF 3, min ISR 2, acks all, unclean election off, check the send callback, commit after processing.
  • Parallelism per group = partitions. Extra consumers idle. Share groups (4.2+) break that limit without ordering.
  • Exactly-once stops at Kafka's edge; use idempotency keys, upserts or offsets-in-DB for external effects.
  • Rebalances: watch max.poll.interval.ms, use cooperative or KIP-848, static membership on K8s.
  • Failure kit: retry topics, DLQ with metadata, re-drive tool, alerts on lag trend and DLQ depth.
  • For scenarios: facts, hypothesis, safe short-term fix, root cause, prevention. Never "just restart".
