The 6-step answer pattern

Our research notes suggest one order that works for nearly every Kafka design question. It keeps you from rambling, and it makes you mention the things SDE-2 interviewers wait for: guarantees, failure modes and monitoring.

When you do not know: reason out loud from first principles. "I have not tuned that setting, but since the consumer must poll within max.poll.interval.ms or be kicked out of the group, a slow batch would trigger a rebalance, so I would shrink max.poll.records first." Interviewers reward this far more than a memorised flag.

The magic phrase: "Kafka gives me at-least-once delivery and ordering per partition, so I key by X and make the consumer idempotent." Say it early in any Kafka design. It shows you know the two things Kafka does and does not promise.
