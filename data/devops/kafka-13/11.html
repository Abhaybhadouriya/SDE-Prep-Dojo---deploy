Quick quiz

A topic has RF 3, min ISR 2 and producers use acks=all. Two brokers holding replicas of partition 5 go down. What happens to writes to partition 5?

  1. They succeed with only the leader
  2. They are silently dropped
  3. They fail with NotEnoughReplicas until a replica returns
With only one in-sync replica left, the ISR is below min ISR 2, so acks=all writes are rejected. Kafka chooses durability over availability here.

Consumers keep leaving the group with a "poll interval exceeded" message. What is the first thing to change?

  1. Raise session.timeout.ms
  2. Reduce max.poll.records or speed up per-record processing
  3. Add more partitions
The member did not call poll() within max.poll.interval.ms because a batch took too long. Heartbeats are separate, so the session timeout is not the cause.

Your consumer charges credit cards. Which technique actually prevents double charges?

  1. An idempotency key per payment stored with a unique constraint and sent to the provider
  2. Kafka transactions on the producer
  3. Setting acks=all
Kafka's exactly-once covers Kafka-to-Kafka. The external side effect needs its own idempotency.

A new consumer group starts and reads nothing although the topic has old data. Why?

  1. The topic is compacted
  2. auto.offset.reset defaults to latest
  3. The group coordinator is down
With no committed offsets, the group starts at the end by default. Use earliest or reset offsets to read history.

Lag is huge on one partition and near zero on the others. What is most likely?

  1. The whole cluster is too small
  2. Too few partitions
  3. A hot key or a stuck consumer on that partition
Uneven lag points to skew or a single bad consumer or poison pill, not to overall capacity.

Why must Kafka on Kubernetes expose each broker separately to outside clients?

  1. Clients send each partition's requests to that partition's leader broker
  2. Kubernetes forbids load balancers for TCP
  3. Brokers do not support TLS behind a proxy
After bootstrap, clients connect directly to leaders using advertised listener addresses, so each broker must be reachable on its own address.

Which statement about adding partitions to a keyed topic is true?

  1. Existing records are rehashed into new partitions
  2. New records for many keys go to different partitions than before, breaking per-key order for a while
  3. It is impossible in Kafka
Old records stay where they are; only new records use the new modulo, so a key's history and its new events live in different partitions.

In the 6-step answer pattern, which steps most separate an SDE-2 answer from an SDE-1 answer?

  1. Clarify and draw
  2. Draw and guarantees
  3. Failure modes and monitoring
Naming failures with fixes and saying what you would monitor, without being prompted, is what SDE-2 interviewers listen for.
