  • Lag = log end offset minus committed offset, per partition per group. Watch the trend, convert it to time, compare it to retention.
  • Five signals: lag, throughput, error outcomes (retry/DLQ), under-replicated partitions, user-facing success and latency.
  • Read dashboards outside-in: users, throughput, lag by group, lag by partition, logs.
  • RF 3 + min ISR 2 + acks=all: one broker can die with no loss; two brokers down means writes are refused (consistency over availability).
  • A hard kill costs a session timeout (45 s here); a clean SIGTERM leave rebalances at once.
  • Replay = reset offsets (group stopped, dry-run first); safe only with idempotent sinks and event-time logic.
  • A hot key lives on one partition; more consumers do not help.
