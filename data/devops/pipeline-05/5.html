Chaos drills: break it on purpose

Every drill exists as bash (.sh) and PowerShell (.ps1) in scripts/chaos/. Run them from pipeline/code with the full 3-broker stack up. For each drill: what to run, what you will see, and why. Keep Grafana open in another window; it is much more fun that way.

DrillbashPowerShell
1. Kill a brokerbash scripts/chaos/01-kill-broker.sh kafka-2.\scripts\chaos\01-kill-broker.ps1 -Broker kafka-2
2. Stall a consumerbash scripts/chaos/02-pause-consumer.sh 30.\scripts\chaos\02-pause-consumer.ps1 -Seconds 30
3. Poison messagesbash scripts/chaos/03-poison-message.sh.\scripts\chaos\03-poison-message.ps1
4. Rebalancebash scripts/chaos/04-rebalance.sh.\scripts\chaos\04-rebalance.ps1
5. Replaybash scripts/chaos/05-replay.sh.\scripts\chaos\05-replay.ps1
On Kubernetesbash scripts/chaos/k8s-drills.sh broker.\scripts\chaos\k8s-drills.ps1 -Drill broker

If PowerShell blocks scripts: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\chaos\01-kill-broker.ps1.
