Drill 3: poison messages

The script writes three bad records straight into orders.created with the console producer (not JSON, a missing items field, an unknown product), sends one good order, and reads orders.dlq with headers.

What you see: three records in the DLQ, each with an error and an origin header (topic/partition/offset); the good order is processed; the DLQ end-offset span panel steps up by about 3 (plus one per enricher transaction, because commit markers also take offsets; usually 4 here, since the three records share a key and normally one batch); Enricher outcomes shows a small dlq bump; the DLQReceivingMessages alert fires after a minute.

Why: the enricher classifies these as PoisonMessage and routes them inside the batch transaction, so the partition never blocks. The full output and code are on Stream Processing, Retries & DLQ.
