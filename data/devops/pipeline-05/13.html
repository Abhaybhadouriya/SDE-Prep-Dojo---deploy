Common mistakes and gotchas

  1. Alerting on a raw lag number with no duration. Every deploy pages someone. Use a duration (for: 2m), a trend, or a Burrow-style status.
  2. Only watching total lag. A hot partition hides inside a healthy-looking sum. Keep a per-partition panel.
  3. No alert on under-replicated partitions. You are one more broker failure from refusing writes and you do not know it.
  4. A DLQ nobody watches. It is silent data loss with extra steps.
  5. Lag that silently goes "down" because the consumer crashed. If a group has no committed offsets or no members, some exporters show nothing at all. Alert on "no data" too, and on consumer health.
  6. Resetting offsets on the wrong group, or with members running. The tool refuses with active members; with the wrong group you replay into a sink that is not idempotent. Dry-run first.
  7. Chaos in production first. Start in a lab, like this one. Have a stop button and a hypothesis ("ISR goes to 2, writes continue") before you break anything.
  8. Liveness probes that kill slow consumers. A long GC or a big batch fails the probe, the pod restarts, the group rebalances, lag grows, more probes fail. Probe the process, not throughput.
  9. Forgetting the consume rate when reading lag. Always ask "how many seconds is that?"
