How the numbers get to your screen

URLWhat it is for
http://localhost:3000Grafana: "ShopStream - Kafka lag & pipeline health" (anonymous viewer)
http://localhost:9090Prometheus: run queries, see targets (/targets) and alerts (/alerts)
http://localhost:8080kafka-ui: browse topics, messages with headers, consumer groups
http://localhost:9308/metricsraw kafka-exporter output, if you want to see the source

Grafana panels empty? Wait 30 seconds for the first scrapes, then open http://localhost:9090/targets: every target should be UP. (This is straight from the project's troubleshooting table.)
