Common mistakes and gotchas

What goes wrong
  • App listens on 127.0.0.1 inside the container, so -p "does nothing".
  • Using localhost to reach another container (it means the same container).
  • Relying on the default bridge and wondering why names do not resolve.
  • Hard-coding container IPs; they change when the container is re-created.
  • Publishing a database with -p 5432:5432 on a cloud VM: it is now open to the internet, and ufw will not stop it.
  • Docker's 172.17/16 or 172.18/16 clashing with a corporate VPN route.
  • MTU too large inside VPNs and overlays: large transfers hang.
  • Expecting EXPOSE to open a port.
What good looks like
  • One user-defined network per app; connect by name.
  • Split frontend / backend networks; --internal for the data tier.
  • Publish only the entry point (proxy or API); bind admin ports to 127.0.0.1.
  • Firewall rules in DOCKER-USER.
  • Pick non-clashing address pools in daemon.json on corporate laptops.
  • Debug with nsenter, tcpdump, conntrack, netshoot.
