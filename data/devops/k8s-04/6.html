Deployment strategies: Recreate, blue/green, canary

Must know (SDE-1)

The Deployment object itself knows only two strategies: RollingUpdate (default) and Recreate (kill all old pods, then start new ones - a short outage, but never two versions at once; useful when two versions must never share a database or a lock). The fancier strategies are built on top:

StrategyHow on K8sGoodCost / risk
RollingBuilt inNo extra infra, gradualTwo versions live; rollback is another rollout
RecreateBuilt inNever two versions at onceDowntime during the swap
Blue/greenTwo Deployments + switch Service selector or routeInstant switch and instant rollback2x capacity during the switch
CanaryTwo Deployments + weighted Gateway API route, or a service mesh; automated by Argo Rollouts / FlaggerReal users test the new version, small blast radiusNeeds good metrics and automatic analysis

A cheap "poor man's canary" without any extra tool: run web-stable with 9 replicas and web-canary with 1 replica, both with label app=web. The Service spreads traffic across all 10 pods, so roughly 10% hits the canary. It is crude (the split follows pod counts, not exact weights), but it works anywhere.

Go deeper. Blue/green, canary and the database side of zero-downtime releases (expand-migrate-contract) are in Zero-Downtime Releases.
