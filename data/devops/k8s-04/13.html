Quick quiz

You change only replicas: 3 to replicas: 6. What happens?

  1. A new ReplicaSet is created and a rollout starts
  2. The existing ReplicaSet is scaled to 6; no rollout
  3. All pods restart
The pod template did not change, so the pod-template-hash is the same. It is a plain scale of the current ReplicaSet.

Which process creates a replacement pod object when a pod of a ReplicaSet is deleted?

  1. kubelet
  2. kube-scheduler
  3. The ReplicaSet controller in kube-controller-manager
  4. etcd
The controller watches pods, sees the count is below desired and POSTs a new pod. The scheduler then only picks a node; the kubelet only runs it.

replicas: 4 with default maxSurge and maxUnavailable. What is the maximum number of pods during a rollout?

  1. 5
  2. 4
  3. 6
  4. 8
25% of 4 = 1 surge, so 4 + 1 = 5 pods at most, and at least 4 - 1 = 3 available.

A new image cannot be pulled. With maxUnavailable: 0, what do users see?

  1. An outage
  2. Nothing - old pods keep serving and the rollout stalls
  3. Half the requests fail
New pods never become available, so the controller never scales old pods down. After progressDeadlineSeconds the rollout is marked failed, but it does not roll back on its own.

Which workload fits a log-shipping agent that must run on every node?

  1. Deployment
  2. StatefulSet
  3. Job
  4. DaemonSet
A DaemonSet runs one pod per (matching) node and adds one automatically when a node joins.

Why add a preStop sleep of a few seconds?

  1. To make the image download faster
  2. To delay the readiness probe
  3. Because endpoint removal and SIGTERM happen in parallel, so traffic may still arrive briefly
kube-proxy on every node (and external load balancers) need a moment to stop sending traffic. The sleep keeps the app serving until they catch up.

An HPA manages your Deployment, but your YAML still says replicas: 3. What is the risk?

  1. Every apply resets the count to 3, possibly during peak traffic
  2. The HPA stops working forever
  3. Nothing
apply sets the field to the file's value; the HPA then has to scale back up. Remove the field when an autoscaler owns it.
