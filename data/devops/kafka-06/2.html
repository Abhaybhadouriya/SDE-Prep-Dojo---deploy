Where messages get lost or doubled

must know

Before fixing anything, find the holes. A Kafka message makes three hops, and each hop has its own failure. Watch the happy path loop, then read the red marks:

  1. Hole A - the producer side. The producer sends a batch. The broker writes it, then the network drops the acknowledgement (or the broker is slow and the request times out). The producer cannot tell "never arrived" from "arrived, reply lost". If it retries, the log may hold the record twice. If it does not retry, the record may be lost. With acks=1 there is a second loss path: the leader acknowledged but died before followers copied it (see Replication & ISR).
  2. Hole B / D - the consumer side. A consumer does two things for every batch: process it, and commit the offset (the bookmark, see Consumers & Groups). A crash between the two either skips or repeats work, depending on the order.
  3. Hole C - the outside world. The consumer charges a card. The gateway charges it, but the reply times out. The consumer retries. Kafka cannot help here at all: the card network is not part of Kafka.

Each hole has a different fix. That is why "how do you get exactly-once?" has a layered answer.
