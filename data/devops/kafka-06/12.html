Key takeaways

Remember this

  • You can only choose "maybe lose" or "maybe repeat". Exactly-once = at-least-once + deduplication.
  • Consumer: commit before processing = at-most-once; process then commit = at-least-once. Rebalances replay uncommitted work.
  • Idempotent producer (default since 3.0): PID + epoch + per-partition sequence; dedups retries in one session; needs max.in.flight of 5 or less.
  • Transactions: stable transactional.id, coordinator + __transaction_state, offsets inside the transaction, COMMIT/ABORT markers.
  • Zombies are fenced by epoch: the newest instance wins.
  • Consumers must set isolation.level=read_committed; they read up to the LSO, so long or hanging transactions stall them.
  • EOS covers Kafka-to-Kafka only. For databases and APIs: upserts, dedup tables, offsets in the sink, idempotency keys, outbox.
