Common mistakes and gotchas

good to know

You might confuse these:

  • retention.ms vs offsets.retention.minutes. retention.ms (topic level, 7 days by default) decides how long the data in a topic is kept. offsets.retention.minutes (broker level, 7 days by default) decides how long a consumer group's committed offsets are kept after the group becomes empty. If a group is offline longer than that, it loses its bookmark and restarts from auto.offset.reset, even though the data may still be there.
  • cleanup.policy=delete vs compact. delete throws away whole old segments by time or size: you keep the recent history of everything. compact keeps at least the latest record for every key, however old: you keep the current state of everything. You can set compact,delete to get both.
  • Segment vs partition. A partition is the logical ordered log (one per leader, replicated). A segment is one file of that log on disk (about 1 GiB by default, segment.bytes). Retention, compaction and tiered storage all work on whole segments; ordering and replication work on partitions.
  • Log end offset vs high watermark. The LEO is the next offset the leader will write; the high watermark is the end of data copied to the whole ISR. Consumers only read up to the high watermark.
MistakeWhat actually happensDo this instead
Giving the broker JVM most of the RAMLess page cache, so tail reads hit disk and GC pauses grow.Keep the heap modest; leave RAM to the page cache.
"acks=all means it is on disk"It means it is in the page cache of every in-sync replica, not fsynced.Spread replicas across racks/AZs; that is the real durability.
Expecting retention to delete at exactly 7 daysOnly closed segments are deleted; the active one can hold old data.Set segment.ms if deletion deadlines matter.
Using compaction as a databaseNo point lookups; old versions linger until the cleaner runs.Materialize into a KTable, cache or DB.
Sending null keys to a compacted topicThe broker rejects the write.Always key compacted topics.
Turning on TLS without CPU headroomZero-copy is lost; broker CPU jumps.Load test with TLS on before production.
Thousands of tiny partitions per brokerMany open files and mmapped indexes, slower recovery and restarts.Size partitions from throughput math (see Ops & Tuning).
Big batch backfill on the same brokers as latency-critical tail readsCold reads evict hot pages; tail latency spikes.Throttle the backfill, use tiered storage, or separate clusters.
