Common mistakes and gotchas

  1. ImagePullBackOff on your own images. You forgot kind load docker-image, or the tag differs, or the pull policy is Always. kubectl describe pod shows the exact image it tried.
  2. Liveness probe that checks dependencies. One Kafka blip and every pod restarts together. Dependencies belong in readiness.
  3. HPA shows <unknown>. No metrics-server, or the container has no CPU request (the percentage has nothing to divide by).
  4. Consumer replicas above the partition count. Extra pods are idle and still cost a rebalance each time they come and go.
  5. A hand-made HPA next to a KEDA ScaledObject. They fight. KEDA owns the HPA.
  6. minAvailable: 1 on a 1-replica app. Node drains hang forever.
  7. Hard-coded transactional.id in a Deployment. All replicas share it and fence each other. Use the pod name.
  8. Exposing Kafka with one LoadBalancer. Clients are redirected to broker addresses they cannot reach. Use per-broker external listeners.
  9. replicas: in an autoscaled Deployment. Every kubectl apply resets the count, and the autoscaler has to undo it. For a consumer that means an extra rebalance. Leave the field out.
  10. A Secret listed in kustomization.yaml plus a hand-made one. The next apply -k overwrites the real one. Pick one source.
  11. Real passwords in the committed Secret. Secrets in git are leaked secrets. Use a secret manager.
  12. deleteClaim: true in production. Deleting the Kafka resource then deletes the data.
  13. Using install/latest forever. Your cluster changes version without a code review. Pin it.
