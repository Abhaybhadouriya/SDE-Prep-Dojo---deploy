  • An operator = custom resource + a controller that reconciles it. Strimzi turns Kafka, KafkaNodePool and KafkaTopic YAML into a running, safely-managed cluster.
  • No depends_on in Kubernetes: apps retry, readiness gates traffic.
  • Liveness checks the process; readiness may check dependencies; startup protects slow boots.
  • Stateless API: HPA on CPU (needs requests + metrics-server). Consumer: KEDA on lag, max replicas = partitions.
  • Pod name as HOSTNAME gives each enricher a unique transactional.id.
  • PDB: maxUnavailable: 1 for apps that can run at 1 replica; never block drains.
  • Kafka clients must reach each leader broker: external access needs per-broker addresses.
