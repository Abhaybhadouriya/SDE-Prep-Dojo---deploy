The story: the shopkeeper's notebook

Explain like I'm 5

A shopkeeper puts toys on shelves every morning from a list: "3 red cars, 2 blue balls". To know what to add, she also keeps a notebook: "I put red car #1 on shelf A, red car #2 on shelf B...". Each morning she compares the list, the notebook, and the shelves, and only fixes what is different.

If she loses the notebook, she cannot tell her toys from the toys the shop next door left on her shelves. If two helpers write in the notebook at the same time, the pages get scribbled over. And if she writes the safe's code in the notebook, anyone who reads it can open the safe.

In Terraform Basics, your .tf files were the list. The notebook is the state file, terraform.tfstate. This whole page is about the three problems in the story: what the notebook holds, how a team shares it without scribbling (remote backend + locking), and how to stop it from leaking secrets.
