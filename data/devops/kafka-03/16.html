Resources

  • Apache Kafka: Producer configs doc - every producer setting with its current default; keep it open while tuning.
  • Apache Kafka: Design doc - read "The Producer" (load balancing, asynchronous send) and "Message Delivery Semantics".
  • KIP-794: Strictly Uniform Sticky Partitioner paper - why keyless records stick to one partition per batch (the default since 3.3).
  • KIP-98: Exactly Once Delivery and Transactional Messaging paper - the design that introduced producer IDs, sequence numbers and transactions.
  • Confluent: Kafka internals course course - short lessons on the produce request path, replication and transactions.
  • confluent-kafka-python repo - the Python client used in this lab and in the ShopStream pipeline code; see its examples folder.
