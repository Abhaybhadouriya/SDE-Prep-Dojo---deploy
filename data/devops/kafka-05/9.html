Key takeaways

Remember this

  • Each partition: 1 leader + followers. Followers pull from the leader. Leaders are spread over brokers.
  • ISR = replicas caught up within 30 s (replica.lag.time.max.ms). Lag is measured in time.
  • HW = min LEO over the ISR. Consumers read only below the HW (committed data).
  • acks=all waits for the whole current ISR; min.insync.replicas sets a floor on the ISR size.
  • Production recipe: RF=3 across AZs, min ISR 2, acks=all, unclean election off.
  • Failover: controller detects (about 9 s), elects from ISR/ELR, bumps the epoch, clients refresh metadata.
  • Durability comes from replication, not per-message fsync.
