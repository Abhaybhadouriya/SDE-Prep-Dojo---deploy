Common mistakes and gotchas

good to know

  1. min.insync.replicas = RF Looks "extra safe" but any single broker restart (even a planned rolling upgrade) blocks writes.
  2. RF=2 with min ISR 2 Same problem: one broker down = no writes. RF=2 with min ISR 1 = acks=1 risk. RF=3 is the sweet spot.
  3. Thinking acks=1 means "on disk" It means "in the leader's page cache". Durability comes from copies.
  4. Internal topics with RF 1 In a single-broker dev setup, __consumer_offsets and __transaction_state get RF 1. Do not copy dev configs to prod.
  5. Ignoring URP alerts UnderReplicatedPartitions > 0 for long means you are one failure away from NotEnoughReplicas or data loss. OfflinePartitionsCount > 0 is an outage.
  6. Leaders piling on one broker After failovers, if preferred leader election is off, one broker may lead most partitions and become the hotspot.
  7. Rolling restarts too fast Restart the next broker only when URP is back to 0, or two brokers may be out of sync at once. Operators such as Strimzi do this check for you (see Operations & Kafka on K8s).
