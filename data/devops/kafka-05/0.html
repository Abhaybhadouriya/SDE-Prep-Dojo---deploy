The story: three lockers

Explain like I'm 5

You wrote a very important story. You do not want to lose it, so you make photocopies and put them in three different lockers. One locker is the main one: whenever you add a new page, you put it there first. Two helper friends check the main locker all the time and copy every new page into their own lockers.

The teacher keeps a list on the wall: "Lockers that are up to date". If a friend is slow and falls far behind, the teacher crosses their locker off the list until they catch up.

One day the main locker gets stuck shut. No problem! The teacher looks at the list, picks a locker that is up to date, and says "this is the main locker now". Nobody loses a page, because only pages that were in every up-to-date locker were ever counted as "saved".

In Kafka, the lockers are replicas of one partition, each on a different broker (server). The main locker is the leader. The helpers are followers. The list on the wall is the ISR (in-sync replicas). The teacher who picks a new leader is the controller. The line that says "everything up to here is safely saved" is the high watermark. That is the whole page; the rest is detail and numbers.
