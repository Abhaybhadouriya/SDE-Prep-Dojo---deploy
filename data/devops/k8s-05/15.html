A Service exists and DNS resolves, but every connection fails. What should you check first?

  1. The Kubernetes version
  2. Whether its EndpointSlices contain any ready pod IPs

Why is a pod's MTU often 1450 with Flannel VXLAN on a 1500-byte network?

  1. VXLAN adds 50 bytes of outer headers
  2. Kubernetes reserves 50 bytes for labels
  3. TCP needs it
Outer Ethernet 14 + IP 20 + UDP 8 + VXLAN 8 = 50 bytes.
