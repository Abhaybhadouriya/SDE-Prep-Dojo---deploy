The story: the school phone number

Explain like I'm 5

Your school has many teachers. Teachers come and go: one is sick today, a new one joins next week. If you had to remember every teacher's own phone number, you would always be calling the wrong person.

So the school has one office number. You call the office, and the office connects you to any teacher who is free right now. The number never changes, even when the teachers do. And there is a phone book on the wall: you look up "Maths office" and it tells you the number.

In Kubernetes, the teachers are pods. Pods are replaced all the time (a rollout, a crash, a node failure), and every new pod gets a new IP address. The office number is a Service: one stable virtual IP and name in front of a changing group of pods. The phone book is cluster DNS (CoreDNS): it turns the name backend into the Service's IP.

Underneath that, there is a second question: how does a packet from a pod on machine 1 even reach a pod on machine 2? That is the job of the CNI plugin (Container Network Interface), which builds the "roads" between machines. We will cover both, from the simple picture down to the iptables rules.
