Remember this

  • Defence in depth: image, runtime, host, pipeline. Each ring assumes the inner one can fail.
  • Smallest image wins: multi-stage, distroless or hardened bases, numeric non-root USER.
  • Secrets: BuildKit --mount=type=secret at build, files at runtime. Never ARG, ENV or a deleted layer.
  • Hardened run: --cap-drop=ALL, no-new-privileges, --read-only, limits, --init, log rotation. Never --privileged.
  • The Docker socket and the docker group equal root. Prefer rootless BuildKit or remote builders in CI.
  • Published ports bypass ufw (DNAT before INPUT). Filter in DOCKER-USER or bind to 127.0.0.1.
  • Patch runc, containerd and the kernel: escapes like CVE-2019-5736 and CVE-2024-21626 live there, not in your image.
  • Build once, scan, sign, deploy by digest; promote the same digest through every environment.
