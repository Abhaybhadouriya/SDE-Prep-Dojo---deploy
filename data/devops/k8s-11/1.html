The four signals and where they live

Must know (SDE-1)

Every running pod gives off four kinds of signal. Each one flows to a different place. The picture shows the common open-source setup. Watch the dots: they are data moving.

SignalWhat it answersWho writes itQuick tool
StatusIs it running, waiting, or dead right now?kubelet (pod status), scheduler (node choice)kubectl get pods
EventsWhat did Kubernetes try, and what went wrong?scheduler, kubelet, controllerskubectl describe, kubectl events
LogsWhat did my program say?Your app (stdout/stderr), saved by the runtimekubectl logs
MetricsHow much CPU, memory, requests, errors, over time?kubelet (cAdvisor inside it), kube-state-metrics, your appkubectl top, Prometheus, Grafana
TracesWhich service made this request slow?Your app, via an OpenTelemetry libraryJaeger / Tempo UI

You might confuse this with: a pod phase and a container reason. The phase is one of only five words: Pending, Running, Succeeded, Failed, Unknown. Words like CrashLoopBackOff, ImagePullBackOff and OOMKilled are not phases. They are reasons attached to one container's state. kubectl get pods shows whichever is most useful in the STATUS column, which is why it looks like there are dozens of states.
