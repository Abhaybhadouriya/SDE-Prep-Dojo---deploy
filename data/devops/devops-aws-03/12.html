  1. Allowed - resource policies win
  2. Allowed - IAM and bucket policy agree
  3. Denied - an explicit deny anywhere wins
  4. Depends on Block Public Access
Explicit deny in any applicable policy beats every allow.

  1. Nothing - the object is gone
  2. All old versions plus a new delete marker
  3. Only the latest version

A simple DELETE on a versioned bucket adds a delete marker; removing the marker restores the object.

What is the smallest allowed size of a multipart part (other than the last part)?

  1. 1 MiB
  2. 8 MiB
  3. 5 MiB
  4. 100 MiB
5 MiB to 5 GiB per part, up to 10,000 parts. 8 MB is only the CLI's default chunk size.

A Lambda signs a pre-signed URL with ExpiresIn of 7 days. It stops working after a few hours. Most likely cause?

  1. S3 caps URLs at 1 hour
  2. It was signed with temporary role credentials that expired
  3. Clock skew on the client
  4. The bucket is versioned
A URL is valid only while the signing credentials are valid.
