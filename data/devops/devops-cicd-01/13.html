Remember this

  • Commits are hashed snapshots with parent pointers; branches are movable labels; HEAD is where you are.
  • Team loop: short feature branch, PR, CI checks, review, merge, delete branch.
  • Trunk-based for continuous delivery; GitFlow-style branches for multi-version, scheduled releases.
  • Merge keeps history and adds a join; rebase replays commits with new hashes for a straight line.
  • Golden rule: never rebase commits others have built on. Push rewritten branches with --force-with-lease --force-if-includes, never a bare --force.
  • Squash the WIP noise, not separate logical changes; delete a branch after it is squash-merged.
  • Resolve conflicts by understanding both intents, then test; ours/theirs swap during rebase.
  • Merge queues test the real future main, catching semantic conflicts that green PRs miss.
