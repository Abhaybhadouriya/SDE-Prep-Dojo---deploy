  • StatefulSet = stable name (kafka-0), stable DNS (headless Service), stable storage (volumeClaimTemplates), ordering.
  • Start 0 to N-1, delete and roll N-1 down to 0; partition gives a built-in canary.
  • Deleting a StatefulSet keeps its PVCs unless a retention policy says otherwise.
  • Unreachable node: StatefulSet pods are not auto-replaced (at most one identity) until the old pod is confirmed gone.
  • CRD = new noun in the API; operator = controller with expert knowledge running a level-triggered reconcile loop.
  • Strimzi: Cluster Operator (Kafka, KafkaNodePool) + Entity Operator (KafkaTopic, KafkaUser); pods via StrimziPodSets.
  • Strimzi rolls brokers one by one and checks min ISR first; Drain Cleaner routes node drains through it.
  • External Kafka clients need one reachable address per broker - not just one bootstrap load balancer.
