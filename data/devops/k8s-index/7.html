  • Kubernetes (K8s) runs and looks after containers across many machines.
  • You describe what you want (desired state) in YAML; Kubernetes keeps making reality match it.
  • It has a control plane (decides) and worker nodes (run containers), like a router's control and data planes.
  • The must-know pages are 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 09, 11 and 13.
  • kind gives you a free 3-node cluster on your laptop in about a minute.
