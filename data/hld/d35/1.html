Requirements

We design Hello Interview's version (YouTube top-K videos) and keep Amazon's per-category twist in mind.

Functional
  • Return the top K items (K up to 1,000) for fixed windows: last hour, last day, last month, all time.
  • Optionally per category (Amazon: 1,000 categories) or per region.
  • Windows are tumbling (aligned to the hour or day) by default; sliding windows are a follow-up.
Out of scope
  • Arbitrary time ranges ("between 3:17 and 5:42 last Tuesday").
  • Personalized ranking (that is Recommendation System).
  • Fraud and bot filtering beyond a mention.
Non-functional requirementTarget
Ingest throughputAbout 700,000 view events per second (70 billion per day)
Read latencyTens of milliseconds - so results must be precomputed, not computed per request
FreshnessA view may show up in the counts up to about 1 minute late
AccuracyStart with exact; discuss when approximate is acceptable (and say so explicitly)
AvailabilityReads stay up even if the pipeline is behind (serve the last good result)

Ask early: "Exact or approximate?" and "Which windows?" Those two answers decide the whole design. Exact + all-time is a database problem; approximate + sliding is a streaming-sketch problem.
