Why not a time-series DB? Prometheus or InfluxDB would make every video id a separate series - billions of series. Hello Interview calls that out as a bad fit: high cardinality kills them and a top() becomes a full scan. Real-time OLAP stores (Druid, Pinot, ClickHouse) do fit, but bring their own operations cost (see Ad Click Aggregator).

From your world

The minute buckets are a ring buffer indexed by minute % 60, exactly like a NIC descriptor ring or the per-CPU stats rings you have used: overwrite the oldest slot, keep a running sum. Heavy hitters are what network people call elephant flows - HeavyKeeper, the algorithm behind Redis Top-K, came from finding top-K elephant flows in switches. Same problem, different packets.
