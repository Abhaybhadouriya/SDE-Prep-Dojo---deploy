Why are new rules deployed in shadow mode first?

  1. To make them faster
  2. To see what they would block on live traffic before they affect customers
  3. Because rules need training
A typo in a rule can block huge numbers of good payments in seconds; shadow mode measures that first.

Which signal catches card testing (many different cards, once each, at one merchant)?
