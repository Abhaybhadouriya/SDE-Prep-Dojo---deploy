How real companies do it

  • Stripe Radar: scores every payment in under 100 ms using more than 1,000 characteristics of the transaction; fraud is about 1 in 1,000 payments; about 0.1% of legitimate payments get blocked. Outputs allow, block or an extra check (3D Secure). In 2022 Stripe moved from an ensemble (XGBoost + a neural network) to a DNN-only model and cut training time by over 85%, to under 2 hours.
  • Apache Flink fraud detection case study: the Flink blog's multi-part series builds a fraud engine with dynamic rules: each rule declares its grouping key and window, events are re-keyed at runtime, and rules reach every task through broadcast state.
  • Razorpay: described on the AWS Big Data blog building real-time anomaly detection with Debezium CDC from transactional databases, stream processing, and Amazon MSK (managed Kafka). A good Indian fintech reference for the streaming half of this design.
  • Feature stores (Feast): open-source online/offline feature store with point-in-time joins, the standard answer to training-serving skew.

Related designs: payment system (where the score is used), digital wallet and ledger, ad click aggregator (windowed streaming aggregation), recommendation system (feature store, offline/online ML), rate limiter (velocity counters), CDC pipeline, big data and streaming, stream processing in the pipeline project.
