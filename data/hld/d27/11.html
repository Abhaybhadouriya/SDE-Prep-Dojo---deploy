  • Three paths: sync scoring (< 100 ms), streaming features (seconds), offline training (hours, with weeks-late labels).
  • Precompute features; the scorer only does batched point reads in parallel.
  • Kafka keyed by card -> Flink sliding windows in event time -> online store. Plus a sync 60 s counter for the freshness gap.
  • Rules for fast, explainable policy; ML for subtle patterns; shadow mode before either goes live.
  • Fail-open with a local fallback policy, never "no timeout".
  • Precision and recall, thresholds by money; accuracy is meaningless at 1 in 1,000.
  • Point-in-time joins stop leakage; one feature definition stops skew.
  • Stale features fail silently: alert on Flink lag in seconds.
