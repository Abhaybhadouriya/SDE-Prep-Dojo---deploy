How real companies do it

  • Uber: real-time push platform (RAMEN). Uber describes a server-to-app push system over long-lived streaming connections (moving to gRPC), with message priorities and time-to-live so stale updates are dropped. A good read on the "in-app, live" side of notifications. Uber blog (opens in a browser; it blocks some scripts).
  • Flipkart Connekt. Flipkart's notification platform. A vendor case study from Lightbend reports that moving it to Akka reactive microservices raised throughput from about 500 to 10,000 requests per second and cut infrastructure by 75% (vendor-reported figures). Case study.
  • ByteByteGo: Design a notification system. The standard interview treatment: notification servers, a queue per channel, workers, third-party providers, plus dedupe, preferences, rate limits, retries and templates. Chapter.
  • AWS Builders' Library: Avoiding insurmountable queue backlogs. How Amazon thinks about backlogs, dropping stale work and protecting latency-sensitive traffic. Directly relevant to OTP vs campaign. Article.
  • Apple and Google docs. Sending notification requests to APNs (HTTP/2, status codes, collapse IDs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (topics, collapse keys, error codes).
