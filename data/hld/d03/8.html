"What if this box fails?"

  • APNs or FCM slow or down: workers hit timeouts, messages go to retry topics, lag grows. Nothing is lost; high-priority TTLs decide what is still worth sending when it recovers.
  • SMS vendor down: circuit breaker opens; OTPs fail over to the second vendor within seconds.
  • A worker pod dies: the consumer group rebalances and another pod resumes from the last committed offset; dedupe hides the redelivered messages.
  • Kafka broker dies: partition leaders move to in-sync replicas; with RF 3 and min ISR 2, writes continue and no acknowledged message is lost.
  • Preference store down: the router uses its cache; if the cache misses, fail safe: send transactional messages (the user needs their OTP) but hold promotional ones (never spam someone who opted out).
  • A bug sends wrong text to 1M users: the kill switch per campaign stops the planner; replay from Kafka is how you resend correct text later.

Common gotchas: one shared queue for all priorities; retrying permanent errors forever; sleeping inside the consumer to wait for retries (blocks the partition); ignoring user time zones (3 AM promos); never cleaning dead device tokens; marking "sent" before calling the provider; logging full OTPs or personal data in plain text.
