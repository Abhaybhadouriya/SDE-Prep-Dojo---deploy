Requirements

First line: "Let me check which channels, which kinds of messages and what volume we are talking about, because OTPs and marketing campaigns behave very differently."

  1. Channels? Push (iOS, Android, web), SMS, email, in-app. (All four.)
  2. Kinds of messages? Transactional (OTP, payment done, order shipped) and promotional (campaigns to millions). (Both.)
  3. Scale? Assume an e-commerce app with 100M daily active users, about 10 notifications per user per day on average, plus campaigns of up to 100M users.
  4. Speed? OTPs within a few seconds; order updates within a minute; campaigns within an hour.
  5. User control? Opt-out per channel and category, quiet hours, frequency caps. (Yes.)
  6. Tracking? Sent, delivered, opened, failed per notification. (Yes, for dashboards and debugging.)

Functional

  • Accept send requests from internal services: one user or a segment (campaign).
  • Pick channels using user preferences and the message category.
  • Templates with parameters and languages.
  • Schedule for later, respecting the user's time zone and quiet hours.
  • Deliver via APNs, FCM, SMS and email providers; keep an in-app inbox.
  • Track status; expose it to the caller.

Non-functional

  • Priority isolation: OTP latency must not grow during a campaign.
  • Reliable: no silent loss; at-least-once delivery inside our system.
  • No duplicates as far as possible (two OTP SMSes cost money and confuse users).
  • Scalable to tens of thousands of sends per second at peak.
  • Polite: respect provider rate limits and user frequency caps.
  • Available: if one SMS vendor is down, fail over to another.

Out of scope (say it): building our own push connection to phones (APNs and FCM do that), the campaign targeting UI, and the machine-learning that decides which promo to send. We are the delivery platform.
