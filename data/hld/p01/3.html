OptionHow a sender finds the gatewayStrengthWeakness

The one sentence to say: "Two hops: SSE or WebSocket to the client, and a registry plus pub/sub to reach the right gateway. The socket is only a notifier; the store is the truth, so a client that reconnects catches up from the store."

Why push wins
  • Instant updates and far fewer empty requests.
  • Saves mobile battery and data (Messenger's 40% data saving).
  • Enables typing dots, presence and collaboration.
What push costs
  • Stateful servers: deploys, draining and rebalancing get harder.
  • Reconnect storms, idle timeouts, heartbeats.
  • You still need a durable store and a resume protocol.
