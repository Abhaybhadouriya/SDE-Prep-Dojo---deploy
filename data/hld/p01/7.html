How real companies do it

  • Slack: stateful Channel Servers mapped by consistent hashing (about 16 million channels per host at peak), ring managers that replace a failed host in under 20 seconds, Gateway Servers in many regions holding WebSocket subscriptions, Presence Servers hashed by user, and roughly 500 ms worldwide delivery. They also see traffic spikes at the top of every hour from reminders and scheduled messages.
  • Discord: one Elixir session process per WebSocket, one guild process per server that fans out to sessions; up to 500,000 sessions per VM; Manifold to parallelise fan-out across nodes; a semaphore to stop stampedes.
  • WhatsApp: Erlang on tuned FreeBSD, over 2 million TCP connections on one box in 2012.
  • LinkedIn: SSE over Play and Akka with one actor per connection; 100,000 connections per node after fixing backlog, fd and heap limits.
  • Netflix Zuul Push: WebSocket and SSE push cluster with a local plus global (Redis/Cassandra/DynamoDB) registry and reconnect dithering.
  • Facebook Messenger: snapshot + delta over MQTT through the Iris service.
  • Figma: WebSockets to one server process per document.
