How real companies do it

  • Shopify: edge leaky-bucket checkout throttle in Nginx + Lua after a 2016 flash sale took down a whole database shard; then a stateless fair queue using signed timestamps and a PID-controlled threshold.
  • Ticketmaster: Verified Fan pre-registration (about 3.5 million registered, about 1.5 million codes sent), queues and bot defence; 3.5 billion requests on the Eras Tour presale, 4x their previous peak.
  • Walmart: Kafka partitioning by item-store so a single consumer owns each key's inventory.
  • LMAX: a single-threaded business logic processor doing about 6 million orders per second without locks.
  • Kubernetes: leader election through Lease objects with optimistic concurrency in etcd.
