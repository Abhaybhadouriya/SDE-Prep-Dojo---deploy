  • Two systems: an async, write-heavy upload pipeline and a read-heavy, CDN-served watch path.
  • Bytes go straight to blob storage through short-lived presigned URLs, in resumable parts.
  • Split at aligned keyframes, fan out one idempotent task per (segment, profile), fan in, package HLS/DASH.
  • At-least-once tasks plus deterministic output keys make retries and spot preemption harmless.
  • Cost follows views: basic ladder for all, extra codecs and 4K for popular videos.
  • Kafka carries lifecycle and view events with pointers, never the video bytes; long tasks need per-task ack.
  • Metadata is small: sharded SQL (Vitess) plus cache. View counts come from a stream, not row increments.
