The one-line rule: static points with uneven density, read-heavy (Yelp): geohash in the database or an in-memory quadtree. Moving points, write-heavy (Uber): in-memory cell index (Redis GEO, or S2/H3 shards), never a disk B-tree. Shapes (zones, geofences): R-tree in PostGIS. Per-area counts (surge, heat maps): H3.
