Where Kafka, Kubernetes and Docker fit

Concrete settings. Partition key booking_id keeps one booking's events (held, paid, ticketed, cancelled) in order. Consumer-group sizing: booking volume is modest, so 12 partitions (12 pods per group at most) is plenty; the search tier is the big fleet. The search HPA scales on in-flight requests, because a search waits on slow suppliers and CPU stays low while it waits.
