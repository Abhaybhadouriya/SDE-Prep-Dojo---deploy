Failure modes

  • Supplier down: breaker opens, cached results only, flagged "price to be confirmed".
  • Price changed at re-price: show new total, require confirmation. Track as quote-accuracy metric.
  • Hold expires during slow payment: our timer is shorter than the supplier's; if payment completes late, retry hold; if seats are gone, void authorisation and apologise.
  • Orchestrator crash mid-saga: state is in the DB; a recovery worker resumes from the last persisted step after retrieving supplier PNR status.
  • Schedule change after booking: consume schedule events, re-validate connections (is the layover still above MCT?), notify and offer rebooking.
