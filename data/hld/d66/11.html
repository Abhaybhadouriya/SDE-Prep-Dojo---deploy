  • Two systems: cache-first, fan-out search (AP) and correctness-first booking (CP).
  • Look-to-book can be about 1,000:1: shield suppliers with caches whose TTL depends on days to departure; always re-price before booking.
  • Connections from the schedule are precomputed; prices are fetched at search time.
  • The airline or GDS owns the seats; we get a time-limited hold. Our own lock does not stop other platforms.
  • Every seat priced: fare bucket + seat fee, frozen in a signed price token.
  • Multi-segment = saga: hold all, authorise, ticket all, capture; compensate in reverse; retrieve before retry.
  • Suppliers behind adapters with deadlines, bulkheads and breakers; reconcile daily.
  • Review rounds: rank issues by impact, minimal fixes, a test for each.
