Requirements

Functional

  1. Search: one-way and round-trip by origin, destination, dates, passengers and cabin. Include connecting itineraries with at most one stop (the CRED constraint). Sort and filter by price, duration, stops, airline, departure time.
  2. Aggregate suppliers: results come from 20-200 suppliers (airlines via direct or NDC APIs, GDSs, consolidators), as in the Agoda design-review prompt.
  3. Price check: before booking, re-price the chosen itinerary live (cached prices can be wrong).
  4. Seat selection: show the seat map; every seat can have its own price (window, aisle, extra legroom, exit row).
  5. Book: hold all segments, take payment, issue tickets, confirm. A round trip or a connection is one booking with several segments that must succeed or fail together.
  6. Manage: view, cancel, refund, e-ticket by email and app. Premium members see member prices (the ClearTrip variant).

  • Search is AP: available and fast; a slightly stale price is acceptable if re-checked before payment.
  • Booking is CP: consistent; better to fail a booking than to sell a seat twice or charge without a ticket.
  • Supplier cost and limits: every live supplier call may cost money and is rate-limited. Amadeus writes that look-to-book ratios once as low as 10:1 can now easily run to 1,000:1, so search load must be shielded from suppliers.
