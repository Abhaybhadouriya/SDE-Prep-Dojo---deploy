Resources

  • Hello Interview: Design a News Aggregator like Google News course - the requirements (availability over consistency, 200 ms, 30-minute freshness) and the deep-dive list; the full answers are premium.
  • Manku, Jain, Das Sarma: Detecting Near-Duplicates for Web Crawling (WWW 2007) paper - SimHash at Google, f = 64 and k = 3 for 8B pages, the permuted-table trick.
  • Das, Datar, Garg, Rajaram: Google News Personalization (WWW 2007) paper - MinHash, PLSI and co-visitation; Bigtable; latency budget; update vs serve split.
  • Broder: On the Resemblance and Containment of Documents (1997) paper - shingles and MinHash; 30M AltaVista documents clustered.
  • W3C WebSub Recommendation doc - hub discovery, subscription, verification of intent, signed content distribution.
  • Google Search Central: News sitemaps doc - 2-day window, 1,000 entries per sitemap, required tags.
  • RFC 9309: Robots Exclusion Protocol doc - 24-hour cache, 500 KiB parse limit, 5xx means disallow.
  • Google blog: The new Google News (2018) blog - storylines, Full Coverage unpersonalised, "For you" briefing.
  • System Design Primer: Web crawler repo - content signatures, recrawl freshness, caching popular queries.
