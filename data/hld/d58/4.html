Why the article id is a hash of the canonical URL. The same article often arrives under several URLs: with ?utm_source=rss, with and without www, via AMP. We first canonicalise (lowercase host, drop tracking parameters, follow rel="canonical"), then hash. The hash makes the insert idempotent: fetching the same feed twice writes the same row twice, harmlessly. That is exact-duplicate removal for free.

SQL or NoSQL? At 10 writes/s either works - say so. We pick a KV/wide-column store for articles (get-by-id, TTL, easy horizontal growth) and keep the small, relational publisher table in Postgres. More in Choosing Storage.
