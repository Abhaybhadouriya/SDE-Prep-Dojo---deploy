Requirements

We use Hello Interview's scope as the "above the line" core, and add story clustering, which Google News has done since it launched and which interviewers love as a deep dive.

Functional (in scope)
  • Show an aggregated feed of articles from thousands of publishers worldwide, by region and by category (Top, World, Tech, Sports...).
  • Infinite scroll: the reader keeps scrolling and gets the next page, with no repeats and no gaps.
  • Clicking a card redirects to the publisher's site. We show only a headline, a short snippet and a thumbnail.
  • Group copies of the same event from different publishers into one story card ("Storm - 40 sources").
Out of scope (below the line)
  • Saving articles, sharing, comments.
  • Full personalisation ("For you") - we cover it as a bonus deep dive.
  • Full-text search over news (see Post Search and Search Engine).
  • Hosting full article text (copyright: we link out).

Say the ratio out loud early: "Writes are a few hundred articles a second; reads are tens of thousands of feed loads a second. This is a read-scaling problem with a freshness constraint."
