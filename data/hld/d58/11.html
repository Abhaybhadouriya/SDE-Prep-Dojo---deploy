  • About 1,000 reads per write: precompute feeds per region x category; the request path only does lookups.
  • Ingest with push (WebSub) where offered, adaptive polling with conditional GET (304) everywhere else, and respect robots.txt.
  • Three kinds of "same": URL hash, SimHash (near-identical text, Hamming distance <= 3), MinHash/LSH or embeddings (same event).
  • Duplicates are dropped; similar articles are grouped into a story card with a lead article.
  • Infinite scroll needs a cursor (keyset or snapshot), never offset.
  • CDN + stale-while-revalidate + request coalescing for breaking-news spikes; serve stale over failing.
  • Personalise as a light re-rank on top of the shared feed, or you lose the CDN.
