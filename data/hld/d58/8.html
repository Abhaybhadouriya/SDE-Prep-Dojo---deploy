Main failure modes: a publisher changes its feed format and items silently stop (alert on items per feed per day); a wrong pubDate pushes an old article to the top (cap it at first-seen time); over-clustering merges two different storms (require shared entities and time closeness); a CDN expiry during a spike causes a thundering herd (coalesce and serve stale). Replay from Kafka after any parser fix, and keep a static snapshot of top stories in object storage as the last fallback.
