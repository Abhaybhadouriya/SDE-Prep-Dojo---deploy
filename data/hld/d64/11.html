  • Broker, not exchange: we route, check risk, and display; the exchange matches.
  • Two systems: market data (lossy, latest wins, huge fan-out) and trading (exact, state machine).
  • Conflate before fan-out: the whole market becomes about 10,000 updates/s; gateways filter per user.
  • Ingest = multicast A/B feeds, sequence numbers, gap recovery, normalise to integer prices.
  • Orders: client_order_id, in-memory risk, journal, then exchange; UNKNOWN on timeout, never resend blindly.
  • GTT: DB is truth, memory is an index; claim with a conditional update; idempotent placement; sweeper for stuck rows.
  • No dual writes: outbox, idempotent consumers, end-of-day reconciliation.
  • Square-off = GTT engine with risk-derived thresholds, plus a timed cut-off.
  • Use 22,500 trading seconds for estimates.
