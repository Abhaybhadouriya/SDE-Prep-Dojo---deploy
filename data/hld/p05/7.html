Real world

  • Notion - 480 logical Postgres shards on 32 hosts, keyed by workspace; double-write, three-day backfill, dark-read verification.
  • Figma - vertical partitioning first, then horizontal sharding on RDS Postgres with colos, a Go DBProxy that parses and plans queries, logical-before-physical sharding with under 10% view overhead in worst-case load tests.
  • Discord - hot partitions in Cassandra; moved trillions of messages to 72 ScyllaDB nodes, with a Rust migrator moving up to 3.2 M messages per second.
  • Amazon DynamoDB - documented per-partition limits and the random/calculated suffix write-sharding recipes.
  • Google Bigtable - commit log + memtable + SSTables + compaction; tablets split automatically at ~100-200 MB.
  • LinkedIn Kafka - 2 M writes/s on three commodity machines through sequential I/O and batching.
  • Shopify - flash sale overloads a shared shard; edge throttling and later a fair queue.
