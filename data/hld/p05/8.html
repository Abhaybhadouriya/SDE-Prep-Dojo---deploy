Remember this

  • Reads can be copied; writes must be split (sharding), shrunk (batching, aggregation) or delayed (queues).
  • Pick a shard key with high cardinality that is in most queries; hash monotonic keys.
  • Use many logical shards on fewer hosts so rebalancing moves buckets, not rows.
  • A hot key defeats sharding: split it into N sub-keys and sum on read or via a rollup.
  • Queues absorb bursts, not sustained overload; watch consumer lag.
  • LSM stores make writes sequential appends; they pay in compaction and read amplification.
  • Sharding costs cross-shard joins, transactions, global IDs and resharding projects - say so.
