StorageAll in RAMRAM for recent, SSD for historyTier by time: most queries want recent posts.
  • One slow shard makes every query slow (fan-out). Mitigate: replicas, hedged requests (send to a second replica after p95 time), return partial results with a flag rather than time out.
  • Indexing falls behind during a spike: queries still work on slightly stale data; scale consumers up to the partition count; alert on lag.
  • Mapping / vocabulary explosion (every random hashtag becomes a term): cap term length, drop junk, keep posting lists for very rare terms on disk.
  • Spam and duplicates: Twitter used MinHash signatures (just 4 bytes per tweet) to drop near-duplicates from results.
