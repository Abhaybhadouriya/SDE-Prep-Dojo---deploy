Requirements

Functional

  • Users create posts and like posts (these services exist; we consume their events).
  • Search posts by keyword(s), including multi-word queries.
  • Sort results by recency or by like count.
  • Paginate ("load more").

Out of scope unless asked: personalisation, privacy filtering per viewer, fuzzy/typo matching, images, live-updating result pages.

Non-functional

  • Latency: median query < 500 ms (Hello Interview's target); Twitter's real-time search reported ~50 ms average.
  • Freshness: a new post is searchable within 1 minute (Twitter got this down to about 1 second in 2020).
  • Completeness: every post, even old and unpopular ones, is findable (old ones can be slower).
  • High availability; eventual consistency is fine (a like count a minute old is OK).
  • Scale: ~1B users, high write rate from likes.
