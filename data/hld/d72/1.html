Requirements

Functional

  1. Accounts Sellers and bidders register. (The Razorpay version made them distinct: one user cannot be both. Model a role on the user, enforced at listing and bidding.)
  2. List A seller creates an auction: item details and photos, starting price, optional reserve price (hidden minimum), optional buy-it-now price, start and end time. Sellers see their listings and their state.
  3. Search and view Bidders search by text, category, price and ending-soon; the item page shows current price, number of bids, time left, and the bid history.
  4. Bid A bid is accepted only if the auction is open and the amount is at least the current price plus the increment (or beats other bidders' maximums with proxy bidding). The bidder learns at once whether they lead.
  5. Real time Everyone watching the item sees new prices and the end time within about a second; outbid bidders get a notification.
  6. Close and settle At the end time the auction closes, the winner and seller are notified, the winner pays (or the next bidder is offered the item), and the order is created.

Out of scope (say so): shipping, reviews and seller ratings, fraud scoring, recommendations. Hello Interview's version of this problem also leaves search out of the core; the Razorpay interviewer explicitly wanted it, so ask.

Ask: "Do we support proxy (maximum) bids, or only direct bids?" and "Hard close or soft close?" Proxy bidding changes the bid logic from "is this higher" to "who has the highest maximum", and a soft close makes the end time a moving value that the closer must respect.
