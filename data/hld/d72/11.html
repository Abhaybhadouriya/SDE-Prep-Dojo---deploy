  • Browse is AP and read-heavy; bids are CP and contend on one row per auction.
  • One conditional update decides a bid: live, before ends_at (DB clock), amount at least price plus increment; bump version, append bid with seq.
  • For a very hot auction, serialise its bids through one Kafka partition (or a Redis Lua script).
  • Proxy bidding: store the leader's secret max; ties go to the earlier sequence.
  • The bid check enforces the end time; the closer only announces. Soft close extends ends_at inside the bid update.
  • Outbox to Kafka, then Redis pub/sub to SSE; versioned, coalesced, refetch on gaps.
  • Search via CDC to Elasticsearch, never dual-written.
  • Settlement is an idempotent saga with a payment deadline and second chance.
