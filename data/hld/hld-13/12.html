The 60-second answer. "First I turn 'highly available' into numbers: an SLO such as 99.9% of requests good and p99 under 300 ms over 30 days, which gives an error budget of about 43 minutes a month, and an RPO and RTO per component. Inside a region I remove single points of failure across three zones, so a zone loss is a non-event. Across regions I pick by data: money and inventory get one writer per item with synchronous replication in-region and a warm standby region; feeds and catalogs run active-active with async replication and read-local. Failover is health-check driven, fences the old primary, and a human presses the button for a whole region. I limit blast radius with cells, keep point-in-time backups because replication copies mistakes too, page on SLO burn rate, and prove all of it with game days."
