A reliability checklist for any design answer

Spend two or three minutes near the end of every design on this. It is often where SDE-2 candidates separate themselves (Walmart's SDE-3 competency list names "NFR - Scalability, HA, DR, Resiliency" explicitly).

  1. State the target. "Reads 99.99%, writes 99.9%, p99 under 200 ms" and what that means in minutes.
  2. Remove SPOFs per tier. Multiple app instances across 3 AZs, a replicated DB with automatic in-region failover, a redundant LB, more than one queue broker.
  3. Pick multi-region shape and write strategy. Active-passive with write global, or active-active with write local / write partitioned, and say why given the data's invariants.
  4. Give RPO and RTO per data store and the DR tier that meets them. Add point-in-time backups and tested restores.
  5. Contain failures. Timeouts, retries with backoff, circuit breakers, load shedding, bulkheads or cells.
  6. Observe. SLIs as metrics, structured logs with trace ids, distributed tracing, burn-rate alerts.
  7. Prove it. Progressive deploys, chaos experiments, regular region-evacuation game days.
