  • Google SRE Workbook: Alerting on SLOs book - burn rates and the multiwindow, multi-burn-rate alert table used above.
  • Google SRE Workbook: Example Error Budget Policy book - what a real freeze policy looks like.
  • Google SRE Book: Monitoring Distributed Systems book - the four golden signals, symptoms vs causes, paging rules.
  • Karan Pratap Singh: System Design repo - chapters on availability (the nines table), SLA/SLO/SLI and disaster recovery.
  • System Design Primer: Availability patterns repo - active-passive vs active-active failover and availability in numbers.
  • AWS: Disaster recovery options in the cloud doc - the four DR tiers, data plane vs control plane failover, write global/local/partitioned.
  • AWS: Reducing the Scope of Impact with Cell-Based Architecture doc - cells, routers and blast radius.
  • AWS Builders' Library: Workload isolation using shuffle sharding blog - the 8-worker and Route 53 combinatorics.
  • AWS: Summary of the December 7, 2021 us-east-1 event blog - a real retry/backoff congestion outage and its lessons.
  • Netflix: Active-Active for Multi-Regional Resiliency blog - stateless services, Cassandra replication test, EvCache cross-region invalidation, Zuul load shedding.
  • Slack: Migration to a Cellular Architecture blog - AZs as cells, siloing, 5-minute drains in 1% steps.
  • Principles of Chaos Engineering doc - the four-step experiment and advanced principles.
  • Netflix Chaos Monkey repo - the open-source instance-killing tool.
  • Google: Dapper, a Large-Scale Distributed Systems Tracing Infrastructure paper - where sampled distributed tracing began.
  • OpenTelemetry: Signals doc - the standard vocabulary for traces, metrics and logs.
  • awesome-scalability repo - curated engineering-blog articles under "availability" and "stability" for further reading.
