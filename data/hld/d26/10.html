How real companies do it

  • LMAX Exchange: the best public write-up of a matching core. A single-threaded, in-memory business logic processor (about 6 million orders/s reported), the Disruptor ring buffers in front and behind it, event sourcing with snapshots, replicas fed the same input, and full restart in under a minute. Martin Fowler's article and the open-source Disruptor are the sources.
  • Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH: the public spec of a real market data feed: add-order, executed, cancel and delete messages, each sequenced, sent over multicast with retransmit and snapshot channels. Reading it shows exactly what the engine's output looks like.
  • FIX Trading Community: the FIX protocol that brokers and exchanges use for order entry and execution reports, with session-level sequence numbers and resend requests.
  • Broker side (India): Zerodha, Groww and others are web-scale apps that forward orders to NSE/BSE; their public talks focus on order management, risk and market-data fan-out to millions of app users, which is the Kafka + Kubernetes part of this page.

Related designs: digital wallet and ledger (another single-writer state machine with an append-only log), payment system, design a message queue (the log underneath), real-time leaderboard (sorted structures), fraud detection, consensus and coordination, Kafka storage internals (sequential logs, page cache).
