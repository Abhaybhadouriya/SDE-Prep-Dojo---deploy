Remember this

  • Order book = sorted price levels + FIFO per level + hash map for O(1) cancel. Price-time priority.
  • Trades execute at the resting (maker) order's price.
  • A sequencer gives one total order; the replicated journal is the truth; acks only after replication.
  • Deterministic single-threaded engine per symbol shard: replay = recovery, standby = same input, audit = replay.
  • Scale by sharding symbols, not by adding threads to one book.
  • Estimate with trading hours, not 86,400 s; all books fit in RAM.
  • Market data: multicast + sequence numbers + snapshot/retransmit.
  • Kafka, K8s and DBs live downstream and around the core, not in it.
