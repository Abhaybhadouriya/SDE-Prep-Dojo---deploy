What if this box fails?

  • Gateway dies: the broker's FIX session drops; it reconnects to another gateway and uses session sequence numbers to resend unacknowledged messages. client_order_id dedup stops double orders.
  • Primary engine dies: the standby is at the same sequence; it is promoted and starts publishing. Brokers see a short pause, nothing lost.
  • Sequencer dies: its backup (or Raft peer) takes over the log at the last replicated sequence. Inputs not yet replicated were never acked, so brokers resend them.
  • Whole data centre lost: the DR site replays the journal it received. Anything not replicated there is at risk, which is why exchanges replicate synchronously within a region and accept slightly higher latency.
  • Market data packet loss: receivers detect gaps by sequence and recover; the book is not affected.
  • Downstream (Kafka, DBs) slow: trading continues; consumers catch up from the journal or topic. This decoupling is the point.
  • Bug produces wrong trades: halt the symbol, fix, and use replay to find every affected trade; exchanges have rules for cancelling erroneous trades.
