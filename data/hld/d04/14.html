Resources

  • Discord: How Discord Stores Trillions of Messages blog - partition keys, hot partitions, request coalescing, Cassandra to ScyllaDB.
  • Discord: Scaling Elixir to 5,000,000 Concurrent Users blog - fan-out to huge guilds on the BEAM.
  • WhatsApp: 1 million is so 2011 blog - the 2M-connections-per-server post (open in a browser).
  • Rick Reed: Scaling to Millions of Simultaneous Connections paper - Erlang Factory 2012 slides; kernel and BEAM tuning.
  • Meta: Building Mobile-First Infrastructure for Messenger blog - MQTT and mobile-friendly sync.
  • HighScalability: The WhatsApp Architecture blog - secondary summary of WhatsApp's stack.
  • Twitter Snowflake repo - the classic time-sortable 64-bit id layout.
