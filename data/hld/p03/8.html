Remember this

  • No transaction spans services, Kafka and Stripe. Use sagas: local transactions plus compensations.
  • 2PC gives atomicity but blocks and needs every participant to support it; keep it inside one database system.
  • Choreography = events, loose coupling, poor visibility. Orchestration = one owner of the flow, explicit compensations.
  • Order steps: compensable first, pivot (point of no return) late, retryable after.
  • Sagas have no isolation: use a semantic lock (PENDING status) and commutative updates.
  • Outbox: business row and event row in one transaction; consumers deduplicate.
  • Durable execution = history + deterministic replay + durable timers + signals. Activities still need idempotency keys.
