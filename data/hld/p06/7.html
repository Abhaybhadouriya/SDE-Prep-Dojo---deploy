Real world

  • Amazon S3 - Warfield's "pretty big storage system" post: millions of disks, ~120 IOPS each, erasure coding across tens of drives, heat management by spreading workloads.
  • Dropbox Magic Pocket - 4 MB content-addressed blocks, metadata kept separately, two-zone replication within a second, later erasure coding; cells of about 50 PB.
  • YouTube-style upload (Hello Interview breakdown) - 5-10 MB fingerprinted chunks, presigned multipart, chunk status in metadata, one completion event into a transcoding DAG.
  • Microsoft Azure - the Valet Key pattern with shared access signatures scoped to one blob, create-only, three-minute window.
  • Netflix Open Connect - pre-positioned video on appliances inside ISP networks.
  • tus - an open resumable upload protocol over HTTP, used when you run your own upload servers.
