  • Metadata in the database, bytes in object storage; the API never streams bytes.
  • Presigned URL = valet key: one object, one verb, minutes, issued after auth, treated as a bearer token.
  • Multipart (S3: 10,000 parts of 5 MiB-5 GiB) makes uploads parallel and resumable per part.
  • Row first as PENDING; flip on the storage event; reconcile and GC orphans; abort stale multipart uploads.
  • Content-addressed blocks give dedup, integrity and cheap delta sync (Dropbox, Docker layers).
  • Downloads: CDN with signed URLs or cookies, versioned keys, range requests, segments for video.
  • Scan after upload in a quarantine area; send references, not blobs, through Kafka.
