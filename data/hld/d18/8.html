When would you not build or pick Kafka? Learner question answered (see Kafka vs RabbitMQ vs SQS vs Pulsar): for a few hundred messages a second of background jobs, a managed queue (SQS) is less to run; for complex routing and per-message priorities, RabbitMQ; for delays per message, SQS delay queues or a scheduler. Trade-off sentence: "I would pick a partitioned log because we need replay and several independent consumers at 1 GB/s; the cost is operating brokers and planning partitions; if the workload were low-volume work items needing per-message retries, I would use SQS or Kafka share groups."
