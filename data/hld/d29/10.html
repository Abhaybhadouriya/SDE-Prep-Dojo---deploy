How real systems do it

  • Twitter (2015) described rebuilding trends from a single-JVM design that could only look at a small window of Tweets into a distributed Storm streaming-MapReduce pipeline, with a data preparation stage that removes replies and low-quality or sensitive Tweets and uses real-time spam signals from its BotMaker system. Building a new trends experience (opens in a browser).
  • Twitter research (2019): "Real-time Event Detection on Social Data Streams" describes detecting events by clustering trending entities in real time, the step after "which terms are spiking". arXiv 1907.11229.
  • Summingbird, Twitter's open-source library for writing one aggregation that runs on both streaming (Storm) and batch (Hadoop) - a concrete Lambda architecture. twitter/summingbird.
  • Redis ships count-min sketch and a HeavyKeeper-based Top-K as built-in probabilistic types. Count-min sketch, Top-K.
  • Apache Flink documents the hopping/sliding windows and event-time semantics used above. Flink windows.
