Requirements

Ask before drawing. These answers are our interview assumptions.

  1. What is a "topic"? Hashtags, plus named entities and short phrases extracted from text ("Virat Kohli", "power cut"). Normalised: case-folded, #WorldCup = "world cup".
  2. Where? Global, per country, and per major city - about 400 locations.
  3. How fresh? A real event (a goal, an earthquake) should appear within about 1-2 minutes.
  4. How many? Top 30 per location. Personalisation (by interests) is a stretch goal.
  5. Accuracy? Approximate counts are fine; a wrong trend is much worse than a slightly wrong rank. No spam or offensive trends.
Functional
  • Ingest every new post (and optionally likes/reposts as weaker signals).
  • Extract and normalise terms; merge variants of the same topic.
  • Compute trend scores per location every ~30-60 seconds.
  • Serve GET /trends?location= fast to every user's home screen.
  • Show "12.4K posts" style counts and a sample of posts per trend.
Out of scope (say it)
  • Full-text search of posts (that is Post Search).
  • Writing the posts themselves (the posting service already exists).
  • Ad targeting on trends.
