Resources

  • ByteByteGo: Distributed Email Service (SDI Vol. 2) book - the classic interview chapter: estimation, metadata DB, search and deliverability.
  • Study notes on the ByteByteGo email chapter blog - a readable summary of the chapter's numbers and tables.
  • system-design-101: Design Gmail repo - one picture of Alice sending to Bob.
  • system-design-101: How is email delivered? repo - MUA, MTA, MDA with the postal analogy.
  • Microsoft: Exchange Online data resiliency doc - DAG copies, lag copy, Shadow Redundancy, Safety Net, page patching.
  • Microsoft Exchange Team: The Preferred Architecture blog - why software replication on cheap disks beat SANs.
  • Fastmail: storage architecture doc - slots, stores, replication and failover of a real mail provider.
  • Facebook Engineering: The underlying technology of Messages blog - why HBase, and the hot-small / cold-huge split.
  • Google: Ridding Gmail of 100 million more spam messages with TensorFlow blog - spam filtering at Gmail scale.
  • Google: Email sender guidelines doc - the SPF/DKIM/DMARC and spam-rate rules every sender must meet.
  • Gmail Help: attachment size limits doc - 25 MB, then Drive links.
  • RFC 5321: SMTP doc - the protocol, including the retry and give-up guidance (section 4.5.4).
  • RFC 5256: IMAP SORT and THREAD doc - the REFERENCES threading algorithm.
  • Grokking the System Design Interview II (Gmail case study) course - another interview walk-through.
