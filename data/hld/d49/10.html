How the real companies do it

  • Flipkart search serving (2016): ~231M docs, ~10K searches/s at Solr, median 11 ms. Price/stock/offers moved to a segment-independent NRT forward store and cached inverted bitsets, consumed from Kafka and bootstrapped from Redis.
  • Flipkart Sherlock ingestion (2017): Ingester pulls from each source service into Kafka and HBase; HBase snapshots let partial updates build whole documents; Storm topologies process idempotently; serving nodes consume a Kafka sink topic. ~10K updates/s average, under 30 s to serving, ~30K/s peak in Big Billion Days.
  • Flipkart AESOP: open-source change propagation (log mining in the style of LinkedIn Databus) from MySQL/HBase to Elasticsearch, HBase, MySQL and Kafka; used for payments and for the wishlist search index.
  • Flipkart Ratings & Reviews (2021): CQRS with RDBMS writes and Aerospike/Redis materialised views; data reduced over 90%, throughput up over 5x.
  • Amazon-style "sales rank by category": the system-design-primer solves it as a batch MapReduce over sales logs into a ranking table, refreshed hourly. Ranking features like this feed phase-1 boosts.
