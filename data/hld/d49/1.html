Requirements

Functional

  1. Catalog Sellers and internal teams create and update products. A product (or SKU, stock keeping unit) is "iPhone 15, 128 GB, black". A listing is one seller's offer for that product, with its own price and stock. Flipkart's search team uses exactly this split.
  2. Search Users type a free-text query ("running shoes men") and get ranked results.
  3. Browse and filters (facets) Users filter by brand, price band, size, rating, and see counts next to each value ("Nike (1,240)").
  4. Product page Shows details, images, best offer, availability and a ratings summary.
  5. Ratings and reviews Verified buyers rate 1-5 stars and write reviews with photos; others read, sort (most helpful, most recent) and vote "helpful".

Say early: "The catalog is the source of truth and is strongly consistent. Search and review read models are derived, eventually consistent views. My job is to keep the lag small and bounded, and to never sell something that is out of stock because checkout re-checks inventory."

Out of scope (say it out loud): checkout and payments (see E-commerce Checkout), inventory reservation (d11), recommendations (d48) and typeahead (d15).
