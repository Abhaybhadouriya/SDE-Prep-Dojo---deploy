  • Redis hash = state, channel = versioned signal; snapshot on connect, refetch on a version gap.
  • Alerts: one active per (sensor, rule), debounce in, hysteresis out, CAS transitions for ack / resolve / ignore.
  • Downsample with sum and count rollups; tiered retention.
