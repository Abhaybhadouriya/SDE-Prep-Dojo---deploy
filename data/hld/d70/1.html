Requirements

Always ask first: which costume are we wearing? They share 80% of the design, but the numbers and the "hard part" differ. Pick one as primary and say how the others plug in.

Functional

  1. Ingest Devices send readings: (device_id, ts, metric, value, location?). Devices may be offline for hours and send a backlog later.
  2. Live view A map or dashboard shows the latest value (or a count) per area, refreshed within a few seconds.
  3. History Query a device or an area over a time range at a sensible resolution (minute for today, hour for a month, day for years).
  4. Alerts Rules per sensor type ("temperature between 10 and 80 C"), optionally overridden per machine. When breached, open one alert; users acknowledge, resolve or ignore it.
  5. Device management Register a device, its type, owner and location; rotate its credentials; push config (sampling rate, thresholds) to it.

Out of scope unless asked: billing calculation from meter data, firmware updates, ML anomaly detection. Mention them as extensions.

Two clarifying questions that change the design: "Is the location fixed (a meter, a sensor bolted to a wall) or moving (a driver)?" Fixed means you bucket once at registration; moving means you bucket every event. And "Does the live view need the latest value, a count, or an average?" Latest-value is an upsert; counts and averages need windows.
