Trade-offs, bottlenecks and failure modes

DecisionOption AOption BSay
RunnersLong-lived VMsEphemeral podsB: clean, isolated, autoscaled; cost is cold caches, solved with remote cache.
Build toolDocker daemon (DinD / socket)Rootless BuildKitB: no root on host.
DeployPush (kubectl from CI)Pull (GitOps, Argo CD)B: no prod creds in CI, audit, drift repair.
RolloutRolling updateCanary/blue-green with analysisB for important services; A fine for internal tools.
Build platformSelf-hosted (Tekton/Jenkins/ARC)SaaS (hosted runners)SaaS for small orgs; self-host at scale for cost, network access and control.
Repo layoutMonorepoPolyrepoMonorepo needs affected-only builds, remote cache and a merge queue.

Webhook service downPushes not builtGit host retries; periodic reconciliation polls repos for missed commits.

Argo CD downNo new syncs; running apps unaffectedData plane keeps running (like a router control plane crash); HA Argo CD.

Measure the platform with DORA's metrics: deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate and time to recover from a failed deployment. They are easy to compute from the ci.run-status and release events already on Kafka.
