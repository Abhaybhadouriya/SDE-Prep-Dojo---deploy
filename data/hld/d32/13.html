  • Amazon DynamoDB: A Scalable, Predictably Performant, and Fully Managed NoSQL Database Service (USENIX ATC 2022) paper - how the managed service evolved.
  • Marc Brooker: notes on the DynamoDB paper blog - leader-based replication, metadata cache, MemDS.
  • Apache Cassandra docs: Dynamo doc - tokens, vnodes, consistency levels, gossip, phi accrual, LWW, repair.
  • Riak KV: Eventual Consistency doc - read repair, R=1 pitfalls with sloppy quorum, PR/PW.
  • ByteByteGo: Design a Key-Value Store course - the classic interview chapter.
  • Hello Interview: DynamoDB deep dive course - partition/sort keys, 400 KB items, per-partition limits, GSIs vs LSIs.
  • Discord: How Discord Stores Trillions of Messages blog - hot partitions, GC pauses, request coalescing, Cassandra to ScyllaDB.
