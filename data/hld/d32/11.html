  • Ring + virtual nodes to partition; next N distinct machines (and zones) to replicate.
  • R + W > N gives overlap on strict quorums; (3,2,2) is the classic setting.
  • Sloppy quorum + hinted handoff keep writes flowing but weaken that overlap.
  • Vector clocks detect concurrent versions; LWW is simpler but drops updates.
  • Merkle trees for long-term repair, read repair for hot keys, tombstones for deletes.
  • Gossip for membership, phi accrual for suspicion, seeds to avoid islands.
  • DynamoDB is leader-based (Multi-Paxos): know why the managed service changed course.
