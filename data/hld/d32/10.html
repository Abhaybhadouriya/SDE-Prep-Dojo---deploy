How real companies do it

  • Amazon Dynamo (2007). Shopping cart and session stores; 99.9995% successful responses in production and no data-loss events reported at publication; well over 3 million checkouts in one day handled by the cart service.
  • Amazon DynamoDB (2012-). Leader-per-partition with Multi-Paxos, TLA+-checked replication, 89.2M requests/s peak in Prime Day 2021.
  • Apache Cassandra. Dynamo-style ring, gossip, hinted handoff and Merkle repair, but Bigtable-style column data model on an LSM tree and LWW instead of vector clocks.
  • Riak. The most faithful open-source Dynamo: N/R/W, sloppy quorum, siblings, read repair, PR/PW primary quorums.
  • Discord. Trillions of messages: 177 Cassandra nodes replaced by 72 ScyllaDB nodes, plus Rust data services doing request coalescing; migrated at up to 3.2 million messages per second.
