Requirements

Functional

  • put(key, value) and get(key); optionally delete(key).
  • Small values: under 10 KB typical (ByteByteGo's version of the problem), Dynamo's objects were "usually less than 1 MB".
  • Keys are opaque bytes; no range queries required (if they are, that changes the partitioning choice, see deep dive 1).

Ask early: "Do we favour availability or consistency on a partition?" The whole design forks here. Shopping cart: availability (Dynamo). Bank balance: consistency (a leader-based, consensus-replicated store, which is what DynamoDB itself later became).
