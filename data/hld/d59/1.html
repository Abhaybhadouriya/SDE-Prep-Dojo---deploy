Requirements

Functional (in scope)
  • Create a profile with preferences: age range, who you are interested in, maximum distance.
  • See a stack of potential matches that fit your preferences and are within your distance.
  • Swipe right (yes) or left (no), one profile at a time.
  • Get a notification when two people swipe yes on each other.
Out of scope
  • Photo upload pipeline (see Handling Large Blobs).
  • Chat after the match (see Chat App).
  • Super likes, boosts and other paid features.
  • Fake-profile detection.
Non-functional requirementTarget (first four from Hello Interview; the rest are our assumptions)
Swipe consistencyStrong: if both users swipe yes, a match must always be created and both told
Scale20 million daily active users, about 100 swipes each per day
Stack latencyUnder 300 ms to load the stack
No repeatsNever show a profile you already swiped on
Match notificationBoth users told within about 1 s of the second "yes" (our assumption: the second swiper sees it in the swipe response; the first gets a push)
Stack load p99Under 300 ms at p99, not just on average (our assumption; served from a precomputed cache)
Location freshnessA moved user shows up in the new area's stacks within about 1 minute (our assumption; stacks themselves may be up to an hour old)
AvailabilitySwiping keeps working if recommendations degrade (our assumption: 99.9%+ for the swipe path)

Notice the mix: strong consistency for one tiny fact (did both say yes?) and eventual consistency everywhere else (a stack that is a few minutes stale is fine). Saying "I will spend my consistency budget only on the match check" is exactly the judgement interviewers look for.
