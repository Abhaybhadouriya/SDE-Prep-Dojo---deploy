Requirements

Open the interview by agreeing the scope. Team chat has a long feature list (huddles, apps, files, threads, emoji...). Pick the core and say the rest out loud as "out of scope unless you want it".

Functional (what it does)

  • Workspaces and channels: public and private channels, DMs and group DMs. Join, leave, invite.
  • Send and receive messages in real time, including threads (replies under a parent message) and reactions.
  • History: scroll back through all old messages in a channel; history kept per the company's retention policy (often forever).
  • Unread counts and mentions: bold channels, "3 new", red badge for @you.
  • Presence and typing: green dot for online, "Asha is typing...".
  • Multi-device: laptop, phone and browser stay in sync; read on one, cleared on all.
  • Search across everything the user is allowed to see.
  • Push / email notifications when the user is away.

Out of scope (say so): voice and video huddles (see Video Conferencing), file preview pipeline (see Handling Large Blobs), bots and app platform.

Non-functional (how well)

  • Latency: a message shows up for online members in about 500 ms worldwide (Slack's published figure for its real-time stack).
  • Durability: an acknowledged message is never lost. This is business data.
  • Ordering: messages in one channel appear in the same order for everyone.
  • Scale: tens of millions of concurrent connections; channels up to hundreds of thousands of members.
  • Availability: 99.99% for send/receive; presence and typing may degrade first.
  • Tenant isolation, compliance: data residency, retention, legal hold, export, customer-managed keys.
  • Burstiness: spikes at the top of the hour (reminders, scheduled messages, meetings starting) and morning "everyone connects" storms.

Say early: "Messages are the important data; presence and typing are cheap, lossy signals." That single sentence lets you justify a durable path for messages and a fire-and-forget path for typing, and it is exactly what Microsoft did in 2020 when it switched off Teams' typing indicator for two weeks to save capacity.
