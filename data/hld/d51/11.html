  • Team chat = big channels + permanent searchable history + tenant compliance + heavy presence. Say how that differs from WhatsApp.
  • Route by channel: channel servers on a consistent-hash ring sequence and fan out; regional gateways hold sockets and subscribe.
  • Fan out once per gateway, not once per user. Push only what is on screen.
  • Send over HTTPS with an idempotency key; receive over one WebSocket; typing is transient.
  • Messages keyed by (channel_id, time bucket) with time-sortable ids; shard by channel, not by tenant.
  • Unread = bookmark compare, not per-member counters. Real counters only for mentions.
  • Presence: heartbeat + TTL + delayed offline, in memory, subscribe to visible users, first to brown out.
  • Kafka feeds search, notifications, compliance; it is not the per-user delivery pipe.
