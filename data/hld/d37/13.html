Resources

  • Customizable Route Planning in Road Networks (Delling et al.) paper - the topology/metric split behind Bing Maps routing, with real timings.
  • Contraction hierarchies doc - clear walk-through of contraction, shortcuts and customizable CH.
  • OSRM backend repo - open-source routing engine with CH and MLD pipelines you can run in Docker.
  • DeepMind: Traffic prediction with graph neural networks blog - Supersegments and ETA gains in Google Maps.
  • Google Maps 101: how AI predicts traffic and picks routes blog - history vs live data, authoritative data, road quality.
  • Google: 20 years of Google Maps blog - scale numbers (2B users/month, 1 trillion km, 100M updates/day).
  • Uber: DeepETA blog - routing ETA + ML residual, latency constraints.
  • Uber: H3 hexagonal index blog - why hexagons, 16 resolutions.
  • Hidden Markov Map Matching Through Noise and Sparseness paper - the classic HMM map-matching method.
  • Lyft: Map matching when the map is wrong paper - on/off-road tracking for map learning.
  • OpenStreetMap: slippy map tile names doc - z/x/y maths and tile counts per zoom.
  • karanpratapsingh/system-design: Geohashing and Quadtrees repo - geohash precision table and quadtree basics.
  • ByteByteGo SDI Vol 2: Google Maps book - full chapter (paid) for a second walkthrough.
  • On this site: Proximity Service, Geo & Proximity pattern, Geo & prefix indexes, Estimation, Food Delivery.
