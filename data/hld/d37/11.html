  • Two paths: read (tiles via CDN, routes from in-memory graph) and write (GPS pings via Kafka into traffic).
  • Tiles are z/x/y, 4z per zoom; the URL is the cache key.
  • Dijkstra takes seconds on a continent; CH/CRP answer in about a millisecond by precomputing shortcuts.
  • Separate topology (rare change) from metric (traffic, every minute): CRP customization takes ~1 s for Europe on 12 cores.
  • GPS points need map matching (HMM + Viterbi) before they become segment speeds.
  • ETA = graph time from history + live speeds, corrected by an ML residual model.
  • Reroute by indexing segment -> active sessions and pushing only to affected drivers.
  • Traffic can be slightly stale; routes must always be available - fall back to historical speeds.
