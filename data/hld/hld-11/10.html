Graceful degradation and fallbacks

When a dependency is broken or shedding, give a smaller answer instead of an error. Decide this before the outage, per dependency:

Dependency downDegraded behaviourWhy acceptable
Personalised recommendationsShow cached "popular in your city" listPage still sells
Reviews serviceHide the reviews block, show stars from cacheNon-critical content
Real-time inventoryShow "usually ships in 2 days", re-check at checkoutCorrectness enforced later, at the write
Payment provider AFail over to provider B; or accept order as "payment pending"Revenue kept, reconcile later
Search ranking modelCheaper ranking (Google SRE: search a subset, simpler algorithm)Worse results beat no results
Auth / payment correctnessDo not degrade. Fail closed.Security and money must stay correct

Test your fallbacks. The SRE book warns that degraded modes that are never exercised tend to break, so use them regularly, monitor them, and keep them easy to switch off. Fallbacks must not make network calls that can also fail (Hystrix advice). Chaos testing (Netflix Chaos Monkey style fault injection, or Istio fault injection) is how you prove the fallback works.
