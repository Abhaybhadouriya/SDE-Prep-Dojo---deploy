The 60-second answer. "Every remote call gets a timeout set from the dependency's p99.9 latency and a deadline passed down the call tree. I retry only idempotent calls, at one layer, a couple of times with exponential backoff and full jitter, under a retry budget. A circuit breaker per dependency fails fast when the error rate crosses a threshold and probes in half-open. Bulkheads give each dependency its own pool so one slow service cannot take every thread. At the edge I rate-limit per client and shed low-priority load when overloaded, and every feature has a fallback, such as cached or default results, so the core path keeps working."
