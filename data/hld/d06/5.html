Architecture: follow one ride request

Click Next or Play. Step 1 is the always-on location stream. Steps 2-8 are one ride being matched. Watch which boxes are on the hot path and which are on the durable path.

  1. The location firehose (always on). Every online driver streams a ping about every 4 s over a long-lived connection. The location service checks it (sane speed, not a jump across the city), updates the driver's cell in the in-memory geo index, refreshes the driver-state TTL, and publishes the ping to Kafka for everything else (surge, tracking, traces).
  2. Rider requests. POST /v1/rides with an idempotency key. The ride service inserts a trip in state REQUESTED (the unique key makes a retried tap return the same ride) and replies 202.
  3. Hand-off to matching. The ride service asks the matching service (for that city shard) to find a driver. Matching is asynchronous; the rider's app shows "finding your ride".
  4. Nearby search. Matching converts the pickup point into a cell and queries that cell plus its neighbours for drivers who are AVAILABLE and of the right product. If too few, widen the ring.
  5. Rank by real ETA. Straight-line distance lies (rivers, one-way streets, flyovers). The ETA service scores the top candidates using the road graph and live traffic. Pick the best one.
  6. Reserve the driver atomically. Compare-and-set the driver's state from AVAILABLE to OFFERED(ride). If someone else got there first, the CAS fails and we take the next candidate. This is the "red sticker".
  7. Send the offer. The push gateway delivers the offer to the driver app with a 15 s deadline. Matching holds an in-memory timer. Decline or timeout: release the driver back to AVAILABLE and offer to the next candidate.
  8. Accept and commit. On accept, one database transaction sets the trip to MATCHED with the driver id (the unique index guarantees one active trip per driver). A TripMatched event goes to Kafka; the rider sees the car, the plate and the live ETA.

Whiteboard version (6 boxes): Driver app -> Location service -> Geo index; Rider app -> Ride service -> Matching (-> Geo index, ETA) -> Driver app. Add Kafka and the trips DB when you talk about events and durability.
