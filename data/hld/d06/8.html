Trade-offs, bottlenecks and failure modes

Say each row as: "I pick X because A; the cost is B; if C becomes a problem I switch to Y." Example for the geo index row: "I pick geohash with Redis GEO because it is built in and simple; the cost is uneven cell sizes and an eight-neighbour search; if surge pricing needs equal-area cells and clean neighbour rings, I switch to H3."

Strengths
  • Hot data in memory: fast, cheap to lose
  • Money paths are strongly consistent and idempotent
  • Geography sharding limits blast radius
  • Kafka lets surge, tracking and analytics scale separately
Costs and risks
  • Hot cells at stadiums and airports
  • Index rebuild after failures
  • ETA accuracy is its own hard system
  • Many moving parts per city to operate
