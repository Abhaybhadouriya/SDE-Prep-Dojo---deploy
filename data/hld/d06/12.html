Quick quiz

1M drivers each send a location every 4 seconds. Roughly how many writes per second is that?

  1. 25,000
  2. 250,000
  3. 4,000,000
1,000,000 / 4 = 250,000 writes per second. That number is why locations live in memory, not in the trips database.

A rider is near the edge of a geohash cell. What must the search do?

  1. Search only the rider's cell
  2. Search the whole city
  3. Search the rider's cell and its neighbouring cells
Points close in space can sit in different cells. Searching only one cell misses drivers just across the border.

What stops two matching calls from giving the same driver two rides?

  1. An atomic compare-and-set on the driver's state, fenced by a conditional DB update
  2. Asking the driver to choose
  3. Sorting drivers by id
Only one CAS from AVAILABLE can succeed; the database's conditional update and unique index are the final guarantee.

Why rank candidates by ETA instead of straight-line distance?

  1. ETA is cheaper to compute
  2. Roads, rivers and traffic can make the nearest driver the slowest
  3. Distance cannot be computed on a sphere
Straight-line distance ignores the road network. Filter by distance, then rank the few candidates by real road time.

Which Kafka settings fit the driver-locations topic best?

  1. acks=all, retention forever
  2. One partition for global order
  3. Key by driver id, acks=1, short retention
Per-driver order matters, a lost ping is replaced in seconds, and the data is worthless after a short time.

A matching server crashes right after marking a driver OFFERED. What frees the driver?

  1. The TTL on the OFFERED state expires
  2. The driver must reinstall the app
  3. Nothing; the driver stays offered forever
A lease (TTL) on every temporary lock means a crashed owner cannot hold a driver forever.

Why is the trips data stored in a strongly consistent (often SQL) store?

  1. Because SQL is always faster
  2. It needs transactions, unique constraints and conditional state changes for money and assignment
  3. Because the trips table is bigger than the location data
Trip state and payments must never be duplicated or lost, so we want transactions and constraints, and the write rate is low enough for that.
