How real companies do it

  • LinkedIn (Akhilesh Gupta, QCon talk on InfoQ): SSE connections managed by one Akka actor each in Play, an in-memory subscription table on each frontend, stateless dispatchers backed by a shared key-value store of subscriptions, and cross-data-center publish. Reported ~100k connections per machine, ~5,000 events/s per dispatcher node, and ~75 ms p90 from dispatcher to client send (InfoQ).
  • Facebook / Meta (2011, blog): live commenting across the site at about 650,000 comments a minute and 16 million new viewer-content associations per second, using "write locally, read globally" because commenting is far rarer than viewing.
  • Hotstar (High Scalability write-up): emoji swarm on Kafka (via its Knol platform), Spark Streaming 2-second aggregation, and an in-house PubSub for delivery; about 5 billion emojis from 55.83 million users during the 2019 World Cup.
  • Discord (blog): fan-out of events to every session in a large server is the bottleneck; Manifold batches sends per remote node, and one Erlang VM can hold up to 500,000 live sessions.
