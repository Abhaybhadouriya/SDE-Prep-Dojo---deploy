SQL or NoSQL?

NoSQL wide-column for comments. There are no joins, no multi-row transactions, a very high append rate across millions of videos, and one dominant query: "latest N for video X". Hello Interview picks DynamoDB and notes Postgres or MySQL would also work at smaller scale; systemdesign.one picks Cassandra and warns that compaction and tombstones (deletes) need care. Moderation "deletes" should be a status flag, not a real delete, to avoid tombstone build-up. See Choosing Storage.
