Requirements

Functional

  1. Post a comment on a live video.
  2. See new comments in near real time while watching.
  3. See recent history when you join late (scroll back a page or two).
  4. Reactions (hearts, emojis) shown as an animated swarm plus live counts.
  5. Viewer count ("2.3M watching").

Out of scope unless asked: threaded replies, full moderation pipeline (mention it as an async hook), video delivery itself (that is Video Streaming), and authentication (Auth & SSO).

Non-functional

PropertyTargetSource / reason
End-to-end latencyunder ~200 ms typical; watch the p99Hello Interview's target; human reaction time is ~250 ms (systemdesign.one). p99 = 99% of deliveries are faster than this; the slowest 1% are what users complain about (like tail latency through a switch buffer, not the average).
ConsistencyEventual; viewers may see slightly different subsets and orderNobody can verify that they saw every comment on a 5,000/s stream.
AvailabilityPrefer availability over consistency (AP)A dropped comment is fine; a frozen comment box is not.
ScaleMillions of concurrent streams; one mega-stream with tens of millions of viewersThe LinkedIn talk cites a cricket semifinal with 25+ million concurrent viewers.
DurabilityComments are stored; reactions only as countsHistory and replays need comments; individual taps have no value later.

Say this early: "This is a fan-out problem, not a storage problem. Writes are thousands per second; deliveries are hundreds of millions per second. Delivery best-effort, storage durable."
