Resources

  • Scaling Memcache at Facebook (NSDI 2013) paper - leases, Gutter, regional pools, cold warmup, mcsqueal; source of the 17K to 1.3K/s and 99% numbers here.
  • Caching challenges and strategies (AWS Builders' Library) blog - local vs external caches, modal behaviour, soft/hard TTL, negative caching.
  • How Discord Stores Trillions of Messages blog - hot partitions, request coalescing, consistent-hash routing, real p99s.
  • TAO: The power of the graph (Meta Engineering) blog - two-tier read cache in front of MySQL.
  • Optimal Probabilistic Cache Stampede Prevention (VLDB 2015) paper - the XFetch early-refresh rule.
  • Hello Interview: Scaling Reads course - the ladder order and read:write ratios (parts are premium).
  • System Design Primer repo - cache update strategies, replication, denormalisation, push vs pull CDN.
  • Karan Pratap Singh: System Design repo - caching, CDN and replication chapters; when not to cache.
  • ByteByteGo: How to implement the read replica pattern doc - proxy-based read/write routing.
  • Azure: Cache-Aside pattern and CQRS pattern doc - Microsoft's descriptions of both patterns.
  • Netflix EVCache repo - ephemeral, volatile memcached-based cache.
  • awesome-scalability repo - more caching case studies from real companies.
