Real world

  • Facebook memcache - leases, Gutter, regional pools, cold-cluster warmup and commit-log invalidation at billions of requests per second. The single best read on this pattern.
  • Facebook TAO - two cache tiers (followers, leaders) in front of MySQL, eventually consistent by default.
  • Discord - request coalescing and consistent-hash routing in Rust data services in front of ScyllaDB.
  • Netflix EVCache - an ephemeral, volatile memcached-based layer; services are built to survive evictions.
  • Amazon - Builders' Library guidance on soft/hard TTLs, negative caching and testing with the cache off.
