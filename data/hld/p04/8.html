Remember this

  • Climb in order: index and denormalise, read replicas, cache, CDN, read models. Say why the lower rung breaks.
  • Replicas lag; give read-your-writes by routing the writer to the primary or by log position.
  • Cache-aside + delete-on-write + TTL is the default. Know the stale-set race and fix it with leases or versions.
  • Stampede: coalesce, lease, jitter, refresh early, serve stale. Penetration: negative cache + Bloom filter.
  • Hot keys live on one node; fix with local L1 caches and suffix-replicated keys, not more nodes.
  • A cache the database cannot survive without is a single point of failure. Plan cold starts.
  • Invalidate from the commit log (CDC/Kafka), so lost invalidations can be replayed.
