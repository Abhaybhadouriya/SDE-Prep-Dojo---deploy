Requirements

We design the CoWIN version, because it contains all the others (search, slots, holds, payment, bursts). Then we show what changes for rides and rooms.

Functional
  • Register (phone + OTP) and add up to a few family members per account.
  • Search centres by PIN code, district or "near me", filtered by date, vaccine type, age group, free or paid.
  • See availability per centre per day per slot (for example 30-minute windows).
  • Hold a slot for N people for a few minutes, then book. Paid centres: pay first, then confirm.
  • Reschedule and cancel (cancel frees capacity at once).
  • Centre admins publish capacity ("200 doses tomorrow, 9-5"), mark attendance, and close a session.
  • Notifications: confirmation, reminder, "slots opened near you" alerts.
Non-functional
  • Never overbook a slot (bookings <= capacity). This path is strongly consistent: under a network split we refuse rather than risk it.
  • Search and availability are fast (p99 under 300 ms) and may be a few seconds stale.
  • Bursts: when a district releases slots at a known time, traffic can jump 10-100x in a minute.
  • Fairness: bots and scripts must not grab all slots; per-user limits.
  • Payments: never charge without a booking; never confirm without payment at paid centres.
  • High availability across zones; audit trail (it is a government health record).

Say this early: "Browsing availability is read-heavy and can be a few seconds stale. Taking a slot is a tiny, strongly consistent write against the database of record. I will keep those two paths separate, and protect the write with a conditional update, not with a cache." Every slot-booking interviewer is waiting for this sentence.

Clarifying questions worth asking: Is capacity per slot or per day? Can one booking take several people or several consecutive slots? How long may a hold last? Is payment always required? Can a user hold more than one slot at a time? What happens on no-show? Is there a waiting list?
