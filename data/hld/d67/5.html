Architecture, step by step

Whiteboard version first. Six boxes; then the stepper adds the detail.

Click Next or Play. Watch where staleness is allowed and the one place it is not.

  1. Edge. Every request passes a CDN and API gateway: TLS, OTP-based auth, per-user and per-IP rate limits, bot checks. Static pages and district-level availability JSON are served from the CDN with a few seconds of TTL.
  2. Search. "Centres within 10 km with Covishield for 18+": the search service runs an Elasticsearch geo_distance query with filters. Centres change rarely, so this is cached too.
  3. Availability. For each centre the app shows per-slot counts from Redis. Counts are updated by booking events and corrected every few seconds from the database. They are a hint only: "3 left" may already be 0.
  4. Admission gate. For a hot release, a gate in front of booking issues signed tokens at the rate the shard can absorb (or puts users in a queue with a position). Without a token, the hold call is refused at the edge with a friendly "you are in line".
  5. Hold. The booking service runs one transaction on the shard that owns the centre: UPDATE slot SET held = held + n WHERE slot_id = ? AND booked + held + n <= capacity, checks one row changed, inserts the hold row with expires_at = now() + 5 min. This is the only strongly consistent step.
  6. Pay. Free centre: convert the hold to a CONFIRMED booking in the same database at once. Paid centre: create a PAYMENT_PENDING booking and a PSP order with an idempotency key; the user pays by UPI or card.
  7. Confirm and publish. The PSP's signed webhook arrives. In one transaction: hold ACTIVE to CONVERTED, held -= n, booked += n, booking to CONFIRMED, and an outbox row. A relay publishes booking.confirmed to Kafka.
  8. Fan out. Consumers send SMS and push, schedule reminders, update the Redis counts and the CDN JSON, feed analytics. Each consumer is idempotent on booking_id.
  9. Expire. A sweeper finds holds past expires_at, marks them EXPIRED and gives the places back (held -= n). The hold query does not depend on it for correctness, only for freshness.
  10. Archive. A nightly job copies bookings older than 60 days (already vaccinated, certificate issued) to object storage and deletes them from the hot database, so indexes stay small and fast.
