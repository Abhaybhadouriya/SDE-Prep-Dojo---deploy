Failure modes

  • Shard primary dies: failover to a synchronous standby (or Aurora-style quorum storage) so no confirmed booking is lost. Booking for that region pauses for the failover seconds; search keeps working from caches.
  • Redis down: availability shows "check centre" or falls back to the CDN JSON; holds go straight to the database at a lower admitted rate. Correctness unaffected.
  • Kafka down: outbox rows pile up in Postgres; the relay catches up later. SMS is delayed, bookings are not lost.
  • PSP slow: holds expire during payment; widen the grace window, raise the timeout, and watch the "late payment refunded" count.
  • Sweeper stuck: slots look fuller than they are. Alert on "active holds older than TTL + 2 minutes".
  • No-shows: a business problem with a tech hook: overbook slightly on purpose (like airlines) only if the product owner agrees, or release unattended places to a walk-in or wait-list queue at slot start.
