Remember this

  • A slot is a counter: capacity, booked, held. One conditional UPDATE plus a CHECK constraint makes overbooking impossible.
  • Reads (search, availability) are AP and cached; the slot write is CP and goes only to the database of record.
  • Holds count against capacity and expire; a sweeper releases them; Redis TTL is a gate, not the record.
  • Keep slot and booking in the same database: one local transaction, no Saga for the invariant.
  • Payment is a Saga: idempotent webhook, reconciler, auto-refund when the hold is gone.
  • Surges are shaped (CDN, waiting room, per-slot queue), not autoscaled away.
  • Rooms: ranges plus an exclusion constraint; UTC instants plus the room's time zone.
  • Estimation: four lines, each ending in a decision.
