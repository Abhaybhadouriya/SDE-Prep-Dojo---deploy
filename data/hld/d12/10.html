How Netflix really does it

  • Open Connect: Netflix's own CDN, with embedded OCAs given free to qualifying ISPs, settlement-free peering at interconnection points, 60+ data centres, partnerships with over a thousand ISPs, and BGP sessions between ISPs and OCAs to steer traffic (openconnect.netflix.com; appliances).
  • How Netflix works with ISPs (2016): everything before you press Play runs in AWS; everything after is Netflix's own delivery network; Open Connect delivered 100% of video traffic, close to 90% over direct ISP connections, from close to 1,000 locations at that time (About Netflix).
  • Serving 100 Gbps from one OCA: FreeBSD, sendfile, kernel TLS and NUMA tuning (Netflix Tech Blog; opens in a browser).
  • Per-title encode optimisation: a ladder per title based on complexity (Netflix Tech Blog; opens in a browser), measured with VMAF.
  • Cosmos: workflow-driven media microservices with chunked parallel encoding (InfoQ).
  • Keystone: the Kafka + Flink event pipeline (InfoQ).
  • Titus: Netflix's container platform on EC2 (InfoQ 2017).
  • Open source: Zuul (gateway), EVCache (cache), Chaos Monkey, Conductor (workflow orchestration, now continued as Conductor OSS).
