Hot merchantSub-accountsBatch postingsBoth, often together.
  • The orchestrator crashes mid-saga. The saga state is in the database. On restart, it resumes STARTED/DEBITED transfers, and the idempotent steps make the replay safe.
  • The bank times out with an unknown outcome. Never assume failure. Mark the transfer PENDING, poll the status API, and let reconciliation decide. Refund only after the bank confirms failure.
  • The ledger shard primary fails. Promote a synchronous replica. Writes to that shard pause for seconds, and other shards are unaffected. Async replicas can lose acknowledged entries, so do not use them for the ledger.
  • The cached balance drifts from the postings. This is a bug or a manual edit. Alert, freeze the account, and correct it with an adjusting entry.
  • Kafka consumers lag. Statements show slightly old data. Never let a consumer be the source of truth for balances.
