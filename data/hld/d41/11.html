  • Double entry: every journal entry's postings sum to zero. Postings are append-only. Fix with reversing entries.
  • Money is integer minor units, never floats.
  • Same shard: one ACID transaction with balance >= amt. Cross shard: saga (debit first), or TC/C, or distributed SQL.
  • Idempotency on every layer: the client key, a server lease on the primary, a ledger UNIQUE constraint, downstream keys.
  • Event sourcing gives reproducibility. Replicate events with Raft, and snapshot to replay faster.
  • Hot accounts: sub-accounts and batch postings.
  • Reconciliation is mandatory. Clearing accounts must reach zero.
  • UPI: the app orchestrates, banks hold the money, NPCI switches. Unknown outcomes stay PENDING until confirmed.
