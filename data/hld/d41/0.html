The story

Explain like I'm 5

You and your friend each have a piggy bank. You give your friend 10 marbles. Mum keeps a little notebook. She never rubs anything out. She writes two lines every time: "Aanya's piggy bank: minus 10" and "Rohan's piggy bank: plus 10". If you add up every line in the notebook, the total is always zero, because marbles only move, they are never made or destroyed.

If Mum wants to know how many marbles you had last Tuesday, she reads the notebook from the first page up to Tuesday. If she made a mistake, she does not scribble it out. She writes a new line that fixes it.

That notebook is a double-entry ledger. Every money movement is a journal entry (one transaction) with at least two postings (lines). One posting takes money from an account (a debit) and another adds it to an account (a credit). Together they always sum to zero. The ledger is append-only: you fix mistakes with new entries, never by editing old ones.

A digital wallet (Paytm, Amazon Pay balance, PhonePe wallet, Cash App) is a product built on such a ledger. The company holds money for you, and your "balance" is just the sum of your postings. UPI is different. It moves money directly between bank accounts through a central switch. The app never holds your money, but it still keeps its own ledger of what it asked the banks to do.
