What every design page contains

All 75 design pages follow the same layout of 12 sections, grouped here into 8 steps. The section ids are story, requirements, estimation, api, data-model, architecture, deep-dives, kafka-k8s-docker, tradeoffs, interview, real-world and quiz; each caption below names the ones it covers. The layout is the same on purpose: it is the order you should use when you talk in the interview. Click Next to walk through it.

  1. Story and requirements (sections 1-2: story, requirements). A kid-level story of the problem, then the functional list (what it does) and non-functional list (how fast, how big, how reliable) with numbers.
  2. Estimation (section 3: estimation). Back-of-envelope maths worked step by step, so you see which number changes the design.
  3. API and data model (sections 4-5: api, data-model). The 3-5 key endpoints, the tables or keys, and why SQL or NoSQL.
  4. Architecture (section 6: architecture). A click-through diagram of the request flow, box by box.
  5. Deep dives (section 7: deep-dives). The hard parts the interviewer will push on: hot keys, ordering, double booking, exactly-once.
  6. Kafka, Kubernetes, Docker (section 8: kafka-k8s-docker). Where the other three tracks of this site show up inside the design.
  7. Trade-offs (section 9: tradeoffs). Bottlenecks, failure modes and an alternatives table.
  8. Interview, real world, quiz (sections 10-12: interview, real-world, quiz). How to drive 45 minutes, 8-12 follow-up questions, what the real company did (with blog links), and a quiz.
