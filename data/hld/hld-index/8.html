The must-know list (if you only have two weeks)

You asked which designs are "standard". These are the ones that come up again and again in reports from Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Flipkart and Walmart loops. Do these first, in this order:

#DesignWhy it is on the listMain idea you practise
1URL shortenerThe most common first HLD questionID generation, read-heavy caching
2Rate limiterShort, and maps to QoS you already knowToken bucket, atomic counters in Redis
3Notification systemEvery product needs itQueues, priorities, retries, dedupe
4Chat (WhatsApp)Real-time and orderingWebSockets, per-chat ordering, offline inbox
5News feedThe classic fan-out questionPush vs pull vs hybrid
6Uber / OlaGeo + matching + state machineGeo index, locking a driver
7E-commerce checkoutAmazon, Walmart, Flipkart favouriteSaga, idempotency, outbox
8Flash saleVery common at FlipkartWaiting room, atomic decrement, async orders
9Payment systemFintech and Amazon Pay loopsExactly-once money movement, ledger

11AutocompleteGoogle, Amazon, BingTrie with top-k, offline build
12Design KafkaTies this whole site togetherLogs, partitions, replication
