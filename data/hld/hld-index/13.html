Quick quiz

The interviewer says "Design WhatsApp". What should you do first?

  1. Draw the database schema
  2. Explain how WebSockets use epoll
  3. Ask questions to pin down requirements and scale
  4. Pick Cassandra
Always start with requirements: which features, how many users, what latency. Everything else depends on those answers.

"Two fans want the same seat" and "two buyers want the last phone" are examples of which pattern?

  1. Fan-out
  2. Contention
  3. Large blobs
  4. Geo queries
Many people competing for one limited thing is contention. The tools are locks, conditional updates, holds with a TTL and waiting rooms.

What does a company tag like "Flipkart" on a design card mean on this site?

  1. The design is commonly reported in that company's interviews
  2. That company always asks it
  3. That company built the design
Tags come from interview reports and prep sites. They show what is common, not a guarantee.

Which behaviour best matches the SDE-2 bar?

  1. Waiting for the interviewer to suggest each box
  2. Spending 15 minutes on kernel internals
  3. Driving the round and choosing deep dives with clear trade-offs
SDE-2 candidates are expected to lead, quantify and discuss failures and trade-offs without being prompted.

Which design should you practise first?

  1. Google Maps routing
  2. URL shortener
  3. Stock exchange
The URL shortener is small, common and teaches ID generation, caching and read-heavy scaling - ideas you reuse everywhere.

The matrix shows a column with many dots. What is the best use of it?

  1. Pick 3 designs from that column to practise one weak idea back to back
  2. Skip that idea because it is everywhere
  3. Memorise the order of the rows
Practising one idea across several designs teaches you to recognise it in a prompt you have never seen.
