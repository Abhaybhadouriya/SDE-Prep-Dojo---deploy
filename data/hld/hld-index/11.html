How to study a design so it sticks

  1. Read the story and requirements only. Close the page. Spend 25 minutes designing it yourself on paper.
  2. Now read the full page. Mark every box or decision you missed. That list is your weak spots.
  3. Redraw the whiteboard diagram from memory in 30 seconds, with at most 6-8 boxes.
  4. Say the answer out loud in 45 minutes, using a timer and the framework. Record yourself once a week.
  5. Answer the follow-up questions on the page without looking, then check.
  6. One week later, do it again in 20 minutes. Spaced repetition beats re-reading.

A sentence pattern that works for any trade-off: "I would pick X because of A. The cost is B. If C becomes a problem, I would switch to Y." Example: "I would pick Postgres because orders need transactions. The cost is harder write scaling. If writes pass a few thousand per second, I would shard by customer ID."
