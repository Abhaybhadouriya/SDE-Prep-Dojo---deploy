SDE-1 vs SDE-2: how much detail is enough?

You asked how deep to go. The same prompt is asked at both levels, but the bar is different:

  • SDE-1 = a working, sensible design plus awareness of the hard parts; the interviewer may steer you.
  • SDE-2 = you drive the round, quantify your choices with numbers, and raise failure modes before you are asked.

The full side-by-side comparison (driving, requirements, estimation, design, deep dives, failure, trade-offs) lives in one place: the Framework page, "SDE-1 vs SDE-2".

Your trap: you said you over-explain internals. In a design round, 10 minutes on how epoll works is 10 minutes not spent on the API, the data model and trade-offs. Go deep only when the interviewer asks, or when the depth changes a decision.
