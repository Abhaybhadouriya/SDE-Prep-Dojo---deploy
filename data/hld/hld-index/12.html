Remember this

  • HLD = draw the big boxes for a product, explain how data flows, and say what breaks first.
  • Start from the user and the API, not from internals. Same order every time: requirements, estimates, API, data, diagram, deep dives, trade-offs.
  • Learn concepts first (start with Fundamentals), then patterns, then designs.
  • Most designs are 2-3 patterns combined. Name them out loud.
  • SDE-2 means you drive, quantify and pick your own deep dives.
