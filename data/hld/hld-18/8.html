Designing an abuse-defense platform

Good to know (SDE-2)

In a design round, "how do you stop bots during the sale?" usually leads here. A good answer separates the fast path (inline, must add only a few milliseconds) from the slow path (asynchronous learning that updates the fast path).

  • Fail open or closed? If the risk service is down, most sites fail open for browsing and fail to a safer default for high-risk actions (for example require OTP on login), so a scoring outage does not become a full outage.
  • Latency budget: edge checks should add only a few milliseconds; the app's risk call only on sensitive actions, with a tight timeout.
  • Feedback labels: chargebacks, confirmed account takeovers and support reports become training labels; without them the model drifts.
  • Adversarial loop: attackers probe your thresholds. Avoid revealing exactly why a request was blocked; vary responses; change rules faster than they adapt.
