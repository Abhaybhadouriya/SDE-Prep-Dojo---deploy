  • Layers, cheapest first: anycast DDoS shield, WAF rules, bot management, rate limits, app risk engine. Lock the origin to the edge.
  • WAF catches bad payloads; bot management catches valid requests from the wrong client; rate limits cap volume.
  • Signals: network (IP, ASN), protocol (JA4, header order), client environment (JS checks), behaviour (timing, paths). Mismatches are the strongest tell.
  • Scores are probabilities: reCAPTCHA 0.0-1.0 (higher is safer); assess server-side and check the action name.
  • Friction ladder: allow, silent challenge, CAPTCHA, step-up, block. Block only on high confidence.
  • Clustering judges the crowd: shared fingerprints, tokens or devices across many IPs reveal coordinated bots.
  • Rule engines: priority order, terminating vs Count, labels, default action, WCU budget; CRS uses anomaly scoring and paranoia levels.
  • Rate-based rules: key + window (AWS: 60-600 s, min limit 10) + scope-down; approximate, and per-IP hurts CGNAT users.
  • Ship rules in Count, analyse, tune, then Block one at a time with a break-glass rule.
