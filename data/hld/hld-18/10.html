60-second answer - "How do you stop bots during a flash sale?"
"I layer it. At the edge, a WAF with managed bot rules, rate-based rules keyed on a session or device token rather than IP, and TLS fingerprints such as JA4 that catch scripts pretending to be Chrome. Before the sale opens, clients get a token through a silent JavaScript challenge, or app attestation on mobile, so cold scripts cannot even reach the buy endpoint. The sale itself goes through a virtual waiting room with a fair, randomised queue, and each account and payment instrument has a purchase limit enforced server-side. A risk score per action decides allow, step-up with OTP, or block, and the score service fails open for browsing but fails to step-up for checkout. I run new rules in count mode first and watch conversion and challenge pass rates, so I do not block real buyers."
