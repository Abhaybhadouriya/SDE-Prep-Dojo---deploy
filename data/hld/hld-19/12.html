  • Recommenders are funnels: cheap candidate generation (CF, item-item, embeddings + ANN) then a heavier ranker.
  • Item-based CF beats user-based in production: stable, offline, online cost depends only on the user's own items.
  • Cosine compares direction, not size; add shrinkage for small overlaps; keep top-K neighbours per item.
  • MF: R ~ U x VT, dot product + biases, fit only observed cells with L2. "SVD" in recsys means this, not textbook SVD.
  • ALS: freeze one side, solve the other exactly, in parallel; no learning rate; WALS handles all implicit cells cheaply.
  • Implicit feedback = preference (0/1) + confidence (1 + alpha x count); evaluate with ranking metrics on a time split.
  • Serve user vectors by key and item vectors through an ANN index, both in RAM, both from the same model version, flipped atomically.
  • HNSW = layered graph over raw vectors; ScaNN = partition + anisotropic quantized scoring + exact rescoring.
