Resources

Watch

ALS on Spark for collaborative filtering, and serving the factors as a service.

  • Google: Recommendation Systems course course - Start here. candidate generation, content vs collaborative filtering, matrix factorization, SGD vs WALS.
  • Linden, Smith, York: Amazon.com Recommendations - Item-to-Item Collaborative Filtering (2003) paper - Start here. why item-based, the offline table and its cost.
  • Amazon Science: The history of Amazon's recommendation algorithm blog - how item-to-item came about and why it lasted.
  • Koren, Bell, Volinsky: Matrix Factorization Techniques for Recommender Systems (2009) paper - biases, SGD vs ALS, implicit signals, temporal effects from the Netflix Prize winners.
  • Hu, Koren, Volinsky: Collaborative Filtering for Implicit Feedback Datasets (2008) paper - preference + confidence and the fast ALS trick.
  • Simon Funk: Netflix Update - Try This at Home blog - the original "Funk SVD": gradient descent on known ratings only.
  • Apache Spark: Collaborative Filtering (ALS) doc - parameters, implicitPrefs, cold-start strategy, runnable examples.
  • Redis: Vector search concepts doc - FLAT / HNSW / SVS-VAMANA, KNN syntax, filters, LIMIT gotcha.
  • Google Research: Announcing ScaNN blog - anisotropic quantization explained with pictures and benchmark.
  • ScaNN on GitHub repo - install, algorithm docs (partition / score / rescore) and the GloVe example notebook.
  • Guo et al.: Accelerating Large-Scale Inference with Anisotropic Vector Quantization paper - the ScaNN paper (ICML 2020).
  • Malkov, Yashunin: HNSW paper paper - the layered graph index used by most vector stores.
  • Covington et al.: Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations (2016) paper - the candidate generation + ranking funnel at YouTube scale.
  • Faiss wiki repo - IVF, PQ and HNSW indexes, choosing an index by dataset size.
