  • The real problem is unique + 64-bit + time-sorted + no coordination per ID.
  • Snowflake = 1 sign + 41 time (ms, ~69 years) + 10 machine (1,024) + 12 sequence (4,096/ms).
  • Uniqueness comes from the machine bits; ordering comes from the time bits.
  • Coordinate once (worker-ID lease), never per ID.
  • Clock goes back: detect, wait if tiny, otherwise refuse; persist a high-water mark.
  • Segments (ranges) are the simple alternative; UUIDv7/ULID when 128 bits is fine.
  • Send 64-bit IDs as strings in JSON; keep public IDs opaque.
