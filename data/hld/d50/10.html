How Uber does it

  • Surge pipeline: Kafka -> Flink with an ML-based pricing algorithm -> key-value sink; favours freshness and availability; drops late events; throughput-tuned Kafka; active-active across regions (Uber, "Real-time Data Infrastructure at Uber", SIGMOD 2021).
  • H3: built for pricing and dispatch; hexagons; 122 base cells and 12 pentagons on an icosahedron; open source.
  • Routing: OSRM and Goldeta (2012-2014), then Gurafu (2015) with partitioned, parallel preprocessing for live traffic; hundreds of thousands of ETA requests/s at single-digit ms.
  • DeepETA (2022): routing ETA plus deep-learning residual, linear transformer, discretised embeddings, asymmetric Huber loss, served via Michelangelo behind uRoute.
  • Tech stack: ByteByteGo's Uber tech-stack guide lists Kafka and Flink for streaming, H3 for location indexing, Pinot for real-time OLAP and Cadence for workflows.

Related designs: Ride Hailing, Google Maps, Food Delivery, Proximity Service, Ad Click Aggregator, Fraud Detection. Patterns: Geo & Proximity, Counting & Windowed Aggregation.
