Failure modes

  • Flink job down or lagging: the KV keeps serving the last values. Add a staleness rule: if the version is older than, say, 5 minutes, decay toward 1.0 instead of freezing a high surge.
  • Bad GPS (tunnels, spoofing): filter impossible speeds, map-match, and ignore drivers whose app state is not "open".
  • Feedback oscillation: drivers all rush to one hex and leave others empty. Dampen with neighbour smoothing and slower decrease.
  • Routing server restart: loading a city graph takes time; use readiness probes so pods only get traffic after the graph and live speeds are loaded.
  • ML model regression: shadow-deploy, compare mean absolute error (MAE) by segment, and fall back to routing ETA plus a simple historical offset.
