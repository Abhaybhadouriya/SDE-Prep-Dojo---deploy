Requirements

Functional

  1. Fare estimate A rider enters pickup and drop-off and sees a price (base fare x distance/time x surge multiplier) and a pickup ETA.
  2. Surge per area The system computes a multiplier per small area, refreshed about every minute.
  3. Price lock The price shown is honoured if the rider books within a short window (a quote).
  4. Driver heatmap Drivers see where surge is high so they can move there.
  5. ETA everywhere Pickup ETA, trip ETA, and ETA per candidate driver during matching (ETAs feed fares, matching and delivery planning per the DeepETA post).

Say early: "Surge is an analytics problem, not a transaction problem. I will optimise for freshness and availability, accept approximate counts, and make the quote the only strongly consistent piece: once a rider sees a price, the booking honours that quote id."

This design builds on Ride Hailing (d06) for matching and location ingestion, and on Google Maps (d37) for routing. Proximity indexing is covered in Geospatial Indexes and Geo & Proximity Queries.
