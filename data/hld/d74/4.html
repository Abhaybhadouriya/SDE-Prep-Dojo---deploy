Why split bytes and metadata? (the JPMorgan prompt said "metadata in DB, bytes in S3"). Databases are bad at large blobs: they bloat backups and replication and waste buffer cache. Object storage is built for large immutable objects. The DB row is small, transactional and queryable. See Pattern: Handling large blobs.
