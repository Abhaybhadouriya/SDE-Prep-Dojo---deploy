How the real systems do it

Seen in 3 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #53. Company view: the Amazon guide.

  • Amazon S3 pre-signed URLs: time-limited bearer links for upload or download, up to 7 days with SigV4, expire early if the signing credentials expire; bucket policies can reject old signatures.
  • Amazon SQS: 1 MiB messages, 4-day default retention (up to 14), 30 s default visibility timeout (up to 12 h), delays up to 15 minutes, DLQ redrive.
  • Amazon Textract: sync for single pages up to 10 MB, async for PDFs up to 500 MB / 3,000 pages with an SNS completion notice - the same async job pattern as this design.
  • Stripe idempotent requests: the reference design for idempotency keys (255 chars, 24 h, parameter mismatch is an error).
  • AWS Builders' Library: Timeouts, retries and backoff with jitter: why retries need caps, backoff and jitter.
