  • Slow or failure-prone work leaves the request: 202 + job ID, job table = truth, queue = who works next.
  • Workers take a lease (visibility timeout) and extend it with heartbeats; a dead worker's job comes back.
  • Delivery is at-least-once: make handlers idempotent (stable output keys, conditional updates, dedupe table).
  • Retry with exponential backoff + jitter, cap attempts, then DLQ + alarm.
  • Autoscale on queue age; separate queues per job class; Little's Law sizes the pool.
  • Tell the user: poll as the fallback, push/webhook/email as the optimisation.
  • Many dependent steps over hours: a workflow engine (Temporal, Step Functions).
