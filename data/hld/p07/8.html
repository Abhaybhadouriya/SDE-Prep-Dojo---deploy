Real-world: how companies do it

  • Dropbox ATF - store + SQS per (task type, priority), explicit claims, self-killing executors after three missed heartbeats, about 9,000 tasks/s.
  • Slack - Kafka placed in front of a Redis job queue after a memory-full outage; 33,000 jobs/s peak.
  • AWS Builders' Library - backlog maths, message age alarms, shuffle sharding, sidelining stale work.
  • Temporal - durable execution: event history and replay for multi-step jobs.
