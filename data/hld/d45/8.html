Failure modes to mention

  • Queue backlog: scores lag but reads work. Alert on depth and on p99 processing time, not only averages.
  • Hot community or domain: one partition overloaded. Split jobs per query type, bucket the key, or pre-aggregate.
  • Cached listing points to missing rows: Reddit saw this in 2011 after a failover bug wrote to a replica that was later rebuilt. Lesson from the slides: "if you denormalize, build tooling to make your data consistent again". Render pages that skip missing IDs instead of crashing.
  • Cold cache after mass failure: database overload. Warm gradually, coalesce misses.
  • Vote brigading and bots: anti-cheat runs inside the async vote worker, so it can be slow and thorough without hurting the user.
