  • The crawl is a loop; the frontier (priority + politeness + seen-sets) is the hard, stateful part.
  • 1B pages/month is about 400/s average, ~1,000 in flight, ~100 TB/month raw.
  • Front queues for importance, one back queue per host, a heap of next-allowed times.
  • Shard by host hash so politeness and robots state are local; use consistent hashing.
  • Bloom filter for URL seen (~10 bits per URL at 1%); SHA + SimHash for content.
  • RFC 9309: cache robots.txt at most 24 h, longest match wins, unreachable means disallow all.
  • DNS, not bandwidth, is the hidden bottleneck; cache it locally.
  • At-least-once tasks with leases plus idempotent stages; Kafka between stages, not as the frontier.
