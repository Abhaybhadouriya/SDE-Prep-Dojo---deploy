How the real systems do it

Seen in 3 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #47. Company view: the Amazon guide and the Flipkart guide.

  • Amazon Connect (AWS's contact-centre service): routing profiles tie agents to queues with priorities and per-channel concurrency; up to 10 active chats per agent, chats last up to 7 days (default 25 hours), and 5 open WebSocket connections per chat participant. Its quotas page shows waiting chats count against the instance's concurrent-chat quota.
  • Slack: gateway servers hold WebSocket subscriptions near the user, channel servers own channels by consistent hashing; messages delivered worldwide in about 500 ms.
  • LinkedIn instant messaging: hundreds of thousands of persistent connections on one machine with Play and Akka; and their presence platform uses heartbeats to decide online/offline - the same idea as agent presence.
  • Skill-based routing in classic ACDs: the "most idle agent" rule and occupancy-aware routing are described in long-standing patents such as US6424709 (skill-based call routing) and US5828747 (distribution by agent occupancy).
