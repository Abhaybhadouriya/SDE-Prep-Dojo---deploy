Agent selectionLongest idleBest skill scoreLongest idle within the top skill band, with an occupancy cap.
  • Hot queue: during a delivery incident one queue (LATE_DELIVERY) explodes. Allow overflow: after T seconds, agents from other queues with a secondary skill become eligible.
  • Ghost agents: agent closed the laptop but status says AVAILABLE. Offers time out repeatedly. Mark an agent AWAY after 2 missed offers; require heartbeats.
  • Reconnect storm: a gateway deploy drops 20K sockets at once. Roll gateways slowly, add jitter to client reconnect backoff.
  • Starvation: VIP priority can starve normal contacts. Use age-weighted priority (priority grows with wait time).
  • Split brain in routers: two leaders for one queue after a network partition. The conditional SQL update makes the second assignment fail - correctness does not depend on the lease.
