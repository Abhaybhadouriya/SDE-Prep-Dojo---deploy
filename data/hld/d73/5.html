Architecture: from "Chat with us" to agent

Click Next or Play. Steps 1-5 are routing, step 6 is messaging, steps 7-8 are transfer and close.

  1. Customer taps "Chat with us". The gateway authenticates and the contact service inserts a contact row (state CREATED) in SQL, using the idempotency key so retries do not create two.
  2. The intent predictor reads the customer's recent orders (late shipment, refund pending, return picked up) and returns an intent with a confidence. Low confidence goes to a general queue or a bot asks one question.
  3. Contact moves to QUEUED and is pushed into the skill queue (a Redis sorted set by priority then wait time). The SQL row stays the truth; Redis is the fast index.
  4. The router for that queue wakes on two events: "contact arrived" and "agent slot freed". It picks the longest-waiting contact and the best eligible agent from presence (has skill, available, free slot).
  5. In one SQL transaction it reserves the agent slot and sets the contact to OFFERED. The agent gets an offer over WebSocket; 15 s to accept or it returns to the front of the queue.
  6. On accept the contact is ACTIVE. Messages go customer -> gateway -> chat service (assign seq, persist) -> the agent's gateway, and back. Both sides ack by seq.
  7. Transfer or escalation is just "re-queue into another queue or to a named agent", keeping the same contact_id and transcript, so the next agent sees everything.
  8. On close, wrap-up frees the slot, and a contact-events stream (Kafka, written through an outbox) feeds analytics, satisfaction surveys and training data for the predictor.
