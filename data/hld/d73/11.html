Remember this

  • Servers autoscale; agents do not. Spikes are solved by durable queues, deflection and callbacks.
  • The contact is a SQL row with a state machine; every exit is explicit, so nothing is silently dropped.
  • Assignment = one ACID transaction with guarded updates on agent capacity and contact state.
  • Offers expire; a reaper puts them back at the front of the queue.
  • Redis queues and presence are indexes, rebuildable from SQL and heartbeats.
  • Prediction = context + rules + model, with a timeout and fallback.
  • Messages: WebSocket, per-contact seq, client_msg_id dedupe, NoSQL by contact_id.
