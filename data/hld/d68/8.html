Trade-offs, bottlenecks and failures

Your own trade-offs, not the public template. A Swiggy SDE-3 candidate followed a well-known public local-delivery breakdown (Elasticsearch, short-TTL inventory cache, invalidation on dark-store updates, pessimistic vs optimistic locking), felt it went well, and still got negative feedback (post). The report does not say why. A safe lesson: anyone can recite the template, so the signal is your decisions with numbers and the costs you accept. The table below is written in that voice.

Failure modes

  • Inventory shard down: only that region's stores stop checkout (store isolation); browse works from caches with "checkout temporarily unavailable".
  • Kafka lag: search shows stale in-stock flags; reservations still correct. Shorten the TTL fallback and alert.
  • Store internet drop: picker scans buffer on the device or a store edge box and replay with movement ids (idempotent); serviceability pauses new orders for that store.
  • Phantom stock: the system says 3, the shelf has 0. Picker marks short, a COUNT_ADJUST fixes it, the customer gets a substitute or refund; a rising rate triggers a recount.
  • PSP outage: fall back to other methods (UPI to card), extend reservation timers slightly, or allow COD for trusted users.
