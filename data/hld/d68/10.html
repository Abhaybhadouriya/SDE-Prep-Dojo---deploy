How the real companies do it

Seen in 5 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #31. Company view: the Food & Quick-Commerce guide and the Flipkart guide.

  • Zepto on DynamoDB: AWS's database blog post How Zepto scales to millions of orders per day using Amazon DynamoDB describes moving high-volume order workloads to a key-value store.
  • Instacart item availability: Item Availability Architecture: solving for scale and consistency covers availability scores for hundreds of millions of items stored next to retrieval, refreshed by a full sync several times a day plus a lazy refresh for items that appear in search (which cut ingestion load by about two-thirds). The same "freshness where it matters" idea applies to dark stores.
  • Swiggy engineering: A brief introduction to engineering challenges at Swiggy lists the hyperlocal problems (serviceability, assignment, ETAs) that Instamart shares with food delivery.
  • Geo cells: Uber's H3 hexagonal index is a common way to map addresses to service areas.
