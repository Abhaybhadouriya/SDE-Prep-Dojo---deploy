Remember this

  • Apps log to stdout; a DaemonSet agent ships from the node. Apps never wait for the log system.
  • Kafka in the middle = durability, replay, fan-out and backpressure isolation.
  • It is a write-heavy, append-only, time-partitioned system: prune by time and labels, then scan.
  • Labels say where a line came from; content says what happened. Never label high-cardinality IDs.
  • Elasticsearch = rich search, costly; Loki = cheap, labels only; ClickHouse = structured analytics.
  • Hot/warm/cold tiers by age; object storage for anything old.
  • At-least-once is fine for logs; audit logs get a stricter path plus a counting check.
