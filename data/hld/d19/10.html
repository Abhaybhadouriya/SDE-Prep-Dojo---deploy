How real systems do it

  • Cloudflare moved HTTP log analytics to ClickHouse: ingest through Kafka with "inserter" services doing large batched writes, tables partitioned by hour, extra sampled tables for fast dashboards, about 10x less space per record than their Elasticsearch setup, and 100% of events instead of samples. Read the post.
  • Grafana Loki documents the labels-only design: distributors, ingesters, queriers, a compactor, and chunks in object storage. Loki architecture, label best practices.
  • Netflix Keystone is a Kafka-fronted event pipeline that also carries logs; it is the classic example of Kafka as a company-wide data highway. Kafka inside Keystone (opens in a browser).
  • Elastic's own sizing guide is the reference for shard counts and sizes. Size your shards.
  • OpenTelemetry Collector is becoming the vendor-neutral agent for logs, metrics and traces in one process. Collector docs.
