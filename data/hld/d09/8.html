Redis failoverAccept the rare oversell riskPause + rebuild from Kafka, WAIT, buffer stockBusiness decision. Always rebuild from the durable log after a failover.
  • The waiting room is down. Fail closed for the sale (show "sale paused"), not open. Letting the full flood through to Redis and the gateway is how outages start.
  • A Redis shard fails. Its buckets are unavailable, so other buckets keep selling. After failover, pause those buckets and rebuild their counters from Kafka/SQL before reopening.
  • Kafka write fails after the decrement. Return the unit (INCR + remove the user) and answer "try again". The user never got a "yes".
  • The order DB is slow. Consumer lag grows, and pending orders appear in "My orders" a little later. Payment pages should read from the reservation (Redis/Kafka-backed) instead of waiting on the order row.
  • The payment provider is overloaded at T0+1 min. Most winners pay at the same moment. Pre-agree capacity with providers, spread users across providers, and extend pay_by automatically if a provider is degraded, so winners do not lose their unit for your partner's outage.
  • Clock skew. The countdown uses server time, and the sale opens when the server-side flag flips, never the phone clock.
