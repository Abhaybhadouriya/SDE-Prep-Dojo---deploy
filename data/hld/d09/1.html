Requirements

Functional
  • A sale page with product details and a countdown to the start time.
  • At T0 the Buy button works. Users who wait get a fair place in a queue and see their status.
  • A buyer who wins gets a reservation and must pay within N minutes (for example 10).
  • Limit: 1 unit per user (per account, device and payment instrument).
  • When stock is gone, everyone sees "sold out" quickly. If unpaid reservations expire, stock comes back.
  • Normal checkout, payment and order tracking after the reservation.
Non-functional
  • Never oversell. Selling 10,001 of 10,000 is the one unforgivable bug.
  • Survive the spike: Flipkart says it handles about 1.5 million requests per second when the Big Billion Days sale opens.
  • Contain the blast radius: the rest of the shop (search, other products, existing orders) must stay healthy.
  • Fairness: roughly first-come, first-served, and bots should not win.
  • Fast answers: a clear "you got it" or "sold out" within seconds. Hanging spinners cause retry storms.
  • Correct after the fact: the database, the stock counter and the payments must reconcile.

A strong first sentence: "Demand is many orders of magnitude bigger than supply, so the design is a funnel. I will reject or park traffic as early and as cheaply as possible, and only a tiny, rate-controlled stream will ever touch the stock counter and the database."
