  • Single-leader, multi-leader, leaderless: the shape decides who accepts writes and where conflicts appear.
  • R + W > N forces the read and write sets to overlap; (3,2,2) survives one dead replica. It is not linearizability.
  • Raft = terms + randomized election timeouts + majority votes + commit on majority. 5 nodes survive 2 failures; use odd sizes.
  • Paxos: prepare/promise then accept/accepted; a proposer must re-propose any value already accepted.
  • Failure detection is suspicion with a timeout. Gossip spreads news in about log N rounds; phi accrual adapts to jitter.
  • Split brain is prevented by majority quorum plus an epoch/fencing number.
  • A lease can expire while its holder is paused; only fencing tokens checked by the storage make a lock safe.
  • Vector clocks detect concurrent writes; LWW silently drops one. Merkle trees find drift in O(difference).
  • Snowflake: 41 bits time + 10 bits machine + 12 bits sequence; watch out for clocks going backwards.
