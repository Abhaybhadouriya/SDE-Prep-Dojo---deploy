The 60-second answer. "For small, critical coordination data, like who the leader is, cluster membership or a lock, I use a consensus store such as etcd or ZooKeeper: Raft elects one leader per term with a majority vote, commits a write once a majority has it, and survives the loss of a minority. Every leader change bumps an epoch, and I pass that epoch as a fencing token so a paused old leader cannot write. For high-volume data I do not use consensus per write; I use leaderless quorums with R + W > N, or a single leader per shard, and I generate IDs with a Snowflake-style scheme so no central counter is needed."
