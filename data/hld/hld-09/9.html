Merkle trees: finding the different rows fast

Two replicas each hold millions of keys. How do you find the handful that differ without sending everything? Build a Merkle tree: hash each key range into a leaf, hash pairs of children into parents, up to one root hash. Replicas compare roots; if equal, they are in sync (one message). If not, compare the two children and walk only down the branches whose hashes differ. You transfer data proportional to the difference, not the dataset. Dynamo and Cassandra use this for anti-entropy repair; Git, ZFS, BitTorrent and blockchains use the same idea.
