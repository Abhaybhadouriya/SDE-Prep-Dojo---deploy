Requirements

Functional
  • Create and delete buckets; PUT, GET (with byte ranges), DELETE, HEAD objects.
  • LIST objects in a bucket by prefix, in key order, with pagination.
  • Multipart upload for large objects (upload parts in parallel, resume after failure).
  • Versioning (keep old versions; delete creates a marker).
  • Access control per bucket/object; time-limited presigned URLs.
Out of scope (mention, then park)
  • Lifecycle tiering to cold storage (Glacier-like) - mention only.
  • Cross-region replication - mention only.
  • Billing, analytics, event notifications beyond a Kafka hook.
  • POSIX semantics (rename, append, locks).
