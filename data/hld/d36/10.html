How real systems do it

  • Amazon S3 - launched March 2006; hundreds of microservices and millions of hard disks; Reed-Solomon erasure coding with each object spread over tens of drives; heat management; ShardStore (the storage node layer, rewritten in Rust) checked against an executable model about 1% of its size on every commit (Werner Vogels, 2023).
  • S3 strong consistency - since December 2020, via ordered replication and an in-memory witness read barrier for the metadata caches (Vogels, 2021).
  • Facebook Haystack (OSDI 2010) - 260 billion images, 20 PB, over 1 million images/s at peak; photos packed into 100 GB volumes with an in-memory index for one disk op per read.
  • Facebook f4 (OSDI 2014) - warm BLOBs in Reed-Solomon(10,4) cells plus geo XOR; effective replication factor from 3.6 to 2.1; over 65 PB stored.
  • MinIO - S3-compatible, erasure sets of 2-16 drives, default parity EC:4 (75% usable on 16 drives), read quorum K.
  • Ceph RADOS - erasure-coded pools and CRUSH placement; commonly run on Kubernetes via Rook.
