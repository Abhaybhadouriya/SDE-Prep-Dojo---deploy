Remember this

  • Split the metadata plane (small, consistent, ordered) from the data plane (huge, append-only, erasure coded).
  • Write data first, metadata last: crashes leave garbage, never dangling keys.
  • RS(8,4) = 1.5x storage, survives 4 losses; 3 replicas = 3x, survives 2. Place shards across disks, racks and 3 zones.
  • Durability comes from redundancy + independence + fast repair + checksums + careful operations.
  • A hard disk does about 120 IOPS: pack small objects, keep the index in RAM, read small objects from one shard.
  • Multipart: up to 10,000 parts of 5 MiB-5 GiB, parallel, resumable, completion is metadata-only.
  • Range-partition metadata for LIST; hot prefixes split, clients back off on 503.
