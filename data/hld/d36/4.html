Which database for metadata? We need (1) strong consistency for a single key, (2) ordered range scans for LIST by prefix, (3) horizontal scale to 100 billion rows. That points to a range-partitioned, replicated KV store (the Bigtable/Spanner/FoundationDB family) or sharded SQL. Hash partitioning on (bucket, key) spreads load perfectly but makes "list everything under photos/2026/" a scatter-gather over every shard. Range partitioning keeps prefixes together and splits hot ranges automatically - which is exactly what AWS's docs describe when they say S3 gives each busy "partitioned prefix" its own resources, gradually, with temporary 503 Slow Down errors while it scales.
