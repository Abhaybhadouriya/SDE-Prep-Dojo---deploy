  • Split control plane (REST + WebSocket signalling + placement) from media plane (SRTP over UDP through SFUs).
  • SFU for groups: one upload per sender, per-receiver forwarding, no decoding. P2P only for 1:1; mixing only for recording and phone gateways.
  • ICE ladder: direct UDP, STUN-reflexive, TURN UDP, TURN or SFU over TLS 443. SFUs with public IPs make it simpler.
  • Simulcast or SVC plus RTCP-driven bandwidth estimation; audio first, active speaker high, last-N for big rooms.
  • Nearest SFU per participant, cascade SFUs across regions; meeting state lives in the control plane so SFU failure is a short blip.
  • The big numbers are egress (Tbps) and packets per second, plus the top-of-the-hour join spike; storage is mostly recordings in object storage.
  • Kafka carries events and QoS telemetry, never media.
