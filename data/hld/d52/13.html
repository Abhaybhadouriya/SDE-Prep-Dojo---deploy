Resources

  • Discord: How Discord handles 2.5 million concurrent voice users using WebRTC blog - the best public SFU fleet write-up: numbers, signalling, no-ICE choice, failover.
  • Zoom: Architected for reliability doc - MMRs, meeting zones, controllers, UDP to TLS 443 fallback, 45% loss tolerance, capacity headroom.
  • Zoom: bandwidth requirements doc - the per-user up/down numbers used in the estimation.
  • Microsoft Azure: Advancing Microsoft Teams on Azure, operating at pandemic scale blog - 200M daily participants, 4.1B minutes, and how they scaled and degraded on purpose.
  • systemdesign.one: 5 reasons Zoom could support 300 million video calls a day blog - SVC, routing not processing, UDP-first, P2P for two.
  • High Scalability: A short on how Zoom works blog - SVC vs AVC, application-layer QoS, fallback order.
  • LiveKit: SFU internals doc - mesh vs MCU vs SFU explained with bandwidth, and how rooms map to nodes.
  • webrtcHacks: SFU simulcast blog - how simulcast and layer switching work inside an SFU; simulcast vs SVC.
  • webrtcHacks: SFU cascading (Jitsi Octo) blog - why and how to connect SFUs across regions.
  • Jitsi Videobridge performance evaluation paper - measured forwarding cost per stream on one server.
  • MDN: Introduction to WebRTC protocols doc - ICE, STUN, NAT, TURN, SDP in plain words.
  • webrtc.org: TURN server doc - configuring ICE servers in a client.
  • RFC 8445: ICE and RFC 8656: TURN paper - the specs, for when you want the real state machines.
  • ashishps1/awesome-system-design-resources repo - lists "Design Zoom" among the interview problems (links the codeKarle video above).
