Bottlenecks to call out
  • SFU egress bandwidth and packets per second (not CPU of business logic).
  • TURN relay bandwidth for users behind symmetric NAT or UDP-blocking firewalls.
  • Signalling and placement at the top of the hour.
  • Inter-region backbone traffic for cascaded meetings.
Failure modes to call out
  • SFU crash: rebuild state from signalling, clients re-publish.
  • Keyframe storms when many receivers switch layers at once.
  • NAT binding timeouts silently killing media: keepalives.
  • Echo and feedback when someone unmutes on speakers: client-side echo cancellation, not a server problem.
