Requirements

Functional
  • Collect metrics from every host, pod, database and service (~55,000 targets).
  • Store each series as (timestamp, value) with labels like service, region, status.
  • Query language for graphs and maths: rates, sums by label, percentiles (PromQL-like).
  • Dashboards (Grafana) and alert rules evaluated every 15-60 s.
  • Route alerts to the right team (PagerDuty, Slack, email) with grouping, silencing and dedup.
  • Keep raw data 15 days, downsampled data 13 months for capacity planning.
Out of scope
  • Log search (Log Aggregation) and traces.
  • Billing-accurate counts (metrics may be approximate; billing uses events - see Ad Click Aggregator).
  • Per-user analytics (that would need high-cardinality data).
