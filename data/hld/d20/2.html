Back-of-envelope estimation

All inputs are interview assumptions. The per-sample size and the 15-day default retention come from the Prometheus docs; the rest is arithmetic you show on the board.

  1. Count series. 5,000 nodes x ~1,000 series each (node exporter, kubelet, container metrics) = 5M. 50,000 pods x ~100 app series = 5M. Total ≈ 10M active series.
  2. Samples per second. Scrape every 15 s: 10M / 15 ≈ 670k samples/s. Plan for 2M/s with growth and a 5 s scrape for critical jobs.
  3. Network for scraping. The text format is about 100 bytes per series line (rule of thumb): 10M x 100 B / 15 s ≈ 67 MB/s of scrape traffic before compression. Spread across many scrapers, it is fine.
  4. Disk per day. Prometheus's docs say a sample takes about 1-2 bytes after compression. 670k x 1.5 B ≈ 1 MB/s → x 86,400 ≈ ~87 GB/day.
  5. Raw retention 15 days. 87 GB x 15 ≈ 1.3 TB; with 3 replicas in a long-term store ≈ ~4 TB. Tiny compared to logs!
  6. 13 months downsampled. Keep 5-minute averages after 15 days: 300 s / 15 s = 20x fewer samples. 87 GB / 20 ≈ 4.4 GB/day x 395 days ≈ ~1.7 TB (x3 replicas ≈ 5 TB).
  7. Memory. Recent data and the index for active series live in RAM. A common rule of thumb is a few KB per active series; at ~4 KB, 10M series ≈ 40 GB of RAM - too much for one comfortable box, so we shard.
  8. Reads. 2,000 dashboards x 20 panels refreshed every 30 s ≈ 1,300 queries/s at peak, plus ~20,000 alert rules every 30 s ≈ 670 queries/s. Reads hit recent data mostly.
