How real systems do it

  • Uber M3 (2018): over 6.6 billion series, 500 million metrics/s aggregated, 20 million/s persisted, 3 replicas per region, 2-day to 5-year retention. Uber blog: M3 · m3db/m3 on GitHub.
  • Google Monarch: planet-scale in-memory TSDB, push-based, regional zones plus global query. Paper page · full PDF.
  • Netflix Atlas: in-memory dimensional time series for operational insight. Introducing Atlas (opens in a browser).
  • Prometheus docs on storage (WAL, 2-hour blocks, 15-day default retention, remote write) and naming (labels and cardinality).
