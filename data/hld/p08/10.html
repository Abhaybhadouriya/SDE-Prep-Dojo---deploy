Why is push the usual default for social feeds?

  1. It uses less storage
  2. It handles celebrities best
  3. Reads far outnumber writes, so moving work to write time saves the most
  4. It makes ranking changes easy
At about 50 reads per write, a single-lookup read path is a big win.

What does the hybrid approach do with an account that has 50 million followers?

  1. Pushes to all 50 million feeds
  2. Stores posts once and merges them into readers' feeds at read time
  3. Blocks the account
  4. Sends an email instead
High-follower accounts are pulled; their posts are merged at read time.

What should the precomputed feed list contain?

  1. Full post JSON with images
  2. Only the author's name
  3. Every post ever, uncapped
  4. A capped list of post IDs (plus small metadata)
IDs keep lists small and make edits/deletes cheap; bodies are hydrated from a cache.

  1. ARP

Which situation favours pull?

  1. Ranking models change constantly and most followers rarely read
  2. A family chat of 6 people
  3. A chronological feed read every minute by everyone
  4. Small, bounded follower counts
Pull ranks at read time and does no work for passive readers (LinkedIn's reasons).
