Real-world: how companies do it

  • Twitter (2012) - push into Redis home timelines (800 IDs, 3 replicas, active users only); search is pull (one write per tweet, scatter queries over all index shards); plan to merge celebrity tweets at read time.
  • LinkedIn FollowFeed - deliberate pull with per-author timelines, 720 partitions, ranking on read, about 140 ms p99.
  • Slack - channel servers on a consistent-hash ring, gateway servers fan out to sockets, about 500 ms worldwide.
  • Discord - guild processes fan out to sessions; Manifold sends once per node and fans out locally.
  • Facebook / Instagram - the hybrid shape appears in Hello Interview's breakdown: precomputed feed plus read-time merge for high-follow accounts.
