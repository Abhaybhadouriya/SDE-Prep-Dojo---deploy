When you hit it: the signals

You need this pattern whenever there is a one-to-many relationship and the "many" side reads a personal, merged view:

  • "Show the user a feed / timeline / home page of posts from people they follow" - News Feed, Reddit / Quora.
  • "Send a message to a group / channel" - WhatsApp groups, Slack channels.
  • "Notify all followers / all subscribers / everyone in the region" - Notification System.
  • "Millions watch the same live stream and see comments" - Live Comments.
  • Any time you see wildly uneven numbers: most users have 200 followers, a few have 100 million.

Here are the numbers that make it hard. The system-design-primer's Twitter exercise assumes 500 million tweets a day with an average fan-out of 10, which is 5 billion timeline deliveries a day, about 60,000 a second, against about 100,000 timeline reads a second. The real Twitter in 2012 (Raffi Krikorian's QCon talk, summarised by High Scalability) was even more read-heavy: about 300,000 timeline reads a second against about 6,000 tweet writes a second.
