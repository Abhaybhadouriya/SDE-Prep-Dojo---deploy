DesignWhat fans outPush, pull or hybrid

d45 Reddit / QuoraPosts to subreddit / topic feedsPull per community (few huge communities) with cached ranked lists; home feed merges
d58 News AggregatorStories to personalised feedsMostly pull + rank at read time over cached topic lists
d43 Calendar & RemindersAn invite or update to all attendeesPush to each attendee's calendar; big all-hands events fan out async
d53 Price Tracker & AlertsOne price drop to every watcherPush alerts via the notification system, deduplicated
d46 Online ChessMoves to players and spectatorsConnection fan-out per game room; spectators may get a slight delay
d52 Video ConferencingEach speaker's media to every participantSFU forwards streams (push), one upload, many forwarded copies
d24 File Sync"Something changed" to every device of the userPush a small notification, then each device pulls the changes
d38 Post Search / d57 Search EngineA query to every index shardRead-side scatter-gather (the "pull" shape); tail latency is the enemy

Notice the "push a hint, then pull the data" shape in file sync and many mobile apps: the notification carries no data, just "go fetch". It keeps the push tiny and lets the pull use normal caching and permissions.
