Remember this

  • Push (fan-out on write) = cheap reads, write cost x followers. Pull (fan-out on read) = cheap writes, read cost x followees.
  • Feeds are read-heavy (about 50:1 at Twitter in 2012), so push is the default.
  • Celebrity problem: huge follower counts break push; the hybrid pulls those accounts at read time.
  • Store IDs, not posts, in capped lists; hydrate from a post cache; filter deletes at read time.
  • Fan out asynchronously via a queue, chunked, idempotent, with priority lanes.
  • For live delivery, fan out once per server (Slack gateways, Discord Manifold) - like multicast.
  • Pull wins when ranking changes often or readers are passive (LinkedIn).
