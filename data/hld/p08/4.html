Group size thresholds in messaging

Messaging uses the same split by group size. Small groups (a family chat): push a copy or a per-member inbox entry for every member, which also drives unread counts and push notifications. Huge channels or broadcast lists: store the message once in the channel's log; online members get it through the connection fan-out above; offline members pull from the channel log with a "last read" cursor when they open it. Our research notes record WhatsApp's group limit as 1,024 members, which is one reason a per-member inbox stays affordable there.

Choose push when
  • Reads far exceed writes
  • Follower counts are bounded or small
  • Order is simple (chronological)
  • Read latency is the top requirement
Choose pull when
  • Some senders have enormous audiences
  • Most receivers are passive
  • Ranking changes often
  • Storage of copies is too costly
