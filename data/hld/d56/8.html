Failure modes to name

  • ECMP rehash during LB upgrades: covered by consistent hashing; drain nodes one at a time.
  • Health-check storm or false positives taking out a healthy pool: fail open, rate-limit ejections (eject at most X% at once).
  • Retry storms: L7 retries multiply load during an outage. Use retry budgets and only retry idempotent requests.
  • Hot backend from stickiness or hash skew: bounded-load hashing, shorter stickiness, P2C.
  • Connection table exhaustion under SYN flood: SYN cookies, upstream DDoS filtering (Cloudflare's l4drop runs before Unimog in the XDP chain).
  • Long-lived connections (WebSockets, gRPC streams) do not rebalance after scale-out: cap connection age and let clients reconnect (see the Real-time Updates pattern).
