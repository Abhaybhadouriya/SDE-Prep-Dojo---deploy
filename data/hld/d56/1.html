Requirements

Functional

  • Accept TCP/UDP traffic on a VIP and forward it to a healthy backend (L4).
  • For HTTP(S): terminate TLS, route by host, path or header, retry safe requests, add headers like X-Forwarded-For (L7).
  • Pluggable algorithms: round robin, weighted, least connections / least outstanding requests, consistent hash.
  • Active health checks; remove failed backends; drain backends gracefully for deploys.
  • Optional stickiness (cookie or hash) for stateful backends.
  • Control-plane API to add/remove backends and change weights.
