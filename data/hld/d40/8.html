  • The inventory shard primary dies. Fail over to a synchronous or semi-sync replica. During the failover, bookings return 503 and search keeps working (AP side). Never take bookings on an async replica that might be behind.
  • Redis is lost. The read path falls back to the database (rate-limit it), or shows "availability on request". Bookings are unaffected.
  • A PSP webhook is lost. A reconciliation job polls the PSP for intents older than N minutes in the pending state. See payment system.
  • The sweeper is down. Nothing breaks. Reads count expired holds as free, and every booking transaction expires the overlapping stale holds itself before it checks capacity. Only stale rows for nights nobody is booking stay in the table. Alert on "held rows past expiry" so you notice the job is gone.
  • Thundering herd at sale open. Use a waiting room, rate limits per user, and pre-warmed caches. See rate limiter.
