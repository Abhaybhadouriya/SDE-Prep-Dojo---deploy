Requirements

Functional

  • Keyword search over the web: return the top 10 results with title, URL and a snippet; paginate.
  • Rank by relevance (text match) and quality (links, freshness, spam signals).
  • Handle phrases, spelling correction and basic synonyms.
  • Fresh content: news pages searchable in minutes; most pages refreshed every few days to weeks.
  • Out of scope unless asked: images, ads auction, personalization, autocomplete (see Search Autocomplete), the Knowledge Graph.
