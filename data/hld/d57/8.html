Failure modes to name

  • Query of death crashing every leaf: canary requests, input limits, crash-loop protection.
  • Bad index push (corrupt or low-quality shard version): canary a few replicas, compare result quality metrics, keep the previous version for instant rollback.
  • One slow replica dragging p99: hedging and probation.
  • Cache stampede after a celebrity event: request coalescing on the cache key.
  • Spam and link farms gaming PageRank: spam classifiers, link-graph analysis, demotions computed offline.
  • Datacenter loss: DNS shifts users to other clusters; each cluster holds a full index copy (Google kept "dozens of copies of the Web" across clusters in 2003).
