Resources

  • Brin and Page: The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine (1998) paper - architecture, barrels, hit lists, PageRank (d = 0.85), anchor text, crawl numbers. PDF.
  • Barroso, Dean, Holzle: Web Search for a Planet (IEEE Micro 2003) paper - document-sharded index, replica pools, docservers, cluster design.
  • Dean and Barroso: The Tail at Scale (CACM 2013) paper - fan-out math, hedged and tied requests, canaries, good-enough results. PDF.
  • Peng and Dabek: Percolator, large-scale incremental processing (OSDI 2010) paper - incremental indexing, 50% fresher results.
  • Google: How Search organizes information doc - index size, crawling and multiple indexes.
  • Google Search Central: In-depth guide to how Google Search works doc - crawling, rendering, canonical clusters, serving factors.
  • ByteByteGo: How do search engines work? blog - crawl, index, serve overview (system-design-101); see also its Bloom-filter URL dedupe guide.
  • awesome-system-design-resources repo - lists "Design Google Search" with the video above.
