Resources

  • The Chubby lock service for loosely-coupled distributed systems (OSDI 2006) paper - sessions, 12 s leases, 45 s grace period, sequencers, lock-delay, proxies, outage stats. PDF.
  • Martin Kleppmann: How to do distributed locking blog - efficiency vs correctness, fencing tokens, Redlock critique. Counterpoint: antirez.
  • ZooKeeper Recipes and Solutions doc - lock, shared lock, revocable lock and leader election recipes.
  • ZooKeeper Programmer's Guide doc - sessions, watches, zxid, ephemeral and TTL nodes, guarantees.
  • etcd: Why etcd (comparison with ZooKeeper, Consul) doc - leases, revisions as fencing tokens, lock safety notes. Also etcd FAQ for cluster sizing.
  • Redis: Distributed Locks with Redis (Redlock) doc - single-instance lock, Redlock steps and its caveats.
  • AWS Builders' Library: Leader election in distributed systems blog - leases at Amazon, DynamoDB lock client, pitfalls.
  • Kubernetes: Leases doc - node heartbeats and leader election with Lease objects.
  • ByteByteGo: Why use a distributed lock? blog - six use cases (system-design-101).
  • awesome-system-design-resources repo - lists this design as a hard problem with the Chubby paper.
