Requirements

Functional

  • Clients open a session with the service and keep it alive with heartbeats (KeepAlives).
  • acquire(lock) exclusive or shared, blocking or try-only; release(lock).
  • If a client dies or is cut off, its locks are freed automatically after its session lease expires.
  • Each successful acquire returns a fencing token (Chubby calls it a sequencer) that only ever increases.
  • Watches: tell me when this lock is released or this small file changes (used for leader discovery and config).
  • Small key/value data next to locks (the new leader writes its address; others read it).

Non-functional

Say this early: "This is a CP system. If there is no majority, I would rather refuse to grant locks than grant two. Availability comes from replication across failure domains, not from relaxing consistency."
