How real companies do it

  • Google Chubby. 5-replica Paxos cells; coarse advisory locks; GFS and Bigtable master election and metadata; most use is actually naming. Snapshot cell: 22k direct + 32k proxied clients, 1-2k RPC/s, 93% KeepAlives.
  • Apache ZooKeeper. ZAB-replicated znodes, ephemeral and sequential nodes, one-time watches (persistent recursive watches since 3.6); locks via the Curator recipes.
  • etcd. Raft; leases, revisions, native lock and election RPCs; reliable up to several GB of data per its docs; the store behind Kubernetes.
  • Amazon. Leases are "the most widely used leader election mechanism at Amazon", usually via the DynamoDB lock client or ZooKeeper, with sharded leaders per data item in DynamoDB, EBS and EFS.
  • Uber. A Marketplace meetup talk covers using distributed locking to build reliable systems (video below).
