Remember this

  • A distributed lock is a lease. Leases expire while the holder may still be running.
  • Fencing tokens (strictly increasing) checked by the resource are what make it safe.
  • CP design: 5 replicas, majority 3, leader with a master lease; refuse rather than double-grant.
  • Heartbeats dominate load; scale with longer leases, proxies and caching.
  • Sessions + grace period let locks survive leader failover.
  • Ephemeral sequential nodes + watch-your-predecessor = fair queue, no herd.
  • Redis locks are for efficiency; correctness needs consensus or a transactional DB.
  • Coarse locks in the service; fine-grained locks via sharded owners or DB conditional writes.
