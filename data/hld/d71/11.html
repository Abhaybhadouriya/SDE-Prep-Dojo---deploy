Remember this

  • A subscription is a schedule plus a payment authority; each cycle becomes exactly one order and at most one successful charge.
  • The cycle row (subscription, cycle number) is the idempotency anchor; downstream keys are derived from it.
  • Scheduler: indexed poll with SKIP LOCKED first, time buckets or delayed queues later; the DB row is always the truth.
  • Act at the action time (cut-off, pre-debit), not the delivery time.
  • Mandate once with bank 2FA; trust signed webhooks; store tokens, never card numbers.
  • Dunning: soft declines retry over days, hard declines need a new method, then suspend or cancel by policy.
  • Cut-off rule plus optimistic locking resolves pause and skip races.
  • Reuse order, inventory, payment and notification; build subscription, scheduler, dunning, reconciliation.
