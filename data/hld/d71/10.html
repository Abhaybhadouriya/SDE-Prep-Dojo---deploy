How real systems do it

Seen in 4 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #40. Company view: the Amazon guide and the Walmart guide.

  • Razorpay Subscriptions: plans (fixed, usage-based, add-ons), subscriptions that charge automatically at plan intervals, an invoice per cycle, cards tokenised with consent, UPI AutoPay and e-mandates, automatic retries and webhooks for every state change.
  • Stripe Billing: Smart Retries: ML-timed retries, by default up to 8 within 2 weeks; hard decline codes are not retried; after the last attempt the subscription is cancelled, marked unpaid, left past due or paused, as configured.
  • Stripe: India recurring payments: e-mandate registration with AFA, a pre-debit notice at least 24 hours before each charge, AFA for debits above Rs 15,000, and a 26-hour wait before charging.
  • Amazon Subscribe & Save and grocery "milk every morning" apps follow the same shape: a cut-off the evening before, orders generated in bulk overnight for picking, and skip or pause up to the cut-off.
