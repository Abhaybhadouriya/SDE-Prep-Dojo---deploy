Failure modes

  • Scheduler down at the cut-off: run at least two instances; alert on "oldest due subscription age"; catch-up logic skips cycles whose delivery has passed.
  • Order service slow: workers back off, the queue grows, lag alert fires before the cut-off budget is gone; dispatch earlier next time (start before the cut-off for subscriptions that cannot change, for example those with no changes allowed after a date).
  • Gateway timeout: cycle stays CHARGING; reconciler queries by idempotency key; no blind retry.
  • Webhook storm or replay: verify signature, dedupe by event id, enqueue, process idempotently, return 200 quickly.
  • Timezone and calendar edges: "the 31st" in February (use the last day of month), daylight saving, public holidays (shift delivery, keep the billing date), leap years.
  • Mandate expiry: warn 2 weeks before; renew with the customer present.
