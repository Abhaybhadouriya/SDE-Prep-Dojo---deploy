Failure modes

  • Feed disconnect: standby handler takes over; a gap in seq triggers a snapshot request to the vendor. Window rules must not fire on a stale price after a gap.
  • Bad tick (a fat-finger price 90% off): sanity-check against recent range and the exchange's circuit limits before evaluating; a false "down 50%" alert to millions is the worst bug.
  • Evaluator crash: partition moves; rebuild as in deep dive 4; fire guard stops duplicates.
  • Notification outage: alerts-fired backs up in Kafka; send late with the price at trigger time in the message.
  • Alerts DB down: evaluation continues from memory; new subscriptions fail (or queue); fire guard writes retry.
