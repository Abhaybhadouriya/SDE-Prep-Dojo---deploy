Requirements

Merged from the seven Uber reports linked in the interview section. Wording varied ("stock broker notification framework", "price change by a delta", "rolling windows"), but the core was the same.

Functional

  1. Ingest An exchange or broker feed sends {symbol, price, closingPrice} for every symbol about once per second.
  2. Subscribe A user creates an alert: (a) move from close: price is up or down 5% vs the previous close; (b) delta: price moved by X (absolute or %) from when I subscribed; (c) rolling window: price moved X% within the last minute, hour, day, week, month or year.
  3. Notify When a rule is met, push to the user (app push, SMS or email) within seconds.
  4. Lifecycle A subscription is removed once it fires, or after one month without firing. Users can list and cancel.

Out of scope: trading and orders (see Stock Broker App), live price streaming to charts (mention it as a sibling), and the exchange's own matching engine (see Stock Exchange).

Non-functional

PropertyTargetWhy
Alert latencyp99 tick-to-push under 5 sA stock alert that is a minute late is wrong
CorrectnessEach subscription fires at most once; none is silently lostThe spec removes a subscription after firing
ThroughputEvery symbol, every second, all market hoursThe feed is constant; the market open is the burst
RecoveryA crashed worker resumes in under a minute without double alertsCrashes at market open are the worst case
AvailabilityPrefer late over lost; degrade by delaying low-priority channelsUsers trust alerts with money decisions

Clarify early: "Is the window a fixed menu (1 min, 1 h, 1 day, ...) or any duration?" A fixed menu lets every subscriber on a symbol share one window state per size. That single answer turns millions of per-user windows into a few per symbol.
