Failure modes

  • Gateway node dies: 100k devices reconnect at once. Exponential backoff with jitter on devices; presence entries expire by TTL so pushes are not sent to a dead node.
  • Push lost: a device misses version N. Next push carries N+1, so it notices the gap and pulls. Also a periodic "what is your version?" heartbeat.
  • Old device dies mid-transfer: setup session tracks applied sections; resume from fallback for the rest.
  • Upload interrupted: chunks already uploaded are kept (within a TTL); the next attempt asks "missing?" and continues.
  • Region outage: restores read from the replica region; new backups queue on devices and retry later.
