Requirements

The prompts are short and unusual (one candidate called the Amazon one a custom, team-specific problem). Drive the requirements yourself and say which of the three parts you are solving first.

Functional

PartMust do
(a) Old-to-new setupUser chooses or logs in to an account on the new device; picks the old device; settings (Wi-Fi, language, wake word, default apps such as music provider, routines, linked smart-home devices) arrive; device ready in 1-5 minutes. Works across models (an Echo Show has screen settings an Echo Dot lacks).
(b) Multi-device stateUp to 10 devices per user. Change on one (turn off a light, change a do-not-disturb schedule) appears on all others in near real time. Offline devices catch up when they reconnect.
(c) Backup and restoreScheduled backups of settings, files and media; restore to a new device; keep several snapshots; user can see "last backed up" time.

0lost backup data (integrity)
  • Security: settings include Wi-Fi passwords and account tokens. Encrypt in transit and at rest; only devices of the same user, and only after explicit pairing, can receive them.
  • Scalable: tens of millions of users, hundreds of millions of devices.
  • Storage efficient: the follow-up in the Amazon round was specifically about reducing storage.
  • Durable and observable for backups: you must be able to prove a backup is restorable.
