How real companies do it

  • Meta (Facebook) memcache. The NSDI 2013 paper describes a lookaside cache serving billions of requests per second and holding trillions of items. Techniques to remember: leases (stop stampedes and stale sets), mcrouter (the routing proxy), regional pools, a gutter pool for failed servers, and commit-log-driven invalidation across regions.
  • Netflix EVCache. A Memcached-based cache that replicates data across availability zones so a zone loss does not empty the cache.
  • Discord. In its 2023 post on storing trillions of messages, Discord describes Rust data services that coalesce concurrent requests for the same data and route by channel with consistent hashing, protecting the database from hot channels.
  • Twitter twemproxy and Meta mcrouter are open-source proxies that shard and route Memcached/Redis traffic.
  • Redis Cluster: 16,384 slots, CRC16, MOVED/ASK redirects, and a gossip bus, all described in the cluster specification.
