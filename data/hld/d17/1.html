Requirements

Functional

  1. Basic operations get(key), set(key, value, ttl), delete(key); nice to have: mget (many keys at once), atomic incr, compare-and-set.
  2. Expiry Each key may have a TTL; expired keys are never returned.
  3. Eviction When memory is full, evict by a policy (LRU, LFU, or TTL-based) instead of failing writes.
  4. Scale out Add or remove nodes while running, moving only a small part of the keys.

Non-functional

Infra vs product side (from the learner notes): building the cache itself is the infra side; deciding what to cache, for how long, and how to invalidate it is the product side. Interviews test both, so this page covers both.
