Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Starting with "I'll use Kafka and Kubernetes"Start with users and requirements. Technologies come at minute 13, each with a reason.
Silent thinking for a minute or moreThink out loud. "I'm weighing two options..." The interviewer can only grade what they hear.
Ten minutes of estimatesThree or four numbers, then say what they change. Skip any number that changes nothing.
Going deep on internals before the flow worksFinish the happy path end to end first. Then deep-dive.
Designing for Google scale when the prompt says a startupMatch the numbers you agreed. Over-engineering is marked down too.
Ignoring hintsWhen the interviewer asks "what about X?", that is usually where they want you to go. Go there.
No failure talkWalk the four failure questions over your main boxes in the wrap-up.
"It depends" with no decision"It depends on A. Here, A is true, so I'd pick X."
Messy diagram with crossing arrowsLeft to right, max 8 boxes, numbered flow, zoom-ins on the side.
