Key APIs

APICallerBehaviour
GET /c/{click_token}User's browser (the ad's link)Verify the signed token, emit a click event, return 302 with Location: advertiser_url. Never waits for counting.
GET /v1/ads/{ad_id}/clicks?from=...&to=...&granularity=minute|hour|day&group_by=countryAdvertiser dashboardReturns time buckets with counts. Picks the coarsest table that covers the range.
GET /v1/campaigns/{id}/clicks?...AdvertiserSame, summed over the campaign's ads.
GET /v1/top-ads?window=1m&k=100Internal opsTop-K for the last closed minute.

The click_token is created when the ad is shown (the impression). It contains impression_id, ad_id and the advertiser URL, signed with an HMAC so nobody can forge clicks for random ads. The impression_id becomes our idempotency key: one impression can produce at most one billable click.

The "final": false flag is honest product design: the latest minute may still change a little as late clicks arrive and as reconciliation runs. Billing uses only finalized numbers.
