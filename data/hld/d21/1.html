Requirements

Functional
  • When a user clicks an ad, redirect them to the advertiser's page.
  • Record every valid click exactly once for billing.
  • Advertisers query click counts per ad (and campaign) at 1-minute granularity for recent data, hour/day for older data, filterable by country and device.
  • Show "top 100 ads in the last minute" for internal ops (a Top-K side feature).
Out of scope (say it)
  • Ad serving, bidding and auctions (a different system).
  • Conversion attribution (purchase after click) beyond a mention.
  • A full ML fraud system - we leave a hook for it.
