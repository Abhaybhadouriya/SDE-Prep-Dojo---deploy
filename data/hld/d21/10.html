How real systems do it

  • Uber - real-time exactly-once ad event processing: Kafka plus Flink with three jobs (aggregation, attribution, union-and-load) running in two regions; dedup with keyed state; 1-minute tumbling windows; Kafka transactions with read_committed; two-phase commit at each checkpoint with a 2-minute checkpoint interval; 3 days of Kafka retention; a UUID per record used as the idempotency key, with Hive deduplicating on it and Pinot upserting on it.
