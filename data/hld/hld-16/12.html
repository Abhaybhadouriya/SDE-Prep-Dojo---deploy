The 60-second answer

The 60-second answer (to "how do you approach an LLD or machine-coding round?"). "I spend the first 5 to 10 minutes pinning requirements and writing them down: the entities, the operations, and what will change later. Then I list the nouns as classes and the verbs as methods, and sketch a quick class diagram with composition versus association. I put anything that varies behind an interface, usually Strategy for policies and State for lifecycles, and keep everything else as plain classes; no patterns for show. I write the core path end to end first, in-memory, with a runnable driver, then handle the shared scarce resource with an atomic per-key operation and a TTL, then edge cases and tests. For the combined round I say which parts are HLD (services, storage, scaling) and zoom into one service's classes when asked."
