Handling a combined HLD + LLD round

A combined round (for example Microsoft's reported 75-minute "distributed LRU cache") starts wide and then zooms into one component. The trap is spending 60 minutes on boxes and having no time for classes, or the other way round. Ask early: "Do you want me to go broad on the architecture, or deep on the classes of one component?" Then budget time.
