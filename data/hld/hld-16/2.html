These formats come from many self-reported interview experiences gathered in our research notes (LeetCode Discuss, GeeksforGeeks, Glassdoor, 2024-2026). Teams and locations vary, so treat this as a pattern, not a rule.

The level rule of thumb from these reports: at SDE-1, LLD/OOD is the main design signal and HLD is light. At SDE-2, you are expected to drive a full HLD (requirements, estimates, storage, partitioning, failures) and pass an LLD or machine-coding round. A neutral design round plus a weak bar raiser can get you down-levelled to SDE-1 at Amazon.

For the HLD half, use the 45-minute interview framework and estimation. The rest of this page is about the LLD half.
