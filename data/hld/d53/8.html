Failure modes

  • Shop blocks or rate-limits us (HTTP 429 / 403 spike): back off per shop automatically, slow that shop's tier intervals, alert the on-call. Freshness degrades; nothing breaks.
  • Parser breaks after a layout change: canary products alarm; the validator quarantines out-of-band prices; no alerts go out from quarantined data.
  • Matcher crashes mid-batch: it restarts from the last committed Kafka offset and re-processes; the unique (watch_id, crossing_id) key stops duplicates.
  • Notification provider outage: alerts queue in Kafka; retry with backoff; the 1-hour budget absorbs short outages.
  • Redis loss: current-price and chart caches rebuild from the database and the compacted topic; the schedule set can be rebuilt from products.next_due_at.
