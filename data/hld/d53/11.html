  • Two loops: collect prices (fetch, feed, extension) and react to changes (match, notify). Kafka keyed by product joins them.
  • The fetch budget forces tiers: hourly for all 500M would be 12B fetches a day; tiered by interest it is about 230M.
  • Index alerts by product, armed flag and target; fire armed watches with target >= new price. The armed flag stops repeats. Disarm on fire; re-arm with hysteresis.
  • Stocks: push feed, in-memory per-symbol "above" and "below" books, sharded by symbol.
  • Every user-visible alert passes a validation gate: sanity band, confirmation, quarantine, canaries.
  • Bursts are the sizing event: decouple with Kafka, re-key the fan-out, cap and digest per user, make sends idempotent.
  • History is small; downsample with rollups and cache charts per product.
  • Be polite: rate limits, robots.txt, official APIs. Never design around evading a site's defences.
