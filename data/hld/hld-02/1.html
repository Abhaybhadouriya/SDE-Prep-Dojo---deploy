Stop here for SDE-1? No - but you can skim the Raft details and the count-min maths. Every other block is fair game at SDE-1 at the "what and why" level. SDE-2 interviewers expect you to pick between options and name the failure mode without being asked.

Before you go on, make sure you know the ideas on HLD Fundamentals (replication, sharding, CAP). This page builds on them.
