  • Consistency ladder: linearizable > sequential > causal > session > eventual. Money needs strong; feeds are fine eventual; give users read-your-writes.
  • Quorum: W + R > N means reads overlap the latest write. N=3, W=2, R=2 is the classic setting.
  • Raft: timeout, higher term, one vote per node per term, majority wins. 2f + 1 nodes survive f failures. Leases need fencing tokens.
  • Across services: saga with compensations, outbox for reliable events, idempotent consumers. 2PC blocks and couples services.
  • Idempotency key: one per logical operation, reused on retries, enforced by a unique constraint.
  • Bloom filter: "no" is certain, "yes" is maybe; ~10 bits/item for ~1% false positives.
  • Count-min sketch: take the minimum across rows; never under-counts; great for heavy hitters.
  • Geohash: shared prefix = nearby, but always search the 8 neighbours. Quadtrees adapt to density.
  • Token bucket = burst + average; do the check atomically in Redis; fail open.
  • Backpressure: every queue is bounded; when full you block, reject, shed, buffer durably, or scale.
  • Failure detection: heartbeats + a timeout; shorter is faster but gives false alarms (GC pauses). Since you can be wrong, failover must be fenced.
  • Beyond the ring: rendezvous hashing (no ring), jump hash (numbered shards), bounded loads (cap per server), Maglev (LB lookup table).
