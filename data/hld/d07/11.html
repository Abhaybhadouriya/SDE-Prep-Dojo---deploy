Remember this

You might confuse this with:

  • Prep time vs ETA: prep time is how long the kitchen takes; ETA is the whole promise to the customer (prep or rider wait, whichever is later, plus the ride).
  • Greedy vs batched matching: greedy gives each order the nearest free rider the moment it arrives; batched collects a few seconds of orders and riders and solves them together for the lowest total wait.
  • Order events vs location pings: order events are few, must never be lost and go through the outbox; pings are tens of thousands a second and only the latest one matters, so losing one is harmless.

Remember this

  • Three sides: customer, restaurant, rider. The core loop is discover, order, cook, assign, track.
  • Orders are small in volume (hundreds/s). Rider pings are huge (tens of thousands/s). Store them differently.
  • Place-order is idempotent (a client key + a UNIQUE constraint). Status changes are conditional updates, and events go out through an outbox.
  • Dispatch is batched matching per zone, timed so the rider arrives as the food is ready. Lock the rider atomically with a TTL.
  • ETA = prep + travel + last mile, re-estimated live and measured against reality.
  • Serviceability = precomputed H3 cells. Nearby riders = in-memory geo index plus neighbour cells.
  • At peak, riders run out before servers. Degrade on purpose: honest ETAs, smaller areas, kitchen caps, surge.
