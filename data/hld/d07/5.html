Architecture: follow one order

Click Next (or Play) to walk one dosa order through the system. Each step adds the boxes it needs.

  1. Discover. The app sends the delivery address. Discovery converts it to an H3 cell, asks Elasticsearch for restaurants whose serviceable area covers that cell, then ranks them (distance, rating, current kitchen load, ETA). Menus come from a cache with a version number.
  2. Place the order. The order service checks the Idempotency-Key, re-prices the cart, and in one SQL transaction inserts the order (status PLACED) plus an order.placed row in the outbox. A double tap returns the same order.
  3. Pay. The order service asks the payment service to collect money (UPI or card). The result arrives by callback. On success the order moves to CONFIRMED (again with an outbox event). On failure it moves to PAYMENT_FAILED and nothing is cooked.
  4. Tell the kitchen. The outbox relay publishes order.confirmed to Kafka, keyed by orderId. The restaurant service pushes it to the tablet. The kitchen accepts and says "15 minutes", which becomes order.accepted with prepMinutes.
  5. Plan the rider. Dispatch for that zone collects orders for a short window, reads free riders near the restaurant from Redis GEO, and solves a matching problem. It does not just pick the nearest rider. It waits until "rider travel time to the restaurant" is about equal to "food ready time".
  6. Offer and lock. Dispatch offers the job to the chosen rider and atomically flips that rider from FREE to OFFERED (compare-and-set), so no other order can grab them. If the rider accepts, the order becomes RIDER_ASSIGNED. If they decline or time out, the order goes back into the next matching window.
  7. Track. Rider pings go to the location service (latest into Redis, the stream into Kafka). The tracking gateway holds the customer's WebSocket and pushes the rider position plus a fresh ETA every few seconds.
  8. Deliver and learn. The rider enters the customer's OTP, the order becomes DELIVERED, and the event feeds ratings, rider payouts, analytics and the next ETA model.

The whiteboard version (6 boxes, redraw it in 30 seconds): App -> Gateway -> Order svc (+ SQL + outbox) -> Kafka -> Dispatch (+ Redis GEO of riders) -> Tracking gateway -> back to App. Add Discovery + Elasticsearch on the side. Everything else is detail you add when asked.
