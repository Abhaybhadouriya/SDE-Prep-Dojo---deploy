Requirements

Scope it the way Hello Interview does: one format only, 3-minute blitz with no increment. Each player has 3 minutes in total, and only the clock of the player to move is running. Each player has a skill rating that goes up when they win and down when they lose.

Functional

  1. Matchmaking A player presses "Play 3+0". Within a few seconds they are paired with someone of similar rating and a game starts.
  2. Real-time play Moves are sent to the server, validated against the rules, applied, and pushed to the opponent. The server runs both clocks and declares checkmate, stalemate, resignation, draw or timeout.
  3. Leaderboard A global ranking by rating. A player can see the top 100 and their own rank, updated shortly after each game ends.
  4. Reconnect If a phone drops off Wi-Fi for 5 seconds, the player comes back to the same board with the right clock.

Out of scope (say so out loud): spectating, chat, puzzles, analysis and replays, tournaments, anti-cheat. Mention that anti-cheat would read the same finished-game event stream later.
