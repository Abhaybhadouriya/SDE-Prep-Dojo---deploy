Why does every move carry a ply number?

  1. To compress the PGN
  2. To compute the rating
  3. So duplicates and out-of-order messages are detected, like TCP sequence numbers
The server accepts only "next expected ply" from the side to move; retries become harmless and reconnect can ask for "everything after ply N".

What is the best way to stop two matchmakers from pairing the same player?

  1. An atomic Redis Lua script that finds and removes both players in one step
  2. A cron job that cleans up duplicate games
  3. Let the client refuse the second game
Atomic check-and-remove closes the race; the cleanup and client options act after the damage is done.

Where does Kafka belong in this design?

  1. On every move between the two players

  3. Holding the WebSocket connections
Game end is a fan-out, replayable event. Moves are a two-party, millisecond path that should go straight to the owner.
