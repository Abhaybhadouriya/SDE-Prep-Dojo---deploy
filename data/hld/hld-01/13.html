Putting it all together

Here is the "standard web architecture" that most designs start from. Learn to draw it in 30 seconds. Every design on this site is a variation of it.

And here is where the other tracks of this site live inside that picture: every service box is a Docker container; the "stateless services" zone is a Kubernetes Deployment behind a Service and Ingress, scaled by the HPA; the queue is a Kafka cluster, maybe run on Kubernetes by an operator (see Deploy on Kubernetes with Strimzi).
