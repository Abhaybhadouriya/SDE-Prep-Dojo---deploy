Best way to show a live viewer count to 50M viewers?

  1. Kubernetes cannot autoscale
  2. CDNs do not support autoscaling
  3. Load arrives in minutes, faster than nodes and pods start
  4. Autoscaling is too expensive

  1. It must run faster than real time in a single pass
