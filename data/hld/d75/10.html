How the real companies do it

Seen in 2 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #60. Company view: the Oracle & Adobe guide and the Food & Quick-Commerce guide.

  • Twitch: Ingesting live video streams at global scale (2022): nearly 100 PoPs on a private backbone; the Intelligest proxy terminates RTMP/WebRTC and a routing service chooses an origin using live capacity data.
  • Twitch: Low latency, high reach (2021): latency from ~15 s to ~3 s with low-latency HLS; transcoding once offered to only 2-3% of channels; hardware transcoding at 10x capacity for 2x cost.
  • ByteByteGo: How Disney+ Hotstar scaled for 60M concurrent users: cacheable APIs moved to the CDN, EKS migration, step-wise node scaling, an Envoy-based central gateway.
  • ICC: World Cup 2023 broadcast and digital records: 59 million peak concurrent viewers for the final.
  • AWS CloudFront: signed URLs vs signed cookies: cookies for many files such as an HLS video.
  • Apple: Enabling low-latency HLS: partial segments, blocking playlist reload, preload hints.
