CDNSingle providerMulti-CDN with steeringMulti-CDN for record events; adds cost and complexity.
  • Encoder or ingest failure: redundant ingest (primary + backup feeds pinned together), automatic failover; Twitch's routing service keeps related feeds together for this reason.
  • Origin overload on cache miss storms: shield + coalescing; never let tokens into the cache key.
  • Playlist stalls: if the packager stops updating, players stall everywhere at once. Alert on "playlist age" per rendition.
  • Token service down at kickoff: allow already-issued tokens to keep working (refresh grace), cache entitlements, pre-issue tokens before start.
  • Bitrate ceiling: if aggregate demand nears CDN capacity, cap the top rendition for everyone rather than letting the CDN fail.
