Requirements

The list below is merged from the real interview reports linked in the interview section. One Amazon SDE-2 report (2025-10) even gave the numbers, so we use them instead of inventing our own.

Functional

  1. Search Find songs, artists and albums by name, tolerant of typos and partial words ("arijt", "shape of").
  2. Play Tap a song and hear it quickly; seek; skip; the next song starts with no gap.
  3. Catalogue browse An artist page lists their albums and singles; an album lists its tracks. A song can be a single or part of an album, and a song can have several singers (Flipkart asked exactly this).
  4. Playlists Create, add, delete, reorder (drag and drop), shuffle play, share with friends, and optionally let friends edit (Apple asked add/delete/reshuffle/share).
  5. Library "Liked songs", saved albums, followed artists.
  6. Listening history Recently played, plus events for recommendations, play counts and artist royalties.
  7. Voice (Amazon variant) "Alexa, play Believer by Imagine Dragons" on an Echo device.

Out of scope unless asked: uploading by artists, podcasts and video, lyrics, social feed, the recommendation model itself (covered in Recommendation System), payments internals (covered in Payment System).

Ask one clarifying question that splits the design: "Is this the whole app, or only the playlist service?" Apple asked only for the playlist service in one round. Flipkart's first half was the data model. Amazon wanted the full microservice picture. Scope first, then draw.
