Seen in 7 recent reports. Every variant, with company, level, date and source post, is in Recently Asked HLD Questions #20. Company view: the Amazon guide and the Flipkart guide.

  • Spotify P2P paper (2010): a measurement study of the original Spotify client: median play start 265 ms, and only 8.8% of played data from Spotify's servers, the rest from local cache and peers. P2P was later removed, but the lessons on caching, prefetch and fast start remain.
  • Spotify engineering: aligning CDN services: audio runs on Akamai and AWS as business-critical content; Fastly carries other content; config is standardised across squads.
  • Spotify engineering: event delivery in the cloud: the event pipeline behind listening history and recommendations, which grew to about 8M events per second at peak.
  • Spotify community: formats and bitrates: the delivery formats (Ogg Vorbis 96/160/320, AAC 128/256, HE-AACv2 24) we used in the estimate.
  • Elasticsearch fuzzy query: Levenshtein edits, AUTO fuzziness, max_expansions and prefix_length.
