Failure modes: signing key leak (rotate immediately, revoke all tokens with that kid, force re-login); clock skew rejecting valid tokens (small leeway, NTP everywhere); JWKS endpoint outage (services cache keys for hours); hashing pool overload during a stuffing attack (rate limit before hashing, not after); replication lag after a password change letting an old session refresh in another region (route refreshes for a user to the home region, or check a "credentials changed at" timestamp).
