Hot keys in the stream

Keying by ad_id means one viral ad sends all its events to one partition and one Flink task. Hello Interview's fix is key salting: write as ad_id:0 ... ad_id:N to spread the load, then strip the suffix and sum when writing the result. Another fix is local pre-aggregation: each producer or click server sums its own counts for a second before sending one combined event. Both trade a little latency or a second aggregation step for even load. More on Scaling Writes.
