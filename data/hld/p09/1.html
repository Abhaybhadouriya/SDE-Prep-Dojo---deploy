When you hit it: the signals

  • "Show the like / view / share count" - Distributed Counter, Reddit votes.
  • "Advertisers see clicks per ad per minute; we bill on them" - Ad Click Aggregator.
  • "Top 10 songs / products / hashtags right now" - Top-K, Trending Topics.
  • "Rank players, show my rank" - Leaderboard.
  • "Daily active users, unique visitors" - unique counting (cardinality).
  • "Requests per second per service, p99 latency per minute" - Metrics & Monitoring. (p99 is 99th-percentile latency: 99 of every 100 requests finish faster than this. It measures the slow tail, not the average, a bit like worst-case versus average interrupt latency.)
  • Any requirement with the words per minute, last hour, rolling, real-time dashboard, approximate is fine, must be exact for billing.

The first question to ask the interviewer is always: how exact, and how fresh? A like count can be a few seconds stale and a little off. An ad-billing count must be exact eventually, but a dashboard may show an estimate first. A unique-visitor count is almost always fine at 1% error. These two answers pick your design.
