Real-world: how companies do it

  • Uber ad events - three Flink jobs across two regions, 1-minute tumbling windows, dedupe in keyed state, Kafka transactions with 2-minute checkpoints, UUIDs for idempotent Hive and Pinot writes; several hundred million ad events a week.
  • Facebook - HyperLogLog in Presto (APPROX_DISTINCT, APPROX_SET, MERGE), sketches stored per day and merged; 7x-1,000x speedups.
  • LinkedIn / Jay Kreps - the Kappa argument: reprocess from a retained Kafka log instead of keeping a second batch codebase.
  • Redis - HLL, count-min sketch and sorted sets as building blocks for uniques, heavy hitters and leaderboards.
