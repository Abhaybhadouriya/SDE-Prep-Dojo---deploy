How real systems do it

  • Amazon Locker launched in September 2011 in New York, Seattle and London (Wikipedia) and now has more than 25,000 US locations. Amazon's own guide says customers get a six-digit code, a barcode, or app unlock, have three calendar days to collect, and that unclaimed items are returned and refunded. Items must be under 10 lb and under 16 x 12 x 14 inches.
  • DHL Packstation in Germany has over 15,500 machines, and about 90% of people in Germany live within ten minutes of one (Wikipedia). InPost runs almost 29,000 lockers in Poland. Parcel lockers are a mature, high-volume edge system.
  • Walmart pickup and Express Delivery use a capacity and slot management system that opens slots based on order size and available labour; it rolled out to about 2,000 stores in 2020. Walmart's Store Assist product splits picking from check-in and dispense.
  • AWS IoT Device Shadow is the textbook cloud model for "desired vs reported" device state with versions, useful for any kiosk fleet.
