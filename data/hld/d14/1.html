Requirements

Functional
  • Browse movies or events by city and date; search by name.
  • See the shows (time, screen, price) and the seat map with live availability.
  • Select up to N seats (for example 10) and hold them for a few minutes.
  • Pay; on success get a confirmed booking with a ticket / QR code by app, email or SMS.
  • Cancel a booking (refund according to policy); release unpaid holds automatically.
  • Admins and cinemas add venues, screens, layouts, shows and prices.
  • For hot events: a fair queue before the seat map, plus per-user ticket limits.
Non-functional
  • No double booking, ever. Seat ownership needs strong consistency (CP: under a network split, refuse rather than risk two owners; see CAP).
  • Browsing is fast and always up (100-300 ms) and may be a few seconds stale (AP: keep answering, even if slightly out of date).
  • Holds are fair and short: abandoned holds must come back quickly, or shows look sold out while seats sit empty.
  • Survive flash crowds: traffic for a hot on-sale can be many times a normal day.
  • Payments are exactly-once from the user's view: never charge twice, never charge without a ticket.
  • Bot defence and per-user limits for fairness.

Say early: "Browsing and the seat map are read-heavy and can be slightly stale. Taking a seat is a tiny, strongly consistent write. I will keep those two paths apart, and the seat write is checked only against the database of record."
