How the real companies do it

  • Ticketmaster's Eras Tour statement (Nov 2022): "Taylor Swift | The Eras Tour Onsale Explained" (opens in a browser; it blocks scripts). It reports about 3.5 billion system requests, 4x its previous peak, and explains the Verified Fan pre-registration. Read it as a case study in demand shaping.
  • Waiting rooms at the edge: Cloudflare: Building Waiting Room on Workers and Durable Objects explains how a queue can live at the CDN instead of the origin, and the Waiting Room docs show the knobs (active users, session duration).
  • Locks done right: PostgreSQL explicit locking (row locks, FOR UPDATE, SKIP LOCKED), Redis distributed locks, and Kleppmann on distributed locking (fencing tokens).
  • Interview walkthrough: Hello Interview: Design Ticketmaster covers TTL reservations, a virtual queue and Elasticsearch for events.
  • Payments side: Razorpay webhooks and Stripe webhooks both tell you to verify signatures and handle duplicate deliveries, which is exactly the idempotent confirm step.
