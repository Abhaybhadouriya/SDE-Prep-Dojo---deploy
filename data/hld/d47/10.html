How it is done in the real world

  • vLLM (UC Berkeley, SOSP 2023): PagedAttention, continuous batching, prefix caching. The vLLM blog reports that LMSYS cut the GPUs serving its Chatbot Arena traffic by 50% after switching, while handling about 30K requests a day with 60K peaks.
  • Orca (OSDI 2022): introduced iteration-level scheduling and selective batching.
  • DistServe (OSDI 2024): disaggregated prefill and decode for goodput under TTFT/TPOT SLOs.
  • Hugging Face TGI: production server with continuous batching and SSE streaming, with an overload error for backpressure.
  • OpenAI API: publicly documents limits in RPM/TPM/RPD/TPD, rate-limit headers and retry guidance.
