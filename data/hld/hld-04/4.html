Availability: the nines Good to know

Availability is the fraction of time the system works. People say it in "nines". Each extra nine cuts allowed downtime by 10x and usually costs a lot more engineering.

Two useful rules: parts in a chain multiply their availabilities (two 99.9% services in series give about 99.8%), and redundant copies multiply their failure chances (two independent 99% replicas: both down only 0.01% of the time, so about 99.99%). That second rule assumes failures are independent - a shared power supply or a bad deploy breaks it. Reliability is covered in depth on Reliability at Scale.
