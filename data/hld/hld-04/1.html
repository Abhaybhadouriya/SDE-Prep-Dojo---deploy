Powers of two (and ten) you must know Must know

Computers count in powers of two, people in powers of ten. Luckily 2^10 = 1,024, which is close enough to 1,000. So every step of 10 in the power of two is a step of a thousand.

Time conversions (the most useful trick on this page)

~10^5 sin a day (86,400 - about 1 lakh)
~2.5 M sin a month (2.59 million)
~3 x 10^7 sin a year (31.5 million)
1M/day = 12/sso 100M/day is about 1,200/s

Rounding a day up to 100,000 seconds makes every per-second number about 14% too low. That is fine - we add a peak factor anyway. If you prefer, use 86,400 and round the result.
