Remember this

  • 2^10 is about a thousand; each step of 10 in the power is x1,000: KB, MB, GB, TB, PB.
  • A day is about 10^5 seconds. 1M per day is about 12 per second.
  • Peak = average x 2-3 (much more for sales and launches). Always state it.
  • Storage = items/day x size x days x replicas. Bandwidth = QPS x size; bits / 8 = bytes.
  • Memory 100 ns, SSD random read 150 us, same-DC round trip 0.5 ms, cross-continent 150 ms.
  • 99.9% is about 8.8 h/year; 99.99% about 53 min/year; 99.999% about 5 min/year.
  • Every number ends with "so..." - the design decision it drives.
