How the real companies do it

  • Netflix (2024): Distributed Counter Abstraction - EVCache incr/decr for best-effort counters; event log + immutable-window rollups for eventually consistent ones; ~75K requests/s at single-digit ms.
  • Meta: TAO keeps association counts in a separate table and caches each in 14 bytes; count cache hits had a 1.1 ms median in 2013.
  • Reddit (2017): Kafka, a rules consumer, Redis HyperLogLogs per post, persisted to Cassandra every ~10 s.
  • SoundCloud: Cassandra counters for live totals, Hadoop batch to repair, Memcached for the ~90% most requested totals.
  • Google Cloud / AWS: Firestore's "distributed counters" and DynamoDB "write sharding" are the official sharded-counter recipes.
  • Cloudflare: per-data-centre rate-limit counters; 0.003% of 400M test requests misclassified.
