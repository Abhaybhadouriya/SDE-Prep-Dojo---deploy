Where Kafka, Kubernetes and Docker fit

ToolRoleDetail
KafkaShock absorber + durable log of deltasTopic like-events keyed by post_id: deltas for a post stay ordered and one consumer owns its aggregation. Consumer lag = how stale counts are. A viral post makes one partition hot, so pre-aggregate in the producer or salt known-hot keys (post_id#k). Exactly-once transactions stop double-adds on restarts. See Topics & partitions, Exactly-once, Kafka Streams & Flink.
KubernetesRun the stateless parts elasticallyLike service and read API are Deployments with HPA on RPS. Aggregators scale up to the partition count, ideally on consumer lag (KEDA). Redis/Cassandra run as StatefulSets or managed services; PodDisruptionBudgets stop a rollout from pausing every aggregator at once. See Autoscaling, StatefulSets.
DockerSame image online and in repair jobsThe nightly "recount from edges" batch job and the live aggregator share one container image and one counting library, so a fix lands in both. Netflix notes that its layered abstractions run as containers on the same host, each reading config for its own scope.
