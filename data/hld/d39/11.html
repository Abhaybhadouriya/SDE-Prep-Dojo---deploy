Remember this

  • Total load is easy; writes per second on one key is the real problem.
  • Like = idempotent edge insert (post, user); the edge table is the truth and the dedup.
  • Hot keys: shard the counter (only when hot), batch deltas through Kafka, serve reads from a rollup.
  • Atomic is not idempotent: make every hop retry-safe (unique keys, outbox, offsets with the write, idempotency tokens).
  • Unique viewers: HyperLogLog, 12 KB, 0.81% error, no deletes. Never approximate money.
  • Eventual total + read-your-writes and monotonic reads for the user.
  • Multi-region: per-region tallies merged as a CRDT; reconcile from edges to fix drift.
