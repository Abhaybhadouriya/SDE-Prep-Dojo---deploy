Why CDC wins
  • No app code change; captures every change including deletes
  • Seconds of latency; tiny DB overhead (reads a log it writes anyway)
  • One stream, many independent consumers, replayable
What it costs
  • Couples consumers to internal table schemas
  • New ops surface: slots, binlog retention, connectors, registry
  • Only per-row ordering; transactions are split across topics
