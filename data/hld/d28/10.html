How real systems do it

  • Shopify replaced a query-based batch extractor (data at least an hour old) with Debezium on Kafka Connect for its sharded MySQL monolith: 100+ shards, about 150 connectors on 12 Connect pods in Kubernetes, 400 TB+ in Kafka, about 65,000 records/s on average with peaks near 100,000/s during BFCM 2020, p99 under 10 seconds from MySQL insert to Kafka. They used Avro with Schema Registry, compacted topics keyed by primary key, and offloaded records larger than Kafka's 1 MB default to cloud storage with a pointer. Capturing every change from Shopify's sharded monolith.
  • Netflix DBLog introduced watermark-based chunk snapshots interleaved with log events, so full table dumps no longer need locks; Debezium's incremental snapshots follow the same idea. DBLog: a generic CDC framework (opens in a browser).
  • Razorpay (Indian fintech) described a pipeline of core transactional databases → Debezium → Kafka Streams → Amazon MSK → anomaly detection. How Razorpay built real-time anomaly detection with Amazon MSK.
  • Debezium documents CDC-driven cache invalidation and the outbox pattern with its Outbox Event Router. Automating cache invalidation with CDC, Reliable microservices data exchange with the outbox pattern.
  • LinkedIn: Jay Kreps' essay "The Log" explains why a single ordered log of changes is the backbone of data integration - the philosophy behind CDC and Kafka. The Log.
