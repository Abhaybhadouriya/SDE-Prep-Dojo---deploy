Failure modes

  • Shard down: tenants on it cannot change tags; reads come from a replica (stale but available). Others are unaffected - a benefit of tenant sharding.
  • Kafka or relay lag: trending and counts lag; tagging itself still works.
  • Redis loss: rebuild trending from the last 24 hours of Kafka; caches refill on read.
  • Automation loop (a rule that tags, which triggers itself): per-tenant write quotas and loop detection on the automation side.
