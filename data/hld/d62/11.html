  • Three tables: tag (unique per tenant + normalised name), entity reference (product:type:id), entity_tag mapping.
  • Two indexes, one per direction: PK (tenant, entity, tag) and (tenant, tag, tagged_at, entity).
  • REST with idempotent PUT/DELETE, cursor pagination, batch get; tenant from the token.
  • Shard by tenant through a directory; giant tenants get their own shard.
  • Concurrency via unique constraints and upserts, not locks.
  • Heavy writes: async bulk jobs, batching, per-tenant quotas; counts maintained from events.
  • Trending: per-tenant hourly Redis sorted sets, unioned with decaying weights.
  • Outbox to Kafka keeps search, counts and trending in sync; products filter by permission.
