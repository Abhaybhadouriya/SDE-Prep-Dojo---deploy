A security checklist for any design answer

  1. Identity. Users log in via an IdP with OIDC (or SAML for enterprise SSO), MFA for sensitive actions, passwords hashed with Argon2id.
  2. Tokens. Short-lived access JWT validated at the gateway and in services; rotated refresh token server-side; HttpOnly Secure SameSite cookies on the web.
  3. Authorization. RBAC at the gateway, object-level ownership checks in each service, deny by default.
  4. Transport. TLS at the edge, mTLS between services with automatically rotated workload certificates.
  5. Data. Encryption at rest with KMS and envelope keys; field-level encryption or tokenization for card and ID numbers; keep PII out of logs.
  6. Secrets. Central vault, short-lived dynamic credentials, nothing in code or images.
  7. Abuse. Rate limits and quotas, WAF, bot protection for business flows, CDN and anycast for DDoS.
  8. Audit. Immutable audit logs of who accessed what, alerts on anomalies.
