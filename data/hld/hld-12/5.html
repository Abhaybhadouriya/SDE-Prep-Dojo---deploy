Batch vs stream processing

BatchStream
InputBounded: "all of yesterday"Unbounded: events forever
LatencyMinutes to hoursMilliseconds to seconds
CorrectnessEasy: all data present, rerun anytimeHard: late and out-of-order events, state, exactly-once
ToolsSpark, Hive, Trino, warehouse SQL, MapReduceFlink, Kafka Streams, Spark Structured Streaming, Samza
Use forBilling, reports, ML training, backfillsFraud, surge pricing, alerting, live dashboards, trending

Ask first: do we really need real time? Hello Interview's Flink deep dive makes this point: many "streaming" problems are fine as a batch job every few minutes, and a simple Kafka consumer is enough for stateless transforms. Reach for Flink when you need state (counts per user per 5 minutes) that must survive crashes, rescaling and out-of-order events.
