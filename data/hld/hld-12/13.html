The 60-second answer. "For analytics I keep OLTP and OLAP apart: CDC from Postgres into Kafka, then Parquet files in a lakehouse for batch, and a Flink job only for the numbers that must be fresh. The Flink job keys by entity, counts in event-time windows, and uses a watermark of max timestamp minus a delay I pick from the measured lateness. Late events go to a side output, and a nightly batch job reconciles anything that touches money. Checkpoints plus a transactional or idempotent sink give me effectively-once results. If I only need distinct counts or top-K, I use HyperLogLog or a count-min sketch plus a heap instead of exact sets."
