Windows: tumbling, sliding, session

A stream never ends, so "count clicks" only makes sense inside a window, a slice of time per key.

Worked example. A click happens at 12:03:40. With 1-minute tumbling windows it belongs to exactly one window, 12:03-12:04. With 5-minute sliding windows that start every minute, it belongs to five windows: 11:59-12:04, 12:00-12:05, 12:01-12:06, 12:02-12:07 and 12:03-12:08 (size / slide = 5 / 1 = 5). With a 30-minute session window, it joins the user's current session if their previous click was after 11:33:40; otherwise it starts a new session.
