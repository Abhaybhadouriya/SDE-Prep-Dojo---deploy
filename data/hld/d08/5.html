Architecture: one Place Order click

Step through what happens after the customer clicks Place your order. The checkout orchestrator runs a saga: a sequence of local transactions, each with an undo step.

  1. Claim the key. The orchestrator inserts a PENDING order row with (customer_id, idempotency_key) UNIQUE and a hash of the request. If the insert conflicts, this is a retry: return the saved response (or "still processing") and stop.
  2. Check the price. Pricing confirms that quote q_9 has not expired and the coupon is still redeemable for this user. If anything changed, end with 409 PRICE_CHANGED. Nothing has been reserved yet, so there is nothing to undo.
  3. Reserve stock. For each line, a conditional update in the inventory service: reserved = reserved + qty WHERE on_hand - reserved >= qty, plus a reservation row that expires in 15 minutes. Its undo step is "release the reservation".
  4. Authorise the payment. The payment service asks the provider to authorise (hold) the amount, using the order id as the provider-side idempotency key. Money is not captured yet. Its undo step is "void the authorisation".
  5. If payment is declined: run the compensations in reverse order (release the reservations), mark the order PAYMENT_FAILED, and tell the customer to try another method. Nothing is half-done.
  6. Commit the order. In one local transaction: order status = CONFIRMED, store the response for the idempotency key, and insert an order.confirmed outbox row. This is the pivot: after it, the saga only moves forward.
  7. Fan out. A relay (a poller or Debezium CDC) publishes the outbox row to Kafka, keyed by orderId. Fulfilment picks a warehouse, email sends the confirmation, analytics and recommendations update. Each consumer is idempotent.
  8. At shipment: the warehouse ships, the payment is captured, the reservation becomes CONFIRMED and on_hand goes down. Capturing at shipment means a cancelled order only needs a void, not a refund.

The whiteboard version: Client -> Gateway -> Checkout orchestrator (+ Orders DB with key) -> [Pricing, Inventory, Payment] -> outbox -> Kafka -> Fulfilment/Email. Six boxes and three side calls.
