  • The orchestrator crashes after reserving. Saga state says "step 2 done". A new instance resumes at step 3. If it never resumes, the reservation TTL frees the stock anyway: a safety net under the safety net.
  • Inventory service is down. Checkout fails fast with "try again in a moment", and the cart is untouched. A circuit breaker stops thousands of threads waiting on timeouts.
  • Payment provider is slow. Per-provider timeouts, a circuit breaker, and routing new payments to a second provider. In-flight ones go to UNKNOWN and are reconciled.
  • Outbox relay lags. Orders are safe, but emails and warehouse picks are late. Alert on outbox age (now minus the oldest unpublished row).
  • A shard of the orders DB fails over. Promote a synchronous replica. Customers on that shard see errors for seconds. Others are unaffected because shards are independent.
  • Price changed mid-checkout. The quote check returns 409, and the client shows the new price. Never silently charge a different amount.
