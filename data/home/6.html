How to use this site

▶️

Click the steppers

Diagrams with Prev / Play / Next buttons build up one step at a time. Read the highlighted caption at each step.

🔵

Watch the dots

Moving glowing dots are messages or requests travelling through the system. Follow one with your eyes.

✅

Take the quizzes

Every page ends with a short quiz. Click an answer to see if it is right and why.

🏅

Mark pages done

Press "Mark as done" at the bottom of a page. The progress bar in the top bar and ticks in the menu remember it in your browser.

🔎

Search anything

Use the search box at the top to jump to any page by name.

🌙

Dark mode

Use the moon / sun button in the top bar. The site also follows your system setting.

🎯

Practise out loud

Open interview questions, try your own answer first, then compare with "How to answer".

🎬

Videos need a local server

YouTube embeds may refuse to play from a file:// page. Run serve.ps1 (or serve.bat) in this folder and open the address it prints.

PS F:\Surbhi\devops> powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\serve.ps1

Tip: do the pipeline project with your hands, not just your eyes. Typing the commands yourself is where Docker and Kafka really click.
