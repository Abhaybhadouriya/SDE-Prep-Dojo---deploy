Preview: the life of one order

This is the kind of system you will build in the pipeline project and draw in interviews. Click Next (or Play) to follow one order.

  1. The shopper's app calls order-api, a container running in a Kubernetes pod. Kubernetes keeps enough copies alive.
  2. order-api writes one "OrderPlaced" event to a Kafka topic and answers the shopper right away. It does not wait for anyone else.
  3. Payments, inventory and notifications each read the same event on their own, at their own speed. Adding a fourth reader needs no change to order-api.
  4. If one reader crashes, nothing is lost: its events wait in Kafka and it continues from its last offset when it comes back. This is why big shops use event streaming.
