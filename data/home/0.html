The whole site in one story

Explain like I'm 5

Imagine a giant toy factory. Each toy-maker works inside a lunch box that has everything they need, so they work the same in any room (that is Docker). A robot captain decides which room each lunch box goes to, and if one breaks it puts a new one in (that is Kubernetes). When a toy is ready, the makers shout it into a giant notebook that never forgets; painters, packers and the shop all read the notebook at their own speed (that is Kafka). And the plan of the whole factory - how many rooms, roads and notebooks you need for a million toys a day - is System Design. And the job interview where you explain all of it out loud is what the Interview Hub trains you for.

That is genuinely the whole idea. Every page here starts with a story like this, then adds the real names, then the internals, then the interview questions. You never have to jump in at the deep end.
