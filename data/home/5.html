From your world

Docker, Kubernetes and Kafka are not magic. They are packaging, orchestration and logging built on the same Linux kernel features and networking tricks you have used for years.

Where this breaks: these tools run across many machines over an unreliable network, so failure detection and agreeing on one truth (consistency) are harder than inside one chassis with a shared backplane. That gap is exactly what the Kafka and system design tracks teach.
