Your learning path

Interview soon and system design is your weak spot? Jump straight to step 5. Otherwise go in this order: each step uses the one before it - Kubernetes runs Docker containers, the pipeline runs Kafka on Kubernetes, and system design uses all three. Low-level design (step 6) pairs with system design: do it after HLD, or alongside it if your loop has a machine-coding round. Coding / DSA (step 7) runs alongside everything from day 1: a lesson and a few problems a day, with the revision queue bringing solved problems back.

  1. 🐳

    Docker step 1

    Pack any app into a container that runs the same everywhere.

  2. ☸️

    Kubernetes step 2

    Run, heal and scale thousands of containers across many machines.

  3. 📨

    Kafka step 3

    Stream events between services with a durable, replayable log.

  4. 🛠️

    Pipeline project step 4

    Build ShopStream: a real event pipeline, locally then on Kubernetes.

  5. 🏗️

    System design step 5

    Fundamentals, building blocks, 10 reusable patterns, then 75 real-world designs.

  6. 🧠

    Low-level design step 6

    OOP from C structs, SOLID, patterns, UML, concurrency, then 45 machine-coding builds that run.

  7. 💻

    Coding / DSA step 7

    Patterns first, then the most-asked interview problems by topic, list and company, with spaced revision.

  8. 🎯

    Interview step 8

    Recently asked HLD questions, company guides, question bank, scenario drills and cheat sheets.
