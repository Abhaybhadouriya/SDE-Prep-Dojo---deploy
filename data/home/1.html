The 5-level learning ladder

Every page shows a coloured level badge. Inside a page, coloured dividers tell you when the level goes up. If something feels hard, scroll back to the previous colour.

🧒

Level 1 - Kid

A story and an analogy. No jargon at all. A 1st grader could follow it.

🌱

Level 2 - Beginner

The real names of things, your first commands, small pictures.

🔧

Level 3 - Intermediate

How it works inside: configs, flows, and the trade-offs between choices.

🚀

Level 4 - Advanced

Internals, failure modes, tuning and what really happens in production.

🎯

Level 5 - Interview-ready

Real questions, production scenarios, the best way to answer, and the follow-ups to expect.
