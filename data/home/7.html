Three questions to make sure the path makes sense before you start. Click an answer to see why.

You have never run a container. Which page should you open first?

  1. Docker: What is a container?
  2. Kafka: Core concepts
  3. A real-world design such as WhatsApp
Kubernetes runs containers, the pipeline runs Kafka on Kubernetes, and designs use all of them. Containers are the first brick, so start with Docker.

  1. Read every page in order and skip practice

  3. Follow the study plan's short variant: HLD fundamentals, patterns and a few designs, plus daily out-loud practice
Reading alone does not prepare you to talk for 45 minutes. The study plan mixes fundamentals, patterns, designs and speaking practice so you can explain trade-offs out loud.

Which idea from your networking background maps to Kafka consumer lag?

  1. A VLAN tag
  2. Queue depth: how far the reader is behind the writer
  3. A MAC address table
Lag is the gap between the newest offset written and the offset a consumer has processed, just like the depth of a queue that a slow interface has not drained yet.
