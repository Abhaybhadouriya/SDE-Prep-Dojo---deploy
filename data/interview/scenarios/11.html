Common mistakes in scenario rounds

  • Jumping to the fix. Saying "restart it" in the first ten seconds. Always gather one piece of evidence first.
  • Forgetting the user. Debugging for ten minutes while customers fail. Mitigate first.
  • Destroying evidence. Deleting the pod before saving logs --previous, restarting a broker before a thread dump.
  • Saying "exactly-once" as a magic word. Kafka transactions do not cover emails, payments or databases outside Kafka. Say "at-least-once plus idempotent consumers".
  • "Add more consumers / pods / RAM" with no reasoning. Explain why more will help, and its limit (partition count, DB connections, cost).
  • Changing many things at once. Then nobody knows what fixed it. One change, watch the metric, next change.
  • Over-explaining internals. Your strength, but keep it to one or two sentences unless the interviewer asks for more. The learner input for this site called this out directly.
  • No prevention. Ending without an alert, test, limit or postmortem. This is the cheapest point to win.
