Write the postmortem (practice template)

After each drill, try writing a five-minute postmortem. It trains the "prevent" step and gives you material for behavioural rounds. Here is a short template in Markdown.

# Postmortem: order emails delayed 40 minutes (2026-11-20)
Severity: SEV-2      Duration: 12:02 - 12:51 IST      Author: you
## Impact
~180k customers got order confirmation emails 10-40 min late. No orders lost.
## Timeline
12:00 sale starts, traffic 6x normal
12:02 alert: email-sender lag > 100k and rising
12:09 lag found on partition 1 only (hot seller key)
12:15 mitigation: hot seller moved to dedicated topic + consumer
12:51 lag back to normal
## Root cause
Partition key = sellerId. One seller had most of the sale traffic.
## What went well / badly / lucky
+ alert fired early   - no runbook for key skew   ~ email is not critical
## Action items
1. Key by orderId for email events (owner: A, due: 12-01)
2. Alert on per-partition lag skew (owner: B, due: 11-28)
3. Add key-skew test to pre-sale load test (owner: C, due: 12-05)
