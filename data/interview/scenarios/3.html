How to practise these drills

Reading a drill is not the same as answering one. Use this routine; it takes about 10 minutes per drill.

  1. Read only the situation. Cover the answers. Set a 5-minute timer.
  2. Answer out loud using the six moves. Say the commands you would run, by name. Record yourself on your phone if you can.
  3. Open the weak answer. Did you say anything like it? The weak answers are real patterns: jumping to a fix, guessing, restarting without evidence.
  4. Compare with the strong answer. Write down the one thing you missed. Usually it is the mitigation step or the prevention step.
  5. Answer the follow-ups in one or two sentences each. Interviewers use these to find the edge of your knowledge.
  6. Try it for real where you can. Many Docker, Kubernetes and Kafka drills can be reproduced on your laptop with the local pipeline build and the failure drills page.
