Remember this

  • Every answer: detect, scope, mitigate, diagnose, fix, prevent. Say the words.
  • First question: "what changed, and who is affected?"
  • Walk the layers from the user down to the disk; prove each one healthy before moving on.
  • Exit 137 = SIGKILL (usually OOM); 143 = SIGTERM; exactly 10 s stops = SIGTERM ignored (PID 1).
  • Pending = scheduler; CrashLoopBackOff = process exits (logs --previous); not Ready = probe.
  • Kafka lag: check per partition. Even = too slow; one hot = key skew. Consumers beyond partitions are idle.
  • Durability recipe: RF 3, min ISR 2, acks=all, idempotence, no unclean election.
  • At-least-once + idempotent handlers. Exactly-once is Kafka-to-Kafka only.
  • Dual write → outbox. Retry storm → timeouts, backoff + jitter, circuit breaker, bulkheads.
  • Close with an alert, a guardrail and a blameless postmortem.
