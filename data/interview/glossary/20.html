QoS class (pods)
Kubernetes		Gold, silver and bronze tickets that decide who leaves first when the room is full.Kubernetes sets a pod's class from its requests and limits: Guaranteed (requests = limits for all containers), Burstable (some requests), BestEffort (none). QPS / RPS
HLD must know		How many questions the shop answers every second.Queries (or requests) per second, the basic load number. Rule of thumb: 1 million requests a day is about 12 per second on average; peaks are often several times the average. From your world: Packets per second, but for whole requests across many servers.Back-of-Envelope Estimation
Quadtree
HLD		Cutting a map into 4 squares, and cutting any square with too many houses into 4 again.A tree where each node covers a square and splits into four children when it holds too many points. Depth follows density, so a city is deep and an ocean is shallow. Often kept in memory for nearest-K search. From your world: Like a radix trie that only splits where prefixes are crowded.Pattern: Geo & Proximity Queries
Quorum
HLD must know		More than half the class must agree before a rule is changed.The minimum number of nodes that must take part for an operation to count, usually a majority (n/2 + 1). With N replicas, read quorum R and write quorum W where R + W > N guarantee reads see the latest write. From your world: Split-brain protection in HA clusters.Replication, Consensus & Coordination
