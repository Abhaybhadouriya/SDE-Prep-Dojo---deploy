Machine coding round
Interview		You get one or two hours to build a small working toy, and it must really work, not just look nice.A 60-120 minute round where you write working, modular code for a small system (a parking lot, a snake-and-ladder game, an in-memory key-value store), usually on your own laptop with no database. Merge queue

Message queue

Microservice

min.insync.replicas
Kafka must know		"At least two helpers must have copied my note, or I will not accept it."The minimum ISR size for an acks=all write to succeed. With RF=3 and min.insync.replicas=2, the topic survives one broker loss with no data loss; if the ISR drops below 2, writes fail with NotEnoughReplicas instead of risking loss. From your world: A quorum requirement before committing.Replication & ISR
