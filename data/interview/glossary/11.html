HCL (HashiCorp Configuration Language)
IaC		The simple form you fill in to describe what you want built.The declarative language Terraform files are written in: blocks (resource, variable, module), arguments, expressions, and references such as aws_vpc.main.id, which also create the dependency graph.Terraform Basics
Headless Service
Kubernetes		A phone book that gives you every helper's own number instead of one shared number.A Service with clusterIP: None. DNS returns the pod IPs directly instead of a virtual IP. StatefulSets use it to give each pod a stable name like kafka-0.kafka.Services & Cluster Networking
Health check
Docker		Asking "are you okay?" every few seconds.A regular test of whether a service works, such as an HTTP /healthz call. Docker has HEALTHCHECK; Kubernetes has liveness, readiness and startup probes; load balancers drop unhealthy targets. From your world: Like BFD or keepalives.Running & Managing Containers
Heartbeat and session timeout
Kafka		Waving to the teacher every few seconds so they know you are still there.Periodic "I am alive" messages. A Kafka consumer that sends none within session.timeout.ms (45 s default) is removed from the group, triggering a rebalance. Separately, taking longer than max.poll.interval.ms (5 min) between polls also gets it kicked out. From your world: BFD or hello timers with a dead interval.Consumers & Consumer Groups
Helm
Kubernetes		A recipe box of ready-made toy sets you can build with one command and your own colours.The package manager for Kubernetes. A chart is a set of templated YAML files plus default values; helm install renders them with your values and tracks releases for upgrade and rollback.StatefulSets, CRDs & Operators
High watermark (HW)
Kafka		The line in the diary up to which every helper has a copy, so it is safe to read.The highest offset that all in-sync replicas have stored. Consumers can only read up to it, so they never see a record that could still be lost in a leader failover. From your world: The "committed" index in a replicated ring.Replication & ISR
Hilbert curve
HLD		A snake path that visits every square of a grid without jumping far, so squares next to each other get numbers close together.A space-filling curve that numbers the cells of a 2-D grid so that cells close in the number order are close on the map. S2 uses it so a range of cell ids is a compact area, which turns "nearby" into a range scan. From your world: Like interleaving the bits of x and y, so a longest-prefix match keeps neighbours together.Pattern: Geo & Proximity Queries
Hinted handoff
HLD		If your friend is out, you keep their letter and give it to them when they come back.In leaderless stores (Dynamo, Cassandra), when a replica is down another node accepts the write with a "hint" and forwards it once the replica returns. Keeps writes available; needs anti-entropy repair as a backstop.Distributed Key-Value Store (Dynamo)
HLD (high-level design)
HLD		Drawing the big map of the town before deciding where each brick goes.The system design interview: requirements, estimates, API, data model, a diagram of the main components, deep dives on hard parts, and trade-offs. LLD (low-level design) is classes and code structure.The 45-Minute Interview Framework
HLS / DASH
HLD		Cutting a long film into short pieces and giving the player a list of the pieces.Adaptive video streaming formats: the video is split into short segment files (typically 2-10 s) at several bitrates, plus a playlist (manifest) file. The player fetches segments over plain HTTP from a CDN and switches bitrate as bandwidth changes. From your world: Like fragmenting a big payload into pieces the path can carry.Pattern: Handling Large Blobs
HM (hiring manager) round
Interview		Meeting the teacher whose class you would join.A round with the manager of the team that is hiring. Often mixes your past projects, behavioural questions and sometimes a design question; at some companies the HM round decides the level.Interview Hub
HNSW (hierarchical navigable small world)
HLD		Finding a friend in a city by asking a well-connected person first, then their neighbours, then neighbours of neighbours.A graph index for approximate nearest-neighbour search. Vectors are nodes linked to close neighbours on several layers; a search starts on the sparse top layer and greedily walks down to the dense bottom layer. Fast and accurate, but memory-hungry; used by most vector databases.Recommendation Algorithms
Horizontal scaling (scale out)
HLD must know		More cooks in more kitchens instead of one giant cook.Adding more machines and spreading the load across them. Needs stateless services or partitioned data, plus a load balancer. Vertical scaling (a bigger machine) is simpler but has a ceiling. From your world: Adding line cards and ECMP members rather than a faster single ASIC.Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP
Hot key / hot partition
HLD must know		Everyone queues at one ice-cream stall while the others are empty.One key, shard or partition getting far more traffic than others (a celebrity, a flash-sale item), so it becomes the bottleneck no matter how many nodes you add. Fixes: split or salt the key, cache it, or special-case it. From your world: An elephant flow pinned to one ECMP link.Pattern: Scaling Writes
HPA (Horizontal Pod Autoscaler)
Kubernetes must know		A helper who adds more cooks when the queue grows and sends some home when it shrinks.A controller that changes a Deployment's replica count from metrics. Roughly: desired = ceil(current * currentMetric / target), checked every 15 s by default, with a scale-down stabilisation window. Needs metrics-server or a custom metrics adapter. From your world: Adding ECMP paths when link utilisation crosses a threshold.Scheduling & Autoscaling
HyperLogLog (HLL)
HLD		Guessing how many different children visited by remembering only the longest run of lucky coin flips.A probabilistic structure that estimates how many distinct items a stream has (cardinality) in a few KB of memory with about 1% error; Redis exposes it as PFADD / PFCOUNT. HLLs from different servers can be merged. From your world: Counting unique flows without keeping a full flow table.Pattern: Counting, Ranking & Windowed Aggregation
Hypervisor
Docker		A grown-up who gives each child a whole pretend house, each with its own kitchen.Software (KVM, Hyper-V, ESXi, Firecracker) that runs virtual machines, each with its own guest kernel on virtual hardware. Containers do not need one; they share the host kernel. From your world: KVM, which you already know.What is a Container?
