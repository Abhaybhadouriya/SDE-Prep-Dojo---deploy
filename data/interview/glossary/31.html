Remember this

  • Most new words are old kernel and networking ideas renamed: namespaces and cgroups, VIPs and NAT, ECMP hashing, queue depth, flow control.
  • Learn the green "must know" words first; they appear in almost every interview without being explained.
  • Always say a word's precise meaning with the real process or setting name, such as kubelet, kube-proxy, acks=all or min.insync.replicas.
  • Watch the look-alike pairs: two namespaces, two watermarks, two partitions, two controllers.
  • When a word confuses you on another page, come back here, read one row, and go back.
