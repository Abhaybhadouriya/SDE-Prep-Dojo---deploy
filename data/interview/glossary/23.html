State locking (Terraform)
IaC		A "someone is writing" sign on the shared notebook so two people do not scribble over each other.A lock taken on the Terraform state before plan or apply so two runs cannot change it at once. The S3 backend locks with a lock file (use_lockfile) or, in older setups, a DynamoDB table; a stuck lock is cleared with terraform force-unlock only after checking no run is active.Terraform State

HLD		A "best before" date on a note.How long a cached entry or DNS record stays valid before it must be refreshed or deleted. Short TTLs mean fresher data and more load; long TTLs the opposite. From your world: The IP TTL idea applied to time instead of hops.Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP
