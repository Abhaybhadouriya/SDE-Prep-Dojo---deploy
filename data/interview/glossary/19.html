p99 latency
HLD must know		How long the slowest few children out of a hundred took to finish the race.The time under which 99% of requests finish; the slowest 1% are slower. Shows tail latency that an average hides. With fan-out to many services, tail latency dominates the user's wait. From your world: Like worst-case jitter rather than average delay.Back-of-Envelope Estimation
PACELC
HLD		Even on a normal sunny day, you must choose between being fast and being perfectly up to date.An extension of CAP: if there is a Partition, choose Availability or Consistency; Else, in normal operation, choose Latency or Consistency. Explains why systems like Dynamo pick low latency even without failures.Fundamentals: Scale, Cache, DB, CAP
Page cache
Kafka		Keeping recent pages of the diary on the desk instead of fetching them from the cupboard.Memory the OS uses to cache file data. Kafka writes into it and serves recent reads from it rather than keeping its own heap cache, which is a big reason it is fast. Leave plenty of RAM free on brokers. From your world: The kernel page cache, used on purpose.Storage Internals & Performance Secrets
Pagination (cursor vs offset)
HLD		Reading a long list one page at a time, with a bookmark.Returning large result sets in pages. Offset pagination (?page=5) is simple but slow and unstable for big or changing data; cursor (keyset) pagination returns an opaque "next" token and stays fast.API Design & Client-Server Communication
Partition assignor
Kafka		The person who decides which child reads which pile of letters.The algorithm that decides which consumer in a group owns which partitions: range, round-robin, sticky and cooperative-sticky on the classic protocol; with the new KIP-848 protocol the broker runs server-side assignors (uniform, range). From your world: Like the bucket-to-member mapping in an ECMP group.Consumers & Consumer Groups
Partition (Kafka)
Kafka must know		One pile of the topic's letters, kept in strict order.An ordered, append-only log that is the unit of parallelism, ordering and replication in Kafka. A topic is split into partitions spread over brokers; ordering is guaranteed only inside one partition. Not the same as a network partition in CAP. From your world: One ECMP member link: order is kept per flow, not across links.Core Concepts: Topics, Partitions, Offsets
Partitioner (key hashing)
Kafka		A rule that sends all letters with the same name on them to the same pile.For a record with a key, the default Java partitioner picks murmur2(key) % numPartitions, so the same key always lands in the same partition and stays in order. Adding partitions changes the mapping. From your world: ECMP flow hashing: the 5-tuple hash keeps a flow on one path and in order.Producers
PDB (PodDisruptionBudget)
Kubernetes		"At least two lifeguards must stay at the pool while others take breaks."A rule limiting how many pods of an app can be voluntarily evicted at once (for example minAvailable: 2), respected by node drains and upgrades. It does not protect against crashes.Deployments, ReplicaSets & Rollouts
Pessimistic locking
HLD		You hold the crayon box the whole time you draw so nobody else can take a crayon.Taking a lock before reading data you plan to change (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE), so others wait. Safe under heavy conflict, but slower and can deadlock. From your world: A mutex around a shared table.Pattern: Dealing with Contention
PID 1 in a container
Docker		The class captain who must pass on messages and tidy up after others.The first process in a container's pid namespace. The kernel gives it no default signal handlers and expects it to reap zombie children. Use the exec form of CMD, handle SIGTERM, or add a tiny init (docker run --init, tini). From your world: Exactly the init process job you know.Running & Managing Containers
PID controller
HLD		A thermostat that turns the heater up or down by how far the room is from the right temperature.A feedback controller that adjusts an output from the error between a measured value and its target, using proportional, integral and derivative terms. In a virtual waiting room it raises or lowers the admit rate based on how far checkout latency is from its target. From your world: The same loop as a fan-speed or rate-shaper controller.Pattern: Dealing with Contention
Pod
Kubernetes must know		A little pod of peas: one or a few containers that always travel together.The smallest thing Kubernetes schedules: one or more containers that share a network namespace (one IP), can share volumes and always run on the same node. Pods are disposable; controllers replace them. From your world: Processes sharing one net namespace, like daemons bound to the same loopback.Pods
Pod Security Standards / Admission
Kubernetes		Three safety levels for toys: anything goes, basic safety, and very strict.Three policy levels (Privileged, Baseline, Restricted) that the built-in Pod Security Admission enforces per namespace via labels. Replaced PodSecurityPolicy, which was removed in 1.25.Security & RBAC
Poll loop
Kafka		The reader keeps asking "any new letters for me?"A consumer repeatedly calls poll() to fetch the next batch from its assigned partitions (a long poll on the broker). It must call poll often enough (within max.poll.interval.ms) to stay in the group. From your world: NAPI polling.Consumers & Consumer Groups
Polymorphism
LLD must know		Say 'speak' and the dog barks, the cat meows.One call, many behaviours: the method that runs depends on the object's runtime type (overriding, via a vtable) or on argument types at compile time (overloading, templates, generics). From your world: Calling dev->ops->xmit() without knowing which driver is behind it.Polymorphism & vtables
PoP (point of presence)
HLD		A small shop in your town that keeps copies of popular toys.One CDN site: a rack of edge caches in a city, close to users. Viewers are routed to the nearest PoP, often with anycast or DNS.Live Streaming
Port publishing (-p)
Docker		Making a hole in the fence so visitors can reach the container's door.docker run -p 8080:80 maps host port 8080 to container port 80 by adding DNAT rules (and a userland proxy for some cases). By default it binds on all host addresses, which can bypass host firewalls like ufw. From your world: An iptables DNAT rule; check with iptables -t nat -L DOCKER.Docker Networking
Postmortem (blameless)
HLD		After the sandcastle falls, everyone sits down and asks "what made it fall?", not "whose fault was it?".A written review after an incident: timeline, user impact, root cause, contributing factors and action items with owners. "Blameless" means it looks at how the system and process allowed the mistake, not who to punish, so people report problems honestly. Interviewers ask for one in behavioural rounds ("tell me about an outage") and expect concrete action items. From your world: The RFO (reason for outage) report after a network incident.Production Scenario Drills
Presigned URL
HLD		A one-time pass that lets you drop your parcel straight into the warehouse.A time-limited, signed URL to object storage (S3) that lets a client upload or download one object directly, so big files skip your application servers.Pattern: Handling Large Blobs
PriorityClass and preemption
Kubernetes		Important guests get seats first, and can ask less important ones to move.A cluster object giving pods a priority number. If a high-priority pod cannot fit, the scheduler may evict (preempt) lower-priority pods to make room. From your world: Like QoS priority queues.Scheduling & Autoscaling
Producer
Kafka		The person who writes letters and drops them at the post office.A client that sends records to Kafka topics. It serializes, picks a partition (by key hash or sticky for null keys), batches per partition, compresses, sends to the partition leader and retries according to its settings. From your world: The DMA producer side of a ring.Producers
Producer-consumer
LLD must know		One kid puts cookies on a tray, another eats them; the tray has room for only ten.A concurrency pattern where producers put work into a bounded buffer and consumers take it, blocking when full or empty (BlockingQueue, condition variables). From your world: A NIC ring buffer between the IRQ handler and the softirq.Producer-Consumer & Blocking Queues
Prometheus
Kubernetes		A helper who visits every machine every few seconds and writes down its numbers.A monitoring system that scrapes (pulls) metrics from HTTP endpoints, stores them as time series with labels and evaluates PromQL queries and alert rules. From your world: SNMP polling, but with labelled time series.Observability & Troubleshooting
Protocol Buffers (Protobuf)
Kafka		A tiny agreed code where each field has a number instead of a long name.Google's binary serialization format defined by .proto schemas with numbered fields. Compact and fast, with rules for evolving schemas safely. Used by gRPC and often with Schema Registry. From your world: Like TLV with type codes.Schemas & Schema Registry
Proxy bidding
HLD		You tell a helper your top price, and the helper raises your bid only as much as needed.Auction feature where a bidder sets a maximum and the system bids on their behalf in small increments up to that maximum. Two proxy bids are resolved instantly, so the server must apply them in a strict per-auction order.Online Auction
Proxy (pattern)
LLD		A receptionist who answers for the boss and only lets some calls through.A structural pattern: a stand-in with the same interface controls access to the real object (lazy loading, access checks, caching, remote calls). From your world: A stub that forwards calls to a remote or lazily loaded module.Proxy
Publish-subscribe (pub/sub)
Kafka		Shouting news on the school radio: everyone tuned in hears it.A messaging style where publishers send to a topic without knowing the receivers, and every subscriber (in Kafka, every consumer group) gets its own copy of each message. From your world: Multicast groups.Why Messaging? (Sync vs Async)
PV and PVC
Kubernetes		A PVC is a note saying "I need a 10 GB locker"; a PV is the actual locker given to you.A PersistentVolumeClaim is a pod-side request for storage (size, access mode, class). A PersistentVolume is the real piece of storage bound to it, often created on demand by a StorageClass through a CSI driver. The data outlives the pod.Storage: PV, PVC, StorageClass
