The words on one journey

Words stick better when you see where they live. Here is one click on a shop website, travelling through the systems this site teaches. Every box is a glossary word; click a box to jump to its row. The moving dot is the request, then the event it creates.

Now the same journey slowly, one handful of words at a time. Press Next or Play.

  1. Build. Dockerfile, layer, image, registry, container.
  2. Run. pod, node, kube-scheduler, kubelet, CRI.
  3. Reach. Service, ClusterIP, kube-proxy, EndpointSlice, CoreDNS.
  4. Produce. producer, key, partition, acks, ISR.
  5. Consume. consumer group, poll, offset, lag, rebalance.
  6. Store. at-least-once, idempotent, upsert, index, cache-aside.
