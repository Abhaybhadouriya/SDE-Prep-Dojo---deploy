Zero-copy (sendfile)
Kafka must know		Passing the book straight from the shelf to the reader without copying it by hand.Kafka sends data from the page cache to the network socket with the sendfile() system call, so the bytes never pass through user space. Saves CPU and memory bandwidth. Does not apply when TLS is on. From your world: DMA and zero-copy paths: page cache to NIC without a user-space copy.Storage Internals & Performance Secrets
Zombie (instance)
Kafka		A player everyone thought had gone home, who suddenly comes back and kicks the ball.An old copy of a worker that everyone thinks is dead (it paused or lost the network) but which wakes up and tries to keep writing. Fencing stops it. From your world: A stale router that comes back after a long pause and re-advertises old routes.Delivery Semantics & Exactly-Once
ZooKeeper
Kafka		The old separate notebook Kafka used to borrow to remember who is in charge.A coordination service (ZAB consensus) that older Kafka versions used for metadata and controller election. Removed in Kafka 4.0 in favour of KRaft. Still used by other systems for locks and configuration.KRaft, Controllers & Cluster Metadata
