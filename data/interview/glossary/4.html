AA round (Microsoft "As Appropriate")
Interview		The last chat with the head teacher, who only joins if the other teachers liked you.The final round in many Microsoft loops, with a senior manager or director. It mixes a deep dive into your past projects (architecture, trade-offs) with behavioural questions on growth mindset and collaboration.Microsoft Guide
Abstract class
LLD		A recipe with some steps written and some left blank for each cook to fill in.A class that cannot be instantiated and may mix implemented methods, state and abstract methods that subclasses must implement. Use it when related classes share code and state; use an interface when unrelated classes share only a contract. From your world: A struct with a half-filled ops table plus common helper functions.Abstract Classes vs Interfaces
Abstract Factory
LLD		A shop that sells matching furniture sets: pick a style and every piece matches.A creational pattern: one factory interface creates a family of related objects (button + checkbox for one OS theme), so client code never mixes products from different families. From your world: Picking one NIC driver's whole set of ops tables at probe time.Abstract Factory
Access modes (RWO, ROX, RWX, RWOP)
Kubernetes		Rules for who may use a shared toy box: one child, many children looking, or many children playing.How a PersistentVolume may be mounted: ReadWriteOnce (one node read-write), ReadOnlyMany, ReadWriteMany, and ReadWriteOncePod (exactly one pod). Most cloud block disks support only RWO. From your world: Like a disk that can be attached to one host vs an NFS export many hosts mount.Storage: PV, PVC, StorageClass
ACID
HLD must know		A promise that a money move happens all the way or not at all, even if the power goes out.Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability: the guarantees of a classic database transaction. Atomic = all or nothing; Isolated = concurrent transactions do not see each other's half-done work; Durable = once committed it survives a crash.Choosing Storage: DB Types & Key Technologies
acks (producer setting)
Kafka must know		How many friends must say "I saved your note" before the writer feels safe.How many broker copies must store a record before the producer is told "done". acks=0: do not wait. acks=1: the leader only. acks=all (default since Kafka 3.0): every replica currently in the ISR. From your world: Like waiting for an ACK from every member of a redundancy group, not just the first hop.Producers
Adapter (pattern)
LLD		A travel plug that lets your charger fit a foreign socket.A structural pattern that wraps an object with an incompatible interface so it can be used where another interface is expected. The adaptee is not changed. From your world: A shim that turns a vendor SDK's callbacks into your driver's ops table.Adapter
Admission controller
Kubernetes		A guard at the door who checks, and sometimes fixes, every request before it is written down.A plug-in inside the API server that runs after authentication and authorization but before the object is stored in etcd. Mutating admission can change the object (add defaults, inject sidecars); validating admission can reject it (Pod Security Admission, policy engines). From your world: Like an ingress ACL plus a policy map that can also rewrite fields.Security & RBAC
Affinity and anti-affinity
Kubernetes		"Sit next to your friend" or "do not sit next to your twin" rules for seating pods.Scheduler rules. Node affinity says which nodes a pod prefers or requires (by node labels). Pod affinity places pods near other pods; pod anti-affinity spreads them apart, for example one replica per zone. Each rule is either required (hard) or preferred (soft).Scheduling & Autoscaling
Aggregation vs composition (UML)
LLD must know		Aggregation: a team has players who can leave and join another team. Composition: a house has rooms that vanish with the house.Both are 'has-a'. In aggregation (hollow diamond) the part can outlive the whole and be shared. In composition (filled diamond) the whole owns the part's lifetime; deleting the whole deletes the parts. From your world: Composition = a struct embedded by value and freed with its parent; aggregation = a pointer to a refcounted object someone else also holds.Class Relationships
ALS (alternating least squares)
HLD		Fix the people and solve the games; then fix the games and solve the people; repeat.A matrix-factorization trainer that holds the item factors fixed and solves every user's small least-squares problem exactly (in parallel), then swaps sides. It parallelises well (Spark MLlib) and handles implicit feedback with confidence weights.Recommendation Algorithms
Anemic domain model
LLD		Toys that cannot do anything by themselves; a grown-up has to move every piece for them.An anti-pattern where entity classes are only fields with getters and setters and all behaviour lives in 'service' classes. It breaks encapsulation: invariants are enforced (or forgotten) far from the data. From your world: A struct whose fields every module pokes directly, with no accessor that checks locks or bounds.Anti-Patterns
Anti-sniping (auction extension)
HLD		If someone bids in the last few seconds, the clock gets a little more time so others can answer.A rule that extends an auction's end time (for example by 2 minutes) whenever a bid arrives close to the end, so a last-second bid cannot win unanswered. It turns closing time into a moving deadline that the closing job must re-check.Online Auction
Anycast
HLD		Many shops share one address, and you are sent to the nearest one.One IP address announced from many locations with BGP; routers deliver each packet to the topologically closest site. CDNs and public DNS resolvers use it to cut latency and absorb attacks. From your world: Exactly the BGP anycast you know; the web just uses it for HTTP edges.Networking for HLD: DNS, Proxies, LBs & API Gateways
Artifact repository (Nexus / Artifactory)
CI/CD		A warehouse where every finished box is labelled and never repacked.A server that stores build outputs (jars, npm packages, images) by version, proxies public registries, and refuses to overwrite a release version, so every deploy can name exactly what it ships.Builds & Pipelines
ASG (Auto Scaling Group)
Cloud must know		A manager who keeps the right number of workers on shift and replaces anyone who falls ill.A group of EC2 instances built from one launch template and spread across subnets in several AZs. It keeps a desired count between min and max, replaces instances that fail health checks, and scales on policies (target tracking, step, scheduled, predictive).EC2, ASG & Security Groups
Apache Flink
Pipeline		A fast machine that counts and sorts things on a moving belt, without stopping the belt.A distributed stream-processing engine with event-time windows, watermarks, large managed state and exactly-once checkpoints. Reads from and writes to Kafka through connectors.Kafka Connect, Streams & Flink
Apache Kafka
Kafka		A giant, never-forgetting conveyor belt of notes that many readers can read at their own speed.A distributed event-streaming platform. Producers append records to partitioned, replicated logs (topics) on brokers; consumers pull and track their own offsets. Records stay for a retention period, so many independent readers can replay them. From your world: A replicated, persistent, partitioned ring buffer system with independent read pointers.Why Messaging? (Sync vs Async)
API gateway
HLD must know		One front door with a guard for a building full of different offices.One entry point for many backend services. It routes requests, checks logins, applies rate limits, terminates TLS and collects metrics, so each service does not have to. From your world: A border router with ACLs and policers in front of the internal network.Networking for HLD: DNS, Proxies, LBs & API Gateways
API server (kube-apiserver)
Kubernetes must know		The only teacher allowed to write in the class notebook; everyone else asks the teacher.The front end of the Kubernetes control plane. Every client (kubectl, kubelet, controllers, scheduler) talks to it over HTTPS. It authenticates, authorizes, runs admission, validates, and is the only component that reads and writes etcd. Others watch it for changes. From your world: The management-plane daemon that owns the config database; line cards never touch the database directly.Architecture: Control Plane & Nodes
Append-only log
Kafka		A diary where you only ever add new lines at the bottom and never rub anything out.A data structure where new records are only added at the end and existing records are never changed. Each record gets the next position number (offset). Kafka partitions, database write-ahead logs and Raft logs are all append-only logs. From your world: A ring buffer's write side, except nothing is overwritten until retention deletes whole old segments.Core Concepts: Topics, Partitions, Offsets
Argo CD
Kubernetes		A robot that keeps the toy room looking exactly like the photo on the wall.A GitOps tool that runs inside Kubernetes, watches a git repository of manifests, and keeps syncing the cluster to match it. It shows drift and can roll back by reverting a commit.CI/CD Platform on Kubernetes
At-least-once delivery
Kafka must know		Every letter arrives, but sometimes the same letter arrives twice.Every record is processed, but some may be processed again after a crash, because the work finished before the position was saved. The usual default. You make the duplicates harmless with idempotent writes. From your world: Retransmission without sequence-number de-duplication.Delivery Semantics & Exactly-Once
At-most-once delivery
Kafka		No letter ever arrives twice, but one might get lost.Every record is processed zero or one time. Nothing is duplicated, but a crash can lose records. Happens when you save your read position before doing the work. From your world: UDP-style fire and forget.Delivery Semantics & Exactly-Once
Autoscaling
Kubernetes		When the queue at the ice-cream shop gets long, more servers come to help; when it is quiet, they go home.Automatically changing capacity to match load. In Kubernetes: HPA changes pod count, VPA changes pod size, and Cluster Autoscaler or Karpenter changes node count. Each reacts to metrics with some delay, so it is not instant. From your world: Adding ECMP members or links when utilisation crosses a threshold.Scheduling & Autoscaling
Availability (the "nines")
HLD must know		How often the shop is open when you come to buy something.The fraction of time (or of requests) a system serves correctly. 99.9% ("three nines") allows about 43 minutes of downtime a month; 99.99% allows about 4.3 minutes. Each extra nine usually costs much more. From your world: Same idea as five-nines carrier gear, but measured per request, not per box.Reliability at Scale: SLOs, Multi-Region & DR
Availability zone (AZ)
HLD		Separate buildings of one school, each with its own power, so a fire in one does not stop the others.One or more data centres inside a cloud region with independent power, cooling and networking, joined to the other zones by low-latency links. Spreading replicas across zones survives a single-site failure. From your world: Like separate PoPs in one metro with diverse fibre.Reliability at Scale: SLOs, Multi-Region & DR
Avro
Kafka		A secret code for writing messages that both sides agree on in advance, so the message can be tiny.A compact binary serialization format with a JSON-defined schema. Records carry no field names; the reader uses the writer's schema (often fetched from Schema Registry by an id) to decode, and evolution rules allow adding fields with defaults. From your world: Like a TLV format where the type dictionary is agreed out of band.Schemas & Schema Registry
