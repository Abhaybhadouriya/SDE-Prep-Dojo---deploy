How to use this site to prepare

Each track feeds one or more rounds. Read this matrix across a row to see which rounds a track prepares you for, or down a column to see what to study for one round.

  1. Follow the plan. Start with the 8-Week Study Plan. It schedules every page on this site, one or two hours a day.
  2. Read each page in level order. Every concept page climbs from Level 1 (story) to its top level. If you are short of time, stop at Level 3 for SDE-1 and push through Level 4 for SDE-2.
  3. Say the 60-second answers out loud. Every interview page has them. Record yourself once a week. Hearing your own answer is uncomfortable and very effective.
  4. Drill with the Question Bank. The Question Bank lets you filter by level, topic and company.
  5. Practise incidents. Production Scenario Drills teach the "stabilise, find, fix, prevent" pattern that SDE-2 interviewers listen for.
  6. Revise from one page. The night before, read the Cheat Sheets, not new material.
