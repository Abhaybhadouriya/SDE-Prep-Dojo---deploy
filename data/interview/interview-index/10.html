Common mistakes systems engineers make in these loops

  • Starting with internals. You open a design question by describing lock-free queues. Start with users, requirements and the API.
  • Underrating scale numbers. You think in packets per second on one ASIC. Practise "100 million daily users times 10 reads is about 1 billion reads a day, about 12,000 per second on average". See Estimation.
  • Vocabulary freeze. Words like idempotent, p99, sidecar and eventual consistency stop you. Keep the Glossary open while you study.
  • "We" in behavioural answers. Interviewers cannot hire your team. Say "I".
  • Ignoring LLD. Many SDE-2 loops have an LLD or machine-coding round. Practise one LLD problem a week (parking lot, Splitwise, rate limiter class design).
  • No questions at the end. Prepare two real questions per company about the team's systems ("How do you handle schema changes on your Kafka topics?").
