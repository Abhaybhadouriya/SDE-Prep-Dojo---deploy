Remember this

  • Every round has a rubric. Show each interviewer the signal they came to check.
  • The loop shape is shared: OA, screen, 4-5 rounds (DSA, LLD, HLD, behavioural), debrief, level and offer.
  • Amazon tests Leadership Principles in every round and has a Bar Raiser; Google L3/L4 is coding-heavy; Flipkart starts with machine coding; Walmart asks a lot of Kafka.
  • SDE-2 means you drive: numbers, technology choices with costs, failure modes and monitoring without prompts.
  • Translate your systems experience into SDE language; do not hide it and do not drown in it.
  • STAR: short Situation and Task, long Action with "I", Result with a real number.
  • Use the trade-off sentence: pick X because A, cost B, switch to Y if C.
