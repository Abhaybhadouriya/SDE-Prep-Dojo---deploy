Company by company

These summaries come from official prep pages where they exist, and otherwise from many anonymous candidate reports (LeetCode Discuss, Glassdoor) collected in our research notes. Teams differ. Always ask your recruiter for the exact format; recruiters are happy to tell you.

Shape. Online assessment (two coding questions plus a work-style survey), sometimes a phone screen, then a loop of four or five one-hour rounds.

  • Leadership Principles (LPs) in every round. Each interviewer is assigned a few of Amazon's 16 LPs (Customer Obsession, Ownership, Dive Deep, Bias for Action, Deliver Results, Earn Trust and others) and asks one or two "tell me about a time" questions, answered in STAR form.
  • SDE-1: mostly DSA plus one LLD/OOD round, light HLD.
  • SDE-2: two DSA, one HLD (often "scale an existing architecture" or a classic like a rate limiter, Amazon Locker, reviews and ratings), one LLD, and a Bar Raiser: an interviewer from another team with veto power, often LP-heavy.
  • Reported outcome pattern: one candidate reported a neutral HLD plus a negative Bar Raiser result led to an SDE-1 offer instead of SDE-2. Behavioural rounds matter as much as code.

Recent data (2025-26). In the reports we collected, each Amazon loop round was about 45 minutes of tech plus about 15 minutes for two LPs. The most reported Amazon HLD prompts were the news feed (8 reports), ticket or event booking (6), log ingestion and monitoring (5) and time-slot reservation (5). Full guide: Amazon SDE-1/SDE-2 guide.

Prepare with: News feed, Ticket booking, Time-slot reservation, Support chat routing, Rate limiter, Amazon Locker, Checkout, the STAR section below, and the official Leadership Principles page.

Shape. Recruiter screen, one online or phone coding round, then a loop of three or four rounds, sometimes plus an "AA" (as-appropriate) round with a senior manager.

  • SDE-2 reports: a 75-minute combined HLD and LLD round ("distributed LRU cache"), a 60-minute HLD ("distributed unique ID generator"), a chat app with message ordering and durability, and parking-lot LLD.
  • Some SDE-2 loops had no dedicated HLD round, with two OOD rounds instead. In 2025-26 reports, HLD and LLD were more often separate 60-75 minute rounds, and some loops merged them into one 75-80 minute round.
  • New in 2026: AI-assisted LLD rounds, where you design, prompt an AI tool and then review its output critically. Senior loops moved toward team-specific prompts (a configuration service, a file system for cloud storage).
  • Behavioural questions are woven into technical rounds (growth mindset, collaboration, learning from failure).

Prepare with: Configuration service, Collaborative editor, Distributed cache, Unique ID generator, Chat app, and Microsoft's own interview tips. Full guide: Microsoft SDE-1/SDE-2 guide.

Shape. Phone screen with one or two DSA problems, then an onsite of four or five rounds: three or four coding rounds (often written in a plain shared document, no IDE) and one "Googleyness and Leadership" round. A hiring committee reviews the packet, then team matching happens.

  • L3 (about SDE-1): candidates report no system design round.
  • L4 (about SDE-2): usually no standalone HLD round, but design discussion can appear inside a coding round or as an "algorithm design" question. A standalone HLD round is typical from L5 (senior).
  • Reported design prompts include a highway toll billing system and "how would you upgrade 5,000 servers safely?" (rolling and canary upgrades).

Prepare with: strong DSA first, then HLD fundamentals and rolling updates. Official overview: How Google hires. Recent L4 India reports still show no standalone design round. Full guide: Google L3/L4 guide.

Shape. A machine coding round first, often eliminating (reports describe 90 minutes plus 30 minutes to explain, or 60 plus 30). Then DSA or problem solving, then design, then a hiring manager round.

  • Machine coding prompts reported: Splitwise, food ordering, buy-now-pay-later (BNPL), property management. They expect working, extensible, runnable code with in-memory storage.
  • SDE-1: machine coding, DSA and hiring manager. HLD is rare.
  • SDE-2: adds HLD, for example "UrbanClap-like system" or "school bus navigation", and LLD such as a task scheduler assigning work to worker threads.

Prepare with: Flash sale (Big Billion Days), Product catalog and search, Job scheduler, and the newer prompts Music streaming, Flight booking and Quick commerce. Full guide: Flipkart SDE-1/SDE-2 guide. Flipkart's chaos engineering for sale days is described in the CNCF case study.

Shape (Walmart Global Tech India, SE-2/SE-3 roughly SDE-1/SDE-2). DSA screen (LeetCode medium), a DSA plus technology round (Java, Spring, Kafka), a system design round (HLD plus LLD), and a hiring manager round.

  • Very Kafka-heavy reports: "how does Kafka partitioning work and how would you scale it?", "how do you make sure related events go to the same partition?", "how do you persist and handle failed messages?", "design Kafka".
  • Design prompts: order management system, ticket booking with optimistic vs pessimistic locking, active-active vs active-passive, and at staff level a Google-Docs-like editor.

Prepare with: the whole Kafka track, the ShopStream pipeline, Inventory management, Ticket booking. Full guide: Walmart Global Tech guide.

Shape. A screen (sometimes coding plus about 15 minutes of design), then an onsite of four to six rounds: two DSA, an LLD or machine coding round, an HLD round, and a hiring manager or collaboration round.

  • LLD reported: parking lot, file system with wildcard search, train-platform management.
  • HLD reported (SDE-2): stock-broking price alerts, a real-time monitoring system (in a bar-raiser-style round), Google Calendar for a frontend role.
  • 2025-26 data: the HLD round is often the bar raiser and weighs most. Stock price alerts was Uber's most repeated prompt (7 reports), then proximity search (4) and a real-time driver heat map (3).

Prepare with: Ride hailing, Metrics and monitoring, Calendar, Surge pricing, Stock price alerts, Real-time heat maps. Full guide: Uber SDE-2 guide.

Also in the same market: Meta (E4, Meta's level for a mid-level engineer, roughly SDE-2: an onsite of two coding, one system or product design, one behavioural), Atlassian (a values round that can reject on its own; system design from P40, Atlassian's level for mid-senior engineers), and Indian product companies like Swiggy, Razorpay and PhonePe (machine coding or LLD, DSA, HLD, hiring manager). The patterns on this page apply to all of them.
