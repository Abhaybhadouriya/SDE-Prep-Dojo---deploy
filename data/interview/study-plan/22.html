LLD week (low-level design and machine coding)

Most SDE-1 and SDE-2 loops have one low-level design or machine-coding round next to the HLD round. Run this week after week 6, or in place of the week 8 design sprints if your loop has a machine-coding round (Flipkart, PhonePe, Razorpay, Uber and Swiggy reports often do). Each day climbs from the lesson to code you can run: the pattern and case-study pages carry Java, Python and C++ that was compiled and run. Start at the LLD overview and roadmap.

From your world

You already think in ops tables, refcounts and state machines. In the LLD round, say so: "this PaymentStrategy is a file_operations table; a booking is a state machine like a TCP connection". Interviewers reward a candidate who maps a pattern to code they really wrote.
