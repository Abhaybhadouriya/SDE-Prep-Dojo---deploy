Company-guide days (add before your loop)

Once you know where you are interviewing, add one company day per company in the last week before that loop, in place of a design sprint day. Each day reads the company guide, runs the 2-4 HLD prompts that company asked most in the 474 public 2025-26 reports we collected, and drills the one round that company is known for. The ranking and every variant are on Recently Asked HLD Questions; the matching practice cards are in the question bank.
