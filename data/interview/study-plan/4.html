Working full-time? Use the 12-week pace. It is the default we recommend. Keep the 8-week pace for when you have 3-4 free hours a day in the second half (for example, between jobs or on leave).

  • Calendar weeks 1-2: plan days 1-14, one plan day per day.
  • Calendar weeks 3-6: plan days 15-35 (weeks 3-5), three plan days every four calendar days. Use the fourth day to finish the lab and redo the design.
  • Calendar weeks 7-12: plan days 36-56 (weeks 6-8), one plan day every two calendar days. Read the first half of the pages on day one, and the rest plus the design on day two.

That is 14 + 28 + 42 = 84 days, exactly 12 weeks, and most days stay near two hours. Keep the DSA every calendar day. The day-by-day list below stays the same; only the speed changes.
