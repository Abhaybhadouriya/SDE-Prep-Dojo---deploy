The 8-week map

Each stop is a week. The dot travels the route you will walk. Colours match the tracks in the sidebar.

  1. 🐳

    Week 1 Docker

    Containers as fenced-in Linux processes, images and layers, Dockerfiles, networking, volumes, Compose, internals.

  2. ☸️

    Week 2 Kubernetes I

    Why Kubernetes, the control plane, pods, deployments, Services, ingress, config and secrets.

  3. 📨

    Week 3 Kubernetes II + Kafka

    Storage, autoscaling, operators, troubleshooting, security; then Kafka's first pages. Daily design starts.

  4. 📨

    Week 4 Kafka

    Consumer groups, replication, delivery semantics, storage internals, KRaft, Connect, schemas, operations.

  5. 🛠️

    Week 5 Pipeline + HLD basics

    Build ShopStream locally and on Kubernetes, break it, watch lag, replay. Then HLD fundamentals and estimation.

  6. 🏗️

    Week 6 HLD concepts

    Building blocks, APIs, data modeling, storage, consensus, transactions, resilience, security, geo.

  7. 🧩

    Week 7 Patterns + question bank

    All ten patterns, the filterable question bank, scenario drills, 2-3 designs a day.

  8. 🎯

    Week 8 Design sprints + mocks

    Timed designs, full mock loops, cheat sheets, STAR polish, and a rest day before the real thing.
