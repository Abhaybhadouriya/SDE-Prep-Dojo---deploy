Why does the plan teach Docker before Kubernetes?

  1. Docker is newer
  2. Kubernetes runs containers, so you need to understand one container before thousands
  3. Kubernetes needs Docker Desktop to work
Kubernetes schedules and manages containers. If you know what a container is (a process with namespaces and cgroups), Kubernetes concepts like pods and limits make sense.

What should you do before reading a design page?

  1. Watch three videos about it

  3. Try the design yourself for about 20 minutes
Struggling first makes the real answer stick, and shows you which parts you could not do alone.
