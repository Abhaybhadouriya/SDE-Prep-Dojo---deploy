Common mistakes with study plans

  • Reading without typing. Watching someone run kubectl feels like learning. It is not. Do every lab yourself, even the boring ones.
  • Reading the design answer first. Twenty minutes of your own struggle teaches more than an hour of reading a perfect answer.
  • Skipping the "say it" drill. Interviews are spoken. Knowing something and saying it clearly in 60 seconds are different skills.
  • Dropping DSA for design. Most loops still have two or more coding rounds. Keep the daily two problems.
  • Going too deep too early. You will enjoy kernel internals. Week 1 day 6 is the place for them; do not spend week 1 inside OverlayFS source code.
  • No mock interviews. Do at least four timed mocks (days 21, 35, 42 and the week 8 loop). A friend, a recording, or a peer platform all work.
