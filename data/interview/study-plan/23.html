Coding weeks (DSA rounds)

The daily DSA slot in the 8-week plan above is the small, steady version. If your loop has two or more coding rounds (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta, Flipkart, Walmart and Uber usually do), run the 12-week coding plan next to it, or its 4-week crash version when the loop is close. Each week reads one or two pattern lessons, then solves problems from the same groups in the topic explorer. Mark a problem solved and the revision queue brings it back on day 1, 3, 7 and 21.
