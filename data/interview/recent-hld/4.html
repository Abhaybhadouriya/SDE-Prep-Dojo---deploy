Trends in 2025-26: what is new

We split the reports into two periods: 2024-06 to 2025-12 (246 reports over about 19 months) and 2026-01 to 2026-11 (228 reports over 11 months). Because the periods differ in length, compare the counts per problem side by side rather than as absolute totals. Everything below is measured from the data; where a signal is thin we say so.

  • AI / LLM systems arrived. The ChatGPT-like chat / AI agent platform problem had 0 reports before 2026 and 6 in 2026 (Amazon, Atlassian, Tekion, Trupeer AI, Uber, eBay). Prompts include streaming tokens, multi-tenant isolation, model routing and token-cost limits, and an enterprise assistant over many tools. See ChatGPT-like LLM serving and AI system building blocks.
  • AI changed the loop, not only the prompt. 2 reports name an AI-enabled or AI-assisted round, and several posts say interviewers asked how you use AI tools day to day. Meta, Atlassian, LinkedIn and DoorDash posts mention AI-enabled coding rounds next to the HLD round.
  • Money flows got more attention. Payment systems went from 4 to 8 reports and wallets/ledgers from 0 to 4. Idempotency is named in more summaries (see the table below). Fintech companies (Groww, Paytm, PhonePe, Razorpay) ask domain prompts: GTT orders, square-off, T+1 settlement.
  • Real-time alerts and dashboards. Uber's stock price alert prompt (threshold and "moved X% in a window" alerts) is its most repeated HLD prompt, with 6 of its 7 reports in 2026. Live viewer counters, live leaderboards and driver heat maps follow the same pattern: stream in, aggregate, push out.
  • Rate limiting and infra classics are up. The rate limiter went from 7 to 11 reports, the job scheduler from 5 to 6, monitoring from 6 to 8, and the web crawler from 0 to 4. Notifications stayed steady (10 then 9).
  • What cooled down. The news feed is still number 1 overall, but most of its reports are older (19 before 2026, 7 in 2026). WhatsApp-style chat (8 then 1) and Atlassian's tagging system (9 then 1) also slowed. Still learn the news feed: its fan-out ideas show up inside many newer prompts.
