SDE-1 vs SDE-2: what changes

Of the 474 reports, 324 are SDE-2 (68%), 30 are SDE-1 (6%), 89 are SDE-3 or senior and 31 did not say. So most public HLD reports are from SDE-2 loops. That matches what the posts describe: many SDE-1 loops have no full HLD round at all, or only a light design discussion after a coding round.

SDE-1 (entry level)
  • Fewer HLD rounds. The SDE-1 reports come from 16 companies, led by Zomato (9), Amazon (3), Myntra (3).
  • Prompts lean on entities, schema and APIs: 6 of 30 SDE-1 summaries (20%) mention schema, data model or entities, against 16% at SDE-2.
  • Typical asks: the order lifecycle, Kafka vs a direct API call, sync vs async, no duplicate notifications, not overselling the last item.
  • Back-of-envelope maths is rarely the focus (named in 1 of 30 SDE-1 summaries).
  • Library management and room booking appear here: they are close to data modelling.
SDE-2 (mid level)
  • A full 45-75 minute HLD round in almost every loop. Uber and JPMorgan candidates call it the highest-weight round, and at Uber it is often the bar raiser.
  • You are expected to drive: functional and non-functional requirements, estimates, APIs, data model, diagram, then deep dives with 2-3 options and trade-offs.
  • Interviewers push on failure cases: "the worker crashed after sending", "the bank timed out", "one key gets half the traffic".
  • You must defend named tech: posts report deep questions on Redis pub/sub, Kafka partition keys and Elasticsearch once the candidate mentions them.
  • A weak design round can down-level an SDE-2 offer to SDE-1 (Flipkart and Zepto reports).
