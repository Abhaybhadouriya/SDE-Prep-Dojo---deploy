Where the HLD round sits in each loop

The same prompt feels different depending on where it lands in the loop and how long you get. Company names link to the full company guide where this site has one. This table shows one recently reported loop per company (from 85 loop descriptions in our notes), summarised in our own words. Loops vary by team and year, so confirm the format with your recruiter.

New words, once each (used in the posts and the table below):

  • FR / NFR = functional requirements (what the system does) and non-functional requirements (how well: scale, latency, availability).
  • OA = online assessment, a timed test before live rounds. YOE = years of experience.
  • LP = Amazon's Leadership Principles, asked in every round. Bar Raiser = an Amazon interviewer from outside the team who can veto a hire. STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result, the shape of a behavioural answer.
  • HM = hiring manager round. AA = Microsoft's "as appropriate" final round with a senior leader.
  • Karat = an outside company that runs first-round technical screens for employers. CoderPad = a shared online code editor. Superday = several back-to-back final rounds on one day (banks such as Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan).
  • GTT = good-till-triggered order; square-off = closing intraday positions automatically; T+1 = trades settle one business day later.
  • Level codes such as L5, E4, P40, IC2/IC3, MTS/SMTS, IN3/IN4 and L61: see the level map in How this list was built.
