  1. Collect. Five research agents searched public interview-experience posts dated 2024-06 to 2026-11 and pulled out every system design (HLD) question: 525 raw items.
  2. De-duplicate. When two agents found the same question in the same post, it was kept once. That left 474 reports from 437 posts. A post that asked two design questions counts once for each.
  3. Group. Different wordings of the same problem were merged into one canonical problem. "Design Twitter", "Design Instagram feed" and "Design a news feed" all count as the news feed problem.
  4. Level. Each report was bucketed as SDE-1, SDE-2, SDE-3/Senior or "not stated" from the title in the post, using one shared level map (below and in tools/level_map.py), the same one the company guides use. Company titles such as Amazon L5, Meta E4, Atlassian P40, Microsoft L61/L62, Oracle IC3/SMTS, Walmart SE-III and Salesforce MTS/SMTS count as SDE-2; Oracle IC2, Nutanix MTS-1 and Salesforce AMTS count as SDE-1; Meta E5 and Google L5 count as senior. When a title is ambiguous the years of experience decide: a "Senior" or SSE title with 4.5 YOE or less counts as SDE-2.
  5. Rank. Problems are ranked by number of reports, then by most recent date.
  6. Windows. The collection windows differ by company, which adds some bias to the ranking: Amazon 2025-04 to 2026-11; Meta 2025-04 to 2026-11; Microsoft 2025-06 to 2026-10; Uber 2024-10 to 2026-09; Flipkart 2024-06 to 2026-10; Atlassian 2025-02 to 2026-11; Salesforce 2024-12 to 2026-11; Oracle 2025-02 to 2026-11; Goldman Sachs 2025-01 to 2026-09; PhonePe 2024-06 to 2026-09.

So Amazon L5 = Microsoft L61/62 = Meta E4 = Google L4 = Atlassian P40 = Walmart SE-III = SDE-2. Titles vary by team and year; this is the mapping used for the counts on this page.

Read the counts with care. These are self-reported posts, not official question lists. Companies never publish their question banks, interviewers change prompts, and people who pass (or fail badly) are more likely to write. 473 of the 474 reports come from LeetCode Discuss, because it was the only source that gave first-hand posts we could read in full (each post's title and posting date were re-checked through LeetCode's public API; hover any source link to see them). Reddit blocked automated access, and Glassdoor, Blind and similar sites only showed search snippets, so they are not counted. Amazon has many more posts (105 reports) than any other company, so the overall ranking leans towards Amazon's favourites. Use the company filter in the full list to correct for that.

Privacy. We cite each post by its link only. No author names or handles are shown. All summaries are written in our own words.
