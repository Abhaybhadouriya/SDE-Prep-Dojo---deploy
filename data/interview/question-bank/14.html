Remember this

  • Answer in five beats: claim, mechanism, number, trade-off, hook.
  • Name the process that does the work (kubelet, scheduler, group coordinator), never "the system".
  • Use the trade-off sentence for every choice: pick X because A, cost B, switch to Y if C.
  • For scenarios: mitigate first, then diagnose with evidence, then prevent.
  • At-least-once plus idempotent effects is the honest way to talk about "exactly once".
  • Company tags mean "commonly reported at", not "always asked at".
  • Speak every answer out loud before reading the model answer.
