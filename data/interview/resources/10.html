Interview preparation

Goal: practise saying answers out loud, under time, in the order interviewers expect. On this site: Interview Hub, the 45-minute framework and the 8-week plan.

  • bregman-arie/devops-exercises repo if you open only one - hundreds of Docker, Kubernetes and Linux questions with answers. Use it as a flashcard deck, not as truth: check doubtful answers in the docs.
  • ByteByteGo system-design-101 repo - one-picture explanations of common topics; good for last-day revision.
  • awesome-system-design-resources repo - interview problems sorted by difficulty, with links.
  • Amazon Leadership Principles doc - Amazon mixes these into every round, including design. Prepare two stories per principle from your networking work.

Interviewers love this. When you mention a resource in an interview, mention what you did with it: "I read the Kafka design doc and then measured the difference linger.ms made on my laptop" beats "I watched a course".
