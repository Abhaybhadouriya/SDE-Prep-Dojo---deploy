How to learn from any resource

Watching a 3-hour course feels like progress but often is not. This loop turns any resource into something you can say in an interview. Press Next or Play.

  1. Watch one short video to get the picture.
  2. Read the official doc page for the same topic.
  3. Do a small lab and break something on purpose.
  4. Say a 60-second answer out loud.
  5. Test yourself with the page quiz and interview Q&A.
  6. Repeat with the next shelf (Build, then Go deep).
