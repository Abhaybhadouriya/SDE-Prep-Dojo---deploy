Verified playlists to binge

Short "watch in this order" sets built only from videos that were checked to exist. Each is under two hours.

  1. Three words in five minutes. Docker in 100 Seconds, Kubernetes Explained in 100 Seconds, Kafka in 100 Seconds (all Fireship). Watch the three below in one sitting.
  2. Containers for a kernel engineer. "Containers From Scratch" (Liz Rice, above), then "Cgroups, namespaces, and beyond" (Docker), then "How Docker Networking Actually Works" (KodeKloud).
  3. Kubernetes networking. "Kubernetes Services explained" (TechWorld with Nana), then "Life of a Packet" (above), then "Kubernetes Networking Intro and Deep-Dive" (Bowei Du and Tim Hockin).
  4. Kafka from zero to interview. "Kafka Tutorial for Beginners" (above), "Why is Kafka fast?" (above), "Kafka Replication - Can You Lose Messages??" (Jordan has no life), "How Kafka Actually Achieves Exactly-Once Semantics" (Arpit Bhayani).
  5. HLD warm-up. "A Step-By-Step Guide" (ByteByteGo, above), "The Delivery Framework" (Hello Interview, above), "Consistent Hashing" (ByteByteGo), then one full Hello Interview design such as Bitly.
