Kafka

Goal: draw partitions, replicas, leaders and ISR across three brokers; explain why Kafka is fast; and trade off acks and min.insync.replicas. On this site: Kafka track.

Start (Level 1-2)

  • Kafka quickstart doc if you open only one - a single KRaft node in about ten minutes: create a topic, produce, consume.
  • Confluent Developer courses course - free Kafka 101, internals, Streams and Connect courses with short videos and exercises. Vendor-made, so skip the product upsell parts.

Build (Level 3)

  • Kafka: The Definitive Guide, 2nd ed. book - Shapira, Palino, Sivaram and Petty (O'Reilly, 2021), free from Confluent. Chapters 1-6 cover producers, consumers and internals. It predates Kafka 4, so read KRaft and new defaults from the official docs.
  • Kafka design document doc - the page that explains page cache, zero-copy, batching, replication and compaction in the designers' own words. Short and excellent.
  • kafka-stack-docker-compose repo - copy-ready single and multi-broker KRaft stacks for local labs.
  • kafbat/kafka-ui repo - a web UI to see topics, partitions, consumer groups and lag on your local cluster.

Go deep (Level 4-5)

  • Kafka: a Distributed Messaging System for Log Processing (2011) paper - the original LinkedIn paper by Kreps, Narkhede and Rao. Eight pages; shows why the log design was chosen.
  • Transactions in Apache Kafka blog - coordinator, markers and zombie fencing explained. Read before the exactly-once page.
  • LinkedIn: customising Kafka for 7 trillion messages a day blog - how the birthplace of Kafka runs it.
  • Cloudflare: 1 trillion inter-service messages blog - a platform team's view of running many clusters for many teams.
  • PhonePe: 100 billion daily events blog - an Indian payments company's Kafka operations story.
  • Hello Interview: Kafka deep dive course - interview-oriented: when to use Kafka in a design and what to say about it.
