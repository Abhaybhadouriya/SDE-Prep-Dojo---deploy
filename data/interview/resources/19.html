Quick quiz

A blog post from 2021 tells you to set up ZooKeeper for your new Kafka 4.x cluster. What do you do?

  1. Follow it; ZooKeeper is required
  2. Run both ZooKeeper and KRaft to be safe
  3. Ignore that part: Kafka 4.x runs only in KRaft mode
ZooKeeper support was removed in Kafka 4.0. Old posts are still useful for concepts like partitions and ISR, but not for cluster setup.

You have one evening for Kubernetes and have never used it. What is the best first step?

  1. A short video, then the official basics tutorial in a browser lab
  2. Kubernetes the Hard Way
  3. A KubeCon deep dive on the scheduler
Start shelf first. The Hard Way and conference internals talks are excellent, but they stick only after you know the words and have run a pod.

Which kind of resource gives the best depth for the time it costs at Level 3 and above?

  1. A 4-hour beginner course
  2. Engineering blog posts and post-mortems
  3. Random forum answers
Company posts are written by the engineers who built and broke the system, and they take about 20 minutes to read.

A vendor blog says its product is 10 times faster than a competitor. How should you use that in an interview?

  1. Quote the number as a fact
  2. Never mention any benchmark
  3. Say it was a vendor-run benchmark, and reason from the design instead
Vendor benchmarks pick favourable workloads. Interviewers prefer reasoning (sequential I/O, batching, replication cost) over borrowed numbers.

Which resource is the best "walk me through what happens when you run kubectl apply" preparation?

  1. The kubectl cheat sheet
  2. what-happens-when-k8s
  3. A Docker Compose tutorial
It names every component and what it writes at each step, which is exactly how that interview question should be answered.

Which single book covers replication, partitioning, consistency and streams for both HLD and Kafka thinking?

  1. Designing Data-Intensive Applications
  2. Kubernetes: Up and Running
  3. The Docker documentation
DDIA is the shared foundation behind the HLD and Kafka tracks on this site.
