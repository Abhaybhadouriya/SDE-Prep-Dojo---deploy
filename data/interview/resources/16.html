From your world

Remember the "infra vs product backend" split you found: your background matches infra, but SDE loops mostly test product design. So do not over-invest in internals talks. For every hour of "Go deep" internals, spend an hour on a design walkthrough video where you pause and answer before the presenter does.

Where this breaks: on infra, SRE and platform teams the split flips, and internals are exactly what they test, so check the job description before you cut the deep material.
