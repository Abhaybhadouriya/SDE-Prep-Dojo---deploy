Resources

  • Jeff Erickson, Algorithms - Chapter 4: Greedy Algorithms book - free chapter with clear exchange-argument proofs (interval scheduling, Huffman, scheduling).
  • Kleinberg and Tardos, Greedy Algorithms I (slides) course - "greedy stays ahead" and exchange proofs on interval problems, the source of the proof styles used here.
  • Interval scheduling (Wikipedia) doc - the earliest-finish rule and its weighted variant (which needs DP).
  • LeetCode 678 editorial doc - compares DP, two stacks and the two-counter greedy.
  • LeetCode 179 editorial doc - the concatenation comparator and the all-zeros edge case.
  • GeeksforGeeks: Greedy Algorithms blog - a long list of classic greedy problems (activity selection, job sequencing, fractional knapsack).
  • Striver's A2Z DSA sheet (greedy step) course - an ordered greedy problem set; the same problems appear in our lists.
