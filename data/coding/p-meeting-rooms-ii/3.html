Recognise the pattern

When you see "minimum number of resources (rooms, platforms, servers, cars) so that intervals never share one", think the answer is the maximum overlap depth. Compute it with a sweep over sorted events (or a min-heap of end times). If the question asks which meetings to drop or merge instead, think greedy by end time (435) or merge (56).
