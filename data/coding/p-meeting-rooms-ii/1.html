The problem (in our words)

You get an array intervals where intervals[i] = [starti, endi] is the booked time of meeting i. Return the minimum number of conference rooms needed so that every meeting gets a room for its whole time and no room hosts two meetings at once. A meeting that ends at time t and another that starts at time t can use the same room (the intervals are half-open, [start, end)). The input is not sorted.

Signature: int minMeetingRooms(int[][] intervals) (LeetCode style).

  • 1 <= intervals.length <= 104
  • 0 <= starti < endi <= 106

Practice it: LeetCode 253 Premium · GfG Meeting Rooms II (free) · GfG Minimum Platforms (same idea, inclusive ends)

Constraints tell you: n ≤ 104 allows O(n log n) comfortably and even O(n²) in a pinch. Times up to 106 also allow a difference array over the whole time range (O(n + T)), which is worth mentioning as a third option.
