How to recognise it:

  • "Minimum number of X so that intervals never share an X."
  • The assignment itself is not asked, only the count (or it is, then use the heap).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 252 Meeting Rooms, then 56 Merge Intervals.
  2. This problem: heap first, then the two-array sweep; check both on random tests. Free judge: 2406.
  3. Then 1094 Car Pooling (weighted), and 2402 Meeting Rooms III (two heaps).
