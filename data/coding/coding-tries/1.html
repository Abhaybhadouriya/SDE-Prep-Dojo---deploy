What a trie looks like

Each node has up to one child per letter, plus a flag that says "a word ends here". The root is the empty prefix. The path from the root to a node spells that node's prefix. Here are the words cat, car, card, do and dog:

Three facts to carry forward:

  • Cost depends on the word, not the dictionary. Looking up a word of length L visits L nodes, whether the trie holds 10 words or 10 million.
  • Prefixes are free. "Does any word start with ca?" is the same walk as a lookup, without checking the end flag.
  • Order is free. A DFS that visits children in letter order lists all words in sorted order, like an inorder walk of a BST.
