Quiz

Keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 are inserted in that order into an empty plain BST. What is its height (in edges)?

  1. 4
  2. 3
  3. 5
  4. 2
Sorted inserts always go right, so the tree is a chain of 5 nodes with 4 edges. This is why plain BST bounds are O(h), not O(log n).

In the BST iterator, what does next() push after popping node x?

  1. Nothing; the stack is filled once in the constructor
  2. x's left child and its right spine
  3. x's parent
  4. x's right child and all of that child's left descendants
The next bigger key is the leftmost node of x's right subtree, so push the right child and keep going left.

Why is next() O(1) amortized even though one call can push h nodes?

  1. Because pushing is cheap in every language
  2. Every node is pushed once and popped once over the whole iteration, so n calls do O(n) work
  3. Because the stack is a linked list
  4. Because h is always O(log n)
Total work over n calls is O(n); divided by n calls that is O(1) per call on average.

You delete a node with two children by copying its inorder successor s into it. Which case is deleting s from the right subtree?

  1. s never needs deleting
  2. Always 0 or 1 child, because s has no left child
  3. Always two children
  4. It depends on the root
s is the leftmost node of the right subtree, so its left child is null. It has at most a right child.

The inorder sequence of a BST with two swapped keys is 1 2 6 4 5 3 7. Which keys are swapped?

  1. 5 and 3
  2. 6 and 4
  3. 6 and 3
  4. 4 and 5
Dips: 6 > 4 (first = 6) and 5 > 3 (second = 3). Swapping 6 and 3 gives 1 2 3 4 5 6 7.

Finding the successor of p from the root without parent pointers: what do you do when p's key is smaller than the current node?

  1. Record the current node as a candidate and go left
  2. Go to the parent
  3. Return the current node
  4. Go right
The current node is bigger than p, so it may be the successor; a better (smaller but still bigger than p) candidate can only be in its left subtree.

Building a BST from the sorted array [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7] with "middle is root" gives which root and children?

  1. Root 1, right child 2
  2. Root 4, children 2 and 6
  3. Root 7, left child 6
  4. Root 4, children 1 and 7
The middle of 0..6 is index 3 (key 4); the middles of [1, 2, 3] and [5, 6, 7] are 2 and 6.
