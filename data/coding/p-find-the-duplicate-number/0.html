The story

Explain like I'm 5

A cloakroom has n hooks numbered 1 to n and hands out n + 1 tickets, so at least two tickets name the same hook. Each ticket slot i holds a hook number. Start at slot 0, read the hook number on it, walk to the slot with that number, read again, and so on. Because two slots send you to the same hook, your walk eventually goes round in a circle, and the place where you first step onto the circle is the hook that was handed out twice.

The puzzle is famous because of its rules, not its task: you may not change the array, you may use only O(1) extra memory, and you should beat O(n2). A hash set or sorting breaks the rules; the two answers that obey them are binary search on the value range (O(n log n)) and cycle detection (O(n)).

From your world

It is the same as finding where a corrupted singly linked list (say a free list or an skb chain) loops back on itself, with only two cursors and no visited bitmap. Here the "next pointer" is not stored in a struct; it is the function i -> nums[i] over array indices.
