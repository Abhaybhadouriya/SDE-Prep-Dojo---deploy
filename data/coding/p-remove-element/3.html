Recognise the pattern

Cue: "remove ... in place" + "return the new length" + "elements beyond the length do not matter" -> same-direction two pointers: a reader r visits every element, a writer w marks the end of the kept part. It is the parent of Move Zeroes and Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array; only the "keep" test changes.
