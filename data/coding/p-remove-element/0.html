Explain like I'm 5

A tray has a row of sweets, and you must take out every lemon one. You are not allowed a second tray. You look at each sweet in turn; whenever it is not lemon, you slide it to the next free place at the left end of the tray. When you have looked at all of them, the good sweets are packed on the left, and you just say how many there are. Whatever is left on the right does not matter.

From your world

This is the in-place filter you write in C to drop entries from an array of descriptors: for (r = 0; r < n; r++) if (keep(a[r])) a[w++] = a[r]; n = w;. It is also how a packet filter compacts a ring after dropping matching frames, or how you purge closed fds from a poll set. The "length" is a separate variable, and the stale tail is simply ignored.
