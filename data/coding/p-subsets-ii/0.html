The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a bag of fruit: one apple and two identical pears. How many different snack plates can you make? The two pears look the same, so "pear #1 only" and "pear #2 only" are the same plate. Line the fruit up so the same kinds stand together. When you choose the next fruit for a plate, pick each kind once at that step: the first pear stands for all pears at that step. You may still take a second pear later, one step deeper.

Subsets (LC 78) has no duplicates and gives 2n subsets. Here equal values make different index choices produce the same subset. The test is whether you avoid duplicates while generating (pruning) instead of generating everything and filtering with a set.

From your world

It is the difference between deduplicating packets at the receiver (a hash of everything seen) and never sending the duplicate at all. Sorting first is like grouping identical flows in one queue: the scheduler then only needs to compare with the previous entry.
