Technique: generate combinatorial objects from a multiset without duplicates by sorting and skipping equal choices at the same recursion level.

  • Different: the judge may expect the subsets in sorted (lexicographic) order.
  • So: the same sorted backtracking already emits them in lexicographic order; check the expected output format.

How to recognise it:

  • "Return all ..." with small n (≤ 10-20) and "the solution set must not contain duplicates".
  • The input itself contains repeated values.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 78 Subsets, 77 Combinations.
  2. This problem, then 40 Combination Sum II (same skip line).
  3. 47 Permutations II (the used[] form of the rule).
