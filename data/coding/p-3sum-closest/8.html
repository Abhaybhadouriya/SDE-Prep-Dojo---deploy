Common mistakes

Returning the distance, not the sum. The output is the sum itself (5 in example 1), not |5 - 6| = 1. Keep best as a sum and compute distances only for comparison.

Starting best at INT_MAX. abs(INT_MAX - target) overflows when the target is negative (undefined behaviour in C++, a wrong sign in Java). Start from nums[0] + nums[1] + nums[2], which is a valid answer.

Forgetting to sort. The pointer moves are only correct on a sorted array; on the raw input, moving lo right may lower the sum.

Moving both pointers after an update. Unlike 3Sum, a "good" sum here is not a final answer: move exactly one pointer, chosen by the sign of sum - target.

Copying 3Sum's "break when nums[i] > 0". That shortcut is for target 0 only. Here, a large fixed value can still be the closest for a large target.
