Technique: interval DP where the cost of the split point depends only on the interval's two boundaries.

Closest match: LC 1547 Minimum Cost to Cut a Stick Hard

  • Same: pad the list (with 0 and n), dp over open intervals between two "walls", try every split point.
  • Different: you choose the FIRST cut (the cost is the current piece length, which is fixed by the walls), and you minimise.
  • So: dp[l][r] = (c[r] - c[l]) + min dp[l][k] + dp[k][r]; the same three loops.

How to recognise it:

  • A line of items is consumed one at a time and the cost of a step depends on the current neighbours or the current segment.
  • n is at most a few hundred (an O(n3) hint) and greedy has an easy counter-example.
