Common mistakes

Splitting on the first balloon burst. The recurrence f(l, k-1) + f(k+1, r) + nums[k-1]·nums[k]·nums[k+1] is wrong: after k bursts, k-1 and k+1 become neighbours, so the two halves are not independent.

Closed instead of open intervals. In this formulation l and r are NOT burst; they are the walls. Mixing "inclusive" indices with the v[l]·v[k]·v[r] product gives off-by-one answers. Pad the array and keep both ends exclusive.

Wrong fill order. Looping l from 0 upwards and r upwards reads best[k][r] before it is computed. Loop by gap, or l downwards and r upwards.

Greedy. "Always burst the smallest" or "the one with the largest product" both fail; [2,4,3] already needs the large 4 to go first.
