The story

Explain like I'm 5

A courier drone flies a closed loop of charging pads. At each pad it picks up some charge, and flying to the next pad burns some charge. It starts with an empty battery at a pad of your choice. Which pad lets it fly the whole loop once and come back without ever running empty? The trick is that one failed attempt tells you about many pads at once, not only the one you tried.

From your world

Think of a token bucket on a ring of links. Each hop adds gas[i] tokens and spends cost[i]. If the bucket underflows between hop a and hop b, every hop in between started with fewer tokens than a would have handed it, so it underflows too. The fix is the same as resetting a credit counter: drop the window and start fresh after the failure point.
