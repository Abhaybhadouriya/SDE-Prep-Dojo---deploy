Stability: what happens to ties

A sort is stable if items with equal keys keep the order they had in the input. That matters when you sort records by one field after another, or when the problem says "if two items tie, keep their original order".

Two practical rules. If you need ties in input order and your sort is not stable, add the original index as the last key; then no two keys are equal and stability stops mattering. And sorting by "B, then by A" can be done as two stable sorts: first by A, then by B. That is exactly how radix sort works, one digit at a time from the lowest.
