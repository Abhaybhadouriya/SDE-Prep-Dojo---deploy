Common mistakes

  • Non-strict comparator. <= in C++ or an inconsistent rule in Java. Symptoms: crash, wrong order, or "violates its general contract".
  • Subtraction comparator. (a, b) -> a - b overflows when values are far apart. Always Integer.compare.
  • Losing the original index. Sorting values when the answer needs positions. Sort index arrays or (value, index) pairs.
  • Counting sort with a huge range. "Values up to 109" means a 4 GB count array. Compress coordinates or use a hash map.
  • Merge counting with overflow. 2 * nums[j] in 32 bits, and also the total count, which can reach n2/2 (use 64-bit for the sum).
  • Advancing i after a swap with hi in Dutch flag. The value that came from hi has not been checked yet.
  • Fixed pivot in quickselect. Sorted or all-equal input goes O(n2) and times out.
  • Sorting inside a loop. Re-sorting after every insert is O(n2 log n). Use a heap, a balanced tree or insertion into a sorted list.
