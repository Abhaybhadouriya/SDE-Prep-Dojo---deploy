Recognise the pattern

When you see "two sorted sequences" + "one sorted output", think two-pointer merge: compare the fronts, take the smaller, advance only that side.

When you see "build a new linked list" where the head may change, think dummy (sentinel) head + tail pointer, return dummy.next.

The front of each list is its smallest remaining value, so the smallest remaining value overall is one of the two fronts. That single fact gives the whole algorithm, and it grows into the k-list version (LC 23) with a heap.

Taxonomy: Linked list → merge and rebuild. Pattern lesson: Linked lists.
