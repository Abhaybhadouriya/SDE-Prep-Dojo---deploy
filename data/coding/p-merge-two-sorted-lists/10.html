How to recognise it:

  • Two (or k) inputs that are each sorted; the output needs a global order.
  • A linked-list result whose first node is not known in advance: use a dummy head.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 88 (arrays, merge from the back).
  2. This problem: iterative first, then the recursive 5-liner.
  3. Then LC 148 (merge sort on a list) and LC 23 (k lists with a heap).
