The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two queues of children are each lined up by height, shortest first. You need one line, also by height. Look at the two children at the front, send the shorter one to the new line, and look again. When one queue is empty, the rest of the other queue just walks over as it is, because it is already in order.

From your world

This is the merge step of merge sort, and the same loop the kernel's list_sort() (lib/list_sort.c) runs on struct list_head lists: it relinks existing nodes and never allocates. The dummy head plays the role of a list_head sentinel: a node that is always there, so inserting the first real node is no different from inserting the tenth.
