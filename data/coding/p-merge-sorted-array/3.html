Recognise the pattern

When you see two sorted sequences to combine, and the output must go into a buffer whose free space is at the end, think two read pointers + one write pointer, all moving backwards. Free space at the front would mean merging forwards.

DirectionWhat happensVerdict
Forwards, writing into nums1Writing 3 at index 1 overwrites 5 before it is read✗ needs shifting or a copy
Forwards into a new arrayCorrect, classic merge step of merge sort✓ but O(m + n) extra space
Backwards into nums1Writes land in free slots or slots already read✓ O(1) extra space
