The story

Explain like I'm 5

A bookshelf holds your books in height order, with some empty space at the right end. A friend hands you a second pile, also sorted. If you start inserting from the left, every insert pushes all your books one step right. Instead, look at the tallest book of each pile and put the taller one in the rightmost empty spot. The empty spots are always to the right of the books you have not moved yet, so nothing gets squashed.

From your world

This is the overlap rule of memmove. When the destination overlaps the source and lies at higher addresses, you copy from the end backwards so no byte is overwritten before it is read. Here the write head w is always at or above the read head i, so the backwards merge never clobbers an unread value.
