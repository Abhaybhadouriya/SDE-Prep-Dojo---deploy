  5. Pop the two nulls: write "#", "#". A null pushes nothing, so the queue never grows from a hole.
  6. Pop 7 and 9: write "7", "9"; each pushes two nulls.
  7. Pop the four nulls: four '#'. Queue empty. Result: "5,2,8,#,#,7,9,#,#,#,#" - 5 values + 6 holes = 2n + 1 tokens for n = 5 nodes.
