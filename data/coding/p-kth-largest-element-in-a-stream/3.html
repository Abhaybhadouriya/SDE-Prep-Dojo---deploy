Recognise the pattern

Cue: "kth largest / top k" with a fixed k and data that keeps arriving -> a min-heap of size k (for kth smallest, a max-heap of size k). The heap's top is the boundary between "in the top k" and "out".

The direction is the part people get wrong. Ask: which element do I need to throw out when a better one arrives? For "largest", you throw out the smallest of your k, so you need fast access to the minimum.
