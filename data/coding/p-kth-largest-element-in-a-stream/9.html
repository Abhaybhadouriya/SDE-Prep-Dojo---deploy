60-second recall

  • Cue: fixed k, values keep arriving, "kth largest / top k" -> size-k heap.
  • Insight: keep the k largest in a MIN-heap; its top is the kth largest.
  • State: heap of at most k values; the new value enters only if it beats the top.
  • Trap: min not max; start may hold k-1 values; duplicates count.
  • Cost: O(log k) per add, O(k) space; sort-each-time is O(n log n).
h = []                                   # top-k largest skeleton
for x in stream:
    if len(h) < k: heapq.heappush(h, x)
    elif x > h[0]: heapq.heapreplace(h, x)
    kth = h[0]                           # valid once len(h) == k

Quick quiz

Which heap keeps the kth largest at its top?

  1. A min-heap of size k
  2. A max-heap of size k
  3. A max-heap of all values
  4. A min-heap of all values
The smallest of the k largest is the kth largest.

k = 3, the heap holds {5,6,8}. add(2) returns...

  4. 8
