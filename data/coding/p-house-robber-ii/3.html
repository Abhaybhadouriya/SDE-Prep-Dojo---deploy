Recognise the pattern

When you see a known line DP + "arranged in a circle" (first and last are neighbours), think "break the circle": fix one end as unused and solve the line, once per end.

When you see "no two adjacent" + "maximise the sum", think pick/skip with two rolling variables (House Robber I).

Try reusing the line answer on the whole array:

numsLine robber on allUses 0 and n-1?Circle answer
[2,6,9,3]11 (2 + 9)no11✓
[5,1,1,5]10 (5 + 5)yes6✗
[6,2,5,3,7]18 (6 + 5 + 7)yes12✗

The line answer is wrong exactly when it takes both ends. Patching that one plan does not work (the best fix may be a totally different plan), so split the problem into two lines instead.
