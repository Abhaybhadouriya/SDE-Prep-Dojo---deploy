How to recognise it:

  • A line problem you know, plus "circular", "ring" or "the first and last are adjacent".
  • The wrap-around adds only one constraint between two fixed positions, so a small case split removes it.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 198 (the line).
  2. This problem: two passes, then the one-pass flag version as a check.
  3. Then LC 918 (circular Kadane), LC 337 (tree), LC 1388 (circle + exact count).
