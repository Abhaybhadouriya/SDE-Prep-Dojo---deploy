Common mistakes

Forgetting n = 1. max(line(0, -1), line(1, 0)) returns 0 for [8].

Excluding both ends in one pass. Solving only 1..n-2 misses plans that take house 0 or house n-1 (for [9,1,1] it returns 1 instead of 9).

Patching the line answer. "If the line plan uses both ends, drop the smaller end" is wrong: the best circle plan may share nothing with the line plan.

Copying slices in a loop. nums[1:] is fine once, but pass bounds (lo, hi) when a follow-up adds many queries or a large n.
