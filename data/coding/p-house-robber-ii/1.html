The problem (in our words)

A security tester checks the payment kiosks placed around a circular food court. Kiosk i holds nums[i] in cash. Because the kiosks form a circle, kiosk 0 and kiosk n - 1 are neighbours, as are kiosks i and i + 1. Two neighbouring kiosks share an alarm, so opening both on the same night raises it.

Given the integer array nums, return the maximum total that can be collected without ever opening two neighbouring kiosks. Opening none is allowed.

Signature: int rob(int[] nums).

Constraints

  • 1 <= nums.length <= 100
  • 0 <= nums[i] <= 1000

Practice it: LeetCode 213 - House Robber II · GfG - Stickler Thief II · TUF - House Robber II

Constraints tell you: n ≤ 100, so anything polynomial passes; the interviewer wants the reduction to the line problem and O(1) space. The total is at most 50 × 1000 = 50,000, far inside an int. n can be 1, and then the only kiosk is not its own neighbour.
