The story

Explain like I'm 5

Sweet jars stand in a ring around a table, and you may never take from two jars that touch. In a straight row you already know the trick (best so far, skip or take). The ring adds just one new pair of touching jars: the first and the last. So play the row game twice: once pretending the last jar is not there, once pretending the first jar is not there. Any good choice in the ring is a good choice in one of those two rows.

From your world

A ring buffer has the same wrinkle: slot n-1 is adjacent to slot 0. Code that handles a linear array plus "is the wrap-around pair involved?" is the usual way to deal with it, instead of writing special ring logic inside the loop.
