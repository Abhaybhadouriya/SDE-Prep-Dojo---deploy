How to recognise it:

  • "Number of subarrays with exactly k ..." where the quantity only grows when the window grows.
  • Binary or non-negative values (or a property you can map to 0/1).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 560 (prefix map), 713 (counting window).
  2. This problem, then 1248 (the same with odds).
  3. 992 (exactly k distinct), then 3306.
