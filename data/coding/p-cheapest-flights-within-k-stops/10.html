How to recognise it:

  • "Cheapest / fastest" + a cap on edges, stops, transfers or hops.
  • Any second resource on the path (fuel, time, coupons): put it in the state.
  • Small n with an edge list given directly: Bellman-Ford rounds are the shortest code.

Practice order:

  1. LC 743 for plain Dijkstra.
  2. This problem with Bellman-Ford, then again with Dijkstra on (city, flights).
  3. LC 1631 and LC 1514 for different path metrics.
  4. LC 2093 for a state-expanded Dijkstra.
