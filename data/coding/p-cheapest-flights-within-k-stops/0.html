The story

Explain like I'm 5

You want to go from your town to grandma's town by bus. Each bus ride costs some coins, and your parents say: "you may change buses at most twice". The cheapest trip might need five changes, so it is not allowed. You make a list: "cheapest price to every town with one ride", then "with up to two rides", then "with up to three rides". Each new list is built only from the previous list plus one more ride. After three rides you read grandma's town off the last list.

From your world

This is a distance-vector routing protocol with a hop limit. RIP is literally Bellman-Ford: each update round, every router learns routes one hop longer from its neighbours' previous tables, and a metric of 16 means "unreachable". Here the hop limit is k + 1 and the metric is the ticket price. The "use the previous table, not the one you are writing" rule is the same reason routers advertise a snapshot of their table.
