Common mistakes

Reversing before checking. If you reverse while counting and find fewer than k nodes, you must reverse them back. Probe first, then change pointers.

Losing the head of the next group. Save after = kth.next before the reversal loop; once the pointers move, there is no way back to it.

Moving prevTail to kth. After the reversal, kth is the group's head. The new tail is the old first, and that is where the next group must be attached.

Returning head. The original head is now inside the first group (at its end). Return dummy.next.
