The story

Explain like I'm 5

A train of numbered wagons must be re-coupled so that every block of k wagons faces backwards, while the blocks stay in their places. You walk along the train: count k wagons ahead to be sure a whole block is there, flip that block, couple its new front to the end of the last flipped block, and move on. If fewer than k wagons are left at the end, you leave them alone.

The flip itself is plain "reverse a linked list". The hard part is the bookkeeping at the two seams of every block.

From your world

This is pointer surgery on a singly linked list, like re-ordering sk_buff fragments in a chain without copying payloads: you may only change next pointers, never the data. A dummy head plays the role of a list-head sentinel (as in list_head), so the first block needs no special case.
