Recognise the pattern

When you see "reverse part of a linked list", think the three-pointer reversal (prev, cur, next) plus a dummy head and a pointer to the node before the part. When the parts repeat (every k nodes, pairs, even-length groups), wrap it in a loop that first checks the part is complete.

List, kResultNote
[1,2,3,4], 4[4,3,2,1]k = n: the whole-list reversal (LC 206).
[1,2,3,4], 2[2,1,4,3]k = 2: swap pairs (LC 24).
[1,2,3,4,5], 4[4,3,2,1,5]One leftover node stays.
[1,2,3], 1[1,2,3]k = 1: nothing to do.
