How to recognise it:

  • "Reverse" + "linked list" + a range, a count or a repeated block.
  • "Do not change values" or "O(1) extra memory": pointer surgery, not copying.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 206 Reverse Linked List, then 92 Reverse Linked List II (one group, two seams).
  2. This problem: iterative with a dummy; test k = 1, k = n and n % k != 0.
  3. Then: 2074 Reverse Nodes in Even Length Groups (changing group sizes).
