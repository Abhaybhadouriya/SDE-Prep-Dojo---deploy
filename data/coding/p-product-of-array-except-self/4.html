Approaches: brute → better → optimal

Hint 1

Split the "everything except i" product into two pieces that do not include i.

Hint 2

The piece to the left of i is the piece to the left of i-1 times nums[i-1]. One running variable is enough.

1. Brute force - multiply everything else for each index

Idea: for each i, loop over all j ≠ i and multiply.

O(n²)time
O(1)space

What is wasted: the products for neighbouring indexes share all but two factors. We recompute the shared part n times. -> rung 2.

2. Not allowed, but say it - total product and division

Idea: compute the product of all elements, then answer[i] = total / nums[i]. It needs a zero count to survive zeros: with two or more zeros everything is 0; with exactly one zero only that slot is non-zero (the product of the others).

O(n)time
O(1)space

Why it is not enough: the problem bans division, and the zero cases make the code fiddly. Mention it in one sentence to show you know it, then move on.

3. Better - prefix and suffix arrays

Idea: pre[i] = product of nums[0..i-1] (with pre[0] = 1), suf[i] = product of nums[i+1..n-1] (with suf[n-1] = 1), then answer[i] = pre[i] * suf[i].

i0123
nums2345
pre (left of i)12624
suf (right of i)602051
answer60403024
O(n)time
O(n)extra space

What is wasted: two extra arrays. The output array can hold pre, and suf is only ever read in order from the right, so a single variable can replace it. -> rung 4.

4. Optimal - left products in the output, right products in a variable

out, left = [1] * n, 1
for i in range(n):            out[i] = left;  left *= nums[i]
right = 1
for i in range(n - 1, -1, -1): out[i] *= right; right *= nums[i]
  1. Exclusive, not inclusive. Write out[i] = left before multiplying nums[i] in, so slot i never contains its own value.
  2. After pass 1, out[i] = product of everything left of i.
  3. Pass 2 walks back with right = product of everything right of i, multiplied into out[i] before nums[i] joins right.
  4. Zeros take care of themselves. A zero simply makes every product that includes it 0; the slot of the zero never multiplies by it.
O(n)time
O(1)extra space

Why it works: multiplication is associative and commutative, so the product over all j ≠ i is (product over j < i) × (product over j > i). Each running variable holds exactly one of these at the moment slot i is visited, because the update to include nums[i] happens after the write.
