The story

Explain like I'm 5

Kids stand in a line, each holding a number card. For every kid, the teacher wants the product of everyone else's cards. Instead of making each kid walk the whole line, one helper walks from the front and whispers to each kid "the product of everyone in front of you". A second helper walks from the back and whispers "the product of everyone behind you". Each kid multiplies the two whispers. Two walks, done.

The tempting shortcut, "multiply everything once and divide by my own card", is banned here, and it would break anyway when a card shows 0.

From your world

It is the exclusive prefix scan you know from GPU or SIMD code (and from computing buffer offsets from sizes): out[i] = op(a[0..i-1]). Doing one exclusive scan from the left and one from the right, and combining them, is a standard way to get "all but me" for any associative operation, even one with no inverse.
