Recognise the pattern

When you see "for every index, combine all elements except this one" + division is banned or unsafe, think prefix (left) result × suffix (right) result. The same split works for sum, product, max, gcd, or any associative operation, which is why it is the general answer.

Try a few and see why division is not just banned but fragile:

InputAnswerTotal / nums[i] works?
[1,2,3][6,3,2]✓ 6/1, 6/2, 6/3
[5,0,2][0,10,0]✗ total is 0, and 0/0 at index 1
[0,4,0][0,0,0]✗ two zeros: every answer is 0
