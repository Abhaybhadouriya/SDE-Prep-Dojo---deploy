  • Insight: exclusive prefix scan into the output, then an exclusive suffix scan in a variable.

out[i] must exclude nums[i]. Folding it in first gives the inclusive product.

Input [2, 0, 3, 0]. What is the output?

  1. [0, 0, 0, 0]
  2. [0, 6, 0, 0]
  3. [0, 0, 0, 6]
  4. division error
With two zeros every 'all others' product contains a zero. The prefix/suffix scans handle zeros with no special case.

Why is dividing the total product by nums[i] not allowed or safe?
