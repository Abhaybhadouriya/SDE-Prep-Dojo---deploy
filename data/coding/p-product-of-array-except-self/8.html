Common mistakes

Inclusive prefix. Multiplying left *= nums[i] before writing out[i] puts nums[i] into its own answer. Write first, then multiply.

Initialising the output with 0. If pass 1 is skipped for some slot or you start from out = [0]*n and multiply, everything becomes 0. The empty product is 1.

Dividing anyway. Even if the judge accepted it, total / nums[i] crashes on a zero and needs a zero count; the interviewer asked for no division on purpose.

Overflow worry in the wrong place. The guarantee covers prefix and suffix products. If you compute the full product of all n elements (the division approach), it is not covered and can overflow.
