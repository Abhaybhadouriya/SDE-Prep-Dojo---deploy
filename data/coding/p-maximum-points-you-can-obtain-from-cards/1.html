The problem (in our words)

Cards lie in a row; card i is worth cardPoints[i] points. You must take exactly k cards, one at a time, and each time you may take only the first or the last card of the remaining row. Your score is the sum of the points on the cards you took. Return the maximum score you can get.

Signature: int maxScore(int[] cardPoints, int k).

Constraints

  • 1 <= cardPoints.length <= 10^5
  • 1 <= cardPoints[i] <= 10^4
  • 1 <= k <= cardPoints.length

Practice it: LeetCode 1423 - Maximum Points You Can Obtain from Cards · TUF - Maximum points from cards · GfG - Maximize sum of K corner elements

Constraints tell you: n and k up to 105: trying every left/right sequence is 2k, and a DP over (left taken, right taken) is O(k2) = 1010. You need O(n) or O(k). The total is at most 105 * 104 = 109, which fits in a 32-bit int. All values are positive, so taking a card never lowers the score.
