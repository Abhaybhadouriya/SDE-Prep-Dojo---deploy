Common mistakes

Greedy on the ends. Taking the larger end each time fails on [1, 50, 2, 2, 3], k = 2. The choice now changes what you can reach later.

Writing the 2k recursion or an O(k²) DP. Correct, but times out at k = 105. Explain why only the split matters before coding.

Wrong indices in the trade. The left card you give back at step i is cardPoints[k - i] (innermost of the left part); the right card you take is cardPoints[n - i]. Check both on i = 1: indices k - 1 and n - 1.

Looping i from 1 to k - 1. Misses the "all k from the right" split. The loop runs i = 1..k inclusive.

Complement version with k = n. A window of length 0 makes "sum of the first m elements" and the slide loop degenerate; return the total directly.
