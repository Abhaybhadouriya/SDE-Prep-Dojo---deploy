The story

Explain like I'm 5

Stickers are lined up in a row, each worth some points, and you may peel exactly k of them, but only from the two ends of the row. However you peel, you end up with a few from the left end and the rest from the right end. So you just try "all from the left", "one fewer from the left and one from the right", and so on, and keep the best.

From your world

Think of a ring buffer of n slots where you may keep k contiguous slots that wrap around the end: "i from the left + k - i from the right" is exactly a window of length k on the circular array that crosses the seam. The slots you drop form one contiguous block of n - k in the linear array. Both views are a fixed-size sliding window.
