The story

Explain like I'm 5

A crowd of supporters from different teams walks into a stadium one by one. Whenever two fans of different teams meet, they leave together. More than half of the crowd supports one team, so even if every other fan pairs up with one of them, that team still has fans left at the end. You only need to watch who is currently "holding the gate" and how many of them are waiting there.

From your world

It is like a quorum vote in a replicated system. If more than half of the replicas report the same value, any pairing of disagreeing votes cancels out and the quorum value survives. Boyer-Moore is the streaming version of "find the value that has a strict majority" with one register and one counter, which is why it also appears in heavy-hitter sketches for network flows.
