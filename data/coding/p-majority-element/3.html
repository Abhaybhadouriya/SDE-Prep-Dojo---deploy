Recognise the pattern

Cue: "appears more than n/2 times" (or more than n/k) plus "O(1) space" → Boyer-Moore voting. Without the space limit it is a plain frequency count with a hash map.

Try a few: cancel one copy of the leader against each different value.

numsAfter cancelling pairs of different valuesSurvivor = majority?
[2, 3, 2](2,3) cancel, 2 left✓ 2
[5, 5, 9, 9, 5](5,9), (5,9) cancel, 5 left✓ 5
[1, 2, 3](1,2) cancel, 3 left✗ no majority exists, the survivor is meaningless

The third row is why the guarantee matters: without it, a second pass must count the survivor.
