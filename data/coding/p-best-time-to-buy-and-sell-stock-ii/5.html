Dry run

Greedy on [4,9,2,6,8]. Green arrows are rises we collect, red arrows are drops we skip.

  1. Prices for five days: 4, 9, 2, 6, 8. We may trade every day but hold at most one share. Compare each day with the day before.
  2. Up-step of 5: buy on day 0, sell on day 1 (or keep holding). Add it: profit = 5.
  3. Down-step of -7: holding through it would lose money, so we are out of the market. Skip it; profit stays 5.
  4. Up-step of 4: buy on day 2, sell on day 3 (or keep holding). Add it: profit = 9.
  5. Up-step of 2: buy on day 3, sell on day 4 (or keep holding). Add it: profit = 11. Final answer 11: the trades (4 -> 9) and (2 -> 8). The rises 2 -> 6 and 6 -> 8 add up to the single trade 2 -> 8.
