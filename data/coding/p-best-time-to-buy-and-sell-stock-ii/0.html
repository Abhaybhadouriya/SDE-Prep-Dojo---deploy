Explain like I'm 5

You can see tomorrow's price of your favourite sweet. If it will cost more tomorrow, buy one today and sell it tomorrow. If it will cost less, do nothing. Doing this every day earns as much as any clever plan, because a long climb is just many small climbs glued together.

Stock I (one trade) is a running minimum. Stock II removes the trade limit, and the interview question becomes: can you justify the greedy? Interviewers expect the telescoping argument, and good candidates also show the cash/hold DP, because the follow-ups (fee, cooldown, k trades) are all small edits of it.
