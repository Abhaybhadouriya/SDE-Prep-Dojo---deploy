The problem (in our words)

You get an integer array prices where prices[i] is the price of one share on day i. On every day you may buy one share, sell the share you hold, or do nothing; you may hold at most one share at any time, and you may buy and sell on the same day. Return the maximum total profit you can make.

Signature: int maxProfit(int[] prices).

  • 1 <= prices.length <= 3 * 104
  • 0 <= prices[i] <= 104

Practice it: LeetCode 122 · GfG Stock Buy and Sell - multiple transactions · TUF article

Constraints tell you: n up to 3·104 means an O(n²) pair search (about 4.5·108 pairs) is too slow; O(n) is expected. The maximum profit is below 3·104 · 104 = 3·108, which fits a 32-bit int. A single day gives 0.
