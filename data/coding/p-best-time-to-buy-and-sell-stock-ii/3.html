Recognise the pattern

Cue: "buy/sell stock" + "as many transactions as you like" + no fee/cooldown -> sum the positive differences. Any extra rule (fee, cooldown, at most k trades) -> the cash/hold state machine with that rule added.

The stock family is the standard introduction to state-machine DP: one state per "what do I hold right now", transitions for buy, sell and rest.
