Common mistakes

Solving Stock I instead. Tracking only the minimum so far and the best single sale returns 6 for [4,9,2,6,8] (2 -> 8) and misses the 4 -> 9 trade; the answer is 11. Read "as many transactions as you like".

Thinking the greedy really trades every day. It does not need to: the sum equals the valley-to-peak trades. If the interviewer asks for the actual transactions, merge consecutive rising days into one (buy at the start of the run, sell at its end).

DP update order. Updating cash and then using the new cash for hold means "sell and buy back the same day", which is harmless here but wrong for the cooldown variant. Compute both from the old values.

Initialising hold = 0. That pretends you start with a free share. Use -prices[0] or minus infinity.
