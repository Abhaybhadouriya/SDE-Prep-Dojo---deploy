The story

Explain like I'm 5

A bakery has one oven and a list of trays to bake, each tray marked with a recipe letter. After a tray of one recipe, the same recipe must wait a few turns, because its mould has to cool. Any other recipe can go in meanwhile; if nothing else is ready, the oven sits empty for a turn. The recipe with the most trays is the problem: it sets how many rounds you need, and everything else is squeezed into the gaps between its trays.

From your world

This is a scheduler with a per-flow minimum gap: like rate-limiting a NIC queue so one flow can send at most once every n + 1 slots, or a timer wheel where a job re-arms n ticks after it runs. The heaviest flow decides how long the whole batch takes; light flows fill the holes.
