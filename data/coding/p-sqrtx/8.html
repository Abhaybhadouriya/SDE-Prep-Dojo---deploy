Common mistakes

mid * mid in an int. With hi = x the first mid is about 109; its square wraps around in C++/Java and the comparison goes the wrong way. Use long long / long, or compare mid <= x / mid.

Rounding mid down with lo = mid. On a two-element range the loop repeats forever. Either round up (our version) or use the "lo <= hi, record answer" style below.

Returning lo in the while (lo <= hi) style. In that style the loop ends with hi = lo - 1, and hi is the answer (or keep ans = mid whenever mid fits). Pick one style and know where its answer ends up.

(int)sqrt(x) "to be safe". It breaks the rule of the problem, and for 64-bit inputs a double cannot hold every integer, so it can be off by one near perfect squares.
