Examples

Input pathOutputWhy
"/music/""/music"Trailing slash dropped.
"/srv//logs///today/""/srv/logs/today"Runs of slashes collapse to one.
"/opt/./app/../../tmp/x""/tmp/x""." does nothing; the two ".." remove app and then opt.
"/../""/"There is nothing above the root.
"/a/.../b/..""/a/...""..." is an ordinary name; the final ".." removes b.
