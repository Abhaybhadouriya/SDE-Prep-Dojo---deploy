The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are walking into a building with nested rooms. A note gives directions: "go into Opt, stay where you are, go into App, go back out, go back out, go into Tmp". You keep a trail of the rooms you are inside. Going into a room adds it to the trail; going back out removes the last one; "stay" does nothing. At the end, the trail is where you are standing.

The trail is a stack: ".." always cancels the most recent directory, never an older one. That "latest first" cancellation is the same shape as matching brackets or backspace in a text editor.

From your world

This is the lexical half of realpath(3) / the kernel's path walk: components are separated by one or more slashes, . is the current dentry, .. is the parent, and .. at / stays at /. The difference: no symlinks are resolved here, so it is pure string processing (like Go's path.Clean).
