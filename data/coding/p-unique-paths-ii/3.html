Recognise the pattern

Cue: "count the paths" + "moves only right/down" (a DAG on a grid) -> grid DP: ways[cell] = sum over the cells you can come from. Blocked cell -> 0.

Moves only go right or down, so there are no cycles and row-major order is a topological order: when you reach a cell, both of its predecessors are already final. That is exactly what makes a single sweep correct. If moves could go in all four directions (Unique Paths III), the graph has cycles and you need backtracking instead.
