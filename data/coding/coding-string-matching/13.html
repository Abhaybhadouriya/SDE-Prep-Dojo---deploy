Revision card

  • Cue: Find a pattern in text in O(n + m), repeated substring, shortest palindrome, rotation check.
  • Insight: A border (prefix that is also a suffix) tells you how far to fall back without moving the text index back.
  • State: pi[] (KMP) or z[] (Z-function) or a rolling hash mod M.
  • Trap: Falling back with k = pi[k] instead of pi[k - 1]; trusting a hash hit without verifying.
  • Cost: O(n + m) time; O(m) space.

More to remember

  • pi[i] = longest border of pat[0..i]. Fall back with k = pi[k-1]; borders of borders are borders.
  • KMP: the text index never moves back; O(n + m) by the "k rises at most n times" argument. After a full match, k = pi[m-1].
  • Z: z[i] = match length of s[i..] with s; reuse the Z-box with min(r - i, z[i - l]).
  • Rolling hash: subtract, shift, add, all mod M. Verify on hits; double hash when hashes are identity; random base.
  • Shortest palindrome: pi of s + "#" + reverse(s). Period: p = n - pi[n-1]. Rotation: search in s + s. Repeat match: q or q + 1.
  • Distinct substrings: trie or hash per length O(n2); name suffix array / automaton for the follow-up.
  • Streams: KMP, carry k across chunks.
