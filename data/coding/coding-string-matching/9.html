How to recognise a string matching problem

  • The words "prefix that is also a suffix", "border", "happy prefix", "repeated pattern", "period": prefix function, read pi[n-1].
  • "Add characters in front to make a palindrome" or "longest palindromic prefix": pi of s + "#" + reverse(s).
  • "Rotation", "repeat a until b fits", "cyclic": search inside a doubled or repeated string.
  • "For every position i, how long does it match the start?": Z-function.
  • "Longest substring that appears twice", "how many distinct", "count equal pairs of substrings": rolling hash, often with binary search on the length (if a length-L repeat exists, so does every shorter one).
  • A stream or text larger than memory: KMP, because its whole state is one integer k plus the pattern's pi.
  • Not this page: "longest substring without repeating characters" and "anagram of p inside s" are sliding window problems with a count table, even though they sound like matching.
