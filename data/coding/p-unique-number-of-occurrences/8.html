Common mistakes

Checking the values instead of the counts. "Are all elements unique?" is Contains Duplicate. Here values may repeat freely; only their tallies must differ.

Indexing an array with a negative value. freq[v] with v = -3 is out of bounds in C++/Java (and silently wraps in Python). Shift by +1000 or use a map.

Comparing only adjacent counts without sorting them. Counts [2, 3, 2] have no equal neighbours but do contain a duplicate.

Deciding during the counting pass. A count that is unique after half the array may clash later; finish phase 1 before checking.
