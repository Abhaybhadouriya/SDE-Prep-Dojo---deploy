How to recognise it:

  • "change at most one cell", "flip one 0", "add one edge / one link" and maximise a component size.
  • n up to 500 or 1000 on a grid: anything per-cell must be O(1) after a precomputation.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 200 Number of Islands, 695 Max Area of Island.
  2. This problem with painting; then rewrite it with union-find.
  3. Harder: 934 Shortest Bridge (BFS from a painted island), 305 Number of Islands II (online DSU).
