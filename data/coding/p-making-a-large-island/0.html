Explain like I'm 5

A square map has land squares and water squares. You are allowed to pour sand into exactly one water square and turn it into land. Where should you pour to make the biggest connected piece of land? First, colour each existing island with its own crayon and write its size on a sticky note. Then for each water square, look at the up to four squares touching it, collect the different crayon colours you see, and add up their sticky notes plus one for the new square.

The catch that makes this Hard: the same island often touches a water square from two sides. Count it twice and your answer is too big. The fix is a tiny set of at most four ids per water square.

From your world

It is "which single link, if added, merges the largest set of network segments?" First label every connected segment once (like a flood of ARP or a spanning-tree pass) and store its size; then each candidate link is scored by summing the distinct segment labels at its ends. Labelling once and reusing the labels is the whole speed-up.
