The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are playing a crossword and you know a word is "c ? t", with the middle letter smudged. You go to the dictionary page for c, then look at every second letter that exists after c (a, o, u...), and check whether any of those continues with t. You never look at words that start with a different letter. A trie is that dictionary, organised letter by letter.

From your world

It is a radix tree lookup, like the kernel's routing or page-cache radix trees: each level consumes one key symbol. A wildcard symbol turns the single walk into a bounded fan-out, similar to matching a topic filter with + in MQTT, or a route lookup that must try several masks. The search keeps a recursion stack of at most L frames.
