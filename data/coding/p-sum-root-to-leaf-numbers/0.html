The story

Explain like I'm 5

A maze of corridors splits like a tree, and every door you pass has one digit painted on it. When you reach a dead end, the digits you passed, in order, are your ticket number. Every dead end gives one ticket. What do all the tickets add up to? You do not need to remember the whole list of doors: just keep the ticket number so far in your head, and each new door shifts it one place left and adds the new digit.

This is the cleanest example of top-down state in a tree: information flows from the root to the leaves as a function argument, and the answer flows back up as a return value.

From your world

It is how you parse a decimal number from a byte stream: n = n * 10 + (c - '0') per character. Each root-to-leaf path is one such stream, and the recursion keeps one accumulator per open path on the stack.
