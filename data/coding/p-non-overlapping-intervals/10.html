How to recognise it:

  • "Remove the fewest / keep the most / schedule the most" intervals with no overlap.
  • A single exclusive resource (one room, one machine, one arrow line).
  • Intervals with weights or costs: the greedy breaks, switch to DP + binary search.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 2446, then LC 56 (sort and scan).
  2. This problem, then LC 452 and LC 646 (same greedy, different count and touching rule).
  3. LC 253 (heap) and LC 1353 (sweep with a heap).
