Explain like I'm 5

One meeting room, many booking requests, and the room can hold only one meeting at a time. You must cancel some bookings so the rest never clash, and you want to cancel as few as possible. The trick: whenever you have to choose, keep the meeting that finishes earliest. It frees the room soonest, so it can never block more later meetings than any other choice would.

From your world

It is admission control on a single exclusive resource, like granting non-overlapping DMA windows or time slots on a TDMA link. The classic "earliest deadline finishes first" rule from interval scheduling is exactly what a slot allocator does when it wants to admit the maximum number of reservations.
