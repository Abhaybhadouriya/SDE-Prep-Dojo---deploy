The problem (in our words)

A meeting room has a list of booking requests intervals; entry i is the pair [start_i, end_i]. Cancel as few bookings as possible so that no two of the kept bookings overlap, and report how many you cancelled.

  • Input: a 2-D integer array intervals of pairs [start, end].
  • Output: an integer, the fewest intervals to remove. Signature: int eraseOverlapIntervals(int[][] intervals).
  • Two intervals that only touch at a point do not overlap: [1, 2] and [2, 3] can both stay.

Constraints

  • 1 <= intervals.length <= 10^5
  • intervals[i].length == 2
  • -5 * 10^4 <= start_i < end_i <= 5 * 10^4

Practice it: LeetCode 435 - Non-overlapping Intervals · GfG - Non-Overlapping Intervals · takeuforward

Constraints tell you: n up to 10^5 rules out the O(n²) DP; one sort plus one scan (O(n log n)) is the target. Every start is strictly less than its end, so there are no empty intervals. Coordinates fit easily in a 32-bit int.
