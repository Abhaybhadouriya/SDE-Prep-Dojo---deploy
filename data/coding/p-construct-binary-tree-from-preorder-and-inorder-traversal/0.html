The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two friends describe the same family tree. The first always says a parent before any of its children, left family first. The second reads the tree left to right, so a parent stands exactly between its left family and its right family. Take the first name in friend one's list: that is the head of the family. Find that name in friend two's list: everyone to its left is the left family, everyone to its right is the right family. Now do the same for each family, and the tree draws itself.

This is divide and conquer on index ranges. The only thing that makes it O(n) instead of O(n2) is finding the root in inorder in O(1), which a value-to-index map gives you because the values are distinct.

From your world

It is a deserializer with two partial encodings. Preorder alone is like a TLV stream with the length fields stripped: you know what comes first but not where a sub-block ends. Inorder supplies the missing lengths, since the root's position in it tells you how many nodes the left subtree has.
