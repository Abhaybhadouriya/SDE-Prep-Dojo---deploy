Recognise the pattern

Cue: a set of [start, end] ranges + "any overlap?" / "merge" / "how many at once" -> sort by start, then scan neighbours.

Cue 2: "can one person / one resource do all" = "maximum overlap is 1". If the question becomes "how many resources", it is Meeting Rooms II (sweep line or heap).

Try a few pairs: two ranges [a, b) and [c, d) overlap exactly when a < d and c < b.
