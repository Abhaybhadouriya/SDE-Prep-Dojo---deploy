Why does "t33l" need no special check for "adjacent replacements"?

Because adjacent numbers cannot be told apart in the text: "33" is read as thirty-three. The parser reads maximal digit runs, which is exactly the meaning the problem gives. For an 8-letter word, 1 + 33 + 1 letters do not fit, so the answer is false naturally.

How to answer
