The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a word written on a strip of paper and a short note like "k2n1l". You read the note with one finger and the strip with another. A letter on the note means "the strip must show this letter here"; a number means "cover that many letters with your thumb and keep going". If every letter agrees and both fingers reach the end together, the note really is a short form of the word.

From your world

It is a run-length style decoder checked against a known buffer. Literal bytes must match the buffer; a count field advances the buffer offset without reading. The classic decoder bugs apply too: a zero-length or padded count field ("01") is malformed, and an offset that runs past the buffer end must be rejected, not wrapped.
