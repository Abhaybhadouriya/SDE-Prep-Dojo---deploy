How to recognise it:

  • Sorted inputs and an order statistic (median, k-th, percentile) with a logarithmic bound.
  • A split whose correctness can be judged locally (by comparing the values next to the split).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 88 merge, then the merge-walk median.
  2. This problem: the cut search; then the k-th element variant on GfG.
  3. Then LC 378 (value search) and LC 295 (two heaps), then LC 2040.
