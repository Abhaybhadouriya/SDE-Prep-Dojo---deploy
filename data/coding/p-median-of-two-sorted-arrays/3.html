Recognise the pattern

When you see "two sorted arrays" + "k-th smallest / median / percentile" + "O(log)" required, think binary search on a partition: search a split position whose validity is monotone (too far left / just right / too far right).

Try a few cuts of a = [7, 12, 14, 15] with b = [1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 11]. The left half must hold 5 numbers, so taking i from a forces j = 5 - i from b.

i (from a)j (from b)a[i-1] ≤ b[j]?b[j-1] ≤ a[i]?So
05yes (-inf)no (9 > 7)too few from a: move right
14yes (7 ≤ 9)yes (4 ≤ 12)valid cut
23no (12 > 4)yestoo many from a: move left
32no (14 > 3)yestoo many

The answers go "too few, valid, too many, too many": a monotone predicate, which is exactly what binary search needs.
