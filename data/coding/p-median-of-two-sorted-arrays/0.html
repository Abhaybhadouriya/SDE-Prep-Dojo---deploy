The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two classes stand in two lines, each sorted by height. The teacher wants the child of middle height across both classes, without making everyone re-line-up. Trick: pick a few children from the front of line A and the rest from the front of line B so that together they are exactly half of all children. If the tallest child you picked is no taller than the shortest child you did not pick, you have found the "short half", and the middle height sits right at its edge.

From your world

Think of two sorted per-CPU latency histograms that you must report a combined p50 for. Merging them is O(m + n) work. The cut method answers "how many samples from CPU A are below the global median?" with a binary search, which is how you would find a combined percentile across shards without copying data.
