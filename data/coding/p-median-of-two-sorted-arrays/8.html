Common mistakes

Searching the longer array. Then j = half - i can be negative or larger than n and you read out of bounds. Swap so that a is the shorter one.

Integer division for the even case. (x + y) / 2 in C++/Java/Go truncates: [1, 2] gives 1 instead of 1.5. Divide by 2.0 or cast first.

Using hi = m - 1. The cut position ranges over 0..m (m + 1 choices, "take all of a" included). With hi = m - 1, a = [1, 2], b = [3, 4] never finds i = 2.

Two separate formulas for odd and even. Using half = (m + n) / 2 makes the odd case read the right side, and people then patch it with off-by-one fixes. (m + n + 1) / 2 puts the median on the left for odd totals.

Sentinels that collide with data. Using 0 or -1 as "empty" breaks on negative readings. Use INT_MIN / INT_MAX (safe here because readings are at most 10^6 in size) or real infinities.
