The problem (in our words)

Two sensors log readings, and each log is already sorted in non-decreasing order: a with m readings and b with n readings. Return the median of all m + n readings together, as if the two logs were merged into one sorted list. If the total count is odd, the median is the middle reading; if it is even, it is the average of the two middle readings.

  • Input: two sorted integer arrays a (length m) and b (length n). Either one may be empty, but not both.
  • Output: the median as a floating-point number (LeetCode accepts an answer within 1e-5). Signature: double findMedianSortedArrays(int[] nums1, int[] nums2).
  • Required: the overall running time must be O(log(m + n)).

Constraints

  • nums1.length == m, nums2.length == n
  • 0 <= m <= 1000, 0 <= n <= 1000, 1 <= m + n <= 2000
  • -10^6 <= nums1[i], nums2[i] <= 10^6

Constraints tell you: with m + n ≤ 2000 the O(m + n) merge would pass the judge, but the statement demands a logarithmic bound, and that is what the interviewer is testing. Values fit in 32 bits and the sum of two of them (2 × 10^6) too, so no overflow when averaging. Empty arrays are legal: you need sentinels for "nothing on this side".
