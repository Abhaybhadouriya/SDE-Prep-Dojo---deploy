The story

Explain like I'm 5

A car's odometer shows 3899 km. You drive one more km. The last wheel rolls from 9 to 0 and nudges its neighbour; that one also rolls from 9 to 0 and nudges the next; the 8 rolls to 9 and nothing else moves. Now it shows 3900. The wheels on the left never moved, because the nudge stopped at the first wheel that was not showing 9.

That is the whole problem: the +1 is a carry that travels left through a run of 9s. Everything else in the number stays as it is, so you can stop early, often after one digit.

From your world

It is a ripple-carry increment on a register stored one decimal digit per byte, least significant byte last. The carry chain length is the number of trailing 9s, and the only overflow case (all 9s) widens the register by one digit, just like a counter that needs one more bit when it wraps.
