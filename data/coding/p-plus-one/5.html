Dry run

On [3,8,9,9] (the odometer). Red = a 9 that rolls over, green = the digit that absorbs the carry, blue = digits already settled.

  1. We add 1 at the last digit: think of it as a carry of 1 arriving from the right.
  2. digits[3] is 9: 9 + 1 = 10, so write 0 and pass the carry of 1 to the left.
  3. digits[2] is 9: 9 + 1 = 10, so write 0 and pass the carry of 1 to the left.
  4. digits[1] is 8 < 9: it becomes 9 and the carry dies here. Return at once; the digits to the left (3) are never touched. Answer [3,9,0,0].
