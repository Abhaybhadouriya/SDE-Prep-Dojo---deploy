Recognise the pattern

Cue: a number given as an array or string of digits, "too long for a built-in integer" -> schoolbook arithmetic from the least significant end with a carry. Adding one is the simplest case: the carry is either 0 or 1 and dies at the first digit below 9.

Try a few and the rule appears:
