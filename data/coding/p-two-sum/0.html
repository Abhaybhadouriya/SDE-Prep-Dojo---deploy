The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a 10-rupee coin to spend on exactly two sweets. You walk along a shelf. For each sweet you think: "if I buy this one, I need a partner that costs exactly what is left". Instead of walking back to look, you keep a notebook of every price you have passed and where. One look in the notebook tells you if the partner was already on the shelf.

From your world

The map is a lookup table keyed by the value you need, like a routing table keyed by destination: you do not scan every entry, you index straight to it. The "complement" key is computed per packet (per element) and the table is filled as packets go by.
