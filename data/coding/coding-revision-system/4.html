Fresh, warm, soon, cold: the health colour

The ladder handles single problems. For whole lessons and for a quick view of a group, the site uses a simpler signal: how many days since you last revised it. It shows up on module home pages, on your profile and in the coding home.

Health and the ladder answer different questions. The ladder says which problem to re-solve today. Health says which topic is drifting. If your Graphs lesson is Cold, reread its "how to recognise" table and templates even if no single graph problem is due.
