Remember this

Revision card

  • Cue: A problem you solved once and will need in four weeks.
  • Insight: Recall before you check; spaced reviews slow forgetting.
  • State: one five-line card per problem; gaps 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 (then 60, 120); Again / Hard / Good / Easy.
  • Trap: Re-reading instead of recalling; adding new problems when more than 25 are due.
  • Cost: 1-2 minutes per card; full re-solve at the 7-day rung.

More to remember

  • Most forgetting happens in the first days; each well-timed review slows it.
  • Recall first, then check. Re-reading is not revision.
  • One card per problem, five lines: pattern, trigger, insight, template, traps + cost.
  • Gaps of 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days (then 60, 120). Again = back to 1, Hard = same, Good = next, Easy = skip one.
  • Card reviews take 1-2 minutes; full re-solve at the 7-day rung and after any Hard or Again.
  • Health: fresh 0-7, warm 8-14, soon 15-29, cold 30+ days.
  • Reviews before new problems; more than 25 due means no new problems today.
  • On a failure: time box, read actively, classify, fix the card, solve a sibling.
