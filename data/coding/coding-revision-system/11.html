Common mistakes

  • Re-reading instead of recalling. Looking at your old solution feels productive and teaches almost nothing. Always try first.
  • Cards that are whole solutions. A 60-line card cannot be reviewed in a minute. Five lines, one sentence each.
  • Grading kindly. If you peeked, it is Hard. If you had a bug you did not catch, it is Again. Generous grades push weak cards out to 30 days and you meet them again in the interview instead.
  • Skipping days, then binge-reviewing. Fifteen minutes daily beats two hours on Sunday, because the short gaps (1 and 3 days) are where most of the forgetting happens.
  • Only new problems for weeks. Without revision, problem count goes up while the number you can actually solve cold stays flat.
  • Repeating a failed card unchanged. Find out why it failed and change the card; otherwise it fails again.
  • Memorising code. The card holds the idea and the template shape. If you can only reproduce the exact code, a small twist in the interview breaks it.
