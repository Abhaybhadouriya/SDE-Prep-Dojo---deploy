How much revision per day

Reviews pile up because every new problem brings about five reviews in its first two months. We simulated the 12-week plan (about 22 new problems a week) on this exact ladder. If every review is Good, card reviews reach about 10 a day by week 4 and about 15 by week 11. If one review in five is Again, which is closer to real life early on, it is about 15 a day by week 4 and about 25 by week 11. Full re-solves at the 7-day rung stay near 3 a day.

~10-15card reviews / day by week 4 (sim)
~15-25card reviews / day by week 11 (sim)
~3full re-solves / day at the 7-day rung (all Good)
20-40 mindaily revision budget

Two rules keep this under control:

  1. Reviews first, new problems second. A new problem you will forget is worth less than an old one you keep.
  2. The 25 rule. If more than 25 cards are due, add no new problems today; clear the queue. A long queue means you are learning faster than you can keep.
