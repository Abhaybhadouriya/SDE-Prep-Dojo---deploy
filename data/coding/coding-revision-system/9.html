How to recognise a problem that is going cold

The schedule catches most decay, but watch for these cues during practice and mocks. Each one means "re-solve this group this week", whatever the dates say.

CueWhat it meansDo this
You recognise the title but cannot say the insight in one sentence.You remembered the story, not the idea.Rewrite line 3 of the card; full re-solve.
You reach for the card before trying.Recognition, not recall.Hide the card; say the hook out loud first, always.
An unseen problem from a group you "finished" takes over 10 minutes to classify.The triggers (line 2) are weak for that group.Reread the lesson's "how to recognise" table; do two unseen problems from the group.
You keep making the same bug (off-by-one in binary search, not skipping duplicates).The template is not in your fingers yet.Type the lesson template from memory three days running.
Your Hard and Again grades cluster in one group.That group is your weak spot.Filter Revise by that group; schedule its lesson for this week.
A lesson shows Revise soon or Cold.You have not touched the topic in 15+ days.Reread its templates and mistakes, then press Revised today.
