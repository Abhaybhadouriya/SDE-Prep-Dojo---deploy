Recognise the pattern

Cue: intervals + a weight per interval + "maximise total weight of non-overlapping ones" -> sort by end, take-or-skip DP, binary search for the last compatible interval. If there were no weights (maximise the count), greedy by earliest end would be enough.

Why greedy fails once weights appear:
