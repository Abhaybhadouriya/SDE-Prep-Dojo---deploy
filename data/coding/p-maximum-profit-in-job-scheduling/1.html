The problem (in our words)

There are n jobs. Job i runs from startTime[i] to endTime[i] and pays profit[i]. Choose a set of jobs such that no two chosen jobs overlap in time, and return the maximum total profit. A job that ends at time X does not overlap a job that starts at time X: you may finish one and start the next at the same moment.

Signature: int jobScheduling(int[] startTime, int[] endTime, int[] profit).

Constraints:

  • 1 <= startTime.length == endTime.length == profit.length <= 5 * 104
  • 1 <= startTime[i] < endTime[i] <= 109
  • 1 <= profit[i] <= 104

Practice it: LeetCode 1235 · GfG Weighted Job Scheduling · GfG article

Constraints tell you: n = 5 x 104 rules out O(n2) (2.5 x 109), so aim for O(n log n). Times go up to 109, so a DP indexed by time is impossible without compression; index by job instead. The total profit is at most 5 x 104 x 104 = 5 x 108, which fits a 32-bit int.
