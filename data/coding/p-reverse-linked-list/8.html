Common mistakes

Flipping before saving. cur.next = prev; cur = cur.next moves cur backwards to prev, and the rest of the list is lost (a memory leak in C, an infinite loop or early stop elsewhere).

Returning head or cur. After the loop head is the new tail and cur is null. The new head is prev.

Recursive version without head.next = null. The old first node keeps pointing at the second, and the second points back at it: a cycle of two at the end of the list.

Reversing values instead of links. Swapping values with an array or stack passes the judge but fails the real requirement when nodes carry other data or are referenced elsewhere.
