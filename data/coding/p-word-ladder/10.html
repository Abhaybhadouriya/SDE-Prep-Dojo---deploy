How to recognise it:

  • "Minimum number of moves / transformations" from a start state to a target state.
  • Each move is a small local change, all moves cost the same, and the state space is too big to list but easy to step through.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 433 Minimum Genetic Mutation (same BFS, small alphabet).
  2. This problem: one-sided BFS first, then bidirectional; compare their speed on a big random list.
  3. Then: 752 Open the Lock, 773 Sliding Puzzle, and 126 Word Ladder II (all shortest paths).
