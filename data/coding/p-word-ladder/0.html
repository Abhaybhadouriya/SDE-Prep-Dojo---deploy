The story

Explain like I'm 5

A word puzzle: turn COLD into WARM by changing one letter at a time, and every step must be a real word from the dictionary. COLD, CORD, CARD, WARD, WARM works. What is the fewest words you need? Imagine dropping a stone in a pond at COLD: the first ripple reaches every word one change away, the second ripple every word two changes away, and so on. The ripple that first touches WARM tells you the answer.

That ripple is breadth-first search. The real work is (1) finding "one change away" quickly, without comparing every pair of words, and (2) stopping the ripples from growing too wide, by sending one from each end.

From your world

This is hop-count routing: every link costs 1, so plain BFS (not Dijkstra) gives shortest paths, the same as a distance-vector protocol counting hops. The two-ended search is like two hosts flooding a route discovery towards each other (AODV-style) and stopping at the first node that has heard from both.
