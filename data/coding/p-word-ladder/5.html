Dry run

Example 1: cold -> warm. Grey lines are the one-letter edges (never built in code, drawn only to explain). The legend under the picture shows which colour marks words claimed from the cold side and which from the warm side.

  1. Start. warm is in the list, so a ladder may exist. One frontier starts at each end; remove both words from the unused set.
  2. Sizes 1 and 1: grow the begin side. The only unused neighbour of cold is cord (change l to r). Claim it.
  3. Grow cord: card (o to a) and word (c to w) are unused, so claim both. cold is not unused any more, so it is skipped.
  4. The begin side (2 words) is now bigger than the end side (1): swap and grow from warm instead. Its neighbours ward and worm are claimed.
  5. Grow ward: changing w to c gives card, which is in the other frontier. The two searches touch: the ladder has words + 1 = 5 words (cold cord card ward warm).
