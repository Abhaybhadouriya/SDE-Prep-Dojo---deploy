Recognise the pattern

Cue: "rewire a tree in place into preorder" -> hang the right subtree on the rightmost node of the left subtree, move left to right, step right.

When you see "rearrange a tree in place into a list in some traversal order", think about which node must come right after each subtree, and rewire pointers so that the traversal order is preserved at every step.

When you see "O(1) extra space on a tree", think Morris-style: use the null right pointer of a rightmost node as a temporary link instead of a stack.

Two facts carry the whole problem. (1) In preorder, a node's right subtree comes immediately after its entire left subtree. (2) The rightmost node of the left subtree has a free right pointer, and anything hung there comes after the whole left subtree in preorder. So moving the right subtree there never changes the preorder; repeat at every node and the tree becomes the chain.
