Dry run

Even-slot fill on "kkkkllllmm" (n = 10). Highlighted slots are the ones written in that step.

  1. Count the letters: k=4, l=4, m=2. The top count 4 is at most (10+1)/2 = 5, so an answer exists. Slots marked e are even.
  2. Place the most frequent letter first, on even slots 0, 2, 4, 6. Copies of the top letter are always two apart.
  3. Continue with l on the next even slot, 8. pos becomes 10 = n, so the even slots are used up.
  4. pos wraps to 1: the rest of l goes to odd slots 1, 3, 5. Its even copy (8) and its odd copies (1, 3, 5) are far apart, because k filled the evens before them.
  5. m takes the next odd slots, 7 and 9.
  6. All 10 slots are filled: klklklkmlm. No two neighbours match. One counting pass plus one writing pass: O(n) time, 26 counters.
