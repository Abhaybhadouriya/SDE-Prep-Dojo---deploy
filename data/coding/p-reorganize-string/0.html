The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are seating children in a row and twins must not sit side by side. The biggest family is the problem: seat them first, on every second chair, leaving a gap between each. Everybody else just fills the gaps. If the biggest family has more children than there are "every second" chairs, no seating works.

Interviewers like this one because it has a feasibility proof (when is it impossible?), two clean greedy implementations, and an obvious generalisation (distance k instead of 1) that leads straight into Task Scheduler. Amazon in particular keeps asking it in SDE-2 onsites (several 2026 reports below).

From your world

Think of interleaving packets from flows so that no flow sends two back-to-back frames, like a fair-queuing scheduler. The heaviest flow decides whether a valid interleave exists, and serving it on every other slot is the classic round-robin with a dominant flow.
