Common mistakes

Filling letters in alphabetical order without placing the top letter first. "aabbb" becomes "ababb". Only the most frequent letter is dangerous at the wrap point.

Wrong bound. maxCount > n / 2 rejects "aba"-style inputs with odd n. The correct bound is (n + 1) / 2 (ceiling of n/2).

Heap version popping one letter per round and pushing it straight back: the same letter comes out again and lands next to itself. Pop two per round, or hold the previous letter out of the heap for one round.

Returning null or "impossible" instead of the empty string the problem asks for.
