Explain like I'm 5

A word game: your card says "TEA". Along a long banner of letters, circle every group of three neighbours that uses exactly T, E and A, in any order: "EAT", "ATE", "TEA" all count. You hold a cardboard frame three letters wide and move it one letter at a time. Each move drops one letter and adds one, so you only re-check those two.

From your world

It is the same as watching the last k packets on a link for a fixed "mix" (two ACKs and one SYN, say): a per-type counter, incremented on arrival and decremented when a packet ages out of the window. Here you also keep "how many types are currently at their target", so detecting the mix is one comparison per packet instead of a scan of all counters.
