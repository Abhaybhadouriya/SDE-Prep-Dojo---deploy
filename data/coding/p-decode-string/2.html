"2[k]3[mn]""kkmnmnmn"Two groups side by side: k twice, then mn three times.
"2[x3[y]]""xyyyxyyy"Nested: the inner group is "yyy", so the outer group is "xyyy", repeated twice.
"ab2[c]10[d]""abccdddddddddd"Plain letters outside any bracket are copied once; 10 is one count of two digits.
