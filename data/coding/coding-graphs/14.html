Remember this

Revision card

  • Cue: Grids, dependencies, connections, "islands", "fewest steps", accounts that share keys.
  • Insight: Name the vertices and the edges first; then one of five templates does the rest.
  • State: adjacency list, seen[] or colours, a queue (BFS) or an explicit stack (DFS).
  • Trap: Wrong edge direction for prerequisites; recursive DFS on a 10^6-cell grid; marking on pop.
  • Cost: O(V + E) for BFS, DFS, components and topological sort.

More to remember

  • First name the vertices and edges. Grids, words and lock states are implicit graphs.
  • BFS = queue = fewest steps when every move costs the same. Mark on push.
  • DFS = stack = reachability, components, cycles, order. Go iterative on big inputs.
  • Components: loop all vertices, flood each unseen one, count floods.
  • Directed cycle: three colours or Kahn's count. Undirected: parent check or union-find.
  • Dependencies: Kahn's topological sort; fewer than V output = cycle.
  • Union-find for "groups that merge over time"; Dijkstra for non-negative weights; MST for "connect all at minimum cost".

Go deeper: when this page feels easy, continue with BFS on grids and implicit graphs, Topological sort, Union-find (disjoint set union): groups that merge, Weighted shortest paths: Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd, MST, Bridges and Euler Paths.
