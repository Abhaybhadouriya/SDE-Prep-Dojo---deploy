Common mistakes

  • Marking seen on pop instead of on push. Vertices enter the queue many times; grids time out.
  • Forgetting disconnected graphs. Starting one BFS from vertex 0 misses other components. Loop over all vertices for component counts, bipartite checks and cycle detection.
  • Using the undirected cycle rule on a directed graph (or the reverse). Directed needs GRAY/BLACK or Kahn; undirected needs the parent check or union-find.
  • Recursion depth. Recursive DFS on a 106-cell grid overflows. Use an explicit stack, or raise the limit only if you know the input is small.
  • Dijkstra with negative edges, or BFS on weighted edges. Check the costs before you pick the walk.
  • Not skipping stale heap entries in lazy Dijkstra: correct answers, but the work can grow far beyond E log V.
  • Building the wrong edge direction for prerequisites. "[a, b] means b before a" in Course Schedule: the edge is b → a. Say the direction out loud.
  • Union-find without union by size or compression: a long chain makes find O(n), and the "nearly constant" claim becomes false.
