The story

Explain like I'm 5

You walk a hilly road and write down your altitude at every milestone, but only the first time you reach each altitude. A friend asks: "What is the longest stretch where you climbed exactly 4 metres overall?" At each milestone you look up "altitude now minus 4" in your notes. The first time you stood there is the farthest-back start, so that gives the longest stretch ending here.

The notes are a map from prefix sum to first index; the "climb of exactly k" is a subarray sum.

From your world

A byte counter again: bytes between two timestamps are counter[t2] - counter[t1]. To find the longest interval with exactly k net bytes (writes minus truncations, so the counter can go down), you index the counter values by the first timestamp they appeared at.
