How to recognise it:

  • "Longest / maximum length" + "sum equals k" on a contiguous run.
  • Negative numbers in the input: the window is out, the prefix map is in.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 303 Range Sum Query, 560 Subarray Sum Equals K.
  2. This problem (or the free TUF version), then 525 Contiguous Array.
  3. 523 (mod k), then 1590 Make Sum Divisible by P.
