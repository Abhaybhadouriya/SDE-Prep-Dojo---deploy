The story

Explain like I'm 5

A cleaner walks an office tower floor by floor, from the top. On the top floor she walks east, on the next floor west, then east again, so she never walks back to the same staircase. She still sees every room on a floor, she only writes them in her notebook in the order she passes them. The rooms per floor are the same as for a plain floor-by-floor walk; only the direction of each line in the notebook flips.

The trap is to flip the traversal (push right child before left on some levels). That works with two stacks, but it is easy to get wrong. Flipping only the write position keeps the plain BFS untouched.

From your world

It is a ring buffer filled from either end: you know the length of the batch (the level width) before you start, so you can place item i at i or at len-1-i with no reverse pass, like writing a descriptor ring backwards when the hardware wants it.
