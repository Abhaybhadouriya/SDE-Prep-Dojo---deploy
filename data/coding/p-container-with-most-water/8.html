Common mistakes

Moving the taller wall. Moving the taller one keeps the short wall as the cap and loses width, so the area can only fall. You skip the answer on [3,7,2,6,4,8,1,5].

Confusing it with Trapping Rain Water. Here the posts between i and j do not matter (they are thin lines, not blocks). Do not subtract inner heights or sum per column.

Width off by one. The width is r - l, not r - l + 1: posts at x = 1 and x = 7 are 6 apart.

Using max instead of min. The water spills over the shorter post.
