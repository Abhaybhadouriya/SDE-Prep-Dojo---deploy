The story

Explain like I'm 5

A fence is made of upright planks of different heights standing in a row. You may pick any two planks, slide a sheet of glass along the bottom between them, and pour water in. The water rises only as high as the shorter plank, then spills over it. Which two planks hold the most water? Far-apart planks give a wide tank; tall planks give a deep one. You want the best mix of both.

The trick is noticing that once you know a plank is the short side of a wide tank, it is useless with every closer partner: the tank gets narrower and can never get deeper than that plank. So you can throw it away without checking.

From your world

It is the same reasoning as a bandwidth-delay product with a bottleneck link: throughput is capped by the slowest hop, so upgrading anything except the bottleneck cannot help. Here the "bottleneck" is the shorter wall, and the only move that might improve the area is replacing it.
