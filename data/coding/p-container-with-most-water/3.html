Recognise the pattern

When you see "choose two indexes i < j" + the score is (distance) × (min of the two values) + a sorted order is not needed, think opposite-ends two pointers. Move the pointer at the smaller value, because that value caps every pair it is part of. If the score were a sum or the array were sorted, think Two Sum II instead.

Try a few tiny inputs and watch which wall can be dropped:

InputBestNote
[1,5,5]5Pair (1,2): 5 × 1. The wide pair (0,2) is capped at 1 × 2 = 2; post 0 is dead weight.
[5,1,5]10The widest pair wins when its walls are tall.
[2,3,10,2]4The widest pair (0,3) wins with 2 × 3 = 6, although 10 is the tallest post: a tall post with short partners does not help.
