The 12 weeks at a glance

The plan adds 236 new problems in 11 weeks, all from the Dojo Curated 450. That is a little over half of Tier A, chosen so that every pattern gets its core problems. Week 12 adds nothing new: it is for mocks and your target companies.

236new Tier A problems in 11 weeks
3-5new problems per study day
6 + 1study days + one revision-only day per week
~8timed mock rounds across weeks 5, 9, 12

Time cost: about 2 hours on a working day and 2.5-3 hours on Saturday, 1-1.5 hours on Sunday. That is roughly 15 hours a week. If you have only 8-10 hours, stretch the plan to 18 weeks at the same daily count of problems per session; do not squeeze the revision out.
