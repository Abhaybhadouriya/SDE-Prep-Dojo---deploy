Common mistakes

  • Starting with DP or graphs because they "matter most". Without recursion from trees, DP looks like magic and you memorise instead of understanding.
  • Chasing problem count. 236 problems you can redo beats 500 you have seen once. The revision system is half the plan, not an extra.
  • Skipping the mock weeks. Solving alone with unlimited time is a different skill. Most people who "know" the patterns fail their first timed round on communication, not on algorithms.
  • Catching up after missed days. Doing 12 problems on Saturday to make up for the week means 12 shallow problems and a review spike. Slide the plan instead.
  • Spending 2 hours on one problem. 25 minutes for a Medium, 40 for a Hard, then read the idea and finish it yourself.
  • Changing language mid-plan. Pick one interview language in week 1 (see the toolkit) and stay with it.
  • Only easy wins. If a week has no Mediums that hurt, you are revising, not learning. Keep the Easy share under about a quarter after week 2.
