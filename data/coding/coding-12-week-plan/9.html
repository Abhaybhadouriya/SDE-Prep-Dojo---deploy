How to recognise you are ahead, on track, or behind

Check these cues every Sunday during the retro. They matter more than the calendar.

CueWhat it meansDo this
You classify most unseen problems in a group within 3-5 minutes.The group's triggers are in place.On track. Move on, even if some Hards are left.
Mediums from this week's group take over 40 minutes, every time.The lesson idea has not landed yet.Re-read the template section, redo 2 anchors, then keep going with the next week's lesson at 2-3 a day.
More than 25 cards due on most days.Learning faster than you keep.Two revision-only days; then continue. Do not drop the reviews.
You missed 3+ days.Normal life.Do not try to catch up the missed counts. Clear the queue, then resume at today's row. The plan slides by those days.
Easy problems in weeks 1-4 take under 10 minutes.You are ahead (common for C engineers).Skip the Easies after the first two of each group; take an extra Medium instead.
A mock went badly on one pattern.A real weak spot, found early.Next week: that pattern's lesson + 4 extra problems from it, before new material.
Mock score 3+ on all four axes twice in a row.Interview-ready for that level.Book the real interview; keep revising and do company lists.
