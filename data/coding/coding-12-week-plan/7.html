The 4-week crash plan

Use this when an interview is booked 4-5 weeks out and you already know a language well. It keeps the same dependency order but takes only the highest-value problems: 100 new in four weeks, about 4-5 a day, with mocks from week 2. It costs about 3 hours a day. It will not make you strong at every pattern; it makes you safe on the common ones.

Crash rules: only the 1, 3 and 7-day rungs of the ladder will fire, so do them all; skip Hard problems unless they are in the top of your target company's list; and read the lesson's template and "how to recognise" table, not the whole lesson. A good source for "highest value" is the overlap of Blind 75, NeetCode 150 and the company list; the sheets guide explains each list.
