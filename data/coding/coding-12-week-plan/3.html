From your world

Weeks 1-4 are the part you will find fastest. Pointer walks, ring buffers, in-place array tricks and intrusive lists are your daily bread in C. Do not skip them, but do let yourself move quickly: if an Easy takes you under 10 minutes, write its card and move on. Spend the saved time in weeks 6-11, where recursion-heavy code (trees, backtracking, DP) is less common in kernel work.
