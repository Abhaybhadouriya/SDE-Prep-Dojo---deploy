How much revision the plan creates

We ran the plan through the revision ladder (gaps 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 120 days) in a small simulation: each study day adds that day's new problems, and every problem comes back when it is due. Two cases: every review graded Good, and one review in five graded Again (back to a 1-day gap, averaged over 200 random runs). The chart shows the average number of card reviews per day in each week.

This is why the revision system has its 25 rule: if more than 25 cards are due, skip new problems that day. In the 1-in-5 case you will hit that a few times in weeks 10-11. That is the plan working, not failing: it means you are keeping what you learned.
