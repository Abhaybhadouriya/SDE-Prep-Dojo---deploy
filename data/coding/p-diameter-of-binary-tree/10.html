How to recognise it:

  • "Longest path", "maximum path", "between any two nodes", "may not pass through the root".
  • The answer at a node combines both children, but the parent can only continue one of them.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 104 Maximum Depth, 110 Balanced Binary Tree.
  2. This problem: one pass with a global best; check against the brute force on random trees.
  3. Harder: 687 Longest Univalue Path, then 124 Maximum Path Sum, then 2246 on a general tree.
