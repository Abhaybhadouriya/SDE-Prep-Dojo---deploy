The story

Explain like I'm 5

Children walk past a window one by one, and you must always say who is the tallest child you can see. When a tall child comes, every shorter child in front of them stops mattering: they leave the window earlier and they are shorter, so they will never be "the tallest" again. You only remember the children who are taller than everyone who came after them. The first one in that line is the answer.

From your world

TCP BBR needs "the maximum delivery rate over the last few round trips". Linux does this in lib/win_minmax.c (Kathleen Nichols' windowed min/max filter), which keeps only three samples and gives an approximate answer. The monotonic deque is the exact version of the same idea: drop any sample that is beaten by a newer one.
