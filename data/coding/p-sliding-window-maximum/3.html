Recognise the pattern

When you see "fixed-size window" + "max or min of each window", think monotonic deque of indices.

When you see a DP of the form "dp[i] = best of dp[j] for j in the last k positions, plus something", think the same deque over dp values (LC 1696, 1425).

A sum or a count can be updated when one value enters and one leaves. A maximum cannot: when the maximum leaves, you do not know the next one. So you must keep the candidates for "next maximum" in order. The candidates are exactly the values that have no larger value to their right inside the window.

Pattern: Sliding window → fixed window → window max · Lessons: Monotonic deque, Sliding window
