The problem (in our words)

A sensor array reports an integer list readings of length n. You may perform at most k operations; one operation picks any index and increases the reading there by exactly 1 (you may pick the same index many times).

The frequency of a value is how many times it appears in the list. Return the largest frequency any value can reach after at most k operations.

Signature: int maxFrequency(int[] nums, int k).

Constraints

  • 1 <= nums.length <= 10^5
  • 1 <= nums[i] <= 10^5
  • 1 <= k <= 10^5

Practice it: LeetCode 1838 - Frequency of the Most Frequent Element · TUF - Highest frequency element · CF 231C To Add or Not to Add (same task, also prints the value)

Constraints tell you: n up to 10^5 rules out O(n²) (10^10 steps). O(n log n) is the target, and a sort fits. The window cost target × size can reach 10^5 × 10^5 = 10^10, which overflows a 32-bit int: use 64-bit arithmetic.
