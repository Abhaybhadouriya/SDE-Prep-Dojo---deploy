The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a row of sandcastle towers of different heights and a bucket with k scoops of sand. One scoop makes one tower one level taller; you can never make a tower shorter. You want as many towers as possible to end at exactly the same height. The smart move: pick a tall tower as the goal and fill up the towers that are already almost as tall, because they need the fewest scoops. Lining the towers up by height makes "almost as tall" mean "right next to it".

From your world

It is like levelling queue depths across worker threads where you may only add work, never remove it: to get the most queues to the same depth with a budget of k items, you top up the queues closest to the deepest one you pick. The cost of a group is "deepest × count - total", one multiply and one subtract per check, the same accounting a token bucket does.
