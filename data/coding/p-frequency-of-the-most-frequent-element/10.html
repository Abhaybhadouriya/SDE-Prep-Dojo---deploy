How to recognise it:

  • "At most k increments / a budget" and "make as many equal as possible".
  • Only one direction of change is allowed, so the target is the group's maximum.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 1004 (budget window), LC 424 (cost = size - max count).
  2. This problem: brute force, then prefix sums + binary search, then the window.
  3. Then CF 231C (print the value) and LC 2968 (moves both ways, median target).
