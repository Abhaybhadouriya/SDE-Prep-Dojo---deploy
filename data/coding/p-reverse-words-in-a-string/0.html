The story

Explain like I'm 5

You wrote a sentence on a strip of paper with letter tiles, and you need the words in the opposite order. Spin the whole strip around: now the last word is first, but every word reads backwards, like "god" for "dog". Then flip each word on its own and it reads right again. While you are at it, push the tiles to the left so there is exactly one gap between words and no gaps at the ends.

From your world

This is the classic "rotate a buffer with three reversals" trick (reverse all, reverse each part) that you may have seen for rotating a ring buffer in place, plus the same compaction you do when stripping whitespace from a command line in a fixed char[] without malloc: a write index that never overtakes the read index.
