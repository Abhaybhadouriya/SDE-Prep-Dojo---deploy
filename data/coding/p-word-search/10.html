How to recognise it:

  • A path in a grid must follow a sequence (letters, increasing values) and may not revisit cells.
  • Limits are tiny (cells ≤ 36ish, length ≤ 15): exponential search is the intended answer.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 200 Number of Islands (DFS on a grid, permanent marks).
  2. This problem: DFS with mark/unmark, then add the two prunings.
  3. Then 1219 (maximise over paths) and 212 Word Search II (trie + the same DFS).
