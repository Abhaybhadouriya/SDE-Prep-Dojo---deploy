The problem (in our words)

The tray of letter tiles is a grid board with m rows and n columns, and the secret word is word. Decide whether you can spell it by putting your finger on any tile and sliding, one tile at a time, to the tile directly above, below, left or right, never landing on a tile already used in this attempt. Answer true if some attempt spells the whole word, else false.

Signature: boolean exist(char[][] board, String word).

  • m == board.length, n == board[i].length
  • 1 <= m, n <= 6
  • 1 <= word.length <= 15
  • board and word contain only lowercase and uppercase English letters (case matters).

Follow-up from the statement: can you prune the search so it stays fast on a larger board?

Practice it: LeetCode 79 · GfG Word Search

Constraints tell you: at most 36 cells and 15 letters: exponential backtracking is expected and fine. After the first letter each step has at most 3 fresh directions (one is where you came from), so the bound is 36 · 4 · 314 in theory, but pruning on mismatched letters cuts real searches to almost nothing. A word longer than m·n can be rejected at once.
