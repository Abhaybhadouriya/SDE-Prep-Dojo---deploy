The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of children holds letter cards, and the teacher wants the word spelled backwards. She does not make a new row. She asks the first and the last child to swap places, then the second and the second-to-last, and so on. When the two children she points at are the same child (or have passed each other), she is done.

From your world

This is the classic in-place strrev / byte-order flip: two char * pointers, while (p < q) { t = *p; *p++ = *q; *q-- = t; }. You have written it to reverse a buffer of bytes for an endianness swap, or as the building block of "rotate an array by k" (reverse the whole buffer, then each part). The interview version is the same loop on a char[].
