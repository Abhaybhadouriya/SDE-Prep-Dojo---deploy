Recognise the pattern

Cue: "appears more than n/k times" plus "O(1) space" -> Boyer-Moore / Misra-Gries with k - 1 candidates, followed by a verification pass. Here k = 3, so two candidates.

Why at most two answers? If three different values each appeared more than n/3 times, together they would fill more than 3 × n/3 = n cells. The array has only n cells. So the answer has 0, 1 or 2 values, and two slots are always enough.
