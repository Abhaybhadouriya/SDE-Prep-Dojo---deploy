Explain like I'm 5

Fans of many teams walk into a stadium. This time the rule is: whenever three fans of three different teams meet, all three go home together. A team that brought more than a third of the crowd can never be wiped out, because each group of three that leaves takes at most one of its fans. Only two "waiting areas" are needed at the gate, since a third team would just form a leaving triple.

This is the Misra-Gries heavy-hitter summary with k = 3. A router that wants every flow using more than 1/3 of the packets keeps two (flow, counter) registers: a new flow either matches a register, takes an empty one, or decrements both. The registers only promise "nothing heavy was missed"; a second pass (or exact counters for the two survivors) removes the false positives.
