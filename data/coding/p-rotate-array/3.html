Recognise the pattern

When you see "rotate / shift / move a block to the other end, in place", think block swap: (A B) -> (B A) is reverse(reverse(A) reverse(B)), three reversals.

When you see "every element moves to (i + k) mod n", think permutation cycles: there are gcd(n, k) of them, each of length n / gcd(n, k).

[1,2,3,4], k = 33[2,3,4,1]Right by 3 = left by 1: right by k = left by n - k.

Taxonomy: Arrays > in-place rearrangement > reversal trick. Pattern lessons: Arrays & hashing, Two pointers (each reversal is a two-pointer swap loop).
