How to recognise it:

  • "Shift / rotate / move the last k to the front" with "in place" or O(1) space.
  • Reordering whole blocks while keeping the order inside each block.
  • Indices that move by a fixed step modulo n: think gcd cycles.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: GfG Rotate by One, 796 Rotate String.
  2. This problem: copy first, then three reversals, then the gcd-cycle version.
  3. Then 151 / 186 Reverse Words (the same trick on words), 61 Rotate List and 48 Rotate Image.
