Example 1, [10,20,30,40,50,60], k = 2, with three reversals. The ring shows the range being reversed.

  1. Start: n = 6, k = 2 (already k % n). Pink = the last k values, which must end up at the front; the rest only shift right by k.
  2. Reverse the whole array. The pink block is now at the front, where it belongs, but backwards (60, 50); the plain block is also backwards.
  3. Reverse the first k = 2 cells: 60, 50 becomes 50, 60. The front block is now correct.
  4. Reverse the last n - k = 4 cells: 40, 30, 20, 10 becomes 10, 20, 30, 40. Done: [50, 60, 10, 20, 30, 40].
  5. Check: old index i moved to (i + k) % n. 10 went from 0 to 2, 50 from 4 to 0. Each cell was swapped at most twice, no buffer used: O(n) time, O(1) space.
