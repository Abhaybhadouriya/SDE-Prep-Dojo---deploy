Recognise the pattern

Cue: "everything comes in pairs except ..." + "O(1) extra space" -> XOR everything. Pairs cancel because XOR is its own inverse.

The three XOR facts you use:

FactFormulaMeaning here
Self-inversex ^ x = 0A pair vanishes.
Identityx ^ 0 = xStart the accumulator at 0.
Commutative + associativea ^ b ^ a = a ^ a ^ bPartners do not need to be adjacent.
