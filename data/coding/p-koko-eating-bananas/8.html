Common mistakes

Using floor instead of ceil. p / k in integer division says 7 pages at speed 3 take 2 hours; it takes 3. Use (p + k - 1) / k, or (p - 1) / k + 1 (valid because p ≥ 1).

Floating-point ceil. Math.ceil((double) p / k) works here but is slower and invites precision doubts with values near 10^9. Integer ceil is exact.

Summing hours in an int. 10^4 jobs × 10^9 hours at k = 1 is 10^13. In C++/Java, use long long / long, or stop early once the sum passes h.

Starting lo at 0. The first mid can become 0 and the check divides by zero. Speeds start at 1.

Mixing loop forms. while (lo <= hi) with hi = mid never ends when lo == hi and mid fits. Pick one template and keep its update rules.
