The story

Explain like I'm 5

A printer room has a stack of jobs, each with some pages, and the office closes in h hours. A printer works on one job per hour slot: if it prints k pages an hour and a job has fewer pages left, it finishes the job and then sits idle for the rest of that hour. What is the slowest (cheapest) printer that still finishes everything before closing? If speed 10 is enough, speed 11 is enough too; if 9 is too slow, 8 is too slow too. So you never test every speed: you halve the range of speeds each time.

From your world

This is sizing a rate limiter or a link. "Smallest token rate such that every queued burst drains within the deadline" has the same shape: the drain time is monotone in the rate, each burst costs ceil(bytes / rate) ticks, and you bisect the rate instead of simulating every value.
