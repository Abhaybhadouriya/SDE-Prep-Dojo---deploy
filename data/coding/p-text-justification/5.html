  2. Justify line 1: 15 - 10 letters = 5 spaces over 2 gaps. 5 / 2 = 2 each, remainder 1 goes to the leftmost gap (orange): "Keep logs in".

  4. Justify line 2: 4 spaces, 1 gap, so the gap gets all 4: "ring buffers". One gap means no remainder question.

  6. Justify line 3: 5 spaces over 2 gaps -> 3 + 2, extra space on the left: "when disk is".
  7. Line 4 holds the last word, so j == n: this is the last line. Left-justify (single spaces between words) and pad the right end with 10 spaces.
