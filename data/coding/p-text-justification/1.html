The problem (in our words)

You get an array of strings words (tokens of a caption) and a line width maxWidth. Lay the words out as text in which every line has exactly maxWidth characters and is fully (left and right) justified, and return the lines as a list of strings.

  • Greedy packing: put as many words on each line as you can, in order. Words on one line are separated by at least one space.
  • Even spacing: pad each line with extra spaces ' ' between the words so that it is exactly maxWidth long. Spread the spaces as evenly as possible; if they cannot be divided evenly, the gaps further to the left get more spaces than the gaps on the right.
  • Last line: it is left-justified: single spaces between words, then trailing spaces to reach maxWidth.
  • A line with a single word is also left-justified (the word, then trailing spaces).
  • A word is a sequence of non-space characters.

Signature: List<String> fullJustify(String[] words, int maxWidth) (LeetCode style).

  • 1 <= words.length <= 300
  • 1 <= words[i].length <= 20
  • words[i] consists of only English letters and symbols.
  • 1 <= maxWidth <= 100
  • words[i].length <= maxWidth

Practice it: LeetCode 68 · GfG Justified Text

Constraints tell you: at most 300 words of 20 characters and lines of 100 characters: any linear or even quadratic pass is instant. The constraint that matters is words[i].length <= maxWidth: every word fits on a line alone, so the greedy loop always takes at least one word and never gets stuck.
