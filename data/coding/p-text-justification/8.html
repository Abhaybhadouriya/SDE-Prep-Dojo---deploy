Common mistakes

Dividing by zero gaps. A single-word line has gaps = 0; spaces / gaps crashes. Check gaps == 0 before the divmod.

Fully justifying the last line. The last line uses single spaces and right padding, even when it has several words. Test with j == n, not with "fewer words than usual".

Fit test without the gaps. letters + len(w) <= maxWidth forgets the spaces between words and packs too many. The minimum length of k words is letters + (k - 1).

Extra spaces to the right. The remainder goes to the leftmost gaps. Writing t >= gaps - extra flips it.

Forgetting the right padding. Every returned line must be exactly maxWidth long, including the last line and single-word lines; assert the length in your tests.
