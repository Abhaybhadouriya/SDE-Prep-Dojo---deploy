The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are setting a newspaper column by hand with metal letter blocks, and every line must be exactly as wide as the column. You put words on the line until the next one will not fit. The leftover width becomes spacer blocks between the words, shared as fairly as possible; if they do not share evenly, the gaps on the left get the extra ones. The last line of the story is not stretched: words sit together on the left and blank blocks fill the rest.

There is no clever trick: one greedy decision per line and one division per line. The difficulty is getting every rule right on the first try.

From your world

It is the same shape as packing variable-length records into fixed-size frames (TLVs into an MTU-sized packet, log lines into a fixed ring-buffer slot): fill greedily while the next item fits, then pad. Here the "padding" is spread between items instead of put at the end.
