Recognise the pattern

Cue: "best contiguous subarray" + a combine operation (sum, product) -> Kadane: the best value ending at i is built from the one ending at i-1. When the operation can flip order (multiplying by a negative), keep the best and the worst value ending at i.

Look at what a negative does to the two extremes:

max 6, min -122max 12, min -24Positive: the order is kept.
max 6, min -12-3max 36, min -18Negative: the old min becomes the new max.
max 6, min -120max 0, min 0Zero: every run through it is 0; start again after it.
