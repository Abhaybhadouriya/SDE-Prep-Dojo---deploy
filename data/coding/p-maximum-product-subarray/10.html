How to recognise it:

  • "Contiguous subarray" + "maximum" -> think "best ending here".
  • The operation can turn the worst partial answer into the best (sign flips) -> keep the min as well.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 53 Maximum Subarray (one state).
  2. This problem with both the max/min scan and the prefix/suffix proof.
  3. Then 1567 (lengths instead of products) and 713 (window over products).
