Technique: an anagram-style count window over fixed-size units, run once per alignment offset.

Closest match: CF 367B Sereja ans Anagrams CF 1900

  • Same: count positions q where a[q], a[q+p], ..., a[q+(m-1)p] is a rearrangement of b: lanes by q mod p, then a fixed window of m elements with counts.
  • Different: the units are integers, not substrings, and the lane step p is given instead of being the word length.
  • So: the same two loops; map values to ids (or use a hash map) and slide m elements per lane.

How to recognise it:

  • "Concatenation of all words / pieces, in any order" with pieces of the same length.
  • Equal-length units in a flat string: think offset lanes.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 438 (letters), LC 76 (grow / shrink with a map).
  2. This problem: brute force with two maps, then lanes + window.
  3. Then CF 367B (integers, given step).
