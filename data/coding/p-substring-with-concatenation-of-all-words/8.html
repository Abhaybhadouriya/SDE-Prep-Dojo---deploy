Common mistakes

Running only offset 0. Matches can start at any index. Without the w lanes, "xdogcat" with [cat, dog] is missed.

Stepping right by 1 inside a lane. Inside a lane the step is w; the "+1" movement is what the lanes give you.

Treating words as a set. ["foo", "foo"] needs foo twice. Store counts in need, and compare have[word] with need[word], not with 1.

Forgetting to reset on a stranger. If you only shrink on over-use, a stranger never leaves the window logic: either reset, or treat it as a word with need 0 (then the over-use loop drops everything up to and including it).

Comparing whole maps at each step. That costs O(k) per step. The count + "no word over-used" invariant makes the test O(1).
