The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have word cards CAT and DOG. A long ribbon has letters printed on it with no spaces. Where can you cut a piece that is exactly your cards laid end to end, in any order? Since every card is three letters long, you can read the ribbon three letters at a time, like reading beads. But you might start reading at the first, second or third letter, so you read the ribbon three times, once from each starting letter.

From your world

Think of a byte stream framed into fixed-size records, where you do not know the framing offset. A decoder tries each alignment 0 .. w-1; within one alignment the stream is a clean sequence of w-byte records, and you can run an ordinary per-record sliding window. The w alignments are independent, like w lanes of a striped buffer, and could even run in parallel.
