Dry run

Example 1. The ring is where the scan started a flood; #k marks the cells sunk by island k.

  1. The grid: land is 1, water 0. Scan every cell in row-major order.
  2. (0,0) is unvisited land: count island #1. BFS sinks (0,0), then its land neighbours (1,0) and (0,1). Each is marked when it is pushed.
  3. The scan skips sunk cells and water until (0,4): island #2. BFS reaches (1,4), then (1,3).
  4. (2,2) is new land: island #3. Its only land 'neighbour' (1,3) is diagonal, and diagonals do not connect.
  5. (3,0): island #4. (2,0) above it is water, so the flood stops at once.
  6. (3,4): island #5, a single cell.
  7. The scan ends. Answer 5. Every cell was read by the scan once and pushed at most once, so the work is O(m·n).
