How to recognise it:

  • A 2D grid of two (or a few) cell types, and the question is about connected groups.
  • Counting or measuring groups -> flood fill; distances -> BFS; groups changing over time -> union-find.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 733 Flood Fill.
  2. This problem with BFS, then rewrite with union-find.
  3. 695 Max Area, 130 Surrounded Regions, then 305 Number of Islands II.
