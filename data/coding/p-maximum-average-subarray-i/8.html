Common mistakes

Starting best at 0 (or INT_MIN then dividing). 0 is wrong for all-negative input. Start with the first window's sum.

Integer division. best / k with two ints in C++/Java/Go truncates: 10 / 4 = 2, not 2.5. Cast to double first.

Off-by-one in the slide. The element leaving when nums[i] enters is nums[i - k], not nums[i - k + 1] (that one stays in the window).

Comparing floating-point averages. It works here, but compare integer sums: exact and cheaper.
