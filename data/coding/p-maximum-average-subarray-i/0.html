The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a row of jars with sweets and a tray that holds exactly 3 jars side by side. You want the spot where the tray holds the most sweets. You do not recount all three jars every time you slide the tray: one jar falls off the left, one new jar comes in on the right, so you subtract one count and add another.

From your world

It is a moving-average filter or a rate limiter's fixed window: a ring buffer of the last k samples and a running total. A new packet count enters, the oldest leaves, the total is updated in O(1). The peak average over any k-second interval is the peak of that running total divided by k.
