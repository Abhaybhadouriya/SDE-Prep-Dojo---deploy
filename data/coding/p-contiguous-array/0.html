The story

Explain like I'm 5

You climb a staircase while reading a coin-toss tape: heads means one step up, tails one step down. You chalk the step number on the wall the first time you stand on each step. Whenever you are back on a step you have chalked before, the tosses since then had as many heads as tails. The longest fair stretch is the one that goes back to the oldest chalk mark.

The chalk marks are a map from "balance" to "first index"; the longest fair stretch is the answer.

From your world

Think of the in-flight counter of a TCP connection: +1 per segment sent, -1 per ACK. Between two moments where the counter has the same value, every segment sent was acknowledged. The longest such window starts at the first time that value was reached.
