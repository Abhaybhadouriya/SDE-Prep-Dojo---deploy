The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a pile of fridge-magnet words and want to put words made of exactly the same magnets in the same box. "stop", "pots" and "tops" use one s, one t, one o and one p, so they share a box. Instead of comparing every word with every other word, you write a label for each word ("o1 p1 s1 t1") and drop it into the box with that label. Same label, same box.

From your world

The key is a flow signature. Packets with the same 5-tuple go to the same flow bucket no matter the order of their fields in your log line; here words with the same letter multiset go to the same bucket no matter the letter order. Choosing a canonical form and hashing it is the same move as normalising a tuple before a flow-table lookup.
