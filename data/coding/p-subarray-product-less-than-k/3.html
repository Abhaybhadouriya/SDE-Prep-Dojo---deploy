Recognise the pattern

Cue: "count the subarrays" whose aggregate (sum, product, distinct count) stays under a limit, and the aggregate only grows when the subarray grows (positive numbers) -> variable sliding window, and at each right edge add the window length.

Look at the subarrays grouped by where they end:

Ends atValid ones (k = 40)Longest valid startCount
index 0 (4)[4]01
index 1 (3)[3], [4,3]02
index 2 (5)[5], [3,5]12
index 3 (2)[2], [5,2], [3,5,2]13

The valid starts for each end form one block [left, right], and left never moves back as the end moves right. That is the sliding-window shape.
