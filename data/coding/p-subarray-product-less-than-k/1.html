The problem (in our words)

You get an array of positive integers nums and an integer k. Return how many contiguous subarrays have a product of all their elements that is strictly less than k. Subarrays at different positions count separately, even if their values are the same.

Signature: int numSubarrayProductLessThanK(int[] nums, int k).

Constraints

  • 1 <= nums.length <= 3 * 10^4
  • 1 <= nums[i] <= 1000
  • 0 <= k <= 10^6

Practice it: LeetCode 713 · GfG Count the subarrays having product less than k

Constraints tell you: n up to 3·10^4 makes O(n²) about 4.5·10^8 steps in the worst case - too slow for a Medium - so aim for O(n) or O(n log n). Every value is at least 1, so the product of a window never drops when it grows: that monotonicity is what makes two pointers work. k can be 0 or 1, and then no product (always >= 1) is small enough.
