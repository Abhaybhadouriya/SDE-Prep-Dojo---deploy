The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of boxes each has a growth factor painted on it (2 means "doubles", 5 means "times five"). A plant placed in a run of neighbouring boxes grows by the product of their factors, and the greenhouse forbids any run that grows a plant k times or more. How many runs are allowed? Walk along the row with a tape measure. Each time you step to a new box, pull the back of the tape forward until the product is small enough again. Every run that ends at your new box and starts inside the tape is allowed, and that is just the length of the tape.

This is the "count the windows" version of the sliding window. The trick is not the shrinking (that is the usual window), it is how you count: you never list subarrays; you add right - left + 1 once per right edge.

From your world

It is a token bucket over a burst: a burst of consecutive packets is fine while its combined "cost" stays under a limit. Because every packet costs something (all factors >= 1), extending a burst can only make it worse, so the left edge of the longest legal burst only ever moves forward, like a TCP send window's left edge.
