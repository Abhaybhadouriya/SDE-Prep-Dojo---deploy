The story

Explain like I'm 5

Children stand in a line and each one wants to know: "who is the first taller kid behind me?" Walk down the line. Keep a small group of kids who are still waiting, the shortest at the front. When a new kid arrives, every waiting kid shorter than them has their answer: this new kid. They leave happy. The new kid joins the waiting group. Kids still waiting at the end of the line never get a taller kid: their answer is "nobody".

From your world

Think of a backlog of pending requests that each wait for a "bigger" event, like out-of-order segments waiting for an ACK that covers them. A cumulative ACK for sequence x resolves every pending entry below x in one sweep, from the most recent backwards. Because each entry is added once and resolved once, the total work is linear no matter how bursty the arrivals are.
