Revision card

  • Cue: Connect all at minimum cost, minimax path, critical connections, every edge once (Euler).
  • Insight: Cut property: the lightest edge across any cut is safe to take.
  • State: Kruskal = sorted edges + union-find; Prim = heap or O(V^2) array; tin/low for bridges.
  • Trap: Not checking that the MST used V - 1 edges; skipping the parent vertex instead of the parent edge id.
  • Cost: Kruskal O(E log E); dense Prim O(V^2); bridges O(V + E).

More to remember

  • A spanning tree has V - 1 edges. Cut property: the lightest edge across any cut is safe.
  • Kruskal = sort + union-find, skip edges inside one component. Prim = grow one tree by its cheapest outgoing edge.
  • Complete graph (points): array Prim, O(V2). Edge list: Kruskal.
  • "Pay per vertex or connect": add a virtual vertex.
  • Minimax between two points = the MST path; offline queries = sorted sweep with union-find.
  • Bridges: low[v] > tin[u]. Cut vertices: low[v] ≥ tin[u], root needs 2+ children. Skip the parent edge by id.
  • Euler: every edge once; check degrees. Hierholzer: stack, emit at dead ends, reverse.
