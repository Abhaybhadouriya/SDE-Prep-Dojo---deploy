Edge cases

InputExpectedWhy
[]trueEmpty tree: height 0, nothing to break.
[1,2]trueHeights 1 and 0 differ by exactly 1.
[1,null,2,null,3]falseA chain of 3: the root sees 0 and 2.
[1,2,2,3,null,null,3,4,null,null,4]falseThe root is fine (3 and 3) but both 2s are not: check every node.
[1,2,3,4,5,6,null,8]trueDifferences of 1 at several levels are all allowed.
5000-node chainfalseRecursion depth 5000: raise the limit or go iterative in Python.
