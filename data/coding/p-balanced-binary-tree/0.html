The story

Explain like I'm 5

A shop hangs toy mobiles from the ceiling. A mobile is "steady" if, at every hook, the two strings hanging from it are almost the same length: they may differ by at most one bead. You do not measure from the top. You start at the bottom beads, each hook tells its parent how long it is, and the moment a hook finds its two sides differ by two or more, it shouts "broken!" and everyone above just passes that message up.

From your world

This is how the kernel's red-black tree (and AVL trees in some file systems) stays fast: lookups are O(log n) only if no branch is much longer than its sibling. Checking it is a post-order walk where each child returns a summary to its parent, like a status word propagated up a call chain, with -1 playing the role of an -EINVAL that short-circuits the rest.
