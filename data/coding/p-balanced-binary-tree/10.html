How to recognise it:

  • "For every node ..." plus a quantity that is built from the children (height, size, sum, min/max).
  • A yes/no answer that can be folded into the returned number with a sentinel.

  2. This problem: top-down first, then bottom-up.
  3. Then 543 Diameter and 124 Maximum Path Sum.
