Explain like I'm 5

Put a photo on the table and turn it a quarter turn to the right. You can get the same result with two flips: first flip it over its top-left to bottom-right corner line, then flip it left-to-right. Each flip only swaps pairs of tiles, so you never need a second table.

From your world

Like rotating a framebuffer or a square tile of pixels in a driver with no spare buffer: you trade the obvious "read old, write new" copy for swaps, the same idea as reversing a buffer in place with two pointers. Rotating an array by k with three reversals is the 1D cousin.
