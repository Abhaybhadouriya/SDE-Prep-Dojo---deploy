Recognise the pattern

When you see "rotate / flip a square matrix in place", think "compose two mirror images": a rotation is a reflection followed by another reflection, and each reflection is just pairwise swaps.

When you see "move every element to a new position, no extra buffer", think cycles: follow where one element goes until you are back, carrying one temporary.

Write the target mapping first: new[c][n-1-r] = old[r][c], i.e. row r of the input becomes column n-1-r. Then look for simpler moves whose composition gives it:
