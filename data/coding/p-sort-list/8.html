Common mistakes

Starting fast at head and cutting after slow: a 2-node list never shrinks, giving infinite recursion (stack overflow). Start fast at head.next, or cut before slow with a prev pointer.

Forgetting slow.next = null. Without the cut, the "left half" still runs into the right half and the merge produces cycles or duplicates.

Looping over the leftover list in the merge. When one side is empty, attach the other with one assignment; it is already sorted.

Claiming the top-down version is O(1) space. The recursion stack is O(log n). For the follow-up, show the bottom-up passes.

Bottom-up: not advancing tail to the end of each merged run, so the next merged pair overwrites the previous one.
