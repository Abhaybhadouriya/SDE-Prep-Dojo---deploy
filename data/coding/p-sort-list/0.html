The story

Explain like I'm 5

A teacher has a long line of kids holding hands and wants them ordered by height. She cannot "jump" to the kid in the middle of the line; she can only walk along it. So she splits the line in half, has each half sort itself the same way, and then zips the two sorted halves together: the shorter of the two front kids steps forward each time.

From your world

This is what the Linux kernel's list_sort() (lib/list_sort.c) does for struct list_head chains: a merge sort that only rewires next pointers, because the nodes are embedded in larger structs and must not move. Quicksort and heapsort want O(1) indexing; a list has none, so merge sort is the natural fit.
