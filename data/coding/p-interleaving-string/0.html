The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two friends each have a stack of letter cards. You are shown a long word and asked: could it be built by repeatedly taking the top card from either friend's stack, never reordering a stack? Greedily taking from whoever matches can trap you when both match, so you keep a map of "after taking i cards from friend A and j from friend B, could the word so far be right?" and fill that map square by square.

From your world

It is checking that a merged log is a valid merge of two per-CPU logs that each kept their own order (like verifying a trace from two ordered ring buffers). Each step consumes from one source; the question is whether some sequence of choices reproduces the merged stream. That is a path search in a grid of (consumed from A, consumed from B).
