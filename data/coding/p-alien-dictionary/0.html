The story

Explain like I'm 5

You find the class register of a school on another planet. The names are in alphabetical order, but you do not know their alphabet. You notice "Wx" comes before "Wz": the names start the same, so x must come before z in their alphabet. "Wz" before "Xw" tells you W comes before X. Collect enough of these little clues and you can write their alphabet down, as long as the clues do not contradict each other.

Each clue is a "must come before" arrow between two letters. Writing the alphabet is lining up the letters so that every arrow points forward: a topological order.

From your world

This is the same job as make ordering targets from their dependencies, or the kernel ordering initcalls and module loads (modprobe reads modules.dep and loads dependencies first). A dependency loop there is a hard error, exactly like a cycle here.
