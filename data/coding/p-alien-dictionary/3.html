Recognise the pattern

When you see "derive an order" from pairwise facts ("a before b") + "return any valid order, or say it is impossible", think topological sort, with cycle detection as the "impossible" test. Here the facts are hidden in a sorted list, so the first job is extracting them from adjacent pairs only.

"ab" vs "ac"✓ b<cFirst difference decides.

"ab" vs "abc"✗ no ruleValid prefix pair: nothing to learn.
