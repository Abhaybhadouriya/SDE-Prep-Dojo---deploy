How to recognise it:

  • "Multiple of k", "divisible by k", "sum mod k" on a contiguous run.
  • A minimum length (≥ 2) or "longest/shortest" -> store indices; "how many" -> store counts.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 560 Subarray Sum Equals K.
  2. This problem, then 974 (count, negatives).
  3. 1590 Make Sum Divisible by P (shortest, last index), then 2845.
