The story

Explain like I'm 5

A clock has k hours. You start at 12 and move the hand forward by each number on a list. If the hand ever points to an hour it already pointed to, the moves in between went round the clock a whole number of times: their total is a multiple of k. You only have to remember where the hand was first seen at each hour, and make sure at least two moves separate the two visits.

The clock face is "prefix sum mod k"; the first-visit notes are a map from remainder to first index.

From your world

This is a ring buffer with k slots: a write pointer that advances by nums[i] and wraps with % k. Landing on the same slot twice means the bytes written in between are a whole number of laps, exactly like sequence numbers wrapping mod 232.
