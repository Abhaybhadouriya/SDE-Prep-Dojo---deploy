Common mistakes

  • Counting one minute too many. A plain "while queue: ...; minutes++" also counts the last level, which infects nothing. Either stop when fresh == 0 (as here) or return minutes - 1 with care for the "no fresh" case.
  • Marking on pop instead of on push. A crate next to two mouldy ones is pushed twice, and fresh is decremented twice (it can go negative and give a wrong 0 or -1).
  • BFS from one source at a time and taking the max of the separate runs. The answer uses the nearest source, so you need the minimum per cell; multi-source BFS gives it for free.
  • Returning -1 when there are no good crates and no sources. The answer is 0; check fresh, not the queue.
  • Flipping in place during a full-grid scan (the simulation): a crate turned this minute then spreads again in the same minute.
