Dry run

Example 1: [[2,1,1],[0,1,0],[1,1,0]]. Red = mouldy, blue = turned this minute (the queue), plain = good, faded = empty.

  1. Scan: one mouldy crate at (0,0) goes in the queue; 5 good crates are counted.
  2. Minute 1: (0,0) infects (0,1). Its lower neighbour (1,0) is empty, so nothing else.
  3. Minute 2: (0,1) infects (0,2) on the right and (1,1) below. Both are pushed and flipped at once.
  4. Minute 3: (0,2) has no good neighbour (below is empty). (1,1) infects (2,1).
  5. Minute 4: (2,1) infects (2,0). fresh hits 0, the loop stops with minutes = 4. The last queue level is not expanded, so no extra minute is counted.
