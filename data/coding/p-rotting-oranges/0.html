The story

Explain like I'm 5

A cold-storage room is a grid of shelves. Each slot is empty, holds a good crate, or holds a mouldy crate. Every minute, mould jumps from each mouldy crate to the good crates directly above, below, left and right of it. How many minutes until no good crate is left? If some good crate can never be reached (walled in by empty slots), the answer is -1.

From your world

This is flooding with a hop counter. Several routers start a broadcast at the same moment; every tick, each node that just got the packet forwards it to its neighbours. The time for the last node to hear it is its hop distance from the nearest source, and the network converges after the maximum such distance. Nodes in a partition never hear it: that is the -1.
