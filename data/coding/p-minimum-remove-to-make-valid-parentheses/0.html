The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of children holds hands. A child with a raised left hand, "(", is waiting for a partner to their right; a child with a raised right hand, ")", wants a partner to their left. Walking left to right, each ")" grabs the nearest waiting "(". A ")" that finds nobody waiting must leave the row, and so must every "(" still waiting when the row ends. The children without raised hands (the letters) always stay.

From your world

It is like pairing request and response frames on a pipelined connection: a response with no outstanding request is a protocol error and is dropped; requests still outstanding when the stream closes are aborted. The stack is your table of outstanding request IDs, and matching always closes the most recent one (LIFO), as in nested calls.
