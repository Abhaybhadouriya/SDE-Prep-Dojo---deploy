Recognise the pattern

Cue: "valid parentheses" + "remove / insert the minimum" -> a single left-to-right balance scan. A ')' that would make the balance negative is bad; whatever balance remains at the end is the number of bad '('. Use a stack of indices (not characters) when you must know which '(' are left over.
