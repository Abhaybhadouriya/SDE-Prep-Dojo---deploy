The story

Explain like I'm 5

You want to spell a message with letter fridge magnets, and all the magnets you own are in one box. Sort the box into 26 little piles, one per letter. Then, for each letter of your message, take one magnet from its pile. If you ever reach for a pile that is empty, the message cannot be spelled. Magnets left over at the end do not matter.

Order is noise again, exactly as in Valid Anagram. The difference is the direction of the check: the magazine may have more of everything, it only must never have less. That turns "all counters are zero" into "no counter ever goes below zero".

From your world

It is a buffer pool with per-type credits. The magazine fills the credit table (credits[type]++), each request for the note consumes one credit, and a request that finds zero credits is refused. Credits left over are fine, just like free buffers after a burst. A 26-slot int array on the stack is the whole table.
