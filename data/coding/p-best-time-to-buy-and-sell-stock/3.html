Recognise the pattern

When you see "max of a[j] - a[i] with i < j", think for each j you only need the best a[i] to its left -> keep a running minimum (a prefix aggregate).

When you see "one buy, one sell" on a price series, think the maximum subarray sum of the daily changes (Kadane), because a sum of consecutive changes telescopes to sell - buy.
