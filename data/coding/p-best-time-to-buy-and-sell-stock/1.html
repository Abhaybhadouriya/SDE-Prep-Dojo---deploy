The problem (in our words)

You get an integer array rates, where rates[i] is the price of one item on day i. Pick one day to buy and a different, later day to sell. Return the largest profit (sell price minus buy price) you can get. If no deal makes money, return 0.

Signature: int maxProfit(int[] rates) - returns one integer.

  • Constraints:
  • 1 <= rates.length <= 10^5
  • 0 <= rates[i] <= 10^4
  • Exactly one buy and one sell at most; the buy must come before the sell.

Practice it: LeetCode 121 - Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock · GfG - Stock Buy and Sell, one transaction · TUF article

Constraints tell you: n up to 10^5 rules out the O(n^2) pair check in many judges; aim for O(n). Prices up to 10^4 mean the profit fits easily in a 32-bit int.
