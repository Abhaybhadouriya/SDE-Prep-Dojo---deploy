How to recognise it:

  • A pair (i, j) with i < j and a score that splits into f(i) + g(j).
  • Only one pair is chosen, so one running best of f over the prefix is enough.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: this problem, then 2016 (strict version).
  2. Same idea: 1014, then 53 (the Kadane view).
  3. Family: 122 -> 309 -> 123 (see the stock state machine).
