Input ratesOutputWhy
[9, 2, 6, 1, 4, 8]7Buy on day 3 (price 1), sell on day 5 (price 8). Buying at 2 and selling at 8 gives only 6.
[12, 10, 7, 3]0The price only falls; every deal loses, so do not trade.
[2, 9, 1, 5]7The trap: the global minimum 1 comes after the global maximum 9. Max minus min (8) is not allowed; 9 - 2 = 7 is.
