Common mistakes

  • Returning max - min. It ignores order: [2, 9, 1, 5] gives 8 instead of 7.
  • Starting best at a negative value (for example rates[1] - rates[0]) and returning a loss for falling prices. The task allows no trade, so the floor is 0.
  • Updating the minimum before computing the sale with a strict "later day" rule in mind. In this problem it is harmless (same-day trade gains 0), but in variants with a fee or cooldown the order matters; keep "sell, then update" as a habit.
  • Initialising cheapest to 0 instead of rates[0] or infinity: every profit then looks like the full price.
  • Treating it as Stock II (sum of every rise). That is for unlimited transactions; here only one deal is allowed.
