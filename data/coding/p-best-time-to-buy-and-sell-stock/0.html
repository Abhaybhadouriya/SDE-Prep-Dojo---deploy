Explain like I'm 5

Mira watches the daily resale price of one used switch on a marketplace for n days. She may buy it once and sell it once, and the sale must come on a later day than the purchase. Looking back over the price history, what was the biggest gain she could have made? If every possible deal loses money, she simply does not trade.

From your world

This is the "low-water mark" you keep when you scan a latency or RTT log for the largest rise. You never store the whole history; one register holds the minimum so far, and each new sample is compared with it. It is the same one-pass shape as tracking a minimum queue depth and the largest jump above it.
