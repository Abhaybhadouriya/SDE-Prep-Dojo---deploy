The story

Explain like I'm 5

A teacher has a pile of name cards. Each card has a kid's first name and some of their nicknames. Two kids can share a first name, so names do not help. But if two cards share even one nickname, they must belong to the same kid. The teacher clips such cards together. Clipped piles grow: card A clips to B, B to C, so A, B and C are one kid even if A and C share nothing.

From your world

It is like merging ARP or neighbour entries: an IP seen with two MACs and one of those MACs seen with a third IP all belong to one host group. Any shared identifier glues records together, transitively. Union-find is the standard tool for that "glue as you scan" job, like grouping interfaces into bonds or merging duplicate device records in an inventory.
