  4. Account #3: one new email. The scan is done; each distinct email appears once in the map.
  5. Group the map by find(owner): ravi.k@w.com's owner #2 has root #0, so it joins #0's group. Sort each group (ASCII: '.' comes before '@') and prefix the root's name. Three accounts come out.
