Closest match: GfG - Any Duplicate Within K Distance GfG

  • Same: equal values at distance at most k.
  • Different: GfG's judge passes the array and k as a function call with its own signature (and some versions say "less than or equal to k", some "within k").

How to recognise it:

  • "Within distance k", "in any window of k consecutive", "at most k apart".
  • The condition between two elements is equality (hash) or closeness (ordered structure).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 217 (global set).
  2. This problem: last-seen map, then the window set.
  3. Then LC 2461 (window with counts) and LC 220 (window with an ordered set).
