The story

Explain like I'm 5

A doorman lets people into a party one by one and can only remember the last three faces. If someone walks in who looks exactly like one of those three, he shouts "twins!". He does not care about a look-alike who came in an hour ago; that one has already fallen out of his memory.

The question is not "is there a duplicate?" (that is Contains Duplicate) but "is there a duplicate close by?". Closeness turns the global set into a window of fixed size k that slides over the array.

From your world

This is duplicate-frame suppression with a bounded history: a redundancy protocol (PRP/HSR) or a replay filter keeps only the last k sequence numbers it saw and drops a frame whose id is still in that history. Older ids age out of the ring. The set here is that ring, and the eviction of nums[i-k] is the ring's tail moving forward.
