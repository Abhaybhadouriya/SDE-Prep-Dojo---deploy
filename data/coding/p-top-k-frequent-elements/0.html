The story

Explain like I'm 5

A shop wants to put its k best-selling snacks in the front window. The cashier has a long roll of receipts, one snack per line. First she makes a tally: chips 4, cookies 3, gum 1. Then, instead of sorting the tally, she lines up empty shelves labelled "sold 1", "sold 2", ... up to "sold as many as there are receipts", and puts each snack on the shelf of its tally. Now she walks from the highest shelf down and grabs snacks until the window is full.

The trick is that the shelf labels are small integers with a known maximum (the number of receipts), so placing a snack is one array write, not a comparison sort.

From your world

This is how you find the top talkers in a flow table: a hash map from 5-tuple to packet count, then either a small heap of the k biggest (what tcpdump | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn | head does in spirit) or a histogram indexed by count. The heap version is the one that streams; the bucket version needs the final counts.
