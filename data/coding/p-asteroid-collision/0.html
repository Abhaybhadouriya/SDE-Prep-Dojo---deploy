The story

Explain like I'm 5

Toy cars race along a single narrow track. Some drive east, some drive west, all at the same speed. Two cars going the same way never touch. When an east-bound car meets a west-bound car head on, the lighter one is knocked off the track; if they weigh the same, both are. Which cars are still on the track at the end? The west-bound car coming in has to fight its way through the east-bound cars nearest to it first, one after another, like plates taken off the top of a pile.

The key observation: a west-bound newcomer can only reach the east-bound car closest to it. Whatever is further left is hidden behind that car until it is destroyed. That "closest first, then the next" order is exactly last-in, first-out: a stack.

From your world

It works like a call stack unwinding on an exception: the throw pops frames from the top one by one until a frame catches it (a bigger right-mover absorbs the newcomer) or the stack is empty (the left-mover escapes). The frames below the catcher are never touched.
