Recognise the pattern

When you see a new element can cancel or be cancelled by the most recent surviving element, and the cancellation can cascade backwards, think stack of survivors. Same shape as matching brackets, "remove adjacent duplicates" and backspace strings.

Try a few tiny inputs and ask "who can ever meet whom?":

InputResultNote
[-3,5][-3,5]Left-mover on the left, right-mover on the right: they fly apart. No collision.
[5,-3][5]The only colliding shape: + then -.
[1,2,3,-4][-4]-4 eats 3, then 2, then 1: a cascade, newest first.
