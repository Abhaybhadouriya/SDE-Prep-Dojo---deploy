Closest match: LC 2211 Count Collisions on a Road Medium

  • Same: cars move left/right on one line; only facing pairs (or a stopped car) collide.
  • Different: colliding cars stop instead of exploding, and you count collisions, not survivors.
  • So: no stack is needed: strip the leading L's and trailing R's, and every remaining moving car collides once.

How to recognise it:

  • A new item interacts only with the most recent survivor, and the interaction can remove it.
  • The answer is "what is left" after all cancellations.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 20 Valid Parentheses, 1544 Make The String Great.
  2. This problem: state the only colliding case, then code the alive-flag loop.
  3. Then 1209 (counts on the stack) and 2751 Robot Collisions (the same stack plus sorting and health).
