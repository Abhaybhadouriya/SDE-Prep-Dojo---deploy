How to recognise it:

  • Two trees (or two halves of one tree) and a yes/no about equality, mirroring or flipping.
  • The answer for a pair depends on the answers for child pairs: a pair helper with nil-first rules.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 100 Same Tree, 226 Invert Binary Tree.
  2. This problem, recursive then iterative.
  3. Then 572 Subtree of Another Tree and 951 Flip Equivalent Binary Trees.
