Recognise the pattern

Cue: "is this tree equal to / a mirror of / a copy of that one" -> walk two trees in lockstep with a helper f(a, b) that checks the pair and recurses on child pairs. A symmetric tree is the special case where the two trees are the root's own children and the child pairs are crossed.
