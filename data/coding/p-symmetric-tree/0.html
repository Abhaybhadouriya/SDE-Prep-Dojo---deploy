The story

Explain like I'm 5

You fold a paper cut-out of a family tree down the middle. It is symmetric if every box on the left lands exactly on a box with the same name on the right. You do not compare left with left: the far-left box must land on the far-right box, and the boxes near the fold must land on each other. If one side has a box where the other side has nothing, the fold fails.

From your world

It is the same as checking that two structures are byte-for-byte equal, except one is read in reverse field order: like comparing a big-endian dump with a little-endian one. You walk the two in lockstep and stop at the first mismatch, the way memcmp would.
