What 1,883 real reports say about difficulty

We read 4,217 interview-experience posts on LeetCode Discuss (January 2024 to November 2026) and pulled out every LeetCode problem a candidate named. That gave 1,883 question reports over 553 distinct problems. The extraction is automatic, so expect a few mistakes, but the overall shape is clear.

63%of reports are Medium
21%are Hard
16%are Easy
553distinct problems named

The big lesson: the level does not change the questions much; it changes what the interviewer expects you to do with them. An SDE-1 who solves a Medium cleanly passes. An SDE-3 who solves the same Medium is then asked to handle a stream, cut the memory, or explain why the approach is optimal.
