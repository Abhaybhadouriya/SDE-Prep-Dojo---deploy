8. Silence

Symptom: you think for six minutes without a word, or type for fifteen. The interviewer cannot give credit for reasoning they did not hear, and cannot give you a hint either.

MomentSay something like
Thinking"I'm checking whether sorting helps... it would lose the indices, so a hash map."
Stuck"The brute force is O(n2); the repeated work is X, which I can cache."
Coding"This loop keeps the window valid; when it is invalid I shrink from the left."
Found a bug"Wait, this fails on an empty array; I'll guard that."
Done"Let me trace the second example... and the edge case with one element."

Talking is not chatter: every sentence is a decision and its reason. A good habit is to practise with a timer and record yourself; most people find they go quiet exactly when it matters.
