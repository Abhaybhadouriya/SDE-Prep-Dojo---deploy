The story

Explain like I'm 5

You walk down a row of sweet jars. Each jar has some sweets. The rule: you may never take from two jars that stand next to each other. Walking left to right, at every jar you ask one question: "Am I better off taking this jar (and so not the one just before it), or leaving it?" If you remember the best you could do up to the previous jar and up to the jar before that, the answer is one comparison.

From your world

The final loop is the same shape as an iterative Fibonacci in C: two locals that slide one step to the right on every iteration. No array, no recursion, and it works on a stream, since each value is read once.
