Dry run

Input [6,1,2,9,4]. Green arrows show the option that wins; dashed ones lose.

  1. i = 0: only one locker so far. Take it: best[0] = 6. (Think of best[-1] = best[-2] = 0.)
  2. i = 1: skip gives best[0] = 6; take gives best[-1] + 1 = 0 + 1 = 1. Skip wins: best[1] = 6.
  3. i = 2: skip gives best[1] = 6; take gives best[0] + 2 = 8. Take wins: best[2] = 8 (lockers 0 and 2).
  4. i = 3: skip gives best[2] = 8; take gives best[1] + 9 = 15. Take wins: best[3] = 15 (lockers 0 and 3).
  5. i = 4: skip gives best[3] = 15; take gives best[2] + 4 = 12. Skip wins: best[4] = 15. Answer = best[n-1] = 15.
  6. Only best[i-1] and best[i-2] were ever read, so two variables (prev1, prev2) replace the whole array: O(1) space.
