The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of parcels stands on a conveyor, in a fixed order, each with a weight. k vans will take them, and each van takes a run of neighbouring parcels (you cannot reorder the belt). The slowest van is the one with the heaviest load, so you want the heaviest van to be as light as possible. Guessing where to cut is hard. Guessing a weight limit is easy: "if no van may carry more than 15 kg, how many vans do I need?" You just load each van until the next parcel would not fit.

From your world

It is the "split an ordered work queue into k contiguous batches for k workers, minimise the makespan" problem: think of handing consecutive ranges of a packet ring or of RX descriptors to k cores, where each core must get a contiguous range. The greedy check is how a batching loop behaves anyway: keep appending to the current batch until it would exceed the budget, then start a new one.
