How to recognise it:

  • "Minimise the largest" or "maximise the smallest" over a split into k groups, days or workers.
  • A yes/no test for a given limit is easy, and a bigger limit never makes it harder.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 875 (independent piles).
  2. LC 1011 (this problem with another story), then this one: DP first, then binary search.
  3. Then LC 1552 and LC 1231 (maximise the minimum: the "last true" variant).
