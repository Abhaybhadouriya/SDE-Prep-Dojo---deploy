The story

Explain like I'm 5

Spill a cup of paint on one tile of a floor. It spreads to every touching tile of the same colour, then to the tiles touching those, and stops at tiles of a different colour. Corners touching do not count: paint only flows through shared edges.

This is the smallest "connected component in a grid" problem and the base of Number of Islands, Surrounded Regions and Max Area of Island. In an interview it is a warm-up: the grading points are the four-direction bounds check, not revisiting pixels, the same-colour trap, and knowing when recursion depth matters.

From your world

It is a reachability walk over a graph whose nodes are pixels and whose edges join equal-coloured neighbours, like finding every host in one broadcast domain: start anywhere, follow links whose property matches, mark what you touched so you do not loop.
