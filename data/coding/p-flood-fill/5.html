Dry run

Explicit-stack DFS on the first example, neighbours checked in the order up, down, left, right. Orange = the pixel being popped, green highlight = pixels painted and pushed in this step.

  1. The start pixel (0,0) has colour 3. It differs from 7, so paint it and push it. Painting on push is the "visited" mark.
  2. Pop (0,0) and look at its 4 neighbours. (1,0) and (0,1) still have colour 3: paint and push.
  3. Pop (0,1). No neighbour still has colour 3 (painted ones now read 7), so nothing is pushed.
  4. Pop (1,0) and look at its 4 neighbours. (2,0) still has colour 3: paint and push.
  5. Pop (2,0) and look at its 4 neighbours. (2,1) still has colour 3: paint and push.
  6. Pop (2,1). No neighbour still has colour 3 (painted ones now read 7), so nothing is pushed.
  7. The stack is empty. Result [[7,7,0],[7,0,3],[7,7,1]]. Pixel (1,2) also had colour 3 but touches the region only diagonally, so it is not part of it.
