Common mistakes

Using the downhill rule in the reverse flood. From the ocean you must step to neighbours that are higher or equal. Copying the rain rule (<=) floods the wrong cells.

Strict comparison. > instead of >= drops plateaus. In example 1, (2,2) joins the Atlantic flood only from (2,3) (both 2), and (1,1) only from (1,2) (both 5); with > the answer loses (1,1).

One shared visited grid. The second flood must start fresh; otherwise it stops at cells the first flood already marked.

Mixing up the edges. Pacific = top row and left column; Atlantic = bottom row and right column. Corners (0, n-1) and (m-1, 0) belong to both.

Deep recursion. A recursive DFS on a 200 x 200 spiral is 40,000 frames: fine in C++ usually, a RecursionError in Python. Use BFS or an explicit stack.
