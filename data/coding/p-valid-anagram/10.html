How to recognise it:

  • "Rearrange", "permutation of", "same characters" - positions do not matter.
  • A small fixed alphabet is stated in the constraints.

Extra practice (competitive programming, optional - not interview questions): Codeforces 141A Amusing Joke (The letters of two names together must equal a pile: count both up, the pile down); CodeChef TECH04 Anagram (Same check with multiple test cases on stdin).

Practice order:

  1. This problem, with the length check and the 26-array.
  2. 383 Ransom Note (subset), then 49 Group Anagrams (count as key).
  3. 438 Find All Anagrams and 567 Permutation in String (counts on a sliding window).
