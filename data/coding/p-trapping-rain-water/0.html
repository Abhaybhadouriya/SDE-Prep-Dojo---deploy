The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of sandcastle towers stands on the beach, all one bucket wide but of different heights. A wave washes over and then pulls back. Water stays stuck in the dips between towers. How much? Look at one dip: the water there can rise only as high as the shorter of the two tallest towers that fence it in, one on each side. Any higher and it pours over that shorter fence.

That one sentence is the whole problem. The work is finding "the tallest tower on my left" and "the tallest tower on my right" for every column without scanning both sides every time.

From your world

The per-column answer is a classic prefix max / suffix max, like computing a running high-water mark of queue depth from both ends of a trace. The two-pointer version is the same idea as a min-of-two-bounds check in flow control: you only commit a value on the side whose bound is already known to be the limiting one.
