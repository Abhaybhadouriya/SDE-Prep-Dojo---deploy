Recognise the pattern

When you see "for each position, the answer depends on the best value to its left AND the best value to its right", think prefix max + suffix max. If only the smaller of the two matters, two pointers can replace both arrays. If you need the nearest taller bar instead of the tallest, think monotonic stack.

Try a few tiny inputs and the rule appears:

InputTrappedNote
[3,0,3]3Both fences 3: level 3.
[3,0,1]1Right fence is only 1: level 1, not 3.
[1,0,3]1Mirror: the left fence decides.
[0,3,0]0The ends have no fence outside them, so water runs off.

Pattern: Arrays > prefix sum > prefix / suffix max · Two pointers > opposite ends · Lessons: Two pointers primer, Prefix sums and prefix max, Stacks (monotonic stack)
