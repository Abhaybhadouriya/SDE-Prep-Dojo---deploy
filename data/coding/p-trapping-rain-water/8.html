Common mistakes

Using max instead of min. Water is limited by the lower fence. max(leftMax, rightMax) overflows the pit at index 6 in example 1.

Adding water before updating the max. Update leftMax = max(leftMax, height[l]) before adding, or you add a negative amount on a new wall.

Excluding the column itself from its max. If leftMax does not include height[i], you get leftMax - height[i] < 0 on walls. Include it, or clamp at 0.

Stack version: forgetting the width. The stack fills a horizontal layer between two walls, so multiply by j - left - 1, and break when the stack is empty after the pop (no left wall).
