How to recognise it:

  • A 2D layout of data that is sorted in reading order: search the flat index.
  • Sorted rows and columns without the "row starts after previous" rule: staircase, or binary search on the value.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 704 and LC 35.
  2. This problem: flat index, then mention the staircase.
  3. Then LC 240 (staircase) and LC 378 (binary search on the answer).
