The story

Explain like I'm 5

You watch cars pass a window and write down "red" or "not red". You want the longest streak of red cars in a row. You keep a counter on your fingers: each red car adds a finger, any other car makes you drop all fingers. On paper you keep the most fingers you ever held.

From your world

It is the "longest run of set bits" in a bitmap, or the longest burst of consecutive successful packets in a loss trace. For a single machine word you would use bit tricks (x &= x << 1 until zero counts the longest run of 1 bits); for an array the running counter below is the general form.
