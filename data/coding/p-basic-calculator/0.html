The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are adding up a shopping bill out loud: "7, minus what's in this bag". You do not know what is in the bag yet, so you write "7, minus" on a sticky note and start counting the bag from zero. Inside the bag is another little bag; another sticky note. When a bag is done, you take the top sticky note and finish its sentence: "7 minus (the bag's total)". When the last bag closes, the notes are gone and you have the answer.

The whole trick is that the sticky note needs only two things: the total so far outside the bracket, and the sign written just before it. No operator precedence is involved, because + and - are equal and left to right.

From your world

This is a tiny hand-written lexer plus a pushdown automaton: the kind of parser you find in a shell's $(( )) arithmetic, a BPF filter expression, or a config-file reader. The explicit stack replaces recursion so nesting depth cannot overflow the C stack; the string is consumed one byte at a time, no tokens array is allocated.
