Recognise the pattern

When you see "visit a grid in a fixed geometric order" (spiral, layer by layer, diagonal, snake), think simulation with boundaries. Keep the limits of the region that is still unvisited, and shrink them as you go. If the order is "turn when you hit a wall or a visited cell", a direction vector plus a visited mark also works.

Try a few shapes and see which side of the lap can be missing:
