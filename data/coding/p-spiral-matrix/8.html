Common mistakes

No guards on the bottom row and left column. Passes every square test and fails on 1×3, 3×1 and 3×4. Always test a single row and a single column.

Using the guard on the wrong side. The bottom row needs top <= bottom (rows still exist), the left column needs left <= right (columns still exist). Swapping them breaks wide or tall inputs.

Updating a wall before its loop. If you do top++ before reading the top row, you read the wrong row. Read, then move the wall.

Looping while out.size() < m*n without guards. The inner loops can still overshoot inside one iteration. Either keep the guards or check the count inside every inner loop.
