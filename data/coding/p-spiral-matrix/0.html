The story

Explain like I'm 5

A night guard checks every shelf in a rectangular warehouse. She starts at the top-left corner and always keeps her right hand on the wall: along the top row, down the right side, back along the bottom, up the left side. Then she steps one shelf inward and walks the next, smaller ring, until no shelf is left. The list of shelves in the order she passes them is the answer.

Nothing clever is hidden here. The problem tests whether you can write a loop with four moving boundaries and get every corner case right on the first try: one row, one column, a wide matrix, a tall matrix.

From your world

A 2D array in C is row-major: m[r][c] sits at offset r*n + c. A spiral reads two sides along rows (stride 1) and two sides down columns (stride n), which is why it has worse cache locality than a plain scan. At 10×10 that does not matter, but it is a good thing to say if the interviewer asks about a huge matrix.
