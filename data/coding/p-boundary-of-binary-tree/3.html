Recognise the pattern

Cue: "outline", "perimeter", "anti-clockwise", "left view + leaves + right view" -> split the answer into disjoint parts, compute each with its own simple walk, and decide in advance which part owns the shared nodes. Leaves are owned by the leaf part; the root by itself.

It is a combination of three classic views: the left view (follow the outer left side), the leaves (any DFS), and the right view. If you can do Right Side View, the only new thing is ownership.
