Example 2. Colours show the part that owns each node.

  1. Start with the root: 10 goes out first. It has children, so it is not treated as a leaf (a lone root would be the whole answer).
  2. Left edge, top-down. Begin at root.left = 20. It is not a leaf, so add it. Next edge node: its left child if it has one (40), otherwise its right child.
  3. 40 is a leaf, so the left-edge walk stops without adding it. Leaves belong to step 2; adding 40 here would print it twice.
  4. Leaves, left to right: a preorder walk of the whole tree (stack, push right before left) meets leaves in left-to-right order. First leaf: 40.
  5. The walk goes into 50 (not a leaf, not on any edge: skipped) and finds 70, then 80. Inner nodes like 50 never appear in the answer.
  6. Then the right subtree: 90 and 100. All five leaves are out, in left-to-right order.
  7. Right edge, collected top-down: 30 (not a leaf). 30 has no right child, so the edge turns left to 60. 60 is not a leaf: collect it. Its right child 100 is a leaf: stop.
  8. Append the right edge reversed (bottom-up: 60, 30), because the boundary is read anti-clockwise. Answer: [10, 20, 40, 70, 80, 90, 100, 60, 30].
