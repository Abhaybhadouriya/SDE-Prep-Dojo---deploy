Common mistakes

Adding the bottom leaf in the edge walk. 40 then appears twice: once as the end of the left edge and once as a leaf. Stop the edge walk when the current node is a leaf.

Starting the left edge at the root when root.left is null. For [5,null,8,6,9] this walks into the right subtree and prints 8 as a "left edge" node: [5,8,6,9,8]. On LeetCode 545 the left edge is empty in this case.

Treating a lone root as a leaf. Root is printed, then printed again by the leaf walk: [7,7].

Only following left children. The edge is "left if any, else right"; following only left stops at 2 in [1,2,null,null,3,4] and loses 3.

Forgetting to reverse the right edge. Anti-clockwise order goes up the right side: [.., 30, 60] instead of [.., 60, 30].
