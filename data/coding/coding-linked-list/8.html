"the head may be removed", "partition", "build a new list", "merge"Dummy head (+ tail)203, 19, 86, 21, 2

"reverse", "in groups", "between positions", "swap pairs", "rotate"Three-pointer reversal on a block, with before/after anchors206, 92, 24, 25, 61
"two lists meet", "intersection"Two pointers that switch to the other head at the end (both walk a + b + c)160
"deep copy", "random pointer", "clone"Hash map original -> copy, or weave-in-place for O(1)138, 1485
"O(1) access and O(1) move to front"Doubly linked list + hash map146

If a list question asks for "O(1) extra space", that rules out copying the values into an array. It is a hint that the intended answer rewires pointers in place: reversal, weaving, or fast/slow.
