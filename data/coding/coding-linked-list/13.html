Revision card

  • Cue: "In place", "O(1) extra space" on a list, middle, cycle, reverse, merge, k-group.
  • Insight: Lists are rewired, not copied: six pointer moves cover almost every question.
  • State: dummy head when the head may change; fast/slow for middle and cycles; prev/cur/next for reversal.
  • Trap: Losing next before rewiring; not cutting the tail (cycles in output); empty and one-node lists.
  • Cost: O(n) time, O(1) extra space.

More to remember

  • Six moves cover almost every list question: dummy head, fast/slow, three-pointer reversal, splice-merge, block reversal with anchors, weave-copy.
  • Head may change: dummy. Middle or cycle: fast/slow. Reverse: save next first.
  • Floyd's phase 2 works because a = (k-1)L + c.
