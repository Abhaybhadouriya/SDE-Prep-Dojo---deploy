How to recognise it:

  • Each element is used exactly once with one of two "directions" (+/-, team A/B, left/right).
  • The total is small (hundreds or thousands), so a sum-indexed table is cheap.

Practice order:

  1. Partition Equal Subset Sum (boolean row), then this problem (count row).
  2. GfG Partitions with Given Difference (same algebra with D instead of target).
  3. Last Stone Weight II, then Coin Change II to feel the loop-direction difference.
