The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a few coins, and each coin goes either into your piggy bank (+) or to your little brother (-). In how many ways can you end up exactly "target" coins ahead of him? Instead of thinking about plus and minus, think only about your pile: if you end up with P and he gets the rest, then P - rest = target and P + rest = all coins. So your pile must be exactly (all + target) / 2. Now count the ways to pick a pile with that value.

The interviewer wants to watch the jump from "2n sign patterns" to "memo on (index, running sum)" and, at SDE-2/3 level, to the algebra that turns it into subset-sum counting. Zeros in the input are the classic trap.

From your world

It is a histogram of reachable sums. Each number shifts a copy of the histogram right by x and adds it in, like convolving with the kernel {0: 1, x: 1}. The final bucket at P is the answer.
