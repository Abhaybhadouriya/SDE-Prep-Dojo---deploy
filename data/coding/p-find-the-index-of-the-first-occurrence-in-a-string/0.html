The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are looking for the word "ababd" in a long line of letter tiles. You lay the word card under the tiles and compare letter by letter. When a letter differs, a beginner slides the card by one and starts again from the card's first letter. A smart player notices: "the last two tiles I matched were ab, and my card also starts with ab", so they slide the card so its start sits on those two tiles and carry on from the tile they were looking at. They never look at a tile twice.

From your world

This is how deep packet inspection and grep-like scanners must work on a stream: bytes arrive once and cannot be re-read cheaply. KMP is a small finite automaton over the pattern; the lps table is its failure-transition table (Aho-Corasick generalises it to many signatures at once, which is what IDS engines such as Snort use).
