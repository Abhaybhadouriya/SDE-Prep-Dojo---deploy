How to recognise a union-find problem

  • "Groups", "connected", "same component", "friend circles", "provinces", "networks" with an undirected relation.
  • Things merge over time and you must answer after each merge: online connectivity.
  • "Which edge can be removed" or "does adding this edge create a cycle" in an undirected graph.
  • Items are related through a shared attribute (email, row, column, equal value) rather than an explicit edge list.
  • The relation is an equivalence: reflexive, symmetric, transitive ("a == b and b == c means a == c").
  • "Connect everything at minimum cost": Kruskal.

Counter-cues: shortest path or "minimum steps" (BFS), any direction or ordering (topological sort), sets that must split (DSU cannot undo; think in reverse time or use another structure).
