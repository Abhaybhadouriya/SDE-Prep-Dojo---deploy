How to recognise it:

  • "Return any" local extreme plus a log-time requirement.
  • A single comparison at mid proves an answer exists on one side.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 852 (one peak, same loop).
  2. This problem: say the linear scan, then the slope invariant, then code the lo < hi loop.
  3. Then 153 (rotated minimum) and 1095 (peak + two searches); stretch: 1901 (2D).
