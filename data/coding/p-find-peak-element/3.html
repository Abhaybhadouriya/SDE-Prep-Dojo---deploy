Recognise the pattern

When you see "return any local max/min" + "O(log n)" + neighbours are distinct, think binary search on the slope, not on values. The predicate "nums[mid] < nums[mid+1]" decides which half certainly holds an answer. Same family: mountain arrays, rotated arrays, "first bad version".

Try a few tiny inputs and look at the slope at the middle:

InputSlope at midPeak guaranteed inNote
[1,3,2]mid=1: 3 > 2, down[0, 1]mid itself is the peak.
[1,2,3]mid=1: 2 < 3, up[2, 2]Keep climbing; the end beats minus infinity.
[3,1,2]mid=1: 1 < 2, up[2, 2]Index 0 is also a peak, but we do not need it: one is enough.
