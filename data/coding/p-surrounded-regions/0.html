The story

Explain like I'm 5

A garden is drawn on squared paper: some squares are hedge (X), some are open grass (O). Rain water pours in from outside the paper. Water can flow from grass square to grass square sideways, never through hedge. Grass the water reaches stays grass. Grass the water can never reach is a closed pocket, and the gardener fills it with hedge. You do not check each pocket; you just let the water in from the edges and see where it goes.

From your world

This is reachability from a set of sources, like marking every host reachable from the uplink ports after a link failure: start a BFS from all the "outside" ports at once, and whatever is not marked is isolated. A multi-source BFS from the border is one traversal, not one per region, the same way you would not run a separate reachability check per host.
