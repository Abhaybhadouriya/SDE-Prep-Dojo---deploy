Dry run

Example 2. Blue # = tagged safe (bright = tagged in this step), purple O = not reached yet.

  1. The board. Instead of asking 'is this O surrounded?', ask the opposite: which O can reach the border? Those are safe; every other O is captured.
  2. Walk the four edges. Every O on the border is safe by definition: tag it # and push it. (Edge order: left and right columns, then top and bottom rows.)
  3. BFS pops the seeds. (3,4) has an open neighbour (3,3); (4,2) has (3,2). Both join the safe set. Most seeds, like (0,0), have no open neighbour.
  4. (3,3) reaches (2,3). The queue empties. The three O cells in the top-left middle were never reached: no path of O cells joins them to the border.
  5. One last pass over the board: an untagged O becomes X, a # goes back to O. Every cell is pushed at most once, so the whole job is O(m·n).
