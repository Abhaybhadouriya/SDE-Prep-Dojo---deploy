The problem (in our words)

You get an m x n board where every cell holds the character 'X' or 'O'. Two 'O' cells are connected if they share a side (up, down, left, right). A region is a maximal group of connected 'O' cells. A region is surrounded when none of its cells lies on the outer edge of the board, so it is completely closed in by 'X' cells.

Capture every surrounded region by turning all of its 'O' cells into 'X'. Do it in place: modify the given board and return nothing. Regions that touch the edge, and everything connected to them, stay as they are.

Signature: void solve(char[][] board).

  • m == board.length, n == board[i].length
  • 1 <= m, n <= 200
  • board[i][j] is 'X' or 'O'

Practice it: LeetCode 130 · GfG Replace O's with X's (same task) · takeuforward article

Constraints tell you: up to 200 x 200 = 40,000 cells, so O(m·n) is the target. A single region can snake through ~20,000 cells, which is deeper than Python's default recursion limit (1,000) and risky for Java's default stack: use BFS or an explicit stack. "In place" means you need a third marker (we use '#') or a separate visited grid.
