Recognise the pattern

Cue: a grid where a region's fate depends on whether it touches the border (or any fixed set of cells): "surrounded", "enclosed", "closed island", "can reach the ocean", "enclaves". Flip the question and run one multi-source flood from the border; whatever it does not reach is the answer.
