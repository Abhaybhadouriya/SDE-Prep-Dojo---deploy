The problem (in our words)

Given an integer numRows, return the first numRows rows of Pascal's triangle as a list of lists. Row i (0-indexed) has i + 1 numbers; its first and last numbers are 1, and every other number equals the sum of the two numbers directly above it in row i - 1 (the one above-left and the one above).

Signature: List<List<Integer>> generate(int numRows).

  • 1 <= numRows <= 30

Practice it: LeetCode 118 · GfG Pascal Triangle · TUF article · CodeChef PASTRL

Constraints tell you: with 30 rows the largest entry is C(29, 14) = 77,558,760, which fits a 32-bit int; at row 34 the middle entry passes 231. The output has 465 numbers, so efficiency is not the question; clean indexing is.
