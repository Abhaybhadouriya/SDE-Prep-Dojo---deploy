How to recognise a monotonic stack problem

When you see "the first / nearest element to the left or right that is bigger or smaller", think monotonic stack. When you see "sum over all subarrays of the min or max", think contribution: previous and next smaller.

  • "Next warmer day", "next bigger price", "how many days until": next greater, answer at pop time, distance i - top.
  • "Span", "how many consecutive previous days were at most today": previous greater, answer at push time (LC 901).
  • "Largest rectangle", "widest area bounded by the shortest": increasing stack, settle each bar when it is popped (LC 84, 85).
  • "Sum of min / max / range over all subarrays", "number of subarrays where x is the min": contribution with one strict and one non-strict side (LC 907, 2104, 1856).
  • "Smallest / largest number or string after removing", "lexicographically smallest subsequence": greedy stack with a budget (LC 402, 316, 1673).
  • "Who can see whom", "collisions", "fleets": a stack where an arrival removes the ones it blocks or merges (LC 1944, 735, 853).
  • A window that slides and you need its max or min: that is the monotonic deque, because elements also leave from the old end. See the monotonic deque deep dive.
