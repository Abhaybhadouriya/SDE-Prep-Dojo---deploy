Common mistakes

  • Wrong strictness. "Greater" versus "greater or equal" decides < versus <= in the pop condition. Read it off the statement, then test on an input with equal values, such as [2, 2, 2].
  • Double counting ties in contribution problems. Both boundaries strict (or both non-strict) over-counts (or under-counts) subarrays with repeated minimums. One strict, one non-strict.
  • Storing values instead of indexes. You then cannot compute widths or distances. Push indexes; read values through them.
  • Forgetting the leftovers. Elements still on the stack at the end have no next greater (-1), and in the histogram they still need their rectangles: use the sentinel 0 or a final flush loop.
  • Wrong width in LC 84. After popping top, the left boundary is the new top, not top itself: width = i - newTop - 1, or i when the stack is empty.
  • Pushing twice in the circular version. Push only when i < n, otherwise the second lap overwrites answers or the stack holds duplicates.
  • Remove K Digits edge cases. Leftover k (cut the tail), leading zeros ("10200", k = 1 gives "200"), and the empty result ("10", k = 2 gives "0").
  • 32-bit overflow. Contribution products and LC 84 areas on big inputs: use 64-bit, take the modulo after every multiplication in LC 907.
  • Using a stack when elements also expire. A sliding window maximum needs a deque: old indexes leave from the bottom.
