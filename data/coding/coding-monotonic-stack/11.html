Remember this

Revision card

  • Cue: Next / previous greater or smaller, histogram area, sum of subarray minimums, remove k digits.
  • Insight: Pop every element the new value answers; what remains is sorted and still waiting.
  • State: stack of indexes, decreasing for next greater, increasing for next smaller.
  • Trap: Strict vs non-strict on both sides double-counts in contribution sums; forgetting the final sentinel.
  • Cost: Each index pushed and popped once: O(n) time, O(n) space.

More to remember

  • Pop whatever the new element answers. Next greater: pop while top < x, stack decreasing.
  • Answers written at pop time give the NEXT element; at push time (the top after popping) give the PREVIOUS one.
  • Push indexes. Each is pushed once and popped once: O(n).
  • Histogram: pop bar, width = i - newTop - 1, sentinel 0 at the end.
  • Contribution: a[i] x (i - prev) x (next - i), strict on one side and non-strict on the other.
  • Greedy stack: the stack is the answer; pop a bigger top while the budget allows.
  • Elements that also expire from the old end: use a deque, not a stack.
