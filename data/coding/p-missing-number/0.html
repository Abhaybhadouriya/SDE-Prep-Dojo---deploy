The story

Explain like I'm 5

A teacher hands out raffle tickets numbered 0 to 10 to ten children, so one ticket stays in her pocket and nobody remembers which. She could line the tickets up and look for the gap. Faster: she knows 0 + 1 + ... + 10 = 55. She adds up the ten tickets the children hold, gets 49, and knows at once that ticket 6 is the one in her pocket.

From your world

This is RAID-5 parity. A stripe stores the XOR of all data blocks; when one disk dies, XOR the parity with the surviving blocks and the lost block falls out, because every surviving block appears twice and cancels. Here the "parity" is the XOR of 0..n, which you can compute without storing anything, and the surviving blocks are the array values.
