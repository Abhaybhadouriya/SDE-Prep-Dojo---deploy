The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two friends walk to the same park along different streets that join into one road. One street is longer. To meet exactly at the junction, each friend walks their own street and the shared road, then teleports to the start of the other friend's street and walks again. Both have now walked "my street + road + your street", the same total, so they arrive at the junction together.

From your world

Think of two sk_buff fragment chains or two route chains that end in the same shared tail of nodes. "Same node" means the same address, not the same payload: you compare pointers, just as the kernel compares struct list_head * values, never the data behind them.
