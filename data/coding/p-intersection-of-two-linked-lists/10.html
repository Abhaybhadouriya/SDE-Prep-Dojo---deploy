How to recognise it:

  • Two chains that share their tail (a Y or a tree seen from two leaves with parent pointers).
  • "First common node" with O(1) memory: equalise lengths, then lockstep.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 876 (pointer distance on one list).
  2. This problem: hash set, then length difference, then switch heads.
  3. Then LC 1650 (same trick on a tree) and LC 142 (the cycle reduction).
