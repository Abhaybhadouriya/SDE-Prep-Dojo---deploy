Recognise the pattern

When you see two singly linked lists that may share a tail, think "line the pointers up": equalise the distance to the end, then walk in lockstep and compare node identity.

When you see "O(1) memory" with two lists of unknown lengths, think switching heads: walking A + B and B + A gives both pointers the same length.

Shared nodes are always a suffix of both lists: once they meet, they cannot split again, because each node has one next. So the problem is only "the two own parts have different lengths a and b". Fix that difference and the join is the first place the pointers are equal.

Taxonomy: Linked list → merge / rebuild (variant: align two lists). Pattern lesson: Linked lists.
