Explain like I'm 5

Friends tell you when they will use the shared bicycle: "from 2 to 5", "from 1 to 3", "from 9 to 12". You want the list of times when the bike is busy, with no repeats. Line the notes up by their start time. Walk along: if the next note starts before the current busy time ends, the busy time just gets longer. If it starts later, the current busy time is finished and a new one begins.
