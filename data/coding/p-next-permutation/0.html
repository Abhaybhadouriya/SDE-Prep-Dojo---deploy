The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have number tiles 1, 3, 5, 4, 2 on a shelf and a rule: every morning, rearrange them into the next bigger "word" in dictionary order, using the same tiles. Looking from the right, 2, 4, 5 keep climbing, so the last three tiles are already in their biggest possible order; shuffling only them cannot make the word bigger. The first tile you can improve is the 3. Swap it for the next bigger tile on its right (the 4), and then put the tiles after it in the smallest order: 1, 4, 2, 3, 5.

The whole problem is about changing as little as possible, as far to the right as possible. A change further left makes the result jump much more.

From your world

It is the same move as incrementing a counter. In 0x2FFF + 1, the trailing F digits are already at their maximum; you find the rightmost digit that can still grow (the 2), bump it to 3, and reset everything to its right to the minimum (0x3000). Here the "maximum" suffix is the non-increasing run, and "reset to the minimum" is the reverse.
