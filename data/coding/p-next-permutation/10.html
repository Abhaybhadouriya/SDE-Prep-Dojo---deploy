How to recognise it:

  • "Next / previous / k-th arrangement" or "smallest bigger number with the same digits".
  • The answer must be found in place without listing alternatives (n! is too many).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 1053 Previous Permutation With One Swap (pivot scan only).
  2. This problem: all three steps; test [3,3,3], [1,5,1] and the full descending case.
  3. Then: 556 Next Greater Element III (overflow check), and 60 Permutation Sequence (factorial jumps).
