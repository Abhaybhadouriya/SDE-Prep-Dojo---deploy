Example 1, grid = [[2,4,1],[1,7,3],[5,1,2]]. Left: the costs, with the current cell ringed. Right: the best table; the two ringed cells are the "above" and "left" inputs of the new value.

  1. Cell (0,0), cost 2: start: best = grid[0][0] = 2.
  2. Cell (0,1), cost 4: top row: 2 + 4 = 6 (only from the left).
  3. Cell (0,2), cost 1: top row: 6 + 1 = 7.
  4. Cell (1,0), cost 1: left column: 2 + 1 = 3 (only from above).
  5. Cell (1,1), cost 7: min(above 6, left 3) + 7 = 10.
  6. Cell (1,2), cost 3: min(above 7, left 10) + 3 = 10.
  7. Cell (2,0), cost 5: left column: 3 + 5 = 8.
  8. Cell (2,1), cost 1: min(above 10, left 8) + 1 = 9.
  9. Cell (2,2), cost 2: min(above 10, left 9) + 2 = 11. This is the answer: 11.
  10. To print the path, start at the bottom-right and step to whichever of above / left has the smaller best value (11 came from 9, 9 from 8, 8 from 3, 3 from 2): 2 -> 1 -> 5 -> 1 -> 2 = 11.
