How to recognise it:

  • Things open and close, and closing must match the most recent open one.
  • "Valid", "balanced", "well-formed", or a repair cost ("minimum removals/additions").

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 921 (counter).
  2. This problem (stack of closers).
  3. LC 1249 (repair with indexes), then LC 32 (index gaps).
