The story

Explain like I'm 5

A magic bean triples every night; how many beans do you have after 13 nights? Counting night by night takes 13 steps. A faster way: you know what 1 night does; two nights is "1 night, twice"; four nights is "2 nights, twice"; eight nights is "4 nights, twice". Since 13 = 8 + 4 + 1, you combine the 8-night, 4-night and 1-night effects and you are done in a handful of steps.

That is the whole algorithm: build x, x², x⁴, x⁸ ... by squaring, and multiply in the ones that the binary digits of n ask for.

From your world

It is a shift-and-test loop over the bits of an integer, the same shape as a software multiply or popcount loop: if (e & 1) ...; e >>= 1;. The same loop with a modulus is what RSA and Diffie-Hellman use for modular exponentiation in TLS handshakes.
