Common mistakes

Skipping the verification pass. Pass 1 always produces a survivor, even when there is no celebrity (example 2). Returning it directly is wrong.

Verifying only one direction. Checking "everyone knows cand" without "cand knows nobody" (or the other way round) accepts wrong answers; the edge-case row with 4 people fails exactly one direction.

Counting knows(i, i). Everyone knows themselves (graph[i][i] == 1). Without the i != cand skip, the check "cand knows nobody" fails on cand itself and you always return -1.

Going over the 3n budget. Re-asking knows(cand, i) for i > cand costs up to n - 1 extra calls; the judge's follow-up counts them. Skip them, or cache the pass-1 answers.

Building the whole matrix first. Calling knows for all pairs to fill a matrix and then computing degrees is the O(n2) brute force in disguise.
