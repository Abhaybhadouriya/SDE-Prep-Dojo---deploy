The story

Explain like I'm 5

At a birthday party there may be one famous magician. Everyone knows the magician, and the magician knows nobody. You may only ask one question at a time: "Does Ann know Ben?" Instead of asking about every pair, you hold one "maybe magician" card. Each time you ask whether the card holder knows the next child, one of the two is crossed off: either the holder (magicians know nobody) or the next child (if the holder does not know them, everyone does not know them, so they are not famous). At the end only one card holder is left, and you check that one.

From your world

It is a tournament-style election, like picking a root bridge or a leader by pairwise comparisons: each comparison knocks one contender out, so n - 1 comparisons leave one survivor. The survivor is only the possible winner; you still have to verify it, the way a leader-election result is confirmed by everyone acknowledging it.
