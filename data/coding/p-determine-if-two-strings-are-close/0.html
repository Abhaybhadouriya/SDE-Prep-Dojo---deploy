The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a row of coloured beads. You may shuffle the beads in any order, and you may also pick two colours that are already on the string and repaint every red bead blue and every blue bead red at the same time. You can never bring in a colour that is not there. So what really describes your string? Which colours it has, and how big the colour piles are ("one pile of 3, one of 2, one of 1"). If your friend's string has the same colours and the same pile sizes, you can turn yours into theirs.

From your world

Think of two histograms of packet sizes per flow ID. Operation 1 is "the order of the samples does not matter" (you only keep the histogram). Operation 2 is "flow IDs are just labels and you may swap two of them". After throwing away both orders, all that is left to compare is the set of IDs in use and the sorted list of bucket heights, which is exactly what this solution compares.
