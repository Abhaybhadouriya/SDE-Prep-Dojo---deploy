How to recognise it:

  • A string must be cut into pieces and each piece is checked against a set or a rule.
  • Greedy cutting fails because one piece can be a prefix of another.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 70, then LC 91 (counting splits).
  2. This problem: recursion, memo, then the bottom-up ok[] with the length cap.
  3. Then LC 2707 (minimise leftovers), LC 140 (all sentences), LC 472 (many words at once).
