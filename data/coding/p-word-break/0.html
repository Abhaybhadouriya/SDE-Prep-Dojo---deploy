The story

Explain like I'm 5

A shop sign lost all its spaces: "seashell". You have a box of word cards. Can you lay cards side by side so they spell the sign exactly? You walk along the sign and put a small flag after every letter where "everything up to here can be made from cards". The first flag goes at the very start. After that, a new flag goes at position i if some card fits right before i and a flag already stands where that card starts. If the last position gets a flag, the sign can be made.

From your world

Think of decoding a byte stream with no delimiters, where an opcode table has variable-length entries and some are prefixes of others, so the encoding is not a prefix code. A greedy decoder that takes the longest (or shortest) match can get stuck. The DP keeps a bitmap of offsets that are reachable by a valid decode, like marking valid instruction boundaries.
