How to recognise it:

  • "Two groups", "equal", "minimum difference" + every item in exactly one group.
  • Values and n are small enough that n x (sum) fits in a few million steps.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: GfG Subset Sum (any target), then this problem with the 2-D table.
  2. Rewrite it with one row; then 494 Target Sum and 1049 Last Stone Weight II.
  3. Contrast with 518 Coin Change II (loop up), then 698 Partition to K Equal Sum Subsets.
