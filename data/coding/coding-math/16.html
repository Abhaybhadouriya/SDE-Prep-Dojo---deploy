How many multiplications does fast power need for xe?

  1. About log2 e (times 2 at most)
  2. About e
  3. Always 32
  4. About sqrt(e)
One squaring per bit of e, plus one multiply for each set bit.

In the sieve, why can the crossing for prime p start at p*p?

  1. It is faster to start anywhere
  2. p*p is the first multiple of p that is even
  3. Smaller multiples k*p (k < p) were already crossed by a prime factor of k
  4. Because p*p is prime
k < p means k has a prime factor smaller than p, which already crossed out k*p.

Which is the modular inverse of a modulo a prime p (a not divisible by p)?

  1. ap-1 mod p
  2. ap mod p
  3. ap-2 mod p
  4. p / a
Fermat: a^(p-1) = 1 mod p, so a times a^(p-2) = 1.

Max Points on a Line: why use a reduced (dy, dx) pair instead of dy / dx as a double?

  1. Different slopes can round to the same double and vertical lines divide by zero
  2. Doubles are slower
  3. Pairs use less memory
  4. Hash maps cannot store doubles
Exact integer keys avoid precision errors and the dx = 0 special case.

Reservoir sampling with one slot: with what probability does item i replace the kept item?

  1. 1/2
  2. 1/n
  3. i/n
  4. 1/i
1/i at item i gives a final probability of 1/n for every item.

  1. Use base 27
  2. Add 1 before taking % 26
  3. Use base 25
  4. Subtract 1 before taking % 26
