Common mistakes

One min variable. It is correct until the first pop of the minimum; then the previous minimum is gone and can only be found by a scan.

Two stacks with < on push. With 5, 1, 1 the second 1 is not pushed to the min stack, the first pop removes the only 1 from it, and getMin wrongly returns 5.

Comparing boxed values in Java. stack.peek() == minStack.peek() on two Integer objects compares references and fails for values outside -128..127. Use .equals or store int[].

Difference trick in 32 bits. val - min for val = 231-1 and min = -231 overflows an int. Use long.
