Dry run

The pair version on example 1. The glowing frame is the top; the right column is the stored minimum.

  1. Empty stack, so 4 is its own minimum: store (4, 4).
  2. 7 is larger than the min below (4): store (7, min(7, 4)) = (7, 4).
  3. 2 is smaller than 4: store (2, 2). The new top now carries the new minimum.
  4. getMin reads the min field of the top frame: 2. No scan.
  5. Pop (2, 2). The frame below already remembers that the min of 4 and 7 is 4.
  6. top reads the value field of the top frame: 7.
  7. getMin is 4 again with no recomputation: the answer was saved when 7 was pushed.
