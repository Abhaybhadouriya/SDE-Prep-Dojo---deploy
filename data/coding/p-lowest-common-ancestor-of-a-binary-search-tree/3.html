Recognise the pattern

Cue: "binary search tree" + a question about two nodes or a value range -> use the ordering to choose ONE child at each node, like binary search. The split point of the range [lo, hi] is the LCA.

The key fact: for a node with value v, everything in its left subtree is < v and everything in its right subtree is > v. So "both smaller", "both larger" and "one on each side (or equal)" are the only three cases, and only the third one stops the walk.
