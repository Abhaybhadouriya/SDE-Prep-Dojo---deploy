The story

Explain like I'm 5

A library sorts its shelves like a decision tree: at each junction a sign says "titles before M go left, the rest go right". Two friends are looking for two different books. They start together at the entrance and walk together as long as both signs point the same way. The first junction where one must turn left and the other right is the last place they stand together. That junction is their "lowest common ancestor".

The general LCA problem (any binary tree) needs a full search, because nothing tells you which subtree holds a node. A BST gives you that information for free at every node, so the search becomes a single walk down one path.

From your world

This is longest-prefix routing in a binary trie: two IP addresses share a path from the root until the first bit where they differ, and the node at that bit is their common prefix. In a BST the "bit" is the comparison with the node's value, and the split node is the LCA.
