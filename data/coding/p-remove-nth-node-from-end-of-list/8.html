Common mistakes

Stopping on the target instead of its predecessor. Looping while lead (not while lead.next) leaves trail on the node to delete, and a singly linked node cannot unlink itself. Either start trail on the dummy and loop to the last node, or open a gap of n + 1.

No dummy, and n == length. Starting both pointers on head and opening a gap of n makes lead null; then lead.next crashes. If you skip the dummy, check if not lead: return head.next right after the gap loop.

Returning head instead of dummy.next. When the head was deleted, head still points at the removed node.

Off-by-one in the two-pass version. The predecessor is L - n - 1 steps from the head (0-based), not L - n.

C/C++: unlinking without freeing, or freeing before relinking. Save victim = trail->next, relink, then delete victim.
