How to recognise it:

  • A position counted from the end of a forward-only list.
  • A delete or cut where the first node may be affected: use a dummy and stop on the predecessor.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 876 (fast/slow speeds).
  2. This problem: two-pass first, then the one-pass gap.
  3. Then LC 1721 (swap with the same walk) and LC 61 (rotate, k mod L).
