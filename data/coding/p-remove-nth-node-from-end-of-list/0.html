Explain like I'm 5

A train of carriages goes into a tunnel and you can only walk from the engine backwards. You are told to unhook the 2nd carriage from the back. Send a friend 2 carriages ahead of you, then both of you walk at the same speed. When your friend reaches the last carriage, you are standing right in front of the one to unhook.

From your world

This is the "keep the last n entries" trick from a trace ring buffer, but with zero buffer: two cursors at a fixed distance. And the dummy head is the same idea as walking a singly linked list in C with struct node **pp = &head; so that unlinking the first element needs no special branch. In the kernel's hlist, pprev exists for exactly this reason.
