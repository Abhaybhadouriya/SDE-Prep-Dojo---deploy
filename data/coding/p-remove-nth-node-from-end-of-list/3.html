Recognise the pattern

When you see "k-th from the end" of a singly linked list (or "last k", "rotate by k"), think two pointers with a fixed gap of k: when the leader reaches the end, the follower is k behind it.

When you see "delete a node" where the deleted node may be the head, think dummy head, and stop the follower one node before the target.

Position from the end is L - n from the front, but L is unknown until you reach the end. Two pointers with a fixed gap turn "distance from the end" into "distance from another pointer", which you can keep constant while walking forwards.

Taxonomy: Linked list → fast/slow pointers (variant: k-th from end). Pattern lesson: Linked lists.
