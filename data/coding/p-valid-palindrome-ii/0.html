The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two friends read a word from both ends at the same time, one from the front, one from the back, saying letters out loud. As long as they say the same letter, all is well. The first time they disagree, they get one eraser. One of the two letters they just read must go. They try erasing the front one and keep reading; if that fails, they try the back one. If neither works, no single erase can fix the word.

From your world

It is like comparing two copies of a packet stream that may differ by one dropped byte: compare until the first difference, then resync by skipping one byte on either side and check the rest with no more slack. One mismatch spends the only budget, so there is never a third branch and never backtracking deeper.
