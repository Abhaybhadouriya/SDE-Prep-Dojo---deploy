How to recognise it:

  • A palindrome check plus a tiny edit budget (1, sometimes 2) -> two pointers, spend the budget at mismatches.
  • A budget of k (input-sized) -> DP on intervals or LPS.

Practice order:

  1. 125 Valid Palindrome.
  2. This problem, then 2330 Valid Palindrome IV (changes instead of deletions).
  3. 1216 Valid Palindrome III (the DP generalisation).
