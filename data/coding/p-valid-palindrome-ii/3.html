Recognise the pattern

Cue: "palindrome" + "after at most one change/deletion" -> opposite-end two pointers. The first mismatch decides everything: the fix must touch one of the two mismatched positions, so branch exactly once into two plain checks. With k deletions instead of one, the branching blows up and it becomes DP (LC 1216).
