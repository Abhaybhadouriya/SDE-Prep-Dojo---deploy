The story

Explain like I'm 5

Kids stand on a number line in order, some on the minus side, some on the plus side. Each kid's "size" is how far they are from zero, and you must line them up from smallest to biggest. The biggest kid is always standing at one of the two ends of the line. So you look at the two end kids, send the bigger one to the back of the new line, and repeat.

From your world

It is the merge step of merge sort, run backwards: the negative half, read right to left, and the positive half, read left to right, are each sorted by magnitude. Two cursors and one output cursor, like merging two sorted run queues or two sorted timer lists, except here both lists live in one buffer and you consume them from the outside in.
