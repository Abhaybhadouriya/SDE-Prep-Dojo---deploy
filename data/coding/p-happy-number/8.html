Common mistakes

Starting slow and fast at the same value with a while (slow != fast) loop. The condition is false immediately and the loop never runs. Either start fast one step ahead (as here) or use a do-while.

Only checking slow == 1. Fast reaches 1 first; checking fast (or the meeting value) is enough because 1 is a fixed point and the two pointers will then meet at 1 anyway.

No termination argument. Saying "it loops until 1" without the "bounded by 243" proof leaves the interviewer's main question unanswered.

String conversion in a hot loop. sum(int(c)**2 for c in str(n)) is fine in Python but in Java/C++ % 10 and / 10 are cleaner and allocation-free.
