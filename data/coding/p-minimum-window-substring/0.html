The story

Explain like I'm 5

Your shopping list says: one apple, one banana, one cherry. The market is a long line of stalls. You walk forward until your basket has everything on the list. Then you ask, "Could I have started later?" and you hand back stalls from the start of your walk, one by one, as long as the basket still has everything. When you would lose something you need, you walk forward again. The shortest walk you ever had is the answer.

From your world

Think of a packet capture: "find the shortest stretch of the capture that contains a SYN, a SYN-ACK and an ACK". You do not re-scan the whole capture for every start packet; you keep a count of what the current stretch holds and move its two ends forward. The counts are like per-type counters in a ring buffer: add on enqueue, subtract on dequeue.
