Revision card

  • Cue: Palindromic substrings or subsequences, minimum cuts, burst balloons, merge stones.
  • Insight: Answers for a range come from smaller ranges: peel the ends or pick the split point that makes sides independent.
  • State: 2n - 1 centres for substrings; dp[i][j] filled by length; 1-D cut[] for partitioning.
  • Trap: Filling with i ascending reads cells not yet filled; even-length centres forgotten.
  • Cost: Centres O(n^2) time O(1) space; interval DP O(n^2) to O(n^3).

More to remember

  • Substrings: 2n-1 centres, O(n2) time, O(1) memory; one loop finds the longest and counts them all.
  • Subsequences: dp[i][j] peeling the ends; equal ends: inside + 2; else max of dropping one end.
  • Fill by length, or with i descending. Never i ascending.
  • Partitioning: a 1-D cut array, cut[0] = -1, relaxed from every palindrome found by centres.
  • Split-point DP: pick the event that makes the two sides independent (last balloon, first cut). O(n3).
  • Egg drop: f[t][e] = f[t-1][e-1] + f[t-1][e] + 1; grow t until it covers n.
