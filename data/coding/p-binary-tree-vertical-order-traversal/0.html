Explain like I'm 5

A family photo is taken on a staircase-shaped stand. Each child stands one row below their parent, half a step to the left or right. The photographer wants a caption listed by vertical stripes: the leftmost stripe first, and inside a stripe, read from the top row down; if two people in the same row share a stripe, the one standing further left on the stand is named first.

This is Meta's favourite tree question (it is in a large share of Meta SDE-2 phone screens reported in 2025). It looks like the Hard LC 987, but its tie rule is simpler, and that difference turns an O(n log n) sort into an O(n) BFS.

From your world

Think of a hash table of per-flow queues filled by one pass over a packet log that is already in time order: you never sort, because arrival order is the order you want. BFS is that time-ordered log, and the column is the flow key.
