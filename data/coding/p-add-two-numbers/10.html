Closest match: LC 445. Add Two Numbers II Medium

  • Same: two numbers as digit lists, return their sum as a list.
  • Different: digits are stored most significant first, and the inputs should not be modified.
  • So: push digits onto two stacks (or reverse copies), add with a carry, and build the result by inserting at the head.

How to recognise it:

  • Numbers too long for built-in types, given as digits.
  • Digit order decides the tool: ones-first means walk forward; most-significant-first means stacks, reversal or recursion.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 415 and LC 67 (strings).
  2. This problem: the one-pass loop with or carry.
  3. Then LC 445 (forward order, stacks) and LC 43 (multiplication).
