Recognise the pattern

When you see "numbers as digit lists / strings / arrays" + "add (or subtract, multiply)", think schoolbook arithmetic from the least significant digit with a carry variable.

When you see "build a new list while walking two lists of different lengths", think one loop with while a or b or carry and treat a finished list as 0, plus a dummy head.

Two facts carry the whole solution: the digit sum is at most 9 + 9 + 1 = 19, so the carry is always 0 or 1; and because the head is the ones digit, the first digits you can compute are the first nodes you need to output.

Taxonomy: Linked list → merge and rebuild. Pattern lesson: Linked lists; digit arithmetic is also in Math.
