Common mistakes

Dropping the final carry. Looping only while a or b turns 5 + 5 into [0]. Add or carry to the condition (or append a node after the loop).

Stopping at the shorter list. while a and b loses the high digits of the longer number. Treat a finished list as 0.

Advancing a null pointer. a = a.next when a is already null crashes. Advance only if the list still has a node.

Reversing the inputs first. The digits are already ones-first; reversing them makes you add from the most significant digit, which is wrong here (that is the LC 445 setup).

Converting to a 64-bit integer. It passes small tests and fails at 20+ digits.
