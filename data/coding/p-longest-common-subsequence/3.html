Recognise the pattern

Cue: two sequences, "longest / fewest edits / how many ways", order must be kept -> DP on two prefixes, dp[i][j] = answer for a[0..i) and b[0..j). Decide by comparing a[i-1] and b[j-1].

LCS is the parent of a family: Edit Distance adds a cost per operation, Delete Operation and Minimum ASCII Delete Sum are "length minus LCS" in disguise, Longest Palindromic Subsequence is LCS(s, reverse(s)).
