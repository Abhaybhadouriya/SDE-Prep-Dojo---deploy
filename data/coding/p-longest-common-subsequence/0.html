Explain like I'm 5

Two friends wrote down the songs they heard on a road trip, but each skipped some. What is the longest list of songs that appears in both notebooks in the same order? You may skip songs, but you may not reorder them. Looking at the last song of each notebook: if they are the same, it is surely in the answer; if not, at least one of them is not, so try throwing away each one and keep the better result.

That "look at the last item, two choices" step repeats on shorter and shorter prefixes, and the same pairs of prefixes come up many times. That is a 2D table indexed by two prefix lengths.

From your world

diff and git diff are built on this: the lines that stay are a longest common subsequence of the two files, and everything else is shown as added or deleted. Patch tools and DNA aligners use the same table (Myers' algorithm is a faster variant for files that are mostly equal).
