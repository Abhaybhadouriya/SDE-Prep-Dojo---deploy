The problem (in our words)

A cipher clerk receives two strings of the same length, s and t. They are isomorphic if you can obtain t by replacing the characters of s: every occurrence of a character is replaced by the same character, the order of characters is kept, and no two different characters may be replaced by the same character. A character may map to itself. Return true if s and t are isomorphic, otherwise false.

  • Input: two strings s and t.
  • Output: a boolean. Signature: boolean isIsomorphic(String s, String t).

Constraints

  • 1 <= s.length <= 5 * 10^4
  • t.length == s.length
  • s and t consist of any valid ASCII characters (letters, digits, punctuation, spaces).

Constraints tell you: n = 5 · 10^4 makes all pairs of positions about 1.25 · 10^9 checks, too slow; one pass is the target. "Any ASCII" means the tables need 128 (or 256) slots, not 26, and digits and symbols must work too.
