How to recognise it:

  • "Palindrome" + "substring" (contiguous) + n in the low thousands.
  • A property of s[i..j] depends only on its two ends and s[i+1..j-1].

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 125 Valid Palindrome, then 680 Valid Palindrome II.
  2. This problem: the DP table first (to see the structure), then expand around centres.
  3. Then 647 Palindromic Substrings (one-line change), 516 (subsequence DP), 132 (table + cuts).
