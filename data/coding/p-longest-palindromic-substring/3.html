Recognise the pattern

When you see "palindromic substring" (contiguous), think expand around each of the 2n - 1 centres, or the interval DP pal[i][j] = s[i]==s[j] && pal[i+1][j-1]. If the word is "subsequence" (not contiguous), it is a different problem: LCS of s and reverse(s), see LC 516.

Try a few and watch where the centre sits:

InputLongestCentre
"xyx""xyx"on index 1 (odd length)
"xyyx""xyyx"between 1 and 2 (even length): an odd-only scan finds just "x"
"aaaa""aaaa"between 1 and 2; odd centres give at most 3
