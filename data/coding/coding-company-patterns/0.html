The story: every restaurant has a house special

Explain like I'm 5

All restaurants in town cook rice, dal and bread. Those are the basics, and a cook who cannot make them is not hired anywhere. But each restaurant also has a house special: one is famous for biryani, another for dosa, a third for pizza.

So you learn the basics first. A few weeks before your trial at one restaurant, you practise its house special until you can make it half asleep.

Coding interviews work the same way. Arrays, maps, trees, graphs and DP are the basics. Meta's special is short tree and stack problems with a twist; Amazon's is graphs; Uber's is hard graphs.

From your world

Before sending a patch to a kernel subsystem, you read the MAINTAINERS entry and a few months of that list's review threads. The C rules are the same everywhere (checkpatch, no sleeping in atomic context), but the networking maintainers ask about different things than the filesystem people. Company lists are those review threads: same language, different reviewers, different favourite questions.
