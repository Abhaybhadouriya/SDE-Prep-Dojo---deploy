Amazon

Rounds: an online assessment (Amazon's own SDE II page describes about 90 minutes for two coding questions plus a work-style survey), then four 55-minute rounds, each with about two Leadership Principle questions (see the Amazon guide). One round is the Bar Raiser.

Favourites (204 reports): graph BFS and topological sort lead (22%): Rotting Oranges (7), Course Schedule II (7), Course Schedule (6). Then DP (16%: House Robber, LCS, Word Break), trees and heaps (Reorganize String). On the all-time list, LRU Cache, Trapping Rain Water and Number of Islands all sit at 7.

What wins: working code with readable names. When a problem comes as a business story (warehouses, delivery routes, order dependencies), the first job is to see "this is a topological sort" through it. The OA is scored on hidden tests, so edge cases and complexity matter most there.
