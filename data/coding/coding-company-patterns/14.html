How to use the company filters

When you have more than one target company, merge their lists. The planner code above does this; here it is step by step with three tiny lists.

  1. Three saved company lists, each a list of (problem, frequency 1-8). The score table is empty.
  2. Add Meta: minrem 8, lru 6, islands 5.
  3. Add Amazon: lru grows to 13, islands to 12, oranges appears with 6.
  4. Add Google: lru 18, islands 17. A problem asked everywhere rises to the top even if no single company ranks it first.
  5. Keep the top 3 with a size-3 min-heap: when a fourth entry arrives, the smallest (oranges, 6) is popped. Plan: lru, islands, minrem.

On this site the same steps are a few clicks:

  1. Open Company lists and pick one, e.g. Meta. The header shows list size, solved count and how many rows are "high frequency" (5+ of 8).
  2. Read the Pattern mix chips: the company's fingerprint. Each chip opens the explorer filtered to that pattern and company.
  3. Filter to unsolved, sort by frequency, do the 5+ rows first. Problems solved anywhere on the site are already ticked.
  4. In the explorer, combine company + pattern + difficulty (e.g. Amazon, graphs, Medium) to drill one weakness.
  5. In the last two weeks, check the Recent reports row on each top problem's page: dates, level and round from 2024-26 posts.

Time windows. Each saved list has one window, shown next to its size: Google, Meta, Amazon, Uber and Apple are "all time"; Microsoft, LinkedIn and Oracle are "last 6 months"; Flipkart, Walmart and Goldman Sachs are "older than 6 months". All-time lists over-weight classics; correct them with the recent reports.
