Microsoft

Rounds: an online test with two DSA problems (Codility or HackerRank), then 2-3 DSA rounds for SDE-1 or 1-2 for SDE-2, where interviewers often ask you to dry-run and sometimes write unit tests (see the Microsoft guide). India loops are often a one-day hiring drive.

Favourites (164 reports): data-structure design leads (12%): LRU Cache (8 reports, the most of any company), Min Stack (4). Then arrays, graphs and stacks: Generate Parentheses, Find the Duplicate Number, Container With Most Water, Next Permutation. The 6-month Premium list (434 problems) has Two Sum, Add Two Numbers and Longest Substring at 7-8.

What wins: classic problems done carefully, with pointer-level correctness (linked lists, in-place array tricks). A kernel C background is a real advantage here: say why your linked-list code cannot lose a node.
