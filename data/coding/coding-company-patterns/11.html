Uber

Rounds: Uber's backend guide lists a HackerRank test, a 60-minute phone screen with coding plus design, and two 60-minute coding rounds; SDE-2 posts add a CodeSignal or HackerRank OA of 2-4 problems in 60-90 minutes and a separate LLD round with running code (see the Uber guide).

Favourites (69 reports): graphs, by far (36%): Alien Dictionary (7), Number of Islands II (union-find), Making A Large Island, Bus Routes (8 on the saved list), Cheapest Flights. Then design: First Unique Number (4), Design Hit Counter, Insert Delete GetRandom O(1).

What wins: graph modelling (what is a node, what is an edge) and union-find. 45% of Uber's reported questions are Hard, so this is the one company where Hard graph practice pays.
