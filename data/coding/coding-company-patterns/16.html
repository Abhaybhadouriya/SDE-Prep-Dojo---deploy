Common mistakes with company lists

  • Starting with the company list. Lists are sorted by frequency, not by pattern, so you jump between topics and never build the template. Do the patterns first.
  • Memorising answers for Meta. Almost a third of Meta reports are variants. Practise the variant: change one rule of the problem and solve it again.
  • Grinding Hards for every company. Only Uber (45%) and Google (30%) have a large Hard share in recent reports. Elsewhere, speed on Mediums matters more.
  • Trusting the "all time" order blindly. Two Sum is 8 on the Google list, but only 2 of 84 recent Google reports named it. Cross-check with the recent reports on the problem page.
  • Skipping Premium-only problems for Meta, Uber and LinkedIn. 20-34% of their reported problems are Premium-only. The problem pages here restate them so you can practise anyway.
