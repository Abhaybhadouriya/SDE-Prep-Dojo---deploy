Google

Rounds: 45-minute coding rounds, usually one Medium-Hard problem plus follow-ups; often a shared doc with no compiler, so dry-running by hand matters (see the Google guide). Mix: 58% Medium, 30% Hard in recent reports, the highest Hard share among the big four.

Favourites: graphs (24% of recent questions: islands, shortest paths with a twist like Find the Safest Path in a Grid and Cheapest Flights Within K Stops), arrays and DP (LIS, Job Scheduling), and small design problems such as Logger Rate Limiter and Detect Squares. The pool is wide: 70 distinct problems in 84 reports.

What wins: clarifying questions, a clean solution, then the follow-up ("what if the grid does not fit in memory?", "what if queries arrive online?"). Practise follow-ups, not a second Hard.
