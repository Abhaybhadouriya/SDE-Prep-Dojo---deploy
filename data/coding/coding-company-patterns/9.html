Rounds: a machine coding round (90 minutes to 3 hours, a runnable in-memory app) that usually comes first and eliminates, then a 60-minute PSDS round (problem solving and data structures) with about two Medium problems (see the Flipkart guide).

Favourites (88 reports): trees (19%), sliding window (14%) and binary search (13%): Sliding Window Maximum (6), Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters (4), Maximal Square (4), Symmetric Tree (4), Koko Eating Bananas (3). The saved list (100 problems) is 29% Hard, the hardest of the seven, with stacks such as Asteroid Collision near the top.

What wins: two techniques sit behind many of these problems. Learn them from Binary search on the answer and Monotonic deque.
