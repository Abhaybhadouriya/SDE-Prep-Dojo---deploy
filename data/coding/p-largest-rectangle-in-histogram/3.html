Recognise the pattern

Cue: "for every element, how far can it extend while it stays the minimum" or "first smaller element to the left / right" -> monotonic stack. A rectangle limited by its lowest bar is exactly "each bar as the minimum of a range".

Fix the bar that sets the height. Any rectangle has a lowest bar k; making the rectangle as wide as possible without lowering it means stretching to the first bar lower than heights[k] on both sides. So:

answer = max over k of  heights[k] * (R[k] - L[k] - 1)
L[k] = index of the first bar on the left  with height < heights[k]  (or -1)
R[k] = index of the first bar on the right with height < heights[k]  (or n)

"Next smaller element" on both sides is the textbook monotonic-stack job. That reduction is the whole insight; the rest is bookkeeping.
