Dry run

Example 1, heights = [3,1,4,5,2,1], with the virtual bar of height 0 at index 6. Grey bars are on the stack, the blue bar is being popped, and the outlined block is the rectangle measured for it.

  1. Bars 3, 1, 4, 5, 2, 1 plus a virtual bar of height 0 at index 6. The stack keeps indices whose heights rise from bottom to top.
  2. i = 0: the stack is empty, push 0. Bar 0 does not know yet how far right it can stretch.
  3. i = 1 (height 1) is lower than bar 0, so bar 0's right wall is index 1. Its left wall is -1 (the stack is empty below it). Width 1 - (-1) - 1 = 1, area 3.
  4. Push 1. The stack is [1]; bar 1 will wait until an even lower bar arrives (only the sentinel).
  5. Heights 4 and 5 rise, so 2 and 3 are pushed without any measuring. Stack [1, 2, 3].
  6. i = 4 (height 2): bar 3 (height 5) is popped. Right wall 4, left wall = new top 2. Width 1, area 5.
  7. Bar 2 (height 4) is also taller than 2: right wall 4, left wall 1, width 2, area 8 - bars 2 and 3 together. Bar 1 (height 1) stays; push 4.
  8. i = 5 (height 1): pop bar 4 (height 2). Right wall 5, left wall 1, width 3 (indices 2-4), area 6.
  9. Bar 1 has height 1 >= 1, so it is popped too (with >=, equal bars are measured by the later one). Width 5, area 5. Push 5.
  10. The sentinel (height 0) flushes bar 5: no left wall, width 6, area 6. Every bar was pushed and popped once. Answer 8.
