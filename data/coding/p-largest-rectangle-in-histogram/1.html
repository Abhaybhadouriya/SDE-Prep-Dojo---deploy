The problem (in our words)

You are given an integer array heights. Each value is the height of one bar of a histogram, and every bar has width 1; the bars stand next to each other with no gaps. Return the area of the largest rectangle that fits completely inside the histogram (its bottom on the base line, made of whole adjacent bars, its height limited by the lowest bar it covers).

Signature: int largestRectangleArea(int[] heights).

  • 1 <= heights.length <= 10^5
  • 0 <= heights[i] <= 10^4

Practice it: LeetCode 84 · GfG Histogram Max Rectangular Area · CodeChef PREP22 · TUF article

Constraints tell you: n up to 10^5 rules out the O(n²) "expand from every bar" (10^10 steps on a staircase input); you need O(n) or O(n log n). The largest area is at most 10^5 × 10^4 = 10^9, which still fits a 32-bit signed int, so no overflow trick is needed here (it is in the matrix follow-up if widths grow).
