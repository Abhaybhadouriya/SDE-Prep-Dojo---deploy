Common mistakes

Width i - t instead of i - leftWall - 1. The popped bar extends left past its own index, down to the bar below it on the stack. Using its own index loses the bars popped earlier: on [2,1,2] the bar of height 1 is measured with width 2 instead of 3, so the answer comes out 2 instead of 3.

Forgetting the leftover stack. Without the height-0 sentinel (or a final drain loop), rising tails like [1,2,3] are never measured.

Reading the left wall before popping. Pop first, then the new top is the left wall; empty stack means -1, not 0.

Storing heights instead of indices. You need indices to compute widths; read the height through heights[st.top()].

Mutating the input to add the sentinel. heights.append(0) works but changes the caller's array; loop to i == n with h = 0 instead.
