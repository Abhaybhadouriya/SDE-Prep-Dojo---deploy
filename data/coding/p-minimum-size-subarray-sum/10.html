Technique: a window whose validity is monotonic in its length, walked with two forward-only pointers; shrink while valid to find the shortest.

Closest match: LC 862 Shortest Subarray with Sum at Least K Hard

  • Same: shortest subarray with sum ≥ k.
  • Different: values can be negative, so the window sum is not monotonic.
  • So: work on prefix sums and keep a deque of increasing prefix values: pop the front while pre[r] - pre[front] ≥ k (record), pop the back while pre[back] ≥ pre[r].

How to recognise it:

  • "Minimum length" / "smallest contiguous" + a sum that must reach a bound.
  • Non-negative values: the window works. Negative values: prefix sums + deque.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 643 (fixed window), 1004 (longest valid).
  2. This problem, then 713 (count) and 1658 (complement window).
  3. 76 Minimum Window Substring, then 862 (negatives, deque).
