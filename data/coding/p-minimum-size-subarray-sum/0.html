The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of jars holds coins, every jar at least one. You need at least 8 coins and want to pick up as few neighbouring jars as possible. Slide your hands along the row: the right hand keeps scooping in the next jar until you hold 8 coins; then the left hand puts jars back from its end for as long as you still hold 8. Each time you hold enough, count the jars between your hands. The fewest you ever counted is the answer.

The two hands are the two pointers of a variable-size window. The trick only works because jars never hold negative coins: putting one back can never increase what you hold.

From your world

A receive ring buffer has a head and a tail that only move forward: the producer advances the tail, the consumer advances the head, and the bytes in flight are tail minus head. This window is that ring: right is the tail, left is the head, and the sum is the bytes in flight. Neither index ever moves backwards, so the whole scan is at most 2n index moves.
