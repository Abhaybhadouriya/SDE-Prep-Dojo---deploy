Common mistakes

Looping to i < n. At i = n-1 the check i == levelEnd can fire and add a hop that lands nowhere. Loop to n-2, or return as soon as levelEnd ≥ n-1.

Greedy on the landing pad. "Hop to the pad with the biggest value" or "hop as far as possible" both fail; the right greedy maximises i + nums[i] over the whole level, and only the range end matters.

Updating farthest after the level check. At i == levelEnd, pad i itself belongs to the current level; its reach must be included before you set the new end.

Ignoring the unreachable case in a variant. If the guarantee is removed, add: if farthest <= i when you close a level, return -1 (GfG's version).
