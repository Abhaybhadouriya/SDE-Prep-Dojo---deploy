The story

Explain like I'm 5

Stepping stones cross a river; each stone has a number saying how far you may hop from it. With one hop from the first stone you can land on a whole stretch of stones. Look at every stone in that stretch and note the farthest any of them can reach: that is the end of the stretch you can reach with two hops. Repeat, stretch by stretch, until a stretch contains the far bank. The number of stretches is the answer.

From your world

It is a BFS where the queue never has to exist, because every level is an index range [start, end]. Like processing a ring buffer in batches: you know where the current batch ends, and while you consume it you track how far the next batch will extend.
