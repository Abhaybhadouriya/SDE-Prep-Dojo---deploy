The problem (in our words)

You are given an array of positive integers candidates (values may repeat) and a positive integer target. Return every unique combination of candidates whose values sum to target. Each element of candidates may be used at most once in a combination. Two combinations that contain the same values with the same multiplicities are the same combination and must appear only once. The combinations may be returned in any order.

Signature: List<List<Integer>> combinationSum2(int[] candidates, int target).

Constraints:

  • 1 <= candidates.length <= 100
  • 1 <= candidates[i] <= 50
  • 1 <= target <= 30

Practice it: LeetCode 40 · GfG: Combination Sum Without Repetition

Constraints tell you: n = 100 means 2100 subsets, so you cannot enumerate subsets and dedup afterwards. What saves you is target <= 30 with values >= 1: a combination has at most 30 cards, and with sorting + break, branches die as soon as the running sum would pass the target. Many equal cards (e.g. a hundred 1s) is the stress case for the dedup rule.
