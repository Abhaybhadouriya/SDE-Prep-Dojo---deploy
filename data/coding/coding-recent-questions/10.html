Turning the list into a plan

A frequency list is a priority queue for your practice, not a syllabus. A plan that works with 4 to 6 weeks before the loop:

  1. Core 20 (week 1-2). The cross-company problems in the "Core" pack below. Solve each one twice: once to learn it, once from scratch three days later, timed.
  2. Company pack (week 2-3). The top 15 for your target company from this page, plus the top of its Premium list for the last 6 months. Where the two agree, that problem is a near-certain topic.
  3. Variants (week 3-4). For each top problem, solve one follow-up from the variants table. This is what turns "I have seen it" into "I can handle what they actually ask".
  4. Patterns behind the gaps (ongoing). If a family is weak (say DP for Amazon), do the primer page for it, not just more problems from the list.
  5. Mocks (last week). Two timed problems in 45 minutes with someone watching, out loud, with the test routine at the end.

Spread the reviews. The revision system schedules each solved problem again after 3, 7 and 21 days. A problem from this list you solved once a month ago is not ready.

About honesty: if you get a problem you have solved before, solve it properly anyway; most interviewers do not mind, and the twist usually arrives within ten minutes. If asked directly whether you have seen it, say so.
