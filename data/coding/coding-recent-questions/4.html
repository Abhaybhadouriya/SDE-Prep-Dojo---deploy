Meta

577 reports from 225 posts, February 2025 to May 2026; levels mostly E4/E5 (SDE-2: 413). Difficulty E 24% / M 64% / H 12%. 100 reports are tagged as phone screens, 168 as onsite. Meta's list is the most concentrated of any company: the same 20 or so problems repeat, often two per round, and usually as a small twist. Many posts describe two problems in one round, so practise finishing a Medium plus an Easy or a second Medium inside one slot. Meta company list.

Amazon

204 reports from 106 posts, March 2025 to November 2026; SDE-2 108, SDE-1 58, intern 21. Difficulty E 6% / M 67% / H 27%. 14 reports come from bar-raiser rounds. Amazon's list is graph-first: Rotting Oranges, Course Schedule I and II and Number of Islands make up 23 reports. DP (House Robber, Longest Common Subsequence, Word Break) is more common here than at any other big company. Each round also includes Leadership Principles questions, which eat into the coding time, so aim to finish a Medium with time to spare. Amazon company list.

Google

84 reports from 56 posts, May 2025 to October 2026; mostly L4. Difficulty E 12% / M 58% / H 30%. Google candidates usually describe the question in prose because Google writes its own problems, so the named ones are the closest public match, not the exact question. The spread is wide (no problem has more than 4 reports), Hard is common, and the list mixes graphs, interval scheduling (Meeting Rooms III, Maximum Profit in Job Scheduling) and small design problems (Logger Rate Limiter, LFU Cache). Prepare patterns, not problems. Google company list.

Uber
