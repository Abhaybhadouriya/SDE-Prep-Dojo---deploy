What jumps out

  • LRU Cache is the most reported problem overall (40 reports, 15 companies). It is a design problem, a linked-list problem and a hash-map problem at once, which is why so many interviewers like it.
  • Graph search is everywhere. Number of Islands, Course Schedule I and II, and Rotting Oranges together give 88 reports from a dozen companies. If you can write BFS, DFS and topological sort without thinking, you cover a large slice of the list.
  • Some problems belong to one company. Valid Word Abbreviation (25 of 25 from Meta), Minimum Remove to Make Valid Parentheses (20 of 21), Simplify Path (17 of 18). These are the well-known Meta phone-screen set.
  • Mediums dominate. Medium is 63% of all reports, Hard 21%, Easy 16%. The Easies that appear (Valid Palindrome II, Diameter of Binary Tree) are almost all Meta, where two problems fit in one 45-minute round.
  • Two of the top 30 are Premium-only (Valid Word Abbreviation, Binary Tree Vertical Order Traversal); our pages restate them in full.
