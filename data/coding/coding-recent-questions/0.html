The story: asking last year's students

Explain like I'm 5

Your school has a big test every year. The teacher can pick from a thousand questions, but you cannot learn a thousand questions. So you find twenty older children who took the test last year and ask each one: "What did they ask you?" Some questions come up again and again: twelve children got the one about trains. A few got a twist: the train question, but with two trains. Now you know where to start. You still learn the whole subject, because the teacher can always pick a new question, but you start with the trains.

This page is that conversation, done with data. Candidates post their interview experiences on LeetCode Discuss after the loop: the rounds, the questions, sometimes the result. We read 4,217 of those posts from January 2024 to November 2026, found the LeetCode problems named in them, and counted. The result is not the company's real question bank. It is a list of what people say they were asked, which is the next best thing.

From your world

Think of the syzbot dashboard or a distro bug tracker sorted by "number of duplicate reports". The top crashers are not all the bugs in the kernel, and a bug with one report can still bite you. But if you have one week before a release, you fix the top crashers first. Treat this list the same way: it tells you what to fix first, not what is safe to ignore.
