The story

Explain like I'm 5

A family tree is drawn on a wall: grandparents on the top row, their children on the next row, and so on. You walk along the right side of the wall and shine a torch straight across each row. On every row the torch lights up only the first person it meets, the one farthest to the right. Write down who gets lit, top row to bottom row.

The catch: on a lower row, the rightmost person may belong to the left branch of the family, because the right branch simply ended earlier. Following right children only would miss them.

From your world

BFS with a "level width" snapshot is how you walk a routing or device tree hop by hop (like a breadth-first discovery of switches by hop count). The DFS version is the same as a depth-first walk that records the first entry it sees at each depth, like keeping the first match per prefix length.
