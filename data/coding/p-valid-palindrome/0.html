Explain like I'm 5

Two friends check a banner from opposite ends. Each one ignores commas, spaces and stars and only calls out real letters and digits, and they do not care about capital letters. They step towards each other, calling one letter each time. If they ever call different letters, the banner is not the same backwards. If they meet in the middle with no disagreement, it is.
