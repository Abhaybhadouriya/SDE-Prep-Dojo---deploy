  • Different: the condition is pre[r+1] ≡ pre[l] (mod k), not a difference of k.
  • So: key the map by ((run % k) + k) % k and look up the same key (watch negative mod).

How to recognise it:

  • "Contiguous subarray" + an exact sum, a sum mod k, or a balance (equal 0s and 1s).
  • Negative numbers in the input: that rules the window out and points here.
