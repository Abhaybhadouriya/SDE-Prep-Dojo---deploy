How to recognise it:

  • "Mirror", "flip", "symmetric", "reflect" on a binary tree.
  • The work at one node needs only that node and its children's results.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 104 Maximum Depth, then 100 Same Tree.
  2. This problem: recursive, then BFS; test that inverting twice is the identity.
  3. Then 101 Symmetric Tree, 951 Flip Equivalent Trees and 2415 Reverse Odd Levels.
