  1. Input [5,3,8,1,4,7,9]. The call on the root does not swap yet: it first asks for the mirror of each subtree. Post-order means children are finished before their parent.
  2. The recursion reaches leaves 1 and 4. Their children are null, which mirror to null, so a leaf is its own mirror (green = finished).
  3. Back at node 3: the mirrored right child (4) becomes its left, the mirrored left child (1) becomes its right. Subtree 3 now reads 3,4,1.
  4. Same on the other side: leaves 7 and 9 return unchanged.
  5. Node 8 swaps its finished children: 9 on the left, 7 on the right.
  6. Finally the root hangs the mirrored right subtree (8,9,7) on its left and the mirrored left subtree (3,4,1) on its right. Level order: [5,8,3,9,7,4,1]. Every node was visited once and did O(1) work: O(n).
