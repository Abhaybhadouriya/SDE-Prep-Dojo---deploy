Recognise the pattern

Cue: "do X at every node" where X uses only the node and the results of its children -> one recursive function with a null base case. Here X is "swap the two (already mirrored) children".

This is the gentlest member of the "compare or transform two subtrees" family: Same Tree, Symmetric Tree and Flip Equivalent Trees use the same shape of recursion with two pointers instead of one.
