The story

Explain like I'm 5

You are threading beads on a string, one at a time, from a long box. The rule: no two beads of the same colour on your string. When the next bead has a colour you already have, you slide beads off the start of the string until that old colour is gone, then add the new bead. The question is: what is the longest string of beads you ever held?

From your world

This is a receive window that only advances. right is the next byte you accept, left is the oldest byte still held. Like a TCP window, both edges only move forward, which is why the whole scan is linear even though the window grows and shrinks.
