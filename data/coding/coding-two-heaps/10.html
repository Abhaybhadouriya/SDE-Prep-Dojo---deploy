Revision card

  • Cue: Running median, sliding window median, IPO / capital, merge k sorted, smallest range covering k lists.
  • Insight: Split the stream into a lower and an upper half; the tops are the middle.
  • State: max-heap lo + min-heap hi with |lo| - |hi| in {0, 1}; lazy deletion map for windows.
  • Trap: Not rebalancing after each add; int overflow when averaging two tops.
  • Cost: O(log n) per add, O(1) per median.

More to remember

  • Median: max-heap of the small half, min-heap of the large half; tops are the middle. Add = push lo, lo top to hi, rebalance.
  • Two invariants: top(lo) <= top(hi), and |lo| - |hi| is 0 or 1.
  • Window median: heaps cannot delete; mark leaving values in a map, count valid sizes, prune tops. Or use multisets.
  • Two keys (gate and rank): sort by the gate, heap by the rank. IPO, hiring, team performance.
  • k-way merge: heap of heads, each entry knows its source. Smallest range adds a running max; k pairs seeds column 0.
  • Averages in double, sums in 64-bit, and stop when a source runs dry.
