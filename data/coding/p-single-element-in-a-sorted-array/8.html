Common mistakes

Probing an odd mid without adjusting. At odd mid, "nums[mid] == nums[mid+1]" means the opposite of what it means at even mid. Either force mid even, or compare with mid ^ 1.

lo = mid + 1 in the even-index version. That makes lo odd and breaks the invariant; the next probe then reads the wrong half of a pair. When you have checked the pair (mid, mid+1), jump to mid + 2.

hi = mid - 1 when the pair is broken. mid itself can be the answer (it is the first "false"), so keep it: hi = mid.

Comparing with nums[mid-1] and nums[mid+1] to "detect" the single. It works only when mid lands on the answer and needs bounds checks everywhere; the predicate approach handles both sides with one comparison.
