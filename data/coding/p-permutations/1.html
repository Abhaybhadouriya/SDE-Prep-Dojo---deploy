The problem (in our words)

A race organiser has an array nums of distinct integer runner numbers and wants every possible finishing order. Return all permutations of nums: every ordering that uses each number exactly once. The orderings may be returned in any order.

Signature: List<List<Integer>> permute(int[] nums) (LeetCode style: return a list of integer lists).

Constraints:

  • 1 <= nums.length <= 6
  • -10 <= nums[i] <= 10
  • All integers in nums are unique.

Practice it: LeetCode 46 · GfG Permutations of a string · TUF All permutations

Constraints tell you: n <= 6 means at most 6! = 720 answers of 6 numbers each. Exponential (factorial) time is required: the output alone has n! rows. "Unique" means no duplicate handling (that is LeetCode 47).
