Revision card

  • Cue: "Exactly K distinct", "at least K", "number of subarrays with", minimum window substring.
  • Insight: Exactly K = atMost(K) - atMost(K - 1); at least K counts left ends.
  • State: the same window skeleton with a counter; need[] and missing for covering windows.
  • Trap: atMost(K - 1) with K = 0; counts in 32 bits; using a window when negatives break monotonicity.
  • Cost: O(n) per pass, so O(n) total.

More to remember

  • One skeleton; four shapes: longest (record after), shortest (record inside), at most (add right - left + 1), at least (add left).
  • The condition must be monotone. Say why before you code.
  • exactly(K) = atMost(K) - atMost(K - 1); guard K - 1 < 0.
  • At least K: shrink while still valid, add left; or total - atMost(K - 1).
  • Shortest covering: need[] may go negative, missing changes only on 0 crossings.
  • Fixed window: a[right - k] leaves; first window ready at right == k - 1.
  • Counts and sums in 64-bit. Negatives: prefix sums, not windows.
