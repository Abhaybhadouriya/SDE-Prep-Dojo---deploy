How to recognise which variant

When you see "number of subarrays / substrings with exactly K ..." on non-negative data, think atMost(K) - atMost(K - 1). When you see "smallest window that contains", think shortest shape with a missing counter.

  • "Longest ... with at most k", or a story with a budget (flips, baskets, replacements): longest shape, one counter.
  • "Smallest / minimum length ... that contains / reaches": shortest shape, record inside the while.
  • "How many subarrays ... at most / less than": add right - left + 1.
  • "How many subarrays ... at least / contains all": add left (or total minus atMost(K - 1)).
  • "Exactly K" with a count: subtraction of two monotone counts.
  • Fixed k or |pattern| plus "same letters / all distinct": fixed window with a count map, matches or map size.
  • Negative numbers with a sum target, or "subsequence": not a window. Prefix sums with a hash map, a monotonic deque, or DP.
