Common mistakes

Not resetting minFreq = 1 on insert. After an eviction, minFreq may point at a high count; the next eviction would pick the wrong list (or an empty one).

Incrementing minFreq whenever list f empties. Only when f == minFreq. If 8 moves from 3 to 4 while minFreq is 1, minFreq must stay 1.

Forgetting that put on an existing key is a use. The value changes and the count goes up. Capacity 2: put(1,1), put(2,2), get(2), put(1,10), put(3,3) must evict 2 (both have 2 uses, 2 is older); without the touch, 1 still has 1 use and is wrongly evicted.

Evicting before checking for an existing key. An update in a full cache would throw out another key for nothing.

Adding to the wrong end. Insert at the front and evict from the back (or the reverse), consistently. Mixing them turns the tie-break into "most recent goes".
