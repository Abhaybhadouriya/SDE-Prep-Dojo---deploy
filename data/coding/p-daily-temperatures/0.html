The story

Explain like I'm 5

Children stand in a queue at an ice-cream van, each holding a ticket with their height. Each child wants to know: how many places behind me is the first child taller than me? Walk down the line. Every short child who is still waiting raises a hand. When a taller child arrives, all the raised hands of shorter children nearest to them go down at once (they have their answer), until we reach someone at least as tall. Then the newcomer raises their own hand.

The waiting children always form a staircase going down: if a shorter child stood below a taller one in the waiting group, the taller one would already have answered the shorter one. That staircase is a monotonic stack, and this problem is its standard "next greater element" form, asking for a distance instead of a value.

From your world

It is like a list of outstanding requests waiting for a sequence number: each incoming ACK completes every pending request it covers, newest first, and stops at the first one it does not cover. Nothing below that point needs to be looked at, because it waits for an even bigger value.
