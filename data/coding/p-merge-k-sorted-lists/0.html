The story

Explain like I'm 5

Three cashiers each hand you a stack of receipts, and every stack is already sorted by time, oldest on top. You need one pile sorted by time. You never have to dig into a stack: the oldest receipt overall must be on top of one of the three stacks. So you look at the three top receipts, take the oldest, and the receipt under it becomes that stack's new top. Repeat until all stacks are empty.

From your world

Tracing tools face the same job. ftrace and perf keep one ring buffer per CPU, and each buffer is in timestamp order. A reader such as trace-cmd report shows one timeline by looking at the next event of every CPU buffer and printing the oldest one. That is a k-way merge with k = number of CPUs. The min-heap is how you do it fast when k is large.
