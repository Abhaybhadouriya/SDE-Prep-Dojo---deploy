Recognise the pattern

When you see "k sorted sequences" + "one sorted output" (or "the k-th smallest across them"), think min-heap of the k current heads: a k-way merge.

When you see "merge many things that are each easy to merge in pairs", think divide and conquer: merge in pairs, log k rounds.

The key fact: in a sorted list every node is at least as large as the list's head. So the smallest node still waiting anywhere is the smallest of the k heads. You need a structure that gives "smallest of k values" quickly and lets you replace one value: that is a min-heap.

Pattern: Heap → k-way merge · Lessons: Heaps and priority queues, Linked lists
