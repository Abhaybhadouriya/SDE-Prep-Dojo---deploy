How to recognise it:

  • Several inputs that are each sorted, and the output needs a global order.
  • "k-th smallest" over sorted rows, streams or files: stop the merge after k pops.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: LC 21 (two lists).
  2. This problem: heap first, then the pairwise version.
  3. Then LC 373 and 378 (heap over a grid), and LC 632 (heap plus a running max).
