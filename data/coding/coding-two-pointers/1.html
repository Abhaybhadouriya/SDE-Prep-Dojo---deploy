The two families

Both families give O(n) time because every step moves at least one pointer and no pointer ever moves back, so there are at most n steps in total. Both use O(1) extra space, which is the usual reason an interviewer pushes you from a hash map answer to two pointers.

The question an interviewer is really asking: "why is it safe to move this pointer?" Write the invariant as a comment above the loop. If you cannot state it, you are guessing.
