When it is NOT two pointers. Unsorted input where you must return original indices: use a hash map (sorting loses the indices unless you sort (value, index) pairs). Negative numbers plus "subarray sum = k": the window cannot be shrunk safely, so use prefix sums with a map. "Longest substring with a condition": that is the sliding window, a same-direction pair with a different invariant.
