Remember this

Revision card

  • Cue: Sorted array, pair or triple to a target, palindrome check, remove in place, container with most water.
  • Insight: Each step drops elements that provably cannot be in the answer.
  • State: lo and hi from both ends, or a read and a write pointer moving forward.
  • Trap: Not skipping duplicates in 3Sum; moving the taller wall in Container With Most Water.
  • Cost: O(n) after an O(n log n) sort; O(1) extra.

More to remember

  • Two pointers = every step drops elements that provably cannot be in the answer. Say why.
  • Sorted pair sum: too small -> lo++, too big -> hi--, loop while lo < hi.
  • Container: move the shorter wall.
  • Palindrome with one deletion: at the first mismatch, try skipping either side once.
  • 3Sum: sort, fix i, two-pointer the suffix, skip equal i and equal lo after a hit. O(n2).
  • Read/write: r always moves, w moves only on keep; return w.
  • Dutch flag: four regions, mid <= hi, mid stays after a swap with hi.
  • Merge into a buffer with free space at the end: fill from the back.
