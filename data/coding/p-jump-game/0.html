The story

Explain like I'm 5

Stepping stones cross a river. Each stone has a number: how far you can jump from it, at most. You do not need to plan the exact route. Walk along and keep one finger on the farthest stone you could reach so far. Every stone before your finger is reachable too (you could just jump shorter). If you ever stand on a stone that is past your finger, you could not have got there: you are stuck.

From your world

It is like the highest contiguous sequence number a TCP receiver has seen. As segments arrive, the cumulative ACK point only moves forward, and there is exactly one question: is the next byte at or below it? A gap past the ACK point means the stream cannot advance, here it means the end cannot be reached.
