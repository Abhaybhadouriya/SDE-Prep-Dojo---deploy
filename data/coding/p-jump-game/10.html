How to recognise it:

  • "Up to k steps" moves on a line: reachable positions are an interval.
  • Yes/no reachability or a min count of moves, no costs on edges.

Practice order:

  1. This problem (greedy reach), also code the move-the-goal version.
  2. LC 45 (layers), then LC 1326 (intervals to jumps).
  3. LC 1306 and LC 1345 (BFS when the prefix property breaks).
