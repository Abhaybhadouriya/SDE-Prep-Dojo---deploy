The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have three toppings: olives, onion and peppers. How many different pizzas can you order? For each topping you say "yes" or "no", so 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 pizzas, counting the plain one with nothing on it. Listing all 8 without missing one or writing one twice is the whole problem.

The trick for "never twice" is to decide toppings in a fixed order. Once you have passed onion, you never go back to it, so "peppers then onion" can never appear next to "onion then peppers".

From your world

A subset of n items is an n-bit flag word: bit b set means item b is in. Counting a register from 0 to 2n - 1 walks every flag combination once, the same way you would test every combination of n feature bits in a driver. That is rung 1 below.
