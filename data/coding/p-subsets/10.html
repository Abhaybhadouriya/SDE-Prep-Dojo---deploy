How to recognise it:

  • "Return all ..." with no optimisation goal, and n small enough for 2n or n!.
  • Order inside an answer does not matter (subsets/combinations: start index) or does (permutations: used[]).

Extra practice (competitive programming, optional - not interview questions): Codeforces 1097B Petr and a Combination Lock (Each rotation is + or -: try all 2n sign masks and test the sum mod 360).

Practice order:

  1. This problem with both the bitmask and the start-index template.
  2. Then 90 Subsets II (one skip line) and 77 Combinations (a size check).
  3. Then 39 Combination Sum (reuse + pruning) and 46 Permutations (used[]).
