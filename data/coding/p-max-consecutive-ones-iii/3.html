Recognise the pattern

Cue: "longest contiguous subarray" + "at most k of something bad" (zeros, distinct values, replacements) -> variable sliding window with a counter of the bad things. The window is valid while bad <= k.

Try a few tiny inputs and look at the longest window with at most k zeros:

nums, kBest windowZeros insideAnswer
[0,1,0], 1[0,1] or [1,0]12
[0,0,0], 5whole array3 (<= 5)3 - k larger than the zero count is fine
[0,0], 0empty00 - the answer can be 0

Validity is monotone: if a window has at most k zeros, every smaller window inside it does too. That is the property that makes two pointers safe.
