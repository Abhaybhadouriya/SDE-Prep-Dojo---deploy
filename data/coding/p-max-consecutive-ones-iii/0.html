The story

Explain like I'm 5

A street has a row of lamps; some work (1) and some are broken (0). The city gives you k new bulbs. You want the longest unbroken stretch of light. You do not need to plan where each bulb goes: any stretch with at most k broken lamps can be fully lit, and a stretch with more cannot. So you walk along the street with a measuring tape, letting it grow, and pull its back end forward whenever it covers more broken lamps than you have bulbs.

The whole trick is the rephrasing: "flip at most k zeros, then find the longest run of ones" is exactly "the longest subarray with at most k zeros". Once said that way, it is the standard variable sliding window.

From your world

It is a receive window that tolerates at most k lost packets: the right edge advances with every arrival, and the left edge slides forward only when too many holes are inside. The window never needs to look back, so each packet enters once and leaves once.
