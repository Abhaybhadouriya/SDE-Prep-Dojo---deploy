How to recognise it:

  • "Longest subarray/substring" with a budget: k flips, k replacements, k distinct, k deletions.
  • Adding elements can only break the rule, removing elements can only restore it (monotone validity).

Extra practice (competitive programming, optional - not interview questions): Codeforces 660C Hard Process (Same window, but you must also print the array after the flips: remember the best left).

  3. Then 1493 and 2024 (same window, one twist each), then 424 (the 26-letter version).
