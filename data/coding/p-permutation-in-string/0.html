Explain like I'm 5

You have three letter tiles: A, A, B. A long row of tiles lies on the table. Is there a spot where three tiles side by side are your tiles, in any order? Put a frame three tiles wide at the start of the row. Each time you push the frame one step right, one tile leaves on the left and one comes in on the right. You only need to update your tally for those two tiles, not count all three again.

From your world

It is a rolling checksum without the false positives. Think of a per-type packet counter over the last k packets of a link: on every new packet you increment one bucket and decrement the bucket of the packet that fell out of the window. Here the "checksum" is the whole 26-bucket histogram, and you also track how many buckets currently equal the target, so "is it equal?" costs one compare.
