The story

Explain like I'm 5

Children stand in a queue for ice cream, and some of them are only empty coats left on the floor. The teacher wants every real child to step forward, still in the same order, and all the empty coats pushed to the back. She walks down the line once: every time she meets a real child, she sends that child to the first free spot at the front.

From your world

This is compaction of a slot array: removing freed entries from a descriptor table or packing live records in a ring after a purge. You keep a "next free slot" index and copy each live entry down, exactly like a memmove-free in-place filter loop in C. The order of live entries is kept, which is what makes it "stable".
