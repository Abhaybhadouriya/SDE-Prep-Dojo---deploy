Edge cases

InputExpectedWhy
[0][0]Single zero; the loop does nothing.
[1,2,3][1,2,3]No zeros: the r != w guard means zero writes.
[0,0,0][0,0,0]All zeros: w stays 0.
[0,0,4,0,4,9][4,4,9,0,0,0]Duplicates keep their order (stability).
[3,0,0,-6][3,-6,0,0]Negative values are non-zero.
[-2147483648,0][-2147483648,0]INT_MIN is fine: we never do arithmetic on values (no XOR-swap or subtraction tricks).
