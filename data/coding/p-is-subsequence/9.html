  • Cue: "same order, gaps allowed" between two sequences -> same-direction two pointers.
  • Insight: match each s letter at its EARLIEST copy in t; any valid match can be shifted left.
  • State: i in s (advances on match), j in t (always advances).
  • Trap: guard i < |s|; order matters (not membership); next search starts after the match.
  • Cost: O(|s|+|t|), O(1); many queries: pos lists + lower_bound, O(|s| log |t|) each.
