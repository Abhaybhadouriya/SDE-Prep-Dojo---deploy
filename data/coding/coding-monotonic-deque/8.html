How to recognise a monotonic deque problem

  • "Maximum / minimum of every window of size k", or a window condition that needs the window's max or min (max - min <= limit, max * length <= budget).
  • A DP whose transition is "best of the last k states": jumps of at most k, subsequences with gaps at most k, "within distance k".
  • "Shortest subarray with sum >= K" with negative numbers allowed. Positives only means a plain sliding window; negatives mean prefix sums plus a deque.
  • n up to 105 and an O(n log n) heap solution feels "almost right": the follow-up is usually "can you do O(n)?".
  • Old items expire and new items make some old items useless. If items never expire, you want a monotonic stack instead.

Not a deque: the window median or k-th largest (use two heaps or an ordered multiset), and "max of a window" when updates are random-access rather than sliding (use a segment tree, see the range-query pages).
