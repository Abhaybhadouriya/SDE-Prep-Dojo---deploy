Recognise the pattern

Cue: buy/sell over days with "at most k trades" -> state-machine DP (buy1, sell1, buy2, sell2), each updated from the previous day.

When you see buy / sell decisions over days with a rule such as "at most k trades", "cooldown" or "fee", think state-machine DP: list the states (holding or not, trades used), write each state's best value from the previous day.

When you see "at most two non-overlapping somethings", think also of the split trick: best on the left of i plus best on the right of i.

The stock family is one model with different limits: LC 121 is k = 1, LC 122 is k = ∞, this problem is k = 2, LC 188 is any k, LC 309 adds a cooldown state and LC 714 a fee. Learn the state machine once and every member is a few lines.

Taxonomy: DP → state machine → stocks. Pattern lessons: DP: stocks, trees and games, DP state design.
