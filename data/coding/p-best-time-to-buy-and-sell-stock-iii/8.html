Common mistakes

Taking the two largest single rises. On [1, 5, 2, 8, 3, 10] the rises are 4, 6, 7, so "two biggest" gives 13, but 1 → 8 plus 3 → 10 gives 14. Trades can span several rises.

Starting buy at INT_MIN and then computing buy + p: in C++ and Java that can overflow before the max. Start at -prices[0] (or use a 64-bit sentinel).

Dropping the "do nothing" option. Writing buy2 = sell1 - p instead of max(buy2, sell1 - p) forces a trade every day and forgets the best earlier buy. Every state must be "max(keep, move)".

Chaining the second trade from the wrong state. buy2 must start from sell1 (profit of trade one), not from 0; otherwise the two trades are computed independently and may overlap in time.

Returning max(sell1, sell2) "to be safe". It is harmless but shows you are unsure: sell2 >= sell1 always holds here.
