The problem (in our words)

You get an array prices where prices[i] is the price of one share on day i. Find the maximum total profit you can make with at most two transactions. A transaction is one buy followed later by one sell. You cannot take part in two transactions at once: you must sell the share you hold before you buy again (selling and buying again on the same day is allowed, though it never helps). If no profit is possible, return 0.

Signature: int maxProfit(int[] prices).

Constraints

  • 1 <= prices.length <= 10^5
  • 0 <= prices[i] <= 10^5

Practice it: LeetCode 123 - Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock III · GfG - Stock Buy and Sell, Max 2 Transactions · TUF - Buy and Sell Stock III

Constraints tell you: n up to 105 rules out trying every pair of trades (O(n4)) and even trying every split day with a fresh scan (O(n2), 1010). The profit is at most 2 * 105, so int is fine, but do not start a "buy" state at INT_MIN and then add a price.
