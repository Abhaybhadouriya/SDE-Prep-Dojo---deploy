The story

Explain like I'm 5

You may buy a toy and sell it later, at most twice, and you can hold only one toy at a time. Each day you ask four questions: "if I had just bought my first toy, what is the most money I could have left?", "if I had just sold it?", "just bought the second?", "just sold the second?". Each answer only needs yesterday's answers and today's price.

From your world

It is a protocol state machine: four states (HOLD1, DONE1, HOLD2, DONE2) and one input per tick. Instead of one current state you keep the best value of every state after each input, like a Viterbi pass. The transitions only go forward, so the update order inside a tick is safe.
