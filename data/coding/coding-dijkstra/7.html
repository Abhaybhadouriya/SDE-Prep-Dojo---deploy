How to recognise it

  • The input has triples (u, v, w), or a grid where moving has a cost that depends on the cells. A plain grid where every move costs 1 is BFS, not Dijkstra.
  • The question says "minimum" or "cheapest" about a route, and the route can revisit structure (cycles exist), so DP over a DAG does not apply directly.
  • Constraints: V and E up to about 105 means O(E log V), so Dijkstra. V ≤ 100 to 400 is a hint that O(V3) Floyd is expected. K ≤ 100 with E ≤ 104 fits K x E Bellman-Ford.
  • "Cost of a path = its worst edge" is minimax: Dijkstra with max, or the union-find trick.
  • Ask yourself out loud: "If I reach this vertex twice, does the cheaper arrival always win?" If not, add the missing information to the state.
