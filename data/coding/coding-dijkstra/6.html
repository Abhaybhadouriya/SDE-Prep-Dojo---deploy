Choosing the algorithm

Signal in the problemReach for
"minimum time / cost / distance", weights given, all ≥ 0Dijkstra, template 1
"minimise the maximum step / height / weight on the route"minimax Dijkstra (template 2), or binary search + BFS, or union-find
"maximise the probability / the minimum capacity"Dijkstra with a max-heap and * or min
"at most K stops / edges / moves"K + 1 rounds of Bellman-Ford with a copy, template 3
"negative cost", "detect an arbitrage loop"Bellman-Ford with the V-th round check
"number of shortest paths"Dijkstra plus a ways[] array
"for every pair", V ≤ ~400, or many queriesFloyd-Warshall, template 4
cost depends on a budget, fuel, time, keys heldDijkstra on (vertex, extra state)
weights are only 0 and 10-1 BFS with a deque
