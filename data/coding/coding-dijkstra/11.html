Revision card

  • Cue: Weighted edges, cheapest / fastest / most probable path, "within K stops".
  • Insight: Every shortest-path algorithm is relaxation; Dijkstra relaxes in order of final distance.
  • State: min-heap of (dist, vertex) plus dist[]; extra state (stops, fuel) when a cheaper arrival is not always better.
  • Trap: Negative weights; not skipping stale heap entries; Bellman-Ford rounds reading the array they write.
  • Cost: Dijkstra O(E log V); Bellman-Ford O(V E); Floyd O(V^3) for V up to about 400.

More to remember

  • Everything is relaxation: dist[v] = min(dist[v], dist[u] + w); the algorithms differ in order and repetitions.
  • Dijkstra: min-heap of (dist, vertex), skip stale entries, a pop is final. Needs w ≥ 0. O(E log V).
  • Change the combine: max for minimax, multiply with a max-heap for probability, add a ways[] count for "how many".
  • If a cheaper arrival is not always better, put more into the state.
  • K stops = K + 1 rounds of Bellman-Ford, each reading a copy of the last round.
  • Bellman-Ford's extra round detects negative cycles.
  • Floyd: k outermost, INF / 4, V up to ~400.
  • Weights 0/1: deque. Weights all 1: BFS.
