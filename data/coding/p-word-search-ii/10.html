Technique: backtracking on a grid, guided by a trie so that all patterns are matched in one walk.

Closest match: GfG Word Boggle

  • Same: a letter board, a dictionary, return the words that can be traced, each cell once per word.
  • Different: moves go in all 8 directions (diagonals allowed), and the output is usually sorted.
  • So: the same trie DFS with an 8-entry direction list; the branching factor becomes 8·7L-1, so pruning matters even more. Sort the result at the end.

How to recognise it:

  • Many query strings against one board or text: build a trie of the queries.
  • Grid path, each cell at most once, 4 directions: DFS with mark and restore.

Practice order:

  1. LC 79 Word Search (one word) and LC 208 Implement Trie.
  2. This problem: first trie + set, then add one-shot words and pruning.
  3. Then LC 211 (DFS inside the trie) and GfG Word Boggle (8 directions).
