The problem (in our words)

You are given an m x n grid board of lowercase letters and a list words of distinct strings. Return every word from the list that can be traced on the grid. A word is traced by starting on any cell and moving to a horizontally or vertically adjacent cell (no diagonals) for each next letter. One cell may be used at most once inside the same word. The answer can be in any order.

Signature: List<String> findWords(char[][] board, String[] words).

Constraints

  • m == board.length, n == board[i].length, 1 <= m, n <= 12
  • board[i][j] is a lowercase English letter.
  • 1 <= words.length <= 3 * 10^4
  • 1 <= words[i].length <= 10, lowercase English letters.
  • All strings in words are unique.

Practice it: LeetCode 212 - Word Search II · GfG - Word Boggle (same task, 8 directions)

Constraints tell you: a board of at most 144 cells and words of at most 10 letters means one path is short (depth ≤ 10, so recursion is safe). But 3 * 104 words times a full board search per word is far too slow, so the work must be shared between words. Sharing prefixes means a trie.
