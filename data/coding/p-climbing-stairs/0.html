The story

Explain like I'm 5

You hop up a staircase, one or two steps per hop. How many different hop patterns get you to the top? Stand on the top step and look back: you just landed either from one step below or from two steps below. So the number of ways to reach the top is the ways to reach one-below plus the ways to reach two-below. Work that out from the bottom up and you never have to list the patterns.

This is the smallest dynamic programming problem: a state (the step), a choice (the last move), and an answer built from smaller answers that would otherwise be recomputed again and again.

From your world

Counting the ways to fill an n-slot buffer with 1-slot and 2-slot frames is the same recurrence. Also the classic "number of n-bit strings with no two adjacent 1s" (a run-length constraint in line coding) is Fibonacci, by the same "look at the last symbol" split.
