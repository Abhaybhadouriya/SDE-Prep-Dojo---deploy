The problem (in our words)

A staircase has n steps. Starting at the ground, each move climbs either 1 or 2 steps. Return the number of different sequences of moves that end exactly on step n. Two sequences are different if they differ in at least one move (so 1+2 and 2+1 count separately).

Signature: int climbStairs(int n).

Constraints:

  • 1 <= n <= 45

Practice it: LeetCode 70 · GfG Ways to Reach the n'th Stair · TUF article

Constraints tell you: n ≤ 45 is chosen so the answer, ways(45) = 1,836,311,903, just fits a signed 32-bit int (max 2,147,483,647); ways(46) would not. It also means plain recursion (about 1.645, billions of calls) times out while O(n) is instant.
