Common mistakes

Submitting the plain recursion. It is correct but makes billions of calls at n = 45 and times out.

Off-by-one base cases. Starting from ways(1) = 1, ways(2) = 1 (pure Fibonacci) gives 3 for n = 4 instead of 5. Check n = 2 by hand: there are 2 ways.

Updating the two variables in the wrong order. prev1 = prev1 + prev2; prev2 = prev1; overwrites prev1 first and makes both equal. Use a temp, or a tuple assignment.

Counting combinations instead of sequences. 1+2 and 2+1 are different climbs here. If the question asked for unordered sums, the DP would loop over move sizes on the outside (like Coin Change II).
