Recognise the pattern

When you see "longest chain where each next item is bigger (or fits inside, or comes later)", with order fixed and skipping allowed, think LIS: O(n²) DP over "ends at i", or O(n log n) with patience tails. After sorting one dimension, many 2-D chain problems (envelopes, pairs) become LIS on the other.
