How to recognise the state shape

  • One array, decisions in order (take or skip, jump, cut here): index state, prefix or suffix.
  • "No two adjacent", "cooldown", "holding", "last colour": index + mode.
  • Two strings or arrays and words like common, edit, match, interleave, transform: pair-index (i, j) over prefixes.
  • Palindrome, "merge neighbours", "burst", "cut a stick", "order of operations": interval (l, r), maybe with a split point.
  • "Choose a subset" with a capacity or target: index + budget, the knapsack family.
  • Grid, moves only right/down: (row, col). Two walkers at once: (step, col1, col2).
  • n ≤ 20 and "assign every item": bitmask.
  • "Number of ways ... modulo 109+7": it is a counting DP; the state is whatever the brute force counts over.

When stuck, ask "what is the last decision?" for prefix and pair states, "what happens at the two ends?" for intervals, and "what do I need to know about yesterday to act today?" for modes.
