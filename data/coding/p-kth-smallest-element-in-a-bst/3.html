Recognise the pattern

Cue: "BST" + "k-th smallest / sorted order / next larger" → in-order traversal (left, node, right) is the sorted sequence. "Stop early" → iterative in-order with a stack. "Repeated rank queries on a changing tree" → augment each node with its subtree size.

Why in-order is sorted: for any node v, everything in v's left subtree is smaller than v and everything in its right subtree is larger. In-order prints the whole left subtree, then v, then the whole right subtree, and by induction each subtree comes out sorted, so the full sequence is sorted.
