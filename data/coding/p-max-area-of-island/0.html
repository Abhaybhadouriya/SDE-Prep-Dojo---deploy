The story

Explain like I'm 5

You have a map made of squares: some are land, some are sea. Pour paint on one land square: it spreads to every land square touching it up, down, left or right, and stops at the sea. Count the squares the paint covered: that is the size of that island. Do this for every unpainted land square and remember the biggest count.

From your world

It is a connected-components walk over a graph whose nodes are cells and whose edges are the four neighbours. Think of finding the largest broadcast domain in a switch fabric: start at any port not yet seen, follow every link, count the ports, and mark each port visited so loops do not double count.
