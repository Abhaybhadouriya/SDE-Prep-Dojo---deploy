Common mistakes

Negative modulo. In C++/Java, ('b' - 'z') % 26 is -24, not 2, so "zb" and "ac" get different keys. Add 26 before the modulo. In Go with bytes, the subtraction underflows instead; add 26 first there too.

Building a difference key without separators. Joining numbers like "1" "11" and "11" "1" both give "111". Use a tuple, a separator, or one character per difference.

Forgetting the wrap entirely. Raw differences put "za" (-25) and "ab" (+1) in different groups.

Thinking anagram keys work. Sorting or counting letters loses the gaps: "abc" and "acb" have the same letters but are not shifts of each other.
