  • (lo + hi) / 2 overflows when both are near 2³¹ in C++ and Java; write lo + (hi - lo) / 2.
  • Under a modulus such as 10⁹ + 7, take the remainder after every addition and multiplication, and do the multiplication in 64 bits.
  • Java: Integer objects compare by identity with ==; values from -128 to 127 are cached, so == works in small tests and fails on large ones. Math.abs(Integer.MIN_VALUE) is still negative.
  • Problems such as Reverse Integer and String to Integer (atoi) forbid 64-bit storage on purpose: check result > (INT_MAX - digit) / 10 before multiplying.
