The story

Explain like I'm 5

At a swap meet, kids trade stickers: "1 star = 3 moons", "1 moon = 4 suns". Someone asks "how many suns is a star worth?" You hop from star to moon to sun and multiply: 3 x 4 = 12. Asked about a sticker nobody ever traded, you shrug: you cannot know. That shrug is the -1.0.

From your world

Think of unit conversion or clock domains: you know the ratio between adjacent domains (PLL multipliers, link speeds), and you want the ratio between any two. Walking the chain and multiplying is the path search. Weighted union-find is the "precompute each domain's ratio to one reference clock" trick: then any pair is one division, ratio(a) / ratio(b).
