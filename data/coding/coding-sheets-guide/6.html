Filtering by company, frequency and window in the explorer

The explorer (/coding/explore) and every list or company page share the same filters: text, difficulty, status (to do / solved / due for revision), list, company, pattern group. Some recipes:

  1. Amazon onsite in 10 days. Open Amazon, keep frequency 4 and above, status "to do". About 209 frequent problems; 194 are Tier A, so if you did the Dojo 450 you have only a handful of new ones.
  2. Weak at graphs. Open the Dojo 450 list at its Graphs section (49 problems), or the explorer at group Graphs, and sort by difficulty. Solve the subgroup that has the most problems first.
  3. Meta phone screen. Meta's top rows are short Easy/Medium problems (Valid Word Abbreviation, Valid Palindrome II, Minimum Remove to Make Valid Parentheses). Practise two per 45 minutes.
  4. Revision day. Status "due". The queue brings back solved problems after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Re-solve each from a blank editor, not from memory of the code.
  5. Find what sheets miss. In the Dojo 450, look for rows with no Blind 75 or NeetCode badge in the lists column. Those are the company-driven problems, such as the 71 on none of the big lists.

A window of "all time" includes problems that were popular years ago. When a company has a 6-month window, prefer it for the final sprint; when it only has "all time", weight the top bars (6-8) more.
