Frequencies are not comparable across companies. Google's window is "all time", Microsoft's is "last 6 months", Flipkart's is "more than 6 months ago". A freq 4 at Flipkart (whose saved list only has bars 4-8) means something different from a freq 4 at Google. Compare problems within one company list, never between lists.

Notice Uber: only about half of its frequent problems are in Tier A. Uber asks more unusual and harder problems (45% Hard in our report data), so for Uber the company list matters more than for Amazon, where 194 of the 209 frequent problems are already in Tier A.

Step through a two-week sprint before a Microsoft loop.

  1. The saved Microsoft list (last 6 months) has 434 problems. Most sit at frequency 2 or 3.
  2. Filter to frequency 4 or more: 116 problems. This is the part that is asked repeatedly.
  3. 105 of the 116 are already in the Dojo 450. If you worked through Tier A, most are revision, not new work.
  4. The 11 outside Tier A are Microsoft-specific: Design Authentication Manager, Battleships in a Board, Excel Sheet Column Title and others. Add them to your sprint.
  5. Sort by frequency and put unsolved ones first: Two Sum (8), then Add Two Numbers, Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters and Longest Palindromic Substring (7 each).
  6. Spread the unsolved ones over the days left, and let already-solved ones come back through the revision queue.
