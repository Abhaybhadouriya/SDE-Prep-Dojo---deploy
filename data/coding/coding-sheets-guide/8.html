How to recognise you are on the wrong sheet

  • Most problems take you over an hour, even with hints. The sheet is ahead of your basics. Drop to A2Z steps or LeetCode 75 for two weeks.
  • You solve everything in 10 minutes. You are re-solving remembered code. Move to Tier B in your weakest group, or time yourself on Hard problems.
  • You keep meeting the same template. Good: that is the pattern. Skip the other problems in that variant and move on.
  • You have done 300 problems but fail mocks. The sheet is fine; revision and talking out loud are missing. Use the revision queue and the 45-minute plan.
  • Your target company's top rows are unfamiliar. Switch to the company list now, whatever the sheet says.
