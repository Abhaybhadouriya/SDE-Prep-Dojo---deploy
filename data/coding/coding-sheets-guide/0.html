The story: ten cookbooks, one kitchen

Explain like I'm 5

You want to learn to cook, and your friends give you ten cookbooks. One has 75 recipes, one has 150, one has 450. You open them and find the same pancakes, the same omelette and the same tomato soup in almost every book.

You do not need to cook every page of every book. You need the recipes that show up everywhere, the ones your family asks for most often, and a few that your grandmother (the restaurant you are cooking for next week) loves.

Problem sheets are those cookbooks. The Dojo Curated 450 is the single shopping list made from all of them, with your family's favourites (company lists) marked.

From your world

It is route selection. A router learns the same prefix from several peers (here: several sheets), keeps one copy of each prefix, and picks the best path with a metric that combines several attributes. Our priority score is that metric: sheet membership, company frequency and recent reports, combined into one number. Tier A is the forwarding table you actually use; Tier C is the full RIB you keep for reference.
