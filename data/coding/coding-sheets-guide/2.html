How much the sheets overlap

The first surprise: the famous lists are nested, not separate. Every Blind 75 problem is in the NeetCode 150, and every NeetCode 150 problem is in the NeetCode 250. LeetCode's own lists overlap with them only partly.

Across the nine big lists (LeetCode 75, Top Interview 150, Blind 75, NeetCode 150 and 250, Striver SDE and A2Z, Love Babbar 450, Top 100 Liked) there are 871 distinct problems, not the 1,800+ you get by adding the sizes. Only 29 problems are on all four of Blind 75, NeetCode 150, Top Interview 150 and Striver's SDE sheet. They are listed at the end of this page.

The second surprise goes the other way: 71 problems in the Dojo 450 are on none of the nine big lists. They come from company frequency and recent reports. Examples: Valid Word Abbreviation (25 Meta reports in 2024-26), Minimum Remove to Make Valid Parentheses and Peak Index in a Mountain Array. A sheet alone would never show you these.
