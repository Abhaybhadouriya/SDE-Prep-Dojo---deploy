How the Dojo Curated 450 and tiers A/B/C are computed

Every one of the 3,408 problems gets one priority number. The formula is public so you can judge it:

priority = lists + companies + recent - penalties

lists     = sum of list weights, capped at 16
companies = 12 × (1 − e^(−S/15)),  S = Σ companyWeight × freq / 8
recent    = 2.5 × log2(1 + reports) + 0.25 × min(8, reports since 2025)
penalties = 1 if Hard and on no core list
          + 1 if acceptance < 30% and on no core list
          + 0.5 if not on LeetCode

Step through the score of the number-one problem, LRU Cache, and of a problem that is on no sheet at all.

  1. Lists: LRU Cache is on 13 lists. The weights add up to 24.5, but the cap keeps it at 16, the maximum.
  2. Companies: 61 companies have it on their saved frequency pages. The saturating curve gives 11.48 out of 12.
  3. Recent: 40 named reports in 2024-26 interview posts, and plenty since 2025, give 15.39.
  4. No penalty applies. Total 42.87: the highest score in the database, so LRU Cache is rank 1.
  5. Now a problem on no list: Valid Word Abbreviation. Its whole score comes from companies (Meta asks it a lot) and 25 recent reports.
  6. 17.46 puts it at rank 178, inside Tier A. This is how the Dojo 450 catches what the sheets miss.

Tier A by difficulty: 94 Easy, 276 Medium, 74 Hard, plus 6 non-LeetCode classics. 48 Tier A problems are Premium-only on LeetCode; their pages restate the full problem so you can still practise them.
