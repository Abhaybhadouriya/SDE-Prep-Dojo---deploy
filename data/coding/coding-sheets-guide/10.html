Common mistakes

  • Hopping between sheets. Starting Blind 75, switching to Striver, then to NeetCode means re-solving the same 75 problems three times in different orders. Pick one main list.
  • Counting sheet sizes as work. Nine big lists look like 1,800 problems; they are 871 distinct ones, and the Dojo 450 covers the useful part.
  • Comparing frequency across companies. Windows differ (all time vs last 6 months vs older). Compare inside one list only.
  • Starting with company lists. They are sorted by frequency, not by pattern, so you jump between ten techniques in ten problems and learn none. Learn by pattern, sprint by company.
  • Skipping Premium-only problems. 48 Tier A problems are Premium-only, including Meta favourites. Our pages restate them in full, so practise them anyway.
  • Ticking "done" without revision. A problem solved once is forgotten in a few weeks. The 1-3-7-14-30 day queue is what makes the count real.
