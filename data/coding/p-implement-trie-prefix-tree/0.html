The story

Explain like I'm 5

A library shelves its books by the first letter, then inside that shelf by the second letter, and so on. To find every title that starts with "car", you do not read all the titles: you walk to shelf c, then to the a section, then to the r drawer, and everything you need is inside. A little flag on a drawer says "a whole title ends here", so you can tell the title "car" from a drawer that only leads to "cart" and "cargo".

That is a trie: a tree where each edge is one letter and each node stands for the prefix spelled on the path from the root. Words that share a beginning share the nodes for it.

From your world

You have walked tries for years. A multi-level page table is a radix trie keyed by chunks of the virtual address: each level indexes a fixed-size table with the next few bits. The Linux IPv4 routing table (fib_trie) is a level-compressed trie used for longest-prefix match. Our node with 26 child pointers is the same idea with a radix of 26 letters instead of 512 page-table entries.
