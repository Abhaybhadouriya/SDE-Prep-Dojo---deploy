Recognise the pattern

When you see "maximum (or minimum) sum / product / score of a contiguous subarray", think Kadane: define the answer for subarrays that end at i, then take the best over all i.

Try a few: when does the best run ending at i drop its past?

Run so farNext xExtend or restart?
sum 15extend: 6 > 5 (a positive past always helps)
sum -32restart: -1 < 2 (a negative past always hurts)
sum 04either: 4 = 4

The decision depends only on the sign of the running sum, not on the values inside it.
