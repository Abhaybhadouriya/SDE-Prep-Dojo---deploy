Explain like I'm 5

You walk along a road picking up coins, but some squares charge you a toll. You may start and stop your walk anywhere, and you want to end with the most money. Rule of thumb: if the money in your pocket ever goes below zero, the walk so far is a debt. Drop it and start again from the next square, because carrying a debt can only make every future total smaller.

This is the "best burst" question on a trace of per-second deltas (bytes in minus bytes out, credits gained minus spent). Kadane is a one-pass, O(1)-state scan, so it runs fine in a kernel fast path or over a ring buffer of samples: one accumulator, one high-water mark.
