The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two runners start together on a path in the fog. One jogs, the other sprints at twice the speed. If the path is a straight road, the sprinter reaches the end and stops. If the path secretly bends back into a circular track, the sprinter keeps going round and eventually comes up behind the jogger and taps them on the shoulder. A tap means "there is a loop".

From your world

A corrupted next pointer in a free list or a descriptor ring can turn a list walk into an infinite loop in the kernel. Floyd's check is how you detect that with no allocation (debug code paths often cannot allocate). It is also the cycle detector inside Pollard's rho and in tools that check for loops in routing tables.
