Recognise the pattern

When you see "values are in 1..n and the array has length n" plus "find the duplicate / missing ones", think index as hash: value v has a home slot v - 1, so the array itself can be the "seen" table.

When you see "exactly one extra and one absent value of a known range", think two unknowns need two equations: sum and sum of squares, or XOR then split by a differing bit.
