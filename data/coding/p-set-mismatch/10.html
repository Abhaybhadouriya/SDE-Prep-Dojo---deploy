How to recognise it:

  • "Numbers from 1 to n" and "array of length n": the values fit the indices one to one.
  • "One repeated, one missing": two unknowns, so either two equations or a seen-marker per value.
  • "Constant extra space": borrow the sign bit, or swap values into their home slots.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 268 Missing Number.
  2. This problem: count array, then sign marking, then the two equations.
  3. Then 448 and 442 (same marking, many answers), 287 (read-only, Floyd) and 41 First Missing Positive.
