The story

Explain like I'm 5

A company's reporting chart is drawn as a tree where every manager has at most two direct reports. HR wants to know how many layers the chart has, from the CEO down to the most junior person on the longest chain. Each manager can answer for their own branch if their two reports answer first: "my branch is one layer more than the taller of my two reports' branches". The question flows down, the answers flow back up.

From your world

This is the same recursion as computing the depth of a directory tree or a radix/trie routing table: each node asks its children and adds one. The iterative BFS version is the one you would use in kernel code, where deep recursion on a skewed tree could overflow a small fixed stack.
