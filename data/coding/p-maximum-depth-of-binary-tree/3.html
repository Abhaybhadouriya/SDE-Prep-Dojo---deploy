Recognise the pattern

Cue: "a property of the whole tree that is built from the same property of the subtrees" (height, size, sum, balanced?) -> bottom-up DFS: answer = combine(left answer, right answer, node).

Cue 2: anything counted "per level" (depth, widest level, right view) -> BFS with a level-size loop.
