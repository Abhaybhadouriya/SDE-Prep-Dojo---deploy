Recognise the pattern

Cue: "palindrome" + "built from / rearrange / permutation of the letters" -> only parities of counts matter: even counts are fully usable, every odd count loses one letter, and one odd letter may take the centre.

If the word "substring" or "subsequence" appears instead, the order matters and you are in two-pointer / DP land (Longest Palindromic Substring, Longest Palindromic Subsequence). Pattern page: Arrays & hashing · explorer node Arrays > hashing > frequency count.
