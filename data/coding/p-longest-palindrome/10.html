How to recognise it:

  • "Palindrome" together with "rearrange", "permutation" or "built from the letters".
  • The answer depends only on counts, so look at parities.

Practice order:

  1. This problem (pairs + one middle), then 266 Palindrome Permutation (odd <= 1).
  2. 1400 Construct K Palindrome Strings and 2131 (pairs of words).
  3. 1177 Can Make Palindrome from Substring (prefix parity masks).
