The story

Explain like I'm 5

Children stand in a line holding hands. "Rotate by 2" means the last two children walk to the front. Instead of moving children one at a time, ask the last child to hold the first child's hand (now they stand in a circle), then let go of the hands at the right spot. The child just after the gap is the new front of the line.

From your world

It is re-pointing a ring buffer's head. In a circular singly linked list (like a kernel free list or a round-robin run queue), "rotate by k" is just moving the head pointer; this problem makes the list circular for a moment so the same trick works, then breaks it again.
