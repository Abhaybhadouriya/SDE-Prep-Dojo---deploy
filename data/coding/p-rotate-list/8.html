Common mistakes

Forgetting the modulo. Walking k steps with k = 2 × 10^9 times out; walking past the end crashes on null.

Rotating left by accident. Right by k means the new head is node len - k + 1 (1-based), not node k + 1. Check with the 5-node example.

Closing the ring and returning early. If you join tail to head before checking k % len == 0 and then return head, the caller gets a circular list and loops forever.

Off-by-one on the walk. The new tail is len - k - 1 steps from head (node len - k counting from 1). Write the loop bound once, test it on len = 2, k = 1.
