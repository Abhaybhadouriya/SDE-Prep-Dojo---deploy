Recognise the pattern

Cue: "rotate", "shift by k", or "move the last k to the front" on a linked list -> find length + tail, reduce k modulo length, re-link at node len - k. Huge k next to a small n is the hint that modulo is the whole game.

StructureRotate right by k
ArrayThree reversals (Rotate Array), O(1) extra
Singly linked listRing + one cut (this problem), O(1) extra
Circular list / dequeMove the head pointer k steps
