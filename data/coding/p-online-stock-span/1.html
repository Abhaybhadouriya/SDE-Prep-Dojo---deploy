The problem (in our words)

Design a class that reads a stock's daily prices one at a time and, for each new price, returns its span. The span for today is the largest number of consecutive days, ending today and going backwards, on which the price was less than or equal to today's price. Today always counts, so the span is at least 1.

For example, if the prices of the last days were [40, 30, 35, 50] and today's price is 50, the span is 4 (50, 35, 30 and 40 are all ≤ 50).

  • StockSpanner() creates an empty object.
  • int next(int price) takes today's price and returns today's span.

Constraints:

  • 1 <= price <= 105
  • At most 104 calls to next.

Practice it: LeetCode 901 · GfG Stock Span Problem · TUF article

Constraints tell you: 104 calls means even an O(n) backward scan per call (108 in the worst case of a rising series) is borderline; the interviewer wants amortised O(1). Prices fit easily in int, and spans are at most 104.
