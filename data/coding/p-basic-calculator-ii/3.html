Recognise the pattern

Cue: "evaluate an expression string" + two precedence levels + no brackets -> stack of signed terms: push on + and -, fold into the top on * and /, sum at the end. The stack only ever changes at its top, so it shrinks to one variable.

Pattern pages: Stack & queue · explorer node Stack > Expression > Calculator. With brackets added, the same loop becomes recursive (see Basic Calculator and Basic Calculator III below).
