The story

Explain like I'm 5

A judge has the scores of n divers and must announce the diver in k-th place. She does not need the full ranking, only the k-th highest score. Ties are not merged: if three divers score 8, those are first, second and third place. A smart judge splits the scores around one score, throws away the side that cannot contain the k-th place, and repeats.

From your world

This is the p99 latency problem. A size-k min-heap is how you keep the "top k slowest requests" in a monitoring agent with bounded memory. Quickselect is what std::nth_element does, and it is how a percentile can be computed from a buffer of samples without sorting it.
