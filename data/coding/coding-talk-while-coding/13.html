Practice: problems to rehearse out loud

Record yourself (audio is enough) solving these with the script above, then listen back for silences over 30 seconds and for "what, not why" narration. Each problem trains one moment of the conversation.

  • Two Sum Easy - the brute-force-to-hash-map sentence.
  • Valid Parentheses Easy - narrate the stack invariant.
  • Merge Intervals Medium - the full script on this page; ask about touching ends.
  • Product of Array Except Self Medium - clarify "no division", explain prefix and suffix products.
  • Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters Medium - say the window invariant every time it shrinks.
  • Top K Frequent Elements Medium - compare heap versus bucket sort out loud.
  • Number of Islands Medium - clarify "can I modify the grid?", state O(rows · cols).
  • Meeting Rooms II Medium - draw examples on a timeline before coding.
  • Course Schedule Medium - clarify the graph format, explain why a cycle means "no".
  • LRU Cache Medium - design talk: operations, costs, then the map plus list.
  • Trapping Rain Water Hard - get unstuck by solving a tiny case by hand.
  • Median of Two Sorted Arrays Hard - fall back to the O(m + n) merge, then explain the binary search.
