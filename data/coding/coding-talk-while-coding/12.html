How to recognise what the interviewer needs right now

  • They are silent and writing. Good: they are taking notes. Keep narrating at block level; do not stop to ask "is this OK?" after every line.
  • They repeat part of the problem. You probably missed a requirement. Re-read it out loud and check your spec comments.
  • They ask "why?" about a line. Explain the reasoning in one sentence. If you cannot, it may be a bug: trace it.
  • They push on an input ("what about duplicates?"). That input is probably a failing case. Run it.
  • They look at the clock or say "in the interest of time". Summarise, finish the main path, describe the rest in words.
  • They ask a follow-up early. Your main solution is likely good enough; the follow-up is where the level is decided. Spend time there.
