Many duplicates, few distinct values (k much smaller than n). Can you beat O(n)?

Yes in comparisons: from slot i, binary-search (galloping) for the first value greater than nums[i] and jump there. That is O(k log n) comparisons and k writes, a win when k is much smaller than n.

How to answer

Offer it as an optimisation for skewed data; the simple loop is still the expected answer.
