How to recognise it:

  • Two trees (or two halves of one) and a question "equal / mirror / contained / equivalent?".
  • The answer for a pair depends only on the root values and the answers for child pairs.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 226 Invert Binary Tree.
  2. This problem, recursively and then with a queue of pairs.
  3. Then: 101 Symmetric Tree, 572 Subtree of Another Tree, 951 Flip Equivalent Trees.
