Recognise the pattern

Cue: a yes/no question about two trees (or one tree against its own mirror) -> a DFS that takes a pair of nodes and recurses on pairs of children. The base cases are the three null combinations.

The same "pair DFS" answers Symmetric Tree (pair the left child of one side with the right child of the other), Flip Equivalent Trees (try both pairings), and is the inner check of Subtree of Another Tree.
