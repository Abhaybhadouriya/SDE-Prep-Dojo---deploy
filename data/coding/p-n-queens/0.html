The story

Explain like I'm 5

A museum hall is laid out as an n by n grid of plinths. You must place n laser guards so that no guard's beam hits another guard. Each guard fires along its row, its column and both diagonals. You want every possible layout, printed as a floor plan. The natural way is the way a person does it on paper: fill the first row, then the second, and when a row has no safe spot, go back and move the previous guard.

From your world

This is a depth-first search with an undo log, like trying a configuration change, checking an invariant and rolling back on failure. The three "used" arrays are bitmaps of claimed resources (like an IRQ or port allocation map): claim before you recurse, release after you return.
