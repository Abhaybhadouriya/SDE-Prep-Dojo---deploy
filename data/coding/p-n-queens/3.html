Recognise the pattern

Cue: "return all configurations" + small n (<= 9 to 15) + "no two may conflict" -> backtracking: choose, check, recurse, undo.

Cue 2: conflicts along lines of a grid -> give each line an id (c, r - c, r + c) and keep a "used" set per family for O(1) checks.
