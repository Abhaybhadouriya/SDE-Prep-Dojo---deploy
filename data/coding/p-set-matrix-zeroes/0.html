The story

Explain like I'm 5

A cinema seats people in rows and columns. If any seat is broken, the manager closes its whole row and its whole column. If she closes them the moment she finds a broken seat, the freshly closed seats look broken too and she ends up closing the whole hall. So she first walks the hall and only notes which rows and columns to close, on the sticky notes already at the start of each row and the top of each column. Only then does she close them.

From your world

It is "mark, then sweep", like a mark-and-sweep pass or a two-phase commit: decide everything from the old state, then apply. Keeping the marks in the matrix itself is like storing flags in spare bits of a structure you already own instead of allocating a side bitmap; the one shared cell is the classic "two flags want the same bit" problem, solved with a separate variable.
