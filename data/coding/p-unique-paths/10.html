How to recognise it:

  • Moves are monotone (only right/down, or only forward), so the grid is a DAG and a fill order exists.
  • The question is "how many", "minimum cost" or "maximum gain" over all routes.

Practice order:

  1. This problem: table, rolling row, then the formula.
  2. LC 63 (obstacles) and LC 64 (costs) - same loop, different combine.
  3. Then LC 174 (fill backwards) and LC 2435 (extra state dimension).
