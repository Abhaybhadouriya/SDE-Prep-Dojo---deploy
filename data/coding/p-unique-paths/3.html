Recognise the pattern

When you see "count the paths on a grid" + "moves only right/down" (a DAG: no cycles), think grid DP where each cell sums the cells it can be entered from.

When you see no obstacles and no weights, think also of the closed form: choose which moves are "down".

Try a few tiny grids and the numbers form Pascal's triangle tilted on its side:

m × n2345
22345
3361015
44102035

Each entry is the one above plus the one to the left: that is Pascal's rule, and the entry for m × n is C(m+n-2, m-1).
