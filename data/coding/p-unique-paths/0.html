The story

Explain like I'm 5

A toy car sits in the top-left square of a chessboard-like mat and may only roll right or down. How many different routes reach the bottom-right square? Write a number in each square: "how many routes reach me". Squares on the top edge and the left edge get 1 (there is only one way to get there). Any other square is reached either from the square above it or from the square on its left, so its number is the sum of those two. The last square holds the answer.

From your world

It is the number of minimal routes between two routers of a 2D mesh network when each hop may pick either dimension (minimal adaptive routing): every minimal route makes exactly m-1 hops in one dimension and n-1 in the other, in any order. The rolling-row loop is an in-place prefix sum over one row buffer, run m - 1 times.
