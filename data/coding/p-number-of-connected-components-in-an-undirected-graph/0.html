The story

Explain like I'm 5

A school has n children, and you are told pairs of children who hold hands. Children holding hands, even through a chain of friends, play in the same group. How many groups are on the playground? Begin by saying "everyone is alone: n groups". Every time a hand-hold joins two children who were in different groups, two groups become one, so you take one away. A hand-hold inside a group that already exists changes nothing.

This is the most direct "count components" question, and it is in Blind 75, NeetCode 150 and the Premium Top 100. Interviewers use it to see whether you can build a graph from an edge list, handle isolated nodes, and write union-find cleanly.

From your world

Think of switches and cables: n switches, a list of links, and the question "how many separate L2 broadcast domains?". Union-find is the same bookkeeping a bridge loop detector does: a link whose two ends are already in one domain is a loop, not a merge.
