After the mock: the 20-minute debrief

The mock itself shows you a problem; the debrief turns it into a fix. Do it the same day, right after grading, in this order.

Classify each mistake into one of four bins, because each bin has a different fix:

BinSounds likeFix
Recognition"I did not see it was a graph problem."Write or rewrite the Trigger line (line 2) of the revision card. Add two more problems of that pattern to the queue.
Technique"I knew it was a sliding window but the shrink step was wrong."Re-solve tomorrow from the lesson template; grade the card Again.
Execution"Off-by-one in the loop; I did not dry-run."Card line 5 (Traps). Next mock's single habit: the dry-run table before "done".
Process"I spent 25 minutes on P1." "I went silent."Next mock's single habit: obey the checkpoints, or narrate every two minutes.

Both mock problems get a five-line revision card whether you solved them or not, and go into the seen set. A problem you failed enters the schedule at the first rung (tomorrow); one you solved cleanly enters at the normal next gap. Read the editorial and top community solutions linked on the problem page only after the card is written.
