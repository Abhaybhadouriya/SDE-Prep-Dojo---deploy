Common mistakes

  • Mocking on problems you just solved. It measures memory. Keep a 14-day seen set.
  • Pausing the clock. "Just one more minute" in every mock trains you for a 55-minute round that does not exist.
  • Peeking at the solution before grading. Once you have seen the answer, every score drifts up. Notes first, grade second, solution last.
  • Grading only "solved / not solved". The four signals matter as much as the result; a silent correct solution can still be a no hire.
  • Too many mocks, no debriefs. Three mocks with a debrief each beat ten without.
  • All mocks on one company's list from day one. Rotate early; specialise in the last three weeks.
  • New topics in the final week. Late learning is shallow and costs sleep and confidence.
  • An all-night session on D-1. You trade a sharp head in the round for one more problem you will not remember.
