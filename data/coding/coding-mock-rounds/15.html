Revision card

  • Cue: Two to three weeks before the loop, and the last seven days.
  • Insight: Only timed, graded rounds show how you perform under the real clock.
  • State: 45 minutes: P1 to minute 22, P2 to 42; checkpoints at 12, 22, 32; four signals graded 1-4.
  • Trap: Pausing the clock; skipping the debrief; choosing problems you solved last week.
  • Cost: Mocks on D-7, D-5, D-3; read-only D-2; rest D-1.

More to remember

  • A mock is a 45-minute drill: intro, P1 (to minute 22), P2 (to 42), your questions. The clock never pauses.
  • Checkpoints at 12, 22 and 32 minutes, each with a pre-decided action if you are behind.
  • Grade four signals (communication, problem solving, code, testing) from 1 to 4, plus problems solved and tested; count misses against your level's bar.
  • Pick mocks from the company's Tier A list: most frequent first, unseen in 14 days, P2 from a different group, difficulty by level.
  • Every mock ends with a debrief: classify mistakes, write cards for both problems, pick one habit for next time.
  • Final week: mocks on D-7, D-5, D-3; card days in between; read-only D-2; rest on D-1; one Easy warm-up on D-0.
