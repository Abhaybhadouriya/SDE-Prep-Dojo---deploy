The story

Explain like I'm 5

A meeting room's calendar lists its booked blocks in time order, and no two blocks touch. A new booking comes in. Every block that ends before the new one starts stays as it is. Every block that touches the new one gets glued to it, so the glued block can become longer than the new booking. Every block that starts after the glued block ends stays as it is too. You read the calendar once, from top to bottom.

From your world

This is inserting a range into a sorted free list or an extent map: find the neighbours that touch the new extent, coalesce them into one, keep the rest. The same shape shows up when you add a route prefix range to a sorted, non-overlapping ACL table.
