Explain like I'm 5

You typed "mart" but meant "karma". Fixing it means rubbing out letters, writing new ones, or overwriting one letter with another, and each fix costs one sticker. What is the fewest stickers? Look only at the last letters. If they are the same, keep them and fix the shorter words. If not, the last letter of "mart" is either overwritten, rubbed out, or a new letter is written after it. Try all three on the shorter words and keep the cheapest.

From your world

diff and git diff solve the insert/delete-only version of this (no replace): the shortest edit script between two files is m + n - 2·LCS. Edit distance with replace is the cost model behind spell checkers and fuzzy matching of command names (git suggesting "did you mean status?" uses a weighted variant of it).
