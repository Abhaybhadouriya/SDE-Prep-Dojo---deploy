Technique: count "exactly k" as the difference of two monotonic "at most" counts; the window state is a count array plus the number of distinct values.

Closest match: LC 1248 Count Number of Nice Subarrays Medium

  • Same: count subarrays with exactly k of something; atMost(k) - atMost(k - 1).
  • Different: the "something" is odd numbers, so the state is a single counter, not a count array.
  • So: replace the freq/distinct update with odd += x & 1.

How to recognise it:

  • "Number of subarrays with exactly k different / distinct ..."
  • The "at most" form is a window you already know (904, 340).

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 904 Fruit Into Baskets (longest, at most 2 distinct).
  2. 930 and 1248 (exactly k with a single counter).
  3. This problem, then 2062 and 3306.
