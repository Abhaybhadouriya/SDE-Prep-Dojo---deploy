The story

Explain like I'm 5

A shelf holds jars of sweets in a row, and each jar has a flavour number. How many runs of neighbouring jars contain exactly two flavours? Asking "at most two flavours?" is easier: for a jar at the right end, walk left until a third flavour would appear; every run ending here and starting on the way is fine. Ask the same for "at most one flavour" and subtract. The runs left over have two flavours, not one and not three.

This is the Hard version of the "exactly k" counting trick from Binary Subarrays With Sum; the only new part is the window state, a count per value and a number of distinct values.

From your world

Think of the number of distinct flows in a burst of packets. A per-flow reference count goes up when a packet enters the window and down when one leaves, and the distinct-flow counter changes only when a refcount crosses 0. That is exactly the window state here: freq[v] and distinct.
