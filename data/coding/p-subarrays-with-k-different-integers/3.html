Recognise the pattern

When you see "count subarrays with exactly k distinct / k odd / k ones", think exactly(k) = atMost(k) - atMost(k - 1). The "at most" window is a standard counting window (add right - left + 1); "exactly" is not monotonic, "at most" is. "Longest with at most k distinct" (LC 340, 904) is the same window without the subtraction.
