Common mistakes

Overwriting in pass 2: give[i] = give[i+1] + 1 without max. On [1,2,3,4,1] pass 1 gives the peak (rating 4) 4 candies; overwriting sets it to 2, below its left neighbour's 3, which breaks the left rule. Always keep the bigger demand.

Treating equal ratings as a rule. "Equal neighbours get equal candy" is not in the statement. [2,5,5] is 4, not 5.

Trying to do both rules in one left-to-right pass with a stored array. You cannot know the length of the descent ahead of you. Either two passes, or the slope counters that fix earlier children retroactively.

Off-by-one at the peak in the O(1) version. The peak gets an extra candy only when down > peak (the descent outgrows the climb), not when they are equal.
