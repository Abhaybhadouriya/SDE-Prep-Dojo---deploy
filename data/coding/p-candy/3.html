Recognise the pattern

Cue: each element's value depends on its left neighbour and its right neighbour, and a single sweep cannot see the right side yet -> two passes (left-to-right, right-to-left) and combine. Same shape as Trapping Rain Water (max from each side) and Product of Array Except Self (prefix and suffix).

Try a few: the answer per child is "1 + length of the strictly rising run that ends at me from that side", maxed over the two sides.
