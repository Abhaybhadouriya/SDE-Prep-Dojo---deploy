How to recognise it:

  • "All unique combinations of k numbers that sum to T" with n up to a few thousand.
  • The output is values, not indices, so sorting is allowed.

Practice order:

  1. Warm-up: 167 Two Sum II (the inner loop), then 1 Two Sum (hash version).
  2. This problem: write the hash version, then sort + two pointers; compare on random tests.
  3. Then 16 3Sum Closest, and 18 4Sum (generalise to k-sum with recursion).
