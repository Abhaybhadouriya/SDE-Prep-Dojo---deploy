The problem (in our words)

A sign painter has a strip of capital letters s and enough paint for at most k repaints. One repaint turns a single position into whichever capital letter (A-Z) the painter likes; repaints may be spread anywhere along the strip.

After at most k repaints, how long can the longest run of one single letter (a contiguous block where every position shows the same letter) be? Report that length.

Signature: int characterReplacement(String s, int k).

Constraints

  • 1 <= s.length <= 105
  • s consists of only uppercase English letters.
  • 0 <= k <= s.length

Practice it: LeetCode 424 · GfG Longest Repeating Character Replacement · TUF Longest Repeating Character Replacement

Constraints tell you: n = 105 rules out the O(n2) "every start" loop (5·109 pairs); aim for O(n) or O(26·n). "Only uppercase letters" means a 26-slot counter. k can be 0 (then it is "longest run") or as big as n (then the answer is n).
