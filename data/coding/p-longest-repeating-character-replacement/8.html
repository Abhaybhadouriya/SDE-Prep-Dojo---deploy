Common mistakes

Recording the answer from an invalid window in the shrinking version. In approach 2 the length is only valid after the while loop. In approach 3, record n - left (or the size after the if), never mix the two styles.

Decreasing maxFreq when the left letter leaves. Not needed, and doing it wrongly (without rescanning) breaks the proof. Either rescan all 26 counts or keep it stale.

Counting distinct letters instead of the majority. "At most 2 distinct letters" is a different problem; here we need size - (count of the most common letter).

Trying only the letters at the window edges as the target. The best letter to keep can be anywhere inside.
