The story

Explain like I'm 5

A row of coloured beads, and a pot of paint that is enough to repaint k beads. What is the longest stretch you can make all one colour? Inside any stretch, the smart move is to keep the colour that already appears most and repaint the rest. So a stretch is fine when "beads that are not the main colour" is at most k.

We never need to know which beads get repainted, only how many: size - maxCount. That one number turns a search over all substrings into a single sweep.

From your world

It is like finding the longest burst of packets you could classify as one flow if you are allowed to "correct" at most k mislabelled headers. A ring buffer with a per-class counter, a head and a tail, updated in O(1) per packet: exactly the window here.
