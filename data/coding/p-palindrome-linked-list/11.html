Interview follow-ups

You may not modify the list, even temporarily. What now?

Then O(1) space is not possible with plain pointers; use a stack of the first half (push while fast/slow runs, pop while walking the second half) for n/2 extra space, or copy to an array. Say the trade-off explicitly.

How to answer

State that the O(1) solution relies on mutation, then offer the n/2 stack.

Likely follow-ups
  • Is the recursive version allowed? (It does not mutate, but uses O(n) stack.)
Why restore the list? The judge does not check.

The function is a predicate; a caller does not expect it to change the data. In a multithreaded program another reader could see a half-reversed list during the call. Restoring costs one more O(n/2) pass.

How to answer

Mention side effects and concurrency in one sentence each.

Likely follow-ups
  • Make it safe for concurrent readers? (Do not mutate: use the stack version, or hold a lock.)
Walk me through the recursive solution and its space.

Keep a front pointer outside the recursion; recurse to the end; on the way back compare the current node with front and advance front. Time O(n), space O(n) for the stack: 105 frames exceeds Python's default limit.

How to answer

Show the code shape, then volunteer the space cost before being asked.

Likely follow-ups
  • Can tail-call optimisation help? (No: work happens after the recursive call.)
It is a doubly linked list. Does anything change?

Yes, it gets easy: walk to the tail, then move one pointer forward from the head and one backward from the tail until they meet or cross. O(n) time, O(1) space, no mutation.

How to answer

Point out that the whole trick exists only because of the missing prev pointer.

Likely follow-ups
  • Stop condition for even n? (Stop when the pointers cross: a == b or a.prev == b.)
The values arrive as a stream and you cannot store them. Can you still answer?

Not exactly with O(1) memory. Keep two rolling hashes: forward h = h*B + v and backward r = r + v*B^k; equal hashes mean "probably a palindrome". With a large prime modulus (or two), false positives are rare; say that it is probabilistic.

How to answer

Say "exact needs O(n)", then offer hashing with its error rate.

Likely follow-ups
  • How do you pick the modulus to make collisions unlikely?
