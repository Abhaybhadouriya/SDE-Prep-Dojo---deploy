Common mistakes

The wrong loop condition. while fast and fast.next leaves slow on the first node of the back half for even n. That also works if you then reverse from slow (not slow.next), but mixing the two conventions compares the wrong nodes. Pick one and draw n = 4 and n = 5.

Looping on the front half. while a walks into the back half (the front still links to it through slow) and compares nodes twice. Loop on the back half.

Returning early and leaving the list broken. return false inside the compare loop skips the restore. Break, restore, then return.

Calling the recursion O(1) space. The call stack is O(n). Interviewers ask this on purpose.

Hashing or building a number. Turning the digits into one integer overflows after 19 digits; a rolling hash is a probabilistic answer to a problem that has an exact one.
