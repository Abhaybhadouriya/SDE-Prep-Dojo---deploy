The story

Explain like I'm 5

A train of numbered wagons is parked on a one-way track: you can only walk from the engine toward the end. Is the number sequence the same read from both ends? Send a fast runner (two wagons per step) and a slow one (one wagon per step); when the fast one reaches the end, the slow one is in the middle. Now turn the back half of the train around on the spot, so it faces the engine. Walk from both fronts at once and compare. When you are done, turn the back half around again so nobody notices.

From your world

It is the kernel's list_head problem without the prev pointer: when a structure is singly linked (an sk_buff fragment list, a free list), "walk from both ends" needs a second pointer per node, an O(n) stack, or a temporary in-place relink. The last one is what we do here, and like any in-place relink in a driver, it must be undone before anyone else looks at the list.
