Example 3, [5,0,8,0,5].

  1. Start: both pointers on the head. fast moves 2 nodes per step, slow moves 1.
  2. Step 1: fast can make two more hops, so both advance.
  3. Step 2: fast reaches the tail and stops. slow is the middle node (8). For an even list it would be the last node of the first half.
  4. Reverse the back half in place (the same 3-pointer loop as Reverse Linked List). Now the tail is the head of the back half.
  5. Compare: front 5 vs back 5. Equal, step both.
  6. Front 0 vs back 0. Equal. b becomes null, so we stop. The middle 8 is never compared: it mirrors itself.
  7. Reverse the back half again and hang it on slow: the caller gets the list unchanged. Answer: true.
