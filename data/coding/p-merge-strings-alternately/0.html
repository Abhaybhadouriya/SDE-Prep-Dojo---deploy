The story

Explain like I'm 5

Two children are threading beads on one shared string, taking turns: first child, second child, first, second... When one child runs out of beads, the other simply keeps threading until they are done too. The finished string is the answer.

The only decisions are "who goes first in a round" (always word1) and "what if one runs out" (skip that child). Both are a single if.

From your world

This is round-robin scheduling of two queues: each round serves one item from queue A, then one from queue B, skipping an empty queue. A NIC interleaving two TX rings with equal weight does the same. The Zigzag Iterator follow-up is this loop over k rings.
