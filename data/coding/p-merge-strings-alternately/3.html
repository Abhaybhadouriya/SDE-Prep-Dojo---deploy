Recognise the pattern

Cue: two sequences consumed in lockstep, each step taking from both, "the rest of the longer one goes at the end" -> one shared index (or two pointers) with an existence check per sequence. It is the merge step of merge sort with the comparison replaced by "alternate".

Try a few:

word1word2MergedNote
"a""b""ab"One round.
"q""rstu""qrstu"Round 0 takes q and r; the rest is word2's tail.
"aaaa""bb""ababaa"Two full rounds, then word1's tail.
