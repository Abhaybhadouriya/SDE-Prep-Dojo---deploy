The problem (in our words)

You get an m x n matrix matrix whose entries are the characters '0' and '1'. Find the largest square sub-matrix that contains only '1's and return its area (side × side). If there is no '1', return 0.

Signature: int maximalSquare(char[][] matrix).

  • m == matrix.length, n == matrix[i].length
  • 1 <= m, n <= 300
  • matrix[i][j] is '0' or '1'.

Practice it: LeetCode 221 · GfG Largest Square of 1s in Matrix

Constraints tell you: up to 9 · 104 cells. O(m·n) is instant; O(m·n·min(m,n)) (grow each square) is about 2.7 · 107 and passes; checking every cell of every candidate square (O(m·n·k3)) does not. The cells are characters, not ints, in C++/Java/Python.
