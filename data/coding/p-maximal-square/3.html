Recognise the pattern

Cue: "largest square of 1s" -> anchor the shape at one corner and DP over corners: side[r][c] = 1 + min(up, left, up-left). "Largest rectangle of 1s" is different: histogram + monotonic stack (Maximal Rectangle).

The anchor choice is the design decision: "largest square ending at this cell" has a recurrence over earlier cells in row-major order, while "largest square containing this cell" does not. The same anchor works for counting all squares (LC 1277: sum of all side values).
