The story

Explain like I'm 5

A field is split into plots; some are good soil (1), some are rock (0). You want to lay the biggest square carpet of good soil. Stand on a good plot and treat it as the carpet's bottom-right corner. The carpet can only be as big as the carpets that end just above you, just left of you and diagonally up-left of you allow: the weakest of the three decides. Add your own plot and the carpet grows by one.

Brute force checks every square and is O((m·n)2) or worse. The DP is short but the recurrence is not obvious, so interviewers expect you to explain why the minimum of three neighbours is right. That proof is the core of this page.

From your world

Think of the largest contiguous free block in a 2D allocation bitmap (for example free tiles in a frame buffer or free cells in a page-table-like grid). The DP is a single raster scan with one row of state, the same access pattern as a streaming filter over image rows.
