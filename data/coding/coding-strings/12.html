Revision card

  • Cue: Anagrams, palindromes, parsing (atoi), encoding a list, reversing words in place.
  • Insight: Strings are arrays of a small alphabet: count with arrays, move with two pointers, build with a builder.
  • State: int[26] / int[128] counts; read and write heads; centres for palindromes.
  • Trap: + in a loop on immutable strings is O(n^2); overflow before r * 10 + d; isdigit() accepts non-ASCII digits.
  • Cost: Usually O(n) time, O(1) or O(alphabet) space.

More to remember

  • Know your language: immutable strings plus + in a loop means O(n2). Use a builder.
  • Small alphabet: count with int[26] / int[128], not a map.
  • In place: two pointers from the ends, or a read head and a write head that never overtakes it.
  • Palindromic substrings: 2n-1 centres, expand, O(n2) time, O(1) space. Name Manacher for O(n).
  • Parsing: list the parts in order, walk once, check overflow before you multiply.
  • Encode lists with a length prefix; it is TLV.
  • Linear matching: KMP lps (fall back with k = lps[k-1]), rolling hash, or the s + s trick.
  • Substring = contiguous (this page or sliding window); subsequence = DP.
