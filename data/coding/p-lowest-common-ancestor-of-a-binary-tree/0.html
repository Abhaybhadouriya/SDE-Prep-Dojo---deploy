The story

Explain like I'm 5

A big company has an org chart. Two employees, Priya and Quentin, need one manager to approve their joint project, and it must be the lowest manager who has both of them somewhere in their team. Ask every manager the same question: "Is Priya or Quentin in your team?" A manager asks each of their two direct reports and waits. If one report says "Priya is down here" and the other says "Quentin is down here", that manager is the one: the two paths split right at their desk. If only one report says yes, the manager just passes that answer upward.

There is one more case: if Priya herself manages Quentin, Priya is the answer. The recursion handles it for free: a call that reaches Priya returns immediately, and nobody above ever finds Quentin on a different branch.

From your world

Finding the common ancestor of two cgroups, two directories (the longest common prefix of their paths) or two processes in a process tree is LCA. With parent pointers (like task_struct->real_parent) it becomes "walk both up and find where the chains meet", which is the LC 1650 variant below.
