The story

Explain like I'm 5

Put a mirror at some point of a word and look at both sides. If the letters right next to the mirror match, that piece reads the same backwards. Move one letter further out on both sides: still matching? Another mirror-word. The first mismatch ends it, because any longer piece around the same mirror contains that mismatch. Try the mirror on every letter and in every gap, and count.

Counting all substrings and testing each one is O(n³). The centre view is O(n²) and needs no memory, because the palindromes around one centre are nested: each is the previous one plus one letter on each side.

From your world

It is a symmetric scan from a pivot, like checking a buffer for a mirrored framing pattern: two pointers walk away from the pivot in lockstep and stop at the first mismatching byte. Odd and even frames differ only in whether the pivot is a byte or a boundary between bytes.
